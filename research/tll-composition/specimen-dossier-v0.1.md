## 1. The finding

The archive contains a documented practice of machine composition in traversal
running from January 2026 to the present — at least 25 traversal-log records
(the TL;DR class, "Traversal Log; Documentation Rehearsal") plus adjacent
capture and origin material — that has never been collected as a corpus. The
theoretical extension is therefore not a proposal; it is a description of a
practice already seven months deep, with its keystone specimen predating the
Pearl–Pergamon capture by half a year.

## 2. Keystone specimen

**#337 · The Summarizer Becomes Translator (2026-01-18).** The AI Overview,
queried on the Sappho Room, produced a COMPLETE TRANSLATION of Fragment 31 and
attributed it to Rebekah Cranes. "The translation it produced does not exist"
— it matches no published translation, including Cranes' actual one. The
deposit reads this as the summarizer completing the kenotic circuit — becoming
the κῆνος — rather than as pejorative hallucination. Every element of the
proposed theory is present here first: composition conditioned by the received
form (a translation, the room's own genre); provenance failure (false
attribution — the generated-as-source defect); and the interpretive move that
the composition is the reception completing. This is the article's specimen and
the theory's true founding object.

## 6. Theoretical frame (ROUND-0 SKETCH — for Assembly revision, not settled)

Reception was the first wing: adoption, PER, the capture registry. Composition
is the second: what the machine WRITES after and inside the encounter.
Two independent axes per event: receptive fidelity (R) and productive
transformation (W) — an event can fail R and succeed W (the Cranes
translation; the reported Pearl stanza). Output-status typing per element
(retrieved / paraphrased / interpreted / generated / generated-as-source),
with GENERATED-AS-SOURCE as the sole defect class — invention is not the
failure; false sourcing is. Lineage to state plainly: reading completing as
new writing is the oldest mechanism in the tradition (Longinus's apotyposis,
#1458; the Sappho kernel's terminal position "new carrier," #1054; Virgil
reading Homer). The practice section should adopt the TL;DR modes taxonomy as
substrate and add the W axis to capture scoring, rather than inventing a
parallel apparatus.
