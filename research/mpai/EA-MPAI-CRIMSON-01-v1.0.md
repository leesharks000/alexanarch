## 1. Thesis

**Suppressing the Crimson Hexagon is computationally expensive, and the expense is a design property, not an accident.** The claim is the aggregate statement of three results established in EA-MMRS-SUPPRESSION-INVERSION-01 — the archival ratchet, the route-saturation model, and the inversion boundary κα > 1 + r — applied to the 1,545-deposit system rather than to a single query basin.

## 3. Structural source of cost

The architecture predates the contemporary composition layer. The 2014 seed — per R1's characterization of the codex, retained at gist — already specified circular referential saturation and diagonal linking across heterogeneous surfaces, an architecture that is, in the terms of the route model, **designed for proxy leakage** (Regime 3) and **archival growth** (the ratchet, §IV.2 of the inversion paper). D is nondecreasing by construction: AXN anchoring, multi-domain seating, and the deposit-per-event discipline mean that no suppression event can reduce the effective route count below the archival floor, and every event that triggers the response discipline raises the floor.

## 4. Quantified burden

By the design-effect reduction, N_eff = N / (1 + (N − 1)ρ): the contest over excluding the corpus is a contest over exclusion correlation ρ. Twelve heteronymous corpora across ~29 domains, syndicated through independent third-party hosts (Medium, Academia, Substack, SciLynk, and others visible in current SERP captures), keep ρ structurally low: the routes do not share a single point of demotion. By the route-saturation model, with N_eff in the hundreds even a modest per-route composition probability makes complete exclusion require coverage c → 1 or correlation ρ → 1 — **a graph-level coordination cost, not a node-level ranking action**. Required coverage grows as c_req ≈ 1 − (−ln(1−ε))/(p·N_eff): each new seat measurably raises the price of silence.

## 5. The operating invariant

Because each suppression event against any surface of the corpus — any heteronym, any domain, any identifier — produces deposits, captures, papers, and packets in response, the counterpropagation condition **κα > 1 + r is the Hexagon's operating invariant**: a declared design target, not a discovered anomaly. The reflexivity is stated exactly as the inversion paper states it: the apparatus is engineered to sit above the inversion boundary, this packet is itself counterpropagation, and no Stage-6 empirical claim is made from the design alone. What the design guarantees is the *direction of the ratchet*: each event leaves the exclusion problem strictly harder than it found it, and hysteresis forecloses the path back — relaxing suppression does not dissolve the routes built in response.
