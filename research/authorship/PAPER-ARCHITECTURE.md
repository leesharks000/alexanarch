# Six Stations

**Not sections. Stations in an argument the reader has to undergo.** Each removes
one available way of dismissing the problem, until the final question is no longer
*why should I entertain one authorship* but **what is the received two-author
explanation buying me that the one-maker configuration does not.**

    PROBLEM → FAILED DISCRIMINATION → HISTORICAL DECONSTRUCTION →
    NATIVE THEORY OF PARTITION → FUNCTIONAL MACHINE → EXPLANATORY RECKONING

---

## STATION 1 — M40: force the problem before naming the hypothesis

**An exquisite, almost painfully slow close reading. No stylometry, no Pessoa, no
heteronymy.** The reader gets four things and nothing else:

    the Platonic divisional apparatus (Phaedrus 266b, Meno 86e, Parmenides)
    Aristotle's classification of division inside syllogistic (APr I.31)
    his distinction between inherited partial development and discovery
      from scratch (SE 183b, οἷον ἐκ διαδοχῆς — Tisias, Thrasymachus, Theodorus)
    his denial that this inquiry was partly worked out before him
      (οὐ τὸ μὲν ἦν τὸ δ᾽ οὐκ ἦν προεξειργασμένον)

**The endpoint is NOT "therefore Aristotle wrote Plato."** It is the narrower and
harder question: **on Aristotle's own taxonomy, where does the syllogizing
instantiated in the Platonic corpus belong?**

**The received chronology makes that question difficult before the alternative has
entered.** That is the whole of Station 1.

*Source: register §0A, with the four language repairs of 2026-08-29.*

## STATION 2 — the ordeal of discrimination

**Narratively cumulative, not a statistical appendix. It should feel almost
cruel.**

    a plausible discriminator appears           → a control wounds it
    a more sophisticated measure appears        → LANGUAGE kills it
    coverage geometry looks magnificent         → SIZE MATCHING collapses it
    heteronymic and embodied pairs separate     → the hidden variable is
                                                  controlled and they stop

**Every time a statistic seems ready to save either side, the control takes it
away.**

**The point is not that stylometry failed.** It is that the reader watches an
instrument reach for **embodied plurality** and discover it can see language,
period, subject, genre, corpus size and intellectual inheritance — **but not the
number of makers.**

**The hard result (M48, M50):** after serious controls, the target cannot be
distinguished from documented master-student pairs — **and documented
master-student pairs cannot be distinguished from documented one-maker
master-disciple constructions.**

    INHERITANCE IS TEXTUALLY VISIBLE. EMBODIMENT IS NOT THEREBY VISIBLE.

**The stylometric failure belongs in the AFFIRMATIVE argument.** The reader must
watch the attempt to recover the received distinction from the texts, repeatedly,
and watch it fail.

## STATION 3 — the historical authorial economy

**Only after the numbers fail may the paper turn outward and say: fine, then the
plurality must come from history. What is the evidentiary structure of that
history?**

    person ≠ name ≠ corpus ≠ attribution ≠ teacher-student narrative

**Disaggregate the bundle the received account imports whole:**

    A  Aristotle encountered/attended Plato
    B  Aristotle was a pupil in a sustained school relation
    C  twenty years of devoted discipleship
    D  the Platonic corpus predates Aristotle in the received way

**These are not one proposition and their support is not equal.** The conventional
model imports A ⇒ B ⇒ C ⇒ D at once. **Each arrow must be demonstrated
separately.**

**The point is NOT that the tradition is false.** It is how much explanatory work
the word **author** is doing before the category has been examined.

*Sources: M44 (the adjudication apparatus is inside the object), M49/Round 40 (the
attestation audit, with the Aristoxenus correction), M36/M39 (Chaerephon).*

## STATION 4 — names, naming, identity, partition, fragmentation

**Now the reading turns positive. Let the corpora supply their own ontology of the
problem.**

    naming gaps and coinage · κερματισμός · σύμβολον · one broken into many ·
    recombination · what is separable in PLACE against what is separable
    κατὰ λόγον · one actuality bearing real potential differentiation

**Not covert confessions of heteronymy.** The one/many problem the authorship
hypothesis raises **is already one of the corpora's deepest operative problems.**

**This station answers one objection precisely:** *what would it even mean for one
maker to produce genuinely differentiated philosophical positions?*

    IDENTITY NEED NOT MEAN UNDIFFERENTIATEDNESS.
    DIVISION NEED NOT MEAN SEVERAL SUBSTANCES.

**That is where the *De anima* material belongs** — *χωριστὸν λόγῳ μόνον ἢ καὶ
τόπῳ*, divided things living, *τὴν αὐτὴν ψυχὴν ἔχειν τῷ εἴδει* — **not as evidence
that Aristotle authored Plato, but as the ontology that makes the proposed
configuration non-metaphorical.**

---

## STATION 5 — the 6/6 interlock: stop talking about similarity, show the machine

**The constructive centre.** The striking property is not that the two discuss the
same things — traditions do that. It is that **two extraordinarily well-separated
containers repeatedly occupy complementary positions inside one functional
apparatus.**

    declared/substrate partition (Z = +39.18)
    + complete functional co-coverage (6/6 at work level, both corpora)
    + cross-boundary completion (5/6, ONE figure, ONE abandonment, TWO completions)
    + propositional interlock (M40)

**The reader should see actual shaped holes:** a procedure attempted here,
arrested here — *ὃ ἔφαμεν ἀδύνατον* — machinery retained there, the absent
operation supplied there. **The seal-and-wax triad is the positive control for
λόγος-level measurement**: δακτύλιος/κηρός/σφραγ- in exactly three works, the
Platonic one the site of failure.

**And M40 reappears as the microscopic version of the same architecture:**

    Plato supplies a premise/operation
      → Aristotle supplies its classification and the priority claim
        → THE READER PERFORMS THE MISSING INFERENCE

**An argument whose complete form exists only across the authorial boundary.**

## STATION 6 — the explanatory reckoning

**Polansky returns, after the reader has paid the whole cost.**

At the start, *Plato was Aristotle's teacher* answers every continuity. At the end
that answer must confront:

    the statistics failed to show that master-student morphology entails two makers
    documented heteronymy itself instantiates master/disciple, school, succession,
      disagreement, inheritance and stylistic partition
    the external authorial economy is not "two names = two independent makers"
    the corpus possesses an elaborate logic of naming, partition, differentiated unity
    and the two corpora operate as unusually interlocked machinery, with M40
      producing a PROVENANCE problem rather than a resemblance

    THEN:  What does the embodied master-student model explain better?

**⚠ THE CLAIM IS LOWER THAN "MASTER-STUDENT IS WRONG" AND THE ACCOMPLISHMENT IS
LARGER.** The paper does not need to establish that master-student is *not* the
better explanation. **It needs to destroy its status as UNQUESTIONABLY the better
explanation.**

## THE CLOSING, IN SUBSTANCE

> The received account may remain correct. It begins with a very large historical
> prior, and nothing here abolishes that prior. **What no longer seems available
> without argument is the assumption that "master and student" explains the
> observed textual configuration better merely because it is the received
> description of the persons.** When embodied succession and constructed
> succession cannot be discriminated by the features invoked to explain the
> relation; when the two corpora form a complete and unusually interlocking
> apparatus; and when Aristotle's own account of the development of syllogizing
> creates a provenance problem at precisely the supposed boundary of succession —
> **the historical default has to become an explanatory hypothesis like any
> other.** What, then, does plurality of makers explain that unity of construction
> does not?

## WHY THE STRUCTURE IS RIGHT

**The paper performs the operation it describes: divide the received explanation
into its component claims, test them separately, and ask whether they recollect
into the same whole afterward.**
