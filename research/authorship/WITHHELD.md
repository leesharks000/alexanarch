# The Many and One paper files are not in this directory

The working set of the authorship investigation lives here: the notebook, the
measurement register, the frozen pre-registrations, the section files, the
apparatus, the architecture, the data extractions and the early drafts.

Two files that were formerly in this directory are not:

- `MANY-AND-ONE-COMPLETE.md` — the assembled reading copy
- `MANY-AND-ONE-MAXIMAL.md` — the maximal assembly

They are withheld from publication while the article drawn from them is under
review at a journal, because a publicly posted assembly of the same argument
would be a prior circulation of the submitted paper. They are held privately and
dated; they will be deposited or restored here when the journal has decided.

The article version is not in this repository at any time.

The records intended for public deposit — the notebook (EA-NOTEBOOK-01), the
measurement register (EA-AUTHORSHIP-MEASURES-01) and the pre-registration ledger
(PREREG-LEDGER) — carry their own AXN identifiers through the ordinary deposit
pipeline and are served as attachments under `/data/attachments/`.

Withheld 2026-09-01 by operator ruling.
