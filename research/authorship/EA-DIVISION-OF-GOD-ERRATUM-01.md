# ERRATUM TO THE CORRECTION

**1.** v0.1 was wrong at its foundation: it claimed *APr* I.31 never supplies the
positive of **οὔτε ὅ τι ἐνδέχεται συλλογίσασθαι διαιρούμενοι ξυνίεσαν**. It supplies
it eleven words later, in letters, and names it — **ὁ μὲν οὖν συλλογισμός ἐστιν** …
**τὸν ἄνθρωπον ἢ θνητὸν μὲν ἢ ἀθάνατον ἀναγκαῖον εἶναι**. That correction stands and
is not revisited.

**2.** **The correction then took three things it had no warrant to take.** This
erratum returns them, in the form the primary texts support, and develops the
argument the return makes available.

---

## I. THE DIVISION OF GOD — RETURNED, AT A DIFFERENT LOCUS

**3.** v0.1 said III.5 "divides the divine." v0.2 struck it, and struck the title
with it. **The strike was right about III.5 and wrong about the corpus.**

**4.** *Metaphysics* Λ 9, 1074b, divides the first substance by name and by the
divisional form:

> ἔτι δὲ εἴτε **νοῦς** ἡ οὐσία αὐτοῦ εἴτε **νόησίς** ἐστι, **τί νοεῖ;** ἢ γὰρ
> **αὐτὸς αὑτὸν ἢ ἕτερόν τι**· καὶ εἰ ἕτερόν τι, **ἢ τὸ αὐτὸ ἀεὶ ἢ ἄλλο.**

**A genus cut at a differentia, and the branch cut again.** The ἢ … ἢ of I.31's
worked example, applied to God.

**5.** And the branches fall by the divisional argument: were it **δύναμις** the
continuity would be laborious; thought another, **ἄλλο τι ἂν εἴη τὸ τιμιώτερον ἢ ὁ
νοῦς, τὸ νοούμενον**. Conclusion: **αὑτὸν ἄρα νοεῖ … καὶ ἔστιν ἡ νόησις νοήσεως
νόησις.**

**6.** ⭑ **THE ASYMMETRY THIS BUYS, WHICH NEITHER PRIOR DRAFT HAD.**

    I.31, of man     division reaches  mortal | immortal   CANNOT SELECT — αἰτεῖται
    Λ 9, of God      division reaches  itself | another    SELECTS — by collapse

**Division fails at man and succeeds at God, and succeeds only because on this one
object the two branches turn out to be one.** Where the divided thing is genuinely
one, division terminates. Where it is not, division reaches the seam and asks to be
given what it was required to prove.

**7.** And the collapse does not hold. The next clause reopens it: **ἔτι εἰ ἄλλο τὸ
νοεῖν καὶ τὸ νοεῖσθαι, κατὰ πότερον αὐτῷ τὸ εὖ ὑπάρχει;** — *if thinking and
being-thought are different, in respect of which does its good belong?* **The
identity is asserted and queried in the same breath.**

---

## II. THE SOCKET — RETURNED, WITH THE CATEGORY CORRECTED

**8.** v0.1 called **ἄνευ τούτου οὐθὲν νοεῖ** a hanging line of Longinus's kind.
v0.2 struck the comparison as a conflation of syntactic incompleteness with
referential underdetermination. **The distinction is right. The strike went one step
too far** — it removed the category along with the misapplication.

**9.** Separate them properly. **They are two species of one genus, and the genus is
what matters.**

    SYNTACTIC INCOMPLETION      ἐπεὶ καὶ πένητα — a subordinator with no main
                                clause, an accusative with no verb. The GRAMMAR
                                specifies what must come.

    REFERENTIAL INDETERMINACY   ἄνευ τούτου οὐθὲν νοεῖ — complete syntax; τούτου
                                has a function but no determinable referent, and
                                οὐθέν parses as subject or object. The SEMANTICS
                                specify what must be decided.

    GENUS                       AN OPENING WHOSE FILLING IS SPECIFIED AND
                                WITHHELD, such that the text cannot be used
                                without a reader supplying the missing term.

**10.** **Both are sockets. They differ in which layer holds the specification.**
v0.1 collapsed the species; v0.2 denied the genus. **The genus is real and is what
the argument runs on.**

**11.** And the Λ passage is a third species. **ἡ νόησις νοήσεως νόησις** is neither
incomplete nor ambiguous. **It is complete, unambiguous, and unusable without being
performed** — a formula that states an identity a reader can only verify by
executing it, since thinking-of-thinking is instantiated rather than described.
**Specification withheld at the level of the operation, not the grammar or the
reference.**

    LONGINUS   grammar withheld       supply the verb
    III.5      reference withheld     supply the referent
    Λ 9        operation withheld     supply the thinking

## III. RECEPTION AS MEASUREMENT — RETURNED, NARROWED

**12.** v0.1 argued that eighteen centuries of irreducible disagreement over III.5
is evidence about the passage rather than about its readers. v0.2 dropped it with
the God material. **It should not have gone, but it must be narrowed.**

**13.** **The bad form:** *the tradition never resolves it, therefore it was built
not to resolve.* That is unfalsifiable and would make every hard passage designed.

**14.** **The defensible form, and the distinction is testable:** obscurity produces
**abandonment**; a socket produces **recruitment**.

    ABANDONED     corrupt or damaged passages accumulate conjectures, then a
                  scholarly consensus that the text is unsound, and then silence.
                  The literature THINS.

    RECRUITED     III.5 draws Alexander, Themistius, Averroes, a condemnation,
                  Aquinas's De unitate intellectus written expressly against it,
                  and university legislation. The literature THICKENS, and each
                  entrant supplies the withheld term and hands on a new one.

**15.** **That is a real difference and it is checkable in the citation record.** It
does not establish design. **It establishes that the passage behaves as a socket
behaves and not as damage behaves**, which is the narrower claim and the one worth
making.

**16.** And the reception performs the doctrine it cannot settle. The passage says
the immortal part **οὐ μνημονεύομεν** — does not remember. **The tradition remembers
for it.** Without the perishable commentary, **οὐθὲν νοεῖ**: nothing is thought.

---

## IV. AND THE TURN THAT SURVIVES BOTH DRAFTS

**17.** v0.1 collapsed μνήμη into ἀνάμνησις. That error is not returned — it was
wrong, and it worked **against** the argument it was serving.

**18.** *De mem.* 451a: **οὔτε μνήμης ἐστὶν ἀνάληψις ἡ ἀνάμνησις οὔτε λῆψις** —
recollection is neither the taking of memory nor its retaking. 453a: **οἷον
συλλογισμός τις.** And: **ἀδυνατεῖ ἀναμνησθῆναι, ζητεῖν δὲ δύναται καὶ εὑρίσκει** —
unable to recollect, still able to search and find.

**19.** So **οὐ μνημονεύομεν** does not close the route. **Memory is what perishes.
Search is a syllogism.** The chapter names the faculty that goes and leaves standing
the one the whole treatise is about.

**21.** **The wheel closes and does not stop, which is the only behaviour consistent
with every locus in it.** Man's division cannot select. God's selects and is queried
again. The intellect's is left forked. **And the operation that would cross any of
these seams is, by Aristotle's own classification, a search that is not memory —
available to a later reader who never held the thing sought.**

---

## VI. WHAT THIS ERRATUM CLAIMS

**22.** **Returned:** the divine division (at Λ, not III.5); the socket as a genus
with three species; reception-behaviour as evidence about the passage, narrowed to
the recruitment/abandonment distinction.

**23.** **Not returned:** the memory/recollection collapse; the identification of
III.5's active intellect with divine νοῦς; the claim that I.31 leaves an open slot.

**24.** **Not claimed anywhere:** authorship. **A single author writing the
*Analytics*, then the *De anima*, then Λ produces this sequence** — and so does a
school. The sequence is offered as a structure in the corpus.

**25.** **And the erratum chain is itself part of the record.** v0.1 overclaimed;
v0.2 overcorrected; this returns what the overcorrection took. **Three passes, each
supplying what the last withheld, none final.** The document behaves the way its
object behaves, which is noted and not offered as evidence for anything.
