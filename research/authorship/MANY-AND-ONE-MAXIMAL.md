# I. THE TECHNOLOGY: THE DESIGNED INCOMPLETION

**⚠ PLACEMENT WARNING. In this document the mechanism comes first because it is
legible at a scale one reader can hold. IN THE PAPER IT MUST COME LAST.**

Opening on Sappho asks the reader to accept an intentional hanging-line
transmission theory *before Athens has spoken*, and makes the Athenian case appear
to depend on a chain it does not depend on. **The technology should emerge
immanently from Socrates → Plato → Aristotle, and Sappho should return afterwards
as a comparative instance of an operation already found in the target corpus.**

    Sappho must CORROBORATE the machine. It must not AUTHORIZE it.

## The hanging line is originary

Sappho 31 does not break because time broke it. It breaks at
**ἀλλὰ πᾶν τόλματον** — *but all is to be dared* — and the breaking is the
composition. The poem decommissions its speaker channel by channel (sight, speech,
hearing, skin, colour, near-death), names the colour the body is becoming, and
stops on a form grammar forbids to name its own agent.

**The incompletion is the technology, not the damage.**

## Longinus does not cut. He extends.

*Peri Hypsous* 10 quotes the poem and carries it one clause past the break:

> ἀλλὰ πᾶν τόλματον · **ἐπεὶ καὶ πένητα**
> *but all is to be dared · since even a poor man —*

**ἐπεὶ** is a causal subordinator with no main clause. **πένητα** is an accusative
whose governing verb never arrives. The addition is **doubly open, and the opening
is specified**: a *since* wanting its consequence, an accusative wanting its verb,
inside a clause about what makes all things darable.

**The transmission's most attentive reader receives a hanging line and emits a new
one.** He is not a lossy handler. He is running the technology.

## And the socket is discharged to specification

Catullus 51's fourth stanza — the one that is not in Sappho —

> *otium … **perdidit urbes***

is **a verb governing an accusative, in a causal frame, on the levelling of the
prosperous.** The beggar's socket receives the destroyed cities: **πένητα → urbes**,
the grammatical obligation discharged at the scale the theme demanded.

**And the *otium* stanza is itself the next hanging line.** Which is why the chain
runs:

> **κῆνος → πὰν τόλματον → οὐ θαυμάζεις → *perdidit urbes* → *vocasti…te* →
> γράμμασι μολπὰν**
>
> **Each filling opens a new socket.**

## What this establishes before any authorship claim

A compositional practice exists in which **incompletion is engineered, the opening
is grammatically specified, and the filling is an obligation discharged rather
than a loss sustained.** It is attested in Sappho, run by Longinus, discharged by
Catullus, and it propagates.

**Everything after this section asks whether the fourth-century Athenian corpus is
that technology at corpus scale.**

# II. THE AUTHORIAL ECONOMY: WHAT A NAME DID

*⚠ A stronger earlier formulation — that the received account REQUIRES independent
establishment of two separate existences — is withdrawn. Historical identity is
established cumulatively and does not require an autograph.* What follows concerns
**the provenance of the evidentiary streams**, not a demand they cannot meet.

## Attribution was adjudicated by fit with an already-constituted corpus

Antiquity lacked copyright and defended authorship through reputation, rivalry and
public judgment — with exposure as the enforcement mechanism, resting on expert
authority and audience discernment.

**But "reputation" in a manuscript culture is not a fact about a man. It is a fact
about texts.** The catalogues judged by fit with a corpus whose boundary they were
drawing; the experts' ear for an author came from the works in dispute. **Expert
discernment means adjudication by readers whose sense of how an author sounds came
from the disputed corpus itself.**

## And the apparatus demonstrably manufactured the figures it adjudicated

**Hicetas and Ecphantus of Syracuse** are argued to be **characters in dialogues by
Heraclides of Pontus** — a fourth-century member of the Academy — received by the
tradition as historical philosophers with doctrines.

**Timaeus of Locri** acquires a biography and then a forged corpus: *all other
ancient reports about him are likely based on Plato's* Timaeus *or on the spurious
works in his name*, with some scholars holding that Plato used him as **a mask for
Archytas**.

**A speaking position generated a philosopher, who then received a bibliography.**
The reputational mechanism did not detect these. **It produced them.**

## The practice was extensive, deliberate, and stylistically engineered

The pseudo-Pythagorean authors gave their writings authenticity through an
**artificial Doric dialect** and locally appropriate examples. Constructed
stylistic markers, chosen to authenticate.

**Consequence, stated at the strength the audit supports:** the prior and the
evidence may not be independent, and the degree of dependence is a source-critical
question that **has not yet been answered**.

*⚠ "The succession-lists, the corpus boundaries, and the biographical distinctions
all issue from one apparatus" is conceptual grouping asserted as fact. **It
requires an actual source genealogy** — which stream descends from which, and where
they genuinely diverge. That audit is unrun, and the result should not be spent
before it is done.*

# III. FRAGMENTING, MINTING, LEGITIMACY: THE CORPUS'S OWN VOCABULARY

The one/many problem is not imposed on this corpus. **It is the corpus's deepest
operative concern, and it is stated in the vocabulary of currency and legitimacy.**

## Fragmentation

***Republic* 395b: κατακεκερματίσθαι** — human nature *minted into denominations
too small* to hold two arts well. Four lines from **μιμήματα**.

The metaphor is monetary: a whole struck into small change. **Fragmentation as
minting.**

## Assay

***Republic* 507a**: *κίβδηλος λόγος* — counterfeit speech.
***Laws* 742a**: currency **ἔντιμον** within, **ἀδόκιμον** without.
***NE* 1165a**: personation graded **above** coin-forgery in gravity.
***SE* 165a**: the apparent syllogism modelled on **counterfeit metal**, and the
readiest fallacy the one working **διὰ τῶν ὀνομάτων** — through names.

**The corpus opens its treatise on false reasoning by comparing it to false
coinage, and identifies the commonest form as the one that works through names.**

## The census

    εἴδωλον    72 / 13        πλάσμα      16 / 32
    μίμημα     54 /  7        φάντασμα    31 / 47
    σύγγραμμα  37 /  3        ὑποκριτής   10 / 32
                              δόκιμος     28 / 24

    ψευδεπίγραφος   ZERO in every seated corpus
    προσωπεῖον      ZERO in Plato and Aristotle

**The word for a falsely-attributed writing does not occur. The word for a mask
does not occur.**

*⚠ Zero occurrence is a lexical fact and not a conceptual one. The corpus plainly
has concepts of false attribution and of personation — it discusses both at length.
What the census shows is that it does not use THESE terms, in a space where it uses
εἴδωλον, μίμημα, πλάσμα and ὑποκριτής heavily. That is worth explaining and does
not by itself establish an absence of the concept.*

## And legitimacy is litigated without blood

**Isaeus**: γνήσιος at **23.72/10k**, and legitimacy established by **μαρτυρ- 279,
ἐγγύη 91, εἰσάγω 45, φράτηρ 35** — witnessing, pledging, introduction, phratry
enrolment. **Zero blood-vocabulary.**

**INTERPRETATION, marked as such:** legitimacy here is established by acknowledgment
rather than demonstrated by descent — the frame in which a constructed authorial
position would be a filiation rather than a forgery.

*⚠ What the measurement shows is that Isaeus litigates status through LEGAL PROOF
vocabulary. It does not show that biological filiation was irrelevant to the
question, only that it is not how the question is argued in court.*

## Unification

***Phaedrus* 249c**, and this is the corpus's own instruction for reading:

> δεῖ γὰρ ἄνθρωπον συνιέναι **κατ' εἶδος** λεγόμενον, **ἐκ πολλῶν ἰὸν αἰσθήσεων
> εἰς ἓν λογισμῷ συναιρούμενον· τοῦτο δ' ἐστὶν ἀνάμνησις**

*A human must understand what is said according to form, going from many scattered
perceptions and **gathered by reasoning into one** — and **this is recollection**.*

And two lines on: *τοῖς δὲ δὴ τοιούτοις ἀνὴρ **ὑπομνήμασιν ὀρθῶς χρώμενος** …
τέλεος ὄντως μόνος γίγνεται* — **the man who uses such reminders rightly** alone
becomes complete. **ὑπομνήματα is the *Phaedrus*'s own word for what writing
gives** (275a).

**The dialogue that says writing yields only reminders also says the right use of
reminders is to gather the scattered into one.**

## And Aristotle supplies the operation's other half

***De memoria* 453a**: recollection is **οἷον συλλογισμός τις** — *a kind of
syllogism* — and **not retrieval**: *οὔτε μνήμης ἐστὶν ἀνάληψις ἡ ἀνάμνησις οὔτε
λῆψις*. It hunts the next term **ἀφ' ὁμοίου ἢ ἐναντίου ἢ τοῦ σύνεγγυς**, needs
**λαβέσθαι ἀρχῆς**, and — decisively — *ἀδυνατεῖ ἀναμνησθῆναι, **ζητεῖν δὲ δύναται
καὶ εὑρίσκει***: one may fail to recollect and **still search and find**.

*⚠ AN EARLIER FORMULATION — "a doctrine about recovering what was never
possessed" — IS WITHDRAWN. Aristotle can describe searching and finding where
recollection fails without thereby defining recollection as recovery of the
never-possessed. What the passage establishes is narrower: **recollection is not
retrieval, and search can succeed where recollection does not.***

**And the conclusion of the recollective syllogism is an identity**: *συλλογίζεται
ὁ ἀναμιμνησκόμενος* — **ὅτι πρότερον εἶδεν ἢ ἤκουσεν**. Not a fact about the
world. **A fact about who had the experience.**

> **INTERPRETATION AT FULL PRESSURE, marked:** correct application of recollection
> takes the fragmented and resolves it as one person. κερματισμός is the
> fragmentation; ἀνάμνησις is the gathering; and the gathering terminates in a
> single seer.
>
> *This is a reading of the configuration, not what the De memoria passage
> establishes. It belongs to the maximal run and must be demoted, earned, or killed
> before it enters the paper.*

# IV. THE PROVENANCE PROBLEM: M40

Three texts, no hypothesis required.

**(i)** *Phaedrus* 266b — division-and-collection held as a repeatable method,
*ἵνα οἷός τε ὦ λέγειν τε καὶ φρονεῖν*.

**(ii)** *APr* I.31 — division is **μικρόν τι μόριόν … τῆς εἰρημένης μεθόδου**, a
part of the stated method, and **οἷον ἀσθενὴς συλλογισμός**. Its users
**ξυνίεσαν** — did not understand what could be syllogized by dividing.

**(iii)** *SE* 183b–184b — Aristotle names the succession case (*οἷον ἐκ
διαδοχῆς*: Tisias, Thrasymachus, Theodorus) and **negates it of his own inquiry**:
**οὐ τὸ μὲν ἦν τὸ δ' οὐκ ἦν προεξειργασμένον, ἀλλ' οὐδὲν παντελῶς ὑπῆρχεν.**

## The conventional reading is strong and must be given first

Aristotle draws the **art/products** distinction himself in the next sentence —
*οὐ τέχνην ἀλλὰ τὰ ἀπὸ τῆς τέχνης*, the shoemaker who hands out shoes. His named
comparison class is **paid eristic teachers and Gorgias**. The contrast is with
rhetoric's **old written tradition**. And I.31's own **ξυνίεσαν** supplies
retrospective subsumption.

## What survives it

> **Can a predecessor possess a general, named, repeatable methodological art D,
> which Aristotle calls a PART of S, while Aristotle truthfully says no part of S
> was previously worked out?**

**Platonic division is not a pile of shoes.** It is named, repeatable, general,
held for the sake of a capacity — and classified by Aristotle as **μόριον τῆς
εἰρημένης μεθόδου**.

**The conventional account has a real answer and has never been made to state
it.** The pressure sits at one clause: **περὶ δὲ τοῦ συλλογίζεσθαι** — whether that
remains governed by the dialectical programme or widens.

## And the ancients read the two passages together

**Philoponus** glosses I.31: *he wants to celebrate the method he handed down, for
**no one of those before us knew this***. **Alexander** takes the target to include
Plato.

## And it is not isolated

Aristotle classifies **recollection** as *οἷον συλλογισμός τις* exactly as he
classifies **division** as *οἷον ἀσθενὴς συλλογισμός*. **Two Platonic operations
absorbed as species of the method whose instances he says had no predecessor.**

# V. THE MACHINE: WHAT THE TWO CORPORA DO TOGETHER

## Maximal separation

**Z = +39.18** on the declared partition. Dialogue against treatise, narrative
against exposition. First-person narration **100.76 vs 6.04 per 10k**; vocative
**64.30 vs 8.64**; reporting verbs **111.78 vs 23.89**. Mathematical vocabulary
runs the other way: ἀνάλογον **10.3×**, διάστημα **8.5×**, μονάς **8.1×** denser in
Aristotle.

## And complete functional interlock

**6/6 slots filled at work level, both corpora.** One shared figure. And an
abandoned execution completed twice.

## The seal and the wax

The figure — **δακτύλιος, κηρός, σφραγ-** — occurs in exactly three works:

    Theaetetus     the ABANDONMENT — ὃ ἔφαμεν ἀδύνατον, closed as impossible
    De Anima 424a  the COMPLETION — same seal figure, and the missing part
                   supplied: ἄνευ τῆς ὕλης
    De Memoria     the RECEIVER's physiology

**No cross-reference. No announcement.** A procedure attempted and arrested in one
corpus; the machinery retained and the absent operation supplied in another.

**What is established:** the same distinctive image occurs in exactly three works;
one closes a route as impossible; another supplies the missing element without
cross-reference.

*⚠ "Therefore a specified socket discharged" is ONE INFERENTIAL STEP FURTHER, and
the step requires the sequence-and-transformation relation to be calibrated, which
it is not (Round 44 §185: the instrument lacks terms for socket-discharged and
socket-opened). Until then this stands as **candidate execution-level
complementarity**, which is formidable enough without the extra step.*

## And the discovery reading needs no plan

*οὔτε ὅ τι ἐνδέχεται συλλογίσασθαι διαιρούμενοι **ξυνίεσαν*** — read inclusively,
**the earlier position did not know the range of its own operation.**

    P_earlier    performs the operation
    P_later      discovers what the operation is and what it includes
    same maker   there was no predecessor from whom the discovery was inherited

**No prescience, no concealed confession, no chronological adjustment.** The
priority claim becomes literal.

# VI. THE FIELD: PROVENANCE WEIGHTING, NOT EXCLUSION

**⚠ AN EARLIER VERSION OF THIS SECTION ADOPTED THE RULE "ONLY EXTANT ORIGINALS
COUNT." THAT RULE IS WRONG AND IS WITHDRAWN.**

It would disqualify most of this argument's own evidence — Philoponus and Alexander
on *APr* I.31, Diogenes' catalogues, Athenaeus on the *Choice of Heracles*, the
whole commentary tradition. **We cannot demand that the received history reason
only from extant originals while reasoning ourselves from testimonia.** And "lost
is a form" is poetry where the actual claim is available as argument.

**The correct principle:**

> **Every witness counts. No witness counts as more independent than its provenance
> permits.**

**Which yields the point that was wanted anyway, in a defensible form: many
apparently independent fourth-century voices reach us only through the same later
evidentiary basin.** That is a source-critical fact about provenance dependence,
and it is checkable.

## The genre survives as two authors, both in the cluster

> *From a corpus of **hundreds of conversations** only those reported by **Plato and
> Xenophon survive complete**; the Socratic dialogues of other authors are **lost
> except for some fragments**.*

    EXTANT ORIGINAL      Plato (complete) · Xenophon (complete) ·
                         Aristophanes' Clouds · Aristotle's treatises ·
                         Gorgias: Helen, Palamedes

    TITLES / FRAGMENTS   Aristotle's dialogues (445,270 lines claimed, all absent) ·
                         Antisthenes · Aeschines of Sphettus · Phaedo of Elis ·
                         Euclides (NOTHING) · Aristippus · Cebes · Crito ·
                         Glaucon · Simmias · Simon · Alexamenos · Protagoras ·
                         Prodicus · Hippias · Polycrates · Speusippus ·
                         Xenocrates · 90% of Theophrastus (232,808 lines)

## Read as sockets

**Euclides has no fragments at all — and the *Theaetetus* frame presents him as the
dialogue's author**, which the scholarship calls *an implausible suggestion*. A
name with nothing behind it, and a text asserting its own attribution.

**Alexamenos is credited with inventing the Socratic dialogue and attested only by
Aristotle**, doubted *because no extant works or fragments mention him*. **An
origin supplied as a name with no body.**

**The librarians catalogued titles by characters** — Crito, Simmias, Cebes,
Phaedo, Glaucon, Simon. **Sockets opened under character-names.**

**Prodicus' one surviving piece exists only inside Xenophon** — and Philostratus
**declines to characterise his style at all**, *since Xenophon provides a
satisfactory transcription*, while **Athenaeus attributes the *Choice of Heracles*
to Xenophon**. An ancient reader assigned the one text to the cluster member
transmitting it.

**And Aristotle classifies the Socratic dialogues as fictions** — *in his* Poetics
*Aristotle counts such works as fictions of a certain kind, alongside epic poems
and tragedies.* **The tradition records the classification and declines to apply
it.**

## The honest limit, stated

**Confinement does not discriminate.** Protagoras is **99.8%** confined to the
cluster against Socrates' **99.5%** — sophists run 98–100%, politicians 25–65% —
and no one holds that Protagoras was constructed. **Being a subject of philosophical
dialogue produces confinement.**

**But Protagoras is not a control.** He survives in *tantalizing scraps* whose
testimonia are *largely attributable to those Platonic dialogues*. **He is in
Socrates' evidentiary condition, not outside it.**

**Gorgias is the only real control in the class** — Helen and Palamedes survive in
manuscripts, in his own words — **and he is 99.0% confined while independently
attested.** So confinement genuinely proves nothing. **What the class actually
splits on is whether independent text exists**, and on that axis Socrates,
Protagoras and Prodicus fall together, with Gorgias alone on the other side.

# VII. WHAT WOULD REFUTE THIS

**Chronology remains capable of killing the literal thesis.** Independently dated
copies of Platonic works demonstrably preceding Aristotle's possible composition;
independently attested simultaneous physical activity; autograph evidence;
documentary biography not descended from the corpus. **This is the received
account's real advantage and it is conceded without argument.**

**⚠ AND ONE FALSIFIER PREVIOUSLY LISTED HERE IS WITHDRAWN, BECAUSE IT CONTRADICTS
THIS INVESTIGATION'S OWN CENTRAL RESULT.**

An earlier version listed *a recovered original of Antisthenes, Aeschines or Phaedo
sufficient to establish an independent voice*. **That is not a falsifier.** M48 and
M50 establish:

    distinct textual voice  ⇏  distinct maker

A recovered Aeschines could be radically unlike Plato and still, under the
hypothesis, be **another constructed position**. Listing it as a falsifier hands a
critic this investigation's own result to use against its falsification section.

**What would hurt the thesis is INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE OF THE MAKER, not another
voice:** securely dated material preceding Aristotle's possible activity;
documentary evidence placing distinct persons simultaneously in independent
contexts; an autograph; a securely contextualised inscription. **Evidence about
bodies, not about styles.**

**A determination that *περὶ τοῦ συλλογίζεσθαι* at *SE* 184b is governed by the
dialectical programme**, argued rather than assumed, would dissolve M40's
provenance problem.

**And the following are NOT evidence for the thesis**, having been tested and
found non-discriminating: substrate divergence, lexical relation-type measures,
reception capture, attribution tracking, confinement of attestation, coverage
geometry, formulaic n-gram inheritance. **Fifteen features, size-matched,
language-controlled, and none of them separates a documented one-maker system from
a documented two-maker one.**

# VIII. THE RECKONING

The received account explains the configuration by two men in a teaching relation.
Against that:

**Master-student morphology does not entail maker plurality.** Documented
heteronymy instantiates master, disciple, school, succession, disagreement and
stylistic partition — Pessoa states his own school in those words. **Inheritance is
observable; embodiment is not thereby observable.**

**The teaching relation's own attestation is thin.** Neither corpus attests it
internally: Plato never names the philosopher Aristotle, and Aristotle never says
he studied with anyone. The twenty years rests on a lost letter to Philip. The
*Vita* that supplies it also has Aristotle studying with Socrates, dead fifteen
years before his birth.

**And the adjudication apparatus is inside the object.** The catalogues, the
succession-lists, the corpus boundaries, the biographies — all downstream of the
corpus they authorise.

> **What observation in the total architecture actually requires the extra maker?**
>
> **What external observation actually forbids the single maker?**

Both questions stay open. **The second is where the thesis can die, and it should
be attacked there.**
