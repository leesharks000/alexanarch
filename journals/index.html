Journals

Seven chartered journals and one province. Every deposit in the archive is assigned to exactly one — 1,489 of them, read per deposit rather than classified by script.

Canonical authority

Canonical is datasets/venues/records/*.json for the venues and datasets/venues/issues/*.json for the issues, joined to data/registry.json for membership. This page is a projection: it reads counts from the registry rather than from the venue records, because the records carry a deposits.assigned field that has not been repopulated since the 2026-08-15 assignment and would report 0 or 1.

The journals

Each is led by duoviri — two editors-in-chief holding the office jointly, each a check on the other. Journals are printed by Pergamon Press; books by New Human Press. The division is by form, not by register.

Issues

An issue is an ordered selection with an argument — something neither the registry nor the venue record can hold, since they carry membership but not sequence, and nowhere to say why a piece sits where it sits. Two are published; five are proposed and await their introductions. Full cards, with a section and an editor's note per item, are in datasets/venues.

The journals dataset — assignments, crosswalks and derived figures. Venue records and issue cards — the canonical store. The heteronyms — who holds which seat. The presses — the two imprints and the Pocket Humans shelf.
