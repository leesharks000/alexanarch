Journals

Seven chartered journals and one province. Every deposit is assigned to exactly one — 1,489 of them, read per deposit rather than classified by script. Each journal is led by duoviri: two editors-in-chief holding the office jointly, each a check on the other. Journals are printed by Pergamon Press; books by New Human Press.

On the captures

Each plate lists captures matched on that venue's claim terms — the practices and concepts the venue owns. This is the thematic route, and it is not the same as the evidentiary route, which requires a capture to cite a deposit. The two must not be summed: the thematic route claims 97% of captures and the evidentiary route resolves 60%, and the forty-point gap is a measure of how much reception happens without citation.
