The entry, short

OECD AI Incidents and Hazards Monitor is an automated monitor of AI harm events in the news, run by OECD.AI (MMRS record), "Automated media discourse monitor of AI incidents and hazards (Beta)" (its page): since November 2024 language models classify its events and write each record (methodology page).

🗂️ What it is

  • Maker: "initiated and is being developed by the OECD.AI expert group on AI incidents", McGovern Foundation support; formalised January 2023 (OECD); operator OECD.AI (MMRS record).
  • Names: AIM; previously AI Incidents Monitor (methodology page).
  • Purpose: an evidence base for policy, "in real time" (its pages; OECD).

📖 Its terms (OECD, May 2024)

  • Incident: an AI system's development, use or malfunction that "directly or indirectly leads to" harm to health, infrastructure, rights, or property, communities, environment.
  • Hazard: an event that "could plausibly lead to an AI incident", near misses included.
  • Graded: serious incident, disaster, serious hazard; misinformation, authors' rights under (c); jurisdictions set scope.

⚙️ How it works (methodology page; MMRS record)

  • Intake: Event Registry's AI-tagged news events, reputable outlets, top 50% by Alexa rank.
  • Classifier: GPT-4o mini filters, GPT-4o reclassifies, articles vote; earlier, traditional machine learning.
  • Record: o3-mini writes title, summary, harm, severity, stakeholders, country from top three articles, marked "[AI generated]".
  • Cadence: daily, events one to four days old; o3-mini clusters similar summaries.

🔍 A machine reception of the news

  • Reading: "its summaries are compositions, and the articles are their sources" (MMRS record).
  • Unseen: "A harm no outlet reports has no event to cluster" (MMRS record); "only what journalists are aware of and deemed newsworthy" (OECD, 2026).
  • Disclaimers: not the OECD's view; articles unverified (methodology page).

📊 Counts and themes

  • Listed: about 18,579 (its page; MMRS record); 10, or 30, a day (methodology page).
  • Trend: 92 to 324 a month, 2022–2025, a falling share of AI news (OECD; its page).
  • Themes: fourteen, the trends paper's k-means clusters of the monitor's LLM summaries; none on the methodology page (Capture Registry).

🧭 Place

  • Neighbours: automated counterpart of the editor-resolved AI Incident Database; the AIAAIC (MMRS record).
  • Holding: the Tow Center's AI search citation study, filed as an incident (MMRS record; its page).
  • Next: open submission under the common reporting framework (methodology page; OECD).
  • Archive: "Media monitors track coverage of events" (#637).
  • Overview: inputs and definitions right, "its classifier left out" (Capture Registry).
