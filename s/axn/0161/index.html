It records how the Rosary Embassy received its first external contribution under conditions of incomplete provenance and uncertain jurisdiction. The protocol’s purpose is to avoid two symmetrical failures: rejecting a meaningful external contribution because it does not yet satisfy full archival requirements, or annexing it into the Embassy as though its authority and provenance had already been established.

It then distinguishes the contribution’s value from those overclaims. This is the Airlock operating under opacity: preserve what arrived, name what cannot yet be verified, and prevent recognition from becoming silent annexation.
