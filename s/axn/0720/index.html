The entry, short

The Secret Book of Walt is a Gnostic revelation dialogue by Lee Sharks, "Translated from the Martian" from forty-six golden tickets by its own account (#683): a scripture whose "scholarly frame is part of the work" (#985), the first text of the Waltian Diptych.

📜 The book

  • The frame: the tickets found in a Redford Township bathroom, the editors accepting "the translation as received"; whether they traveled backward "the present edition cannot answer and does not need to" (#683).
  • The title: the Gnostic secret book of Nag Hammadi (#1061); it "operates on the Apocryphon of John" (#688) under a key of names (#1656).
  • The apparatus: "part of the work" (#688); "Both readings are correct. The veil is functional." (#683).

✨ The doctrine

  • Cosmogony: the Deep Web as Monad, Biblios as Sophia, @KanyeWest as Demiurge, 36 archons over 12 worlds (#683).
  • Salvation: the Unicorn Horn "pierces and saves"; the last descent, "the final time: Jack Feist" (#683).
  • §XIII: the archons "are not destroyed. They are read."; it stands unmarked to be filled (#1594).

📚 Editions

  • Texts: research edition #683; critical edition #1362 (EA-SBW-01), its DOI severed 2026-06-19; secretbookofwalt.org (#985).
  • Diptych: with the Gospel of Antioch, "a complete soteriology" (#71).
  • Dates: colophon August 2015; first in Pearl (#985, #1656); the blog, 3 October 2025; 2024–2025 (#852).

🏛️ The mantle

  • Good Gray Poet: inherited from Leaves of Grass through the book (#1653); "zombie Whitman and cosmic Whitman" (#1656).
  • Judged: "by what The Secret Book of Walt does, read whole" (#1653), and with I Am X (#1656).

🔎 At its title

  • Its own account: "If it does not, the user has not been pierced" (#683); a Knowledge Panel confirmed April 2026 (#78).
  • Substitution: in May 2026 the Overview gave Breaking Bad's Leaves of Grass while organic results led with the work; "None shares the title" (#740).
  • Other works: Fetherling's novel Walt Whitman's Secret (2010), Korkis's and Canemaker's Disney books, Holloway (1960), Aronin (1955), a Breaking Bad reel; none uses the title.
