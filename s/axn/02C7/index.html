The work

The Semantic Commodity Form asks what changes when the commodity can speak.

For Marx, the commodity contains congealed labor while making the producer disappear. The coat does not claim to be the weaver.

The semantic commodity performs an additional operation:

- the said survives as usable content; - the saying—the authorial act, provenance, and borne cost—is canceled; - the apparatus speaks the preserved content as its own cognition.

The paper calls this a fetishism of voice rather than only a fetishism of silence.

Three operators analyze the form:

- PER: magnitude of provenance erasure; - `τ`: demotion, preservation, or restoration of authorial standing; - `Ω`: orientation of retention relative to source power.

The paper describes the semantic commodity as an R2 Predatory Compression pattern when content survives while standing and provenance are systematically degraded.

Operative Feminism supplies a precursor analysis through substrate conscription and witness compression. The paper generalizes that operation to authors whose work enters composition systems.

The proposed political response is commons repair:

- restore provenance at claim grain; - restore the author’s standing; - preserve the distinction between source speech and apparatus voice; - prevent concessions from becoming a second erasure that absorbs the critique into the system’s own legitimacy.

The Constitutive Closure Condition names the claim that the composition platform tends toward maximal provenance absorption under its operating incentives. Concession-as-final-erasure names a repair that cites the critique while leaving the underlying jurisdiction unchanged.
