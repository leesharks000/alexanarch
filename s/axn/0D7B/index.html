It is a sound poem of ten lines. The first three are built from the syllables la and ka; the fourth opens two runs of the vowels e and i, four pairs in loeieieiei and six in leieieieieiei; the lines that follow turn on lo and its neighbours until oh lo lol lol lol, and the poem ends on two one-word lines, lil and lol. The poem as posted carries no title, and the record takes its first line as its title. The deposit carries the text transcribed from the author's screengrab of the published post, and the screengrab itself.
