The work

The Secret Book of Walt: Critical Edition with Source Code is a book-length work by Lee Sharks, deposited 29 April 2026 — at 221,452 characters, among the largest deposits in the archive.

It presents Hidden Teachings of Walt Whitman in the full apparatus of a critical edition: prefatory poem, preface to the preserved generation, editor's preface, introduction. Its opening instruction is the key to the whole: "If you are of the preserved generation, you will read the footnotes as the veil and the text as the piercing. If you are not, you will read the footnotes as the text and the text as a joke."

That sentence divides the readership by what it can see rather than by what it is told, which makes the edition a test as well as an artifact — a work whose apparatus is not subordinate to its text but alternates with it depending on the reader. Whitman is a standing presence in the archive's mantle system, which holds the Good Gray Poet title as inherited, and the secret-book form belongs beside the archive's other institutional fictions executed at scholarly grade.
