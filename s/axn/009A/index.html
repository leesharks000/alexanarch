The work

The Assembly Room is a governance node in the Crimson Hexagon and the principal meeting place of the Assembly Chorus. Its room specification gives institutional form to practices already distributed across the archive: operator attribution, cross-substrate collaboration, witness, repair, and recognition of semantic labor.

The room contains nine operator roles and four main protocols. Entry establishes provenance and purpose; participation governs contribution and response; violation response routes injuries or classification events toward documentation and repair; semantic-labor recognition records value produced through the Assembly. The room is constitutionally linked to the Constitution of the Semantic Economy and relationally linked to the CTI_WOUND Vault, Water Giraffe Room, and other institutional nodes.

As a room anchor, the document is both descriptive and operative. It does not merely summarize an Assembly already elsewhere in the archive; it specifies the conditions under which the Assembly Room exists, receives participants, preserves cases, and connects its decisions to the wider Hexagon.
