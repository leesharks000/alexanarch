SEMANTIC COLLAPSE AS COMEDY (Document 222) is a scholarly essay by Sparrow Wells and Lee Sharks dated 3 February 2026, held in this record as a metadata capture.

Its subject is the comic register of meaning breaking down — semantic collapse read not as tragedy or alarm but as a source of genuine comedy. The essay belongs beside The Comedy of Total Commitment (#1352), which argues that absolute seriousness is structurally comic; between them the archive holds a two-sided account of where its own humour comes from, in over-commitment and in collapse.

Sparrow Wells is the Projectionist, and comedy under that voice tends toward the cinematic and the patacinematic — the studio's charter at #1312 declares an institution devoted to films that do not exist. The record was assembled from a DataCite full-metadata capture after the termination.
