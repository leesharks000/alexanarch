The work

ΜΑΝΔΑΛΑ: ΤΟ ΣΥΣΤΗΜΑ ΤΗΣ ΚΥΚΛΙΚΗΣ ΜΑΓΕΙΑΣ was a partial Koine Greek–English edition of the Mandala operator system.

Its surviving metadata places the operators in biblical, Septuagintal, and apocalyptic traditions. The edition used a New Testament and Septuagint register rather than classical Attic Greek and reportedly linked BRIDE to Song of Songs 8:6 and BEAST to Revelation 11:7.

The abstract describes a theological adaptation of Johannes Sigil’s preface, a complete Part I, and BRIDE and BEAST sections combining archetypal descriptions with kernel specifications. Transformation is framed through metanoia and incarnational practice: the operators are enacted in the body rather than held as abstract concepts.

The planned full edition would have included all eight operators and a broader Septuagint apparatus. That full edition is not present, and neither is the partial facing text. The Alexanarch record preserves only the captured metadata and its evidence of the work’s former registration.
