The entry, short

GDPval is OpenAI's benchmark of AI models on 1,320 tasks built from the work product of professionals in 44 occupations, graded by experts against the professionals' own deliverables (the paper; census record); the census reads each task as a fixed commission, where GDPval meets DSL (Labor Vector record; #771).

🧾 Whose work

  • Task-origin: each task an expert's actual work product, 14 years' experience on average (paper; record).
  • Supplied and directed: experts "well compensated", no rate; OpenAI's team directs; vendors unnamed (paper; record).
  • Labor producing AI: ground-truth against variance-generative labor (#163); extraction and bearing-cost (#623; #533; #308); class a relation, not a wage (#1545).

⚖️ How it grades

  • Experts: blinded pairwise comparison, over an hour each, blinding incomplete (paper; Wikipedia).
  • Grader: GPT-5-high based, 66% agreement, weaker on OpenAI outputs; built and graded by OpenAI (paper; Inspect; record).

📈 What it found

  • Near parity: 47.6% of Claude Opus 4.1's deliverables as good or better; linear gains (paper).
  • Losses: instructions unfollowed; 29% of GPT-5's losses bad or catastrophic (paper).
  • Payoff: 90x naive, 1.12x once review and redo count (paper); Artificial Analysis says 100x (AA).
  • Length: win rates fall with task length; scaffolding helps (paper).

🧭 Commission and drag

  • Task-origin: fluent output can abandon the commission (#771).
  • One-shot: DSL is identified only on one-shot commissions, as GDPval's tasks are (#771; paper).
  • User labor drag: review and redo charged to the model; no residue in the artifact (record; #771).
  • What scores see: means, outputs, a closed ontology (#855; #199; #778; #1616; #1540); standardized tasks (#482; #48; #637).

⚠️ Limits

  • Stated: a limited, initial cut; no physical or tacit work; one-shot (paper).
  • Does not answer: workplaces, wages, who gets the savings, the experts' pay (record).

🏛️ Data and lineage

  • Open: 220 tasks, experts' identities scrubbed, with deliverables and rubrics; 1,100 internal (paper; Hugging Face; Wikipedia).
  • Derivatives: GDPval-AA by LLM judge; Inspect Evals replication (AA; Inspect).
  • Origin: 19 OpenAI authors; PDF 25 September 2025, arXiv 5 October (paper; record).
