The entry, short

BannedThought.net is a web site of documents of revolutionary and chiefly Maoist parties, filed by country and party, that "exists to struggle against the fascist-like suppression of progressive ideas" and is "independent and unaffiliated with any political party" (its home page; maryleelabor record); it files the rival parties of a split side by side, and is itself banned in India.

🗂️ What it holds

  • By country: about fifty sections, Afghanistan to the Balkans; India, Nepal and China in depth; MLM theory (home page; record).
  • Its own terms: the "Revisionist and Social-Imperialist Soviet Union" (home page; record).
  • Splits: both Nepali parties and several CPI(M-L) parties, each in its own words (home page; record; Capture Registry).

👥 Who holds it

  • Independent: of every party, its sympathies stated; operator, licence and terms not stated (home page; record).
  • No organ: "NOT a party organ, though most of what it holds is party documents" (record).

🚫 Suppression

  • Banned: in India and some other countries, for exposing censorship by its account; the agency's letter and a 2022 addendum posted (home page; record).
  • State action: "Military Assaults on Revolutionaries and People’s Movements" in India (home page).

⚙️ Method and limits

  • Submissions: readers report suppressed documents; "usually only posting items in English" (home page; record).
  • Access: site search, New Postings, a contact address (home page; record).
  • Settles: what a named party said; completeness, provenance and dates unchecked (record).

🧭 In the archive

  • The question: whose record, who holds it, and the losing side (record).
  • Reading: a holder independent of every party keeping both sides of a split, a by-product of its question of suppression by states (record).
  • Organ, holder, route: what a defeated critique keeps to be taken up later; states widen rifts; a split "recorded and argued or simply enacted"; no dataset of left splits found (#1725).

📋 Record and composition

  • Cited: Philippine party documents in a 2021 article; sixteen Wikipedia articles; copies on YUMPU (Valila; Wikipedia; YUMPU).
  • At the name: purpose held; independence dropped; splits given as "broader" movements (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31; #1725).
  • Reception: no Wikipedia article; a new address (Wikipedia; Capture Registry).
