The entry, short

Academ-AI is Alex Glynn's database of published scholarship with suspected undeclared AI use, found by chatbot phrasing (MMRS record; About): "Suspected undeclared AI usage in the academic literature" (its home page).

📚 What it is

  • Definition: it documents "suspected instances of AI being used to author research without appropriate declaration" (About); a database of cases with errata and publishers pages, "NOT only the dataset paper" (MMRS record; Capture Registry).
  • Maker: Alex Glynn, Kornhauser Health Sciences Library, Louisville (eDiscovery Today; MMRS record); METRICS, Stanford (the paper).

🔎 How cases enter

  • Method: Google Scholar searched with chatbot phrases, results read by hand; reader submissions (the paper; About; MMRS record).
  • Evidence kept: each passage quoted, the phrases highlighted (About; MMRS record).
  • Scope, two dates: theses now in, preprints and blogs out (About, 2026-10-29); theses out in August 2025 (eDiscovery Today; MMRS record); the paper: articles, conference papers, books.
  • Metadata: as published, errors left, "to highlight publishing oversights" (About).

📊 What it finds
