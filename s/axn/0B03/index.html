The entry, short

Turkopticon is a worker-run organizing and advocacy project for data workers, begun in 2008 by Lilly Irani and M. Six Silberman as a review tool on which Amazon Mechanical Turk workers rate the requesters they work for (its FAQ; CHI 2013; Labor Vector record); after Amazon announced MTurk's closure for 30 September 2026, it wrote "we are still here" (Turkopticon on LinkedIn).

🛠️ The review tool

  • Four ratings: communicativity, pay (now pay/hr), fairness, promptness, from a workers' survey; "tactical quantification" (CHI 2013; FAQ; record).
  • Requesters: "not employers in the legal sense" yet in power through pay; the founders call them employers (FAQ; CHI 2013; record).
  • Accountability: no deletion; moderator flags; addresses obfuscated (FAQ; CHI 2013).
  • Stated limits: a compromise that "sustains and legitimizes AMT"; academic subsidies (CHI 2013).

👥 The organization

  • Worker-led: since 2019; a committee of Turk workers with Tech Workers Coalition volunteers (blog; FAQ; record).
  • Not Amazon's: yet a working relationship: reinstatements, weekly reports, forums (FAQ; blog; record).
  • All data workers: AI training, annotation, transcription, moderation; the mass rejection campaign to continue (blog; LinkedIn; Reddit; record).
  • After MTurk: "isn’t tied to one platform" (LinkedIn).

🧭 Its class, in the archive

  • Semantic labor: moderators' and annotators' work, invisible, uncompensated, its context stripped in training data (#18; #256; #478; #533; #534; #1070).
  • Ground-truth labor: ghost work, "paid badly" (#163, hypothesis); stops at the worker, not the provenance of meaning (#144).
  • A relation: an annotator "can occupy" a proletarian relation; not a demographic (#1545).
  • Organizing: data-worker organising set apart (#1439); "do not organize on platforms that can shut you down" (#499); summarizers soften it (#49, hypothesis).

🔒 Its data

  • Not research: "not implicit consent to being a research subject" (FAQ; record).
  • Access: a public API, rate-limited; AGPL code (FAQ; record).

📋 Record and composition

  • What it settles: ratings of named requesters, not representative pay (record).
  • At the name: held; its stance on its data and the present work left out; requesters made employers (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
  • Reception: no Wikipedia article; no card cites the record (Wikipedia; Capture Registry).
