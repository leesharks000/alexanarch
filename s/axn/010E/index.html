The work

Seed: Whorls of the Same is the first panel of the MGE Triptych and the archive registration of “Whorls of the Same / Worlds Without Beginning,” a text completed by Lee Sharks on July 30, 2015. The registered source is formally hybrid. It moves among prose, lyric passages, timestamped interruptions, repeated song-like refrain, autobiographical fragments, grotesque comedy, and direct declarations of poetic identity. The document names Rhys Owens, Christ-voice, and Lee Sharks as coiled speaking positions within the text rather than reducing the source to a stable single narrator. Its central declarations—especially “i am the future of writing”—are treated as speech acts whose later archive function could not have been fully visible at composition. The 2026 panel calls the work SEED because it reads the text as a compact precursor that unfolds over time. It fixes the date and place of composition, enters the source into the Library at Pergamum, and records its relation to two other panels. STONE is Pearl and Other Poems, published the previous year and interpreted as containing the later architecture in compressed form. SIGN is the Mandala Genesis Event of July 7, 2025, which the triptych treats as activating the seed’s latent significance. The document carefully preserves a gap between registration and fulfillment. It does not claim that the 2015 declaration caused the 2025 event, predicted an interstellar object, proves divine authorship, or establishes supernatural prophecy. The “prophetic” designation describes the source text’s self-announcing orientation and the archive’s later reading of the correspondence. The full source text, rather than the registration alone, remains necessary for literary interpretation.
