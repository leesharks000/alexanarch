The work

Operative Semiotics is a research program developed by Lee Sharks within the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

It studies signs, documents, metadata, and symbolic structures as interventions in material, institutional, computational, and retrieval systems. Its areas include document action, provenance, compression economics, machine-mediated entity formation, and institutional feedback.

The executive summary lists associated protocols and metrics and distinguishes the program from Charls Pearson’s Theory of Operational Semiotics.
