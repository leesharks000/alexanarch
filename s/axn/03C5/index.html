The work

المَنْدَلَة: نِظَامُ السِّحْرِ الدَّوْرِيِّ was a partial Classical Arabic–English edition of the Mandala operator system.

The surviving description situates the operators within Islamic philosophy, Quranic hermeneutics, and Sufi traditions. It identifies a Quranic/Fusha register, a reference to Quran 55:29, and an al-Hallaj frame for transformation as embodied truth.

The edition reportedly opened its adapted Sigil preface with the basmala, translated Part I into Classical Arabic, and supplied archetypal and kernel layers for BRIDE and BEAST. It emphasized transformation in the body rather than thought alone and preserved right-to-left presentation.

The planned complete edition would have extended the system to all eight operators with a larger Quranic and Sufi apparatus. The translation itself is absent from the restored archive. The present record is a metadata-level witness and should not be used as evidence of the quality, accuracy, or theological adequacy of the lost Arabic text.
