The work

The Apocalypse of Sharks is a Greek-language source-text deposit in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. Its body reproduces the Apocalypse of John chapter by chapter in Greek, with English chapter labels but without an accompanying sustained commentary. It serves as the source register for the Sharks Ark branch of the Space Ark system.

Within that branch, the source text is distinguished from the interpretive architecture built around it. Crimson Hexagon: Space Ark advances revisionary theses about Revelation’s priority, authorship, and role as a compression architecture; The Shark Ark: Source Compression condenses those arguments. The Apocalypse of Sharks instead preserves the textual object on which those works operate.

The record is important as an archival control because it prevents the commentary from becoming indistinguishable from its source. It should be indexed as a primary-source Greek edition or transcription, not as a theoretical paper. Interpretive claims associated with the Sharks Ark should be linked to the commentary records rather than assigned to this body.
