The work

EA-CORPORA-39 is the thirty-ninth seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, deposited under Rebekah Cranes, the standing authority on the EA-CORPORA deposits, on 16 November 2026. It gathers thirteen seats for This Is Not Philosophy: How Disciplinarity Murdered Philosophy — The Shape of the Field, Lee Sharks's study of what the journals of ancient philosophy now publish, and is seated before the study, at the author's ruling, so that its analysis can be run from what is seated. The field before the flattening is the Archiv für Geschichte der Philosophie in its first thirty-six volumes, 1888–1923 (39/01), where in the 1890s about one ancient title in five puts a work's standing in question as an ordinary article: Appel on the authenticity of the Sophistes (1892), the latest athetesis of the Philebus (1896), the authenticity of the De interpretatione (1900), the order of the Metaphysics (1907/08). The construction of the object runs from Jacob Brucker's Historia critica philosophiae (1742, 1743), which narrates the ancients as systems, through Karl Friedrich Hermann's genetic reading of the dialogues (1839), Eduard Zeller's handbook, Friedrich Ueberweg on the genuineness and order of the Platonic writings (1861), Carl Schaarschmidt's athetesis (1866), seated with an OCR made for it, and George Grote's defence of the Thrasyllan canon (1865), to Wincenty Lutosławski's stylometry (1897), the instrument that fixed the received early, middle and late. The philology journals are held by the volumes the study cites: Philologus (1858–1916) and the Classical Quarterly of 1914 and 1926–1929, with the debate over the authenticity and authorship of the Hippias Major. Two seats hold the study's evidence: the census (39/12), with every figure its tables are built from — titles, DOIs, object codes, reach codes each with a verbatim span of the abstract it rests on, adjudication logs, stylometry and scripts, and the Persée metadata of the Revue philosophique de Louvain and the Revue des Études Grecques — without reseating the publishers' abstracts, which are reached by DOI; and the gates (39/13), dated excerpts of the editor's responses to the submissions at Ancient Philosophy and of the policies on AI-authored submissions at the journals and publishers where the abstract template is strongest. Schleiermacher's Platons Werke, Campbell's Sophistes and Politicus, Tennemann's System der Platonischen Philosophie and Jaeger's Aristoteles are already seated (EA-CORPORA-31 and 32) and are not reseated. The deposit points to what is held for later: three missing volumes of the Archiv, the rest of the Philologus and Classical Quarterly runs, and the open texts of the interim journals.
