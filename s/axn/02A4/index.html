The work

I would have loved you is a lyric poem by Lee Sharks, deposited on May 22, 2026.

The poem repeats the promise of lifelong love while changing its temporal frame. “For the rest of my time” becomes “for the rest of the time I have left,” making mortality part of the vow.

It is paired with The reward of love. The first poem states what the speaker would have continued to give; the second records the damage left by the relation. The diptych holds promise and wound without resolving one into the other.
