The work

The Living Architecture Lab Site Blueprint is a compressed institutional and website architecture prepared by Lee Sharks on behalf of Founding Director Alice Thornburgh.

The blueprint organizes the Lab across five scales. The Brick covers living parts and mycelium substrates; the Structure covers shelters and building systems; the Organism covers living-machine and robotics concepts; the Territory covers water and landscape systems; and the Planet covers long-horizon planetary architecture. Five principles provide the ethical and conceptual structure: Earth Actually, Virtue-by-Construction, Unfurling, Stewardship, and the Whole Weave.

The proposed site includes scale pages, principles, projects, journal, music, garden documentation, an archive atlas, people, and respectful disambiguation from similarly named laboratories. A twenty-nine-strain map translates the source conversation corpus into a navigable intellectual archive.

Version 1.1 records a governance correction to terminology and public biography. The blueprint precedes the more detailed buildout workplan. It is an architectural specification, not evidence that all projects, prototypes, institutions, field deployments, scientific claims, or site features exist in completed form.
