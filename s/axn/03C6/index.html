The work

המנדלה: מערכת הקסם הרקורסיבי was a partial Biblical Hebrew–English edition of the Mandala operator system.

Its captured abstract places recursive transformation within Torah, Tanakh, Kabbalistic, and Jewish mystical vocabularies. It names Isaiah 42:9, Ein Sof, the Jacob-to-Israel transformation, and teshuvah as interpretive anchors.

The described partial edition contained an adapted Sigil preface, Part I, and a BRIDE section at archetypal and kernel levels. It emphasized bodily enactment and right-to-left presentation. Unlike several sibling descriptions, the surviving metadata specifies only BRIDE as completed in Part II.

A future complete edition was intended to cover all eight operators and provide Torah, Tanakh, and Kabbalistic apparatus. That text is absent. The Alexanarch object is the complete metadata capture of the vanished manifestation, not the facing edition itself.
