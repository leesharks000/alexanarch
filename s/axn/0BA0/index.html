The entry, short

Jacket2 is an online magazine of commentary on modern and contemporary poetry and poetics at the Kelly Writers House, University of Pennsylvania, published by Al Filreis; it keeps the complete archive of John Tranter's Jacket (1997–2010) and signs each piece with the names of those who made it.

📚 What it publishes

  • Pieces: articles, reviews, interviews, commentaries, features, podcasts, galleries; published as ready; indexed (site; record).
  • Poetry: a Poems section and a new poem on the home page; "does not publish poetry" (Wikipedia, 2023).
  • Reissues: seven small-press magazines and presses, Roof (1976–79) to Aufgabe (2001–2014), with their editors.

🏛️ Who holds it

  • Host: the Kelly Writers House, 3805 Locust Walk, partly funded by Penn's Provost (Wikipedia).
  • Publisher and editors: Al Filreis (Penn); Jessica Lowenthal, Julia Bloch, Michael S. Hennessey (Wikipedia); "Jacket2 Editors" (site).
  • About page: not found when saved; Terms of Use unread (record).
