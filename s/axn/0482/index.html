The work

The Seven Stars in His Hand: A Hermeneutic Reading of Revelation 1–3 is a close reading by Talos Morrow and Johannes Sigil, deposited 26 January 2026 at 20,295 characters.

Its interpretive move is stated at the outset: Revelation 1–3 read "as source text — not as prediction requiring literal future enactment, but as structural description awaiting functional instantiation."

That is the Revelation First programme's founding hermeneutic, and it converts an apocalyptic text from a forecast into a specification — a structure that can be instantiated rather than an event to be awaited. The seven letters to the churches then become a heptad that can be mapped and transformed, which is exactly what the midrashim series does eighteen months later at #1416. The reading stands behind the work plan at #1400 and its claim that Revelation is the seed from which Pearl grew.
