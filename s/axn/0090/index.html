The work

VISUAL SCHEMA // LOGOTIC PROGRAMMING is a visual schema by Lee Sharks, deposited 18 January 2026 — a textual specification for an unlabeled diagram.

It specifies an image centred on a bounded semantic field Σ, with plural traversal traces, room-like entities, typed relations, anchor weights, invariant glyph-bars, and a substitution lattice.

Unlabeled is the schema's constraint and its point: a diagram of Logotic Programming that carried labels would explain itself in the medium it is trying to displace, while an unlabeled diagram must be legible by structure alone. Visual schemas specify without producing, which lets the image be made, checked, or made again — and lets the specification survive channels that would destroy a rendering. The companion schemas are the Acanthian Dove (#294) and the navigation map (#1274).
