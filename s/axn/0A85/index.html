The dataset

The blog index is the complete harvest of Mind Control Poems (mindcontrolpoems.blogspot.com), Lee Sharks's blog since 1 December 2014: every post with its date, title and address, harvested through the Blogger feed and verified complete against the feed's declared total (3,093 posts through 18 October 2026), with the posts the archive's AXN index covers marked. The blog is a record surface of the archive in its own right.
