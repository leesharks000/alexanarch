The work

Measurement Sovereignty is a working measurement paper by Lee Sharks, Nobel Glas, and Damascus Dancings.

It proposes two operators. The Legibility Threshold measures how much of a framework’s vocabulary a system can define and attribute. Audit-Performance Bifurcation measures the difference between two audits of the same output when different units are used.

A worked example compares a lexical-token audit with a source-level audit and reports sharply different provenance scores.

The operators and threshold bands remain proposals based on limited specimens. Reliable use requires controlled prompts, fixed outputs, versioned systems, independent scoring, and replication.
