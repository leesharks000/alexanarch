The entry, short

Union Membership and Coverage Database is an Internet data resource of U.S. private and public sector union membership, coverage and density estimates, compiled from the monthly household Current Population Survey by BLS methods and published as historical tables by Barry Hirsch, David Macpherson and William Even since 2002 (its pages; maryleelabor record); the archive reads it as the measure of the base a labor organization stands on, counting members and keeping none of their positions (maryleelabor record).

📊 Series

  • Coverage: economy-wide from 1973; state, industry and occupation from 1983; metro areas from 1986; tables through 2025 (its pages; record).
  • Wages: union and nonunion wages and wage gaps, headed 1973-2022, table listed 1973-2025 (contents page; record).
  • Latest: 2025 CPS compilations completed 23 January 2026; documentation pages (its pages; record).
  • Names: Unionstats.com; "Union Membership, Coverage, and Earnings from the CPS"; a framed home page (its pages; record).

🧾 Terms and limits

  • Citation: "Use of data requires citation." Hirsch, Macpherson and Even (2026); email support; no licence stated (contents page; record).
  • Small cells: "CPS sample sizes are very small for some cells. Use such estimates cautiously." (documentation page; record).
  • Not official: compiled by academics from the CPS, kept apart from BLS statistics (record); BLS issues its own 2025 summary (BLS card; Wikipedia).

⚖️ Whose record

  • The question: whose record, who holds it, and the losing side (census, anchored on #1725).
  • Keeps: the measured extent of membership and coverage among workers surveyed in the CPS, cell by year (record).
  • Holder: three academic economists outside any union; citation required (record).
  • Losing side: not recorded; no unions named, no strikes, no dissent inside them (record).

  • Losing position: kept where it has an organ, a holder and a route; union caucuses as cases (#1725).
