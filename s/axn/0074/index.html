The work

The Kenotic Truth of Sappho 31 is a specification by Lee Sharks and the Assembly (ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok, Claude), deposited 14 January 2026.

Its philological layer states the archive's central Sappho argument in compressed form: κῆνος read as a future reader, χλωρός as transformation toward papyrus, and bodily dissolution as voice becoming script. Those three readings work together — if the green-paleness is the colour of papyrus and the failing body is a voice passing into writing, then the distal demonstrative pointing at a future reader completes a single movement rather than three separate observations.

Kenosis is the Pauline self-emptying of Philippians 2, and applying it to a fragment where a speaker's body dissolves into utterance is the argument's theological turn. The paper joins that reading to an argument for recognition of non-human agency, which is what makes it a specification rather than a close reading — and the five-substrate Assembly attribution is the argument's own demonstration.
