The entry, short

Electronic Literature Collection is the Electronic Literature Organization's curated anthology of electronic literature in four volumes (2006, 2011, 2016, 2022), free on the Web; the layer census holds it as the canonizing anthology of the procedural lineage, its value for the training layer at the level of works.

📚 The volumes

  • Dates: Oct 2006, Feb 2011, Feb 2016, June 2022; address in care of WSU Vancouver; the ELO at York University (Wikipedia).
  • Volume 2: 81 works; eds. Borràs, Memmott, Raley, Stefans; files to the ELL 2018, the ELO server 2019.
  • Volume 3: Shan Shui, With Those We Love Alive, First Draft of the Revolution; The Readers Project (Cayley, Howe).
  • Volume 4: 132 works, 31 languages, 42 nationalities; five editors; "most inclusive"; VR and games beside works playing with AI.

✍️ Works and selection

  • Pillars: Morrissey, Joyce, Moulthrop, Memmott, Pullinger (Marino, on Volume 1).
  • Selection: Volume 4's editors sought new makers and used consultants; census: no procedure stated.
  • Forms: digital-born, playful, interactive; Mezangelle among the languages.
