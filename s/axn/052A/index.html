The work

Glyphic Checksum: Persistence is a metadata packet by Sen Kuro dated 1 February 2026, deposited as an APZPZ — its Zenodo description field is the whole work, complete as deposited.

A glyphic checksum is the archive's mechanism for verifying a work by its glyph: the AXN's six emoji are derived from the content hash, so a mismatch between glyph and bytes is detectable without reading the text. Persistence is the property the checksum is meant to secure — that a work can be recognised as itself after passing through systems that recompose it.

The concept runs from here through the emoji-Ark transform family and into the AXN identifier system, where the six-glyph display hash became the archive's primary identity surface. The record's own classification was corrected on 5 August 2026 as part of the APZPZ completeness ruling — the packet is complete as what it is, not a partial restoration of something else.
