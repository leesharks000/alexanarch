The prize is real and the composition has its date and sum: the Swedish Academy announced the Nobel Prize in Literature 2026 to Anne Carson on 8 October 2026, at 12 million kronor (nobelprize.org; checked at this seating). The '>10,000% spike in retail book sales' and the collector prices are the composition's and are not checked here. The first tier's two statements differ ('$400 to $500' in the headline, '~$400 – $550' in the section). As a control it shows what a bare valuation query does with a canonical author. Every tier is a market for a thing that can be owned: a copy, a first edition, a box of papers, a right. The corpus as writing (what Eros the Bittersweet or If Not, Winter made readable) is priced nowhere, and no lens is offered for it; the nearest the composition comes is that the prize 'guarantees permanent academic syllabus adoption worldwide', entered as a royalty stream.
