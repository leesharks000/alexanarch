The work

EA-CORPORA-32 is the thirty-second seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, deposited by Lee Sharks on 2 November 2026. Its eleven seats hold twelve works and some 331,000 lines of text layer: the remaining public-domain debts of the study Legitimately Many, seated in their original languages, each seat naming the research it was made for. The pseudo-Archytan writings, with the book on the ten categories that Simplicius reads as Aristotle's source, are seated in Mullach's Fragmenta philosophorum Graecorum (32/01). The Aldine Aristotle's fourth volume of 1497 (32/02) prints Aristotle's and Theophrastus's Metaphysics consecutively, files both under one heading in its register, and prints after Theophrastus's the scholion recording that Andronicus and Hermippus did not know the book and that Nicolaus assigned it to Theophrastus; its leaves are held as images with the passages transcribed. Wimmer's Didot Theophrastus (32/03) names Brandis's edition of the two Metaphysics. Philodemus's Index Academicorum in Mekler's edition (32/04), OCR'd in session, shows at its list of Plato's pupils that the names of Speusippus and Xenocrates are supplied from Diogenes. Dionysius's First Letter to Ammaeus (32/05) dates a corpus by declared evidence, and Cicero's letter to Atticus IV.16 (32/06) takes the exoteric works as Aristotle's form. Schleiermacher's Einleitung of 1804 (32/07), Campbell's Sophistes and Politicus of 1867 (32/08) and Tennemann (32/09) carry the modern ordering of Plato's dialogues; Hegel's Differenzschrift and his lectures on Plato, which fault Tennemann's extraction and hold the esoteric to be the speculative that is written and printed, are OCR'd from the Fraktur (32/10). Sixteen modern editions and unfound witnesses are declared at 32/11.
