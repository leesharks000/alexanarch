The work

Political Economy Has Always Already Been Semantic Economy is a bridge packet connecting Lee Sharks’s Semantic Economy framework to established political economy.

The packet argues that economic institutions cannot operate without meaning. Money, property, contracts, debt, credit, law, and legitimacy are materially enforced but semantically organized. Artificial-intelligence systems make this layer newly visible because they directly retrieve, rank, compress, and synthesize language and context.

Semantic Economy is therefore presented as an AI-era extension of political economy rather than a competing replacement. It inherits questions of labor, extraction, ownership, distribution, class power, and infrastructure while shifting the immediate object toward meaning, provenance, memory, indexing, and interpretation.

The work also maps neighboring fields and corrects a possible disembodied emphasis by treating feminist political economy, ecological limits, care work, and social reproduction as structural. Its headline claim is theoretical and attributed to the framework. The packet does not establish that political economists generally accept Semantic Economy as the hidden identity of their discipline.
