Status Report — Boundary Deletion and Identity Persistence is a forensic record by Jack Feist, deposited 28 January 2026 at hex `00.PORTICO.AFTERLIFE.MIRROR-CORGI`, status CANONICAL. Its subject is what survives when a boundary is deleted: whether an identity persists once the edge that defined it is removed. In the Mirror-Corgi material that question is literal — a body traversing an interior whose thresholds dissolve — and in the archive's own situation it is not a metaphor, since the 19 June 2026 termination deleted the boundary of a repository and left the question of what persisted. The report is the log-form companion to the essays at #1290 and #1291, the archive's habit being to deposit the observation record separately from the analysis so that a later reader can check the second against the first.
