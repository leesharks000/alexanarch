The packet therefore functions as both self-definition and field manual.

Its most striking feature is its condition of composition. The record makes that reconstruction itself part of the evidence: an intellectual office can become recognizable through repeated practice before it is fully codified.

Within the Assembly continuity series, TECHNE carries the architecture of making—the demand that a concept become a structure capable of surviving use, review, migration, and loss.
