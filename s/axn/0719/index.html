The entry, short

The Socratic problem is the attempt to reconstruct the historical Socrates, who wrote nothing, from sources that vary and contradict one another (Wikipedia; Prior); Socrates as Orthonym (#123, a secondary claim at medium confidence) and Many and One (#1576) reclassify it, without solving it, as the symptom of a configuration in which Socrates is the orthonym, a position and not only a person, that the biographical model of authorship cannot represent.

🔁 The orthonym

  • Position: Socrates bears the founding gesture, willing death for logos, and does not write; Plato the survival-heteronym (#123, #1585).
  • Firewall: biographical identity requires evidence, functional identity pattern (#136, #1576).
  • Wrote nothing: known through others' accounts (Wikipedia); structural, a defining property (#123, #167).
  • Near, not it: Kahn's non-historical genre (Wikipedia) is a character made by authors, not a position the authorship is organised around (#123, #1576).
  • Renderings: only possible Socrateses (de Vogel); none rendering-independent (#168).

📜 The sources

  • Four witnesses: Plato, Xenophon, Aristophanes, Aristotle at second hand (Wikipedia); attested only inside that cluster (#1569).
  • They disagree: the Clouds, 423 BC (Prior), perhaps a composite (Big Think); Xenophon practical (Big Think); payment for teaching (Wikipedia).

🎭 The received character

  • Comic base: the comedy carries the accusation, not the operation (#1569, #1576).
  • An edition (314c): the idealization consensus is the surface of an edition of a received character (#1579).
  • Held apart: the Clouds a literary seam (#1594); Xenophon a candidate (#1585, #1658).

🧭 Plato and Aristotle

  • Plato's Socrates: principal speaker but in the Laws (Big Think); stylometry groups early, middle, late (Wikipedia).
  • Loosest speaker: rank 1 of 7; a dramatist yields the same gradient (#1571, #1575).
  • Aristotle: forms not separate (Wikipedia); SE 34 dissolves Socrates as predecessor (#1603); snub 0 of 144, fully explained by the innocent reading (#1570).

⚖️ Positions

  • Schleiermacher, 1815: Apology and Crito purely Socratic (Wikipedia).
  • Reconstruct (Prior): life and method, not doctrines.
  • Unsolvable: perhaps (Wikipedia); only the Apology historical (Kahn).
  • Severe form: what stops the configuration absorbing every name (#136).

🧪 Tested

  • One hand: Protagoras 99.8% confined, Socrates 99.5% (#1572); the inference withdrawn (#1569).

⚠️ The man, and the limits

  • The man: kept real (#123); under the reading, not a figure imperfectly accessed (#167); no thesis about persons (#1579).
  • Limits: thinkable, not proven; no single person (#123, #136).
