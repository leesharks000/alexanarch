The entry, short

Marxists Internet Archive is an all-volunteer, non-profit public library of the texts of the tradition, the writings of close to 1000 authors, Marxist or relevant to Marxism, in 83 languages (its introduction; Wikipedia), held under a Charter that keeps it politically independent and does not judge writers; the archive reads it as a holder without a route: what it keeps stays quotable, and what is kept depends on a willing volunteer (ML record; #1725).

🏛️ What it keeps

  • Aim: "any and all writings" Marxist or relevant to Marxism and lawfully publishable (introduction).
  • Writers unjudged: Charter tenet (6); no Marxist/non-Marxist division since 2007; nothing filtered, nothing endorsed (introduction; Wikipedia).
  • Splits: no section named in the introduction; EROL served under its History path (ML record).

⚖️ The holder condition

  • Organ, holder, route: synthesis where the losing position kept all three; "defeat!=erasure of the defeated" (#1725; #1637; census question).
  • Without a route: quotable indefinitely, never a seated minority; the obelus keeps a passage under a signed mark (#1725; ML record).

📜 Origin and governance

  • 1990–1998: Zodiac's transcriptions; his 1998 decision to remove all writers but Marx and Engels; the volunteers' marxists.org (Wikipedia).
  • Committees: a steering committee of all active volunteers; 76 volunteers (2025); a California 501(c)(3) (Wikipedia; introduction).
  • Terms: free; public domain unless noted; Creative Commons; "not necessarily free of copyright" (introduction; Wikipedia).

📚 Scale and text

  • Count: over 400,000 documents, 1.1 TB, April 2026; Wikipedia's 180,000 rests on a 2014 reading; the home page says 720 or 1000 authors.
  • Transcriptions: bounded by willing volunteers and intellectual property; no scholarly edition (ML record); an edited-text hazard (#1531); notes on the 1844 Manuscripts (#843; #1647).
  • Met at its pages: no translator on Capital, a dead MECW mirror (#1668); two Ruge translations (#1725).

🛡️ Press, survival, 2014

  • Publications: since 2008; Erythros distributes nine titles, no approval implied (Wikipedia; Erythros).
  • Survival: mirrors; copies dispersed against a shutdown; the 2007 attacks (Wikipedia).
  • 2014: Lawrence & Wishart's Collected Works removed at their request; a 4,500-name petition (Wikipedia).
  • Reach: eBooks, audio, mirrors; archived by the Library of Congress; Wikipedia tagged (introduction; cards).
