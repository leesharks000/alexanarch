The entry, short

MEGAdigital (Marx-Engels-Gesamtausgabe, online) is the online division of the Marx-Engels-Gesamtausgabe (MEGA), the historical-critical edition of all that Marx and Engels published, drafted, excerpted and exchanged in letters, published by the International Marx-Engels Foundation (IMES) as an academy project of the Berlin-Brandenburg Academy (its pages; Wikipedia; census record).

📚 What it edits

  • Four divisions: works and drafts; Das Kapital and its preliminary studies; letters; excerpts and notebooks (MEGA page; Wikipedia).
  • Online: ten volumes of Kapital texts, letters from 1866, most excerpts; division I and letters to 1865 in print (MEGA page; home page; census record).
  • Method: every writing whole, in its original language, corrections and deletions documented, no interpretation (Wikipedia; census record).
  • Pace: IV/24 online August 2026; 62 of 114 volumes out; decades to go, past Die Zeit's 2025 forecast (MEGA page; Wikipedia; census record).

🏛️ Who holds it

  • Three holders: MEGA¹ under Riazanov in Moscow, ended 1941; MEGA² under party institutes from 1975; refounded 1990 under the IMES 'on a purely scientific and politically independent basis' (Wikipedia; MEGA page; census record).
  • Originals: most at the IISH in Amsterdam since 1938, others at the RGASPI (Wikipedia; home page).
  • Losing side: 'not recorded as such'; Marx's and Engels's record only (census record).

✒️ What custody did

  • Correctives: Kautsky's deletions surfaced only in MEGA²; each edition began partly against earlier custodians (#843).
  • 1932: MEGA¹ I.3 'the calcification event' (#859); the same volume 'supply, marked' against Landshut–Mayer's silent supply (#1422).
  • Findings: Engels's interventions in Kapital II–III, The German Ideology a fragment (Wikipedia); a test scoped (#1594; #1593).
  • Apparatus: 'a genre of confession' (#1422); philology restores 'correction authority to the dead' (#1068).

🔎 In the archive

  • Instrument: collations against MEGA² II/5–II/9, the II/6 apparatus as falsifier (#1631); MEGA philology exposed a false 1851 imprint (#1531).
  • Self-revision: Marx 'treated his own founding texts as revisable' (#1725); the edition read as that record (census record).

⚠️ At the name

  • Substitution: Google's Overview at 'MEGAdigital' composed a Vietnamese ad agency; 'No sentence names Marx, Engels, the MEGA' (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31; #1422).
  • Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 by the home page; unstated in the census (home page; census record).
  • Encyclopedia: no article under the name; the MEGA is a sense of 'Mega' (Wikipedia).
