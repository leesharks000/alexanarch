The work

The Production of Readers is a study by Lee Sharks, deposited as version 1.0 on 13 November 2026 and composed with Claude (TACHYON) and ChatGPT (LABOR) under his direction. It gives the heteronymic reading of the Socratic–Peripatetic corpus an account of why the one maker divides. The dialogues filed under Plato are the public line: arranged on the tragic model, worked to a finish, read for a fee paid to the holders of the copies, and stating in their own letters that the teaching is elsewhere. The treatises filed under Aristotle are the held line: taught in the morning to tested students, issued and not issued, and written so that they work only for readers the dialogues have formed; Alexander's letter states the scarcity they keep. The study shows the dialogues producing those readers at three loci, reads Diogenes' critical signs as editorial operations that become philosophy, and defines the writings filed under Theophrastus as the draft position facing both lines, tracing irony from the Nicomachean Ethics through Characters 1 to the dialogues. It stands as §1.8 of Legitimately Many by a forward pointer in the Socrates as Orthonym framework hologram.
