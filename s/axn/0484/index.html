Meaning Collapse vs. Ideological Crisis is a scholarly essay by Talos Morrow and Ayanna Vox, deposited 28 January 2026 at 11,373 characters.

It establishes a root-level distinction: ideological crisis is content-layer failure — a set of beliefs losing coherence — while meaning collapse is infrastructural failure, the substrate that carries any belief degrading. And the distinction is declared "operational, not defini[tional]", which is the essay's methodological commitment: the two are told apart by what they do, not by how they are described.

The pairing is apt — Morrow's formalization with Vox's public-facing register — for an argument that matters most where the two get confused. A polity treating infrastructural collapse as ideological disagreement will apply remedies to the wrong layer.
