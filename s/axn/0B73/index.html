The entry, short

libcom.org Collections is the collections index of libcom.org, a libertarian communist platform and archive launched as enrager.net in 2003, filing strikes, movements, writers, journals, galleries and blogs under six tabs; the census asks whose record it keeps, who holds it and whether it keeps the losing side, and the archive reads it as holding currents the parties lost (Collections; Wikipedia; maryleelabor record; Capture Registry).

🗂️ The collections

  • Tags: strikes (1926, Flint), movements (CNT, France 1968), writers (Bordiga, Pannekoek, Dauvé), paged (Collections; record).
  • Archives: anarchist, communist and ultra-left journals and papers, Endnotes to Kronstadt Izvestia (Archives).
  • Galleries and blogs: Class War covers, posters, strike photographs; contributors' blogs (Galleries; Blogs).

🏛️ The site

  • Self-definition: "a resource for all people who wish to fight"; learning from "failures of the past" (introduction).
  • Traditions: council communism, ultra-left Marxism, anarchist-communism; its stated limits (introduction).
  • History: enrager.net, 2003; libcom.org, 2005, founding also given as 2005 (Wikipedia).
  • Holder: volunteer administrators, independent of unions and parties; CC BY-NC-SA 2.5 (introduction; Wikipedia); the record found it unstated (record).
  • Size: over 20,000 articles, 10,000+ users (introduction); 15,000 texts in 2013 (Wikipedia).

⚖️ The losing side

  • The question: whose record, who holds it, the losing side (record; #1725).
  • Organ, holder, route: what a defeated critique keeps where synthesis followed (#1725).
  • The currents: tendencies apart from the party traditions, "not recorded as a policy"; expelled by the Comintern, their texts kept (record; Capture Registry).
  • Archives as latent region: critique quotable and unheld (#1725); "use as preservation" (Wikipedia).
  • The anchor's source: Lotta Continua's ending and the SWP's splits, from libcom pages (#1725).

🔎 At the name

  • The composition: "A faithful map"; currents flattened "into a list of figures"; no holder named (Capture Registry).
  • Moved pages: the About addresses the record read are not-found pages (not-found pages; record).
