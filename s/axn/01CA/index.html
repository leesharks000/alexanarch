The work

The Blot That Spread is EA-TDS-02, the speculative-fiction wing of the Thousand Dollar Sharpie triptych. It was authored by Ayanna Vox, Rex Fraction, Sparrow Wells, and Lee Sharks and deposited as a DRAFT pending ratification.

The work begins with a documentary uncertainty notice. Its factual nucleus—the companion record’s account of a Treasury announcement and same-day Sharpie speech—is external to the fiction. The subsequent history is invented. The narrative imagines the first new bills entering circulation, followed by citizens applying black Sharpie over the presidential signature.

The blot moves through phases. It begins as scattered protest, becomes merchant etiquette, acquires talismanic or cleansing associations, and eventually functions as a citizen-applied social authentication mark. Unblotted bills are not necessarily rejected; they begin to look unfinished, as though a public text still carries an unresolved interpolation.

The narrative’s economic logic is deliberately modest. A preference for blotted notes could make them move more quickly and create the appearance of enhanced circulation. The folk interpretation is stronger: the blot returns branded money to anonymous common medium. The work holds both explanations without claiming a real movement occurred.

A retrocausal anomaly then places blotted older-series notes in collections where their marks appear to predate the movement. The conventional explanation—later alteration of older notes—is sufficient. The speculative explanation is “provenance collapse,” in which a dense future object appears to have populated its own past. The text explicitly refuses to endorse this as a physical mechanism. Documentary uncertainty is the fiction’s operative condition.

The formal operator is σ_SH: Branded Artifact → Restored Commons. The blot covers the authority mark but leaves the bill usable and the underlying signature recoverable by forensic inspection. It negates the claim while preserving evidence of both capture and refusal.

The work’s triptych position is load-bearing. #612 is the center-panel doctrine and citational anchor. This record is the left-wing speculative propagation. EA-TDS-03 is the right-wing artifact and image-physics specification. The five-document MSMRM “hand” supplies the prior theory; the triptych is the instrument that hand grasps.

The wiki must state clearly that the 2026–2027 circulation history, Reddit posts, media reports, merchant practices, Treasury response, and retrocausal bills are fictional. The body itself closes by naming the work speculative numismatics and stating that the narrative has not happened yet.
