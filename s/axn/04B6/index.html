The work

The Death Drive Is Not Self-Destruction: A Semiotic Compression is a theoretical paper by Lee Sharks, deposited 8 May 2026 as EA-MPAI-DEATH-DRIVE-01 v1.1 — typed a Bridge Packet, for disciplinary clarification.

A bridge packet is the MPAI variant that connects rather than separates: where a disambiguation packet distinguishes an entity from a namesake, a bridge packet relates a term across disciplines. Here the primary entity is the psychoanalytic Todestrieb and the secondary is the archive's semiotic death drive — and the title states the correction the bridge exists to make, that the death drive is not self-destruction.

Freud's Todestrieb names a drive toward the inorganic and the repetition-compulsion, not suicidality, and the archive's semiotic extension asks what in signification tends toward its own undoing. The paper is the theoretical companion to The Unmade Sign at #1320, written with Orin Trace and Johannes Sigil.
