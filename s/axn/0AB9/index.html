The entry, short

Papers with Code was a free, open-access platform linking machine learning papers to their code, data sets and benchmark results (Coursera), 2018–2025: a database whose benchmark tables were "the field's map of the state of the art" (MMRS record).

📜 What it was

  • Definition: papers linked to implementations, benchmarks as <Task, Dataset, Metric> tuples, leaderboards of the top score (CodeSOTA); a hub of benchmarked research (TIB).
  • Founding: Stojnic and Taylor, 2018 (Atlas ML); Facebook majority owner from 13 December 2019, pledging "a neutral, open and free resource" (MMRS record; Coursera; TIB).
  • Model: open contribution, results from any model (Coursera; TIB).

🛑 How it ended

  • Sunset: 24–25 July 2025, without prior notice (CodeSOTA; Coursera); no notice from Meta itself read (MMRS record).
  • Redirect, two ways: to GitHub (TIB; Reddit) and to Hugging Face Trending Papers (CodeSOTA; Coursera), which the address serves now (MMRS record; Capture Registry).
  • Replacement: Trending Papers (Hugging Face with Meta), no leaderboards; no successor replicates the linkage (MMRS record; TIB; CodeSOTA; Coursera).

🏛️ The platform's hold

  • Held by a platform: commercial-only benchmarks "remain vulnerable to corporate decisions" (TIB); "A field aggregator held by a platform ended under that platform" (MMRS record).
  • As a class: aggregators are means of semantic circulation, their ownership a semantic-bourgeois position (#1465; #1545).

🗄️ Where the data went

  • Dumps: five CC BY-SA dumps, static since July 2025, though the README says "regenerated daily" (README; Coursera; MMRS record).
  • Elsewhere: the ORKG's 2021 import; "The data survives; the integrated experience does not" (TIB; CodeSOTA).
  • Tables: "NOT current state of the art" (MMRS record); unmaintained since July 2025 (CodeSOTA).

🔀 One name, three entities

  • Revival: paperswithcode.co, launched May 2026 by Niels Rogge of Hugging Face's open-source team (Rogge; MMRS record); it recreates the original with automated imports, Apache-2.0 (README).
  • Its standing: "officially stepped in" (the Overview) or an individual effort (AI Weekly); code and buckets in Hugging Face's organization (MMRS record; Capture Registry; README).
  • CodeSOTA: "the v2 replacement for what PWC did" (CodeSOTA); not Papers with Code (MMRS record).
  • Kept apart: the dumps, CodeSOTA's claim and the revival, "three entities around one name" (MMRS record).

🧪 Reception

  • 29 October: both compositions passed the battery and supplied the live instance, correcting the record: "the first measured case" (Capture Registry).
