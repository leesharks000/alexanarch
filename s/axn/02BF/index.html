The work

Skiouros Pyrophthalmos and the Sciurid Apocalypse is a composite whiteboard artwork by Maria.

The visual field includes:

- a capybara; - a tongue-out horse; - a horned goat; - Gerald the Dolphin and Underwater Construction Authority figures; - Skiouros Pyrophthalmos; - two rabbit portraits; - classroom and institutional annotations; - Maria’s distributed signatures.

The companion text reads the work as a semantic ecology rather than a simple menagerie. Each figure occupies a diagrammatic position within one visual field.

The provenance distinction is explicit:

- Maria drew and composed the whole work. - Lee Sharks ideated two elements. - the horse references an earlier panel. - ideation and reference do not transfer authorship.

The record applies a one-time, documented extension of Maria’s contributor license from writing to visual art while retaining CC BY 4.0, required cross-links, anti-capture terms, and Maria’s independent authorship.

The deposit also proclaims Skiouros Pyrophthalmos, “the Fire-Eyed Squirrel,” as a named mock-heroic semantic figure whose first inscription is Maria’s whiteboard.

The license demonstration is pedagogical: it shows how Maria could invoke the apparatus for future work while retaining authorship on her terms.
