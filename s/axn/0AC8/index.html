The entry, short

Problematic Paper Screener is an online platform, run by Guillaume Cabanac with Cyril Labbé and Alexander Magazinov since 27 February 2021, that screens the scientific literature for papers generated or disguised by tools and for references to retracted work, pairing machine detection with human assessment (MMRS record; arXiv:2210.04895).

🔎 What it detects

  • Tortured phrases: paraphrasing-tool output, "Joined Together States" for United States (The Conversation), "counterfeit consciousness" for artificial intelligence (Nature, quoted on its page).
  • Generators: SCIgen and Mathgen papers; ChatGPT fingerprints.
  • Feet of Clay: articles citing retracted, concerning or flagged references.
  • Detectors: ten on its menu (2026-10-28); nine in January 2025.

⚙️ How it works

  • Sources: Crossref with the Retraction Watch Database, Dimensions, PubMed, PubPeer.
  • Scale: 130 million papers a week.
  • Review: machine detection with human assessment; flags reported on PubPeer.

📊 What it has found

  • By January 2025: nearly 19,000 tortured-phrase papers, 280+ SCIgen, 764,000+ citing retracted work, 70+ with ChatGPT fingerprints.
  • Retractions: more than 1,000 (its makers; MMRS record).

⚖️ Limits

  • Iceberg: the fingerprinted papers are "the tip of the tip of the iceberg" (its makers).
  • Left out: "A detector finds what leaves a fingerprint"; clean machine text and real but misstated sources pass beneath it (MMRS record).
  • Proxy regime: paper-mill output gathers proxy signals "indistinguishable from legitimate scholarship" (#741).

🤝 Uptake

  • Signals Graph: over 12,000 unretracted flagged publications (June 2024).
  • Episciences: references checked against its database of over a million (July 2026).
  • Publishers: some in the editorial workflow (its makers).

🧭 Place

  • Record: "the scholarly record's automated immune response", beside Academ-AI (MMRS record).

👁️ On the Overview

  • Recorded: the makers, sources and detectors right; the adoptions new to the record (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
