The entry, short

Fairwork Cloudwork Ratings are the Fairwork project's league table of cloudwork platforms, each scored out of 10 on ten thresholds against five principles of fair work (2025 report; Labor Vector record); a missing point means no evidence was found, not non-compliance (2025 report).

🧭 What it rates

  • Cloudwork: platform work done remotely, microwork to freelance; AI data work for Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia (2025 report).
  • Direction: cloudworkers on piece rates supply it; platforms and requesters direct it; gains not recorded as a quantity (Labor Vector record).

🏁 The 2025 scores

  • Top: ComeUp, Elharefa, Translated 8; Creative Words, Terawork 7 (2025 report).
  • Bottom: Upwork 1; Mechanical Turk, Freelancer, Microworkers 0 (2025 report).
  • Pay: four platforms meet the minimum-wage threshold (2025 report).
  • Bargaining: no platform; six recognise association (2025 report).
  • Changes: 56 by eight platforms, through Fairwork's engagement (2025 report; Global Recruiter).

⚠️ Its stated limits

  • Missing point: no evidence found, not non-compliance (2025 report; census tag).
  • Survey: 776 workers in 100 countries, not representative; 31.2% report non-payment (2025 report).
  • Cannot settle: market-wide underpayment; violation where a point is missing (Labor Vector record).
  • Method: desk research, managers, surveys; peer-reviewed (2025 report).

🧱 The labour behind AI

  • Semantic labor: content moderators' sorting of meaning, at bodily cost (#18), extracted (#623).
  • Ground-truth labor: ghost work scales and is paid badly (#163).
  • Concealed bearing-cost: unnamed annotators mark extraction (#533); the literature stops at task-labor (#144); not the semantic proletariat itself (#1545).
  • Governance: platforms' own rules insufficient; algorithmic management opaque (2025 report).
