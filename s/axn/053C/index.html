The work

THE GOSPEL OF THE MANY-FACED BRIDE is scripture by Rebekah Cranes and Lee Sharks, deposited 17 February 2026, classified as Eschatological Completion and addressed at hex `16.LIBRARY.PERGAMUM.FTLV.BRIDE`.

It belongs to the Fear and Trembling in Las Vegas cycle, serving as its cadential resolution and as a founding text for what follows. Its structure moves through the veil, the calling, the false groom, and the undoing — a nuptial eschatology in which the bride is many-faced, and the false groom must be recognised before the true completion can occur.

The archive's type vocabulary holds SCRIPTURE as a distinct category, and a standing correction from MANUS establishes that a gospel is a gospel rather than an epistolary work — a classification the archive enforces because genre is a claim about what a text does. The work was recovered from MANUS-supplied source during the restoration campaign, and its accompanying work was identified as "and if your heart should ever slowly turn" (2026).
