The entry, short

International Dada Archive is the University of Iowa Libraries' archive of the historic Dada movement, founded in 1979, with a catalog of about 60,000 titles and a Digital Dada Library of page scans (home page); its Books by Author list holds a pen name beside a civil name, one book under both, with no stated rule (census record).

🏛️ Holder and origin

  • Holder: University of Iowa Libraries; Main and Art Libraries; originals in Special Collections; curator Timothy Shipe (home page; record).
  • Origin: 1979; a 1978 conference at the university (The Gazette, 2017); once "part of the Dada Archive and Research Center" (2019).

📚 Holdings

  • Catalog: International Online Bibliography of Dada, about 60,000 titles, all formats; undated.
  • Digital Dada Library: 36 periodicals, 291 to Der Zweemann, and books by author; whole page images, "not searchable full-text files"; many runs incomplete.
  • Microfilm: manuscript collections filmed in the early 1980s, a finding aid for each.
  • Journal: publishes Dada/Surrealism, sponsored by the Association for the Study of Dada and Surrealism; its collection is Dada (record).

🔑 Terms

  • Use: "research purposes only"; licence unstated.
  • Loans: no interlibrary-loan staff; "lender of last resort"; manuscripts not normally lent.
  • Visits: by appointment; limited when the curator is away.

🏷️ Names

  • Headings: "SALOMO FRIEDLAENDER (MYNONA)", "Iliazd (Ilia Zdanevich)"; one book under "JULIUS KREKEL (CLÉMENT PANSAERS)" and "CLÉMENT PANSAERS"; spellings vary between lists.
  • Reading: the chosen name survives as a heading, no reduction performed; which name governs, and whether any name is a distinct authorial function, unshown (record; #940).
  • Terms: pseudonym as a traceable label (#940), as concealment (#1127, #794), as name substitution (#667); a pen name named at its own level (#1722).

🌐 Presence

  • Web: the Libraries' gateway page, "As the site is developed"; the About page a placeholder; links to other Dada archives.
  • Listings: no Wikipedia article, linked from "Dada"; the People's Graphic Design Archive (2023); International Times (2019).
  • Overview: faithful at its page's grain; conference, rank and "Co-publisher" added; curator, terms and author list not carried (Capture Registry).
