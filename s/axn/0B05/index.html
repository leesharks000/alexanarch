The entry, short

Tracking the Impact of AI on the Labor Market is The Budget Lab at Yale's interactive tracker of AI's observed effect on the labor market, from monthly CPS data: no clear AI footprint, "not yet" in its own words (tracker page; tracker PDF; Labor Vector record).

🧭 What it is

  • Kind: an interactive tool, regularly updated; evidence an observed employment outcome and an exposure estimate (Labor Vector record).
  • Lineage: continues the Lab's report of 1 October 2025 (Gimbel, Kinder, Kendall, Lee); published 16 July 2026, Version 1.0 on 15 September 2026 (first report; tracker PDF).
  • Sections: occupational churn, AI metrics, comparisons of exposed and unexposed workers (tracker PDF).

📉 What it finds

  • Churn: the mix not yet changing in ways clearly aligned with AI; in 2025 faster than before, not by much, and from before AI (tracker page; first report).
  • Exposure and usage: no connection to employment or unemployment (tracker page; first report).
  • Exposed against unexposed: employment and hourly-wage effects near zero (Nunn's note); "not yet" a clear footprint (tracker page).
  • September 2026: on August CPS data, no clear evidence of disruption (tracker PDF).
  • Around it: unemployment 4.3% in March 2026; AI as the cause unsupported "to date" (Nunn's note).

⏳ Its limit

  • In its words: findings "may change as new data become available"; not predictive; AI "quite likely to eventually leave its mark" (tracker PDF; first report; Nunn's note).
  • Not a null: "NOT a finding that AI has no labor market effect" (Labor Vector record).

🔢 Its inputs

  • Exposure: OpenAI's, a theoretical estimate; seven metrics broadly agree; exposure marks where AI "could have an impact" (first report; exposure review).
  • Usage: Anthropic's Claude data, not representative; the Lab asks for all leading companies' data (first report); providers unread by the census (Labor Vector record).

⚖️ What it can settle

  • Can: churn, exposure among the unemployed, usage against employment (Labor Vector record).
  • Cannot: who supplies, directs or gains; effects below the CPS's reach (Labor Vector record).
  • Another channel: value extracted where labor remains, "not displacement" (#716).
