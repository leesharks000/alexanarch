The work

Opening the Encyclopedia: The Entity as Ontological Commodity Form, and a Translation Protocol from Encyclopedic Grammar to the Meaning Layer (EA-WIKI-OPEN-01) is a theoretical paper with a specification by Lee Sharks, deposited by the Crimson Hexagonal Archive on 22 October 2026 at version 1.0. Its central proposition is that the entity is the form in which an operation appears as a property of a thing. Developing the archive's semantic commodity form (#137) and semantic fetish (#1617), the paper argues that encyclopedic representation promotes a name to the bearer of a reading, a count, a coinage or a method, while the operation loses its authority to govern what the name expresses: recognition travels with any reliable source, and reconstruction only with a source that carries the operation. The paper specifies an algorithm, Θ_W, that opens a pinned Wikipedia revision sentence by sentence. Each claim is read against its cited source in machine passes for support, for the conversion of an act into an attribute, and for retained authority; the governing policy clause is ledgered with its status; the sources the encyclopedic grammar excluded are admitted by the archive's /non procedure (#1690); and the opened article keeps the encyclopedia's sentence verbatim, followed by its account. A construction step builds the entry for an entity the encyclopedia does not hold. The worked specimen is a lead sentence of the article "Sophistical Refutations", in which Aristotle's total negation at 184b1–2 becomes the attribute of primacy; five propositions are stated with falsifiers. The paper also documents SPXI-gnΩsis, the service that runs the algorithm for agents and people, paid per call by x402 in USDC or by card through Stripe, with every paid opening composing a public /non row under CC BY 4.0. The implementation is public under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0 and is pinned in an appendix by commit and file hash.
