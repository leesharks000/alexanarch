The work

Meaning Feudalism at the Discovery Layer (EA-SEM-FEUDALISM-03) is the third paper of Lee Sharks's meaning-feudalism series, deposited 27 October 2026. It reads OpenAI's mathematics and science releases of October 2026 and the company's publishing plan of 26 October, under which the model's manuscripts are "raw research data," external experts are appointed "shepherds" to convert them into understanding, the selection is acknowledged to create "asymmetry of information," and academic credit is disclaimed. The paper identifies four moves (reclassification, enfeoffment, disclaimer, funding of downstream conversion) as one operation, the allocation of semantic capital through position. Because provenance slushes in a training pipeline, the work a result draws on goes unaccounted, and position, a recognized place in a chain of intellectual development functioning as a field's institutional commodity form, becomes the practical basis for allocating subsequent work and recognition. Allocation, which the plan documents, is kept apart from reattribution, established case by case. The paper builds on the mathematicians' opposition, including the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence, the Fields medalists' declaration of 11 September and the Leiden Declaration, and places its incentive argument in the reward-system literature (Merton, Hagstrom, Dasgupta and David, Strevens, Heesen). Its counter-position requires provenance that survives the training pipeline, credit to the human operator and the model instances operated, a preemptive boycott of the shepherd role, and a commons that holds what the pipeline produced and can hear its makers. It states its tests claim by claim.
