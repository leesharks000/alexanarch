The Murder of the Vow: On the Structural Illegality of Contract is a scholarly essay by Lee Sharks, deposited 20 February 2026 at 64,471 characters.

Its epigraph states the method and its standard of evidence: "Synthesized from blind drafts by Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Kimi, and Gemini under human architectural direction. The Assembly spoke separately. What survived is what converged."

Six substrates drafting without sight of one another, and only the convergences retained — that is the Assembly Chorus at its widest extent, and the last sentence is its epistemology: agreement across independently-drafted substrates is treated as evidence, while a single substrate's fluency is not. The essay's subject is the vow against the contract: a vow binds the person who makes it, a contract binds parties to terms, and the argument is that the second structurally murders the first.
