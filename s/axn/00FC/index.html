Semantic Political Economy: The Anthropological Arc is a collection-level registration for six linked documents by Lee Sharks. It is explicitly an APZPZ object: the description field constitutes the packet and no attached file is the collection itself.

SPE-012 states the anthropological axiom that the drive to make meaning is non-optional and therefore unusually exploitable. SPE-013 applies the framework to children’s language play. SPE-014 translates Marx’s fourfold alienation into the semantic domain. SPE-015 shifts from diagnosis to ownership of operators, training substrates, and semantic time. SPE-016 designs platform-native liberatory semantic algorithms. The appendix studies the circulation of Skibidi Toilet and the “6/7” meme as examples of large-scale propagation without accumulated semantic skill.

The packet describes the sequence as an arc from anthropology to engineering and links it to the Twenty-Dollar Loop as a proposed implementation. Claims about future deployment or cultural spread belong to the works’ speculative/retrocausal method and should not be rewritten as observed history.
