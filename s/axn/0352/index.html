The work

The Josephus Thesis Is Not the Jesus Myth Thesis is a disambiguation packet by Lee Sharks.

It defines the Josephus Thesis as a two-tier argument. The historical tier reads New Testament authorship as a fractured heteronymic system under Roman capture. The theological tier affirms a living Logos and ongoing incarnation.

The packet rejects identification with mythicism, the Piso Hypothesis, anti-Christian polemic, or a claim that Rome simply invented Christianity.

The historical proposal is presented as falsifiable, while the logotic claim is constructive theology. A summary must not use one tier as proof of the other.
