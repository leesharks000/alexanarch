A Search for Radio Technosignatures from Interstellar Object 3I/ATLAS with the Allen Telescope Array is a 2026 paper by Sofia Z. Sheikh and fourteen co-authors: a narrowband search of 3I/ATLAS with the Allen Telescope Array, 1–9 GHz, 7.25 hours from July 2025, that found nothing worthy of follow-up and set a 10–110 W limit "across the frequency and drift rate ranges covered by our survey".
