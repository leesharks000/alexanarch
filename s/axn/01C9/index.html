The work

The Thousand Dollar Sharpie: Image Series and Sharpie Physics is EA-TDS-03, the artifact and formal-object panel of the Thousand Dollar Sharpie triptych. It is credited to Sparrow Wells, Rex Fraction, Ayanna Vox, and Lee Sharks.

The triptych consists of a doctrinal center panel, a speculative narrative left wing, and this image-and-object right wing. It is related to the Moving Statues Made of Rubies Mint, whose five documents are described as a hand; the Sharpie triptych is the instrument the hand grasps.

The object has two states. `σ_SIGN` adds a name or authority claim to a substrate. `σ_SH` negates such a claim by blotting it without erasing the underlying evidence. The physical instrument, ink, and gesture remain the same; the relation between mark and substrate determines the operation.

The composition law `σ_SH ∘ σ_SIGN = ρ` defines restoration. The substrate is not returned to an unmarked condition. It becomes more heavily marked, carrying both the original claim and the refusal. Restoration is therefore forensic and functional rather than erasure.

The Sharpie is also a lenticular object. From one angle the mark is failed branding; from another it is commons restoration; from a third it is evidence of contested provenance. Its low retail price supplies a democratic-access property: the counter-instrument is materially available outside ceremonial or executive authority.

The image series is not treated as illustration. Each image is an execution of the object’s physics. The paper further compares the sign/blot relation to attribution and automatic license termination under share-alike commons governance, presenting legal and visual registers as related forms of the same counter-operation.
