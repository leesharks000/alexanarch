The entry, short

The Oxyrhynchus Papyri is the Egypt Exploration Society's collection of texts on papyrus and parchment from Grenfell and Hunt's excavations at Oxyrhynchus, 1896–1907, housed in Oxford and the largest of its kind (its pages; Wikipedia), the find-site of 52 of 127 registered New Testament papyri (Wikipedia), which the census reads as a principal find-site of early Christian papyri (Revelation First record).

🏛️ The collection

  • Owner and house: the EES; Oxford's Art, Archaeology and Ancient World Library, by appointment; many papyri held elsewhere (its pages; Wikipedia).
  • Scale: thousands of texts, or over 500,000 fragments; 6000 published (site; EES; Wikipedia).
  • Range: second or third century BCE to the seventh century CE, by page; Greek first among a dozen languages (site; EES; Wikipedia).

⛏️ The find

  • Excavation: Grenfell and Hunt at a rubbish dump, 1896–1907; Antinoopolis too (site; EES; Wikipedia).
  • Survival: the tip kept what was thrown away, not what mattered (#1601); compression density, on #567's reading.

📜 What it holds

  • Documents: most; an estimated 10% literary (Wikipedia).
  • Literature: Menander, Ichneutae, Pindar, Sappho, Euclid, Livy (Wikipedia); the Tithonus poem, P.Oxy. 1787, read as reperformed variants (#858); no Oxyrhynchus witness to Sappho 31's fifth stanza (#1052).
  • Christian texts: six witnesses to Revelation, 616 in 𝔓115 (Wikipedia; #1101); Thomas in Greek, three hands, rolls or codices disputed (#1531; #71).

🔎 The record's reading

  • Find-site: of early Christian papyri, to be checked against the collection's own lists (Revelation First record).
  • Inversion: the earliest NT witness Johannine; papyrus dates are ranges and do not date compositions (#202; #832; #636).
  • 𝔓52: placed among these sites' finds (#828); no recorded findspot (Capture Registry).

📚 Publication and access

  • Series: since 1898, 86 volumes or LXXXVIII (Wikipedia); Graeco-Roman Memoirs (EES); the archive's works cite it (#1601; #683).
  • Site: images, publications, location list, exhibition; licence unstated (site; record).
  • Papyrology: reads damaged inscription by authors who cannot be consulted (#1443).

🧭 At the name

  • Overview: held the collection; took range and count from other cards; counted Papyrus 52 among the finds (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31).
