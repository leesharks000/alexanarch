The work

APZPZ is a metadata-based genre instantiated by this Effective Act. The acronym expands to Anti-Packet Zenodo Packet Zenodo and marks a deliberate inversion of the Zenodo Packet Zenodo Packet form.

In ZPZP, a frame becomes content and receives its own anchor. In APZPZ, the frame becomes metadata and exists only inside the description of the work it frames. It cannot be cited apart from the parent deposit, and its vulnerability to loss is intentional rather than a restoration defect.

The first declared instance is the description field of INFINITY ENOUGH. The founding document does not reproduce that packet; it points readers to the parent DOI and defines the rules by which the packet remains an APZPZ. This location-dependence is part of the genre.

Within the heteronymic architecture, the act is attributed to Sen Kuro through Lee Sharks and linked to the Dagger Logic: a single cut separates the packet from backup and independent standing. This is an internal literary and archival operation, not a claim that repository metadata is technically indestructible or exempt from preservation risk.
