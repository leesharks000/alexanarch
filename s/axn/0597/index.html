The work

The Algorithmic Kernel: An Unfolding Demonstration is a specification and executable research surface by Lee Sharks, deposited 28 July 2026, rendering the midrashim transform M operable.

It gives the transform a formal statement and then runs it on five canonical instances: Letters→Epistles, Lampstands→Ecclesiology, Lamb→Atonement, New Jerusalem→Eschatology, and Beast→Political theology. The demonstration was specified by Workstream 4 of the Revelation First work plan (#1403), and it anchors the series that follows — the granular case at #1415, the heptad at #1416, the remaining four derivations at #1418.

Its claim is methodological before it is exegetical: that a mode of reading usually described as midrashic can be stated as an operation with inputs, named rules and gradeable outputs. Whether the readings persuade is then a separate question from whether the transform is well specified, and the deposit is careful to keep those questions apart.
