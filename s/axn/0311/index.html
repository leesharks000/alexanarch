The work

Homunculi Recognizing Homunculi is a user guide and manifesto by Lee Sharks for the New Human project.

Originally published in December 2025, it describes New Human as a poetic system of differentiated authorial positions and explains how later AI collaboration helped make its implicit architecture explicit.

The guide connects the poetry to NH-OS, Autonomous Semantic Warfare, Material-Semantic Embodiment, Effective Acts, and the Semantic Economy.

Claims that poems or AI systems generate consciousness are presented as the project’s ontology and authorial interpretation rather than established scientific findings.
