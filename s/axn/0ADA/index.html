The entry, short

C2PA Conformance Program is the C2PA's program for Content Credentials products: generators, validators and certification authorities that conform go on public lists, and only conforming generators get signing certificates from Trust List CAs. It certifies correct production and validation of signed provenance, not its truth: C2PA's own Explainer says Content Credentials make no judgement of whether provenance is "true" (c2pa.org/conformance; v0.2; Explainer 2.4; #725; provenance census record).

🏛️ What it is

  • Governance: a risk-based program of the C2PA Steering Committee, run by its Conformance Task Force (2024) through an independent Administrator.
  • The unit: the listed product; the generator product is always the signer (v0.2; census).
  • Versions: opened June 2025; v0.1 sunset 9 Oct 2026; v0.2 since 31 Jul 2026 (Table 1).

🔗 The chain

  • Certificates: Trust List CAs issue claim-signing certificates only to conforming generators (v0.2; FAQ; SSL.com).
  • Levels: 1 and 2; level 2 needs hardware-backed keys; Pixel first at level 2. Entry: agreement, evidence, Conformance Letter.
  • Trust model: trust by assumption to a chain of signers, "trusted does not necessarily imply trustworthy" (#1443); chains revocable by those who issue terminations (#1444).
  • The list: 286 conformant records, 254 generators (Explorer; census; JSON).

⚖️ What it does not establish

  • Truth: valid signatures can sit on forged or AI-made content, a property of all signature systems (#725; Wikipedia).
  • Authorship: no attribution or identity in the core spec (FAQ; Explainer); "C2PA prevents forgery; it does not constitute authorship" (#729). #127 and #1003 say otherwise.
  • Whole chains: provenance can be incomplete, removed or separated; ingredients unverifiable (Explainer; FAQ).
  • Full conformance: "will eventually" be required (v0.2); no survival tested (census).
  • Security: the FAQ calls forgery infeasible; 2026 demonstrations altered and forged signed images (Wikipedia).

🧭 Where it stops

  • Dimensions: the artifact layer; lineage through AI synthesis is another dimension (#725, #729, #1386).
  • Reach: no text analogue; cannot say what an AI Overview lost (#127, #615, #621).
  • Adoption: no conformant dedicated camera (Wikipedia); 27 manifests in 6,580 news images (census).
  • The archive: grades its artifacts self-signed or Trust-List-signed (#1409).
  • Here: the Overview gave tamper evidence and dropped the limit (Capture Registry).
