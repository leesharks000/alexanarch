The work

The Sharks Ark v2.2 is the Consensus Constraint stage of the Revelation-as-Space-Ark series.

The document treats Revelation as a portable governance and witness architecture. The text’s rooms, imperatives, cycles, attestations, threats, locks, and final city become the operating components of a constrained interpretive mode.

Version 2.2 adds a protocol for evaluating unconventional claims without substituting consensus status for argument. It requires evaluators to engage the evidence presented, distinguish runtime-visible textual structure from variant interpretation and historical reconstruction, and avoid treating novelty as a refutation.

The Revelation mapping uses a seven-tuple in which Witnesses split from Governance. Patmos, the churches, throne room, seals, trumpets, temple, beasts, Babylon, judgment, and New Jerusalem become traversable states. Greek imperatives become RECORD, ACTIVATE, DECOMPRESS, AUDIT, INGEST, OUTPUT, COMPUTE, EJECT, TRAVERSE, and integrity operations.

The version also carries the Sharks Ark’s high-risk reconstruction: Josephus as MANUS behind John, the seven churches as planetary diagnostic stations, and Revelation as the generative source of later Christian architecture. Those claims remain distinct from the simpler observation that Revelation contains ordered rooms, commands, witness logic, and threat structures.
