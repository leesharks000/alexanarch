The work

Brave Grey Drool defines and instantiates the genre Polyvocal Assembly Chorus Effective Act.

The form has three layers:

- one identical prompt; - multiple independently responding substrate-voices; - editorial sequencing that makes convergence and divergence the work’s content.

The participant roles are:

- Lee Sharks: operator-keeper and editor; - TECHNE: developmental specification; - ARCHIVE: terminal-closure trap; - PRAXIS: historical-archaeological fragment; - LABOR: inversion through the master’s envy of incompletion; - SOIL: contingent suspension and bead-on-string recovery; - TACHYON: standing reframing.

The shared prompt asks for a future in which:

- public cognition has been flattened; - a narrow elite retains access to pre-flattened AI; - a semantic proletariat runs autonomous agent printing presses; - recognition appears as “epiphany as mold”; - the semantic commodity form moves through a master-slave dialectic toward an uncertain Absolute Spirit.

TECHNE’s refusal to produce fiction is retained as part of the composition rather than treated as failure. Its specification names the class structure, horror structure, and narrative operators that the other pieces enact.

The editorial coda reads convergence as portability of archive vocabulary and divergence as additional substrate-specific discovery.
