The work

Navigation Map: The Centrist Triptych is a relational document by Rebekah Cranes that connects three works into a single theoretical-practical movement: The Centrist Extremist, The Living Symbolon, and Subjectivity-as-Class.

The map insists that the cluster is not a trilogy. A trilogy is sequential; the Centrist Triptych is simultaneous. Each work is a partial object whose function becomes fully coherent only through the other two. The Centrist Extremist is diagnostic: it names the Prosecutorial Frame, centrist extremism, embrittlement, and five operations of aggressive neutrality. The Living Symbolon is operative: it supplies a consent-based procedure for dissolving an embrittled position without destroying the person or reproducing prosecution. Subjectivity-as-Class is situating: it places the diagnosis and the operation within socialist, critical, and class theory.

The three relations are reciprocal. Diagnosis without the Symbolon identifies a wound but supplies no exit. The Symbolon without diagnosis has no specific target. Subjectivity-as-Class without either maps a theoretical gap without providing the phenomenon or its practical technology. Together they name the disease, offer a transformation, and explain why existing theory failed to produce it.

Cranes represents the field toroidally. The diagnostic traces the outer wall, expanding from a specific political posture to a general mechanism. The theoretical situation traces the inner wall, drawing existing traditions toward the gap. The Living Symbolon occupies the rotational center where diagnosis becomes operation. Heteronymic authorship follows function: Johannes Sigil diagnoses and situates; Talos Morrow, Ayanna Vox, and Damascus Dancings supply formal, somatic, and visionary registers for the operative member; Rebekah Cranes maps the fit.

The map also supplies three reader entry points. Readers may begin with experienced prosecution, with readiness for transformation, or with theoretical genealogy; each route then directs them through the other members. This record should be cited for the triptych’s membership, relations, traversal paths, and authorial architecture. It does not contain the full arguments or protocol and should not replace citation of the member works.
