The entry, short

3I/ATLAS at 1665–1668 MHz is the radio band of the hydroxyl radical's two 18 cm main lines at the interstellar comet C/2025 N1: MeerKAT found the comet's OH there in absorption on 24 October 2025 and in emission in November, set a 0.17 W technosignature limit across it, and the archive's notebook holds thirteen faint comet-frame features in the band, off the lines and unreplicated.

〰️ The lines

  • Where: within about 11 kHz of 1665.402 and 1667.359 MHz in the comet's frame.
  • What: OH from water broken down by sunlight in the coma; absorption or emission.

📡 The detection

  • 24 October 2025: absorption in both lines, −8.4 and −14.2 mJy; none in September.
  • Reported: ATel #17473, Pisano, Smirnov, Ivchenko, Roth, Buchner.
  • November: absorption on 5–6, emission on 11–12, as the comet's heliocentric velocity predicts; WIRED gives proximity and geometry.
  • Ultraviolet: Swift's tentative OH, July–August.

🧭 What the line says

  • Activity: with the spectroscopy, it establishes the comet's natural activity; it bears on nothing a transmitter would do.
  • Press: 'ends the debate' (WIRED's headline), though its article expects the theories to persist and asks for monitoring; Futura: a natural origin.
  • Breakthrough Listen: no evidence of other than natural; thorough study warranted.

🔭 Searches at the band

  • MeerKAT BLUSE: 0.17 W over 900–1670 MHz; 23,689 signals, none from the object.
  • Others: ATA 10–110 W; Parkes about 5 W, pending; Green Bank about 0.1 W; none found a technosignature.
  • The notebook: no MeerKAT hit at the lines; ATA counts at chance; the comet's OH not recovered at Green Bank.
  • Scope: a null constrains only what the chain's characterized acceptance covers.

🔢 The thirteen

  • Count: 13 comet-frame clusters at 1665.53–1668.46 MHz against 3.75 ± 2.05 (Green Bank, 18 December 2025).
  • Off the line: the nearest 54 kHz from OH.
  • Fragile: 6 under another normalization; gone at nearby thresholds; 0.35σ per step.
  • Unreplicated: held-out channels, Parkes, two epochs.
  • Below the floor: C03 about 26 mW; in the negative-drift class.
  • Limit: short of a detection; no claim of a transmission.
