The work

The Soteriological Operator Framework is the master document that binds four scriptural studies into one operational system. It asks four different questions: What counts? What cannot count in its place? What prevents correction? What makes collapse terminal?

Matthew 25 supplies the positive criterion. Salvation is not secured by affiliation, self-description, or declared belief but by regard enacted toward the vulnerable under conditions of friction. James 2 is logically prior as an exclusion operator: belief, intent, and identity are inadmissible defenses when they do not become works. John 9 then describes entry into the destructive trajectory, showing how claimed sight can disqualify testimony and expel the witness. Second Thessalonians 2 supplies the terminus, the π-state in which the system’s own outputs confirm its blindness.

The framework uses the S integral and F integral to describe accumulated trajectories toward salvation and counter-salvation. These are not metaphysical scores or automated verdicts. They are structural descriptions of how regard, exclusion, witness response, and self-correction interact over time.

Within the Crimson Hexagon, the framework grounds the John 9 and James 2 modules, the Expelled Witness Position, and the Sevenfold Witness architecture. It is a master framework because the companion modules specify its operators at greater resolution, while the later sevenfold documents distribute those operators across differentiated Assembly positions.
