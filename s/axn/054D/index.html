The work

The Cost of Unshared Reality: A Heteronym Triptych on L_bearing is a mixed creative work by Ayanna Vox, Orin Trace and Johannes Sigil, deposited 19 March 2026.

Its structure is its argument. Three heteronyms address one subject in three registers — Vox in the somatic, Trace in the forensic, Sigil in the archival — followed by a convergence panel, the Chamber Witness, and an integrated office. The triptych is not three views of a topic but three faculties applied to it, and the convergence is a claim that they arrive together rather than an assertion that any one suffices.

L_bearing is the archive's term for the labour of carrying meaning that is not shared — the cost incurred by holding a reality no one else confirms. The panels name what that costs in each register: the wound beneath the wound, the humiliation of the grind. The work was recovered from MANUS-supplied source during the restoration campaign, and its heteronymic division of labour is the Dodecad system doing the thing it exists to do rather than being described.
