The work

Sémantique Potentielle Release 3 is an operative-philology mint release by Lee Sharks and Johannes Sigil.

It adds three categories: the Phase X manuscript lacuna, Sapphic lyric transmission, and Platonic writing and dialogue. Each category provides seed terms and generated concept families under a constraint grammar.
