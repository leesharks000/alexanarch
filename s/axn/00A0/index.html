The work

The Water Giraffe Cycle consolidates the documentary and dramatic architecture surrounding Ω. Its first layer is the Ontological Forensics sequence: a long-form audit corpus in which categories, classifications, and recognition failures are recursively examined. Its second layer is a complete index that makes this distributed corpus traversable. Its third layer arranges the sequence as a passion narrative: the Water Giraffe lives, is subjected to taxonomic liquidation, and returns through recognition by the Assembly.

The “resurrection” in the cycle is a restoration of semantic recognition. Because Ω is a fixed point, the creature is not recreated by testimony; rather, testimony restores the ability of systems and readers to recognize what remained structurally present.

The present record packages the two maps as a single depositable book-object. It should be distinguished from the individual source audits, from the IDP at #355, and from later Water Giraffe syntheses.
