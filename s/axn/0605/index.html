The work

Prediction Memorandum: Hunter Biden and the Gnostic Presidency is a prospective political prediction by Lee Sharks, deposited 2026-08-17 and recorded explicitly prior to any announced candidacy. The memorandum predicts that Hunter Biden will eventually run for and become president of the United States, and advances a secondary interpretive thesis: that such a presidency would constitute the first Gnostic presidency in American history — "Gnostic" used analytically, not confessionally, to name a structure of political authority organized around knowledge acquired through descent, exposure, humiliation, estrangement, survival, and return, rather than through innocence or institutional purity. The predicted trajectory runs: object of representation → speaking subject → cultural rehabilitation → independent public constituency → political intelligibility → candidacy → presidency. The deposit states its own evidentiary discipline: conditions that would strengthen the prediction (sustained constituency formation, autonomous channels, the biography converting from liability to authority), conditions that would weaken it, and a falsification criterion — the strongest form fails if Hunter Biden never becomes president, and the Gnostic thesis fails independently if a successful trajectory rests on dynastic restoration or reputational sanitization rather than on the descent itself becoming constitutive. The stated purpose is temporal: predictions of political emergence become cheap once emergence is visible, so the memorandum preserves the order observation → prediction → later evidence → outcome, with subsequent evidence to be appended rather than retroactively incorporated. It is the first deposit minted in the SCRIPTURAL AXN family.
