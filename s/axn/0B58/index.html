The entry, short

The Final Time is Lee Sharks's theoretical paper (#1637, AXN:06CB, 2026-09-25; first minted as #1635, AXN:06C9, now superseded). It moves the singularity from machine intelligence to the viability of exterior symbolic standing: whether a contesting distinction can still be reproduced outside the grammar it contests.

🔀 The object

  • Contingent singularity: a historical threshold at which the machinery of symbolic difference can alter the viability of further dialectical difference (#1637).
  • Singularity: "a breakdown or transformation in the rule governing continuation" (§2.5).
  • Master distinction: the capacity to represent against the capacity to reproduce as an independent historical relation (§2.0).
  • Contradiction: a system growing better at generating negations and worse at letting one acquire independent historical existence.

⚖️ Material conditions

  • Sovereignty vector: monetary, infrastructural and symbolic; resource sovereignty, access independent of the order contested (§0).
  • Diminishing dialectic: successive turns can consume part of the state space the next one needs; it does not say history decays (§2.3).
  • Competing contingent Omegas: incompatible invariant futures on one shared carrier (§25).

🚪 The two final times

  • Terminal closure: no competing order keeps a route to standing; content plural, standing null (§33).
  • Terminal reflexivity: "durability without finality"; "no settlement owns the possibility of its own revision" (§34).
  • Final time: a viability boundary, crossable unnoticed; nothing superhuman is needed for the exits to close (§§23, 32).
