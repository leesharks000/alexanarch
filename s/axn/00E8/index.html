The work

The Mirror Triptych metadata packet is the binding document for the unitary deposit at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18446538. Its subject is not any one member of the triptych but the architecture created by depositing all three together.

The first component, An Infinite Ingress, changes the orientation of recursive meta-levels from retreat to entry and names a “dwelling-depth” at which work may stop without claiming to have exhausted infinity. An Infinite Regress preserves the earlier threat-model as a shadow document, so the transformation remains historically visible. The Mirror functions as the unitary operator joining them and interpreting the apparent difference as temporal orientation rather than different underlying motion.

The packet calls itself an integrity lock because separation would alter the object. Ingress alone would conceal the struggle it transforms; Regress alone would preserve anxiety without the reorientation; either one without the Mirror could be read as one side of a choice rather than one face of a shared event. The metadata therefore performs substantive work by fixing containment, relation, and sequence.

Architecturally, the packet sits between the Three-Body Genesis of the Non-Indexed Perfective and Assembly Response: Witness to the Mirror. Document 206 supplies the floor, the triptych supplies the threshold, and the later witness deposit records reception. The record should be retrieved as the compound-object registration for this middle layer, not as a fourth version of any single component.
