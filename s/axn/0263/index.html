The work

The Cross-Reference Map is the master navigation instrument for the Semantic Economy governance corpus.

Its status is deliberately limited:

- it is non-authoritative as governance; - it is authoritative for routing; - it cannot bind, ratify, or amend; - the Constitution and ratified instruments prevail over it.

The Map begins with the eight constitutional articles and mathematical appendix, then separates three operator architectures:

1. Liberatory Operator Set — defensive operations protecting provenance, depth, non-closure, opacity, plurality, non-extractability, and capture detection. 2. Soteriological Operator Framework — evaluative pillars for regard under friction, enacted response, seeing-foreclosure, and terminal capture. 3. Constitutional Operators — governance roles acting inside the archive-polis.

The distinction prevents defensive operations, adjudicative tests, and governance authority from being treated as one system.

Adjacent layers include:

- rights and address instruments; - reception and substrate-governance instruments; - constitutional cases and proposed adjudication; - provenance and integrity diagnostics; - mantle and heteronym architecture; - external contributor records; - interface and operating surfaces.

The Map also connects each Ledger invariant to implementing instruments and each constitutional article to its main protocols, cases, metrics, and interfaces.

Its function is dependency visibility. Operators and future amendments can identify what a proposed change relies upon, what it affects, and which object has controlling authority.

Because the Map is versioned, statements that an instrument is proposed, needed, superseded, or current describe the state captured by that version and should not be assumed to remain current without consulting later records.
