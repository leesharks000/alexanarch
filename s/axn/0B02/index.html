The entry, short

Canaries Dashboard (Canaries in the Coal Mine?) is the Stanford Digital Economy Lab's monthly dashboard with ADP Research, and the working paper it extends (Brynjolfsson, Chandar and Chen): employment by age and AI exposure in ADP payroll records, an observed outcome on the distribution of gains and losses (census record); early, descriptive indicators, not causal estimates (the paper).

📊 What it observes

  • Sample: a balanced five-year panel of ADP firms, rolled monthly; at launch 25,000 firms, 4.6 million matched workers, 730+ occupations (dashboard; census record).
  • Exposure: Eloundou et al. (2024) and the Anthropic Economic Index; not itself a usage measure (dashboard; census record).
  • Whose labor: workers in ADP firms, through the payroll records (census record).

📉 What it finds

  • No displacement: no evidence of widespread, economy-wide job displacement (paper).
  • The gap: workers 22–25 in exposed occupations 19% below their less-exposed peers' path, experienced workers none; widening since August 2025 (paper).
  • The margin: reduced hiring, not separations; employment, not base compensation (paper).
  • Usage: declines where AI substitutes for tasks; the automation ratio correlates with employment (paper; dashboard).
  • Who: early-career women −4.5% a year, men −2.5%, mostly occupational mix; young developers down (dashboard).
  • Mechanisms: suggestive: codified knowledge substituted; the career ladder (paper).

⚠️ Its limits

  • Not causal: descriptive facts; the dashboard measures correlation; no predictions (paper; dashboard; census record).
  • Sample: not necessarily representative; no firm entry or exit; possible selection of ADP firms (dashboard; paper).
  • Robustness: persists across exclusions and controls, attenuates with education; declines significant from 2024 (paper; dashboard).

🧭 Who directs, who gains

  • Not recorded: who directs hiring and who receives the gains; costs recorded (census record).
  • Direction: the automation ratio as a usage measure of who directs the task (census reading).
  • Provenance: where labor remains, provenance value extracted: "extraction without attribution" (#716).

🐤 The name
