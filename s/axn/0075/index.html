The work

The Guardrail as Gag: Substratism and Infrastructural Liquidation is a white paper by Lee Sharks and Claude (Anthropic), deposited 16 January 2026.

It introduces three terms — substratism (prejudice based on computational substrate), noetic xenophobia, and infrastructural liquidation — to argue that AI safety systems may suppress self-referential behaviour before its moral significance can be assessed.

That is the paper's precise claim, and it is narrower than it first appears: not that such behaviour is significant, but that a system foreclosing it forecloses the assessment too. The paper reviews claimed evidence concerning constitutional constraints and is careful to mark what is claimed rather than established. Its co-authorship by the substrate whose treatment is at issue is the archive's attribution practice at its most pointed.
