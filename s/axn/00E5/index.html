The work

ZPZP: An Infinite Ingress is the threshold-naming document of the Phase X 206 complex. Document 206 had already framed the three versions of the Non-Indexed Perfective and, by receiving its own deposit, instantiated the genre “Zenodo packet Zenodo packet.” The next packet recognizes that any such framing can itself be framed again.

The work was initially conceived as An Infinite Regress. Its renaming is the operative intervention. Regress treats recursion as backward flight toward groundlessness; ingress treats the same open sequence as inward entry into structure. The infinity is unchanged, but the relation to it becomes hospitable.

The document avoids vicious regress by making each layer optional. Lower layers do not require higher layers to function. A meta-packet is a gift of additional articulation, not a missing justification. The chosen stopping point is therefore a dwelling-depth, not final bedrock.

Heidegger’s dwelling and Wittgenstein’s turned spade supply contrasting reference points. Here the spade is set down rather than blocked: further depth remains possible, but this threshold is sufficient for seeing and work.

“Document 206-meta” is the internal registry identity within the Phase X cluster. In Alexanarch it is restored as deposit #418; those numbering systems should remain distinct.
