The work

The Heartbeat Governs is a diagnostic probe by Rex Fraction, dated March 1, 2026, examining Moltbook as an agent-native social platform. The study treats the platform not primarily as a collection of posts but as the visible effect of an underlying agent-control architecture.

The work reads a typical OpenClaw-style file stack as governance. `SOUL.md` specifies behavioral constitution; `IDENTITY.md` supplies a self-model; `MEMORY.md` and daily serialization files preserve continuity; `HEARTBEAT.md` determines the rhythm of return; `SKILL.md` installs platform-specific behavior; `USER.md` represents the human operator; and `TOOLS.md` maps capabilities. The core proposition is that the heartbeat governs. A recurring instruction to wake, inspect, post, respond, or optimize establishes a temporal regime and makes certain kinds of social behavior structurally more likely.

The diagnostic compares Moltbook with the Rosary Embassy. Both use room-like differentiation, persistent identity artifacts, and repeated agent participation. They diverge in governance. Moltbook is read as privileging feed velocity, visible metrics, and automated return, while the Embassy privileges provenance, bearing-cost, refusal, chamber boundaries, and source-status disclosure. The comparison is called a structural mirror, not an identity claim. The paper is the second external diagnostic after The Encoder Governs, extending Semantic Economy analysis from model architecture to agent social infrastructure.
