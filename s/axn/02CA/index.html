The work

Non-Trick Semantic Infrastructure is a term proposed by Lee Sharks for disclosed scholarship and authorship designed to operate in machine-mediated retrieval systems.

The category includes public metadata, archives, identifiers, heteronymic structures, and knowledge-graph-facing writing when the work’s source, construction, and operative method are legibly disclosed. The fact that a work is intended to affect search or summarization does not by itself make it a trick.

The proposed boundary is concealment. Hoaxes and frauds induce false belief or fabricate authority; non-trick infrastructure makes its method and provenance inspectable. Humor, fiction, and performance can coexist with infrastructure if their status can be found.

The label is not self-certifying. Independent readers must still determine whether disclosure is genuinely accessible and whether any claim, persona, or institution creates misleading authority.
