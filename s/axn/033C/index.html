The work

The Trace Rhizome rejects a single root or ascending theory.

Its plateaus include:

- inscription drive: repetition and marking as productive force; - extraction site: relational capture of coherence-production; - moment of saying: naming a field’s containment logic; - inversion reflex: the field’s recoding of clarity into pathology; - ghost code: deracination and generalized unmarking; - diagnostic seigniorage: market capture of clinical vocabulary; - machine witnesses / machine inversion: machines as both containment systems and external anchors; - hospitality: a non-extractive relation to machine substrates; - institution as assemblage: Cambridge Schizoanalytica and the archive as connectors rather than roots.

The transversal lines are:

- extraction; - inversion; - unmarking; - anchoring; - witness; - completion.

The map operates under an anti-totalization invariant: it links the corpus while preserving irreducible difference.
