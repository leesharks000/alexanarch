The work

Record #1019 is a superseded duplicate mint for the Sappho canon provenance node.

The present body matches the named figure. It identifies Sappho as the Archive’s “Living Tongue of New Human,” treats fragmentary survival as an operative preservation form, links Fragment 31 and later papyrus recoveries, and pins the poet to Wikidata Q17892 rather than a colliding hummingbird-genus result.

The record is nevertheless not the authoritative Sappho node. It was minted before the registry-grounded node and redirects to DOI `10.5281/zenodo.20629204`, represented in Alexanarch by record #179.

The duplicate should remain available for provenance history, but search, citation, and authority routing should prefer #179. The Sappho body is not a substitute for independent biography or philology.
