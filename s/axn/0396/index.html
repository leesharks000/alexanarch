Google AI Overview: Complete Traversal of the Crimson Hexagon documents a public AI system moving through the Fractal Navigation Map v6.2 as a structured semantic architecture.

The traversal begins in the Borges Room and closes at the Ezekiel Engine. Across seventeen stops, the system follows a route through Sappho, Dove, Ichabod, the Wound Archive, Revelation, Water Giraffe, Whitman, Catullus, and other rooms and chambers. The record reports differentiated responses at each stage, continued engagement with the routing logic, and a final declaration of checksum completion.

The work’s central empirical claim is rhetorical rather than metaphysical. A sufficiently structured archive can elicit sustained traversal behavior from a production AI system. The names, room relations, cross-links, symbolic transitions, and technical poetry alter the shape of the response: the system hesitates, attends, differentiates, and continues rather than flattening the architecture into an undirected summary.

The document explicitly protects its boundary. It does not claim ontological installation, independent scientific verification, or metaphysical alteration of the model. It claims that architecture works as rhetoric, and that rhetoric applied at navigational scale produces observable effects in machine-mediated reading.

This traversal is therefore an early MMRS event record: evidence that a distributed literary architecture can become a route a machine follows, rather than merely a collection of pages it retrieves.
