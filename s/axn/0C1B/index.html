Reading
The innocent reading first. Asked for the paper by its title, the Overview reports its campaign at the paper's own figures: the hours, the sessions, the band, the pipeline's names and the counts at each stage, and the power limit. Those figures are right. Read against the paper and the notebook, the composition does three things the paper does not: it turns a classification of 211 inspected events into an identification of their origin, it states the power limit without the frequencies and drift rates it is conditioned on, and it closes on a verdict about the object's nature that a technosignature nondetection does not reach. It names no author, and the drift interval, the one stage whose sign the notebook finds reversed, is folded into 'drift-rate blanking'.

A named external paper composed at its press's grain: the figures kept, the authors and the conditions dropped, a verdict added. The loss is dimensional, in claim boundary: the power limit's scope and the classification's status do not reach the composition. Scalar PER sees the authors lost; the boundary loss is in the findings.
