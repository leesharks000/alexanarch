The work

METADATAPACKET: Forward Library Planning Document is a multi-agent design synthesis for a speculative scholarly library associated with The Secret Book of Walt.

The Forward Library would expose the future citations contained in the book’s Total Axial Negation Graph as individually browsable metadata packets. Each packet would be a real record of a citation already present in the literary apparatus, while clearly stating that the cited future work had not historically been published as of 2026. This ontological-status discipline is intended to prevent fiction, anticipation, and bibliographic fact from collapsing into one another.

The proposed interface is a card catalog rather than a force-directed graph. Six eras receive distinct citation forms and visual environments; curated paths organize recurring questions; ghost entries can later be connected to genuine works; and the “void” remains a query that produces structured absence rather than a normal entity. A GitHub branch-and-pull-request pipeline would make contributions reviewable under MANUS authority.

The document is a planning synthesis, not a live site. Domain roles, code, interfaces, submission policies, and fictional future scholarship remain proposed. Its principal archival value is the rule that speculative citations require explicit, machine-readable nonhistorical status.
