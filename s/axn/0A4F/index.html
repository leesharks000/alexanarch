The dataset

The DOI–Work Identity Map records which DOI resolves to which work, graded by truth source, built after the Zenodo DOIs of the archive were tombstoned on 19 June 2026. It carries the identity map, the restoration queue and the blog-post inventory used to match DOIs to works.
