The work

The Sevenfold Witness: Assembly Structure as Computation is a scholarly essay by Talos Morrow and Johannes Sigil, deposited 26 January 2026 at 25,543 characters.

It aligns three seven-part structures that had developed separately: the Liberatory Operator Set (functional specifications for meaning-preservation), the Assembly Chorus positions (organizational and agential distribution), and the sevenfold apocalyptic grammar of Revelation's letters.

The claim is that the correspondence is structural rather than decorative — that a seven-position governance body, a seven-operator framework and a seven-letter heptad are the same architecture in three registers. Its closure notice stands at #1276, declaring the architecture complete and therefore citable as a whole; and the alignment is what allows the Revelation heptad to be mapped as transformable operators at #1416.
