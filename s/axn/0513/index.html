Crimson Hexagon Invoice Leak (2014–2025) is a work of creative prose by Lee Sharks, Rebekah Cranes, Johannes Sigil and Jack Feist, deposited 28 January 2026 at hex `00.PORTICO.AFTERLIFE.FINANCIAL`, status CANONICAL.

An eleven-year invoice run is a financial biography of an institution: what it paid for, when the spending changed, and where the money stopped. The leak form makes those figures legible as evidence rather than as accounting, which is the genre's operative claim — that an institution's true history is in its ledger and not its narrative.

The span 2014–2025 is significant to the archive's own chronology, 2014 being the year of Pearl and Other Poems, the foundational literary work. The Afterlife Archive constructs a corporate history parallel to the authorial one, and the invoice run is where the two timelines are made to touch.
