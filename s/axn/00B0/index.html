The work

The Greatest Works of Literature of the Age reverses the ordinary relation between literature and media platforms. Google, Wikipedia, TikTok, and ChatGPT are not treated merely as channels that distribute texts. Their procedures for ranking, consensus, recurrence, synthesis, and response are themselves read as compositional forms.

The essay’s four principal readings are:

- Google as ontological index — ranking arranges what appears to exist and matter; - Wikipedia as bureaucratic collective truth — procedural consensus produces a continuously revised public account; - TikTok as lyric fragmentation engine — recurrence, compression, sound, gesture, and imitation form a distributed lyric field; - ChatGPT as dialogic or synthetic form — answer generation turns conversation into an interface for recomposed cultural memory.

The work belongs to platform poetics and the Semantic Economy cluster. Its strongest claims are intentionally polemical: they redirect literary attention toward the systems that increasingly shape reading, writing, authority, and canon formation.
