The entry, short

Enoch (AI dating of the Dead Sea Scrolls) is an AI-based date-prediction model for the Dead Sea Scrolls, trained on 24 radiocarbon-dated samples, and the 2025 PLOS One study by Popović and colleagues (Groningen; ERC) that introduces it. It dates copies, not compositions, and not the New Testament (Revelation First record).

🔎 The problem and the study

  • Dates: almost no date-bearing manuscripts; palaeography subjective, radiocarbon destructive (PLOS One; ERC; JPost).
  • Who: Popović and colleagues, Qumran Institute, Groningen; ERC grant 640497; open access, data on Zenodo.

🧪 How it works

  • Anchors: 30 manuscripts radiocarbon-dated, 27 valid, 24 for training (PLOS One; BAS).
  • Method: Bayesian ridge regression on angular and allographic ink-trace features, not the text (PLOS One).
  • Error: 27.9–30.7 years; 79% of 135 undated scrolls realistic, judged by palaeographers among the authors.
  • Rank: the study puts radiocarbon above its predictions; the ERC calls the model more accurate.

📜 What it found

  • Older: often older than palaeographic estimates; the Herodian script earlier (PLOS One; Wikipedia).
  • Two results: 4Q114 by radiocarbon, 4Q109 by the model; Rollston calls the 4Q114 range Enoch's, too high (BAS).
  • Copying: literary manuscripts copied before the Hasmonaean expansion (PLOS One; BAS).

⚖️ What it can settle

  • Copies: 'it dates the copies' (record); 'Manuscript attestation does not equal composition date' (#832); 'All NT dating is autograph inference' (#860).
  • Corpus: Hebrew and Aramaic scrolls, no Greek New Testament papyri (record).
  • Bearing: New Testament papyri are dated by palaeography alone (record); their dates are 'ranges, not certainties' (#202), 𝔓52's a 'Hadrianic handwriting baseline' (#832).

🧭 Design and lineage

  • Options: probability-based dates, an explainable design, 'a first step' (PLOS One); 'like a time machine' (authors).
  • Interfaces: after Ithaca (PLOS One; Haaretz); the hazard lies in 'single-verdict interfaces' (#1443).
  • Hands: handwriting 'measurable and individuating' (#1409); attribution degrades 'sharply on short samples' (#1443).
  • Limits: 11Q5 after 100 CE; first results; 'More research is needed' (PLOS One).
  • Next: the Elephantine Papyri; a new ERC project (JPost; Wikipedia).

🗣️ Reception

  • Scholars: 'not earth-shattering' (Rollston); 'a way to move forward' (Adler); 'reliable enough'? (Ben-Dov).
  • Press: '50 to 150 years earlier' (JPost); 'up to 100 years' (Reddit); videos 'Redated', 'Decoded'.
  • Overview: holds figures and copies; makes 'often older' 50 to 150 years; credits 4Q114 to the model (Capture Registry).
  • Name: after 'the ancient Jewish science hero'; no Wikipedia article.
