The entry, short

Data Provenance Initiative is a volunteer collective of AI researchers auditing AI training data: 1,800+ text datasets in its 2023 first release, now four public measurement systems from datasets and web consent to model markets and real use (its pages); the archive sets it in the licensing dimension of provenance, bounded at the corpus (#725).

🗂️ What it is

  • Operator: a "volunteer effort"; paper by Longpre and 16 others (README; arXiv; census); MIT researchers among its founders (MIT Sloan).
  • Data provenance: a dataset's "sourcing, creation and licensing heritage" (Nature MI), traced to source, creators, licences, use (arXiv; census).
  • Its dimension: training-corpus licensing: "Under what permissions did this data enter this system?" (#725).

🧾 Origin and extent

  • Founding audit: 1800+ text datasets, 25 Oct 2023 (arXiv); 1,858 in 44 collections (Nature MI, 2024).
  • Tools: Explorer, Collection and Card, "symbolic attribution for all constituent datasets" (README).
  • Now: 4,000+ datasets audited, 14,000 web domains tracked, 851k open models, 23,158 conversations (home page).
  • Later audits: Consent in Crisis (NeurIPS 2024); four thousand text, speech and video datasets (ICLR 2025).
  • The record: the census keeps the first release's count, the composition "ahead of the record" (Capture Registry).
  • One record or three: "distinct records", and "the same entity" as the explorer's MMRS record (census).

🔎 Findings

  • Licences: omission 70%+, errors 50%+ on hosting sites (arXiv); 66% of Hugging Face licences recategorized (Nature MI).
  • Attribution: 85% of licences request it, 30% share-alike (Nature MI; #725).
  • Divide: closed datasets hold lower-resource languages and creative tasks (arXiv); non-commercial licences 61% in 2023 (Nature MI).
  • Skew: Western-centric (Nature MI; MIT Sloan).

⚖️ Limits and bound

  • Limits: not legal advice; self-reported licences (README; Nature MI; About); a collection does not show which model used it (census).
  • Bound: "audits whether datasets were licensed, not whether synthesized outputs preserve attribution" (#725).
  • Held beside: studies of "attribution loss in AI outputs" (#1003).
  • Layer: "Documentation project, not infrastructure"; L4; its schema "Implemented (in CHA)" (#98).
  • Pipeline: "Provenance has to survive the training pipeline" (#1714; #95; #623).

📰 Reception

  • Press: major press (home page); no Wikipedia article; DPI papers predicted for broad queries (#725).
  • Overview: operator from the MIT Sloan card; no author; no card cites the record (Capture Registry).
