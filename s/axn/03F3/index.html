The work

Corpus Navigation Map is a November 19, 2025 snapshot of the Crimson Hexagonal corpus during an early phase of theoretical consolidation.

The map arranges the corpus in tiers. Tier 0 provides an entry point. Tier 1 contains foundational statements and constitutional texts. Tier 2 gathers major theoretical works. Tier 3 contains technical architecture. Tier 3.5 marks the poetic, Whitmanian, and network-facing layer. Tier 4 gathers integration documents and operator frameworks.

Several reading paths are offered for readers approaching the project through political economy, theology, poetics, machine systems, or the integrated architecture. The map also records approximate corpus size, composition chronology, and documents that were still absent or planned.

Its strongest archival value is temporal. It shows how the project understood itself on one date, before later deposits, institutions, registries, and sovereign infrastructure changed the shape of the archive.

Statements that the theory, architecture, or integration were “complete” are historical self-assessments. They do not mean the corpus stopped developing or that every named component was externally validated.
