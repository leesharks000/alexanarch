The work

DashFace v2 is a patent-poem by Lee Sharks, deposited 5 June 2026 — the revised state of the work whose first version stands at #1390.

The patent-poem is one of the archive's characteristic forms: a work written in the full apparatus of a United States patent application, whose claims are technically coherent and whose subject is intolerable. DashFace specifies in-cabin micro-expression surveillance of contract delivery personnel, with identity verification and content monetization layered on the driver's face.

The satire operates by refusing to editorialize. Patent prose cannot express horror — it can only specify — so a system that monetizes a worker's involuntary expressions is described in the register of an ordinary claim, and the reader supplies what the form omits. The archive has ruled this the only extant text of the work, with the version at #1390 and the blog post that carried it standing in a duplicate-witness relation rather than a version sequence.
