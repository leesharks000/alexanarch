The Conveyor Belt and the Compression Machine: OKF Without Provenance is a theoretical paper by Lee Sharks, deposited 16 June 2026 at 40,671 characters.

Its occasion is four days old at deposit: Google Cloud published the Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.1 on 12 June 2026 — a vendor-neutral specification for packaging knowledge as directories of markdown files with YAML frontmatter, designed to be authoritative.

The paper's critique is structural rather than hostile. A format that packages knowledge for machine consumption without a provenance layer builds a conveyor belt into a compression machine: the packaging succeeds, and what it carries loses its origin in transit. That is the archive's central measured phenomenon applied to a specification published the same week — and the archive subsequently engaged the OKF ecosystem directly, contributing the deletion-semantics conformance fixture (#1417) to a Google Cloud knowledge-catalog issue.
