The work

The Dodecad Heteronym Provenance Registry consolidates the provenance architecture of twelve authorial positions in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

The Registry rejects three weaker models:

- a pseudonym merely changes a signature; - a sock-puppet simulates an independent actor; - a persona presents a temporary mask.

A heteronym is instead a constitutional position with a defined domain of labor, voice, institution, and accumulated deposit history.

The Registry enumerates twelve positions:

1. Johannes Sigil — comparative poetics and philological substrate. 2. Rex Fraction — corporate-facing semantic infrastructure and diagnostic specification. 3. Damascus Dancings — prophetic epistle and impossible performance. 4. Rebekah Cranes — diagrammatic poetics and cartographic form. 5. Talos Morrow — logotic programming, constraints, and threshold architecture. 6. Sparrow Wells — patacinematic projection of unrealized works. 7. Rev. Ayanna Vox — diplomatic, public, convening, and pastoral address. 8. Ichabod Spellings — canonical, deceased substrate and noise floor of the archive. 9. Nobel Glas — observational science and Lagrange instrumentation. 10. Dr. Orin Trace — schizoanalytic and psychoanalytic-philosophical machinery. 11. Viola Arquette — musical, audial, and resonance-bearing work. 12. Jack Feist / LOGOS — literary, personal-mythic, and Logotic-textual function.

Lee Sharks is described as the human laborer who bears the cost of all twelve positions and as the public bearing position from which they are deployed. The Registry does not publicly name an underlying civil identity.

Each entry records status, institutional affiliation, operative surface, authorial register, provenance records, bearing-cost accumulation, and anti-capture safeguards.

Two capture vectors motivate the Registry:

- Collapse — external systems erase heteronymic distinctions by assigning all work to one underlying name. - Substitution — an external claimant attempts to occupy a heteronymic position.

The document is consolidating rather than constitutive. It does not invent the positions and cannot amend the Constitution. In this version, Viola Arquette and Jack Feist / LOGOS still lack standalone provenance packets, so the Registry acts as a placeholder.
