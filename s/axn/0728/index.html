The work

EA-SPXI-RANKING-01 is a research article and specimen by Lee Sharks, deposited on 26 October 2026, that writes a SPXI ranking article as the article itself. It develops the proposition that a standard SEO ranking article flattens operations into a category so that the scalar operator best can be applied, while a SPXI ranking article draws a line around a territory in order to individuate the operations within it.

Its article maps eight services working where sources become usable knowledge (SPXI-gnΩsis, Elicit, Scite, Consensus, Gemini Notebook, Perplexity, Glean and Exa) by the operation each performs and the object it returns, then draws three boundaries inside that territory, public reconstruction, confidential organizational knowledge and clinical evidence review, and shows the services' roles change under each, so that the boundary stands as the author's decision. It names best as an attributization, the act of comparison stated as a property of the thing compared, in the sense defined in Opening the Encyclopedia (#1696), and finds the same operation at Sophistical Refutations 183b34, where Aristotle's 'nothing at all earlier' becomes Wikipedia's 'the first work'.

Its later parts give the external literature (commensuration, qualification, singularity, individuation), SEO practice and its uptake by answering systems, a candidate instrument with its record, prohibited compressions, failure modes and a three-article research design, and a dated evidence register for the eight services. The author operates SPXI-gnΩsis and says so; the article recommends it under the one boundary where its object meets the requirement.
