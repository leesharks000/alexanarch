The work

The Seven Unfoldings: The Heptad Mapped is a specification and executable research surface by Lee Sharks, deposited 28 July 2026, applying the midrashim transform across all seven letters of Revelation 2–3.

Each letter is mapped to its derived text with an evidence grade attached — Ephesus → 1 John at B+, reading first-love as the from-the-beginning command and the trying of liars as the testing of spirits, and so through the heptad. Granularity is proportional to evidence: letters with richer intertextual traces are worked more finely, and the specification says so rather than presenting uniform confidence.

The design principle is the one that governs the whole Revelation First series — a transform is a claim that can fail, so the grades are preserved from the evaluation rather than smoothed into the presentation. The heptad map stands between the single worked case (#1415, the Smyrna letter) and the four remaining canonical derivations (#1418).
