The entry, short

NewsGuard AI Tracking Center is NewsGuard's catalogue of AI content farm websites, unreliable news sites run with little or no human oversight: 3,749 in 16 languages as of 23 June 2026 (MMRS record; the center's page), launched in June 2023 to show how generative AI is deployed "to turbocharge misinformation operations and unreliable news" (NewsGuard).

📇 What it catalogues

  • Criteria: all four: mostly AI-made; no significant human oversight (chatbot error text a sign); passes for journalism; no disclosure (NewsGuard; MMRS record).
  • Left out: AI used with effective human oversight (NewsGuard); and "Disclosure takes a site out" (MMRS record).
  • Count: 49 "Unreliable AI-Generated News" sites in May 2023, 150 at launch; the list on request (NewsGuard).
  • The sites: generic names, dozens of articles a day, often originating false claims (NewsGuard).

💰 Why the farms pay

  • Programmatic ads: "In many cases" the revenue model, through "ad-tech firms such as Google"; unless brands exclude them, "an economic incentive for their creation at scale" (NewsGuard).
  • Compression: content farms "compress journalism into SEO-optimized paraphrase", burning "citational provenance" (#641); the Macedonian farms, now cheaper (Brill).

📊 What it holds

  • Series: the AI False Claim Monitor, a part of the center (MMRS record): monthly from July 2024, then quarterly; journalistic red-teaming; false claims "more than 28%" of the time (NewsGuard).
  • Reports: Sora videos missed in 78-95 percent of prompts; Pravda claims repeated 33 percent; 67% of top news sites block chatbots (NewsGuard).
  • Debunks: from the Fingerprints catalog (NewsGuard).

🧭 Place in the field

  • Supply side: "the sites that answer surfaces may retrieve and cite"; the series records the composition side (MMRS record).

🏷️ Not the center

  • Products: datastreams and red-teaming for AI companies, Brand Misinformation Monitoring for brands (NewsGuard); filed under the center, "the entity widens from a catalogue to a company's offer" (Capture Registry).
  • Operator: NewsGuard, "a rating system for news and information websites" (Wikipedia), since 2018 (NewsGuard); the center active, read 28 October 2026 (MMRS record).
  • Encyclopedia: NewsGuard's AI audits, no section on the center; no article names it (Wikipedia).

🧪 Reception

  • 29 October 2026: the Overview's count is the record's; NewsGuard's products filed under the center; two figures unverified (Capture Registry).
