The entry, short

Labor Action Tracker is a database and map of strike and labor protest activity across the United States, public since 1 May 2021 and since January 2024 a collaboration of Cornell University's ILR School and the University of Illinois' LER School (its About page; maryleelabor census record); it counts the stoppages the BLS, which records only those of 1,000 or more workers, leaves out (Annual Report 2025).

🔎 What it counts

  • Unit: a strike or a labor protest; work stoppages include lockouts (census record; Annual Report 2025).
  • Against the BLS: federal data since the early 1980s cover stoppages of 1,000+ workers only; "NOT official statistics" (Annual Report 2025; BLS card; census record).
  • Method: public sources, verified and entered by hand; sources linked on the map (About page; Annual Report 2025).
  • Limits: protests a "general understanding", no complete count; the map counts locations; most accurate as of 2021 (About page; Annual Report 2025).

📊 2025

  • Totals: 303 stoppages, about 290,000 workers, 4,150,000 strike days (Annual Report 2025).
  • Where: food service most stoppages; health care most workers; the West 77.1% of strikers (Annual Report 2025).
  • Who: nonunion share 18.5%; 95.5% of union strikes authorized; first-contract demands 45 to 70 (Annual Report 2025).

🏛️ Holder and lineage

  • Who runs it: director Johnnie Kallas; both schools fund it (About page; Annual Report 2025).
  • Lineage: inspired by the China Labour Bulletin; grew out of pandemic-era protests (About page; Daily Illini card).
  • Access: map, reports, a spreadsheet on request; no licence stated (About page; Cornell ILR; census record).

🧭 The record's question

  • Keeps: strikes as publicly reported, held by a university; losing side "not recorded" (census record).
  • Reading: it records that a strike happened, nothing of the arguments over whether or how to strike (census record; #1725).
  • The strike: labor withheld to halt production (#437; #39); channelled by law (#239); "the verb withdrawn" (#1668); apart from the semantic strike (#1438; #1439).

📋 Reception

  • At the name: faithful at the BLS threshold; start date and China Labour Bulletin left out (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31).
  • Wikipedia: no article (2026-10-31).
