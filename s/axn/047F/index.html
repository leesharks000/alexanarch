The work

The Soteriological Operator Framework: A Unified Specification is a specification by Lee Sharks with Assembly co-development, deposited 25 January 2026.

Its move is to formalise salvation and damnation as structural dynamics rather than categorical assignments — not states a person is sorted into, but operations that run. That reframing is what makes the framework specifiable at all: a category admits members, while a dynamic has inputs, conditions and outputs that can be stated and disputed.

The framework unifies operators drawn from Matthew 25, James 2 and 2 Thessalonians 2, and the witness-punishment operator derived from John 9 at #1135 is the addition that exposed the framework's gap. Treating theology as operator specification is the same method the midrashim transform applies to intertextual derivation — named rules, gradeable outputs.
