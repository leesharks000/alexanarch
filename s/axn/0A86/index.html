The dataset

Books gathers the archive's book-length works by work, where the registry stores them by deposit: each work with its editions, samplers and critical editions, and the apparatus around it (navigation maps, metadata packets, negation graphs) listed apart, since a navigation map is not the book. It records twenty works, the editions deposited, two works not yet recovered, and the reassignments made in building it.
