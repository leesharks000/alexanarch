Analysis
Entrance at a new address one day after deposit, composed from the work's own surface with its author attributed through the heteronym. The slip is the dialogue's own subject performed on it. The dialogue is about a summarizer cutting the sentence at the comma, so that 'by nature and not by chance' never arrives; the Overview carries the cut as a topic and loses the clause it names, turning 'and not by chance' into 'or by chance', the form of the triad Sharks leaves open at the close. Aristotle's sentence excludes chance; the dialogue's close restores chance as an open option; the composition merges the two and assigns the open option to Aristotle.
