The dataset

The Capture Registry (EA-WG-CAPTURES-01) is the archive's record of machine reception: dated observations of how composition surfaces (Google AI Overview and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Bing, Perplexity and others) compose answers about this corpus, each with its transcript, its source cards, the analysis that restores the work at its own grain, and its measures (PER and its dimensional rubric). The unit is the address, the exact issued string on one surface; each observation, sealed with its analysis, is one AXN (AXN:<HEX>.CAPTURE.<glyphs>). Its canonical surface is alexanarch.org/captures/; each release freezes the registry, its sealed observations and their AXN ledger.
