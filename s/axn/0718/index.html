The entry, short

The singularity is, in Wikipedia's definition, a hypothetical event in which technological growth accelerates beyond human control; The Final Time (#1637) holds it a family-resemblance term and defines the contingent singularity, a historical threshold at which the material machinery of symbolic difference can alter the viability of further dialectical difference (#1635).

🧭 What it names

  • Definitions (Wikipedia, IBM, Third Way, Marr, Britannica): growth beyond control; AI surpassing itself; a synthesis of new technologies.
  • Re-objected (#1635): runaway closure, or durable non-closure, beside runaway intelligence; a change in the rule governing continuation.
  • Superintelligence needed? Vinge: yes (Wikipedia); #1635: no, "Nothing has to be superhuman".

📜 Lineage

  • Von Neumann 1958, Good 1965, Vinge 1993, Kurzweil 2005 (Wikipedia, Third Way, The Hill, singularity.com): an essential singularity; the intelligence explosion, the last invention if docile; the end of the human era; The Singularity Is Near, accelerating revolutions.
  • Coordinates (#1635, #1637): Good's recursion, Vinge's discontinuity, Kurzweil's horizon, Chalmers's AI++, Bostrom's takeoff.

🌫️ Discontinuity and mechanism

  • Unpredictable (Wikipedia, Miessler): past superintelligence; "the moment we lose the ability to see what's coming".
  • Self-improvement (Third Way, Marr): AGI to ASI; AI improving itself without human direction.
  • Generate against reproduce (hypothesis; #1635, #1637): better at generating negations, worse at letting them stand; standing has material conditions.
  • Narrowing (#1637): no exponential acceleration needed; diminution possible, not presumed universal.

⏳ When

  • Dates: Vinge 2005–2030, closed in Miessler's judgment; Kurzweil 2045 (Wikipedia, IBM); AGI this decade, an emerging consensus (Third Way).
  • Not a date (#1635): a viability boundary, not claimed crossed.

⚖️ Plausibility and limits

  • Disputed (Wikipedia, IBM): Allen, Pinker, Penrose, others; S curves; exponentials that never last; perhaps insurmountable obstacles.
  • Growth not its variable (#1635): depends on none of the growth assumptions.
  • Capability apart: human-level machines need no singularity (Wikipedia); capability can rise as exterior history contracts (#1637).
  • Counterexample (#1635): robust exteriority under near-total concentration, or collapse by intelligence growth alone.
  • Non-claims (#1635, #1637): not that self-improvement or a technological singularity is impossible.

🌓 Two singularities, and their signs

  • Coexisting (#1635): the technological can accelerate the contingent or be irrelevant to it.
  • Sign unknown (Britannica): "amazing or apocalyptic".
  • Double (#1635, #1637): terminal closure or terminal reflexivity, "durability without finality"; competing contingent Omegas.
  • Closure (#1643): an end of history, in the bad sense.
