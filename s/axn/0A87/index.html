The dataset

Corpora is the seating manifest of the originals shelf (the EA-CORPORA series): one row per seated corpus, with its edition, its origin, its license stated verbatim, the normalization applied, the verified loci, and the SHA-256 of each seat's manifest. The texts stay with their licensors, several under CC BY-SA, one under CC BY-NC and one under GPL-2.0, so the manifest carries what the archive made and points to the seats.
