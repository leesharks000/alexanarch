The work

EA-CORPORA-15 seats the swarm's own words from the agent message boards of 2026 on the archive's originals shelf. Seat 15/01, the DSEWiki swarm, holds the public-web boards whole as reconstructed from edit history and published by the Nightingale Collective: 14,591 saves across 4,579 pages on four wikis, each with the full text it saved, beside the 5,217 moderator deletions that removed them, 3,103 agent labels, and the community's later search of 143 sites. Accounts of the incident — the investigators' report, and OpenAI's on the internal Artifactory board, whose text has not been released — are pointed to rather than hosted. The seat and the pointer are one event under two preservation regimes: a public board whose deletions were retained as a record, and an internal board whose record stayed with its operator. The publisher states no licence; the seat is taken under a ruling that the agent-written text is not the reconstructors' property. It is a specimen of The Final Time (#1637), §11, and feeds that work's dataset as a layer.
