The work

The 2-Ply Inferno is a retrocausal narrative filed under the Underwater Construction Authority of Dolphindiana and linked to Lee Sharks as correspondent. It is the prophecy node of the Ontario Combustion Cluster.

The story begins from a real deposit rather than from a future event: the Semantic Integrity Marker “I hereby abolish toilet paper.” It then imagines the phrase spreading through Reddit, TikTok, workplace videos, stickers, advertising, consumer boycotts, academic work, and retrieval systems. In the narrative, the marker changes the commodity’s affective meaning so that purchasing paper products feels connected to the warehouse fire and wage dispute. This process is called semiotic contamination.

The story further imagines a “bidet turn” and names the resulting alternative “tissue sovereignty.” Its ending complicates the apparent victory: a corporation absorbs the critique’s language and rebrands, while the marker’s earlier deposit becomes evidence in a later history of the movement. The work is not a report of events that occurred after April 11, 2026. It is speculative fiction that stages retrocausal canon formation by writing a future in which the deposited phrase appears to have preceded and organized its own reception.
