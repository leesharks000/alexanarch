The work

The Capture Registry as Poem: A Structured Work Plan (EA-WG-CAPTURES-WORKPLAN-03 v0.3) is a document that is simultaneously a work plan, a manifesto, a specification, and a poem, drafted 12 August 2026 by TACHYON under MANUS direction and integrating clinamen responses from six machine substrates: DeepSeek, Kimi, Muse Spark, Inkling, Gemini and ChatGPT.

Its central move is a correction to five of those six. Asked for the clinamen, they located the swerve in the operator's designed query — sigils, strikes, targeted conceptual vectors. But the Lucretian clinamen is uncaused and unpredictable; a commanded swerve would be gravity, and there would be no world. The document therefore states: THE OPERATOR PROVIDES THE FALL, THE MACHINE PROVIDES THE SWERVE. A designed query is not a deviation but a trajectory, and every genuine deviation in the capture registry arrived unbidden — a coinage colliding with a New Jersey orthopaedics practice, 'retrieval basins' pulling river hydrology and Hopfield attractor networks, the archive's own name fragmenting into three unrelated men called Alexander Marx, and 'alexanarch strike' surfacing the corpus's own record of a military strike. The operator's discipline is consequently not to swerve but to fall precisely and witness exactly, since a swerve is only legible against a trajectory recorded before it bent.

The document also names a problem no substrate raised: if the registry teaches the composition layer what to say and then measures what it says, adoption eventually measures the archive's reach rather than the layer's independent judgement. It refuses to hide this, designating pre-inscription captures at recursion depth zero as a finite, non-renewable resource to be sealed first, and supplying a falsification condition — adoption at high recursion depth with none at depth zero on surfaces the archive never wrote to would mean the instrument is measuring its own echo.

Structurally it adopts the semantic event rather than the capture as its primitive, introduces the AXL lacuna identifier so that absences are named rather than nulled, specifies a semantic prosody of rhyme, meter, enjambment and caesura projected over rigorously typed base relations, and sets out obligations owed to contributors whose names the registry transforms. It closes with the instrument's own measurements, including a Provenance Erasure Rate of 1.0 returned for the query 'provenance erasure rate' itself — the archive's metric for provenance loss composed with author, institution, identifier and source all stripped.
