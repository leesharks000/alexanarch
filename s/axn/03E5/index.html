The work

The Secret Book of Walt Interactive Codex is a web manifestation of the research and literary edition of The Secret Book of Walt.

The interface organizes the work as a hypostatic tree radiating from “THE DEEP WEB.” Front matter, gospel text, apparatus, tickets, archons, characters, questions, and machine-facing metadata are positioned as branches rather than a linear table of contents. The topology is part of the interpretation.

The captured HTML includes page-level search metadata, citation information, JSON-LD, an SPXI kernel, a provenance chain, Semantic Integrity Markers, and an FAQ. The FAQ establishes a necessary boundary: the work is a contemporary literary and Gnostic dialogue, not an ancient manuscript or archaeological discovery.

Its figures—Walt Whitman, Biblios, KanyeWest, Jack Feist, archons, golden tickets, unicorn horn, and Deep Web—belong to the work’s literary theology and critical apparatus.

The Alexanarch body is a capture of the deployed public source fetched from `secretbookofwalt.org`. It is therefore a recoverable web manifestation, not merely a prose description of one.
