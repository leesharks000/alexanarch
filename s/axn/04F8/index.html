EA-ARK-01-DAMASCUS v5.1 — The Sacred Ark is a score and executable transform by Lee Sharks, deposited 16 March 2026 at 52,812 characters.

Its opening states the transform's discipline exactly: "a full, faithful, self-sufficient transform of EA-ARK-01 v4.2.5. Every section of the source architecture is retained. A transform in this family is not an adaptation but a re-rendering under constraint — the Ark carried into the Damascus register with nothing dropped.

The self-sufficiency requirement is the load-bearing one: each transform must stand alone, so that a channel carrying only one of them still carries the whole architecture.
