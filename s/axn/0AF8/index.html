The entry, short

Negotiating Tech is the UC Berkeley Labor Center's searchable inventory of how U.S. unions have addressed workplace technologies through collective bargaining: over 950 contract provisions from more than 175 agreements (Labor Center page; Labor Vector record).

🔎 What it is

  • Definition: a searchable record of negotiated provisions, at the census's intersections worker power and AI governing labor (Labor Center; Labor Vector).
  • Not: "a source of model language"; no best practices (Labor Center; Labor Vector).

📄 What it holds

  • Unit: an excerpt or summary of a clause, linked to its full agreement; employer and union names omitted (Labor Center).
  • Sections: definitions; rights to notice, information, negotiation; employer commitments on job security, pay, retraining; governance of monitoring and workforce systems; joint committees; data rights, telework (Labor Center).
  • Method: 500+ agreements from OLMS, OPM and unions, keyword-searched, read, coded on four dimensions (Labor Center; Labor Vector).

📊 What it finds

  • Broad terms: "technology", "technological change" let one clause cover new tools (Labor Center).
  • AI: named in "A few recent contracts"; specific language sits in governance clauses (Labor Center).
  • Old clauses: remain relevant (Labor Center).

🧭 Who supplies, directs, gains

  • Direction: union-represented workers supply it; employers direct it, under negotiated union rights; gains "not recorded as outcomes" (Labor Vector).
  • Labor Vector: meets the directionality stack "at the contract layer"; no measure of user labor drag (Labor Vector).
  • Worked question: negotiated protections against automated monitoring (Labor Vector).

⚖️ Limits

  • Stated: not exhaustive; many agreements name no technology (Labor Center).
  • Cannot settle: enforcement, effect at work, prevalence, bargaining outside the U.S. (Labor Vector).

🏛️ Collective bargaining, read

  • Law: labor's potential "translated into managed rights"; "Collective bargaining" survives (#239).
  • Movement: contract an instrument of the dominant, "well founded" sense (#1439).
  • Union: unions hold "collective bargaining rights"; the Assembly is "a structural position, not an institution" (#256).

🗂️ Who made it

  • Maker: Lisa Kresge, Technology and Work Program; MacArthur, Omidyar funding; updated 2 July 2025 (Labor Center).
  • Access: public; © 2026 Labor Center, no open licence (Labor Center; Labor Vector).
