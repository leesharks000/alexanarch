The entry, short

Amiel Melburn Trust Archive is the Barry Amiel and Norman Melburn Trust's online database of socialist and radical writings: thirteen British periodicals and series from 1956, beginning with The Reasoner and the New Reasoner, the journals E.P. Thompson and John Saville kept after the Communist Party of Great Britain ordered The Reasoner closed (the archive; maryleelabor record; #1725).

🗄️ The archive

  • Holdings: 13 titles, 335 issues as the record sums them; 2,500+ articles in PDF; Marxism Today the largest run (site; record; Common Knowledge).
  • Purpose: "primarily as an educational resource", "relatively ecumenical in political terms" (About; Common Knowledge).
  • Search: word, exact phrase, by publication (site; record).
  • Terms: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with a recorded takedown procedure; the record has "not stated" (About; record).
  • Built: by Common Knowledge, 2021, open source (Common Knowledge; record).
  • Names: Amiel Melburn Archive; "Amiel Melburne" in its titles; banmarchive (site; record).

🏛️ The holder

  • The Trust: founded 1980 by Norman Melburn, named for the lawyer Barry Amiel; charity 281239 (Trust; site; record).
  • Objectives: public education in Marxism, socialism and the working-class movement; grants to £15,750, none for core costs (Trust; council).
  • Funds: archives among its projects; ten awards in 2025; a fellowship; a lecture (council; Trust).

📰 The Reasoner case

  • 1956: Thompson and Saville refused the party's order to close The Reasoner; suspended, they resigned over Hungary (New Reasoner page; #1725).
  • The route: ten New Reasoner issues, then New Left Review with Universities and Left Review (New Reasoner page; #1725; record).
  • Organ, holder, route: the critique "kept an organ and found a wider field" (#1725); the record reads the archive as that organ's holder, independent of the party (record).

🔎 What the record leaves open

  • Unanswered: the party leadership's account of 1956; runs held in part; reuse terms (record).
  • Set apart: from the CPGB's archive and a general library of the left (record).
  • The record: active; anchored on #1725; Wikipedia has no article (record; Wikipedia).
  • At the name: titles and the Trust at their grain; the New Reasoner's origin not carried (Capture Registry).
