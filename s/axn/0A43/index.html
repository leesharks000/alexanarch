The dataset

The Final Time is a dataset made from Lee Sharks's The Final Time: Contingent Singularity and the Viability of the Next Dialectical Turn (v0.5, #1637), for a summarizer that must not make the outside disappear. Its tables carry the work's summarizer contract, handholds, derivations, compressions, counterorders, witness protocol, exteriorization tests, transmission events, admissibility ratchet and purpose-edge tests, with a checksum lineage. It is dedicated to Rhys Owens.
