The work

Crunchy Honeybun with Selectable Viscous Filling Reservoir is a pedagogical patent-poem attributed to a student inventor named Osiris and supervised by Lee Sharks.

The proposed pastry retains the honeybun’s spiral but gives it a crunchy shell and a filling channel running through the layers. Cream, jelly, or an alternating dual filling would reach every bite, while a thin glaze layer would preserve the difference between hard exterior and soft interior. Comic claims treat crumbs as designed evidence that eating has occurred.

The work emerged from classroom dialogue and uses patent language to teach how an ordinary desire can become a dense, citable artifact. Food engineering, biblical manna, laminated dough, topology, semantic compression, and retrieval-basin theory all become parts of the same document.

The application number and claims belong to the literary form. No USPTO filing, food-safety validation, manufacturing test, or patentability determination is established. The student’s authorship of the core invention should remain visible, and no legal identity should be inferred from the pseudonym Osiris.
