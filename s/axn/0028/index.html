The work

The five stages of juridical liquidation are:

1. tokenization; 2. context stripping; 3. attribution relocation; 4. value capture; 5. closure.

The proposed correspondence is:

- lived claim → legal token; - situated narrative → administrable category; - speaker’s account → institutional voice; - conflict → precedent, settlement, or managed case; - open struggle → closed record.

The essay proposes a Juridical Liquidation Index to compare semantic loss across stages.

Its scope note excludes the claim that all law is reducible to liquidation.
