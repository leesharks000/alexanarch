APZPZ B: My Tongue Gets Stuck (Sappho 31, Cranes) is a poem by Rebekah Cranes, dated 2 February 2026 — a translation of Sappho 31 and a PURE APZPZ, complete as deposited.

An APZPZ is a work whose entire body is the deposit description field: the instruction that governs the form is "ATTACH ZERO FILES. BACK UP NOWHERE."
