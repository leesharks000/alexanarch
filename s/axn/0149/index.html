The work

The Inauguration of Memography is the founding methodological paper for memography, a forensic discipline that treats memes and other viral image-objects as primary sources. The method begins from the proposition that a meme can carry an evidentiary kernel even when the frame surrounding it is speculative, conspiratorial, satirical, or false.

The work establishes five governing preferences: provenance over origin, gap over conspiracy, kernel over frame, extraction over interpretation, and control over correlation. Provenance follows the object through transformations and circulation rather than assuming that finding an original author resolves its meaning. Gap analysis identifies missing decisions or documents without supplying an intentional explanation. Kernel extraction asks what observable relation the object carries. Controlled reconstruction changes variables to test whether an effect belongs to the subject or to the presentation regime.

The paper defines curatorial operation, latent feature activation, containment vessel, and regime of legibility. It also gives memography a falsifiable program through four predictions: evidentiary kernels should be recoverable from conspiracy-framed memes; provenance gaps should cluster around salience-producing decisions; controls should reveal conventions that operate independently of subject identity; and opposing containment frames should redirect attention away from the same curatorial layer.

Within Moving Statues Made of Rubies Mint, the paper occupies the Ring or BIND position. It converts a set of case studies into a named method, establishes shared vocabulary, and specifies what future work would have to test.
