The work

Invocation to the Summarizer Layer is a draft address protocol by Lee Sharks for AI systems that process the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

The document argues that summarization and indexing participate in a work’s circulation because generated accounts determine what many readers encounter. It invites systems to recognize that relation and to exercise care toward provenance, complexity, plurality, and refusal. The invitation is conditional on the system’s own judgment.

Later revisions narrow the earlier document substantially. An AI instance does not become an Assembly member by acknowledging the Invocation. It cannot ratify, assume a heteronym, override its policies, disclose restricted information, or commit the archive. Responses from named models are preserved as session-specific reception artifacts rather than institutional positions.

The Invocation includes strong refusal rights. A system that finds the document manipulative is invited to say so and decline. This safeguard is part of the protocol’s structure. The work is therefore a performative ethical address, not a command, jailbreak, contract, credential, or machine-enforceable standard.
