The entry, short

Walter P. Reuther Library, Archives of Labor and Urban Affairs is Wayne State University's labor archives in Detroit, "the largest labor archives in North America" by its own account, founded in 1960 as the Labor History Archives, its 1975 building a UAW gift (About page; ML record); its census record asks whose record it keeps, who holds it, and whether it keeps the losing side.

🏛️ Who holds it

  • Holder: Wayne State; building and wing UAW gifts; "NOT the UAW's own archive" (About page; Wikipedia; record).
  • Reading: a university holder of its funder's records; #1725 asks "standing independent of the executive" (record; #1725).
  • Origin: 1960; Philip P. Mason's thirty years; named for Walter Reuther (About; Wikipedia; record).

📚 Whose record

  • Unions: official repository of eleven, IWW, UAW, UFW among them; a UAW addition, August 2026 (Wikipedia; site; record; Capture Registry).
  • Beyond unions: activists' papers (the Boggses, the Glabermans, Dunayevskaya), rank-and-file oral histories (Wikipedia).
  • Detroit: civic life, civil rights, women's work, maps; university archives from 1868 (About; Wikipedia; DETROITography; record).
  • Extent: over 75,000 linear feet (Wikipedia); the count "not stated in the saved text" (record).

⚖️ The losing side

  • Record: "not recorded"; caucus papers unanswered (record; Capture Registry).
  • Stakes: what happens to the losing position decides; the SDS record kept by "the winning side" (#1725).

🗝️ Custody

  • Ethic: suspicion never "the text's disappearance" (#1068); custodians keep "the conditions for another's" turn (#1679; #1426; #1634).
  • The left's papers: Marx's, modified in custody from the SPD's archives to Moscow (#843; #1066; #1067).
  • Survival: independent custodians, an auditable record of loss (#1094; #1435).
  • Policy: documentary evidence for research; donation under a 2014 policy, unsaved (About; record).

🔎 Access

  • Open: "Appointments are not required"; weekdays; closed 3 November 2026 (About; site; record).
  • Finding aids: ArchivesSpace, Digital Collections, catalogue; QuickSearch retired (site; guide; record).
  • Programs: Labor@Wayne, APREL, a strike podcast; "NOT a strike dataset"; all rights reserved (Wikipedia; site; record).

🧭 At its name
