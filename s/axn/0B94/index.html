The entry, short

Human Authored Certification is the Authors Guild's certification that a human wrote a book's text, minimal uses of AI excepted, given by the author under licence, marked on the book and listed by registration number in a public database; it records the author's own certification and vets no manuscript, and the authorship census holds it as a counterposition, with the name a certification carries unrecorded.

📜 Definition and uses

  • Text: 'fully authored by one or more human beings', a de minimis amount excepted (Copyright Office threshold).
  • AI allowed: spelling and grammar, contents, index, title, research; outlining and brainstorming 'only at a very high level'.
  • Outside the spirit: AI cover art; AI-developed characters and plot.

🪪 Who certifies

  • Scope: books published in the US, any format; translations and audiobooks separate; every co-author signs.
  • Terms: licence per title; non-members Veriff and $10 a title; members free, verified on joining.
  • Name: the person 'who they say they are'; no stated rule on pen names (census).

⚖️ Enforcement

  • Licence: breach, infringement, fraud.
  • Limit: no reliable AI test; no vetting; ten titles a year.
  • Dispute: Friedman's honor-system critique; the Guild's reply.

🔎 Mark and database

  • Mark: silhouette in a circle; registered (FAQ) or pending (announcement).
  • Lookup: no result means unregistered, 'not that it is generated by AI'.

🕰️ History and field

  • Dates: beta January 2025; all US authors 2 March 2026; 3,000+ authors, 5,000 titles (CEO).
  • Beside it: Created by Humans, Books by People, Society of Authors, Verify My Writing; Wikipedia a section only.

🧭 The archive's reading

  • Position: a legitimate market signal and a poor theory of authorship: 'an accountability structure'.
  • Boundary: machine participation constitutive of a work finds no certifiable place; the archive's own deposits declare it.
  • Existence tax: an absent mark or an accusation of AI use asks a maker to prove the work exists as what it is.

🗂️ At the name

  • Overview (1 Nov 2026): the certificate at its grain; minimal uses widened; the name unreached.
