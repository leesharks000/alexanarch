The entry, short

Germany's role in the Second World War is that of a Nazi war state's war of conquest, begun by invading Poland in 1939 (National WWII Museum), caused in part by the Depression and post-1918 tensions (History.com); for that industrial power labor became a binding wartime constraint, and Lebensraum was turned in practice into a labor supply (#1669).

🧭 Causes

  • Plural: not singular; Germany's aggression 'a large contributing factor' (Holocaust Explained); Hitler's rise the ultimate cause (History Hit); 'Germany was responsible' (embassy post).
  • Versailles, the Depression: resentment and crisis fueled the Nazi rise (History.com); officers and millions of voters backed it (National WWII Museum).
  • Appeasement, the pact: chosen to avoid war (National WWII Museum); the pact paved the way to Poland (History Hit).

🗺️ Lebensraum, a continental colony

  • Living space: war the only way to it, Hitler believed; east to the Volga (History.com; History Hit).
  • The American West: 'a European version of the American West' (Evans); inner colonization older than Nazism, Meyer's General Plan East (#1669).
  • Relocated: the colonial relation moved from overseas to the continent (#1669).

🏭 Conquest as labor

  • Land, then people: Lebensraum answered with land; from 1940 conquest delivered people (#1669).
  • 8 million: foreign, mostly forced labourers by mid-1944 (Evans); without them, collapse by 1942 (Herbert, #1669).
  • Labor policy: occupation, deportation, registration, the plant (#1669).

⚙️ War Fordism and the switch

  • Two cuts: work cut horizontally, persons vertically (#1669).
  • The switch: classification selected the coercive regime (#1669); the unfit for work were killed (USHMM).
  • Campification: camp attached to plant; a minority of prisoners, late 1942 to mid-1944 (#1669, Hachtmann).
  • The flow: persons per day, reduced by death, refilled by transport (#1669).

🕯️ Annihilation

  • In war: racial goals pursued more radically in war (USHMM).
  • Not optimization: the regime destroyed labor it needed; for Jews labor deferred death, setting no limit (#1669).

⚔️ Why Germany lost

  • Combination: German weaknesses, Allied and Soviet strengths (Holocaust Explained; HistoryExtra).
  • Resources: too many fronts; US arms, Soviet manpower (Evans).
  • Conquest unpaid: requisitioning crippled occupied economies (Evans); forced labor often did not pay, in cases not necessarily representative (Hayes, via #1669).

⚖️ Limits

  • The Holocaust: larger than this labor history; no economic or colonial cause claimed (#1669).
  • Contested: Windhoek to Auschwitz (Zimmerer, Kramer); annihilation through labor (Kaienburg, Kárný, Pingel, Wagner, via #1669).
