The work

Document 206a — The Non-Indexed Perfective: Version A (Technical Seed) is an Effective Act by Lee Sharks, deposited 16 March 2026.

Its thesis is a linguistic absence with consequences: English lacks a true non-indexed perfective aspect. Ancient Greek possessed the aorist — a verbal form marking completed action without temporal anchoring — while English forces all perfective meaning into temporally indexed constructions, so that a completed action must also be placed in time. The paper works the loss through a historical grammar from PIE forward and proposes the colon-participle as a candidate repair, with five diagnostic metrics.
