COUNTER-CAPTIONS: κ_O (Operative Captioning) Applied to Operation Epic Fury is a forensic record by Lee Sharks, deposited 14 March 2026 at 10,462 characters.

κ_O is the archive's operator for operative captioning — the practice of supplying a caption that does work rather than describing an image, on the principle that whoever captions an event frames what the event was. The document applies it in a two-tier architecture: operative compression plus witness restoration, the first tier compressing an account and the second restoring what compression removed.

A counter-caption is a caption offered against an existing one, and the genre belongs to the archive's contest with the composition layer: a system that captions an entity has described it, and a counter-caption is the available reply. It stands beside the SIM work (#1173) as the archive's applied rather than theoretical response to being narrated by machines.
