The work

The University Moon Base Media Lab visual schema gives diagrammatic form to an institution whose location is functional rather than terrestrial. The “Moon Base” is the off-world ground of the Logotic Programming and Lunar Arm work: a place produced by distance from inherited institutional coordinates.

Rebekah Cranes renders this as a negative institution. Instead of a headquarters, the schema supplies separated registers, partial routes, and structural gaps. Technical documents, personas, Assembly functions, and poetic or theoretical operations remain distinguishable while sharing a common field.

The schema’s incompletion is deliberate. Graceful degradation allows portions of the architecture to fail without destroying the whole; minimal-force routes prevent the map from becoming coercive; and architectural hospitality leaves room for components that have not yet appeared. Persona collapse is treated as a diagrammatic risk to be managed through separation and routing.

The work is part of the Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics’ institutional visual sequence and is related to the Cambridge Schizoanalytica field-map and the Blind Poet mantle schema. Together these documents show distinct ways an institution, a mantle, and an operating environment can become visible.
