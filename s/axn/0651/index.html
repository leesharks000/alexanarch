Eight measurement hooks are specified, including a benchmark composition audit requiring nothing but the benchmarks themselves. Anchored across the archive's model-collapse wing, with the generative-monoculture term cited to Wu, Black & Chandrasekaran per ERRATUM to AXN:0341 (#1554).
