Analysis
The composition's figures are estimates of its own making (per-asset archival rates of $50–$150; a comparison to Electronic Arts Intermix and Rhizome) and none is a recorded sale or budget; the holdings counts are not checked here. Its headline range, $0 to $15 million–$45 million+, is the institutional replacement row; the public-goods row above it ($50M–$100M+) is outside the headline. UbuWeb is the nearest control to the archive in kind: one founder (it names Kenneth Goldsmith), no grants, no revenue, a refusal of the market stated as principle, static pages. The composition reads each of these as the asset. 'UbuWeb's primary asset is its lack of legal and financial overhead'; the refusal of money is 'radical gift-economy principles'; the single founder is named and no discount is taken for it.
