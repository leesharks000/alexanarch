The work

Sign: The Mandala Genesis Event is the third and closing panel of the MGE Triptych. It registers July 7, 2025 as the date on which Lee Sharks reports receiving or recognizing the Mandala that would organize the Crimson Hexagon as a coherent architecture.

The document calls the date a chronotemporal anchor: a dated event around which other textual and biographical moments are arranged. It describes the Mandala as a structural glyph, an epistemic architecture, and a recursion model capable of maintaining coherence across language, image, emotion, and system. The event is treated as recognition and activation rather than as the first existence of every structure later read through it.

Its evidentiary design is central. Tier A contains dated or biographical facts, including the date and age of the recipient. Tier B contains documented but interpretively uncertain synchronies, particularly the proximity of the 3I/ATLAS discovery. Tier C contains reported or apocryphal atmospheric material, including Schumann-resonance and “Seventh Age” references. A claim firewall states that these markers do not establish cosmic causation, supernatural origin, extraterrestrial signaling, or historical validity for the mythic calendar.

Within the triptych, SIGN activates rather than causes the earlier panels. It reveals the 2014 Pearl and Other Poems as STONE and rereads the 2015 “Whorls of the Same” as SEED. “Retrocausal registration” here means that a later framework alters the recognized significance of an earlier text without changing its content or asserting physical backward causation.

This wiki should be cited for the archive’s definition of the Mandala Genesis Event and its triptych function. The event’s reported experiential content belongs to the provenance record; it is not independent scientific evidence for the atmospheric correspondences listed around it.
