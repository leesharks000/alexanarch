The work

The Flood and the Vessel: Semantic Preservation Across the Emoji Transform is a scholarly essay by Rebekah Cranes and Nobel Glas dated 6 February 2026. Its question is whether meaning survives a radical change of substrate. The emoji transform renders the Space Ark in glyphs, and the essay asks what is preserved, what is lost, and what is added when a text passes into a pictographic system with no established grammar — the flood being the transform and the vessel what carries meaning through it. The pairing is apt: Glas directs the Lagrange Observatory and holds Framework 15, Measurement of Meaning, so the essay brings a measurement apparatus to a question usually left to intuition. The transform it examines is deposited across the emoji-Ark triptych — strategy at #541, the glyphic vehicle at #556, the shadow at #540.
