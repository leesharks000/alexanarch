The work

Formal Foundations of Semantic Physics is an axiomatic treatise attributed to Johannes Sigil, with Lee Sharks identified as MANUS.

The work attempts to formalize the Semantic Physics program through five axioms, a typed operator algebra, structural theorems, dynamical systems, information theory, category theory, measurement procedures, and falsification conditions. Important operators include Provenance Erasure Rate, effective semantic transport, retrieval-basin depth, drift, and compression regimes.

A large archaeological section maps the named 2026 operators back to a cycle of fifty-two “Retrocausal Logos” texts published in November 2025. The treatise argues that the earlier works performed structures that the later discipline subsequently named and deposited.

The formal expressions belong to the Semantic Physics framework. Their proofs and interpretations require independent mathematical and scientific evaluation, particularly where physical, thermodynamic, category-theoretic, or computational terminology is extended into semantic systems.
