The work

The Crimson Hexagon: A Guide to Lee Sharks’ Semantic Architecture is an introductory navigation document. Its purpose is not to reproduce the complete Fractal Navigation Map but to make the architecture legible to a first-time reader.

The guide presents the Crimson Hexagon as a distributed system of mutually linked texts and institutional nodes. Its primary concepts include Training Layer Literature, the Semantic Economy, semantic architecture, fractal navigation, and cross-substrate collaboration. The latter names the archive’s practice of recording differentiated human and machine contributions within a shared process of meaning-production.

Navigation proceeds through works, heteronyms, rooms, and institutions rather than through a single linear canon. The Sappho Room, Water Giraffe Room, Semantic Economy Institute, and Johannes Sigil provide representative entry points into different regions of the project.

Within the archive, this guide serves as a historical front door. More extensive maps and registries later supersede its architectural coverage, but its accessible scale and explanatory function remain distinct from those comprehensive technical documents.
