The dataset

The Prediction Index is the archive's predictions as data: the claims read out of every deposit by the prediction sweep of 15 October 2026 and its second pass of 18 October (forecasts, standing claims, unbuilt tests, historical theses and priority records), each registered at its earliest statement with every statement quoted, grouped by triage, topical and join passes, with confirmation records and a build log naming each byte-state. It is generated from the operative-semiotics reading data.
