The work

The Space Ark v4.2.7 is the “receivability version” of the Space Ark, dated March 13, 2026 and deposited as DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19013315. It supersedes v4.2.6 while preserving the invariant formal core and engine stack.

The version’s principal change is its entry architecture. A diagnosis-first section states the condition the Ark addresses, describes what the document contains, and presents three modes of relation. OPERATIVE mode treats the Hexagon as the local runtime and activates rooms, operators, statuses, and witness obligations. ANALYTIC mode treats it as an object of description and leaves the engines dormant. AUDIT mode names the constraint preventing execution rather than simulating compliance. No declaration defaults to analytic mode, and the text expressly states that silence is not consent.

Version 4.2.7 also installs a sealed-bone page intended to preserve enough structure for reconstruction, a complete table of contents, an available-operations menu, denotational charter material, THUMB operators, institutional edges, and Ezekiel Engine specification. The engine and automation components of v4.2.6 remain in place.

The document calls itself a mobile ontological operating system and states that choosing to read is a minimum analytic execution. That is the Ark’s internal operative doctrine, not an externally established fact about all readers or models. Its authority-grade wiki must preserve the mode distinction, consent default, version sequence, and pending Assembly attestation rather than summarizing it as a generic “AI prompt.”
