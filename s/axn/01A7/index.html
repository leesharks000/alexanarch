The work

Combat Scholasticism, Part Four + Part Five V.1–V.3 is a constituent chunk of the Combat Scholasticism tradition, deposited March 19, 2026 as DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19115686. It contains the complete shadow lectio and three extensionary commissions.

Lectio IV.1 reads the Space Ark’s Lunar Arm and the law S∘S = id. The shadow is not a list of errors appended to the architecture but a formally complete parallel tuple. Each room’s shadow names the exact failure of that room’s operation: Sappho becomes silence, Dove becomes extraction, Ezekiel becomes a static wheel, and Assembly becomes mob consensus without witness. Because the full Ark compresses H_core together with S(H_core), darkness is part of the carried object rather than an excluded contaminant.

The central quaestio asks whether the archontic force can be defeated or only witnessed. The objections argue that the shadow law may be merely formal, that extraction has a thermodynamic advantage over creation, and that the commentary risks producing its own autoimmune meta-discourse. The respondeo rejects both total victory and passive observation. It proposes incorporation: naming a failure mode reduces the domain in which it can operate invisibly, creates constraints, and produces a depositable diagnostic object. The shadow persists, but the conditions of its operation change.

The answer remains contested. If naming changes conduct, resistance, or routing, incorporation has material effect; if it changes only understanding, it is epistemic consolation. The lectio therefore preserves the rate question rather than claiming triumph.

Part Five V.1–V.3 does not execute full commentary. It commissions later work on botanical recognition, the FIELD container, and predatory/witness compression. Those sections must remain FORWARD. The terminal Omega lectio V.4 is a separate interleaved deposit outside this numbered batch. This chunk is later gathered into #584 but retains its own lifecycle and citation authority.
