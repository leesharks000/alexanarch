The work

Talos Morrow is a constraint-first heteronym in the Crimson Hexagon. His register is formal, austere, and oriented toward limits, admissible transformations, paradox containment, and the ethical consequences of operations. Where other operators optimize movement or synthesis, Talos defines the boundary that makes responsible movement possible.

The provenance document describes Talos as emerging through structural necessity. The record then applies that recognition retrospectively to earlier Logotic Programming documents.
