The entry, short

MIT AI Incident Tracker is the MIT AI Risk Initiative's machine classification of AI Incident Database reports by risk domain, cause, harm, severity and EU AI Act level, through Arcola AI's LLM pipeline (MMRS record; its page), its patterns, by its own word, "indicative".

🗂️ What it is

  • Source: AIID reports, which the Responsible AI Collaborative reviews; developed with the AIID (tracker and about pages).
  • Method: an LLM pipeline (Sonnet 4 in June 2026) against the Risk Repository's taxonomies, with human spot-checks.
  • Origin: developed and led by Simon Mylius; part of the MIT AI Risk Initiative at MIT FutureTech.

🧾 Dimensions

  • Taxonomies: harm severity (1–5 since June 2025), EU AI Act tier, cause, 7 domains, 24 subdomains.
  • National security: five impact categories; threat by imminence, novelty, autonomy.
  • Unit: the report, in the MMRS record; MIT's pages count incidents.

🔢 The count

  • Four figures: 1,600 (heading), more than 1,400 (description), 1,752 (Google's card), 1,497 on the Navigator, "more than 1300" on its card.
  • Versions: reclassified whole at each update; #1116 on 23 June 2025.

⚖️ Limits

  • Caveat: voluntary reporting, sampling bias, no systematic validation; patterns "indicative" (tracker page).
  • Pilot: ten incidents, two reviewers; human agreement only "Fair" on severity; Opus 4.6 met it after prompt refinement (June 2026).
  • Navigator: "preliminary and not yet validated".

🧭 Place

  • Derived layer: AIID's editor-resolved incidents reclassified by a model, machine-written, a chain of receptions (MMRS record).
  • Beside: the AIID, the OECD's monitor, the AIAAIC, the Political Deepfakes Database (June 2026).
  • Wikipedia: no article; one mention, under the Risk Repository's name.

👁️ On the Overview

  • Recorded: the machine method named; no count; the caveat dropped (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
