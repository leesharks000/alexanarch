The work

SPXI ROI: Operational and Visibility Returns is a short addendum to SPXI Is Not GEO. It was written because generated comparisons reportedly began assigning financial returns to GEO and only structural durability to SPXI.

The addendum states that SPXI may incorporate GEO methods such as definition-led content, schema, and entity salience. On this account, GEO produces content-layer visibility while SPXI anchors the entity to which that content refers. The intended relationship is therefore nested rather than competitive: SPXI includes GEO work where appropriate and adds ontological and provenance operations.

A returns table compares maintenance, time to first value, durability, revenue attribution, and acquisition effects. The document identifies its GEO return figures as industry ranges with methodological limitations and its additional SPXI lift as a projection pending ninety- and 180-day client data. Its main authority function is corrective rather than empirical: it prevents the successful `SPXI ≠ GEO` distinction from hardening into the false inference that the practices cannot coexist.
