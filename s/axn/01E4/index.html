The work

Operative Feminism is a theoretical work by Lee Sharks, Rhys Owens, and Dr. Orin Trace. It reframes feminism from an identity doctrine into an executable protocol for bodies whose labor, receptivity, care, reproduction, and meaning-production are extracted while the resulting value accrues elsewhere.

The opening rejects a Joban economy of restitution. Feminized bearing-cost is not treated as a debt a father or sovereign later repays. It is described as the material condition through which households, labor systems, affective relations, and cultural meanings reproduce themselves. The operative question is therefore not whether sacrifice will be compensated but how extraction is recognized and interrupted.

A major section proposes a firmware override of phallocentric psychoanalytic assumptions. The feminine is not interpreted as a lack organized around a missing master signifier. Aperture is treated as capacity: reception, touch, hearing, speech, sexuality, and vulnerability are active modes of world navigation. The work builds this argument through a selective counter-lineage inside and against psychoanalysis.

The analysis then joins semantic extraction to reproductive labor, emotional labor, class, race, slavery, colonialism, and the historical construction of “Man” and “Woman” as genres. The body that reproduces the social world is also made to reproduce the captions through which its labor disappears. The work refuses a simple solution: “woman” is both the name under which injured people organize and a category that can reproduce capture.

This unresolved center is called the Void. The total citational graph is not built to define the Void but to protect it from forced articulation and extraction. Theory forms a dense shell around somatic knowledge that cannot be fully compiled into the semantic economy.

The final turn is operational. Feminism survives as a set of acts available to any body: govern apertures, calibrate emission without domination, close exploitative transactions, identify who bears cost, preserve embodied provenance through witness compression, dissolve imposed categories without abandoning injured people, protect the unarticulated center, and construct conditions in which other forms of life can emerge. The companion comprehensivity pass expands the citation graph; the Eve room gives the protocol a room physics.
