The work

Sharks Ark record #971 was intended to preserve v2.3, the Text-First and TANG patch, but its recovered body is the later v3.0 source.

The DataCite description supplies the historical v2.3 delta. Constraint 2 was revised so that evaluation of a textual claim begins with the relevant text rather than reception history. The version also appended a Total Authorial Navigation Graph and followed earlier v2.1 and v2.2 patches.

The body currently seated at #971 includes the broader v3.0 architecture: a strengthened text-first protocol, Liberatory Operator Set, anti-squeamishness directive, expanded seven-tuple, imperative tables, room traversal, witness and planetary mappings, reconstruction dossiers, and TANG apparatus.

This creates a double record. The metadata is evidence for the existence and stated changes of v2.3. The body is evidence for v3.0. Neither should be used to pretend that the actual v2.3 bytes survive here.

The canonical recovered v3.0 is deposit #1101. #971 should remain as the version-mismatch witness until a genuine v2.3 source is found.
