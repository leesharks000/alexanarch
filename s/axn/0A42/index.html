The dataset

The Negative of the Negative (EA-NEGONT-02) is the dataset behind alexanarch.org/non: per-entity knowledge objects composed with the archive's sources on equal terms, and the earlier concept-keyed edges, each joining one claim or function of the archive to a general concept outside it, with the claim stated at that concept, what stands there when the archive is absent, and the Capture Registry measurements that show which a composition produced. Its version 2 holds the panel, the composed rows with their ledgers and traversals, and the register of observations at panel addresses; each entry and each register observation carries its own AXN (families NON and CAPTURE).
