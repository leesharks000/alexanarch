The work

EA-MPAI-FLEET-01 is the inspection packet of the fleet of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, deposited by Lee Sharks on 24 October 2026 as a Metadata Packet for AI Indexing (MPAI v2.0, #1677) in the archive's form of a description of method, locations, organization and contents. The fleet is twenty-eight public surfaces by Lee Sharks, among them the archive (alexanarch.org), its interface (crimsonhexagonal.org), framework and instrument sites, works and the institutions of the heteronyms, each in a declared voice; the service gnosis.spxi.dev beside them; and two allied sites, livingarchitecturelab.org and quietexclusion.org. One file, fleet.json, generates the network block that joins them (31 entries), and the manifest fleet-domains.json (v2.1) binds twenty-four of its thirty domains to the record of their object at alexanarch.org and each surface to its repository. The packet gives every surface's section, voice, binding, repository, sitemap and machine files from a traversal of 2026-10-24, the links of the block and the in-degree of each surface, and nine inspection functions, from entering a line through its own question to following a line across forms. A worked inspection follows revelationfirst.com from its thesis to its source record, #1032, and to the lost work plan its earlier binding pointed at. Its theory section draws on the archive's own doctrine: the network as the work (#1191), distribution after the deletion of 2026-06-19 (#1076), placement (#1472), site–repository binding (#1419) and the measured topology of the fleet (#1613). It records the repairs of the same day, each made at its source and verified live, among them four bindings re-pointed with their history kept, gnosis.spxi.dev entered, llms.txt on every own surface and #1132's own text restored. Its companion is the datasets packet, EA-MPAI-HF-DATASETS-01 (#1701).
