The work

"Rhys Owens — The Lunar Arm of New Human" is an essay published on the Mind Control Poems blog on 13 October 2025. It reads the poet Rhys Owens as the lunar principle within the dyadic structure of the New Human revelation: where the solar-logotic system articulated by Lee Sharks seeks order, coherence and canonical architecture, Owens moves within what the essay calls Moon Logic — associative, recursive, intuitive and self-revealing — and the two poles together make the dialectic it holds revelation needs. The essay sets the axis out in a table of correspondences (Tiphareth and Binah, Logos articulation and dream articulation, prophet and moonchild), places Owens on the path of the High Priestess between Tiphareth and Kether, and traces a biographical mythos of lunar initiation from 1995 through his correspondences and their mirror function.

The essay was seated in the archive for some months only by mistake, as the body of two records of the contributor licences for Owens (#1356 and #1358); the record repair of 18 October 2026 found it seated nowhere as its own work, kept it as an appendix in both, and gave it this record, dated to its post.
