The entry, short

PennSound is an ongoing project of the University of Pennsylvania's Center for Programs in Contemporary Writing, producing new audio recordings of poets and preserving existing audio archives, free to stream and download; the archive reads it as a witness that credits author, performer and co-composer as separate attributions.

🎙️ What it holds

  • Filing: author, series, anthology and singles pages; readings cut into singles, complete readings kept.
  • Credits: Clare performed by Bernstein; Weiner with Mattlin and Kraut; Khlebnikov translated and performed by Bernstein; music by Christopher Reiner, "included with permission".
  • Scope: Apollinaire's 1913 recording to contemporary poets; Pound, Stevens, Stein, Hejinian; classics to Shakespeare.
  • Size: over 1500 recordings and well over 50,000 sound files (Wikipedia); "more than 50,000 recordings" (Instagram).

👥 Who runs it

  • Directors: Charles Bernstein, Al Filreis, Chris Mustazza; editor Michael S. Hennessey; two other descriptions list two directors.
  • Founded: 2005, by Filreis and Bernstein; the "iTunes for poetry".

📜 Rules and terms

  • Manifesto: free, singles, every file named, retrievable "under the poet’s name".
  • Terms: noncommercial and educational use only; writers and artists keep all copyrights; rights per recording unstated in the census's sources.
  • Limits: documentation added over time; Ruas collection under construction.

📻 Programs

  • Collections: Charles Ruas's radio programming (March 2026); Eric Mottram Centennial Collection.
  • Podcasts: produced by Filreis at the Kelly Writers House; PennSound Rewind; PoemTalk with the Poetry Foundation.
  • Active: listings to July 2026; publicly funded.

🔗 Around it

  • Neighbours: Kelly Writers House, UbuWeb, the Electronic Poetry Center, Jacket2.
  • Elsewhere: poets.org, ModPo, X, YouTube.

📚 The archive's reading

  • Question: whose voice a recording carries and under whose name it is filed.
  • Heteronymy: the civil name as attribution's unit; performance, translation and collaboration outside heteronymy; collapse as loss (#940).
  • Unanswered: rights; full bibliography; an author with several names.

🔍 At the name

  • Overview: host and directors exact; Ruas carried; credits apart and 2005 absent (Capture Registry).
