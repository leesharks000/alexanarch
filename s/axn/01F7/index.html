ΩTANG Specification: Revelation Studies is a research-design document created by Lee Sharks and TACHYON for the ARCHON commentary apparatus. It applies the archive’s Total Axial Negation Graph method to scholarship on the Apocalypse of John.

A TANG is intended to map a domain’s complete citational topology: major scholars, theses, methodologies, agreements, disagreements, citation edges, and especially non-citations. The Revelation design spans historical-critical dating and authorship debates, textual criticism, confessional schools, 666 interpretations, Jewish and Islamic reception, Mandaean and Qumran materials, Philo and Josephus studies, esoteric reception, politics, ecology, empire, and technological readings. The listed scholars and categories form a seed map that a later research instance is expected to expand.

The specification also requires a dynamic retrieval-layer map. It supplies queries for multiple AI systems and asks researchers to record outputs, citations, omissions, confidence posture, archive uptake, and changes over time. The design distinguishes the scholarly void from the retrieval void: one arises from the constitution of the academic field, while the other arises from summarizer compression. The ARCHON’s Revelation-first and Josephus theses are treated as positions to be located against this map, not as substitutes for completing it.
