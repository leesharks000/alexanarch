The work

Version C is the most developed form of The Non-Indexed Perfective: A Citational Intervention. It begins from a linguistic distinction between tense and aspect and argues that ordinary English lacks a dedicated, productive morphology equivalent to the temporally neutral perfective uses available in some languages.

The document then gives that gap a political reading. It connects indexed completion to legal records, wage time, colonial administration, historical sequence, platform recency, and algorithmic ranking. These sections are a synthetic theoretical argument; they should not be summarized as a demonstrated single-cause history in which capitalism directly removed an English aorist.

The constructive sections propose ways to make completion-without-index more available in English, including punctuation, participial, symbolic, and polyphonic markers. The goal is not to claim that a new grammatical aspect has already become conventional English, but to install a usable literary and logotic notation for the archive.

Version standing is essential. #434 is the Technical Seed, #433 is the Compression Artifact, and #432 is the final Citational Intervention. The packet at #422 preserves the three together and explains why their differences are part of the work’s evidence.
