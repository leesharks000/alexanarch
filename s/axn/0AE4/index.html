The entry, short

VroniPlag Wiki is a volunteer wiki, started 28 March 2011 at Wikia (now Fandom), that documents plagiarism in German doctoral theses as fragments, each classified and checked by a second person, attaching provenance to a thesis passage and the source it reproduces without attribution (English Wikipedia; provenance census record).

🔍 What it documents

  • Relations: unattributed reuse and the source text; the unit a thesis, as fragments (census record).
  • Fragments: compared with their originals, classified by kind, verified by a second person, shown as a bar code (its 2011 report; English Wikipedia).
  • Measure: the share of a thesis's pages with plagiarism, as a percentage.

📜 Origin

  • Name: after Veronica Saß, the first thesis documented.
  • GuttenPlag: it grew from that wiki and is not it.
  • Founder: German Wikipedia's infobox names Martin Heidingsfelder; the project rejects his claim to sole founding.

⚖️ Policy and consequences

  • Publication: only serious cases, after notifying the university, which alone revokes.
  • Independence: "ohne politische oder kommerzielle Interessen" (its overview).
  • Revocations: at least 90 doctorates and two habilitations (30 April 2024); most upheld in court.
  • Disagreement: some universities rejected findings; six cases without revocation (2019), von der Leyen's among them.

📊 Counts, at their dates

  • 2016: 152 dissertations, 8 habilitations.
  • 2023: 201 dissertations, 13 habilitation theses.
  • Its overview: "Es dokumentiert 204 Disse…", a later count, truncated on the card.
  • Wiki: 41014 articles, registered and unregistered authors.

🗣️ Reception and criticism

  • Prize: Zedler Prize for Free Knowledge, 1 July 2012.
  • Reception: an authoritative source in German media; a national debate.
  • Criticism: anonymity ("Es komme nur auf das Gesagte an sich an"), alleged politics, its citation rules.
