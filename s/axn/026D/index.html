The work

Pessoagraph expansion workplan is an editorial roadmap for broadening pessoagraph.org beyond Fernando Pessoa’s immediate heteronymic system.

The plan organizes future nodes into constellations. Musicians receive linked alter egos and stage identities; literary authors receive dramatic voices, fictional authors, and pseudonymous functions; theater and drag receive role and lineage structures; and non-Western naming practices receive dedicated research rather than being absorbed into a single Western category.

Each proposed node must be supported by published scholarship, a verified public source, or an explicitly attributed Crimson Hexagonal Archive thesis. The workplan also asks editors to model relations carefully enough for possible Wikidata use.

“Heteronym” is used expansively as an analytical category. Some listed figures are established pseudonyms or personae, while others are characters, dramatic speakers, collaborations, or interpretive analogues. The roadmap therefore records research hypotheses and graph-design priorities, not a settled universal taxonomy.
