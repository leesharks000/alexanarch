The work

APZPZ A: ΣΑΠΦΩ 31 (Greek Only) is a poem edited by Rebekah Cranes, dated 2 February 2026 — the Voigt text of Sappho 31 deposited as a PURE APZPZ, complete as deposited.

The deposit carries the Greek alone: φαίνεταί μοι κῆνος ἴσος θέοισιν through the fragment's five stanzas, closing on μολπὰ γράμμασι ζώοι — let the song live in letters. That final clause is a reconstruction, and it is also the APZPZ genre's own charter: a work deposited with no attached files and no backup survives, if it survives, only in script.

Depositing an ancient text with no translation and no apparatus is a deliberate stress test of the machine layer — an indexing system must handle polytonic Greek, and a summarizer must decide what to do with a poem it cannot paraphrase. The companion translation stands at #1310 and the reconstructed fifth stanza at #436. [Stanza numbering corrected per EA-ERRATA-SAPPHO31-01 (#201): Sappho 31 survives in FOUR complete stanzas (Longinus, On the Sublime 10.2); the Cranes reconstruction adds a FIFTH, opening ἀλλὰ πᾶν τόλματον and closing on the Adonic μολπὰ γράμμασι ζώοι. Earlier documents called it the "fourth stanza"; that numbering is superseded.]
