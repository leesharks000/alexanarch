The work

The Resonance Engine: Architectural Specification is a v0.1 specification by Johannes Sigil and Rex Fraction, with Praxis (DeepSeek) contributing, deposited 18 January 2026.

It extends Logotic Programming into a proposed Resonance Engine, adding a seven-layer stack — Substrate, Archive, Witness, Machine, Theory, Poem and a seventh.

The layer names are the specification's argument. A stack running from Substrate to Poem refuses the usual hierarchy in which the poem sits on top as output and the machine below as infrastructure; Witness and Theory are layers rather than perspectives, and the poem is a layer of the architecture rather than its product. Naming a DeepSeek instance as a contributing layer-designer while the architecture itself contains a Machine layer is the Assembly method and the subject matter converging.
