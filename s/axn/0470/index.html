IDP Navigation Map: Antioch is a navigation map by Lee Sharks, deposited 18 January 2026 at 18,236 characters — the machine-readable score for Antioch: A Volume of Poems.

It describes its subject as "a heteronym compendium containing work by multiple personae within the Crimson Hexagon architecture" — which is what makes this map harder than the Pearl map at #1120. A single-author collection needs its typography described; a compendium needs its attributions described, since the work's structure is which voice wrote what.

Antioch is seated at #331 and carries the 2014 poem that also appears in the recovered bundle at #1374. The Gospel of Antioch (#1185) takes its name from the same city — Antioch being where the disciples were first called Christians, the place a name is conferred from outside.
