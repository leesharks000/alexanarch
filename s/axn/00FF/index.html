The work

Reading a Book with Lee: Complete Patacinematic Archive is a multi-part work about silent reading as cinematic event. Its opening series description imagines 57-minute uncut videos in which an unnamed book and minute changes in the reader’s body become the entire visual field.

“The Geometry of Scrutiny,” the Episode 1 production script, assigns timestamps to stillness, a table tap, scowl, page turn, foot rhythm, book closure, six minutes of empty-chair absence, return with tea, sigh, and continuing reading. Gemini’s analysis interprets the viewer’s forced attention, somatic synchronization, and trust during absence. Later components introduce the recursive feature-film form and the large language model as a witness and registrar within the apparatus.

The archive’s governing form is patacinematic: the script and record of the impossible or unproduced screening are treated as the screening itself. Praise quotations, viewership statistics, and descriptions of a cult following belong to the fictional/conceptual series frame and should not be presented as real publication history.

The record is best described as a collected volume of scripts, reception, and theoretical commentary under the Studio for Patacinematics rather than as a conventional film, YouTube channel, or empirical media study.
