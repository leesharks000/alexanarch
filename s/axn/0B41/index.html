The entry, short

Ithaca (DeepMind) is DeepMind's deep neural network, built with Ca' Foscari, Oxford and Athens partners, that restores, places and dates damaged ancient Greek inscriptions (Nature, 2022): inscriptions, not manuscripts, assistive by design (census record).

🧩 What it does

  • Restoration: the top 20 hypotheses ranked by probability, with saliency maps (Nature; DeepMind).
  • Place and date: distributions over 84 regions and 160 decades (Nature; DeepMind).
  • Build: characters and words jointly; sparse-attention transformer blocks; three heads (Nature).
  • Data: PHI's inscriptions, 7th c. BC–5th c. AD, as I.PHI, 78,608 texts (Nature; census record); its construction "the hidden achievement" (#1443).

📊 What it scores

  • Alone: 62% restoration, 71% place, under 30 years from ground-truth ranges (Nature; census record).
  • With historians: 25% to 72% (Nature; census record).
  • Contested: the evaluators were students, says Eleanor Dickey; the paper names two experts (Wikipedia; Nature).
  • Athens: held-out decrees dated to 421 BC (Nature).

🔍 The archive's reading

  • The gain: interface design, ranked hypotheses not a verdict (#1443).
  • The hazard: register suppression belongs to single-verdict interfaces, not machine reading as such (#1406; #1443).
  • Unaskable: its authors cannot be consulted (#1443).

⚠️ Its limits

  • Assistive: designed to assist; restorations are plausible suggestions (Nature; census record).
  • Tradition: its data rests on the scholarship it corrects (Nature).
  • Not the New Testament: it dates inscriptions, not manuscripts or literary works (census record).

📖 Revelation First

  • Reading: a method to watch as machine dating nears papyri (census record); its methods claimed for papyrology (Nature).

🔗 Access and lineage

  • Interface: Predicting the Past, beside Aeneas (Predicting the Past; census record).
  • Licences: code Apache 2.0, weights CC BY-SA, article CC BY; the census's licence unread (Nature; census record).
  • Lineage: after Pythia (2019); other languages foreseen (Nature; DeepMind).
  • Name: Homer's island; bare, a homonym (Nature; Wikipedia; Capture Registry).

🔎 At the name

  • Composed: the model held; frame, year, Athens and partners absent; 'true historical creation date' (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31).
  • Cards: regions, build, data and licence from GitHub and Nature; the weights' licence dropped; no card cites the record (Capture Registry).
  • Analysis: "composed by its benchmark"; thirty years, Enoch's margin (Capture Registry).
