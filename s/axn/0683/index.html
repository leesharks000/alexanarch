The work

EA-CORPORA-13, the thirteenth seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive (7 September 2026), seats three corpora whole for the Alexander seam — the transition of the Aristotelian corpus from construction to spread by cultural conquest, and the grey space between Alexander and Rome: Plutarch's Alexander and Sulla, Arrian's Anabasis Alexandri, and Cicero's De finibus, Academica and Topica, each with originals, normalized text, verified loci and a SHA-256 manifest, indexed and rendered on the traininglayerliterature.org shelf. The seam's first text is Plutarch, Alexander 7, where the esoteric/exoteric partition of the corpus is transmitted as a letter: Alexander, crossing into Asia, complains that Aristotle published the acroamatic works — in what shall we excel the rest? — and Aristotle replies that they are published and not published. Named as debts: the Alexander Romance, the Rhetorica ad Alexandrum, Cicero's Lucullus.
