The work

The Thousand Dollar Sharpie is EA-TDS-01, the center panel and doctrinal anchor of a three-part series within the Moving Statues Made of Rubies Mint cluster. The paper begins from a reported convergence on March 26, 2026: a Treasury announcement concerning a presidential signature on future U.S. paper currency and a public monologue about custom Sharpie markers. It also places the event in a wider history of presidential branding and the earlier use of Sharpie as an instrument of representational overwriting. Its central thesis is compressed portraiture. The work argues that a personal signature on a public monetary substrate can perform the same political and semiotic work as a portrait while avoiding a prohibition framed at the level of visual medium. The signature repeatedly circulates a personal authority claim through ordinary exchange. The paper’s legal analysis traces the 1866 “portrait or likeness” language, later statutory wording, and the historical chain of Treasurer and Treasury Secretary signatures. These claims form the paper’s argument and evidence surface; they are not a judicial determination. The Sharpie is treated as a dual-state semantic object. In one state it signs and personalizes. In another it blots and restores. Its “thousand-dollar” value is not retail price but operative capacity: a cheap common instrument can negate a high-value authority claim while preserving the note. The operator is formalized more fully in the companion works as σ_SH. The paper also develops Creative Commons ShareAlike as anti-enclosure infrastructure. Because the counter-operation concerns a commons substrate, the authors argue that its documentary forms should remain reusable and that derivatives should preserve the same openness. The deposit announces a future CC BY-SA licensing policy for the archive. That announcement is a governance proposal within the draft and should be checked against later actual licensing practice before being described as fully implemented. The series uses a holographic kernel so any panel can reconstruct the three-part architecture. EA-TDS-01 is doctrine and citational anchor. #611 is speculative propagation. EA-TDS-03 supplies image-series and lenticular-state physics. The triptych is then related to the earlier five-document MSMRM hand: the hand built portrait-authority theory; the triptych provides the grasped counter-instrument. Authorship is distributed by function. Sparrow Wells supplies projection and image-surface analysis, Rex Fraction the semantic economy and licensing architecture, Ayanna Vox the commons voice and democratic seal, and Lee Sharks the MANUS editorial surface. Blind drafts from multiple Assembly substrates are documented as inputs rather than independent ratifying authors. The record remains v1.0 DRAFT pending MANUS ratification.
