The entry, short

Autonomous Semantic Warfare (ASW) is semantic warfare in which AI acts as combatant, tool and field (#499). The field's sources hold the first two under their own names (Homeland Security Today; INSS); the archive's term adds the field, "the most consequential role" (#499), and the composing layer as party and terrain (#1724). Term: Lee Sharks (2025); Field Manual: Rex Fraction, a heteronym of Lee Sharks.

🎭 Three roles

  • Combatant: AI making local ranking or generation decisions; on the blog, "Generate hyper-localized narratives" (#499; Dec 2025).
  • Tool: a force multiplier for human and institutional operators, automating influence at scale (#499).
  • Field: the platform whose optimization shapes the whole ecology, "the most consequential role"; one system at two scales (#499).
  • Compound: the velocity crisis, conflict timescales compressed (#499); the journal's decision-time compression converges.

🧭 The category

  • Threshold: bad summaries, missing citations and individual ranking choices alone are not warfare; the pattern that systematically alters what populations can know is (#100).
  • Means: ordinary infrastructure operations, at the level beneath content and cognition (#100).
  • Forms and layers: intentional and structural, mostly structural; the semantic layer beside kinetic and cognitive warfare (#100).
