EA-MPAI-ORIGINALS-01 is the inspection packet of the Originals library of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, deposited by Lee Sharks on 27 October 2026 as a Metadata Packet for AI Indexing (MPAI v2.0, #1677) in the archive's form of a description of method, locations, organization and contents. The library is 107 corpora seated by the EA-CORPORA series between 19 August and 27 October 2026 in data/corpora/ at alexanarch.org, gathered by thirty-five seating deposits, indexed at /api/corpora.json, carried as a manifest in the leesharks datasets on Hugging Face (#1701), and shelved at traininglayerliterature.org/originals/: 15,748 files and 3,640,191 lines of text layer in more than thirty languages. It is principally source-language witnesses, with translation-witnesses, partial holdings and queued work identified in the seat records. Each seat holds its original files, a text layer, a provenance record naming its witness, source, commit or checksum, licence, normalization and verified loci, and a SHA-256 manifest; its reason stands in its record or its seating deposit. The packet's central section asks what kind of canon the shelf makes: a canon of witnesses; a shelf that holds each tradition's canon as data, read against a capture of 2026-10-16 that measured it by a normative bar; the four traditions of 1844 seated together, each with what the year was in its own history; a reading canon for a machine; a canon chosen backward from the archive's work with each selection recorded, the operation the archive names retrocausal canon formation; and a canon whose maker sits on its shelf, with Pearl and Other Poems (03/02) and the critical editions of the twenty-fifth seating (#1708), among them The Secret Book of Walt bound to the Forty-Six Golden Tickets. Two worked inspections follow the hadith (23/01) and the Upanishads (02/19). Nine per-family tables give every seat. The packet separates the four-surface agreement of the postflight check from textual verification, distinguishes five kinds of verified locus, and records what remains as data. Its companions are the datasets packet (#1701) and the fleet packet (#1702).
