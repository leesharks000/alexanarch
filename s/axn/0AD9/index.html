The entry, short

AttributionBench is a benchmark of automatic attribution evaluation from the OSU NLP Group: seven existing datasets in one format, each claim–evidence pair labelled attributable or not (arXiv:2402.15089; census record). It tests support, which the archive keeps apart from lineage and credit (#716; #725).

🧪 What it tests

  • Task: is every generated claim "fully supported by its cited evidence"; built because no standardized benchmark existed (arXiv).
  • Unit: a claim, its references, a binary label; citation links reserved (README; census).
  • Data: ExpertQA, Stanford-GenSearch, AttributedQA, LFQA in distribution; BEGIN, HAGRID, AttrEval-GenSearch out; label-balanced (project page).
  • Credit: the makers ask that the original datasets be cited; the census leaves them unchecked (project page; census).

📊 What it found

  • Headline: a fine-tuned GPT-3.5 reaches about 80% macro-F1 (arXiv; census).
  • By setting: zero-shot 60–70% on long evidence; fine-tuning helps, stronger models and prompts little (project page).
  • Errors: over 66% fine-grained insensitivity; about 26.8% a model–annotator information gap that questions the labels (project page).
  • Limits: annotator-extracted references; "large room for improvement" (project page).

🧭 Support is not lineage

  • Attempt: support is checked where citation is attempted; PER measures failure to attempt, complementary (#716).
  • Lineage: a faithful answer can still strip authorial framework membership (#725).
  • Credit: "evidence support is not credit to the original author" (census; Capture Registry).
  • Power: whether attribution failure is power-conditioned, unmeasured (#141).
  • Identity: true statements can compose a false identification (#1695; #1705; #1680).

🏷️ Record

  • Makers: Li, Yue, Liao, Sun (Ohio State); ACL 2024 Findings; apache-2.0 on Hugging Face (arXiv; ACL; dataset card).
  • Not AttriBench: support of a claim, not who wrote a quote (census).
  • Census: answer engines; tests generation with citations (census).
  • Elsewhere: no Wikipedia article; the Overview names neither makers nor record (Capture Registry).
