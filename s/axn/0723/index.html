The entry, short

Sophistical Refutations 183b34–184b8 is the closing chapter's denial that anything of Aristotle's inquiry was there before it: 'it was not that one part had been worked out beforehand and another had not; rather, nothing at all was there' (183b34–36), and of syllogizing 'we had absolutely nothing else earlier to say' (184b1–2) (#1574; Pickard-Cambridge: 'Nothing existed at all'). The field reads it as marking 'the first work to treat the subject of deductive reasoning' (Wikipedia), 'the earliest formal study of logic' (Smith); The Particle (#1574) and Nothing Before Us (#1603) read the denial as total within its stated domain.
