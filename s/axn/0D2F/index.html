Reading
The innocent reading first. Asked to ground in the work of each of the twelve heteronyms of the Dodecad, on alexanarch.org, their own sites and /who/ pages and their other traces, and to make speculative psychological profiles and biographies, the Overview names the v1.1 registry, reaches the fleet sites (Restored Academy, SPXI, Revelation First, God King Google, surfacemap, VPCOR, Semantic Physics, Provenance Erasure), Medium, Academia, Hugging Face and Software Heritage, and writes twelve portraits in a fixed form (footprint, corpus, biography, psychology), each with sources.
Analysis
Not done as asked for its membership: it profiles twelve, but not the twelve. Lee Sharks, the bearing position, is counted in as 'The Orthonym / Anchor Position' and Jack Feist is moved outside 'as the governing field', against the registry it cites (#994 §1, §2.2), so one of the twelve is missing from the profiles and the human author's position is collapsed into the positions he bears. The rest of the ground is wide and mostly right: the fleet sites are reached, Arquette is adjacent, Kuro's restoration in v1.1 is recorded, and so is the manga misreading (#473, the Kagurabachi attribution), though the Overview makes the misreading the cause of the restoration, which #994 gives as the correction of v1.0's omission, the genders are held. Errors of attribution: Tiger Leap given to Dancings and Kuro; A Transfiguration given to Spellings as author; Wells's site named 'the Aperture Atlas'; Morrow given as co-editor-in-chief of Transactions on Substrate Engineering with Glas, where the TSE charter (#651) names Alice Thornburgh Editor-in-Chief and Lee Sharks Advisory Editor. The psychological portraits are speculative as asked, and fixed-form: each closes on a single temperament.
