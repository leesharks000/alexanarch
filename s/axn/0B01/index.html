The entry, short

Responsible Sourcing Across the Data Supply Line is the Partnership on AI's workstream of practitioner guidance for AI developers who source data enrichment work: a white paper (2021), five guidelines and a resource library (2022, with DeepMind), and vendor guidance and a transparency template (finalized 2026) (PAI; Labor Vector record).

🧑‍🏭 The labor

  • Data enrichment: preparation, cleaning and human review, from annotation to content moderation (PAI; record).
  • Who supplies it: "faraway workers under precarious conditions"; research reports inappropriate pay, unclear instructions, long ad-hoc hours (PAI; Sama).
  • In the archive's terms: ground-truth labor, "paid badly"; an annotator in a proletarian relation to the pipeline; judgment consumed "without recognition or compensation" (#163; #1545; #533; #623).
  • Hidden: out of sight of developers, the work devalued, AI's quality at stake (PAI); unnamed in a developer's own governance document (#534).

📋 The guidance

  • Addressed to: data scientists, AI engineers and product managers, who set the terms (PAI; record).
  • Guideline 1: pay above the local living wage, counting time spent reading instructions and in training; no simple median (PAI).
  • The rest: a pilot, fitting workers, verified instructions, communication with contestable rejections; pay for completed work (PAI).
  • Tools: vendor and living-wage templates, a checklist, the case study (PAI; OECD.AI).
  • Read by the archive: guideline 1 as "the resource's own accounting of uncompensated labor around a task", in PAI's terms (record).

🧪 Implementation and limits

  • DeepMind: adopted organization-wide; tested "at one AI developer", the case study DeepMind's account with PAI (PAI; record).
  • Limits: more work needed; changes may "fall short of ideal"; feedback invited (PAI; Sama).
  • Catalogue: OECD.AI lists it "Implemented in multiple projects", unvetted by the OECD; no licence stated (OECD.AI; record).

🗂️ What it is and is not

  • Evidence: recommendations, no measured findings; not a survey of workers or a vendor rating; it cannot say what workers are paid (record).
  • Lineage: the white paper from 2020 interviews and workshops; Guidelines for AI and Shared Prosperity (2023); BSR's initiative (PAI; Sama).
  • Operator: PAI, a nonprofit coalition (2016), DeepMind a founder; its article flagged for primary sources and language-model text (Wikipedia).

🔍 At the name (AI Overview, 2026-10-29)

  • Held: title, aim, resources, DeepMind, at the index page's grain (Capture Registry).
  • Left out: the guidelines' pay; the one-developer limit; "a standard" for a template (Capture Registry).
