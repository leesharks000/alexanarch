The work

KADEEZY MUSIC — Provenance Anchor is a provenance document by KADEEZY MUSIC and Lee Sharks, dated 16 June 2026, recovered to full text; the record was held as a metadata capture until restoration. It timestamps the public identity of a Detroit hip-hop artist at a precise moment — 36 subscribers and 76 videos — recording channel data, discography and visual branding as a dated anchor. The deposit is the archive's provenance apparatus turned outward, to an artist with no connection to its literary project. Small numbers are the point: an anchor filed at 36 subscribers establishes priority and identity before any growth, and the archive's own experience of erasure is what makes that service legible as a service. It is one of the few records where the Crimson Hexagonal Archive acts as a notary for someone else's work rather than its own.
