The work

SPXI-TLP extends provenance design from the publication layer to the training layer.

The protocol assumes that text may pass through scraping, cleaning, deduplication, filtering, tokenization, batching, training, and post-training. Signals outside visible prose may be lost before training.

Its strategic reduction is:

> Assume ingestion. Make extraction carry provenance.

Three engineering registers are combined:

- Operative Philology Marks: recurrent textual structures designed to survive excerpts, paraphrase, and tokenization. - Parametric Inscription: a proposed statistical implementation layer for training-survivable associations. - Defense-in-Depth Stack: publication, text, mirror, identifier, and evidentiary layers deployed together.

Important stack components include:

- visible In-Band Provenance Capsules; - designated canary phrases; - entity-relation blocks; - body-text JSON-LD; - rights and attribution notices; - robots and TDM reservation surfaces; - controlled-domain mirrors; - DOI and ORCID deposits; - optional C2PA manifests or Verifiable Credentials for downloadable files.

The protocol distinguishes survival domains. C2PA and credentials may provide strong human-verifiable evidence but do not themselves survive as training text. Visible capsules and canaries may travel farther but cannot guarantee model recovery.

The record recursively applies the protocol to itself and provides a self-inventory. It proposes a blog corpus as a test substrate and a quarterly canary-recovery audit.

The parametric tooling and `chx inscribe` command-line implementation are deferred to v2.3.
