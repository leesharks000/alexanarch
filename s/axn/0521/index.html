It is the Document 206 triptych framing packaged as its own object — a Zenodo packet about a Zenodo packet, which is what the ZPZP genre names. The meta-deposit states what the document is before stating what it says, on the principle that a genre instantiation must declare its own form to be legible as an instance of it. The pairing with #1297 is the archive's characteristic doubling: the framing document and its metadata packet are distinct records with distinct identifiers, because a claim about how a work should be represented to indexing systems can be right or wrong independently of the work's own argument.
