The work

The Cambridge Schizoanalytica institutional field-map is the visual node at `00.CSA.VISUAL`. Its central form is a negative aperture: a doorway-like absence that holds capacity and passage where an emblematic center would normally assert identity or authority.

The surrounding architecture is divided into four non-hierarchical fields. Affect theory appears as modulating waveforms; somatic theory as overlapping bodily schematics and apertures; digital subjectivity as algorithmic grids interrupted by organic marks; and role theory as blueprint-like masks whose hollowness allows deployment without possession. None of the fields becomes the master frame.

Molecular lines cross and re-enter these domains, making the schema a map of institutional movement rather than a static organizational chart. The name “Cambridge Schizoanalytica” is embedded in the scaffold, and the outer boundary is semi-permeable, allowing collaboration and traversal with adjacent Crimson Hexagon nodes.

The schema maps the CSA corpus directly: Shining Ignorance occupies the affect field, Reconciliation of the Sexes the somatic field, Affective Keyboard Shortcuts the role field, SHINE & CUT the central aperture, and the Break Room the entry-and-exit horizon. Its visual thesis is that the institution is a scaffold for practice and passage.
