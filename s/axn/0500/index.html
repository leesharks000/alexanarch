The structure is the archive's falsifiability discipline working as designed. A paper that names in advance both what would refute it and what would confirm it converts a subsequent event from anecdote into evidence, because the criterion was fixed before the observation. Depositing the confirmation as a dated addendum rather than editing it into the parent keeps the sequence auditable: the prediction and its satisfaction remain separately timestamped.
