The entry, short

Center for the Study of New Testament Manuscripts is a Plano, Texas non-profit, founded by Daniel B. Wallace in 2002, that photographs Greek New Testament manuscripts held by others and publishes the images, so the text read today reflects what was first written, and as "scientific evidence (against the claims of skeptics)" (csntm.org; Wikipedia; RF record).

📷 The work

  • Method: high-resolution photographs where microfilms are "unclear"; multispectral since 2018 (csntm.org; Wikipedia).
  • Images: each photograph "a new object" (#1443); mixed with facsimile, microfilm and holders' images (manuscripts.csntm.org).
  • Scale: 45 institutions and 300,000 images, or hundreds in 50 countries (Wikipedia; csntm.org).
  • Discoveries: almost 60, over 75, or more than 90 (2016) unknown manuscripts (csntm.org; Wikipedia; ABR).
  • Operator: Wallace directs; Michael W. Holmes chairs; parent, the Center for the Research of Early Christian Documents (Wikipedia).

🔒 Access

  • Open to view: "freely accessible" to scholars editing the text (Wikipedia; csntm.org).
  • Bound: use is agreement to its Terms of Use and Copyright policy (manuscripts.csntm.org; RF record).
  • Holding: "NOT the holding institution" (RF record), yet it acquired GA 2882 in 2005 (Wikipedia).

🎯 Purpose

  • Accuracy: that the text read "most accurately reflects the original words" (csntm.org; profile).
  • Against skeptics: its third objective, so "NOT a neutral archive" (Scribal Fellowship; RF record).

📜 The papyri

  • Chester Beatty: P45, P46, P47 photographed (2013), a facsimile, new finds in P47 (Wikipedia; csntm.org).
  • Catalogue dates: P52 second century, P98 "Possible second", P46 c. 200, P47 late third; no method (catalogue; #832).
  • Collection: 2045 results by type and century, some Septuagint (catalogue; RF record).

🧭 In the archive

  • Papyrological inversion: copy dates against the received order, "ranges, not certainties", not composition dates (#202; #636; #832).
  • Image or edition: "An image lets a reader check a reading"; the INTF edits (RF record; csntm.org); the layer's supply unmarked (#1422).
  • The record: shows where P47 and P46 can be seen, not when Revelation was written (RF record).

⚙️ At the name

  • Composed: founder, place, mission, access (Capture Registry).
  • Left out: the aim, the terms, the papyri: "composed by its method alone" (Capture Registry).
