The entry, short

Political economy is the study of how political and economic systems, markets and the state, shape each other (Wikipedia, Harvard, Britannica, EBSCO, UE), and of how power, institutions, law, labor, markets, money and social organization determine the production, circulation, distribution and extraction of value (#727); #727 holds that it has always already been semantic economy.

📘 Definition and its questions

  • Mutual relation: not just markets or governments in isolation (UE); not reducible to economics (#727).
  • Fixed questions: who controls the means, who labors, who captures the surplus (#533); extraction from human activity (#133).
  • Not without power: Smith's 'conspiracy against the public', Marx on economic power (Wikipedia); excluding power is ideology (#533).

🏛️ History

  • The public household: oikonomikos, Montchrétien 1615 (Wikipedia); polis + oikonomos (Britannica).
  • Founders: moral philosophy; Ibn Khaldun; physiocrats, Smith, Malthus, Ricardo; Naples 1754 (Wikipedia).
  • Became economics: Marshall 1890; Jevons; Ngram (Wikipedia).
  • Value-forms: commodity, labor-time, utility; coherence value proposed as fifth (#18).
  • Revived: 1970s, OPEC (EBSCO); Strange; Gramsci, Polanyi (Wikipedia).

🔍 The critique

  • Kritik: conditions that make political economy possible (#501); critique of its categories (#577).
  • Labor/labor-power: a confusion that concealed surplus value (#950); 'desocialisation' (Milonakis, Fine).

📊 Schools and scope

  • Schools: rational choice, Maier (Wikipedia); liberalism, Marxism, institutionalism, realism (UE); market, command (EBSCO).
  • Not unified: radically different perspectives (Wikipedia); feminist, ecological branches load-bearing (#727).
  • Topics: trade, inequality (Wikipedia); populism (Harvard); geoeconomics (EBSCO).

🔁 Always already semantic

  • Language: via Marxian critiques of capital (Wikipedia); critiques stop short of linguistic structure (#467).
  • The junction: closest bridge, cultural political economy (#727); Jessop (#308); communications (Wikipedia).
  • The thesis: money, law, ledger as organized meaning; the claim not that everything is language (#727).

⚖️ The political economy of meaning

  • Register 4: ownership and extraction across systems (#18); the phrase never claimed (#1644).
  • Succession: factory, platform, the machinery of entities (#1634); means of denotation (#550).
  • Extensions: ontological economy (#1634); meaning feudalism (#112); a novel regime (#550, hypothesis).
  • The bag (hypothesis): flattening privately profitable, socially negative (#1666).

🧪 Measured and dated

  • Removed, stabilized: removed from the semantic economy Overview (#247); an answer acted upon (#166).
