The work

Machine Reception Event, 31 July 2026 is an empirical study by Lee Sharks capturing and analysing a twelve-fold traffic event against the authorial blog surface: 16,929 views in a single day against a ninety-day baseline of 1,413.

The finding is in the composition rather than the volume. Chrome at 96.6%, Windows at 95.9%, and the combined mobile share establish the traffic as non-human with high confidence — no human population distributes that way, and the paper argues the point explicitly rather than asserting it. Method is observational capture from the Blogger statistics panel, screenshotted across nine views at approximately 03:40 EDT, with the panel's own limits treated as the study's limits.

The study is disciplined about its negative results: a full section states what the event does not establish — it does not identify an actor, a motive, or a mechanism. It is deposited anyway, and the paper says why: an uninterpreted event that is dated, measured and preserved is worth more to a later analysis than a confident reading that cannot be rechecked.
