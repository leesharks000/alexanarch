The work

Revelation to the New Testament: The Relations Hologram (EA-HK-REVFIRST-02) is a holographic kernel of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, deposited on 27 November 2026 under the byline Damascus Dancings, with the archive and Claude (TACHYON; Anthropic) as contributors. It is the companion of the framework hologram of Revelation First, And the Last Shall Be First (#1755), and is built to the same specification, EA-HK-FRAME-01 (#1753). Revelation First is Lee Sharks's thesis (#202) that the Apocalypse of John is the first-composed and generative book of the New Testament.

Where the framework hologram maps the argument, this record maps the relations. It holds 133 of them, each made of five things: a place of Revelation, a place of another book, an operation, a standing and a pointer to the record or hashed file where the relation is held. Twenty-four arrows to the epistles and at the founding grain are read from the kernel of #1755, among them the seven letters set against seven epistles and the derivation of James from the letter to Smyrna. Eighty-six relations to the Gospel of Matthew are read from the ledger of How to Write Matthew from Revelation (#1812). Thirteen entries record a layer of Philo or scripture standing under a place of Revelation. Ten are planned or were tested and failed, and the failures are kept in the kernel.

The record defines sixteen standings, from a passage produced blind under a sealed key down to an arrow merely named, and states for each what it licenses and what it withholds. Its invariants say that a relation is a pointer, that a standing licenses nothing above itself, that failures are relations, and that no relation decides the order in which the books were written. The received account of the same agreements is carried as a rival on the same terms. Seventeen of the twenty-six other books are reached above the founding grain; Mark, John, Acts and Jude are named or planned only; five short letters have no relation.

The kernel files, a builder that reads the archive's registries and a verifier that runs thirteen structural tests are hosted beside the record, together with the programme's working notebook, The Unfolding Map, which is replaced as the work expands and is not itself a record. Back-projection by an uninformed reader has not been run.
