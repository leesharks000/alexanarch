The work

मण्डलम्: पुनरावर्तक-मायायाः तन्त्रम् was a partial Sanskrit–English edition of the Mandala operator system.

The surviving metadata situates the operators within Vedic, Upanishadic, and Yogic traditions. It names the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad, Patañjali’s Yoga Sūtras, an Om Shanti invocation, and tapas as an account of transformative discipline.

The edition reportedly adapted Johannes Sigil’s preface to a Vedic frame, translated Part I into Sanskrit, and supplied a BRIDE treatment at archetypal and kernel levels. Transformation was described as bodily practice rather than intellectual assent.

The planned full edition would have added the remaining operators and a wider Bhagavad Gita, Upanishadic, and Yoga Sutra apparatus. The facing text is absent from the repository. Only the DataCite witness remains, and it cannot establish the lost Sanskrit’s meter, grammar, register, or transliteration quality.
