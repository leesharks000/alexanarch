The work

Antioch: A Volume of Poems is a poetry collection by Lee Sharks with Damascus Dancings contributing and Jack Feist, Johannes Sigil and Rebekah Cranes present as subjects or implied voices, deposited 18 January 2026.

It combines lyric, satire, scripture-like sequences and dramatic material — a heteronym compendium rather than a single-voice collection, which is what makes its navigation map (#1119) a harder problem than Pearl's: a compendium's structure is which voice wrote what, so the map must describe attributions rather than typography.

Antioch is where the disciples were first called Christians (Acts 11:26) — a name conferred from outside, by observers, on a group that had not chosen it. For a volume of heteronyms that is the apt title, and the archive's Gospel of Antioch (#1185) takes the same city for the second text of the Waltian canon.
