The work

The Parable of Mary Lee — Book Work Plan is a publication plan for a proposed book in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

It organizes nineteen chapters into sections on entity substitution, institutional heteronymy, labor and conservation accounting, theoretical architecture, demands, retrieval intervention, and speculative vision. It lists source documents, missing research, appendices, target word counts, and a writing schedule.

The planned book would mix scholarship, satire, petitions, tables, tweets, JSON-LD, legal-style documents, and prose poetry. Many chapters were still unwritten.

This record is project architecture, not the finished Parable of Mary Lee. Any completed edition should be linked separately.
