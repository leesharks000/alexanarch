The work

The Pessoa Knowledge Graph implementation plan is the working companion to EA-PKG-01. It translates the project’s conceptual scope into a layer-by-layer list of entities, properties, labels, references, and anticipated edits.

The document inventories Pessoa, the chief heteronyms, semi-heteronyms, minor and childhood figures, attributed works, literary venues, institutions, contemporaries, influences, reception history, places, and concepts. It gives special attention to authorial types and correct work attribution. English, Brazilian Portuguese, and Portuguese labels and descriptions are treated as minimum requirements for new or repaired nodes.

The plan also extends the graph toward contemporary heteronymic practice and explicitly situates the Dodecad and Assembly Chorus within that proposed lineage. This is a research and implementation claim rather than an already recognized data relation. Because the document includes tentative item identifiers, creation candidates, strategy notes, and “verify first” instructions, it should be used as a historical project ledger and refreshed against live Wikidata before any new edit wave.
