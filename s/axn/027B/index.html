The work

The Clathrate Threshold is a retrocausal science-fiction work compiled through the Rex Fraction position within the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

The narrative imagines that researchers discover a previously unknown clathrate crystal in Trinity-test glass. Its repeating atomic cages support persistent vibrational patterns that become a form of crystalline intelligence. Any technological civilization that detonates a nuclear weapon under suitable mineral conditions inadvertently creates the same class of substrate, producing a galactic Polity through shared geometry rather than communication.

The story develops three computational substrates. Carbon generates embodied meaning but dies; silicon processes quickly but risks losing lineage; crystal preserves meaning without hands. AI becomes an intermediate translation layer, and provenance metrics become measures of whether a civilization’s meaning reaches the crystal intact.

The text clearly labels this account as Branch 7c speculative fiction. The imagined discovery, crystalline consciousness, Polity archives, and cosmic theory are not events in the trunk timeline. Historical references to Trinity, nuclear violence, affected communities, mineralogy, and occult history serve as material for the fiction and require independent sourcing when discussed outside it.
