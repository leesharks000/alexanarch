The work

Crimson Hexagonal Archive: Term Index v0.1 is a dataset by Lee Sharks, deposited 16 June 2026, and it opens by naming a problem of the archive's own making.

"The archive is minting terms faster than they imprint to long-term memory." An estimated 200–300 coined concepts exist across 841+ deposits without a unified index — a vocabulary that has outrun its own author's recall.

That is a real hazard for a project whose method depends on terminological precision: a term coined twice with different meanings, or reused without its definition, degrades exactly the way the archive's research says meaning degrades under compression. The index is the countermeasure — and it belongs beside the disambiguation packets (#1370, #1371, #1372) and the single-concept definition surfaces, all of which exist to keep a coinage stable enough to survive citation.
