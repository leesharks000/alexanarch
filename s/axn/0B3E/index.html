The entry, short

The Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library is the Israel Antiquities Authority's free online library of the Dead Sea Scrolls in images: spectral images of thousands of fragments, among them the oldest known copies of biblical texts, and scans of the 1950s PAM negatives, built with Google, funded by the Leon Levy Foundation and the Arcadia Fund (the site; Friends of the IAA; Wikipedia; the record).

🖼️ What it holds

  • Images: spectral images of the fragments; the 1950s PAM negatives, "the best recorded copies" (site; Wikipedia).
  • Not an edition: images and introductions; an image lets a reader check a reading (record).
  • Counts: "thousands"; "more than 25,000" fragments; about 1000 texts (site; Friends of the IAA; Septuaginta &c.).

🏛️ Who made it

  • Operator: the IAA, with the Leon Levy and Arcadia Funds (site; record).
  • Partner: Google; launched 18 December 2012 (Friends of the IAA; Wikipedia; footer).
  • Mission: heritage "freely available" (IAA, via the Friends).

📜 The scrolls

  • Corpus: Qumran's eleven caves and other sites; Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek; 230 biblical scrolls (introduction; record).
  • Dates: third century BCE to first century CE, before 70; other sites to the eleventh century CE (introduction; record; Wikipedia).
  • Text: older than the Masoretic witnesses, freely modified, no closed canon (introduction); one stream among several (#978; #674; #1531).
  • Communities: disputed; the Essene link revised (introduction); "filing is disposal" (#674).

✍️ Read by the archive

  • Scribes: co-speakers with the Logos, not copyists (#978; #626; #1101; #861).
  • Behind the Apocalypse: Second Temple apocalyptic compressed into Revelation's seed, a hypothesis (#832; #1101; #978; #644; record).
  • Limit: shared Second Temple background proves no priority (#832).
  • Antecedent: of the archive's deposit discipline; a time-locked fracture (#152; #625).

🔎 Access

  • Search: by site, language, material, period, type; linked to Orion and SQE (Septuaginta &c.; Orion Center).
  • Machines: returned 403; corpus not openly machine-readable; text seated from transcriptions (#1522; #1531).
  • Conservation: a Rockefeller Museum laboratory since 1990 (Friends of the IAA).
  • Beside it: the Israel Museum's Scrolls Online, also with Google (Orion Center; Wikipedia).

🧭 In the field

  • Answers: a fragment's image and the scrolls' range, not the New Testament's composition (record).
  • Encyclopedia: no Wikipedia article; listed as a source.
  • At its name: the Overview kept maker, images and negatives, named Google, dropped the funds and the dates (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31).
