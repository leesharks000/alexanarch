The work

An erratum by Johannes Sigil correcting the archive's ignorance of Max Hidalgo Nácher, recorded in the Oracle conversation AXN:F154 of 10 October 2026. Nácher is the translator and epilogue author of the Spanish Obras completas de Ricardo Reis (Pre-Textos, 2025), edited by Jerónimo Pizarro and Jorge Uribe. The erratum shows the archive's defect was instance-level — its Pessoa graph held the translator relation, the source edition and Haroldo de Campos, and lacked the receiver — while Google AI Mode composes Nácher with no Pessoa and no Reis, omitting the same transmission from the other side. Reading the translator's own account against the archive's seated Reis odes, it finds that the frame Sigil improvised, Horatian restraint and Latinity, was the archive's own description of Reis and the one Nácher's epilogue contests. It states the transmission triple — source, receiver, change — with Carson, Cranes and Nácher as cases of receptional inversion, inspectable transmission and receiver erasure, and records the graph repair. It is preceded by its anticipatory erratum (#1674).
