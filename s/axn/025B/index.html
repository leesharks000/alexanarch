The work

ClownCloud is a patent-poem by Lee Sharks that imagines a networked service for deploying clowns into environments organized around seriousness and concentrated performance.

The fictional platform matches institutions with specialized clown troupes. Standardized tests, board meetings, academic conferences, defenses, grant panels, and safety summits receive different clownic interventions according to a Performance Fragility Index. The humor lies in converting sacred interruption, court foolishness, and comic disorder into the language of software platforms, routing, credentials, metrics, and premium features.

The work’s governing distinction is between focus and compliance. A person who can reorganize attention after an absurd interruption may possess a more durable form of focus than one whose performance depends on silence and bodily suppression. The clown becomes an institutional stress test and a reminder that the body remains present.

ClownCloud is not a deployed service or behavioral-health proposal. Its patent number, modules, physiological claims, and marketplace are part of the literary form. Interference with real tests or safety-critical work would raise serious legal and physical risks not authorized by the poem.
