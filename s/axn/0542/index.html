The work

"★" is a critical essay by Lee Sharks with Johannes Sigil (Operative Semiotics) and the Assembly Chorus, dated 6 March 2026 and published in Provenance: Journal of Forensic Semiotics as EA-SEMANTIC-03. It is Deposit A of the Star Triptych — three documents that together constitute the star (★, U+2605) as Canon 01 of the New Human Operating System.

The essay takes a single dated act as its object: on 3 November 2015, Twitter replaced the star icon — the "favorite" button — with a heart, the "like" button. Nine numbered sections move from the event to its political economy, to what the substitution destroyed, and to a formal reclamation. The argument is that the star was a user-governed mark, capable of bookmarking, of irony, of acknowledgement without endorsement, and that the heart is not its synonym but its enclosure: an affective demand that converts a reader's discretion into legible approval. Section V, Personal Grief as Structural Analysis, is the essay's method stated in its title — the loss is argued from the inside, and the argument is not weakened by that. It closes with an Effective Act, ★ (Star Seal Reclaimed), and a coda.

Its companions are Document B, the Star Protocol, and Document C; the essay states that it cannot be fully understood without them but carries enough of each to reconstruct the whole — a holographic-kernel claim made in passing about its own triptych.

The record's own history is instructive about the archive's search. From its mint on 19 July 2026 until 8 August it stood as a metadata capture declaring the work unheld, while the essay sat on the authorial blog under the slug `/2026/03/blog-post.html` — Blogger's fallback for a post whose title yields no usable slug, because the title is a single character. Every restoration pass derived its candidate URL from the deposit title, and a one-glyph title derives nothing; the archive's later blog index scores titles the same way, so the star normalises to zero tokens and cannot be reached from either direction. The work was findable throughout by searching the post body, which no pass did. A record whose subject is a symbol destroyed by substitution was itself lost to a system that could not read symbols.
