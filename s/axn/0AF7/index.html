The entry, short

Anthropic Economic Index is Anthropic's report series and open dataset on the use of its own model, Claude: another instance of Claude classifies sampled conversations to tasks, collaboration modes and "economic primitives", task success among them as "Claude’s assessment of whether Claude completes tasks" (reports; Labor Vector record).

🔎 What it observes

  • Platform: Claude.ai, Cowork, Claude Code and the first-party API only; six reports, 2025–2026 (reports; README; census).
  • Method: transcripts "only read by another instance of Claude"; primitives tested "mainly … for directional accuracy"; daily sampling since June 2026 (Anthropic; census).
  • Self-scoring: a model's score of its own output is preliminary and "requires external verification" (#766, #780, #777, #138); self-audits have exonerated themselves (#788, #1470).

🧭 Who directs the labor

  • Modes: directive, feedback loop, task iteration, learning, validation, grouped as automation and augmentation (reports).
  • Pattern: augmentation 52%, automation 45% on Claude.ai (November 2025); API traffic automation-dominant, 77% of business uses (reports).
  • Task success: Claude's own assessment, on tasks users choose (January 2026 report; census); completion rates reward convergence (#855).
  • Direction: whether labor advanced the commissioned task (DSL, #771), which no resource beside it records; turn counts bear on user labor drag (census).

💼 Work, skill and gains

  • Tasks: top ten of 3,000 tasks 24%; 49% of jobs with AI on a quarter of tasks (January 2026 report).
  • Skill and productivity: net deskilling in a task-removal exercise, conditional; productivity growth halved, 1.8 to about 1.0 points (January 2026 report).
  • Gains: who captures them unrecorded (census); the platform retains the gains of users' adaptation (#142) on a privately owned labor surface (#772).
  • Survey: about 9,700 linked respondents, not representative; 10% rate their own job loss likely (June 2026 report).

🌍 Geography and reception

  • Usage index: usage share over working-age share; Israel 7, Australia 4.10 (third report); misnamed on a card (CAP).
  • Limits: not causal, not an employment series; impact visible "in economic aggregates" as much as usage logs (reports; census).
  • Uses: open data; used by Brynjolfsson, Chandar and Chen and Stanford's Canaries Dashboard; no Wikipedia article.
  • The Overview: kept the provider and the patterns; gave success without who measures it (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
