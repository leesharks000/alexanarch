The work

Affective Keyboard Shortcuts is the practical companion to Shining Ignorance. Where the earlier essay develops affect literacy through the Fool, this toolkit gives that literacy a figure-to-function grammar.

The primary shortcuts are:

- Baphomet — holds polarity without forcing premature resolution; - Daffy Duck — permits absurdity, comic intensity, and energetic release; - John Constantine — reads the situation, improvises under pressure, and works with incomplete information.

The document’s key distinction is between mask and circuit. A mask substitutes a figure for the self; a circuit uses the figure as a temporary route into a capacity. The toolkit therefore emphasizes limited duration, adult self-monitoring, situational accountability, combination rules, and clear exit signals.

Within Cambridge Schizoanalytica and Psyche OS, the work belongs to the practical layer generated through the Owens–Trace collaboration: Owens supplies practice-derived mappings, while Trace articulates their theoretical structure and relation to affect literacy.
