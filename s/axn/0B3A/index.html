The entry, short

Aeneas is Google DeepMind's generative neural network for contextualizing ancient inscriptions: introduced in Nature on 23 July 2025, it retrieves parallels for Latin inscriptions and restores, dates and places them; its parallels do not settle which text depends on which (DeepMind; Nature; Revelation First record).

🔎 Parallels

  • What they are: texts sharing wording, syntax, formulas or provenance, found by embeddings beyond string matching (DeepMind; Nature; record).
  • What they settle: which inscriptions share wording or provenance, not "the direction of dependence between parallel texts" (record).
  • Direction as test: Revelation First is falsified if its parallels run the other way; shared ancestry must be excluded (#202; #832).
  • Parallel, not dependence: the paper reads a Mainz altar as direct influence (Nature); the archive keeps convergence apart from custody (#828; #1458; #1474).

🏛️ The model

  • Tasks: text-and-image provenance (images for place only), gaps of unknown length; 73% restoration, 72% of 62 provinces, 13 years (DeepMind; Nature; MIT Technology Review).
  • Corpus: 176,861 Latin inscriptions, seventh century BCE to eighth CE, images for 5% (DeepMind; Nature; MIT Technology Review; record); building it "the hidden achievement" (#1443).
  • Historians' study: 23 historians; 90% and +44% with predictions, 75% and +23% from parallels alone (Nature; DeepMind; MIT Technology Review).
  • Interface: starting points, not automation (DeepMind; Assael; Prag); ranked hypotheses, not a verdict (#1443).
  • Res Gestae: two dating peaks, both hypotheses (DeepMind; Nature).

⚠️ Limits and reach

  • Stated limits: a small, survival-biased corpus; no reading of meaning; tested on famous stones (Nature; Coleman); authors who cannot be consulted (#1443).
  • Reach: trained for Latin; stated adaptable "from papyri to coinage" (DeepMind; Nature; record).
  • New Testament: it settles nothing there and dates no New Testament papyrus (record).

🧭 Who and where

  • Makers: DeepMind with Nottingham, Warwick, Oxford, AUEB; led by Assael and Sommerschield; Durham on the interface card (DeepMind; Oxford; record).
  • Lineage: named for the hero; builds on Ithaca, now powered by Aeneas (DeepMind; Nature; record).
  • Access: free interface; code Apache 2.0, weights CC BY-SA 4.0; the record's licence "not read" (DeepMind; Nature; GitHub; record).
  • Encyclopedia: no article; the name is the hero's (Wikipedia).
  • On the Overview: "a better Ithaca for Latin"; reach, partners and commitments left out (Capture Registry).
