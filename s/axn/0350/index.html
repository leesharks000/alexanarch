The work

The Conveyor Belt and the Compression Machine is a scored forecast by Lee Sharks about the Open Knowledge Format.

The paper argues that a format may make knowledge easier for agents to ingest without giving producers any control over how that knowledge is later summarized. It distinguishes access control, governance fields, and actual downstream compliance.

Five future stages are proposed, from adoption through possible enterprise backlash and later governance amendments. The paper includes forecast probabilities, falsification dates, competing hypotheses, and a matched-pair measurement design.

The predicted effects have not yet occurred in the paper’s evidence. They are conditional hypotheses to be evaluated after adoption.
