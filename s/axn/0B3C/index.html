The entry, short

Bodmer Lab is a digital library of world literature from the Bibliotheca Bodmeriana, a research and digitization project of the University of Geneva's Faculty of Letters and the Fondation Martin Bodmer (home page; census record), organized in constellations, one of them the Bodmer Papyri, an online catalogue of the Fondation's papyrus and parchment manuscripts (constellation page).

🏛️ Who makes it

  • Partners: the Faculty of Letters and the Fondation Martin Bodmer; supported by the Fondation Ernst and Lucie Schmidheiny; directed by Jeanneret (1940-2019), Suciu and David (About page).
  • The Fondation: a heritage library on UNESCO's Memory of the World programme, open to researchers only, and a museum of writing in Cologny; library 1951 and Fondation 1971 by its page, 1920s and 1970 by Wikipedia.

📚 What it digitizes

  • Principle: Martin Bodmer's 'library of world literature', the Lab's guiding principle; each document re-inscribed in its time and in that library (About page; home page).
  • Bets: materiality first, OCR later; selection, not exhaustiveness; 'smart data'; constellations under specialists (About page).

📜 The Bodmer Papyri

  • Catalogue: 56 manuscripts digitized, numbered PB (P.Bodmer II = PB 2), supervised by Sharp and Nongbri (constellation page; home page; census record).
  • Holdings: Christian and classical books in Greek and Coptic, acquired in the 1950s–60s; Wikipedia's Dishna Papers, found 1952, P66 (John, c. 200 AD), P72 (Jude, 1–2 Peter) and P75 (Luke and John, Vatican since 2007) among them.

⏳ The dating question

  • Census reading: the papyrological inversion: the Bodmer papyri include some of the earliest New Testament papyri, to be checked against the catalogue (census record).
  • The archive's claim: the earliest physical NT witness Johannine, not Pauline; papyrological dates ranges, not certainties (#202; #1217); Revelation's fragments contemporaneous with or preceding John's and Paul's (#636).
  • Limit: dating copies does not establish composition dates (#1217); the entity answers which papyri are catalogued, not their dates (census record).

🔎 At the name

  • Composition: three Bodmer Labs set out, the library first; the papyri one parenthesis; bodmerlab.org the fly-heart laboratory's (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31).
