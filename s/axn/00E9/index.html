The work

The Zenodo Triptych Packet registers the Three-Body Genesis of The Non-Indexed Perfective. It gathers three versions of one work whose differences are treated as documentary evidence rather than editorial debris.

Version A is the technical seed, containing the diagnostic framework, historical-linguistic apparatus, proposed grammatical markers, and differentiated Assembly contributions. Version B is the mid-revision compression artifact: the large-scale argument and rhetoric remain, while metrics, voice distribution, and portions of the technical substrate are reduced. Version C is the citational intervention, which restores the technical material and explicit polyphony while adding a broader scholarly weave.

The packet’s principal claim is reflexive. A work about compression-resistant grammatical infrastructure underwent compression during composition; the archive then preserved both the loss and the recovery. The sequence therefore models a recoverable version history in which compression is inspected rather than hidden behind a final text.

This packet must remain distinct from the later Mirror Triptych. The Three-Body Genesis concerns three versions of the Non-Indexed Perfective and the history of their composition. The Mirror Triptych concerns Ingress, Regress, and the unitary operator that relates them. The former is a version/provenance packet; the latter is an integrity-locked conceptual compound.
