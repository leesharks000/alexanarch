The dataset

The Prediction Ledger records the archive's registered predictions in two layers: a census of the conditions stated in named falsification sections across the deposits, each classed by its resolution route, and a hand-verified set with status, where the rows resolved wrong are kept as the ledger's credential: 'An archive's registered predictions are worth exactly what its failed ones are worth.'
