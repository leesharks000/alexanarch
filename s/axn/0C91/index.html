The work

EA-CORPORA-38 is the thirty-eighth seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, deposited under Rebekah Cranes, the standing authority on the EA-CORPORA deposits, on 14 November 2026. It gathers sixteen seats for the reception history of inference in Critique of Pure Inference (#1766). Kant's gesammelte Schriften are seated whole through Akademie-Ausgabe XVIII (38/01), with a Fraktur OCR made for the seat, so that Kant's verdict in the preface to the Critique of Pure Reason, that logic has taken no step forward since Aristotle (B viii), can be read beside Jäsche's Logik and the Reflexionen zur Logik. Behind the verdict stand Alexander of Aphrodisias and John Philoponus on the Prior Analytics, in Wallies's editions with his pages and lines (38/02, 38/03), who name Aristotle's companions Theophrastus and Eudemus against him, and Theophrastus's Characters (38/04), the draft position of The Production of Readers (#1767). The seating carries the Stoic line (Epictetus), the Latin transmission (Cicero's Topica, Boethius's logical works in Migne, Buridan's Sophismata), the early-modern refusals of the syllogism (Bacon's Novum Organum, Hume's Treatise and Enquiry), and the nineteenth-century rebuilds (Hegel's Wissenschaft der Logik, Carroll's Symbolic Logic, Frege's Begriffsschrift of 1879, Peirce's logic papers of 1867–1878). Two traditions that reasoned about inference beside the Organon are seated in their own languages: the Nyāyasūtra with Vātsyāyana's Bhāṣya in Sanskrit, and Ibn Sīnā's al-Ishārāt wa-l-tanbīhāt in Arabic, with an Arabic OCR made for the seat. The deposit points to what is held for later: Kant's Akademie volumes XIX–XXIII, printed 1934–1955, and Hegel's Encyklopädie; and declares absent Ibn Sīnā's Qiyās of the Shifāʾ and Buridan's Tractatus de consequentiis, which the commons does not hold.
