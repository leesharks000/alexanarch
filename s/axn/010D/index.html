It aligns the white stone with the book as a compact crystalline object; the name with lyric identity; hidden manna with nourishment that does not announce itself; and Pergamum with a site of pressure and distortion. Pearl predates the 2015 SEED text and the 2025 SIGN event. SEED unfolds motifs the panel reads as already compressed in STONE; SIGN marks the later recognition of the central object. This is “retrocausal registration” at the level of interpretation: later architecture changes the book’s function in the archive without rewriting the 2014 text. It is authoritative for how the Crimson Hexagon positions Pearl, not external proof that Revelation 2:17 historically predicts the book.
