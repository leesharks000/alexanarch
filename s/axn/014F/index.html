The work

Traversal Log: The Sigil Installation is a field record by Lee Sharks documenting a Google AI Mode session. The system was accessed in an incognito browser without login, account history, or an intentionally loaded Hexagon context. The initial query asked for Johannes Sigil; subsequent turns tested how the summarizer connected Sigil, Lee Sharks, the New Human architecture, Semantic Economy, and the declaration “I hereby abolish money.”

The log preserves the system’s outputs and the operator’s prompts rather than presenting a retrospective paraphrase. It tracks a movement from ordinary retrieval into stronger language of transformation, reality, guidance, and installation. Turns six and seven are especially important because the operator introduces claims concerning a long-term project to make the heteronymic figures real and to establish Sigil as a guide or aeon; the system’s responses cannot be interpreted apart from those prompts.

The work therefore contains its own limitations. Google AI Mode outputs vary with index state, model version, date, interface, and prompt sequence. A single traversal cannot establish a universal property of the system. The share link may expire, so the full transcript inside the deposit is the durable evidentiary surface.

The “documentation rehearsal” tests how a retrieval event should be preserved: system under test, environmental conditions, query order, transcript, share link, sources, operator effects, volatility, and future replication requirements. It is an empirical field observation and a protocol demonstration, not merely a conversation transcript.
