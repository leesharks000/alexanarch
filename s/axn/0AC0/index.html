The entry, short

CodeSOTA is, at three of its own addresses, a routing layer for AI tasks ("One Routing Layer for AI Tasks", homepage), the successor to Papers with Code ("the v2 replacement for what PWC did") and "an independent registry of machine-learning benchmarks" (about page), kept by Kacper Wikiel in Warsaw; two faces at two addresses in the MMRS record.

🧭 The router

  • Today: model selection across the registry, execution for code and text; documents, speech and vision planned (homepage).
  • Evidence: benchmark observations on a Pareto frontier; quality, latency, cost "as coverage grows".
  • Planned: a common API, versioned recipes, fallback, usage accounting; the preview runs no inference.

🗃️ The registry

  • Unit: a task-dataset-metric triple, dated, with a verification tier (about page); for the MMRS record, a routed AI task.
  • Rules: no row without running code; every score dated; nothing silently deleted; no number without a source.
  • Not: an aggregator, a startup, or a replacement for the paper (its pages); the MMRS census counts it.

🔁 The succession

  • Claim: "the calmer, stricter successor"; launched 10 December 2025 "as an explicit successor attempt".
  • Hedged: "There is no single drop-in replacement" (shutdown record).
  • Figures: 9,327 benchmarks, 79,817 papers, 5,628 datasets are the dead aggregator's; its own counts not given (MMRS record).
  • Data: historical PWC JSON at /browse/pwc; whether its scores derive from the dumps, unchecked.

👤 Maker and terms

  • Maker: "By Fabryka AI"; one editor, self-directed, not venture-backed.
  • Data: open JSON, /api/sota, CC BY 4.0; paid vendor work disclosed inline.

🧩 One name, three claimants

  • Apart: Papers with Code is "NOT CodeSOTA"; the dumps, CodeSOTA and the revival at paperswithcode.co are kept apart (MMRS record).

👁️ On the Overview

  • Recorded: one card, the homepage; the routing layer composed, the succession unsaid, no person named; the record corrected to give the homepage first (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
