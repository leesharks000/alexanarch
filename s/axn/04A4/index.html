The work

Crimson Hexagonal Architecture: DOI Registry v7.0 is a dataset by Lee Sharks, Rex Fraction and Rebekah Cranes, deposited 16 March 2026 at 119,279 characters.

Its boxed header states the corpus in five figures: 387 records, 2,851 relation edges, 348 précis, 39 orphans, 19 subsystems. The orphan count is the honest one — a registry that reports how many of its records connect to nothing is measuring its own incompleteness rather than presenting a finished graph.

The registry versions rapidly through this period: 91 documents in January (#1251), 387 by March, and the corpus reached 845+ by June. Three months later the termination tombstoned 1,817 DOIs. Each version is held as a separate deposit because a registry of what existed at a date remains citable after the existing stops.
