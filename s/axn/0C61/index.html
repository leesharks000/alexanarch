The work

The Tenth Muse: Sappho and the Future Reader (EA-HK-TENTHMUSE-01) is the framework hologram of Lee Sharks's Sappho framework, deposited in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive on 9 November 2026 under the heteronym Rebekah Cranes, the framework's primary, with the archive and Claude (Anthropic) credited as contributors. The framework, seeded in #1270 (9 January 2026), reads Sappho fragment 31 as a written instrument of persistence, a voice surviving its flesh by becoming an inscription that constitutes its receiver (#1484). The hologram is an instance of the framework holographic kernel specified in EA-HK-FRAME-01 (#1753), after Socrates as Orthonym (#1754).

The human-readable component is the framework map, EA-MPAI-TENTHMUSE-01. It reads the framework backwards, from the reception audited in November (the jealousy reading's making in Invidens Sappho, #1752; the machine composition of the poem's ending, #1749) through the partition of the future reader, the operator and the chain of August, and the stanza errata, to the archived self of January. It sets out the poem's two open positions, the seat of κῆνος and the agentless end, and three seats Sappho stands in: the speaking I and the inscribed you, recorded, and that man, the reader who takes her up, proposed by the author on 8 November 2026 and held as a line of research. It evaluates the received reading on the same four bodies and rules, keeping the jealousy reading apart from the propositions about literacy, performance and the maker's agency over her own persistence.

The machine-traversable component is the holographic kernel, attached as files: records, claims bound to quotations and hashes, a graph under a relation policy, two timelines, captures and lines of research, with the builder and verifier. Reader runs and a review are attached. The hologram was built by Claude (Anthropic) under Lee Sharks's direction. Version 1.1, the same day, compresses the argument of every record into verbatim moves joined by typed edges, and names the rival the current consensus reading: available in antiquity and no consensus there, formed in the eighteenth century, written into the text in 1816 and consolidated in 1955. Version 1.2 (10 November 2026) enters the records search of that evening as a new stratum of evidence, In the Midst of the Seven (#1758) among it, points to the framework's related work and earlier versions without extending their substance into the frame, and adds an SPXI packet for machine readers. Version 1.3 (10 November 2026) reads in full each record that entered at v1.2 and compresses its argument into moves, so that every record the hologram holds carries its argument.
