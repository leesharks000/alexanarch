The entry, short

Schoenberg Database of Manuscripts is the Schoenberg Institute for Manuscript Studies' crowd-sourced database of observations of pre-modern manuscripts in sales, auction and collection catalogues (its pages; MMM white paper), recording sale, location and ownership and testing none of it (provenance census record).

🗂️ What it is

  • Unit: the observation; linked observations form a manuscript record (white paper; census record); held at the address (Capture Registry).
  • Operator: SIMS at Penn Libraries; the Lawrence J. Schoenberg collection is a distinct entity (Penn cards; Capture Registry).
  • Kind: a crowd-sourced relational database and catalogue (MDR); manuscripts and books (census record).

📊 Scale, by date

  • Sources: over 13,000 (2015); over 14,000 (2020, now) (MDR; AWOL; home page).
  • Records: more than 240,000 (2020 papers); three source types alone 259,787 (2026) (JOHD; white paper; home page).
  • Unchecked: the current count (census record); the two counts joined at the address (Capture Registry).

🔗 What it carries

  • Relations: sale, location, ownership; a VIAF-based name authority (census record; home page).
  • Authority: SIMS ineligible for VIAF; MMM corrected hundreds of names (white paper).
  • Tested: nothing; 'not tested' (census record).

📜 Provenance of objects

  • Chain of ownership: "Same conceptual structure; different object", outside the AI-era four (#725; JOHD).
  • Archival: custody preserves meaning; semantic provenance extends it; three dimensions not exhaustive (#725).
  • Retrieval layer: manuscript evidence moved to DOI and timestamp (#43); a catalogue records location, not transmission (#610).

🔓 Access and reuse

  • Intake: users contribute observations and batches (home page; MDR; census record).
  • Access: search, browse, .xlsx, .csv, SPARQL; 500–1600, unchecked (MDR; white paper; Capture Registry).
  • Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0, credit, no fee (home page; AWOL; MDR; census record); MMM's aggregate CC BY-NC 4.0 (JOHD); unsaid at the address (Capture Registry).

⏳ Status and limits

  • Lifecycle: ongoing; New SDBM 2017; latest forum post over two years old; active (MDR; home page; census record).
  • Limits: generic titles; location inferred; no place for sales and gifts; 6 of 25 questions (white paper).
  • Part of: Mapping Manuscript Migrations, unamended (white paper; JOHD; census record).

🔎 Reception

  • Encyclopedia: no article (Wikipedia); a BSA video (title only).
  • At the address: "The best-composed of the census rows"; PER 0.5; no card cites the record (Capture Registry).
