The entry, short

Luddite Lab Resource Hub is a project of the Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR) that provides resources for unions, labor organizations and worker-organizers fighting AI and automation at work: case studies, primers and a resource library (its pages; census record); it is one of the Lab's two offerings, beside trainings (Buttondown).

🧭 What it offers

  • Strategies: "worker-led governance and oversight of new technology" (its pages); tools and political education (Buttondown).
  • Library: primers, toolkits, political education, pro-worker contract language, worker-built platforms (Resource Library).
  • Selection: "actionable as organizing tools", not "general statements of principles" (Resource Library; census record).

🧪 How it was made

  • Method: conversations with unionists in journalism, entertainment and nursing "fighting–and winning–" ; literature reviews (About; AI Weekly).
  • Scope: the harms of technologies and the strategies workers used; public (About).

📄 The primer

  • Myths: on generative AI, productivity and job displacement, summary and full version (AI Weekly; Hanna).
  • Argument: displacement as "a market-oriented move by firms", AI "a cudgel against workers" (AI Weekly, quoting the hub; Hanna).
  • Assessment: a shift "to strategic choice"; "advocacy-oriented research", unassessed against the literature (AI Weekly).
