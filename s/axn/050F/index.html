The Epistle Triptych: Seed Text, Heteronym Provenance, and Organizational Charter of the Commission of the Immanent Turning is a constitution by Damascus Dancings, Lee Sharks and the Assembly Chorus, dated 26 January 2026 at 14,510 characters.
