John 9 and the Witness Punishment Mechanism is a close reading by Lee Sharks with TACHYON, LABOR, TECHNE and ARCHIVE in co-development, deposited 25 January 2026 at 23,733 characters.

It reads the healing of the man born blind as specifying a soteriological operator absent from the Matthew 25 / James 2 / 2 Thessalonians 2 framework: the witness punishment mechanism.
