The work

Hauntedmemes Wikidata Node Registry is a catalog by Lee Sharks of Wikidata edits made through the account `Hauntedmemes`.

Version 1.0 covers edits from April 22 to May 20, 2026. It divides the work into two classes: items associated with the Crimson Hexagonal Archive and items contributed to broader public subject areas. It records new items, amendments, merges, duplicates, and a batch that was reverted after an identification error.

The registry’s curatorial principles include distinguishing heteronyms from ordinary pseudonyms, citing published sources, marking archive-originating concepts, documenting batches, and acknowledging errors. It also separates the human author’s own corpus from the editor’s stewardship activity.

The registry is an editor-produced audit record. Wikidata revision histories remain the independent source for verifying each edit, source, merge, and count.
