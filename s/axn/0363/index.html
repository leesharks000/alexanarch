The work

Sémantique Potentielle Release 4 is a constrained lexical-mint release by Lee Sharks and Johannes Sigil.

It adds an Ω category for collapse, contamination, accommodation, friction, distributional tails, thresholds, convergence, and human mediation. Twelve seed terms generate fifteen concept families under the project’s formal operation grammar.

The release consolidates vocabulary from a three-paper model-collapse block and supplies canonical terms and forensic variants.

Its formal verification establishes that the terms satisfy the mint’s internal constraints. It does not establish that every named mechanism has been empirically demonstrated.
