The dataset

The Work Bears the Mantle holds the archive's mantle-bearing under one standard, that a title means something because what it names is carried and can be read (#1655, #1670, #1678, #1679): the titles the archive claims for works of its own (the Good Gray Poet, the King of May, the Prince of Poets), judged by readers in the works, a contest mantle, a conferred title, a critical program and a transmitted mantle. Its rows are evaluations and findings with their loci.
