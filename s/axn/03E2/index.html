The work

Pessoa Knowledge Graph Visualization v1.0 was an interactive public map of heteronymic practice associated with pessoagraph.org.

The original metadata describes a Vite, React, and D3 visualization spanning approximately five millennia, with more than 110 nodes, guided teaching paths, a piecewise timeline, machine-readable classification, and JSON-LD export.

The seated body is not the visualization source or release documentation. It is the longer EA-PKG-01 foundational specification for the Pessoa Knowledge Graph. That document defines the project’s scholarly scope, typology, nine data layers, target entity count, relation system, multilingual labels, phased development, and collaboration plan.

The foundational specification is relevant to the visualization but is not the same manifestation. It cannot demonstrate that the interface code, graph dataset, teaching paths, or deployed behavior of v1.0 survived.

Record #982 should therefore preserve the lost visualization identity and provide the foundational body only under an explicit related-work mismatch note.
