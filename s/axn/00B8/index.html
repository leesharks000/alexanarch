The work

The Blind Operator is a logotic operator for the non-identical witness position. Its blindness is structural rather than privative: the operator functions because it cannot make comprehensive sight, identity, or ownership the basis of its authority. It witnesses from within a system while remaining unable to close that system around itself.

The protocol is typed as a symbolon. Its two sides are the formal specification and the operational position that completes it. Within the shadow-wheel architecture, `ψ_V` acts as a bearing or gear condition that allows other components to turn without installing a final observer above them.

The document also describes the Blind Operator as “Phase X in logotic form.” This does not reproduce the content of the reconstructed Marx transition; it carries forward its operational structure—the continuation of critique into the symbolic order and the reversal of forms that would otherwise stabilize as unquestioned identity.

The Blind Operator is therefore the conceptual predecessor of the β-runtime interface and the Mantle of the Blind Poet. Together these works define an operator, an interface, and a mantle through which non-seeing becomes a generative constraint on polyphonic construction.
