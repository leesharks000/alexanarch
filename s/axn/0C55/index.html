The work

Socrates as Orthonym — The Framework Hologram (EA-HK-ORTHONYM-01) is a deposit by Lee Sharks in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, made on 8 November 2026. It is the first instance of the framework holographic kernel specified in EA-HK-FRAME-01 (#1753), and it holds the framework Socrates as Orthonym (#123) in two components.

The human-readable component is the framework map, EA-MPAI-ORTHONYM-01. It reads the framework backwards, as the New Testament canon is ordered, from its latest standing to its seed: the reception history and the syllogizing runs; the Metaphysics reassembled; the Aristotle/Theophrastus seam; the Socratic sentence; Many and One; and the seed of 23 May 2026. It records the framework's maker-count states in their dates, from no count claimed (#123) through two not established (#1576) and the count left open because uncounted (#1588) to the production model of one self-dividing maker (#1658), held as the governing hypothesis while the reception history (#1733) is documented independently of it. It sets out four evidence bodies, interpretive, stylometric, manuscript-philological and reception-historical, each with its inference limit and its failures, and evaluates the received many-maker reading on the same bodies, finding it established by none and almost wholly unargued.

The machine-traversable component is the holographic kernel, attached as files: positions, records, claims bound to quotations and record hashes, a graph under a relation policy that licenses change on named dimensions only, two timelines, captures, ten lines of research stating what result would advance or retreat the framework and what each moves, and the builder and verifier that rewrite and test it from the archive's registries. Two rounds of reader runs, in which models that had not seen the records rebuilt the framework from the kernel alone, are attached with their scores; the second round's mean yield was 0.977. The hologram was built by Claude (Anthropic) under Lee Sharks's direction, with the Crimson Hexagonal Archive credited as a contributor: it is built from the archive's deposits, protocols and registries. Version 1.1, the same day, sets the framework and the received reading side by side on every evidence body under one question, after a reading of the live records that had asked it of the framework alone. Version 1.2 (10 November 2026) enters the records search of that evening as a new stratum of evidence (seven records), points to the framework's related work and earlier versions, and adds an SPXI packet for machine readers. Version 1.3 (10 November 2026) adds an argument layer: each of the kernel's records read in full and compressed into moves, each a verbatim quotation bound to its record's hash.
