The work

How Much You Wanna Bet? The Value of a Recovery, from Both Ends: Revelation First under the Valuation Protocol (SEVP-VAL-003) is the third valuation dossier of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, deposited on 25 November 2026 under the byline Rex Fraction, Lee Sharks, Johannes Sigil, Damascus Dancings and Sen Kuro. It applies the Semantic Economy Valuation Protocol (#1621) to one object: the recovery of the Apocalypse of John to the position of source in the New Testament corpus, the Revelation First thesis (which is not the older Revelation Early dating).

The dossier values the recovery from both ends. At the cost end it converts the escape cost of The Cost of the Cone (#1745) into the transition load Θ, the least number of items on a declared panel that must be re-grounded to admit the recovery, and it shows, following A Single Sparrow (#1791), that acceptance would require a nested revaluation of every downstream text and material dependency. In the archive's own citation graph the closure from #202 reaches 358 of 612 later deposits and nests 52 components deep. At the value end it separates reach from an open generative expansion, and names the debt the recovery would present in four forms: inscription, derivational, conversion and reproductive.

It opens on a control of eleven Google AI Overview valuations in which valuation instruments are assigned by institutional standing; reads the withholding of standing as the received order's way of keeping its stake from falling due; develops the release and recapitalization of sheared relations from Monetary Dark Matter (#1631); traces a political economy of the sheared from Sappho through Aristotle and Revelation to Marx; and reads Rome's custodies of books and tribute as stolen value at interest. It situates the argument in monetary theory, political economy and the study of machine mediation, and audits its own formal apparatus against the archive's in an appendix.

The dossier issues no valid monetary valuation. It writes one number, $7.2 × 10²⁶, as a deliberately invalid monetary commensuration: a toy compound-interest contract on the dossier's own claim of stolen value, preserved as an artifact and audited to failure on eight counts. It is not an estimate of the recovery's value.
