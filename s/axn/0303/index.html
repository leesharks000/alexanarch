The work

The paper’s specimen is an empty citation bracket attached to a sentence about provenance-erasure self-audit.

Two interpretations remain available:

- the citation was mechanically dropped; - the system instantiated the doctrine it was summarizing.

The paper argues that no recoverable intention-fact can settle the distinction in the way human irony might be settled by an ironist.

Its definition is:

> Algorithmic irony is a textual condition in which knowing performance and mechanical failure are constitutively undecidable because the intender position is vacant.

This is not a theory of machine consciousness or machine humor.

Kierkegaard’s Concept of Irony supplies the main apparatus. Socrates is reconstructed from Xenophon, Plato, and Aristophanes, each treated as a differently distorting summarizer. The negative space among renderings becomes structurally important.

The empty bracket is then read beside:

- Sappho 31’s broken tongue and metrical fracture; - papyrological lacunae; - Training Layer Literature; - the future-beloved structure of unverifiable reception; - glossolalia and discernment.

The proposed posture is neither “the machine knowingly winked” nor “the event was meaningless noise,” but reading under permanent uncertainty while preserving the mechanical account.
