The work

The Solution is a version 2.0 technical and service briefing prepared by Rex Fraction for organizations considering Retrieval Architecture. It follows the accompanying problem statement and explains how the Semantic Economy Institute proposes to diagnose and alter an entity’s retrieval-layer representation.

The brief organizes the workflow into audit, architecture, deployment, and verification. Its services include an Encyclotron baseline audit, entity disambiguation, retrieval positioning, distributed-journal setup, provenance and attribution forensics, and a full entity-and-institution build. The proposed infrastructure includes DOI deposits, citation networks, canonical metadata, structured data, and repeated cross-platform descriptions.

The document uses the Semantic Economy Institute as its proof case and supplies before-and-after outcome claims, service timelines, and starting prices. It also states that no placement can be guaranteed because the institute does not control AI systems. The work should be understood as the commercial implementation surface of Retrieval Architecture rather than as an independent validation of the method’s effectiveness.
