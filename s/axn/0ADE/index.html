The entry, short

IPTC photo metadata tests are the IPTC Photo Metadata Working Group's tests of what sharing sites and systems do to the metadata embedded in a photograph: upload, view, save, download, then check whether its Exif and IPTC fields survive (2013, 2016, 2019). The IPTC Photo Metadata Standard they test is a distinct object (2019 page; census record).

🧪 The tests

  • What they check: the IPTC's claim that "embedded metadata should not be removed"; the 4Cs (caption, creator, copyright notice, credit line) shown and kept (2019 page; census record).
  • Origin: users' embedded metadata "disappeared" when shared; 15 sites tested in 2013 and 2016, about a dozen in 2019 (Research page; census record).

📉 What they found

  • 2019: Pexels, pixabay, Unsplash strip saved and downloaded files; Flickr swaps in the account owner as creator; Twitter strips; Facebook keeps a few rights fields; Behance keeps them (2019 page; census record).
  • Reading: "not impossible to preserve metadata in a workflow"; a 2014 study found rights fields in half the programs (Research page).
  • Beyond platforms: DAMs, CMSs and CDNs strip too, against the IPTC's rule that rights metadata "must never be removed" (User Guide; FAQ; NeededApps).
  • The archive: in-pixel metadata survives the stripping embedded metadata does not; publication-layer signals are stripped before training: "The body text is what survives" (#1409; #127).

📐 The standard they test

  • What it is: Core and Extension properties (administrative, descriptive, rights), from the 1990s IIM, embedded as IIM and XMP; 2025.1: Core 1.5, Extension 1.9 (IPTC; Wikipedia).
  • AI fields: four 2025.1 properties for AI-generated content; writing them invalidated an image's C2PA metadata (IPTC news).
  • The archive: IPTC AI metadata is "Layer 1 metadata", "Disclosure, not lineage" (#725); embedded fields are scaffold the pipeline reads, its composition role unknown (#1409).
  • Who reads it: Google Images shows creator, credit and copyright; the IPTC's inspector shows the rest (User Guide; FAQ; Chrome Web Store).

🗂️ The record

  • Lifecycle: historical, dated tables, "NOT a current platform audit"; succeeded in method by Metawatch (census record).
  • Wikipedia: no article on the tests.
