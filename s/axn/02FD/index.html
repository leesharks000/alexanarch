The work

Mary Lee Is a Heteronym is an essay by Lee Sharks about institutional authorship and machine-mediated entity substitution.

It describes Mary Lee, a tagged great white shark whose first-person social-media persona was written by human operators, as an institutionally authored heteronym. It contrasts this with Lee Sharks as a self-authored heteronym.

The essay argues that a composition system may prefer the entity with the denser public trace rather than the stronger authorship relation. The proposed category extends literary heteronymy into institutional and nonhuman personae.
