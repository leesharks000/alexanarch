The work

Revelation First means compositional priority:

> Revelation was the first-written New Testament document.

It is not equivalent to:

> Revelation was written somewhat earlier than the dominant late-date consensus allows.

The document’s claim ladder includes:

1. the Domitianic dating consensus is not self-evident; 2. Pauline priority is inferential rather than directly material; 3. manuscript attestation should be allowed to unsettle inherited confidence; 4. later New Testament forms may elaborate structures compressed in Revelation; 5. a Josephan heteronymic reading becomes available if Revelation stands first; 6. the comparison is inference against inference, with symmetrical burden.

The midrashim transform is the proposed structural-genetic operation mapping Revelation’s compressed forms into later elaborations:

- seven letters → epistolary form; - Lamb → atonement theology; - New Jerusalem → eschatological architecture; - throne room → worship structure.

The document states explicit falsifiers and distinguishes the thesis from preterism, dispensationalism, and conspiracy theories.
