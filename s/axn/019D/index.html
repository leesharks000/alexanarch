The work

The Three Compressions is a provisional theoretical paper by Lee Sharks, Rex Fraction, Johannes Sigil, and the Assembly Chorus, dated March 16, 2026. It supplies a transfer law intended to connect the archive’s Semantic Economy with Semantic Physics.

The paper’s governing question is not whether compression should occur. Memory, poetry, archives, media, and models all reduce complex material into transmissible forms. The decisive question is what the compression burns and where the unrecovered cost lands. From that relation it defines three regimes.

Lossy compression is relatively thin and computationally cheap. A single event may not be directly extractive, but repeated low-density reduction can normalize pre-shaped reality and reduce sensitivity to depth. Predatory compression is dense and somatically effective, but its fuel is collective semantic capital and its ledger externalizes cost while privatizing benefit. Witness compression is also dense and effective, but its source is visible private bearing-cost and its output preserves a path back to the suppressed reality, enriching the commons rather than depleting it.

The formal vocabulary includes semantic density, temporal compression, propagation, back-projection yield, bearing-cost source, fuel type, ledger structure, join strength, amplification apparatus, net commons effect, and hijackability. The paper carefully distinguishes molecular recoverability from molar social effect: a compression can be recoverable yet have little public consequence, or irrecoverable and massively amplified.

The work rereads the Liberatory Operator Set as a typology of ways compression externalizes cost. It also emphasizes iteration: repeated predatory exposure can lower resistance, repeated witness compression can build diagnostic literacy, and repeated lossy compression can habituate a field to thinness.

The phrase “semiotic thermodynamics” is formal-poetic and diagnostic. The variables are ordinal and comparative, with paradigmatic cases rather than universal numeric thresholds. The record is authoritative for the three-regime distinction and fuel-plus-ledger transfer law, not for a calibrated physical science of meaning.
