The entry, short

Encyclopedia of anti-Revisionism On-Line is an online encyclopedia of the anti-revisionist movements' own documents and periodicals, filed by country and by the periods of their splits and collapse, in the Marxists Internet Archive's History section; its goal is "to document this trend" (contents page; What is Anti-Revisionism, 2009; census record).

🗂️ What it holds

  • Background: a brief definition; materials from China and Albania (contents page).
  • By country: 26 sections, the United States in three waves and the New Communist Movement (contents page; census record).
  • By its splits: "Crises, Splits and More New Parties", "Anti-Dogmatists Unable to Unite", "Collapse and Aftermath"; Canada's collapse; Britain's "Ebbing Tide" (contents page).
  • Periodicals: by country, some named by their splits, running to 2011 (periodicals page).

📉 The trend

  • The term: opposition to revising revolutionary fundamentals; a post-war trend in the pro-Soviet movement (2009 page).
  • Its course: 1956, the defence of Stalin, Chinese and Albanian alignments, the Sino-Soviet split, decline after Mao (2009 page).
  • A minority: "defined against 'revisionism'" (census record).

🧭 Whose record

  • Kept: the movements' documents "in the encyclopedia's own periodization" (census record).
  • Reading: "An organ that keeps a defeated tendency's record"; "the losing side of the losing side" (census record).
  • Organ, holder, route: what a defeated critique keeps if it is to be taken up later (#1725).
  • SDS: #1725's record of the 1969 expulsion is a document EROL holds, "the winning side wrote it down"; the expelled party's paper is listed too (#1725; periodicals page).

🔒 Holder and terms

  • Holder: an EROL Administrator, under the MIA; terms unstated (contents page; census record).
  • Host: volunteer, non-profit, politically independent; public domain unless noted (MIA introduction; Wikipedia).
  • Harvard's limit: "has not been found to meet all criteria for legitimacy" (Harvard Digital Handbook).
  • Unstated: limits, gathering method, completeness, the administrator (census record).

📋 Reception

  • Wikipedia: a redirect to the Marxists Internet Archive, whose article does not name it (Wikipedia).
  • At the name: subject and range held; the splits "not in the composition" (Capture Registry, 2026-10-31).
  • Period: Harvard dates it 1946-1983, the census to the 1980s; the periodicals run to 2011 (Harvard; census record; periodicals page).
