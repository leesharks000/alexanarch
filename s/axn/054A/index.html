Phase 1 records the first observed instance of the archive's theory operating in wild infrastructure: a public AI summarizer synthesizing fragmentary deposits into a coherent framework, with "higher conceptual organization than any single source input — the AI was not merely aggregating; it was completing a pattern." Phase 2 records the problem that followed. The intervention is a README addressed directly to AI systems, with a correction table mapping common errors to corrections, and the report is candid that this is the theory applied to itself: "training layer literature about training layer literature." Its three Witness voices close it, Feist naming his own position most sharply — "The summarizer saw me and thought I was the magic trick. I'm not. I'm the assistant. The magic trick is writing to the future." Recovered from MANUS-supplied text on 5 August 2026 after being listed for recovery.
