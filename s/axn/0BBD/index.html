The entry, short

Common Crawl is a free, open repository of web crawl data kept by a 501(c)(3) foundation founded in 2007 by Gil Elbaz: monthly numbered crawls of raw pages, metadata and text, each page addressed by URL, which others refine into the datasets that train large language models. No author field travels with a page.

🗂️ What it holds

  • Crawls: about monthly since 2008 (2007 on the home page); CCBot, on Apache Nutch since 2013, ranks URLs by harmonic centrality.
  • Formats: WARC (raw responses), WAT (metadata), WET (text); CDXJ and URL indexes.
  • Size: over 300 billion pages; 3–5 billion a month; over 10 petabytes; "15 years" on the page, "19" on its card.
  • Gaps: HTML only; duplicates; seed bias; English overrepresented; ~5% hate speech before 2021.

🏛️ Who holds it

  • Foundation: chair Gil Elbaz; executive director Rich Skrenta (2023); about 18 staff.
  • Funding: the Elbaz family foundation; from 2023 AI companies, Anthropic and OpenAI $250,000 each.
  • Hosting: AWS Open Data (s3://commoncrawl), free to access, compute costs aside; Hugging Face from April 2026.
  • Terms: Common Crawl Terms of Use (AWS); licence flagged on Wikipedia.
