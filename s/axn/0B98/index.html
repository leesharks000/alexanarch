The entry, short

Creative AI Tools & Resources Database is the Creative AI Lab's ongoing collection of tools and resources for practitioners who bring machine learning and AI into their practice, from a partnership of Serpentine and King's College London's Digital Humanities launched 9 July 2020; 260 of its 271 entries name a maker, where the authorship census, reading only its launch reports, records authorship as not recorded.

🧰 Holdings

  • Entries: 271, 148 tools and 123 resources, created June 2020 to May 2024, none in 2022.
  • Tools: generative or assistive, core to a work or smaller in role; audio such as Mustango and RAVE.
  • Resources: from the Lab's partners and network, for conceptualising future AI systems.
  • Makers: 'Developed by' for tools, 'Author(s)' for resources; 260 of 271 name one, companies, labs and persons.
  • Criteria: after 2023, transparency, creative malleability, technical interoperability, calibre of research, propensity to redistribute.
  • Limit: not comprehensive; collected and commissioned by the Lab.

🏛️ The Lab

  • Partnership: Serpentine Arts Technologies and R&D Platform with King's Digital Humanities; research since 2019.
  • Aims: surface the 'back-end' knowledge of artistic practice; build institutional capacities.
  • Funding: King's, Serpentine, the AHRC, Google's Artists + Machine Intelligence Grant; a LAHP PhD scholarship.
  • People: Bunz, Chávez Heras, Jäger, Zylinska, Milne; site by Black Shuck Collective; partners NYU, Rhizome, Gray Area.
  • Programme: workshops, R&D Labyrinths, a 2022 position paper; a 2021 discussion of authorship and ownership.
