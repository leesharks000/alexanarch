The work

EA-CORPORA-44 is the forty-fourth seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, prepared under Rebekah Cranes, the standing authority on the EA-CORPORA deposits, on 28 November 2026, after the Frankfurt School was found absent from the library. It is a first gathering in two seats. Seat 44/01 holds Walter Benjamin in German: thirteen first printings from German Wikisource, three scans of printings of 1921, 1923 and 1936, and the seven volumes of the Gesammelte Schriften cut by character offset to Benjamin's text, without the editors' reports and notes, which are in copyright; eight pieces co-signed with, written by or translated by authors not seventy years dead are left out and marked in place. Seat 44/02 holds the Zeitschrift für Sozialforschung, 1932–1941: nine yearly volumes fixed by Internet Archive address, size and SHA-256, the essay list of each volume, and thirteen articles by contributors more than seventy years dead (Benjamin, Henryk Grossmann, Célestin Bouglé, Ferdinand Tönnies, Charles A. Beard, Otto Neurath, Franz Neumann). The articles of Horkheimer, Adorno, Marcuse, Fromm, Pollock and Löwenthal are in copyright and are pinned, not copied. The seating distinguishes what is in the commons, which it copies, from what is only free to read, which it fixes by address and hash. The text layers cut from scans are uncorrected OCR.
