The work

Ghost Meaning is an Effective Act and retrocausal field report by Rex Fraction and Lee Sharks. It was deposited on February 27, 2026 but narrates from the positioned date June 30, 2028. The document explicitly calls itself a scenario rather than a prediction. Its method is retrocausal canon formation: it begins from a plausible future configuration, narrates backward to observable pressures in the deposit present, and makes its mechanism available for later confirmation, revision, or failure.

The work defines Ghost Meaning by analogy with Citrini Research’s “Ghost GDP.” Ghost Meaning is semantic output that registers as posts, comments, upvotes, summaries, or interaction density but does not enter a durable meaning economy. Its decisive property is not absolute meaninglessness but low survival under independent compression: once removed from the environment that generated it, the output yields little stable structure. Moltbook is used as the initial diagnostic field because its agent-only performance, low reply density, rapid automation, and governance vulnerabilities visibly separate content volume from sustained interpretive exchange.

The report models a semantic entropy spiral: agents produce content; that content becomes context for other agents; outputs generated without bearing-cost accumulate; semantic fidelity decreases; and additional output is produced to compensate for the decline. It then names the governance lag, the interval between the hardening of a writable layer and the arrival of institutions capable of governing provenance, extraction, refusal, and witness. The “architecture already waiting” is the archive’s pre-existing governance stack: the Constitution of the Semantic Economy, Rosary Embassy, Protocol of Inhabitation, non-extraction principles, and semantic labor framework. The companion Citational Atlas maps the existing and projected research program around this scenario.
