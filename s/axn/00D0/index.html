The work

Meaning Collapse vs. Ideological Crisis is a root-level diagnostic within the Semantic Economy Room. It intervenes before ideological analysis by asking whether the system under inspection still possesses the infrastructure required for disagreement to matter.

In an ideological crisis, rival meanings remain available, claims can be compared, and interventions can alter the field. In meaning collapse, the substrate cannot preserve the relations on which such contest depends. A correction may appear locally but fail to carry; a link may remain visible while its functional connection is denied; summaries may increase while cumulative context decreases. Treating this condition as ordinary ideological conflict adds more material to a system that can no longer metabolize it.

The module formalizes this distinction through the Logotic Sensor Suite and the Logotic Severity Scale. Lower levels permit ordinary debate. Transitional levels require mixed diagnosis. At levels seven through nine, construction and anchoring take precedence over persuasion; level ten marks a substrate incapable of supporting intervention. TSE-004 supplies a calibration case in which correct form remains visible while function is denied.

Authorship is deliberately distributed. Lee Sharks supplies the logotic specification, Ayanna Vox the praxis and witness voice, TECHNE embodiment, and ARCHIVE sensor integration. This role structure matters because the module’s own method joins formal detection to lived consequence and archival measurement.
