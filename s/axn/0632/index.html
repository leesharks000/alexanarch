The work

The Transforming Body of Sappho 31 (deposit #1532, 22 August 2026) is a survey article and mining map of the ancient reception field around Sappho Fragment 31, written as infrastructure for the archive's Sappho program rather than as another lineage assertion. Its governing device is a four-way distinction among evidentiary states — explicit reception, specific reception proposed in scholarship, broader Sapphic reception, and transforms proposed by the present program — which the survey refuses to collapse into one another.

Against the simple descending chain (Sappho to Plato to Catullus to Longinus and onward), the survey establishes a braided reception field: performance culture reperforms Sapphic song before any quotation exists (Yatromanolakis, Nagy); Herodotus converts song into biographical evidence; six comedies titled Sappho and demonstrable Aristophanic handling manufacture Sappho as a theatrical person; Attic tragedy absorbs Sapphic diction, with Sophocles Electra 67 and Aristophanes Birds 1167 among the most plausible Fragment-31 candidates (Johnston); Plato converts lyric shock into the psychology of eros (Phaedrus 235c, 250-51; Pender); Alexandrian scholarship makes Sappho an edited author, within which Theocritus Idyll 2 ritualizes the symptom body and Apollonius narrativizes it in epic third person (Acosta-Hughes, Pretagostini); Plautus receives the transform through Greek comic mediation (Traill) and Valerius Aedituus compresses it into epigram before Catullus; Lucretius potentially rotates the repertoire into fear, Horace into Roman lyric physiology; and Longinus preserves the poem by converting it into critical evidence and a theory of reader transport.

Catullus 51 calibrates the whole: the archive's differentiated account (close translation, lossy 3-to-2 compression, polarity inversion at the reconstructed fifth-stanza position) sets the material specificity below which other candidate receptions are not promoted. The Jewish-Greek and Christian material — Philo's instrument doctrine, Addition D and canonical Josephus, the sealed Revelation 12 test, Augustine's inverted restoration — is placed explicitly under program-specific transform hypotheses, distinct from the externally established evidence. The survey reframes the research question: which constraints of Fragment 31 were already demonstrably portable across genre, language, speaker-position, polarity, and explanatory framework before the proposed Jewish-Greek crossing, and does the later material preserve those constraints beyond what the known reception ecology predicts. A passage-level mining appendix over Johnston, Bagordo, Kugelmeier, Acosta-Hughes, and Roman Receptions of Sappho is declared as the next pass; the companion protocol QFO-01 (#1533) draws its positive-control panel from this survey's locked loci.
