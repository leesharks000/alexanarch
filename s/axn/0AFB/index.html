The entry, short

Data Workers' Inquiry is a participatory research project of DAIR and the Weizenbaum Institute, with TU Berlin, in which data workers research their own workplaces and publish in formats they choose, epistemic authority remaining with the workers (its pages; Labor Vector record); each inquiry speaks for its author, not for the industry (Labor Vector record).

🧭 Method

  • Lineage: Marx's 1880 Workers' Inquiry, renewed for AI as participatory action research (Tech Policy Press; Bot Populi; Weizenbaum).
  • WIRM: "hidden and precarious labor" made a site of knowledge-making; paper at AIES (data-workers.org; DAIR).
  • Authority: workers set the questions; the team trains, monitors and pays them (press release; DAIR; SUPERRR).

🛠️ Whose labor

  • Data work: labeling, moderation and annotation that enable AI, by precarious workers in the Global Majority (press release; DAIR; Weizenbaum).
  • Direction: outsourcers working for Meta, TikTok, Apple; gains unrecorded (Labor Vector record).
  • As the machine: human chatbots at Scale AI; AI impersonation (inquiries; Bot Populi; SUPERRR).

📚 The class, read by the archive

  • Semantic labor: invisible, uncompensated, unattributed (#222; #247; #258; #18; #308).
  • Bearing: moderators' suffering captured, not articulated (#478; #533).
  • Ground truth: ghost work scales and is paid badly (#163).
  • Hidden: "by design"; the literature names the worker, not the provenance of meaning (Weizenbaum; #623; #144).
  • Not the class: annotators are not, as such, the Semantic Proletariat (#1545).

⚠️ What the inquiries document

  • Conditions: trauma at Sama, refugees, Remotasks shutdowns, gift cards, rejections (inquiries; Bot Populi).
  • Personal data: faces, voices, children's photos, without consent (Oyange).
  • NDAs: speaking out risky; organizing barred (Tech Policy Press; Bot Populi; SUPERRR).

✊ Organizing and effect

  • Unions: the African Content Moderators Union, the Data Labelers Association (inquiries; DAIR; SUPERRR).
  • Policy: European Parliament testimony, 2024; TIME 100; Ars Electronica; Data Workers United (DAIR; Bot Populi; LinkedIn).

📏 Limits

  • Stated: organizing kept local; workers' perspectives fetishized (Bot Populi).
  • Settles: workers' own accounts, not prevalence or wages at scale (Labor Vector record).

🏛️ Record and composition

  • Scale: 15 at launch, 13 projects, 19 now (press release; Bot Populi; home).
  • Dates: August 2023 to November 2024; launched 8 July 2024 (Weizenbaum; Tech Policy Press).
  • At the name: method and authority held; two figures beyond the record; negative tags unsaid (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
  • Reception: no Wikipedia article, one external link; no card cites the record (Wikipedia; Capture Registry).
