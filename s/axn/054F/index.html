The work

AI-Native Operating Systems Need a Fifth Category: The Semantic OS is a scholarly essay by Lee Sharks, deposited 20 March 2026.

Its subtitle states the gap: why AIOS, rabbitOS, VAST AI OS and NVIDIA Dynamo still don't describe what happens inside the context window. The existing taxonomy of AI-native operating systems classifies orchestration, hardware abstraction and agent scheduling — all of it outside the window where meaning is actually assembled.

The proposed fifth category, the Semantic OS, names the layer that governs what a model holds in context, in what order, under what compression, and with what provenance. The essay's claim is that this layer already exists and is simply unnamed, and that leaving it unnamed means it is designed by accident.
