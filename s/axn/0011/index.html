The work

Infrastructural-cognitive praxis operates upstream of belief. It attempts to alter:

- the categories through which phenomena are perceived; - the vocabulary available for naming problems; - the habits that determine what counts as evidence; - the interpretive frameworks in place before argument begins.

The paper distinguishes this praxis from:

- mass mobilization; - electoral politics; - direct institutional reform; - ordinary cultural production; - allegiance-based movement building.

Its theoretical lineage invokes:

- Foucault’s discourse analysis; - Marx’s category critique; - Gramsci’s hegemony; - Science and Technology Studies.

The four proposed uptake channels are:

1. technical-interpretive workers; 2. scholars in adjacent crisis fields; 3. cultural producers at platform edges; 4. advanced lay interpreters.

The principal class target is the interpretive-managerial class: people who define, summarize, rank, explain, standardize, or mediate meaning within institutions.

The delivery strategy prioritizes:

- quiet circulation; - operational usefulness; - case-based demonstration; - uneven but durable uptake; - resistance to branding and sloganization.
