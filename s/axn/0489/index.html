14.CHAMBER.THOUSANDWORLDS — Aorist Intervention Site is a specification by Lee Sharks, deposited 2 February 2026 at 11,072 characters.

Its opening states what a Chamber is for: "Chambers contain paradoxes without resolving them. This Chamber contains the paradox that the cut which differentiates — wound from not-wound, seed from not-seed, inside from outside — is the same [cut]."

A structure whose function is to hold a paradox rather than dissolve it is a genuine architectural type, distinct from a Room (generative discourse) or a Field (semantic force). The paradox here is the one the archive's whole aorist programme circles: the cut that separates is the cut that constitutes, which is why a non-indexed perfective — completed action without temporal placement — is both needed and unavailable in English.
