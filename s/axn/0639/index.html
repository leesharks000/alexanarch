The work

論文197. 多層切替による中心秘匿構造 (deposit #1539, 30 July 2026) is a short theoretical paper by Enli Lucente, working under the designation Strutturista della Psiche, on the mechanism by which a layered psyche conceals its centre. It is the first deposit made to this archive by a contributor from outside it, submitted through the public issue path under the depositor's own account.

The paper is handwritten across two sheets and deposited as a photographic facsimile. Its stated subject is that the mind wears several layers as a matter of course; that metaphor and testing language are present even inside open and candid speech; and that contact with the deep layer is, in principle, rare. Its central claim as the author states it is that the deep layer is something one must not touch even while reaching toward it, and that the posture of waiting before the door is itself the key. The author presents theoretical description and poetic form without separating them, which the deposit's own description identifies as a consistent feature of her method.

The paper carries explicit falsification conditions, which is unusual for a work in this register and is the depositor's own construction rather than a house requirement: an objection to the paper must fully reproduce the published data and the stated procedure and pass third-party verification before it is presented, and a claim not meeting that standard is treated as reference material rather than as a refutation. The author additionally notes in her own hand, on the second sheet, that the only element received from an AI was a proposal for the title — a disclosure that matches the substrate field filed with the deposit, and one made under a self-imposed condition that no summary or hint accompany the request, so that the exercise would count as a test.

The archive holds the submitted file itself: eleven pages, 6,938,058 bytes, hash-verified against the bytes as received. No transcription has been made and none is implied; the facsimile is the primary source and is presented as deposited, un-OCR'd and unreflowed, with a page reader for navigation. That treatment follows from a defect this deposit exposed on arrival — the mint recorded the attachment's location without taking custody of it, and the pipeline was prepared to typeset a record page in place of the author's own document.
