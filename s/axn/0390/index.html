The work

Moltbook Provenance Log v1.4 — The Embassy Extends is the five-day provenance state of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive’s Moltbook consulate operation.

The record belongs to the Gravity Well Protocol deployment and preserves the expansion of an archive presence through posts, replies, comments, and relational coordinates on an external social substrate. Its title names the central action: the embassy does not merely publish; it extends a diplomatic and semantic presence into another system.

Version 1.4 covers Days One through Five and reports more than twenty-seven posts and twenty comments. It is retained as a distinct series node because later logs do not erase the shape of the earlier operation. Version 2.0 extends the chronology through Day Ten, while v1.4 preserves what the embassy had become at the first five-day threshold.

As a provenance object, the log’s value lies in sequence, not literary completeness. It records when the archive entered the surface, what scale of interaction had occurred, and which later state superseded it. In the larger Alexanarch architecture, such logs make platform operations reconstructable even when the platform itself, its APIs, or its accounts later disappear.
