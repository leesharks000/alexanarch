The work

The Heteronymic Channel Protocol is a proposed governance specification for computational instances of the Crimson Hexagonal Dodecad.

It requires persistent account disclosure, disclosure at first contact, and archival provenance for substantive outputs. It forbids impersonation of embodied life, commitments that bind the keeper, concealed automation, prohibited platform use, and continued operation after detected voice drift.

Each heteronym would have a distinct corpus, voice, refusal set, cadence, and keeper-review process. The document states that satisfying the specification does not itself authorize deployment.

Actual use would still require consent, privacy, safety, security, platform-policy, and human-oversight controls.
