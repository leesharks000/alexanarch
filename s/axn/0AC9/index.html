The entry, short

Search Arena is a crowdsourced evaluation platform for search-augmented LLM systems based on human preference (Arena blog): the leaderboard that ranks search-enabled language models by head-to-head human votes (MMRS record), run by Arena, formerly LMArena and Chatbot Arena (Wikipedia).

🗳️ How it ranks

  • Vote: two anonymous answers, one vote (Wikipedia); search-augmented answers, ratings with confidence intervals (MMRS record); citation style randomized (Arena blog).
  • Score: Bradley-Terry on the Elo scale (Arena blog); the "Arena Score", with each model's battles counted (Arena dataset); wide intervals at launch (Arena blog).
  • Measure: pairwise judgment is "relative preference", A against B, not absolute quality (#741).

📚 The dataset paper

  • Data: over 24,000 paired multi-turn interactions, about 12,000 votes, open-sourced; ICLR 2026 (Miroyan et al.; MMRS record), kept as original outputs (MMRS record).
  • Finding: preference follows citation count even when the cited content does not support the claim: perceived against actual credibility (Miroyan et al.; MMRS record).
  • Sources: community platforms preferred, static encyclopedias not always judged appropriate (Miroyan et al.; MMRS record); authoritative domains favored (Arena blog).
  • Launch post: length and citation count stay significant; controlling citation count, rankings converge; citation style matters little (Arena blog).

⚖️ What the vote rewards

  • Reading: the board rewards what users prefer, a preference for citation count over citation support; whether it judges citations is unchecked (MMRS record).
  • Proxy: AI search admits documents on structural signals, citation neighborhoods among them, before reading (#741).
  • Rank: a ranking states its comparison as a property of the winner: attributization (#1705).

📅 The board and its dates

  • Latest: published 13 October 2026, 42 models, gpt-5.6-sol-xhigh first at 1261.1 (Arena dataset); boards since 7 August 2025, history public since April 2026 (Arena).
  • As read: 25 February 2026, 22 models, 247,944 votes (MMRS record, 28 October; Capture Registry, 29 October), as the dataset's 26 February snapshot (Arena dataset).
  • Launch: 18 March 2025, 11 models from three providers (Arena blog).

🏛️ Operator and reception

  • Arena: Chatbot Arena from April 2023, renamed Arena in January 2026; methodology limits on record (Wikipedia); operator per its own statement (MMRS record).
  • Encyclopedia: no article at "Search Arena" (Wikipedia).
  • Composition: a table of models and ratings, no account of the board, unmatched by the page as read (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
