The entry, short

Irish Left Archive is an online archive of Irish left politics, begun in 2007 by the Cedar Lounge Revolution blog and kept by its co-curators Ciarán Swan and Aonghus Storey, holding the documents of hundreds of organisations, rivals and splinters side by side under their own names, linked to no party (its pages; maryleelabor record).

🗂️ The holdings

  • Counts: 1231 documents, 436 organisations, 875 people; a timeline of organisations from 1900; search by section and years (home page; record).
  • Scope: "materials from all on the left", and beyond it; "large gaps still remain" (About page; record).
  • Collections: the British left on Ireland, Wood Quay, the Hunger Strikes; documents from 20 demonstrations (home page; record).
  • Accounts: participants' testimony, which "depend on contribution" (home page; record).

🏛️ Who holds it

  • Origin: the Cedar Lounge Revolution, 2007; a database site from 2013; documents still discussed on the CLR (About page; home page).
  • Curators: Ciarán Swan and Aonghus Storey, who also host the podcast; the record names no operator beyond the origin, its About source unread (About page; record).
  • Terms: open to all, linked to no party; fair use, copyright with the authors, attribution asked (About; Copyright; record).
  • Submissions: donated, some by parties, added weekly with commentary (Content Submissions; record).

⚖️ The losing side

  • Side by side: rivals' documents under their own names; splits are no category of the saved text (home page; record; Capture Registry).
  • Organ, holder, route: synthesis followed where a defeated critique kept all three (#1725); the record reads the archive as such an organ (record).
  • Unmeasured: no cross-organisation dataset of splits was found (#1725); how a dispute was settled lies beyond the documents (record).
