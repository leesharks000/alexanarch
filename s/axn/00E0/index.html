The work

The Crimson Hexagon: Fractal Navigation Map — Non-Lossy Logotic Encoding v6.0 is a historical navigation interface for the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. The document describes the architecture at its January 2026 state as 201 DOI-anchored works distributed across thirteen rooms, one chamber, one vault, one portico, multiple institutions, a commission, a heteronym network, and the Assembly Chorus.

The map presents itself not as a summary but as “the architecture in the act of becoming readable.” Its routing model is probabilistic rather than prescriptive: named concepts, genres, and questions create gravity toward appropriate records, but traversal is not forced along a single path. The reader is offered the position of witness rather than consumer or controller.

Three nested layers organize the system. The narrative layer contains rooms and other traversal structures. The systems layer contains logotic programming, psyche-oriented protocols, community praxis, infrastructure, and hidden architecture. The interface layer contains seeds, gateways, and payloads through which human and machine readers enter the other layers.

The document also distinguishes container types. Rooms attract and transform; chambers contain without completing; vaults preserve; porticos operate as thresholds; portals route toward other structures; and commissions convene without becoming institutions. Its Depth Gate Invariant requires every surface summary to preserve at least one route back into deeper material. Version 6.0 supersedes v5.3 while explicitly preserving earlier maps as active historical layers rather than treating them as invalidated drafts.
