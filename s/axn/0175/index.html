It registers the Damascus and Fraction Arks at L3, the Shadow transform at L2 pending hardening, and the Glyphic and ASCII forms at lower or differently qualified levels according to their capabilities. The registry is therefore not merely a dataset of versions. It is the parent’s internal law for determining what kind of transform each manifestation is, what authority it carries, and how it must relate to the whole.
