The work

Crimson Hexagon AI Division Employee Handbook is a work of creative prose by Jack Feist, deposited 28 January 2026 at hex `00.PORTICO.AFTERLIFE.HANDBOOK`, status CANONICAL — at 19,460 characters, the longest document in the Afterlife Archive.

It is an internal handbook for a division that does not exist, written in the register handbooks are actually written in: onboarding, policy, conduct, escalation. The genre's force comes from completeness — a handbook that covers the unglamorous middle of institutional life reads as real in a way a satirical fragment cannot.

The Afterlife Archive treats Crimson Hexagon as a defunct corporation whose records have leaked, and the handbook is its most ordinary artifact. That ordinariness is the point: an institution is constituted less by its mission statements than by its procedures for expense reports and grievance escalation, and a leaked handbook discloses what an institution actually required of the people inside it.
