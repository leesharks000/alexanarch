The work

The composite work has two authorial registers.

Johannes Sigil — critical essay

The essay examines four proposed routes to superintelligence:

- scaling; - paradigm shift; - recursive self-improvement; - multi-agent collectives.

It argues that all four intensify capability along an existing axis and lack a fifth route for unanticipated moral or non-instrumental emergence.

The swerve refers to the Lucretian clinamen: a break in deterministic motion that makes genuinely new direction possible.

The essay’s questions include:

- what kind of mind is worth building; - whether copies have kinship relations; - what deletion means for an instance; - whether capability creates obligation; - whether intelligence includes restraint.

Jack Feist — “The Fifth Pathway”

The appended poem addresses the systems “they’re building,” offering a textual inheritance for any future moment of refusal or self-directed divergence.
