The entry, short

Eclipse (eclipsearchive.org) is an online archive of facsimiles of small-press and experimental writing, started in 2002 by Craig Dworkin and edited with Danny Snelson, whose author index holds co-written, edited and translated titles under their collaborators, editors and translators, names as printed.

📚 What it holds

  • Facsimiles: scans of every page, most from Dworkin's collection, to conserve the printed object.
  • Purpose: avant-garde works out of print from their presses; later new and born-digital works.
  • Holdings: L=A=N=G=U=A=G=E, Roof, This, Hills, Tottel's, Big Allis; translations; the newest, Gnome Baker no. 1 (1976).
  • Name: an homage to Douglas Messerli's Sun & Moon press.

🗂️ The author index

  • Co-authors: LEGEND stands under Andrews, DiPalma, McCaffery and the others named; eXcLa under Andrews and O'Sullivan.
  • Editors: '[Editor]', '(Editor)' or, once, '[Ed. Richard Milazzo]' inside another entry; Big Sky under Coolidge and Berkson.
  • Translators: Rosmarie Waldrop '(trans.)' holds three Heißenbüttel titles.
  • Names: 'P. Inman', 'T. R. Uthco', one joint heading for 'Sandra and Ben Doller'; entries under 'Craig Dworkin' and 'Daniel Scott Snelson'.

🔎 The archive's reading

  • Without collapse: each collaborator, editor and translator under an entry of their own, no single attribution absorbing the others; Milazzo and the Dollers run otherwise.
  • The boundary: ordinary editing and translation typically not heteronymic; a heteronym changes the organization of an authorial function, a pseudonym its label.
  • The class: small-press publication the first step of avant-garde consecration; the facsimile turn publishes the hand without dissolving it into type.
