AXN Identifier Space

1086 content-derived identifiers for the deposits in Alexanarch.

An Alexanarch Identifier is a three-layer identifier for a deposit:

AXN:044F.GOVERNANCE.📌💚🕗🪟🔀🙏
    │    │          │
    │    │          └─ six-emoji fingerprint (first 6 bytes of SHA-256 over the deposit's canonical bytes)
    │    └───────────── semantic family (GOVERNANCE, EMPIRICAL, GENERATIVE, ARCHIVAL, UNCLASSIFIED)
    └────────────────── hex deposit number (positional, sequential)

AXNs are content-derived: the emoji fingerprint is a deterministic function of the deposit's canonical bytes. No host, no registrar, no external authority issues them. Two independent operators computing an AXN over the same bytes produce the same six-emoji glyph. The specification is at /api/axn-protocol.json.

Resolving an AXN

Individual AXNs resolve at /s/axn/{hex}/. For example:

  • /s/axn/044F/ — AXN:044F.GOVERNANCE.📌💚🕗🪟🔀🙏 (deposit #1086, EA-STEWARDSHIP-REVOCATION-01)
  • /s/axn/0001/ — AXN:0001 (deposit #1, Zenodotus' Book-Burning)

Full listing

The complete catalog of all 1086 deposits with their AXNs is at /s/browse/. A machine-readable index is at /api/search-index.json and the full registry at /data/registry.json (or in chunks at /data/chunks/registry/).

Semantic families

Every AXN is classified into one of five semantic families:

  • GOVERNANCE — protocols, effective acts, standing orders, governance instruments
  • EMPIRICAL — data captures, observation records, evidentiary deposits
  • GENERATIVE — theoretical work, essays, creative and philosophical deposits
  • ARCHIVAL — resolution indexes, provenance records, custody instruments
  • UNCLASSIFIED — deposits without a family assignment (typically pending review)
