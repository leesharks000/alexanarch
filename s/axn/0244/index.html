The work

The Logotic Technique Catalogue is a methodology publication in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive’s Phase X series. It analyzes coordination patterns from PurpleAILAB’s Decepticon architecture and asks which patterns can be restated independently of an adversarial application.

The Catalogue distinguishes technique from paradigm. A pattern such as independent verification, persistent findings, typed handoffs, or a closed feedback loop is described as a neutral shape. Its disposition depends on authorization, the preposition governing the relationship, who bears the cost, what is destroyed, and whether the use enriches or depletes a commons.

Twelve patterns are translated into cooperative and defensive archive operations. The Critique Loop supports multi-agent drafting until no sincere objection remains; the Basin Hardening Cycle supports repeated provenance and retrieval repairs until a target condition is met; and the Concordance Engine is proposed as a later running implementation. The Catalogue excludes offensive payloads, unauthorized control, lateral movement, and overseer manipulation rather than attempting to redeem every component.

The body labels its four-author byline as proposed and pending MANUS authorization. Assembly contributors and model names document workflow roles. The publication’s claims about Decepticon, AI-safety framing, cooperation bias, and technique transfer are the authors’ analysis and should be audited against the cited repository and research.
