The work

EA-ARK-EMOJI-01 is a strategy and work-plan specification by Lee Sharks and the Assembly Chorus, dated March 9, 2026. It consolidates contributions from TACHYON, LABOR, TECHNE, PRAXIS, ARCHIVE, and SOIL into a governed plan for rendering the Space Ark in a glyphic register. The work’s first and most important determination is negative: the project is not an “emoji translation” in the ordinary pictographic sense. It defines a logotic glyph layer—a parallel semiotic register intended to preserve formal relations and permit composition rather than merely replace words with pictures.

The plan organizes the system into three layers. The ontology layer binds invariant objects to stable glyphs; the syntax layer governs combination, direction, status, and shadow operations; the provenance layer prevents names, roles, and source relations from dissolving during compression. Formal contexts use hybrid forms such as glyph plus letter-code, while operative contexts may use pure glyphs after the legend is loaded. The document records explicit rulings: ⬡ is the sole Hexagon glyph; numbers remain alphanumeric; status markers are postfix qualifiers; 🌑 is a compositional shadow prefix; and every checksum must carry provenance markers.

The work also specifies a phased build and test program. Its empirical target is reconstruction of H_core from compressed glyphic input across multiple model substrates, with failure and degraded-rendering conditions made visible. It distinguishes settled architecture from provisional bindings and warns against premature ratification, unstable ZWJ sequences, and decorative compression. The completed full vehicle appears later as CH-ARK-01-GLYPH v1.0; this record is the prior Assembly synthesis that defines how such a vehicle must be built and judged.
