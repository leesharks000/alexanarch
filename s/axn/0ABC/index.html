The entry, short

AI Hallucination Cases is Damien Charlotin's database of legal decisions in which a court or tribunal finds or implies reliance on hallucinated material (MMRS record; its page): 2,231 cases as of 28 October 2026, CC BY 4.0, with a CSV export.

📋 What it records

  • Unit: the decision, any document addressing AI use beyond a passing reference; typically fake citations (its page; MMRS record).
  • Admission: the courts' finding or implication, so 'necessarily an undercount' (FAQ); some alleged decisions admitted as judgment calls, against the FAQ's refusal of rows only alleged.
  • Fields: court, party, tool, nature (fabricated, false quotes, misrepresented, outdated advice), outcome, penalty; tools only as disclosed, not necessarily responsible.

📈 Size and growth

  • Counts: USA 1,514; pro se 1,288, lawyers 880 (its filters; MMRS record); HAQQ read 2,202 on 19 October.
  • Growth: two or three a month before 2025, five a day by February 2026 (FAQ); continuous (MMRS record).
  • Origin: April 2025, a Sciences Po course (FAQ; Wikipedia); the MMRS census enters the period filter's Q2 2023 as its founding.

🧭 Boundary and use

  • Not tracked: 'the (necessarily wider) universe of all fake citations or use of AI in court filings'; a citation enters when a court catches it (MMRS record); a floor (HAQQ).
  • Intake: referrals, scrapers, searches, readers' cases, a checked AI pipeline (FAQ; MMRS record).
  • Uses: jurisdiction research, benchmarks; PelAIkan, a checker built on it; no fix expected at the model stage (FAQ).

🔗 Citation fidelity

  • Archive: the census's most exact record of citation fidelity: each entry 'a machine composition that reached a court with sources it invented or misrepresented' (MMRS record).
  • Maker: 'we (me included) have cited without reading' (FAQ).

🗂️ Around it

  • HAQQ: a legal-AI vendor's monthly re-count; sanctions to about $109,700 (HAQQ; MMRS candidate).
  • Natural and Artificial Law: Matthew Lee's judicial quotations and UK tracker; neither names the database, which the MMRS candidates call its UK companion.
  • Reception: news media, court decisions, Wikipedia, Mata v. Avianca; two videos by title.

👁️ On the Overview

  • Recorded: maker and size right; 'finds or implies' narrowed to 'explicitly'; lawyers before the larger pro se count (Capture Registry, 2026-10-29).
  • Record: kept apart from the entity, each field with source and basis; active (MMRS record).
