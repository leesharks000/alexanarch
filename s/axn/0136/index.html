The work

Visual Schema — Maybe Space Baby Garden Lanes is a concise render specification produced under the auspices of Rebekah Cranes and the Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics. It translates the MSBGL charter into a non-representational diagram governed by hearing.

The schema defines three primary axes. The vertical axis is breath, descent, and reception: sound enters a body and passes downward before it becomes interpretation. The horizontal axis is lane, traversal, and return: a listener can move through the room, leave, and return without replaying the original event. The diagonal oblique is Operator // Swerve, the deviation that interrupts a fixed crossing and creates a new relation between the axes.

The intended image is an ancient-future reliquary, dual-axis mandala, and semantic–physical map. It should not depict the couch event literally, turn the room into a fantasy interior, or represent hearing as a conventional audio-player interface. The room’s central problem is that the founding song-event cannot be replayed. The diagram must therefore show how an unrepeatable sound can organize a durable architecture without pretending the architecture contains the event itself.

The brief is natively short and contains a coherent set of render instructions. Registry audit notes nevertheless state that a restoration pass may not have seated all canonical bytes and direct a later reviewer either to recover additional material or mark the record explicitly as a fragment. That open question concerns completeness, not the validity of the instructions that are present.

This record should be cited as the visual authority for MSBGL’s axes and non-representational audial form. It is not another instance of Visual Schema — The Unmade Sign merely because both records begin with “Visual Schema”; their subjects and geometries are distinct.
