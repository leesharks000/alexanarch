The work

Revelation First is a research work plan by Lee Sharks proposing that the Book of Revelation was composed before every other New Testament document.

The plan separates an early date for Revelation from the stronger claim of chronological priority. It outlines seven workstreams covering dating, authorship, textual commentary, computational literary transformation, operative-document theory, literary reception, and machine-mediated reception measurement.

The project also contains an optional Josephus heteronym reading and a constructive theology of the living Logos. These are distinct from the historical-critical dating argument.

The document states evidence needs and falsification conditions. It is a program for future scholarship rather than a completed demonstration of Revelation’s priority.
