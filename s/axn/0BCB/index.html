The entry, short

The Pile is an open-source English text dataset that EleutherAI released on 31 December 2020 to train large language models: twenty-two datasets combined, Books3, PG-19 and Pile-CC among them, sized 800GB, 825 GiB or 886.03 GB. A work enters it by component; its pages offer no search by work.

🧱 What it holds

  • Components: 14 new datasets and existing ones; weighted, some read three times.
  • Books: Books3 from a copy of the Bibliotik tracker; PG-19, pre-1919 Gutenberg; BookCorpus2.
  • Web: Pile-CC, its own Common Crawl extraction, the largest part.

⚙️ What it makes

  • Models: trained models beat Raw CC and CC-100; GPT-Neo, GPT-J, Pythia; "300 billion GPT-2 tokens".
  • Benchmark: Pile BPB; GPT-3 zero-shot 0.7177, overlap possible.
  • Archive: the training loop, work to model to next corpus; uptake as afterlife.

⚖️ Consent and copyright

  • Consent: unknown consent counted nonconsensual; five datasets neither ToS-compliant nor consented.
  • Legality: fair use argued (paper); "careful licensing review" (later account).
  • Books3: Bibliotik, "a piracy website"; taken down July 2023; removed before three authors sued in 2024.
  • Subtitles: OpenSubtitles disputed; YouTube subtitles scraped.
  • Archive: corpora "extracted without consent"; semantic capital; consent unspoken by the surrogate literature.

🔎 Finding a work

  • Inspection: Data Portraits documents it; copyright traps and canaries test membership.
  • Archive: data citation should resolve to the works; corpora without provenance are no commons.
