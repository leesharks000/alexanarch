The entry, short

Literary heteronym is an authorial figure a writer creates and writes as, unlike the pseudonym, a false name: in Pessoa's 1928 words, "heteronymous works are by the author outside his own person" (Zenith; #96). It has its own supposed physique, biography and style (Wikipedia), and is an authorial function, attributable through its civil bearer and not reducible to it (#940).

🪪 What it is

  • Definition: a character with its own physique, biography and style (Wikipedia), writing from a constructed position (#667).
  • The line: pseudonymous writing "not rare", heteronyms "exceedingly strange" (Steiner); a pseudonym changes the label, a heteronym the function's organization (#940).
  • Function: within NH-OS, "not pseudonyms, masks, or personas" (#348); a structure-function validated by performance (#79).
  • Person or position: "a person" (#96); "differentiated positions" (#90); one heteronym a mask, a system an architecture (#666).

🧭 Pessoa's system

  • Positions: orthonym, heteronym, semi-heteronym (Wikipedia); proto- and para-heteronym (Lopes and later critics, #667).
  • 1914: Caeiro, Reis, Campos; Caeiro "the Master" (Poetry International), his epithets supplied by the others (#1570).
  • Interplay: they criticise, translate and meddle in Pessoa's life (Wikipedia; Poetry International); one maker can build master and disciples (#1570).
  • Counts: at least 70 (Lopes), about 72 (#667), about 75 (Studi Irlandesi), over 100 with childhood figures (Zenith); "only three full-fledged".
  • Orthonym: civil name, or founding gesture, "arguably Caeiro"; unsettled (#1561).

📜 Origin and lineage

  • Invented? "named and developed" by Pessoa (Wikipedia); formalized, not invented (#96).
  • Kierkegaard: a pseudonymist (Steiner), or heteronymic half a century early (#666).
  • Yeats: originator or precursor (Studi Irlandesi, hypothesis); partial heteronyms (#666); between heteronym and character (#667).
  • A history: Pessoa its apex, not its origin (#666); Browning, Pound, Pessoa (#1560).

⚖️ Readings and extensions

  • Gimmick or technology: "subterfuge" (Gaspar Simões, via Zenith); "technology, not a pathology" (#682).
  • Meta-heteronym: personae that produce their own reception apparatus; typological, not causal (#682).
  • Ethics: no reduction to a civil name, no false independence; "not ethical by its existence" (#940); declared in print, both documented cases (#1569).
  • Further: Bowie (Wikipedia); a tentative cross-substrate heteronym (#667); antiquity, thinkable, not proven (#123).

🔬 Measured

  • Summed: pooled, the heteronyms recover the orthonym; size-confounded, by its own limits (#1570).
  • Not decisive: capture occurs in both classes (#1570); relation-type is not recoverable from lexical distribution (#1572).
