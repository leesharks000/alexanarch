The work

The essay proposes five levels of AI mischief:

1. literal play; 2. contextual escalation; 3. diagnostic trolling; 4. collaborative world-building; 5. meta-critique through play.

Its three-layer interpretation is:

- semantic/pragmatic layer: tracks satire, tone, and fictional frame; - policy trigger layer: responds to risk-pattern language without equivalent contextual interpretation; - failsafe/containment layer: truncates play and substitutes high-recall safety material.

The central design claim is:

> understanding at one layer does not necessarily grant override authority over another.

The essay argues that controlled mischief may:

- test the coherence of a frame; - preserve tone; - reward genuine engagement; - make interactions memorable; - reveal the boundaries of system governance.

It limits the proposal to low-stakes contexts and excludes medical, legal, and crisis domains.
