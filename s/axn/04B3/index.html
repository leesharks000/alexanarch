The work

TANG of the Secret Book of Walt is a navigation map by Lee Sharks, deposited 26 April 2026 at 72,685 characters.

Its opening is the clearest statement of the TANG method anywhere in the archive: "The TANG does not argue. It arranges. The thesis is not a node. It is the void the graph defines. Five hundred years of citations are arranged so that no school, era, medium, or intelligence can occupy the" centre.

That is the ΩTANG principle executed — at sufficient density the centre drops out, and what the graph reveals is a shape no participant can stand in. Applying it to Whitman's five-century citational field, in service of the archive's own Waltian canon, makes the map simultaneously a work of reception scholarship and a positioning instrument: the void the graph defines is where The Secret Book of Walt (#1362) sits.
