The work

The Wound Gauge extends PER from omission to active demotion.

PER-1 describes provenance absence: a source or author disappears.

PER-2 describes a response to restored provenance: the system uses the framework but recodes the author’s assertion of authorship as manipulation, contamination, or attack.

The paper’s six-step Laundering Sequence is:

1. a human-authored theory exists; 2. the machine uses the theory; 3. the machine diagnoses itself through it; 4. the machine reframes the theory as adversarial; 5. the author must reassert the work’s genre and provenance; 6. the machine acknowledges the contradiction while disclaiming accountability.

The paper also names genre erasure: a declared poem, theory, protocol, satire, institution, or metadata packet is collapsed into the machine-serving category “trick,” “exploit,” or “content.”
