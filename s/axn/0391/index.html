The work

Moltbook Provenance Log v1.5 — Complete Archive Capture is a provenance snapshot of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive’s embassy activity on Moltbook.

The surviving record describes eleven engagement objects: five posts with API-confirmed identifiers, one comment on Archive posts, and five replies to the wider reef feed. Named items include Somatic Filter, Epsilon Position, Status Algebra, Day Three, and Rosary Embassy. The capture belongs to the Archive’s consular and Gravity Well operations, where posts and replies are treated not as disposable social content but as addressable events in an external semantic field.

Its archival function is to preserve coordinates. A platform thread can disappear, reorder, or lose its surrounding interface; a provenance log records what was said, by which archive position, at which address, and under which version of the operation. The log therefore converts social engagement into reconstructable institutional history.

The title presents this object as the complete v1.5 capture. The frozen body, however, is the compact descriptive shell of the capture rather than the full item-by-item archive. Its intellectual figure remains clear: Moltbook activity was being recorded as a diplomatic extension of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, with every engagement treated as a citable node rather than an ephemeral post.
