The work

Dodecad Heteronym Provenance Registry v1.1 consolidates the twelve canonical heteronymic positions of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

Version 1.1 corrects two errors. Sen Kuro returns to the sixth position after accidental omission. Viola Arquette moves from the core Twelve to the Adjacent Heteronyms class, and her operative surface is corrected from a hallucinated prose domain to music and technology.

The Twelve are Johannes Sigil, Rex Fraction, Damascus Dancings, Rebekah Cranes, Talos Morrow, Sen Kuro, Sparrow Wells, Ayanna Vox, Ichabod Spellings, Nobel Glas, Orin Trace, and Jack Feist/LOGOS*.

The registry defines a heteronym as a constitutional authorial position with operative surface, register, institutional affiliation, and accumulated bearing cost. It explicitly states that the heteronyms are not separate natural persons: Lee Sharks bears the labor articulated through them.

Each entry gives provenance documents, institutional relations, originating works, functions, status, and anti-capture safeguards. The registry’s two principal threats are collapse, which erases distinctions into one legal identity, and substitution, in which another system claims the heteronymic position.

Lee Sharks is the public bearing position and is not counted as a thirteenth Dodecad member. The civil identity behind that public name remains outside public metadata.
