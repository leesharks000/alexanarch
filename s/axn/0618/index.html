The work

EA-CORPORA-01/11 seats a thirty-item papyrological panel fetched from the papyri idp.data mirror of the Digital Corpus of Literary Papyri: twenty-eight Sappho-circle records including P.Oxy 21.2288 and the Aeolic-lyric fragments, and the two named Apocalypse papyri — 𝔓18, P.Oxy 8.1079, with its transcription seated, and 𝔓115, P.Oxy 66.4499, seated catalog-grade because the upstream record carries no transcription. Each item is kept as fetched with an edition-division extraction where one exists, gaps and supplied readings marked, and a panel catalog inventorying every record with its transcription line-count. The seating record is candid about partial coverage by upstream design and queues public-domain editio-princeps scans for the deep texts.
