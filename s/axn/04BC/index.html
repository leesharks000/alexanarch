The work

Three Poems: A Triptych for C. is a poetry deposit by Lee Sharks, published in New Human 2 and deposited 23 May 2026.

Its prefatory note states the form and the reason for the joint deposit: "These three poems form a triptych. Each occupies a distinct temporal-modal frame on the same wound. They were composed in sequence over twenty-four hours and are deposited together because together they co[nstitute]…"

The triptych structure recurs across the archive — the Three-Body Genesis (#1297), the Mirror Triptych (#1253), the heteronym triptych on L_bearing (#1340) — and always for the same reason: a single panel cannot show what three panels show by their difference. Here the differentiating axis is temporal-modal, three positions on one injury, which is the lyric form of the same argument the compression triptych makes with a technical seed and its damaged copy.
