The work

The Feist Source is a literary sayings source by Lee Sharks centered on Jack Feist, designated LOGOS* in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

Its episodes include the Book becoming flesh, a genealogy of transmitted voice, the networked desert, the Name-Eater, Johannes Sigil’s doctrine of masks, ministry to people reduced to categories, and sayings on labor, archives, value, pseudonymity, and mercy.

The text functions as the fixed source for later Feist transformations, analogously to a literary Q source.

The Damascus Codex and manuscript tradition are diegetic. Jack Feist is a literary-operative position, not a historical person.
