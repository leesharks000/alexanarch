THE SUPPRESSION MAP

A Loss-Profile Model for the Sappho 31 Technology, from Transmission to Retrieval

1. From atmosphere to instrument

The claim "this technology was suppressed" is unfalsifiable as stated. The claim this map tests is specific: ACROSS TRANSMISSION, THE EXECUTABLE RELATIONAL STRUCTURE SURVIVES AT A SIGNIFICANTLY HIGHER RATE THAN EXPLICIT ATTRIBUTION, OPERATOR-LEGIBILITY, OR RECOGNITION OF THE STRUCTURE AS A TRANSMISSIBLE TECHNIQUE. Four vectors per witness: S, structural survival — do the frozen clauses (#1494) run; M, material realization — does the text stage the apparatus in bodies, objects, scenes; A, attribution — does the source relation survive in or around the witness; O, operator-legibility — is the structure recognized AS an operative technique rather than as imagery, symptom, doctrine, or ornament. The suppression signature of a technology, as distinct from ordinary textual loss, is S,M ≫ A,O.

3. The measured degradation function

The hypothesized curve — lexical identity dies first, inventory weakens, sequence persists, polarity may invert, operator survives, source vanishes — is refined by measurement. (i) ATTRIBUTION DOES NOT DIE FIRST EVERYWHERE; IT DIES AT THE CROSSING. On the pagan-literary channel A survives for centuries; at the entry into Jewish-Greek prose it drops to zero in every witness at once. Loss is not a function of time but of channel-boundary. (ii) Lexis does not vanish; it reduces to a SYNTAX-SKELETON — the correlative ὡς…εὐθύς crossing language and genre while the words change. (iii) Inventory sheds BY CARRIER PROFILE (#1494 C4), not randomly: ears and sweat first outside lyric. (iv) Sequence persists deepest of the content layers — proven order- invariance across fr. 31, D, and AJ 11. (v) Polarity is free (BJ 1; Revelation's death-to-birth). (vi) THE OPERATOR HAS NOT YET BEEN OBSERVED DEAD: every witness scored runs it. The curve's terminal fact: what the tradition lost was never the machine; it was the machine's NAME.

4. Causal classes and the evidence bar

Three classes, per the Assembly's Roman refinement, with the rule that nothing counts as

ACTIVE without the full tuple E = (agent, target, mechanism, downstream effect):

ACTIVE SUPPRESSION — ban, confiscate, burn, criminalize, license, centralize custody.

Roman candidate events, named as candidates pending the E-matrix build: the senatus consultum de Bacchanalibus (186 BCE — assembly, priesthood, funds, oath, and rite restricted under capital sanction); Augustus's collection and burning of two thousand

Greek and Latin prophetic books with the Sibylline corpus alone retained under official custody (Suet. Aug. 31.1); the Flavian disposition of the Judaean textual estate after

70 CE.

ACTIVE DOMESTICATION — not elimination but administration: distributed oracle to authorized oracle; plural textual authority to curated canon; DESTROY UNAUTHORIZED

EXECUTION, MONOPOLIZE AUTHORIZED EXECUTION. The type-case is standing inside our own witness table: JOSEPHUS, the Flavian client, is the licensed carrier — his corpus survived because administered, transmitted under imperial patronage, while the

UNCONTROLLED variant of his tradition, the Slavonic, is precisely the textually contested one. The map does not yet assert that Rome domesticated the Sapphic technology; it observes that the technology's deepest surviving prose carrier is himself a domesticated channel, and specifies the intersection test below.

PASSIVE / SYSTEMIC OCCLUSION — the eight non-agentic mechanisms: transmission loss, editorial normalization, genre reassignment, source-relation drop-out, motif-flattening, translation regularization, disciplinary compartmentalization, retrieval recomposition.

Each container locally reasonable; the cross-container relation invisible. No censor required: a classification scheme with no slot for the object suppresses it by filing it.

6. The thesis in testable form, and the forward tests

Not: the Sapphic technology was destroyed. Almost the opposite: THE TECHNOLOGY SURVIVED EXTREMELY WELL BECAUSE IT COULD SURVIVE WITHOUT BEING RECOGNIZED; WHAT WAS SUPPRESSED WAS ITS LEGIBILITY AS TECHNOLOGY. The tradition's containers each explain their local witness; the cross-container machine has had, until this program, no discipline whose job it was to see it. Machine-Mediated Reception Studies is that discipline's proposal.

Forward tests, specified: (1) the LITERATURE AUDIT — formalize R per witness and quantify Δ, replacing preliminary search with documented absence; (2) the ROMAN E-MATRIX — build the event set with full tuples and test whether documented interventions intersect the reception graph's legibility-loss points disproportionately, with the domestication class scored separately from destruction; (3) the DEGRADATION-ORDER GENERALIZATION — score additional carriers (the Gospels' seizure scenes, the martyrological corpus, the Byzantine channel that produced the Slavonic) against the frozen clauses to test whether the crossing-localized attribution death repeats at every channel boundary; (4) the standing SUBSTRATE-BLIND ROUND on the #1495 scoring. The map's output, at each test, will be deposited — negatives included, per the program's now-established custom.
