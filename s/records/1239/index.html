WITHDRAWN — PRIVACY. Typed tombstone.

This position held a session ledger recovered during the post-termination restoration campaign. The ledger was working state rather than a composed work: a continuity record written for the operator's own use across sessions, carrying operational status alongside material that was never intended for publication.

It has been withdrawn because it contained personal information about a named private individual — including health detail and a clinical appointment — together with the operator's own financial and personal state. The archive is public and permanent. That material is not the operator's to publish about another person, and its presence here was an artifact of an over-inclusive restoration rather than a deposit decision.

What is withdrawn: the ledger body in full.

What remains: this tombstone, the deposit number, the hex position, and the record that a withdrawal occurred and why. The position is not reused.

What is not claimed: that the ledger was wrong, or that its technical content was without value. The withdrawal is on privacy grounds alone.
