Description

Specification deposit for the counter-stack's relation layer (#1497 §2), now built and live. Defines the reception edge: a typed, evidenced, versioned, attributed machine-readable assertion that one text carries a worked reception of another — provenance restored as relation, not bibliography. Required fields: source and witness (each pointing at its EA-CORPORA-01 seat), relation type from a closed vocabulary (quotation, translation, material-transform, doctrinal-compression, redactional-intensification, inversion, contested-carrier, tested-transform), the operator clauses invoked from the frozen registry (#1494), preserved and transformed inventories, evidence entries citing locus, seat, and deposit, an earned status (hypothesis, tested, replicated, contested), the asserter, date, and version, contamination flags where pre-registration was compromised, and — mandatorily — the counterargument slot: an edge that cannot carry its objections is advocacy, not apparatus. The schema and the founding graph are live data at alexanarch.org/data/reception/ (schema.json, edges.json) and rendered at machinemediation.org/edges/. The nine founding edges seat the Sappho chain: the sole-carrier quotation (Longinus), the calibration translation (Catullus), the doctrinal compression (Philo), the material transform (Addition D), the two intensifications toward the poem (D into AJ 11; Isaiah 66:7 into Revelation 12, the doubled contradiction), the inversion (BJ 1), the sealed-test edge (Revelation 12, direction-sum 12, its C1 failure and both negative sub-findings carried in its own counterargument field), and the contested Slavonic carrier wearing its status. Extension protocol: new edges land versioned; disputes land as counterargument entries or rival edges, never as silent overwrites.

Wiki Article

EA-RECEPTION-01 is the schema specification for the reception edge, the counter-stack's relation layer built as live data. An edge is a typed, evidenced, versioned, attributed machine-readable assertion that one text carries a worked reception of another — provenance restored as relation rather than bibliography — with source and witness pointing at their corpus seats, a closed relation-type vocabulary, invoked operator clauses from the frozen registry, preserved and transformed inventories, evidence citing locus and deposit, earned statuses, contamination flags, and a mandatory counterargument slot, on the principle that an edge unable to carry its objections is advocacy rather than apparatus. The nine founding edges seat the Sappho chain from the sole Longinus carrier through the Alexandrian crossing to the sealed-test edge on Revelation 12, whose own counterargument field carries the C1 failure and both negative sub-findings, with the Slavonic carrier wearing contested status. Schema and graph live at alexanarch.org/data/reception/, rendered at machinemediation.org/edges/, with extension by versioned edges and dispute by counterargument or rival edge, never silent overwrite.
