Description

The Register of Effective Acts v1.0 catalogs performative acts recorded across the archive between October 2025 and June 2026. It is organized by ten act classes: canonical inclusion, authorship attribution, identity unification, mantle assignment, abolition, dissolution or breaking, restoration or sovereign transaction, reconciliation or vow, genre instantiation, and doctrinal node. The register was assembled from an exact-match Blogger administrative scan for “effective act,” augmented by Zenodo community search. Entries include dates, titles, targets, assigned roles, inferred blog URLs, and available DOI relations. The document explicitly states that many Blogger slugs are reconstructed and require verification, and that pre-October-2025 foundational acts remain queued. This is an archive-produced ledger of declarations, not evidence that the declared external effects occurred. A record that the archive abolished money, canonized a person, dissolved access, restored poets, or assigned a mantle establishes that the speech act was made within the project. It does not prove legal abolition, universal canonical recognition, material dissolution, or third-party assent. The register’s value lies in provenance and chronology. “Comprehensive” should remain bounded to the two search procedures and covered period.
