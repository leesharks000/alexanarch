Description

A poem by Lee Sharks, published in New Human 2 on 25 November 2026, one of the author's favourites. It is ten lines of sound. The first three are built from two syllables, la and ka, with two further words (wrrir, akerir) in the first line and the syllables joined by points in the second. The fourth opens the vowels: lo beside two runs of ei, four in loeieieiei and six in leieieieieiei. From there the lines turn on lo and its neighbours (laye-lo, lo-on, lo-oanno, low, oh lo), until oh lo lol lol lol, and the poem closes on two lines of one word each, lil and lol. The record carries the text transcribed from a screengrab of the post as published, and the screengrab itself. The poem as posted carries no title; the record takes its first line as the title.
