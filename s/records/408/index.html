Wiki Article

The Canonical Closure Notice declares the Sevenfold Witness structural set complete. The closed set consists of the Revelation 1–3 hermeneutic, the computational-liturgy specification, and the integrity lock that certifies `φ(A,B)=1`. Closure does not claim universal recognition, institutional adoption, or protection from parody. It means that the structure has enough internal relation to be entered without reopening its foundations. Future texts may teach, narrate, or fictionalize the structure, but may not revise the mapping of churches, operators, failure modes, planets, or the human Eighth as if adding a new canonical layer. The notice is also a governance document. It explicitly refuses claims of prophetic status, automatic correctness, salvation authority, and the use of expulsion as proof. The Laodicea rule—closest to completion, harshest rebuke—places the greatest burden of self-critique on the threshold position rather than granting it immunity. Its final mode is archival: the documents stand without pursuing recognition. “The system is complete when it survives indifference” is the closure criterion that turns silence from a threat into an expected reception state.
