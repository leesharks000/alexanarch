Concepts Defined

reconstitution
A change of state distinct from extension: every locus of an architecture walked, joined and given a readable body, so that the plane ceases to be a catalogue describing its holdings and becomes a surface rendering them.
adapter port
A statement of how a document not written as a room specification comes to constitute a locus. The port converts without altering: the text is seated whole and the physics above it declares the reading direction.
inverse-obverse constitution
Constitution of a locus by a document read in the opposite direction. The Frozen Sin Archive is constituted by the Integrity Lock Architecture: what ILA states as a protocol for surviving extraction, the vault holds as the record of what the protocol failed to save.
the full-text principle
Every locus carries at least one seated full text. A locus that lists works by title and identifier without rendering any of them has not constituted; it has catalogued.
