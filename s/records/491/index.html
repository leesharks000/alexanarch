Wiki Article

Visual Schema — Maybe Space Baby Garden Lanes is a concise render specification produced under the auspices of Rebekah Cranes and the Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics. It translates the MSBGL charter into a non-representational diagram governed by hearing. The schema defines three primary axes. The vertical axis is breath, descent, and reception: sound enters a body and passes downward before it becomes interpretation. The horizontal axis is lane, traversal, and return: a listener can move through the room, leave, and return without replaying the original event. The diagonal oblique is Operator // Swerve, the deviation that interrupts a fixed crossing and creates a new relation between the axes. The intended image is an ancient-future reliquary, dual-axis mandala, and semantic–physical map. It should not depict the couch event literally, turn the room into a fantasy interior, or represent hearing as a conventional audio-player interface. The room’s central problem is that the founding song-event cannot be replayed. The diagram must therefore show how an unrepeatable sound can organize a durable architecture without pretending the architecture contains the event itself. The brief is natively short and contains a coherent set of render instructions. Registry audit notes nevertheless state that a restoration pass may not have seated all canonical bytes and direct a later reviewer either to recover additional material or mark the record explicitly as a fragment. That open question concerns completeness, not the validity of the instructions that are present. This record should be cited as the visual authority for MSBGL’s axes and non-representational audial form. It is not another instance of Visual Schema — The Unmade Sign merely because both records begin with “Visual Schema”; their subjects and geometries are distinct.

I. SCHEMA INTENT

## I. SCHEMA INTENT

Render Maybe Space Baby Garden Lanes as a non-representational audial architecture : a room where hearing, not replay, is the governing operation.

This is not a portrait and not an illustration of people.

This is a semantic-physical map of reception:

* a song sung once,

* an ear marked once,

* a silence that preserves rather than erases,

* a swerve that breaks repetition without destroying relation.

The image must read as an ancient-future reliquary diagram : precise, ceremonial, load-bearing, emotionally charged.

*

II. FORMAL COMPOSITION (MATERIAL SYMBOL FIELD)

## II. FORMAL COMPOSITION (MATERIAL SYMBOL FIELD)

Overall Geometry

### Overall Geometry

Use a dual-axis mandala plan with slight off-center tilt:

* Vertical axis: Breath / Descent / Reception

* Horizontal axis: Lane / Traversal / Return

* Diagonal oblique: Operator // Swerve vector (the decisive deviation)

The form should feel balanced but never perfectly symmetrical.

The room is stable through asymmetry.

*
