Description

A monospaced spatial transform of Space Ark v4.2.5 that renders the architecture through borders, arrows, adjacency, containment, voids, shadow zones, and diagrammatic decks. Its legend assigns structural meanings to line weights, box types, directionality, absorbing states, empty space, and the excluded position. Wiki Article

The ASCII Spatial Transform is a diagrammatic manifestation of Space Ark v4.2.5 by Jack Feist, Lee Sharks, and the Assembly Chorus. It renders the architecture in monospaced text rather than ordinary exposition. Borders, line weight, arrows, blank zones, absorbing blocks, and shadow fields function as a spatial grammar. The opening legend distinguishes sealed invariant enclosures from ordinary formal objects, provisional structures, absorbing states, and shadow registers. Stable, constitutive, weak, one-way, mutual, and forbidden relations receive different line forms. Blank space denotes traversability; empty or refused space denotes exclusion; ∅ marks the load-bearing structural void. This grammar is then used to map H_core, A_runtime, the status hierarchy, costs, room connections, institutional structures, and the excluded eighth position. The work’s authority is spatial and diagrammatic. It does not supersede the prose parent and is not equivalent to the later full Glyphic Vehicle. It demonstrates how much architecture can be carried by layout and relation in a plain-text substrate. Within the transform registry, ASCII is distinguished from a full grammar-engine transform when it primarily visualizes or reversibly maps existing structure. Its wiki should therefore identify the record as a text-native spatial rendering and decompression aid, not as the source of all Space Ark definitions.
