Wiki Article

The Mini Macro-Maquette Maquette: Seed is the third and most compressed deposit in the three-scale Macro-Maquette sequence. It encodes twelve micro-physics modules as minimal invariant statements designed to survive renormalization from the full charter through the compressed charter to a seed-scale specification. The modules govern fold, divergence, knot, interference, rotation, channel, patch, furnace, integral, membrane, wager, and hourglass operations. Together they preserve rules concerning irreversibility, voluntary rather than forced convergence, linking-number continuity, minority-signal protection, completed rotation, finite channel capacity, local incompatibility, fixed points under coarse-graining, irreducible residue, selective permeability, a Caritas floor, and conservation of temporal budget. The seed functions as a recoverability test. Its claim is architectural: a sufficiently compressed fragment should still carry the constraints required to reconstruct the larger system. The formula `D3 = RG↓(D2) = RG↓(RG↓(D1))` situates it as Deposit 3, generated through two downward renormalization passes from the full charter. It belongs with the full Macro-Maquette and the mid-scale compressed charter but has a distinct evidentiary role: the full work explains the modules, the compressed charter preserves their load-bearing organization, and the seed tests their survival at minimum scale.
