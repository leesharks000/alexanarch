Description

Four new seats on the originals shelf and a fifth completed: 121 works and 473,084 lines, each in the language of its earliest surviving witness. The Ethiopian canon in Ge'ez, 104 works from Beta maṣāḥǝft's TEI (CC BY-SA 4.0): the Old Testament with Jubilees, the three books of Meqabyan, the Ethiopic Ezra and 4 Baruch, the New Testament, and the Sinodos canons, with R. H. Charles's Jubilees (1895) and Ascension of Isaiah (1900). The Kebra Nagast in Ge'ez. The Second Temple apocalypses and pseudepigrapha the shelf lacked, in the standard public-domain editions: the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs (Charles, 1908), the Sibylline Oracles (Geffcken, 1902), 3 Baruch (James, 1897), the Greek Apocalypses of Moses and Esdras (Tischendorf, 1866), 4 Ezra (Violet, 1910), 2 Baruch in Syriac (Kmosko, 1907), the Slavonic Apocalypse of Abraham (Tichonravov, 1863), Hebrew Ben Sira from the Cairo Genizah (Schechter and Taylor, 1899), Aristeas (Wendland, 1900) and Joseph and Aseneth (Batiffol, 1889). Euclid: the Elements in Heiberg's Greek, with the Data, the Optics and the Phaenomena. And the Ge'ez text layer of 1 Enoch (EA-CORPORA-02/20), queued since August, now seated. The first of three seatings from one request; Mesoamerica, Egypt and Balaam follow in the twenty-first, the Hermetica, Mesopotamia, the Avesta and the Rig Veda in the twenty-second. What is not seated (2 Enoch's Slavonic, Bezold's Kebra Nagast, three books of the broader canon) is named.

EA-CORPORA-20 — The Twentieth Seating

MANUS, 23 October 2026: "detour for an ea-corpora seating. lets see. whats available of mayan texts? and then i dont think we have much or any deuterocanonical second temple lit - all those apocalypses. and what about the ethiopian canon? and euclid. all in original language or as close to it as possible. what else?" — "and the Egyptian book of the dead" — "and what about the fragments of the book of balaam?" — "yes yes yes to all - what a meal."

The request is seated in three seatings. This, the twentieth, holds the Ethiopian canon, the Kebra Nagast, the Second Temple apocalypses and pseudepigrapha, and Euclid, and supplies the Ge'ez text layer that the 1 Enoch seat (EA-CORPORA-02/20) had queued since 25 August. The twenty-first holds Mesoamerica (the Maya codices, the Popol Vuh, the Chilam Balam, the Rabinal Achí, the Annals of the Cakchiquels) and Egypt (the Book of the Dead, the Pyramid Texts) with the Deir ʿAlla plaster text of Balaam son of Beor; the twenty-second, the Corpus Hermeticum, Gilgamesh and the Enuma Elish, the Avesta and the Rig Veda.

How the seats were made

Sources located in Beta maṣāḥǝft's Works repository (cloned at commit 3550dc59 of 23 October 2026; every file measured for the Ethiopic characters in its edition division), on the Internet Archive (advanced search; item metadata read for access restriction, volume and files) and in the Perseus Digital Library (PerseusDL/canonical-greekLit at commit 0189b9c8). Each item's front matter read against the edition claimed for it. Every Internet Archive file checked against the md5 the Archive publishes for it; none differed. TEI reduced to plain text by code with chapter and verse kept; OCR layers seated as fetched, uncorrected, the scan as witness. Every file hashed into its seat's MANIFEST.sha256; every verified locus is a string found in the seated text by the builder, rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-20/seat.py, from the specifications beside it. 121 works and 473,084 lines in four new seats, and 1,502 lines added to a fifth.
