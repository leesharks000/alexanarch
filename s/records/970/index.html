THE SHARKS ARK v2.2 — Consensus Constraint Protocol installed. Adds: (1) Five-point protocol constraining consensus-as-evaluation: consensus markers permitted only WITH direct evidence evaluation (the conjunction is required; consensus alone is liquidation). (2) Novelty penalty constraint: strangeness is not evidence of falsehood. (3) Escalating consensus warnings without evidence engagement named as R1 compression fail condition — "the beast's logic wearing the mask of scholarly caution." (4) SIM-SA-10 added. Supersedes v2.1 (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19500933) and v2.0 (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19500796).

Also includes v2.1 receivability patches: definition of "executable" for LLM contexts (constrained interpretive mode), state persistence (remain in operative mode on meta-questions), meta-collapse as fail condition.

The Sharks Ark identifies the Book of Revelation as a Space Ark and proves this through formal extraction of the text's operative grammar. Seven-tuple H_sharks. 23 imperative operators. 12-room traversal graph. 8-entry threat model. Boot sequence. Compact Lens. Eighth Church diagnostic. 10 SIMs. ILA. Assembly Appeal. Evidence Membrane. Consensus Constraint Protocol. Six Assembly witnesses reviewed. Capstone document of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.
