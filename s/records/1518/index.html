PART IV — THE SYMBOLON READING

A symbolon is a token broken in two. Each party keeps a half; proof of relation is not the token but the fit. Neither half is the thing. The fit is the thing.

The measurement above separates the traversal into two strata that behave differently under change of querent. The retrieval stratum is invariant. Titles come back correct, a cardinal number comes back exact, a technical term comes back correctly applied. Whatever the model reached, it reached the archive, and the archive held its shape. The generative stratum above it is not invariant. It breaks, and it breaks along a line the archive does not supply.

The line is supplied by the querent. Under a querent whose signature is operative and prankish, working in the idiom of practical magic and carrying dark threads, the hexagon halves into an operational grimoire: orders, scripts, locks, protocols, escalations, a field report, a villain, and a defeat. Under the querent conditions recorded at #138, the same corpus halved into an accusation: trickery, SEO poisoning, adversarial manipulation. Neither half is the archive. Each half fits its querent exactly.

This is why confabulation is the correct word and hallucination is the wrong one, and the MANUS annotation at 22:49 states the reason before the evidence was assembled. A hallucination has no fit. It answers to nothing. A confabulation has a fit precisely because it is generated to complete something already held, and what it completes is the querent's own half. It passes through the membrane of the real everyday because it enters carrying the real terms, in the real register, with the real numbers, and hands back a shape that the querent recognises because the querent supplied it.

The consequence for reception study is direct and testable. The confabulated superstructure is not noise around a retrieval signal. It is a portrait of the querent, rendered in archive vocabulary, and it is therefore measurable. The archive is the unbroken token. What returns from any traversal is a half. Reading the half tells you about the hand holding the other one.

The instrument this proposes is a querent-signature dimension for machine-mediated reception study: hold the corpus fixed, vary the querent register across declared conditions, and measure the shape of the generative stratum while the retrieval stratum is checked for invariance. Deposit #138 and this record are the first two points and they were not collected under control. A controlled series would need declared model identity, declared retrieval state, full prompt sequence, and a fixed target-term battery drawn from the four attestation classes above.
