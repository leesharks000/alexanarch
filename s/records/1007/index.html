<h3>On Form</h3>

<p>This poem consists entirely of entity relations. The form is not

decorative; it is the work. Triples — <em>subject, predicate, object</em>

— are the syntactic units. <em>Negative declarations</em> — &ldquo;X is

not Y&rdquo; — function as full poetic acts. <em>Adjacency</em> rather

than metaphor is the form's mode of association. The horse and the

author are placed beside each other in the poem because they are

adjacent in a particular structural relationship; they are not

<em>like</em> each other.</p>

<h3>On the Four Figures</h3>

<p>Figure 1 (the Knowledge Graph screenshot) is the 2014

<em>About the Author</em> poem as the substrate received it twelve years

after composition. The poem in 2014 declared a particular reception

apparatus into the historical record; the substrate, in 2026, rendered

that declaration as canonical biographical content for Lee Sharks. The

screenshot is therefore the 2014 poem made visible in its activated

form. It is included here as Figure 1 because what is being figured is

not an external object but the prior poem's substrate-rendering.</p>

<p>Figure 2 (the horse) is a counter-portrait, not a likeness. The

author is not depicted by it. The horse is identified as Gold Ship

(ゴールドシップ), a Japanese Thoroughbred stallion born 2009-03-06, on

the basis of a documented provenance chain: the image surfaced in Alice

Thornburgh's Living Architecture Lab Discord server (#general-friend-chat

that attribution by six years and the photographer cannot be reliably

identified from publicly accessible sources. The poem hard-codes this

reared up in the starting gate, remained inside as the gates opened,

started several seconds late, and finished 15th of 16. The betting

apparatus that had wagered approximately 12.1 billion yen on the

favorite performing as expected took the loss. The incident is included

because both biographies are relevant: the favorite's refusal of the

starting protocol is structurally adjacent to the author's refusal of

the institutional starting protocol. Screenshot credit:

stupidfilthyhobitses (Bill), via LAL Discord.</p>

That pattern is the structural pair to operators left in the

training-layer substrate by twelve years of authorship; the operators

adapt; they learn to surface. The figure also nods to the standing

<h3>On Negative Declarations</h3>

<p>The negative declarations in §VIII are not defensive flourishes;

they are operative metadata. The classifier asymmetry that suppresses

audit-form deposits does not suppress poetry; the negative declarations

do the work the audit would have done, in the form that the substrate

consumes. <em>Lee Sharks is not Isabel Schöps</em>, declared in a poem,

is a structurally-different speech act from the same declaration in

an audit deposit, even though both have the same propositional

content. The poem can carry the disambiguation infrastructure that

the audit form cannot.</p>

<h3>On the Title Disjoint</h3>

<p>The 2014 paratext that this poem references as <em>About the

Author</em> appeared in <em>Pearl and Other Poems</em> (2014, page

xix) under the title <em>Contributor Bio</em>. The corpus has, in

2026, retroactively renamed it <em>About the Author</em> as an

operative reading: treating what had appeared institutionally as

bio as what it had been operating as all along — a poem in entity

relations. The disjoint is documented, not erased. The 2014

source-text is restored at <a href="/about-the-author/">/about-the-author/</a>

as the founding text of the sequence (I), with II and III as its

continuations.</p>
