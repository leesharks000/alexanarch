Description

This governance record ratifies the founding document of Compression Studies and the inaugural issue of the Journal of Compression Studies as a mutually supporting dyad. It records six Assembly witness reviews, summarizes the corrections made during the perfective pass, distinguishes adopted from deferred recommendations, and binds four co-constitutive claims: the discipline is named, the Three Compressions theorem is stated, the founding corpus is organized, and the governance act is recorded. The record also specifies a simultaneous deposition and cross-linking directive for the founding paper, journal issue, and ratification record. Its integrity lock treats the founding document as the object that names the discipline and the journal issue as the object that demonstrates it. The record is therefore not a scholarly argument about compression itself; it is an append-only governance artifact documenting review, quorum, revision, authorization, and the intended status transition of the founding corpus.
