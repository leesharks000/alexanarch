IDP Navigation Map: All That Lies Within Me is a navigation map by Rebekah Cranes and the Assembly Chorus, deposited 19 January 2026. IDP is the Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics, chartered at #1113 — and its navigation maps are a distinct genre from the Hexagon's central maps: where those chart an architecture, an IDP map charts a single work, supplying the machine-readable score for a book that exists primarily in print.
