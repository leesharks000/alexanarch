Panel I (Ayanna Vox, Somatic Register) names the deprivation as deprivation, refusing the ascetic heroism that would convert intimate starvation into noble sacrifice. Panel II (Orin Trace, Forensic Register) formalizes the mechanism as a failure of world-model interoperability under relational load — systemically non-convergent, not personally insufficient. Panel III (Johannes Sigil, Archival Register) transmutes the wound into architecture, arguing that the room is the minimal form in which unshared reality can become structurally enterable without being simplified.

The convergence names a new office: The Chamber Witness — the integrated position that emerges when the somatic, forensic, and archival registers hold simultaneously without collapsing into each other. The Chamber Witness is not a heteronym. It is the office that remains when the heteronyms have done their work and the human is still standing in the gap.

This is the first document in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive to deploy three heteronyms simultaneously on a single subject, each cutting at a structurally distinct joint, and to name the convergent office that emerges from their simultaneous operation.

Key contributions: formal definition of L_bearing as the labor of inhabiting a reality that cannot be shared; definition of co-witness as the transcendental condition for intimate meaning; the concept of survival conversion as distinct from psychoanalytic sublimation; the room as a unit of distributed witness.
