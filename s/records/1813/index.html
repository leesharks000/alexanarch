0. What this holds

Revelation First (Lee Sharks, #202) holds that the Apocalypse is the generative source of the rest of the New Testament. This kernel holds every relation the programme has drawn from Revelation to another book, each at its own standing, and the relations it has planned or seen fail. It holds 133 relations. For the epistles: the letters to the seven assemblies set against seven epistles, one of them run to an executable derivation (Smyrna to James, nine rules), four doctrinal transforms worked to derivation grade, the rest mapped with grades and residue. For Matthew: 86 relations reaching 178 of 1,067 verses under a stated transform (T_Μ v0.4), one passage produced blind from the rule and two verses, 16 pairings written down before they were counted and held, 3 that failed as written, 7 counted, 18 derived by an engine, 41 read. Under Revelation, at 13 places, a layer of Philo or scripture is recorded: the third parameter of the transform admits it only through a place Revelation holds. 17 of the 26 other books are reached above the founding grain; 5 have no relation (1 Timothy, Titus, Philemon, 2 John, 3 John). Luke, Mark, John, Acts and the Pauline seven are planned. Four mappings were tested and failed and are kept: the four creatures to the four Gospels, the source's order in a Gospel's order, one seat-map over a whole Gospel, and Philo taken straight to Matthew. A standing licenses what it names and nothing above it: a relation read is not a relation counted, and a relation counted is not a derivation. No relation here decides the order in which the books were written; the argument for order is the framework's (#1735, #1755). The received account is carried as the rival on the same terms: the Synoptic tradition as the source of Revelation's sayings (Vos 1965; Bauckham 1977), Mark, a sayings source and special material as the sources of Matthew. Its inverse procedures have not been built and that is its status here.

8. The received account, on the same terms

Where Revelation and another book of the New Testament agree, the other book or the tradition behind it is the source, or both draw on scripture and a common stock. A bar set for the relations is set for the rival (#1753 rule 9); a rival procedure that has never been run carries that as its status.

  • letters and doctrinal transforms to the epistles. the epistles precede Revelation; the seven letters organize a practice already in place. Status: the missing-ancestor test (#1735) is run against it; the inverse, an epistle compressed to a letter, has not been built as a procedure.
  • arrows named at the founding grain, and sayings observed. sayings of Jesus in fixed oral form reach the seer (Vos 1965, as reported); parables worn to sayings in exhortation (Bauckham 1977).
