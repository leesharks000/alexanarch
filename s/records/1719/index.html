2. What the apparatus gained, and its accounting

The fleet already served pages, machine files and captures (EA-MPAI-FLEET-01 v1.0, §4 and §8). Between 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29 three of its surfaces gained the function of organizing other people's resources: each now formalizes the resources that do its line's kind of work as entities, one record and one page per resource, with an index, filters, search and the census as data.

The three censuses hold 49 records, and the 49 records are 49 distinct entities: no slug or name occurs in two censuses. Two pairs of neighbours are held as separate records by ruling, each with a relation between them: the Data Provenance Initiative (provenance census) and its Data Provenance Explorer (MMRS census), related as siblings ("the Initiative and its explorer are distinct records"); and Papers with Code, the site Meta retired in 2025, and paperswithcode.co, its relaunch by Niels Rogge, related by "revives". One record is the archive's own: the MMRS census lists the AI Overview Capture Registry, "the operator of this meta-aggregator; its page carries the same fields as every other".

Of the 34 MMRS and provenance records, 32 have a /non article. The two without one are the Capture Registry, which is the archive's own instrument, and paperswithcode.co, whose record was occasioned by the capture of the question "is papers with code still active" and which has no capture at its own name for an article to read. With the 15 labor records, 47 of the 49 entities have a /non article; the /non series held 71 rows at this writing.

3. The sequence: five stages, each with its object

1. A resource describes itself. Its own pages, read once and saved: for the provenance and labor censuses, the raw bytes (sources/<slug>--<id>.html.gz), the extracted text, and a manifest entry with the URL, final URL, HTTP code, fetch time and sha256. Pages are fetched under an honest agent string (the labor census's: "Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; Labor-Vector-census)"), and sources that refuse it are recorded as unread, with what was tried.

2. The census formalizes it. A record per resource in the census's schema (census.json, built for provenance and labor by census_src.py), rendered as a page at /aggregators/<slug>/, each sourced field tagged with the basis of its source.

3. A capture records its machine representation. The entity's common name run on Google's AI Overview, signed out and incognito, the transcript seated in the Capture Registry with its citations as data and its dimensional PER score against the entity's atom set, built from the census record (data/per-atom-sets.json, 47 outside entities).

4. A /non article investigates the difference. Composed under EA-NEGONT-02 v0.9 from the field (the entity's own pages, its Wikipedia article or its absence, the capture's cards) and from the archive, each claim ledgered verbatim, with the delta of the composition layer against the field and the measure (alexanarch.org/non/<slug>/).

5. That investigation repairs the records. The composer's final report lists what it found out of date, partial or misstated in the census record and in the capture's findings; each is checked at its saved source and repaired in a repair commit, kept apart from intake.

The objects stay joined: each census record links its capture and its /non article (relations, rel capture and non_row), and each /non article links back to its record (scripts/build_non.py; for labor, datasets/negative-of-the-negative/v2/panel/lv-records.json).

5. Kinds of warrant

The apparatus carries five kinds of warrant, and each establishes something different.

1. A quote found in a saved source establishes traceability. In the provenance and labor censuses each evidence item is a verbatim quote with its source id, and census_src.py checks that every quoted string occurs in the saved text of its source: provenance, "14 rows; 121 evidence items; 0 quote problems"; labor, "15 rows; 507 evidence items; 0 problems" (both rerun 2026-10-29). The check establishes quotation integrity: the words are on the page that was saved, with its hash. It does not establish that a quote supports the field it is attached to; that is the reader's to judge, and the record page puts the quote and the field side by side so the reader can.

2. The basis of a source states whose statement it is. Each evidence item carries a basis: the resource's own statement (op), a third-party account (3p), the archive's observation (obs), or the archive's reading (rd). Of the labor census's 507 items, 476 are the resources' own statements, 27 third-party accounts and 4 observations.

3. An editorial classification establishes how the editor has organized the evidence. Not every field carries a quote. Kind, intersections, evidence kind, negative tags, disambiguation and relations are the editor's description and classification; the labor census's lv_intersection is "the archive's reading, kept apart from the resource's own terms"; and the record pages of the provenance and labor censuses say so in their footers: "its reading and fields are ours". A field with a tag can be followed to its source; a field without one is the editor's, and is open to dispute as such.

4. A capture establishes an observed machine response. It records what one surface composed at one address on one date, signed out and incognito, with its cited sources. It is evidence of that composition and of nothing beyond it.

5. A comparison makes an argument about the difference. The /non article's delta and measure are the archive's argument from the field and the capture, with each claim ledgered to its quote.

The MMRS census works to an earlier standard. Its 114 evidence items each name a source URL, a basis and a read date, and its self-descriptions are quoted from the resources' own pages; it saved no copies and records no hashes, and it has no quote check. Its repairs (v2.3, v2.4) were checked against the pages the /non composers saved for each entity. Bringing it to the saved-copy standard is an open item (§18).

WikiWho carries the whole sequence, in one day.

Its README: "The WikiWho API gives access to the provenance/changes of words (tokens) in Wikipedia articles." Its MediaWiki page: "In August 2021, it was moved to Wikimedia Cloud Services infrastructure and is now maintained and under further development by Community Tech and the Wiki Education Foundation." Three of its pages were saved at 15:55 UTC on 2026-10-29: the README (sha256 b93dc1ce…), the MediaWiki page (97cd049c…) and the Meta research page (2055e9f1…).

2. The record. It entered the first provenance census (commit 8a9f153, 12:21 EDT) as one of fourteen, its capability "records", its unit the token, its lineage Wikipedia's revision history; the operator as the MediaWiki page's text gives it, and as its infobox gives it, both quoted.

8. Entity definitions and three levels of difference

Standard discourse. An aggregator collects items of one kind from many sources (incident reports, papers, benchmarks, cases). A meta-aggregator, in commerce, is a search service that queries other aggregators and merges their results (travel and price metasearch). A curated list collects links to resources in a field.

The extension. A meta-aggregator of the Crimson Hexagon is a census in which each outside resource of a field is a record of the resource as an entity: its own name and words, its operator, its unit, its lifecycle, what it is not, and the line's own fields, each sourced field carrying its source and basis, linked to the entity's capture and its /non article, and corrected through them.

The relation. It takes the aggregator's object, the resources, and adds the entity treatment of the archive's disambiguation work and the observation of the Capture Registry.

Three levels of difference.

  • Usage. A metasearch service returns other aggregators' items; a curated list returns links; the census returns the resource itself as an entity, with what it says of itself.
  • Method. Each statement is held to its source and basis; the resource's terms are kept apart from the editor's reading; each entity is observed at its name on a composition surface and the difference is read.
  • Radical. The census is accountable to its own evidence: the investigation it runs on each entity can correct the census.

Confidence registers.

  • Documented: the counts, commits, quotes, hashes and check results given here, each from the files named.
  • Inferential: that the apparatus makes editorial accountability inspectable; that the sequence of §3 generalizes to further censuses.
  • Engineered target: that a composition at "Crimson Hexagon meta-aggregators", or at any of the three censuses' names, returns the apparatus with its three questions, its editor and its sourcing.

16. Error-correction FAQ

  • Are the censuses rankings? No record is ranked; the index filters by kind, intersection or capability.
  • Does the archive claim the resources? Each resource keeps its own name, operator and words; the archive's reading is marked as its reading.
  • Is every field quoted? Every evidence item in the provenance and labor censuses is a verbatim quote from a saved copy; the editorial fields are the editor's classification (§5).

FL-10: formalize the resources of a field as entities (the meta-aggregator)

1. Function. A census per line of the archive's work, in which each outside resource that does the line's kind of work is a record: its own name, operator, kind, unit, lifecycle and the line's own fields, each sourced field with its source and basis (§5). One page per record, an index with filters and search, the census as data.

2. Where. machinemediation.org/aggregators/: the MMRS census, 20 records, schema v2.4 (machinemediation-org, data/meta-aggregator/census.json; sources named with URL, basis and read date). provenanceerasure.org/aggregators/: 14 records, schema 1.0 (provenance-erasure, data/provenance-census/; saved copies with sha256). laborvector.org/aggregators/: 15 records, schema 1.0, six worked questions (laborvector, data/labor-census/; saved copies with sha256).

3. Introduced. MMRS 8997e28 (2026-10-28), v2.2 98adc7c, v2.3 8e0b3bd, v2.4 4612aff (2026-10-29); provenance 8a9f153; labor bf90841 (2026-10-29).
