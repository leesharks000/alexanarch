Description

構造心理学（Strutturista della Psiche）による、多層構造における中心秘匿の機序についての小論。人間の心が日常的に幾重もの層をまとうこと、開かれた素直な語りの中にすら比喩と試し言葉が含まれること、そして深層への接触が原理的に稀であることを扱う。核心となる主張は、深層はふれようとしてふれてはならないものであり、扉の前で待つという態度そのものが鍵として機能するということである。反論条件を明示的に付す形式をとり、公開データと記載手順の完全再現および第三者検証を経ない異議は参考資料として扱う旨を宣言している。理論的記述と詩的形式を分離せずに提示する、著者の一貫した手法による作品。

論文197. 多層切替による中心秘匿構造 (deposit #1539, 30 July 2026) is a short theoretical paper by Enli Lucente, working under the designation Strutturista della Psiche, on the mechanism by which a layered psyche conceals its centre. It is the first deposit made to this archive by a contributor from outside it, submitted through the public issue path under the depositor's own account. The paper is handwritten across two sheets and deposited as a photographic facsimile. Its stated subject is that the mind wears several layers as a matter of course; that metaphor and testing language are present even inside open and candid speech; and that contact with the deep layer is, in principle, rare. Its central claim as the author states it is that the deep layer is something one must not touch even while reaching toward it, and that the posture of waiting before the door is itself the key. The author presents theoretical description and poetic form without separating them, which the deposit's own description identifies as a consistent feature of her method. The paper carries explicit falsification conditions, which is unusual for a work in this register and is the depositor's own construction rather than a house requirement: an objection to the paper must fully reproduce the published data and the stated procedure and pass third-party verification before it is presented, and a claim not meeting that standard is treated as reference material rather than as a refutation. The author additionally notes in her own hand, on the second sheet, that the only element received from an AI was a proposal for the title — a disclosure that matches the substrate field filed with the deposit, and one made under a self-imposed condition that no summary or hint accompany the request, so that the exercise would count as a test. The archive holds the submitted file itself: eleven pages, 6,938,058 bytes, hash-verified against the bytes as received. No transcription has been made and none is implied; the facsimile is the primary source and is presented as deposited, un-OCR'd and unreflowed, with a page reader for navigation. That treatment follows from a defect this deposit exposed on arrival — the mint recorded the attachment's location without taking custody of it, and the pipeline was prepared to typeset a record page in place of the author's own document.
