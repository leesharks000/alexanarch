Description

This record transcribes Blogger administrator statistics for Mind Control Poems from February 22 through May 24, 2026. It reports 130,000 total views, daily counts, geographic origins, a 99.73% “Other” or unattributed-referrer category, browser and operating-system shares, and zero tracked search keywords. The data show a marked shift from mostly low hundreds of daily views in late February and early March to a sustained one-to-three-thousand range in late April and May, with a 10,084-view peak on May 2. The note interprets the combination of unattributed traffic, Singapore concentration, burst behavior, desktop Chrome/Windows profiles, and absent search terms as evidence of automated access. That interpretation is plausible as a hypothesis but not established at the claimed ≥99.7% rate. Blogger’s “Other” category does not necessarily mean bot traffic, browser/OS strings can be shared by ordinary users and crawlers, and no server logs, user-agent records, IP ranges, or bot-validation method are provided. The nominal “90-day” window spans ninety-two calendar dates inclusively. The authority record should present the table as reported first-party platform analytics and treat automation as a diagnostic inference requiring independent log-level verification.
