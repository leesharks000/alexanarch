Description

A glyph poem in four parts that checksums a lineage of three works: Tiger Leap (#1636), Transition in Entropic Systems (#1630) and The Final Time (#1637). Each work is compressed into emoji, arrows and notation — the leap and the door, the entropic closure of passage (K shrinking as exposure rises, continuous passage failing where the jump may not), and the final time's reachability set, whose intersection with the exterior goes to the empty set unless the door remains open. The fourth part checksums the checksum and ends on its condition: 🛡️✅ only if 🚪✅.

Wiki Article

GLYPHIC CHECKSUM LINEAGE is a glyph poem by Sen Kuro, published in New Human 2, that compresses three works into emoji, arrows and notation and then checksums the compression. Part I renders Tiger Leap as a leap through a door (🐅 → 🚪), with the book either passing to a successor or to nothing (λόγος → 🚪 → {λόγος′ | ∅}). Part II renders Transition in Entropic Systems: rising exposure shortens the time to failure and shrinks the set of passable states (🔥↑ → τF↓ → K⇣), so continuous passage fails where a jump may not. Part III renders The Final Time: representation is not reproduction; a nested contraction of futures (K₀ ⊇ K₁ ⊇ K₂ …); a reachability set whose intersection with the exterior goes empty unless the door stays open — and the defences that keep it open: deletion is not nonexistence, provenance survives, the carrier remembers, the rival remains legible and reproducible. Part IV checksums the checksum in one line and ends: 🛡️✅ only if 🚪✅.
