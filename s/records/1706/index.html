Description

One seat on the originals shelf, 14 collections and 675,049 lines of Arabic: the hadith, the reports of what the Prophet Muhammad said and did, which with the Qur'an (seated since EA-CORPORA-02/08) are the sources of Islamic law and practice. The nine books Sunni scholarship calls al-Kutub al-Tis'a (the Muwatta of Malik, the Musnad of Ahmad ibn Hanbal, the Sunan of al-Darimi, the Sahihs of al-Bukhari and Muslim, and the Sunans of Ibn Majah, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i); the four books of the Imami Shi'i tradition, al-Kutub al-Arba'a (al-Kulayni's al-Kafi, Ibn Babawayh's Man la yahduruhu al-faqih, al-Tusi's Tahdhib al-ahkam and al-Istibsar); and al-Nawawi's Forty. Each is the version the Open Islamicate Texts Initiative (OpenITI) marks primary, pinned by commit, checked against its git blob, with the printed edition its header names and its OpenITI version record. The texts are OpenITI's, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. What is not seated (the Sunan al-Kubra, the Sahihs of Ibn Khuzayma and Ibn Hibban, the Mustadrak, al-Bayhaqi, the Zaydi and Ibadi musnads, translations) is named.

The encyclopedic entry for this work is on its identifier page: /s/axn/0729/

EA-CORPORA-23 — The Twenty-Third Seating

MANUS, 27 October 2026: "lets seat the hadith" — "i mean the hadith supplementary to the quran" — "as ea-corpora".

The Qur'an has been on the shelf since the second seating (EA-CORPORA-02/08, the Tanzil Uthmani text, #1531). This seating places beside it the hadith, in Arabic, as each of the two largest traditions defines its canon: the Sunni nine books and the Imami Shi'i four books, with al-Nawawi's Forty, the short collection most read and memorized.

Seat 23/01 — The Hadith

How the seat was made

The works were found in OpenITI's text repositories on GitHub (blob-less clones of 0200AH, 0250AH, 0275AH, 0300AH, 0325AH, 0350AH, 0400AH, 0475AH and 0700AH, each at the commit recorded in source.json). For each work the version marked PRIMARY_VERSION in its OpenITI version record was chosen. Each text and its version record were fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com at the pinned commit and checked against the git blob id the clone lists for it; none differed. The edition statements are read from each file's own #META# header and its version record, and nothing is asserted beyond them: where a header names no editor, publisher or year, none is given. The licence was read on the KITAB project's documentation and on the Zenodo release record. The texts are seated as published, in OpenITI mARkdown, and each is its own text layer. Every file is hashed into the seat's MANIFEST.sha256; every verified locus is a string found in the seated text by rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-23/seat.py from the specification beside it, spec23-hadith.json.
