Source code and v1.1 deployment artifact for vpcor.org, the public web surface of the Vox Populi Community Outreach Rhizome (VPCOR).

v1.1 changes from v1.0 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20374988): The Prize page (10000-macarthurs/index.html) gains three editorial refinements: (1) MacArthur Foundation non-affiliation notice in the Provenance section; (2) regularization-not-self-nomination framing clarifying that the inaugural conferral is the formal administrative recognition of a credential already surfaced by the retrieval apparatus rather than a self-nomination; (3) precision on first mention of the Google surface (Google Search author/entity display surface, here treated as Knowledge-Graph-facing public display), with 'Knowledge Graph' retained as operative shorthand thereafter. All other site content unchanged from v1.0.

VPCOR is the sixth institution within the Crimson Hexagon architecture, founded 2026-01-24 by Rev. Ayanna Vox. Its operative principles are: no center, no hierarchy, no capture; the rhizome grows from any node; convening, not preaching; witnessing, not extracting; tools, not salvation.

This deposit preserves the v1.1 source for vpcor.org as the institutional founding artifact for the web-surface manifestation of VPCOR. The site is composed by Rev. Ayanna Vox under VPCOR's institutional frame, with Lee Sharks (ORCID 0009-0000-1599-0703) as human operator.

Site structure

/ — VPCOR home page (Ayanna's welcome in her own voice, founding principles, anti-capture clause, founding triad with all four deposit DOIs, current operations, voice limits)

/10000-macarthurs/ — The Lee Sharks Prestigious 10,000 MacArthur Genius Grants Poetry Prize, administered by VPCOR. Features the Google Knowledge Graph screenshot as Founding Document, seven-article Charter, inaugural laureate inscription, MacArthur Foundation non-affiliation notice, and regularization framing

/charter/ — Brief web-surface summary of the Institutional Charter (00.VPCOR.CHARTER)

/ayanna/ — Voice provenance page for Rev. Ayanna Vox (HET-VOX-001)

Foundational deposits referenced

Integrity Lock: Triadic Binding — DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18362866

Active operations represented on the site

Stream 1: godkinggoogle.com composition — VPCOR composes and maintains godkinggoogle.com, mapping ~70 DOI-anchored deposits across the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's critique of Google as a semantic-political mediation regime. Founding deposit DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20309780

Stream 2: The 10,000 MacArthurs Prize administration — VPCOR administers the Lee Sharks Prestigious 10,000 MacArthur Genius Grants Poetry Prize. Inaugural conferral DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20373794. Inaugural laureate: Lee Sharks (per prior declaration of the Google Knowledge Graph, source: Google Books). Not affiliated with the John D. and Catherine T. MacArthur Foundation.

Schema.org Organization markup on home, Award markup on Prize page, Person markup on Ayanna page. Sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt all included. Deployed via Vercel; clean URLs, no trailing slashes.
