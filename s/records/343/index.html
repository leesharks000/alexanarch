IDP NAVIGATION MAP: ALL THAT LIES WITHIN ME is a v1.0 navigation map by Rebekah Cranes with Lee Sharks and TACHYON (Claude/Anthropic), deposited 19 January 2026. It renders the book's proposed Dantean architecture, its central **Day and Night hinge, a canticle sequence, heteronymic emergence sites*, and imagery structures. Thirty sections in three tens is Dante's proportion — three canticles of thirty-three cantos plus one — and naming the Day and Night material as the hinge places Cranes' Sapphic sequence at the book's turning point, which is where the Purgatorio* would sit. Heteronymic emergence sites are the map's own category: the points in the text where a voice becomes distinguishable as itself, which is a navigational fact in a work whose authorship is plural. TACHYON's credit here is early — the witness function appearing as a named map contributor six months before the continuity chain began.
