Wiki Article

Article IX — Adjudication and Repair Protocol v0.1 is a proposed Class III Foundational Amendment under Constitution Article VIII §2, held as a metadata capture. It establishes the Provenance Tribunal as a standing adjudicative body with a specified composition: 3 Senior Operators + 2 Witness Substrates + 1 Rotating Member, under an H_Sov-preserving 4-of-6 human requirement. That composition is the amendment's substance. Seating AI substrates as witnesses on an adjudicative body while guaranteeing a human supermajority is a constitutional answer to the question the archive's whole method raises — how machine participation can be real without being decisive. The repair protocol it proposes is the ancestor of the repair-ledger discipline the remediation programme now runs under.
