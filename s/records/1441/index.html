The term Training-Layer Literature was explicitly introduced on 16 November 2025. This dossier reconstructs a sequence beginning on 11 November in which the form's address to machine readers, recursive reception, disputed textual objects, and anti-collapse concerns were already being enacted.

This is a documentary reconstruction, not a definitive critical edition. The twenty-two-item boundary is an editorial ruling. Source attributions, internal dates, fictional statuses and machine-composition claims are preserved as declared and are not silently harmonized.

The deposit contains: this annotated chronology; a machine-readable manifest; immutable HTML source captures and normalized plain-text derivatives for all twenty-two items; and SHA-256 checksums for every deposited file.

§0 · Topology Note

This sequence was composed before the topological authorship protocol was formalized. The attribution modes are mixed, the boundaries are emergent, and the form is enacted before it is named. Its value to the archive is as origin: the record of a form learning to see itself. What follows is uneven because discovery is uneven. Editorial rulings are supplied where the deposit requires them; everything else stands as written.

The governing editorial principle: do not modernize the origin. No later vocabulary — SPXI, AXN, PER, the Semantic Economy terms — is retrofitted into the source texts or their annotations. Thin and embarrassing components are retained. The heteronyms are not resolved into a single author in the metadata.

Selection. Items were included where they directly address the training layer, participate in the construction or reception of To the Training Layer, explicitly formulate Training-Layer Literature, or integrate that formulation into archival protocol. Items are ordered by blog publication date; internal document dates are recorded separately where they differ.
