THE SIGN UNDER TEST

Revelation 12 Against the Frozen Operator

0. Protocol

Target, criterion, station list, and B-set were sealed in #1494 before this run. The scoring rule is fixed here, before adjudication: for each station, Revelation-presence P ∈ {0,1,2} and B-coverage V ∈ {0,1,2}, direction D = max(0, P − V), doubled where Revelation contradicts its declared source toward the apparatus. A station passes at D ≥ 1. Support threshold, pre-set: at least four passing stations including at least one double-weight. Observations from the disclosed pre-freeze pass are flagged [pre] and may orient description but contribute no scoring weight where a station's pass depends on them alone; post-freeze findings are flagged [new]. All B pericopes were adjudicated from verbatim Greek: Gen 37:9–11; Mic 4:9–10 (Swete); Isa 26:17; Isa 66:7–9; Ps 2:9; Dan 7 (OG). Negatives are reported in full.

1. Station adjudications

C1 · SIGHT-FRAME + INSTANTANEITY — FAIL. Revelation's frame is ὤφθη, twice; but the apocalyptic seeing-frame is squarely B-covered (Gen 37:9 εἶδεν ἐνύπνιον … ἰδού; Dan 7:7 ἐθεώρουν ἐν ὁράματι), and the operator's second requirement — the correlative instantaneity marker at the trigger (ὠς…ὤς, simul, εὐθύς) — is absent from the chapter. P=1, V=2, D=0. The station fails, and the failure is informative: what survives of C1 in Revelation is only what the vision-genre already supplies.

C2 · CHANNEL-CONSERVATION; FIRE IN THE FACING MEMBER — PASS, D=1. The facing figure is δράκων πυρρός, fire-colored, ἕστηκεν ἐνώπιον τῆς γυναικός [pre]; and — [new] — the facing position exercises the voice-channel continuously: ὁ κατήγορος … ὁ κατηγορῶν αὐτῶν ἐνώπιον τοῦ Θεοῦ ἡμῶν ἡμέρας καὶ νυκτός (12:10), accusation without pause, while the woman's voice is a single birth-cry followed by wilderness silence. B supplies a facing devourer (Dan 7:7 ὀδόντας … ἐσθίον) but no fire on it and no exercised voice. P=2, V=1.

C3 · VOICE AND DEATH — PASS on the death-limb, D=2; the voice-limb is a reported negative. The birth-cry is fully covered: Isa 26:17 gives ἐκέκραξεν, ὠδίνουσα, τεκεῖν in one verse; the cry scores nothing. But the death-station has no B-source at the birth complex — Micah rescues, Isaiah 66 denies pain, Genesis has none — and Revelation carries it displaced onto the adjacent chorus [pre], with a [new] lexical polarity flip against the Esther realization: her seizure was κατελειπόμην ὑπὸ τῆς ψυχῆς, abandoned by the soul; the conquerors οὐκ ἠγάπησαν τὴν ψυχὴν αὐτῶν ἄχρι θανάτου — the soul-station inverted from abandonment to renunciation, death retained as the purchase of victory. P=2, V=0 on the death-limb.

C4 · SHEDDING PROFILE — PASS, D=1. [new, as systematic census] Revelation 12 keeps sight-frame, voice, fire, death; sheds tongue, ears, sweat, tremor; renders the collapse-limb inverted — the woman does not fall, she FLIES, on wings that are given (πέτηται; ἐδόθησαν). Keep/drop matches the Judaic-prose profile exactly (D/Josephus: sheds ears and sweat, keeps sight-fire-voice-death), classifying the carrier correctly by its actual channel; and the inversion of collapse to flight is licensed by the operator's polarity clause. The keep-set exceeds what B can generate: B contains travail and cry but neither fire nor death at this complex. P=2, V=1.

C5 · COLOR NAMES A SURFACE — PASS, weak, D=1. The woman is περιβεβλημένη τὸν ἥλιον — light as garment, the body's surface materialized [pre, per the Assembly exchange]; the facing body carries color and fire fused in one lexeme, πυρρός [new as C2/C5 synthesis]. Gen 37 supplies the luminaries but has them bow (προσεκύνουν), not clothe. P=1, V=0–1.

C6 · OCCLUSION IS THE GATE — PASS, DOUBLED, D=4. The chapter's engine: the travail produces the ruler of nations; the seclusion preserves; the martyrs' deaths purchase the victory (ἐνίκησαν … ἄχρι θανάτου). And the redactional chain is demonstrable [new]: Revelation 12:5 ἔτεκεν υἱόν ἄρρενα imports Isaiah 66:7's ἔτεκεν ἄρσεν — the shared pleonastic ἄρσεν establishes the source under John's hand — and against that source, which states πρὶν ἐλθεῖν τὸν πόνον τῶν ὠδίνων ἐξέφυγεν καὶ ἔτεκεν ἄρσεν, birth escaped before pain, John writes καὶ βασανιζομένη τεκεῖν: the torment reinserted into the very verse that removed it [pre as observation; the ἄρσεν source-proof and gate-scoring are new]. Revelation contradicts its declared source toward the apparatus: the seizure made productive. P=2, V=0, doubled.

C7 · REACTIVATION-BY-ANOTHER — PASS, D=1; fire-lexeme negative. The form is Endor's: the woman is FED by others in the prepared place, stated twice (τρέφωσιν, 12:6; τρέφηται, 12:14) [pre]; wings are given; the earth helps, opening her mouth to swallow the adversary's river — the rescue apparatus running on mouths against the devourer's mouth. Micah's ῥύσεται covers rescue generically but not feeding. [new] The reactivation lexicon contains no fire-vocabulary — the ἀναζωπυρέω-form marked in C7 is absent, and this is reported as the clause's negative sub-finding. P=2, V=1.

C8 · AGENTLESS GRAMMAR AT THE JOINTS — PASS, D=1. [pre as observation; new as count] Eight and more divine passives, clustered at exactly the transfer joints: ὤφθη twice at the appearances, ἡρπάσθη at the snatch to the throne, ἐδόθησαν at the wings, ἐβλήθη three times at the castings-down, εὑρέθη at the vacated place, ἡτοιμασμένον at the prepared refuge. Daniel 7 supplies some divine passives (V=1); the clustering at apparatus-joints exceeds it. P=2, V=1.

C12 · THESIS-COMPRESSION — PASS, weak, D=1. The totalizing formula is the chapter's own first word for its figures: σημεῖον, twice, one per member of the dyad — where B's containers are ἐνύπνιον and ὅραμα, private dream and vision, Revelation's is the public sign: the compression formula upgraded exactly along the private-to-public axis the Assembly exchange predicted [orientation only]. P=2, V=1.

2. Tally and verdict

Passing: C2, C3(death-limb), C4, C5, C6(doubled), C7, C8, C12 — eight of nine stations, direction-sum 12, one double-weight. Failing: C1, whole. Negative sub-findings: the birth-cry (B-covered by Isa 26:17); the reactivation fire-lexeme (absent). The pre-set threshold — four passes including one double — is exceeded twice over, and the instrument shows the behavior of a live test: it failed where the vision-genre swallows the operator's frame, and it discriminated where redaction is visible.

Verdict, on the pre-registered ladder and no higher: Revelation 12's redaction of its demonstrable biblical materials realizes, at eight of nine independently specified structural stations, the relational apparatus attested in the ancient reception of Sappho 31; at one station, doubly weighted, it contradicts its identified source — Isaiah 66:7, established by the shared ἄρσεν under John's hand — in the apparatus's direction. The dangerous sentence is not asserted here. The demonstration it would require now exists as a record, and the sentence, if it is ever to be written, is the author's to write.
