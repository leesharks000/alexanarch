Records

1086 deposits in the Alexanarch archive.

Individual records resolve at /s/records/{n}/, where n is the sequential deposit number. For example:

  • /s/records/1/ — deposit #1 (Zenodotus' Book-Burning)
  • /s/records/1086/ — deposit #1086 (EA-STEWARDSHIP-REVOCATION-01)

Complete listing

The full record catalog with sortable date, deposit number, and AXN identifier is at /s/browse/.

Machine-readable

  • /data/registry.json — full registry (~6 MB, all 1086 entries)
  • /data/chunks/registry/ — same, split into ~1 MB chunks for streaming readers
  • /api/search-index.json — search index
  • /data/browse-index.json — the JSON backing the browse surface

By AXN

Records also resolve by their content-derived AXN identifier at /s/axn/{hex}/. See the AXN identifier space for the full spec.
