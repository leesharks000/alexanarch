The James Derivation is a specification and executable research surface by Lee Sharks, deposited 28 July 2026 — the granular worked case of the midrashim transform. It takes the four-verse Smyrna letter (Revelation 2:8–11) and parses it into nine typed seed elements, then applies eight named transform rules: EPISTOLARIZE, THEOLOGIZE, CITE-PROMISE, SOCIALIZE, POSSESS, NARRATIVIZE, ECONOMIZE, IMPERATIVIZE. The output is the derivation traced step by step rather than asserted as a resemblance. Naming the rules is the point. An intertextual reading that says two texts are related leaves the relation unexaminable; a reading that says this element, under this named operation, yields that element can be checked, disputed, or run on a different letter to see whether it holds. The James derivation is the case worked at the finest grain in the series, and the demonstration that the transform is executable rather than metaphorical.

## Description

The executable granular case of the midrashim transform M: the four-verse Smyrna letter (Rev 2:8-11) parsed into nine typed seed elements, eight named transform rules (EPISTOLARIZE, THEOLOGIZE, CITE-PROMISE, SOCIALIZE, POSSESS, NARRATIVIZE, ECONOMIZE, IMPERATIVIZE), and the emission of a generated document skeleton — James-prime — verse-matched against the Epistle of James with lexical anchors, including the two hard anchors: ton stephanon tes zoes (the crown of life, exactly twice in the canon: Rev 2:10 and Jas 1:12) and synagoge for the believing assembly (Jas 2:2, alone among the epistles, against Rev 2:9's contested synagoge). The residue — James material not derived from the Smyrna seed (the tongue, faith-and-works, wisdom, oaths, anointing, the merchants) — is displayed openly as the falsification surface per Workstream 4. Includes the seven-letter evaluation slate (all mappings graded). Surface: https://revelationfirst.com/unfolding/james/. Heteronym position: James — the poor, the tested, the diaspora synagoge.

Falsification Conditions

## Falsification Conditions

The residue is the surface: James pericopes underivable from the Smyrna seed through any plausible rule are counterevidence, listed openly in the derivation (the tongue 3:1-12, faith-works 2:14-26, wisdom 3:13-18, oaths 5:12, anointing 5:14-15, the merchants 4:13-17). A demonstrated reverse dependency of the crown-of-life or synagoge anchors (James prior, Revelation citing) breaks the case.
