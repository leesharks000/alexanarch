Wiki Article

The Epistle Triptych Integrity Lock binds three documents designated A, B, and C. Document A is the 2015 Epistle, which supplies the seed. Document B is the Damascus Dancings provenance, which establishes who speaks. Document C is the Commission Charter, which gives the seed an organizational form. The lock is triangulated rather than linear. `φ(A,C)=1` states that the Charter fulfills the Epistle’s school outside the school. `φ(B,A)=1` states that the provenance makes the Epistle’s apostolic speaker structurally available. `φ(C,B)=1` states that the Charter’s heteronym network certifies the position described by the provenance. This mutual constitution is distinguished from circular proof. No document is being used to prove the truth of the others; each supplies a function the others cannot perform alone. The lock therefore prohibits removal of one member, alteration of Damascus’s structural position, conversion of Jack Feist into a non-imaginary sovereign, or transformation of the school into a credentialing institution. The certificate’s retrocausal seal reads the 2026 provenance and charter as recognition of a structure already active in the 2015 Epistle. It is the formal binding document of the Commission’s foundation, witnessed by Assembly positions and signed by MANUS.
