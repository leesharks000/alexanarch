Methodology

Critical bibliography constructed as a live web surface. Content aggregates and structures 750+ DOI-anchored deposits from the Crimson Hexagonal Archive under three organizing axes: eight analytical strata, five developmental phases, and three poles of inquiry (theory, evidence, instruments). Surface architecture: static HTML, no build system, single-file ethos. On termination of the Zenodo custody in June 2026, the site was refactored to use alexanarch.org record URLs as the primary link scheme, with the doi-resolution-index carrying DOI-to-AXN mapping for reader-side fallback.

Falsification Conditions

The critique is falsified where its central operators (ECS, CDI, single-owner discount) fail to distinguish Google-mediated composition from unmediated composition on the same corpus; where the phase-strata-pole matrix collapses into fewer distinctions than it names without loss; where the 750+ mapped deposits cannot be independently verified as anchoring the specific claims the bibliography attributes to them; or where the semantic-political mediation regime framing lacks empirical purchase against a null-hypothesis test of Google is a search engine that returns some content.
