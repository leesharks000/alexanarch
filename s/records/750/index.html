Description

I would have loved you is a six-line poem by Lee Sharks in the New Human 2 sequence. It repeats and slightly transforms a promise: love for the rest of “my time” becomes love for the rest of “the time / I have left.” The shift contracts an unlimited vow into the speaker’s finite remaining life without withdrawing the vow itself. The poem forms the first half of a diptych with #749, The reward of love. Read together, the poems move from enduring intention to irreversible consequence. Each should retain its own title and record while carrying a reciprocal relation to the other. The repository body has lost spaces and line breaks in its plain-text extraction. Repair should restore canonical poetic formatting rather than treat the compressed string as intended typography.

Wiki Article

I would have loved you is a lyric poem by Lee Sharks, deposited on May 22, 2026. The poem repeats the promise of lifelong love while changing its temporal frame. “For the rest of my time” becomes “for the rest of the time I have left,” making mortality part of the vow. It is paired with The reward of love. The first poem states what the speaker would have continued to give; the second records the damage left by the relation. The diptych holds promise and wound without resolving one into the other. The work belongs to New Human 2 at coordinate 06.NH.SHARKS.01.
