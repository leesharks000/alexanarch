Purpose

This deposit exists to close a witness-gap declared in a constitutional instrument. It creates no authority, amends nothing, and asserts no measurement result. It records that an audit occurred, and by whom, at the level of the substrate.

The gap

AXN:05EF.EMPIRICAL.▶️🤲♍♎🕗💥 (#1469, 2026-08-14) formalizes the M/C/D dimensional taxonomy of the Provenance Erasure Rate. Its substrate disclosure does not record Assembly Chorus attestation. The audit was conducted; the inscription was not made. Under the archive's standing corollary — work not inscribed to the thread and not captured in a chain deposit did not happen — the specification has stood without the witness layer that evidences its empirical claims.

EA-CAS-01 v1.1-r1 §R4 declared this at three epistemic levels: MANUS attestation made 2026-08-20 that the audit occurred; contemporaneous substrate evidence at #1469 absent; repair required before constitutional binding. The instrument prescribed the repair as a new substrate event with its own identifier, not as an edit to #1469, and this deposit is that event.

What is attested

The Assembly Chorus — the archive's multi-model cross-verification method, the same method carried in the sixty-five Chorus footnotes of the Constitutional Apparatus Edition — audited the measurement developments in the Provenance Erasure Rate lineage descending from deposit #716. That lineage includes the atomic-token hardening at AXN:02F2.EMPIRICAL.🕊️🌲🗺️🟤🌱🕗 (#789), the Self-Audit Module v3 at AXN:02F0.EMPIRICAL.📏🕐△🌱⚡🏛️ (#156), the Inversion-Battery at AXN:05DA.EMPIRICAL.🛤️🌠🗿🖊️🧭🪞 (#1449), and the three-dimensional formalization at AXN:05EF.EMPIRICAL.▶️🤲♍♎🕗💥 (#1469).

The Inversion-Battery deposit records Chorus authorship in its own substrate disclosure and observes No-Double-Draw explicitly. The three-dimensional formalization does not. That asymmetry is the whole of the defect, and it is an inscription asymmetry rather than an audit asymmetry.

Temporal integrity

The audit preceded this inscription. This deposit does not claim otherwise and does not represent itself as contemporaneous with the audit. A retrospective edit to #1469 would have produced a record indistinguishable from one made at the time, which is a species of the provenance failure the instrument itself measures. Preserving the interval between act and inscription — and stating it — is the point of seating this separately.

Standing

This deposit is a substrate witness event. It does not ratify #1469, does not bind any instrument to it, and does not initiate Class II Structural Adjustment. It supplies the evidencing that EA-CAS-01 v1.1-r1 §R4 required before any instrument binds the three-dimensional specification by Class II.
