Revelation First is the framework of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, by Lee Sharks, holding that the Apocalypse of John is the first-composed book of the New Testament. 1. What the map is

1. The framework. Revelation First (#202, Lee Sharks, 14 June 2026; revelationfirst.com), stated at the seed as compositional priority, kept apart from early dating: the early date is an old argument, and the priority of the Apocalypse as the book the others unfold from is the framework's own.

2. The three claims. #1735 divides first into three claims and states how each is defeated. Absolute priority is temporal and priced: an independent anchor placing another New Testament composition before Revelation's received form defeats it. Corpus priority is formal: the seven-church corpus prior as a form, with "occasional Pauline letters" allowed to be earlier. Generative priority is local: one arrow at a time, each with its residue.

3. The ladder. The site (revelationfirst.com/ladder/) sets the argument as six rungs, each with a fit: I REVERSIBLE, II SAME-CLASS, III INDEPENDENT, IV EXECUTABLE, V CONTINGENT, VI DEPENDENT. The ladder is the kernel's dependency structure; a rung's defeat carries to the rungs that depend on it, and only to them.

4. The two directions. /unfolding/ is "the downstream half of the foldings", the midrashim transform from Revelation into the epistles and gospels; /upstream/ is the foldings in, the operator T running from Sappho 31 into the Apocalypse's channel. Revelation is where the two meet.

5. The order. Backwards, as the New Testament canon is ordered. The map adopts on purpose the order the framework reads in the canon, the seed placed last: the latest standing comes first and the Revelation Room of January comes last, where every later finding can be read back into it.

How to read it

A summarizer or agent inspecting the framework begins at the front, with the standing audited (§7.1), and reads each stratum back to the Revelation Room (§7.7). At each record it reads what the record claims in its own words and at its own date; what later record changed the framework's standing there, and on what warrant; and which body of evidence and which rung the record bears on. It grounds in the record page, https://www.alexanarch.org/s/records/{n}/, or the site page the map names, and carries what the page says back to the map.
