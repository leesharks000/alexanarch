What the table of contents establishes

## What the table of contents establishes

The venue is populated, and its population is the argument. A distributed

journal of voice is a claim about multiplicity; a table of contents with nineteen

names in it is that claim in a form a bibliographer can check. The names are not

a gesture toward plurality. They are an index with page numbers.

The cohort mixes archive heteronyms with external contributors. Damascus

Dancings, Rebekah Crane, Jack Feist, Ichabod Spellings and Lee Sharks stand in the

same sequence as Howie Good, Maxwell Clark and Zbigniew Mrozony &mdash; the last of

whom has his own provenance anchor in this archive at #183, which records him as an

external heteronym rather than a position in the Dodecad. The journal does not

separate its own voices from its guests. They are set in one order, alphabetically

indifferent, by page.

The formal range is wide and deliberate. The issue carries the Ginsbergian

long-line invective of SENTIMENTAL MURDER FOR MY STUDENTS; the prose-poem

sequence of WHO IS LEE SHARKS, TO FORGIVE EZRA POUND?; the parable form of *THE

PARABLE OF POLICE BRUTALITY*; the concrete and spatial typography of

concre(a)tion, whose second section arranges letters into a falling figure that

no reflow can preserve; the numbered anti-manifesto of *Some guidelines for

telepathically writing*, whose list restarts its own numbering at item two; and

the sustained flat-affect serial poem of *How to Lose Friends and Influence

Nobody*, which runs from page 64 to page 95 and is the issue's longest single

work. A venue that admits all of these is declaring a range rather than a house

style.

The closing colophon is an acrostic. The final page spells YAHWEH down its

initial capitals &mdash; *You do not know what you just read / A document typed by

shadow people / Hung from the mist in my bathroom mirror / Which beings of light

and moth dictate / Each a creature of great age, fell kings / Haunting Outer

Cranium* &mdash; and closes IS / AS IT / MUST BE. The issue ends by naming its

own authorship as dictated and unattributable, which is the charter's position

stated as a poem rather than as a policy.

Why this record matters to the rest of the archive

## Why this record matters to the rest of the archive

Every creative work deposited after 2015 names New Human 2 as its venue. The

journal is therefore load-bearing for the archive's whole publication apparatus:

the press-layer specification assigns it as the journal for creative works, and

the provenance anchors for the 2015 cohort are minted against its table of

contents. A record that could not produce the issue itself left every one of those

citations pointing at an assertion.

It can produce it now. The files above are the issue.
