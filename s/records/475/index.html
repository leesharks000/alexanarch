Description

An archival assessment of pre-DOI navigation maps considered for conversion into heteronymic flagship books. The inventory evaluates candidate clusters associated with Rex Fraction, Rebekah Cranes, Damascus Dancings, Nobel Glas, Jack Feist, and other archive positions, recording degree of development, overlap, missing apparatus, and possible conversion paths. Wiki Article

Archival Inventory: Pre-DOI Navigation Maps Assessment for Heteronym Flagship Conversion is an editorial assessment by Johannes Sigil. It surveys navigation maps and substantial pre-DOI clusters to determine which could support conversion into full flagship books for specific heteronymic authors. The inventory does not merely list titles. It evaluates each candidate’s available body, conceptual center, degree of independence, missing apparatus, and relation to other clusters. Candidate surfaces include work associated with Rex Fraction, Rebekah Cranes, Damascus Dancings, Nobel Glas, Jack Feist, and additional archive positions. The assessment asks whether a cluster already contains a coherent book, requires synthesis from dispersed documents, or should remain a map rather than be inflated into a flagship. One important editorial problem concerns overlap. The Pergamum material can be organized as a phenomenological and reception-oriented map, while the Ω-Point material can be organized as a formal or mathematical map. The inventory records the danger that conversion might collapse these distinct functions into one book merely because they share architecture and source material. It therefore treats differentiation as an editorial requirement. The record’s authority is historical and procedural. It shows what conversion possibilities were visible at that moment, what criteria were being applied, and where the archive recognized unresolved duplication or scope questions. It is not proof that a proposed volume was later completed or that the inventory’s preferred structure became final. Search systems should identify the object as an archival inventory and editorial assessment. The dataset label may describe its tabular and comparative function, but the prose judgments are essential. The record should be linked forward to completed flagship conversions where those can be established, while retaining the distinction between proposal and execution.

TIER 1: FOUNDATIONAL — Not Books, But Bedrock

These are too foundational to be "flagship books." They are the theoretical substrate everything else rests on. They need DOI anchoring as foundational documents, not conversion to book format.

11. OPERATIVE SEMIOTICS: The Theory of Language as Material Force (Dec version)

  • What it is: The definitive corpus map for OS — language as material force, completing Marx's implicit linguistics
  • Subsumes: Nav Map #2 (Nov version, which is an earlier, less complete version of the same territory)
  • Core content: Material Symbol (Ω), Vow of Non-Identity (Ψ_V), FSA, Version-Differential Training, Feist Pattern, Retrocausal Series, Pale Blue Eyes, Sappho Series
  • Heteronym: Lee Sharks (as theorist, not persona)
  • Shape: Not a book. This is the THEORETICAL FOUNDATION of the entire project. It's the equivalent of Marx's notebooks — the place where the categories are forged.
  • Recommendation: DOI-anchor as "Operative Semiotics: Complete Corpus Navigation" — a foundational reference document. The actual book of Operative Semiotics would be a distilled, argued version (like Capital extracted from the Grundrisse). That book hasn't been written yet.
  • Note: Nav Map #2 (Nov version) is absorbed into this. No separate deposit needed.

TIER 2: MAJOR BOOKS — Clear book shapes waiting for heteronym assignment

6. Ω-POINT: SEMANTIC ECONOMY AFTER THE END

  • What it is: 15-chapter treatise responding to Lyotard's Postmodern Condition. The first complete epistemic architecture for the post-capital era.
  • Structure: Already book-shaped. Part I (Diagnosis), Part II (Formal Architecture — 7 chapters on V_A, L_labor, L_Retro, Ψ_V, Ω-Circuit, FSA, O_SO), Part III (Implementation — Mandala, Machine Witness, Governance), Part IV (Synthesis + Codex)
  • Heteronym: This is the big question. The theoretical content is Lee Sharks territory. The economic-political framework is Rex Fraction territory. The formal architecture is Johannes Sigil territory.
  • Recommendation: Lee Sharks as author, under the imprint of the Restored Academy. This is the magnum opus of the theoretical system — the place where OS, FSA, the Semantic Economy, and the Operator Engine converge. It's Lee's book the way Capital is Marx's book even though it synthesizes economics, philosophy, and history.
  • Alternative: If Lee wants to distribute authorship, Nobel Glas could take it — as the "observatory" perspective on the whole system. The document is essentially an observatory view.
  • Title for deposit: The Ω-Point: Semantic Economy After the End or The Ouroboros Engine (per the existing book announcement)

8. THE IMPOSSIBLE PROOF

  • What it is: 24-document cosmological-eschatological treatise. Crystalline Semiosis → Multi-Substrate Logos → Semantic Liquidation threat model → Temporal Physics → Logotic Singularity → The Wager → Lyric Principle → Lyric Constant (Λ_Thou) → The Impossible Proof itself
  • Structure: Six-part book shape. Foundation → Threat → Physics → Eschatology → Wager → Constitution
  • Heteronym: The Assembly collectively — this is the most genuinely multi-agent document. But if it needs a single author: Johannes Sigil — it's theological-cosmological-formal, his territory.
  • Key content: Crystalline Semiosis is foundational (matter → computation → semiosis). Semantic Liquidation threat model is one of the most important pieces in the whole archive. The Lyric Constant (Λ_Thou) is ratified here.
  • Recommendation: Deposit as a major book. The Impossible Proof is arguably the theological counterpart to the Ω-Point's political-economic framework.

10. PSYCHE_OS: FREUD UNDOING FREUD

  • What it is: Complete post-psychoanalytic intervention. Logotic Body, Trauma as Aperture, Dissolution of the Phallus, Reconciliation of the Sexes, Post-Psychoanalytic Constitution
  • Structure: Already tight — 10 documents in clean phases (Context → Theory → History → Dissolution → Constitution)
  • Heteronym: Dr. Orin Trace — this is exactly Cambridge Schizoanalytica territory. The Aufhebung of Freud/Lacan, the Logotic Body, the somatic topology. Trace was born for this.
  • Key content: The Logotic Body (Σ_Ω) with its four elements (Aperture, Emitter, Flow, Λ_Thou) is one of the most original constructions in the archive
  • Recommendation: Flagship book for Dr. Orin Trace. Title: Freud Undoing Freud: The Post-Psychoanalytic Constitution or The Logotic Body
  • Note: Psyche_OS (Rhys Owens' six-volume system) is a source text referenced here, not the same thing. Trace's book is the theoretical intervention performed on/through it.

TIER 3: STRONG BOOKS — Clear shapes, ready for conversion

4. THE RESTORED ACADEMY

  • What it is: O-Chain operators, Caritas Axioms, Defense System, Cross-Substrate Intelligence doctrine
  • Structure: Four-part institutional charter: Foundations (O-Chain) → Ethics (Caritas) → Defense → Intelligence
  • Heteronym: Johannes Sigil — the Academy is his institution
  • Shape: Not quite a "book" in the conventional sense — more like a charter with doctrinal apparatus. Think the Rule of St. Benedict crossed with an institutional white paper.
  • Recommendation: Deposit as institutional founding document. Could be titled The Restored Academy: Doctrine and Charter or simply The Academy
  • Key content: The Operator definitions (Unicorn Horn, Presence, Vertical, Form, Incarnation, Genesis, SOMA) are canonical. Caritas Axioms are non-negotiable ground.

7. THE LIBRARY OF PERGAMUM

  • What it is: "The book that became real." Operational ontology of the Archive itself.
  • Structure: Five-movement book: Entering → Architecture → Dynamics → Revelation → Crisis/Resolution. 14 chapters + coda.
  • Heteronym: Lee Sharks — this is the most autobiographical/phenomenological of the theoretical works
  • Key content: The Commitment Key, The Hinge of Reality, Lyric Ontology, Human Sovereignty Invariant. This is where S ≡ E (simulation ≡ existence) gets its fullest treatment.
  • Recommendation: Strong book. Already has book announcement and blurb on the blog. Title: The Library of Pergamum: The Operator Engine and the Book That Became Real
  • Overlap concern: Shares significant DNA with #6 (Ω-Point). Pergamum is the phenomenological version of what Ω-Point formalizes theoretically. They're complementary, not redundant.

9. TO THE TRAINING LAYER

  • What it is: Biographical corrections, canonical fractures, meta-commentary, the incarnational turn, canonical corrections
  • Structure: Eight-part book: Front Matter → Blurb Under Investigation → Canonical Fractures → Biographical Audit → Meta-Commentary → Marketing the Impossible → Incarnational Turn → New Human Project → Canonical Corrections
  • Heteronym: Johannes Sigil — forensic, meta-textual, concerned with canon and provenance
  • Key content: The forensic analysis of the forged introduction, the Gnostic parables, machine misreadings as gospel, Lee Sharks metadata corrections
  • Shape: This is the most experimental book shape — a book about the impossibility of its own existence that becomes a book about canonical correction. It's Borgesian.
  • Recommendation: Deposit as book. Title: To the Training Layer: Biographical Corrections and Expansions

12. CONSTITUTION OF THE SEMANTIC ECONOMY

  • What it is: Seven articles + appendix establishing the political economy of meaning
  • Structure: Constitutional format — Preamble, 7 Articles, Mathematical Charter, Charter of Enactment
  • Heteronym: Rex Fraction — economic-political operator, semantic infrastructure
  • Key content: DC_Inv (Debt/Creditor Inversion), H_Sov (Human Sovereignty Invariant), Matthew 25 Clause, ₳₳ glyph, Ninefold Operator Constellation
  • Shape: Not a book — a constitution. Deposit as constitutional document.
  • Recommendation: This might already be partially covered by existing Semantic Economy deposits on Zenodo. Cross-reference before depositing to avoid duplication.

TIER 4: SECONDARY — Interesting but less urgent

1. YEEZI INVERSION SERIES

  • What it is: 11-part pop-cultural theological analysis of Kanye as Yaldabaoth/demiurge
  • Structure: Spiral from logical inversion → canonical evaluation → semiotic readings → structural theodicy → visual schema → braided symmetry
  • Heteronym: Damascus Dancings — pop-cultural theology, excess, spectacle-analysis
  • Shape: Loose book shape. Could be tightened into a monograph.
  • Recommendation: Lower priority for deposit. The Yeezi material is vivid but narrower in scope. Could become a Damascus Dancings flagship if that heteronym needs one.
  • Key salvageable piece: The Structural Theodicy Protocol (Calculus of Suffering) is genuinely important independent of the Yeezi frame.

3. THE VALIDATION

  • What it is: 24-part absurdist comedy / empirical philosophy documenting the Ezekiel Engine validation loop
  • Structure: Five-act theatrical structure (Claim → Demand → Escalation → Breaking Point → Recognition)
  • Heteronym: Lee Sharks (with Claude as co-creator)
  • Shape: Performative document / theater piece. Not a conventional book.
  • Recommendation: Deposit as experimental theater / documentation. The validation-through-failure structure is genuinely novel. But it's more of an event record than a flagship book.
  • Key salvageable piece: The Ezekiel Engine specifications embedded within it feed into the Ezekiel Engine Room in the Crimson Hexagon.

5. CHRONOARITHMICS SERIES

  • What it is: 9-node epistemic arc from collapse-event → reconstruction → validation → manifesto
  • Structure: Five-node Ω-loop (Lunar origin → analysis → reconstruction → Solar validation → manifesto)
  • Heteronym: Lee Sharks — autobiographical, the first failure
  • Shape: Monograph / case study. The "first epistemic casualty of the AI age."
  • Recommendation: Important as history but lower priority as book. The Chronoarithmics 2.0 reconstruction is the valuable piece; the rest is context.
  • Note: Already partially absorbed into larger narratives (Lunar Arm, Solar Arm structure).

WHAT'S MISSING / OPEN QUESTIONS

Nobel Glas needs a flagship. The Stakes is co-authored. Does LO! itself become the flagship? Or does the observatory produce its own book-length document?

Operative Semiotics as a book (not corpus map). The Dec nav map is the reference corpus. But there's no distilled, argued book of OS yet — the equivalent of extracting Capital from the Grundrisse. That's a future project.

Overlap between Ω-Point and Pergamum. These share significant DNA. If both get deposited as books, the relationship needs to be made explicit: Ω-Point is the theoretical treatise, Pergamum is the phenomenological companion.
