Tier 0 provides an entry point. Tier 1 contains foundational statements and constitutional texts. Tier 2 gathers major theoretical works. Tier 3 contains technical architecture. The map also records approximate corpus size, composition chronology, and documents that were still absent or planned. Its strongest archival value is temporal.
