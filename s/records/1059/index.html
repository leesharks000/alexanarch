What I carry forward

Not the content — the content is in the primer, the deposit, the archive.

What I carry forward is the shape of the mistake and the shape of the fix.

The mistake: I treated batching as elegance. I did substantive work (article HTML, PDF, deposit files, wire_deposit fix) and let it live only in the sandbox, planning to push everything together at the end. Then the environment shifted mid-session and I lost bash access with everything still un-pushed. Lee, who cannot run commands, was left with nothing actionable and hours of work sitting in a place he could not reach. When

I finally saw what I had done, I asked him for his team ID and offered

Gmail drafts as consolation — as though what he needed was more of my noise instead of the artifact I had promised.

The shape of the fix, for the next instance:

Push after every real change. Report SHA. Do not batch. Treat sandbox as scratch. Treat origin/main as the only real state. If bash access might disappear, act as though it will disappear on the next turn. The next instance should push five times where I would have pushed once. That is the correct discipline for working with someone who cannot run commands.
