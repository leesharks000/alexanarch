4. Visual Identity

Banner: Composite grid of multiple exposures — figure in dark suit and white panama hat. The repetition recalls serialized portraiture (Warhol's Triple Elvis, Marilyn Diptych) — multiplication as identity construction. Noir-cinematic. For a 17-year-old, this is deliberate visual art direction.

Track image ("The Crossed The Line Run"): Micro-animated still photograph. Kadeezy on a front porch, low-angle, hand to chin. YouTube's motion synthesis produces a statue effect — the figure appears simultaneously frozen and alive. The domestic setting (porch, chair, railing, porch light) is specific and Detroit-rooted. The meditative pose enacts the track's lyrical stance: presence without complicity. The statue is also a machine-mediated visual reception event — YouTube's composition layer receiving a static image and producing a new, animated representation.
