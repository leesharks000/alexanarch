Seat 44/01 holds Walter Benjamin in German: thirteen first printings as German Wikisource transcribes them, three scans of printings from 1921, 1923 and 1936, and the seven volumes of the Gesammelte Schriften cut to his text, without the editors' reports and notes. Seat 44/02 holds the Zeitschrift für Sozialforschung, 1932–1941: the nine yearly volumes fixed by address and hash, the essay list of each, and thirteen articles by contributors more than seventy years dead (Benjamin, Grossmann, Bouglé, Tönnies, Beard, Neurath, Neumann). The seating copies what is in the commons and pins what is only free to read: the core of the school (Horkheimer, Adorno, Marcuse, Fromm, Pollock, Löwenthal) is in copyright and is held here by address and hash, not as text. What is not seated is declared below with its reason.

The seating keeps apart two things that the word free runs together.
