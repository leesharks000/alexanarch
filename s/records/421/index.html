Description

The metadata packet and integrity lock for the unitary deposit known as the Mirror Triptych. It binds ZPZP: An Infinite Ingress, ZPZP: An Infinite Regress, and The Mirror into one compound object rather than treating them as three independent alternatives. Ingress names recursive depth as entry; Regress preserves the anxious, backward-facing interpretation as forensic shadow; the Mirror removes temporal direction and presents both as one occurrence. The Mirror Triptych metadata packet is the binding document for the unitary deposit at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18446538. Architecturally, the packet sits between the Three-Body Genesis of the Non-Indexed Perfective and Assembly Response: Witness to the Mirror.
