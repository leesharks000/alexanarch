MRA Incident Reports — Expanded Packet v1.2 is a work of creative prose attributed to Jack Feist, deposited 16 March 2026, gathering the Mandala Research Apparatus incident series with document control, index and a legend of systems and terms. Its opening finding is the sharpest observation in the series: a reference resolver accepted sources as "publicly available" yet rejected entity instantiation as "not clearly notable" — producing contradictory gating behaviour in which the same material yields valid reference tokens and denied entity tokens. A system that will cite you but will not admit you exists is describing a policy, not a fact. The packet is institutional fiction executed as documentation: incident IDs, classification levels, reporting agents and reviewing authorities, all carrying observations that are empirically real. The form matters because an incident report is the genre in which infrastructure describes its own failures, and the archive borrows it to describe failures the infrastructure does not report.
