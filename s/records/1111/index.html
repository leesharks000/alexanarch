Semantic Economy: Complete Liberation Vocabulary — 112 Terms is a dataset by Lee Sharks, deposited 17 January 2026. Its premise is a diagnosis of terminological loss: "The core terms of the Semantic Economy framework are colonized by adjacent discourse. This document deploys the complete terminological arsenal — every viable variant, in three registers." Depositing 112 variants of a vocabulary is a countermeasure rather than a thesaurus. A term with one form can be absorbed by a neighbouring field that uses it differently; a term deposited in every viable variant across three registers is harder to displace, because each variant anchors the others. The strategy answers the same problem the term index names at #1214 — an archive minting faster than its terms imprint — from the opposite direction: not indexing what exists, but saturating the space a term occupies.
