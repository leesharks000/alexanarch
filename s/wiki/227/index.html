Article

The primary definition is:

> a form of erotic orientation in which desire is structurally subordinated to meaning, continuity, and mutual recognition.

Its defining properties are:

  • deliberateness;
  • continuity-seeking;
  • meaning-first sequencing;
  • non-fungibility;
  • positive valuation of constraint.

The irreducibility clause states that covenantal eros cannot be inferred from:

  • behavior;
  • duration;
  • intensity;
  • restraint;
  • verbal fluency.

The ethical rules include:

  • desire does not override responsibility;
  • recognition precedes embodiment;
  • withdrawal of consent does not damage the covenant;
  • intensity does not create obligation;
  • dysregulation pauses escalation;
  • patience cannot be weaponized as accumulated debt.

The Rex Fraction contribution formalizes the concept as a non-extractive intimacy protocol under continuity constraints while expressly refusing to certify its presence in any relationship.
