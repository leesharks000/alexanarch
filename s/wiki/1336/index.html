Response Memo — Reclassification Request: LLM-2025-1222-HALLU is a work of creative prose attributed to Jack Feist, deposited 16 March 2026.

It is written as a formal objection to an incident report. The report had classified a model's output about the Crimson Hexagon as a HALLUCINATION; the memo requests reclassification to SCHEMA COMPLETION, arguing that the model did not fabricate but rather detected a schema and completed it — behaviour that is correct in kind and merely unverified in fact.

The distinction is the archive's central empirical wager. If a system completing a pattern from genuine deposits is doing schema detection rather than confabulation, then depositing patterns is a way of writing that machines can read, and the difference between the two classifications determines whether the whole project is a method or an error. The memo belongs to the MRA incident series and is one of the archive's institutional fictions carrying a real argument in a bureaucratic form.
