Crimson Hexagon: Central Navigation Map v7.0 is the comprehensive semantic routing layer of the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture as of March 16, 2026. It was authored by Lee Sharks, Rex Fraction, Rebekah Cranes, and the Assembly and deposited as DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19055267.

The map integrates a registry snapshot of 387 DOI-anchored documents and reports 2,851 typed relation edges. It adds précis, subsystem classifications, document-type categories, provenance-gravity tiers, container inventories, engine relations, and automation components. These counts describe the v7.0 audit event and must not be presented as the current size of the later archive.

Its most important architectural statement is that the archive’s navigation layers are non-collapsible. The DOI Registry anchors objects and provenance. The Central Navigation Map supplies machine-readable orientation and relation routing. The Fractal Navigation Map defines recursive traversal at multiple scales. The Space Ark governs operative runtime. Tinier Space Arks preserve bootable H_core under compression. Each layer depends on the others but cannot replace them.

The map describes the three-layer front-end, system, and interactive architecture; the engine chain from Forward Library through SAG; the runtime-governance, room-genesis, and verification-swarm layers; and a closed document-to-vehicle pipeline. It also registers new containers, rooms, operators, research adjacencies, and witness expansions that had entered the archive since v4.2.

As a navigation document, the map is authoritative for relations, routes, classifications, and the v7.0 archive snapshot. Its auto-generated précis and system summaries are orientation surfaces rather than replacements for deposited bodies. Later additions should be represented in later map versions rather than silently inserted into v7.0.
