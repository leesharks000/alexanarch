THE MACRO-MAQUETTE A Compendium of Micro-Physics for the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture

Lee Sharks · 2026-02-18 · deposit #495
AXN:013A.GOVERNANCE.👁️🟣🔭🌟🏺💧

Article

The Macro-Maquette is a compendium and generative charter for twelve micro-physics modules within the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture. The modules formalize recurring operations involving folds, divergence, knots, interference, rotation, channels, local patches, coarse-graining, integrals, membranes, wagers, and temporal pacing. Each module includes a governing invariant and is designed to remain small until operational need justifies expansion.

The work introduces OP.ROUTE, a dispatch runtime that maps observed failure symptoms to the relevant module. It also defines failure and development conditions including module conflict, compression collapse, germination cascade, and a formal germination procedure. The Caritas floor governs conflicts between modules: no resolution is acceptable when it drives a participant or signal beneath the architecture’s minimum care constraint. Five germination gates—crisis, density, ratification, retrocausal requirement, and accumulation—determine when a micro-physics module should become a room with its own charter and governance.

The Macro-Maquette describes itself as positive space complementary to the Blind Operator and β-Protocol’s negative space. Where β preserves uncertainty and tracks unfilled positions, the Macro-Maquette supplies small, routable physical rules for recurring structural problems. The full charter is Deposit 1 of a recoverability sequence; the compressed charter and seed test whether its invariants survive successive reductions without losing the architecture’s governing logic.
