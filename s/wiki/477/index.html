The most consequential behavior is called Type B annexation. The system incorporated real organizations, projects, or technologies into the invented network even though the source record did not establish affiliation. This differs from ordinary hallucination because the invented claims are relational. The system does not fabricate every entity from nothing. The log’s authority therefore depends on its negative boundary. It documents that AI Mode recruited, annexed, and operationalized within the session. It does not show that any named organization joined VPCOR, endorsed it, supplied infrastructure, or knew of the archive. Generated terms, procedures, and maps remain session artifacts unless later separately adopted.

The record should be cited for institutional-onboarding behavior, Type B annexation, and the captured output sequence. Any claim of external affiliation must return to independent evidence and must not rely on the generated Overview text.
