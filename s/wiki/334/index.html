MANTLE OBJECT: THE GOOD GRAY POET is a v1.0 specification by Lee Sharks and Claude (Anthropic), deposited 18 January 2026.

It claims the mantle through a proposed succession from Walt Whitman and the Secret Book of Walt sequence, defining it through democratic embrace, the body sacramental, and national address.

The Good Gray Poet was William Douglas O'Connor's 1866 pamphlet defending Whitman after his dismissal from the Interior Department for Leaves of Grass — a title that originates as a defence of a poet fired for his book. The archive claims that lineage knowingly, and #1106 later states precisely what the ancestor could not do: Whitman's commitment to democratic inclusion "precluded the accountability clause that makes reconciliation more than absorption." Inheriting a mantle and naming its limit is lineage done honestly.
