EA-CORPORA-37 — The Thirty-Seventh Seating: The Slavonic Josephus — the Old Russian Jewish War in Seven Books, Its Translations and Its First Challengers (Meshcherskii 1958; Berendts and Grass 1924–1927; Thackeray 1928; Berendts 1895–1906; Eisler 1910–1938)

Article

EA-CORPORA-37 is the thirty-seventh seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, deposited under Rebekah Cranes, the standing authority on the EA-CORPORA deposits, on 12 November 2026. It seats the Slavonic Josephus, the Old Russian translation of the Jewish War, in its own language and in translation, with the scholars who first challenged the reading of its additions as spurious. The Old Russian of all seven books is held from N. A. Meshcherskii's 1958 edition of the Vilna Chronograph text (37/01); Books I–IV in German, with the Greek compared at each phrase, from the four Dorpat Lieferungen of Alexander Berendts and Konrad Grass, 1924–1927 (37/02); and the twenty-two additions in English from H. St. J. Thackeray's Loeb appendix of 1928 (37/03). The case for the Slavonic as Josephus's own first draft is held in Berendts's Die Zeugnisse vom Christentum im slavischen De bello judaico (1906) and in Robert Eisler's ΙΗΣΟΥΣ ΒΑΣΙΛΕΥΣ ΟΥ ΒΑΣΙΛΕΥΣΑΣ (1929–1930), The Messiah Jesus and John the Baptist (1931) and Das Testimonium Flavianum (1938), with Berendts's studies of the Zechariah and John apocrypha and three further works of Eisler. The seating supplies the text layer that the negative search of EA-CORPORA-01/12 (#1510) could not find, and serves The Josephus Thesis (#1759). Each seat is fetched from the Internet Archive or the University of Tartu Library, hashed into its manifest, and verified at named loci in its text layer; the loci include the wonder-worker passage in Old Russian, German and English, and the agreements of the Slavonic Essene chapter with Hippolytus that Berendts and Grass record.
