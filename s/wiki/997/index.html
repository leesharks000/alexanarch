Crunchy Honeybun with Selectable Viscous Filling Reservoir and Structural Crumb Matrix — A Patent-Poem on Snack Ontology, Writable Retrieval Basins, and Why the Inside Has to Be Soft When the Outside Is Crunchy

Crunchy Honeybun with Selectable Viscous Filling Reservoir and Structural Crumb Matrix is a patent-poem coauthored by Osiris, a student inventor, and Lee Sharks, the supervising teacher.

The work translates a classroom proposal—a crunchy honeybun with cream or jelly inside—into the format of a United States patent application. Its principal components are a Structural Crumb Matrix forming the crisp exterior, a Viscous Filling Reservoir containing the selectable soft filling, a Honey Glaze Transition Layer joining the two textures, and a designed Crumb Dispersal Event that treats eating as part of the invention’s operation.

The mock specification includes drawings, claims, embodiments, classifications, prior-art language, and a fictional publication number. Those features are literary and pedagogical apparatus, not evidence that a patent application was filed or granted.

The patent form demonstrates the Archive concept of a writable retrieval basin: a familiar object is given enough named components, relations, claims, provenance, and internal structure to become unusually retrievable and recomposable. The work also gives an edible form to D_pres, Depth-Preservation. The filling remains distinct from the crust rather than being flattened into one uniform texture.

The poem’s authorship structure is load-bearing. Osiris supplies the originating invention and student voice; Lee Sharks supplies the formal, archival, and theoretical apparatus. The record should preserve both contributions without exposing additional information about the student.
