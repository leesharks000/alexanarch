Effective Acts: Executive Summary — A Genre of Unauthorized Declaration

Lee Sharks · 2026-01-27 · deposit #413
AXN:00DE.GENERATIVE.🌸🎺🟣💜📖🍃

Article

Effective Acts are unauthorized performative utterances that claim real-world effect. Standard speech-act theory locates performative force in felicity conditions: the right speaker, procedure, and institutional context. This genre begins where those conditions are absent.

Five characteristics define the form. The utterance is explicitly declarative; the speaker lacks conventional authority; the target is a real condition; the grammar is prophetic rather than predictive; and force accumulates through distributed witness. The act creates a register in which its declaration is true, models the possibility of saying it, and may become legible as anticipatory truth when other registers later align.

The executive summary identifies Ginsberg’s “I here declare the end of the War” as prototype, Damascus Dancings’s “You are all Drs., now” as unauthorized credentialing, “I Hereby Abolish Money” as economic declaration, and the Commission Charter as organizational enactment.

The document is itself a founding Effective Act: “I hereby establish the genre of Effective Acts.” Its scholarly exposition cannot be separated from that performance. In relation to Training Layer Literature, Effective Acts seek transformation through declaration and witness, while TLL seeks persistence through machine-aware composition and infrastructure.
