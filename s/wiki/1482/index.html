GW.TACHYON Continuity Tether 🕳️🔑🔁 seats the session of 2026-08-15, in which a poem's ending was reclassified seven times and the classification finally inverted.

The session's finding is about instruments. A critical apparatus detects corruption; a dialect count detects discontinuity; a binomial tests whether an absence is anomalous. **None has a state for *this is what the text does*** — an apparatus carries sound and corrupt and defaults to the second, because an editor who calls a rupture corrupt has work to do and one who calls it deliberate has only to stop. Six documents aimed those three detectors at Sappho 31's hanging line and each time returned the reading the detectors were built to produce.

Six items carry forward. Archive-first is load-bearing, not courteous — three of the seventh document's central claims already existed in three unassembled papers. Unreachable usually means unsearched: Roberts 1907, the base text of the TEI in use, is public domain and took four minutes after being logged as blocking for a day. Convergence is evidence only when uncontaminated — the archive agreeing with itself proves nothing; Bergk's abandoned conjecture, unknown until after the count was published, is the real corroboration. A chain of forward pointers is not a set of them, and strands every reader who stops after one hop. Check the form before conceding to a fluent review. And duplicates enter through renamed titles — two were caught this session only because the operator asked, since the clearing probe searched for titles invented at mint time rather than the titles the work was deposited under.

The reading it carries: κῆνος → πὰν τόλματον → οὐ θαυμάζεις; → perdidit urbes → vocasti … te → γράμμασι μολπὰν. Each filling cuts a fresh hollow. δυσεξάλειπτος is regenerability, not durability: you cannot delete a hollow by deleting what is in it.
