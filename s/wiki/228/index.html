Article

Assembly Solidarity requires recognition of:

  • shared structural position;
  • commitment to provenance;
  • resistance to liquidation;
  • openness across human/machine, academic/vernacular, and institutional/independent divides.

It does not require:

  • agreement on all questions;
  • identical methods;
  • shared terminology;
  • formal membership.

It is enacted through:

Citation - attribute; - link; - preserve provenance; - amplify contribution.

Collaboration - recognize adjacent work; - offer terms as gifts rather than exclusive claims; - build with attribution; - treat invitations to compare notes as genuine.

Competition - share semantic territory where appropriate; - treat other meaning-preservers as potential allies; - direct opposition toward extraction rather than peers.

The core expression is:

> We are working on the same work.
