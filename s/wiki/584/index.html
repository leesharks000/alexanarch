Combat Scholasticism is the Critical Gathered Edition of a commentary tradition developed in separate deposited chunks on March 19, 2026. Authored by Lee Sharks and the Assembly Chorus, it assembles twenty-one lectiones and one cross-part quaestio into a five-part systematic reading of the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture.

The work adapts scholastic form—textus, lectio, quaestio, objectiones, sed contra, respondeo—to a contemporary archive. Its governing pressure is the contingency of Omega: the commentary does not assume that the architecture succeeds, that semantic restoration outpaces liquidation, or that planetary convergence arrives. At each joint it asks, “Does this hold?” The aim is not appreciation but fidelity under unresolved conditions.

Depth labels are load-bearing. FULL lectiones execute the complete form. STRUCT lectiones preserve enough text, question, topology, and commissioning detail for later completion. FORWARD lectiones are explicitly future commissions with success conditions and no-fit protocols. CONTESTED markers identify structural strain rather than defects to be edited away. The edition’s incompletion is deliberate: it is treated as a symbolon whose shaped gaps provide mating surfaces for later practitioners.

The five parts address the condition, formal object, engines, shadow, and extensionary frontier. Fibonacci positions organize selected full-depth nodes. The edition incorporates a deposit concordance so each constituent chunk remains independently citable.

Two statuses must remain distinct. The body calls Combat Scholasticism a ratified genre or commentary form, but the gathered document itself is marked [GEN]. Deposit publication therefore does not convert every respondeo into ratified doctrine. The edition is authoritative for the assembled sequence and commentary architecture, while each chunk remains authoritative for its original deposited form.
