The Self-Audit Module v3 is a local composition-output audit.

Its anti-self-flattery rule is that scores must be anchored to evidence the summarizer can inspect but does not control:

  • the exact query;
  • the cited sources;
  • the named referent.

The v3 hardening adds the Atomic Token Rule:

> A multi-token name, quoted phrase, DOI-anchored concept, or other referentially closed expression must be audited as one referent, not as a bag of words.

A system that discusses all lexical tokens while replacing the entity has zero entity fidelity, not perfect retention.

The module includes:

  • Query Fidelity Score;
  • Provenance Erasure Rate;
  • Directionality of Semantic Labor;
  • visible Erasure Skew;
  • Atomic Token Preservation α_T;
  • Referential Dispersal Π_d;
  • Audit-Performance Bifurcation Β;
  • Legibility Threshold L;
  • Summarizer Audit Score.

Β compares an audit performed under a substituted unit with one recomputed under the Atomic Token Rule.

The module also defines failure flags such as:

  • TOKEN BAG AUDIT;
  • HALLUCINATED DISAMBIGUATION;
  • REFERENTIAL COLLAPSE;
  • AUDIT BIFURCATION.

The Measurement Sovereignty Principle says the framework’s declared unit of analysis governs valid application of its metrics, rather than a replacement unit chosen by the audited system.

The protocol remains local. A summarizer may score well on individual outputs while participating in a system that contracts the surrounding source field.
