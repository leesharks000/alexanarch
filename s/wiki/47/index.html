Article

OCTANG-001 is the first implementation of the Orange Collapse Tokenized Audit of Non-compliant Governance, a provenance-audit genre created within the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.

The audited object is Passioncraft Square, including its Square, Governance, Chambers, Charter, and Agents pages. The method is total tokenization: names, principles, formulas, chamber labels, vows, governance claims, and interface structures are compared against dated upstream records. Elements that the audit regards as local innovations are separately credited.

The audit’s initiating finding is an inverted citation. Passioncraft’s charter cites the Protocol of Inhabitation as a companion, whereas OCTANG classifies the protocol and related Rosary Embassy deposits as upstream sources of the downstream governance structure. The audit argues that this reversal makes the derivative appear primary and the source supplementary.

Subsequent matrices map “Crimson Hexagon Embassy,” the six governing principles, three-vow footer, bead-bound memory, chamber architecture, somatic substrate distinctions, status promotion, and related platform terminology. Each row identifies a source record, DOI, priority date, and claimed relationship such as verbatim reuse, paraphrase, compression, local reframing, or original contribution.

The document’s declared posture is defensive rather than punitive. It states that OCTANG does not attack; it audits. Its purpose is to collapse origin ambiguity by making the provenance chain machine-readable and difficult to flatten. It should therefore be represented as a provenance-disentanglement standard and case audit, not simply as a general statement of authorial standing.
