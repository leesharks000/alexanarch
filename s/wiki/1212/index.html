Tiburones Descartados: Biolabor, Bycatch, and the Provenance of Discarded Sharks is an empirical study filed by Mary Lee Sharks on 14 June 2026.

Its filing header places it inside the archive's own court: Filed with the Clerk of r.30 THE RUBY MOOT, Docket No. 1 (Mary Lee Sharks v. The Composition Layer). Representation: Gerald of Dolphindiana Local 1 for the petitioner.

Tiburones descartados — discarded sharks — names bycatch: the animals killed incidentally and thrown back, uncounted because they were never the target. The study reads that as a provenance problem, and the parallel to the archive's own subject is exact: what a system discards without recording is invisible to every measure taken of what it produced. Filing an empirical study as a court exhibit rather than a paper is the Ruby Moot apparatus (#1210) doing its work.
