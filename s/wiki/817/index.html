Self-Audit Module for Public Summarizers (v3.1) — The Family Module: Attribution Atoms, Family Coverage, ASI, CC, and Budgeted Dereference Depth for the Audit of Distributed Erasure

Self-Audit Module v3.1 is a family-level provenance-audit specification by Lee Sharks.

It extends earlier single-output metrics by treating attribution integrity as a property of a preregistered query family. Its principal measures include Family Coverage, Atomic Co-presence, the Attribution Sharding Index, Recoverability Ratio, budgeted Dereference Depth, and pairwise complementarity between attribution atoms.

The module distinguishes distributed attribution from uniform erasure and proposes both summarizer-side repair protocols and an external audit battery.

The formulas define the instrument, but calibration, atom selection, thresholds, and validation across larger corpora remain open research tasks.
