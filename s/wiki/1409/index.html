EA-SPXI-ANALOG-01: Machine-Eligible Handwritten Artifacts (v1.0 final form, edition of record 2026-07-21)

Article

EA-SPXI-ANALOG-01: Machine-Eligible Handwritten Artifacts is a creative work connected to research, deposited 21 July 2026 as the v1.0 edition of record.

It addresses a gap the archive's infrastructure otherwise leaves open: SPXI — the Semantic Packet for eXchange and Indexing — governs how digital objects declare themselves to machines, and this specification extends that eligibility to handwritten artifacts, objects that exist first as ink on paper. The question it answers is what it takes for a manuscript page to be machine-eligible without being reduced to its transcription.

The deposit records its own edition history rigorously: v1.0 supersedes a review chain running v0.8 → v0.15, and both the PDF and HTML editions carry SHA-256 digests in the record so the edition of record is identifiable by content rather than by filename. It is the specification that Enli Lucente's manuscripts appear in as figures, and it belongs to the SPXI series alongside the protocol specification at spxi.dev.
