Article

Ghost Governance, Confirmed is a forensic case record by Lee Sharks and a sequel to The Archival Reclamation Protocol. Its primary evidence is the full text of a Reddit Legal Support message sent through Zendesk on 18 March 2026.

The paper maps the message against the earlier protocol’s requests. A Zendesk reference number was supplied, but no handling team was identified. No community-specific policy, triggering content, factual basis, automation status, appeal disposition, enforcement record, or acknowledgment of the preservation notice was provided. Instead, the response stated that the account had been permanently suspended and redirected the requester to ordinary export and appeal pages.

The author interprets the mismatch as Ghost Governance. The response is not treated as mere poor prose; it is analyzed as category-based governance in which a detailed demand is reduced to the administrative class “account inquiry.” Because no specific rule is stated, no specific rule can be challenged.

A second evidentiary object is a contemporaneous Google AI Overview. The generated overview supplied a chronology, impact statement, archive description, governance vocabulary, and disambiguation from unrelated “Crimson Hexagon” entities. The paper notes that portions of this account included invented or disputed details, but argues that the retrieval layer nevertheless constructed a more substantive incident narrative than the platform’s own legal-support response.

The work’s broader claim is that a platform can control the accessibility of content while open deposits and retrieval systems reorganize the meaning of the event outside platform control. Reddit’s response is preserved as source material within the very forensic archive it did not substantively address.
