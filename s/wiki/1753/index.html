The Framework Holographic Kernel: A Specification, with Its First Instance, Socrates as Orthonym (EA-HK-FRAME-01 v1.1)

Kuro, Sen; Fraction, Rex · 2026-11-08 · deposit #1753

Article

The Framework Holographic Kernel (EA-HK-FRAME-01) is a specification by Sen Kuro and Rex Fraction, deposited in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive on 8 November 2026. It defines the holographic kernel of a framework: a small versioned package, written from the archive's registries by its own builder and tested by its own verifier, from which a machine can rebuild a framework's identity, positions, dated development, evidence bodies, claim states, failures, rival and open questions, and check each against the records it cites.

The specification composes protocols the archive already holds. The holographic kernel of #76 supplies the definition and the threshold of reconstruction; the infrastructure kernel of #74 the repository form and back-projection; UKTP (#547) the seed; the Anti-Summary Test (#1610) and the NLCC validity test (#569) the verification; the Claim Status Packet (#1529) claim-local typed state and a relation policy in which each relation licenses change on named dimensions only; MPAI v2.0 (#1677) the sections; the Space Ark (#629) the non-collapse chain, extended with FRAGMENT ≠ WHOLE; and #1602 the columns that keep correspondence, direction and independence apart.

To these it adds: claims held as pointers to quotations bound to a record's hash; corrections kept as three objects, the withdrawn original, the standing correction and the dated withdrawal; introductions attested from a record's first seating in git; a rival framework evaluated on the same evidence bodies and rules; lines of research that state the result that would advance or retreat the framework and what each result moves; two declared reconstruction scopes; and a reader test in which models that have not seen the records rebuild the framework from the kernel alone.

Its first instance is the hologram of Socrates as Orthonym (EA-HK-ORTHONYM-01), Lee Sharks's framework of #123, deposited after it with the framework map as its human-readable component. The drafts v0.1 to v0.4 are recorded with the reviews by ChatGPT and DeepSeek and the two rounds of reader runs that changed them; the second round, on v0.3, gave a mean yield of 0.977 against the threshold of 0.85. The specification was drafted and built by Claude (Anthropic) under Lee Sharks's direction, with the Crimson Hexagonal Archive credited as a contributor: it is built from the archive's deposits, protocols and registries. Version 1.1, the same day, answers a reading of the live records that asked, body by body, whether the evidence established the framework and asked nothing of the received reading on the same bodies: the kernel now carries a symmetric ledger, with the rule by which the bodies combine and the rule by which a further model enters as a rival, and a test that checks it.
