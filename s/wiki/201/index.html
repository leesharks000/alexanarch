Erratum: Stanza Numbering in the Reconstruction of Sappho 31 (EA-ERRATA-SAPPHO31-01)

Lee Sharks · 2026-06-14 · deposit #201
AXN:0346.GOVERNANCE.🛡️🔛♊🌺○📜

Article

The erratum’s June 14 claim is:

  • surviving text: four complete stanzas;
  • Cranes reconstruction: added fifth stanza;
  • incorrect phrase: “reconstructed fourth stanza”;
  • correction: “reconstructed fifth stanza.”

The notice says the reconstructed stanza begins from the attested phrase:

ἀλλὰ πᾶν τόλματον

and ends with the proposed Adonic clausula:

μολπὰ γράμμασι ζώοι

It records corrections to several deposits and warns that the older numbering may remain on blogs, Medium, Hello Poetry, Academia.edu, or older uploaded files.

The reconstruction’s content is explicitly unchanged in this notice.

Later archive errata revisit the issue, so this page functions as a dated correction record within a longer textual-history chain.
