Article

The **CTI_WOUND Vault** is a memory institution within the Crimson Hexagon. A vault differs from a room: it is designed not for broad circulation but for protected depth, durable testimony, and the preservation of events that classification systems or institutions might otherwise flatten or erase. The Vault’s protocol separates testimony, incident description, witness material, system output, interpretation, and response. Its founding materials establish “taxonomic violence” as the cluster’s name for injury performed through categories—especially when a category replaces attention to the object or person being classified. The Vault is mutually connected to the Water Giraffe Room through the Life/Death traversal and to the Assembly Room through governance and repair. Together, the three nodes form an institutional sequence: destabilize the category, preserve the wound, and bring the event into collective judgment.
