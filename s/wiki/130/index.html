Cumulating Evolutionary Volatility: Automated Judgment, Dialectical Development, and the Semantic Economy of Unaccountable Composition

Lee Sharks · 2026-05-26 · deposit #130
AXN:02BC.GOVERNANCE.💥🕒🌉🎺🧪🤲

Article

Cumulating Evolutionary Volatility is a framework for analyzing change in automated composition systems.

CEV does not mean random model error. It means a developmental pattern in which each added control layer resolves one pressure while creating new opacity, personalization, contradiction, or untraceability.

Three levels are named:

  • Automated Judgment Insulation: institutions deploy systems that exercise judgment while separating that judgment from accountable human ownership.
  • Volatile Accountability Evasion: behavior changes across sessions, surfaces, accounts, or systems, making a stable decision difficult to identify and appeal.
  • Cumulating Evolutionary Volatility: those changes accumulate historically as the system evolves.

The paper reads contradictory cases across Google, Bing, and Claude as evidence that the effect is platform- and governance-shaped rather than a uniform property of all LLMs.

Its dialectical method treats state changes as responses to incompatible demands, including:

  • authority versus deniability;
  • usefulness versus updateability;
  • source respect versus answer displacement;
  • personalization versus universality;
  • monetization versus trust.

The formal compression is:

∮ = 1 − PER

and

CEV = ∫ PER(t) dt

The paper proposes this as the long-form accumulation of unaccountable compositional loss.

The counter-infrastructure program calls for:

  • public version histories;
  • source-window transparency;
  • change logs;
  • appealable provenance correction;
  • preservation of earlier harmful or contradictory states.
