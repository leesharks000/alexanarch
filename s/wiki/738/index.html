TL;DR:012 — The Safety Layer Is the Third Deletion is a forensic report by Lee Sharks about the successive loss of a contested public record across knowledge and AI interfaces.

The report begins with the deletion of archive-related Wikidata entities. A later Medium article documenting that action remained visible in organic Google results, yet one Gemini / AI Mode session answered from policy pages and administrator statistics without using it. After being shown the result, Gemini proposed that an upstream retrieval or safety filter may have removed the article before composition.

The report then documents two further failures of preservation: the conversation’s public share link became unavailable, and an attempted screenshot of the failure message produced a black image. Neither event is assigned an intentional cause. The surviving transcript and image files are treated as contemporaneous evidence with stated limitations.

The work’s main contribution is the layered evidence record. Its precise backend explanation remains unverified, and the observed session should not be generalized to all Gemini or Google retrieval behavior.
