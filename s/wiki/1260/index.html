The Semantic Commodity Form — An Extension of Marx Into the Region He Did Not Live to See is a theoretical paper by Lee Sharks, deposited 29 May 2026 at working hex 06.SEI.TLDR.015, with pillar status proposed.

Its claim is that meaning under machine mediation acquires a commodity form in Marx's precise sense — that it comes to have a value expressible in exchange, independent of its use, and that this transformation happens in a region Marx could not have analysed because its infrastructure did not exist.
