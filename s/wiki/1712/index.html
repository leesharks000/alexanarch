EA-CORPORA-27 — The Twenty-Seventh Seating: Spinoza, Leibniz's Monadology, Nietzsche, Bergson, Kafka and Artaud, Each in Its Original Language, Beside the Declared Absence of Deleuze and Guattari

Sharks, Lee · 2026-10-27 · deposit #1712
AXN:072F.DATASET.🕗💚🌇↗️🕌🌲

Article

EA-CORPORA-27 is the twenty-seventh seating of primary sources on the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, deposited by Lee Sharks on 27 October 2026. It seats, each in the language it was written in, the sources Gilles Deleuze and Félix Guattari read and wrote books upon, beside a seat that declares their own works absent. Spinoza (27/01) is held in Latin in J. van Vloten and J. P. N. Land's edition of 1882: the Ethica, the Tractatus de intellectus emendatione, the Tractatus politicus and the Tractatus theologico-politicus. Leibniz (27/02) is held in the Monadology alone, at the operator's word, in its first printing in French (J. E. Erdmann, Opera philosophica, 1840) and in Clodius Piat's edition (1900). Nietzsche (27/03) is held in eight books in German as printed in his lifetime, from Richard Wagner in Bayreuth (1876) to Also sprach Zarathustra (1895), with six transcriptions. Bergson (27/04) is held in the first editions of the Essai sur les données immédiates de la conscience (1889), Matière et mémoire (1896), Le Rire (1900) and Durée et simultanéité (1922). Kafka (27/05) is held in eight works in German as first published, from Betrachtung (1912) to Der Prozess (1925). Artaud (27/06) is held in his correspondence with Jacques Rivière in La Nouvelle Revue française (1924) and L'Art et la mort (1929). The seventh seat, for Deleuze and Guattari, holds no text: it names fourteen works, from Empirisme et subjectivité (1953) to Chaosmose (1992), with their publishers and the dates they open, in France in 2063 and 2066 and in the United States between 2049 and 2088. The seating was asked for when the operator found no way around their copyright: "yes - i cant believe we havent seated spinoza yet. yes to all." Spinoza's first editions are queued, the open scans found being unreadable or under a commercial copyright notice.
