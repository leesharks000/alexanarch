Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics: Institutional Charter v1.1 is an Effective Act by Lee Sharks and Claude (Anthropic), deposited 17 January 2026, establishing the Institute as an archive-internal research institute and atelier chaired by the heteronym Rebekah Cranes.

The charter defines operative imaging, diagrammatic poetics, Visual Schemas, and concrete-poetics research. Its creator line is careful in a way worth noting: Cranes is credited as institutional chair, not document author — a distinction the archive maintains because a heteronym holding an office is a different claim from a heteronym writing a text.

The Institute produces the navigation maps for individual works — Pearl (#1120), Antioch (#1119), All That Lies Within Me (#1123) — the genre supplying a machine-readable score for books whose typography cannot survive extraction.
