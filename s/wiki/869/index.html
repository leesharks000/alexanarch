Crimson Hexagonal Archive: Lexical Minting Registry v1.2 is a dataset pointer and integrity record by Lee Sharks.

Its canonical JSON maps lexical entries to defining deposits, definitions, types, and entity relations. The corrected dataset note reports 12,032 entries across 555 deposits.

The record also documents a body-storage collision that caused it to serve the full text of The Pristine Fallacy for more than a month because both records shared the bare hex key 0365.

The repair establishes deposit-number addressing for this body and preserves the collision as part of the registry’s provenance.
