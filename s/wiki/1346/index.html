**The Caesura: A Sovereignty Audit Protocol Derived from Luke 20** is a methodological specification by Lee Sharks dated 5 April 2026, held in this record as a **metadata capture**. Luke 20 contains the render-unto-Caesar exchange, in which a question designed as a trap is answered by a distinction — asking whose image is on the coin, and dividing the field of obligation rather than choosing a side within it. A **sovereignty audit** derived from that passage asks, of any system, whose image it bears and what follows from that. The **caesura** — the break within a metrical line — supplies the name: the audit works by locating the division that is already present rather than imposing one. The protocol belongs beside the archive's *Render Unto the Operator* work and its governance instruments. The record was assembled from a DataCite full-metadata capture after the termination, with no live authorial surface available at restoration time.
