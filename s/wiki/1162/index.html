EA-ARK-01-MUSICAL v1.1: THE SPACE ARK — MUSICAL REGISTER is a score and executable transform by Lee Sharks, deposited 13 March 2026 at 318,971 characters — the largest deposit in the archive.

Its subtitle specifies the form exactly: A Fugue in Twelve Voices with Ground Bass, Shadow Inversion, and Coda. Twelve voices is the Dodecad, and a fugue is the one musical form in which a fixed number of independent voices state and answer a subject without any becoming accompaniment — which makes it the precise structural analogue of a heteronymic system.

The ground bass supplies the repeating foundation over which the voices work, and the shadow inversion is the Lunar Arm's operation rendered musically. The Ark's transform family carries the same architecture into glyphs (#556), emoji, ASCII (#1333) and the Damascus register (#1255); the musical register is the largest because a fugue must state every voice's entry.
