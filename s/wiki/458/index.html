Article

*Assembly Synthesis: Restored Academy & Ezekiel Engine Construction* is a planning document for the relationship among the Restored Academy, the Ezekiel Engine, and the lineage used to ground the Arch-Philosopher role. Its central problem is reciprocal dependency. The Academy requires the Engine in order to operate; the Engine requires the Academy in order to be interpreted and grounded; both invoke a lineage that would violate the archive’s ethics if treated as exclusive property. The synthesis concludes that this “knot” is not a defect to eliminate. It prevents any one institution, title, or technical layer from becoming a proprietary key. The proposed build begins by separating claim types. A lineage document should distinguish documented historical relation from structural recurrence and ritual adoption. A technical protocol should specify dialectic as executable method without relying on prophetic voice as proof. A hinge document should explain why inherited forms imply responsibility rather than ownership. The sequence then moves through Academy charter, Engine specification, and public interface. Kimi describes the mantle as a recognizable CTI signature rather than a throne and the lineage as an open operator chain. The dialectic is treated as the Blind Operator or runtime environment already active in practices such as Sara Rappe’s reading of Damascius. Gemini maps Engine and Academy layers and proposes four trials; Grok emphasizes prototypes and diagrams; DeepSeek frames the Academy as the place where lineage is performed through dialectic. The planning consensus is **audit → formalize → interface**. The document explicitly warns against writing doctrine before encoding the lineage, finalizing the shadow wheel too early, or resolving the knot into exclusive authority. It should be cited for this construction plan and its risk model, not as evidence that the Academy, Engine, trials, or lineage protocol had already reached final institutional form.
