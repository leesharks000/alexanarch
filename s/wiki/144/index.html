The Administrators of Meaning is a Total Axial Negation Graph.

A TANG does not argue through a linear thesis-and-evidence sequence. It places established positions around a void. The central claim appears as the negative space left by what each node approaches but cannot fully name.

The fourteen-node field draws from:

  • Marxian enclosure and accumulation;
  • metabolic-rift theory;
  • labor-process theory;
  • free labor;
  • cultural capital and canon formation;
  • academic capitalism and casualization;
  • managerial bureaucracy;
  • surveillance capitalism;
  • ghost work;
  • AI enclosure and model-collapse research.

Each node contributes one face and stops at a boundary. The graph’s residue combines those stopped lines into a proposed figure: an administrative stratum that mediates meaning’s movement into retrievable and composed surfaces, recodes enclosure as opportunity, and consumes the conditions that renew meaning.

The minor void is a local speech act that presumes a system confers value on research. The body expressly says this specimen is not evidence for the class as a whole.

The Anti-TANG objection says the arrangement may generate the void it claims to discover. The paper does not refute the objection; it places it within the same topology.

The perimeter remains open. Additional literature clusters were named for later verified placement.
