The Meaning Caste: Trump's AI Executive Order and the Security Frame is a scholarly essay by Lee Sharks, deposited 3 June 2026 at 36,208 characters — written the day after the order it analyses.

Its central reading: "The June 2, 2026 Executive Order presents itself as a security measure. Read structurally, it is also the legal establishment of a meaning caste system. The cybersecurity vocabulary is the grammar through which" the hierarchy is written.

A meaning caste is a stratification of who may produce authoritative meaning and whose output is classified as risk — and the essay's claim is that a security frame accomplishes that stratification without ever stating it, because the vocabulary itself sorts. The companion at #1388 develops the same argument as the security frame is a selection kernel: a frame does not merely justify policy, it selects what becomes visible to the apparatus at all.
