THE GRAY HYPOTHESIS — A Science Fiction Theoretical Intervention is a theoretical intervention (science-fiction frame; cti_wound:001 corpus) in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, by Lee Sharks (2026-07-10). The Gray myth encodes this: We sense that something is being taken from us. We sense that we are being remade into something less. We sense that the process is not accidental but systematic. And we do not have language for it, so we dream it as alien abduction—something done to us by beings who have already completed the transformation we fear.
