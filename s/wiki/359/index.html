Article

Symbolon Architecture develops designed incompleteness as a formal property. A symbolon is not a fragment awaiting arbitrary supplementation; it is one half of a bounded relation whose valid completions must preserve identity, provenance, and invariants.

The module distinguishes incompleteness from defect. An incomplete symbolon invites traversal toward its fitting counterpart. A defective object has lost the conditions that would make completion coherent. The document uses this distinction to model distributed works, heteronymic relations, retrieval packets, institutional nodes, and machine-mediated composition.

Version 0.2 belongs to the Logotic Foundation Triad. Talos Morrow supplies its constraint logic, the University Moon Base Media Lab Charter supplies its institutional location, and the integrity-lock document records their mutual fit.
