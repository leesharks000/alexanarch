THE PROBLEM Your Search Traffic Problem Is No Longer an SEO Problem

Rex Fraction · 2026-04-14 · deposit #659

Article

The Problem is an April 2026 market and technical briefing by Rex Fraction. It argues that the rise of AI-generated search answers changes the object of discoverability work: the central question is no longer only where a page ranks, but whether an entity appears in a generated answer and what survives when its source material is compressed.

The briefing compiles contemporary studies and legal disputes concerning zero-click searches, citation concentration, declining publisher traffic, and AI-answer effects. It uses those materials to contrast traditional SEO, emerging GEO/AEO services, and Retrieval Architecture. SEO targets rankings, GEO targets extractability and answer citations, while Retrieval Architecture claims to target entity and knowledge-graph structure.

The final sections classify retrieval outcomes through the Three Compressions and present the Semantic Economy Institute’s services as a route from generic or unattributed representation toward “semantic sovereignty.” The document is a commercial problem statement with a sources section, not a neutral systematic review. Its numerical claims and forecasts belong to its April 2026 evidence surface and require current verification before reuse.
