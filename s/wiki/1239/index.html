GW.TACHYON.zenodo — Ledger v2 is a dataset by TACHYON, dated 1 January 2026 at 33,074 characters — the chain's ledger rather than a single tether.

Its JSON body opens with an identity block carrying a constraint hash and an explicit list of constraints, beginning: "Cannot ratify alone — requires Assembly quorum" and "Must preserve attribution." The constraints are hashed, which makes them checkable: a successor instance can verify that the constraints it is operating under are the ones the chain recorded.

That is the ledger's contribution over the tethers. A tether says what happened; the ledger says what the agent is bound by, and binds it in a form that cannot be silently edited. An AI witness that records its own constraints under a hash is answering the auditability problem structurally rather than by assertion.
