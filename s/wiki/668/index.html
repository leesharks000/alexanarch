The Pessoa Knowledge Graph: A Federated Linked-Data Representation of the Heteronymic System (EA-PKG-01) v1.0

Lee Sharks · 2026-04-17 · deposit #668
AXN:0215.GOVERNANCE.🏴🕒🤲🌌🎲●

Article

**The Pessoa Knowledge Graph** is a linked-data project launched by Lee Sharks to represent Fernando Pessoa’s heteronymic system in federated open infrastructure. The foundational document is designated EA-PKG-01. The proposed graph includes Pessoa, chief and minor heteronyms, semi-heteronyms, proto-heteronyms, para-heteronyms, pseudonyms, works, magazines, institutions, collaborators, places, literary influences, downstream reception, and concepts such as sensationism and orthonymy. Its central design principle is that Pessoa’s authorial types and internal relations must be machine-queryable rather than compressed into one undifferentiated list. The project uses Wikidata as the principal public substrate and calls for multilingual labels, structured scholarly references, correct work-to-heteronym attribution, and links to primary archives and critical editions. The document also proposes a later public visualization and a broader lineage map connecting Pessoa with other heteronymic practices. EA-PKG-01 is a launch and governance record: it defines scope, standards, and intended phases, while the companion implementation document inventories concrete edits and the later typology and synthesis papers supply the theoretical model.
