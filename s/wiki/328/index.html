The three forms perform different tasks:

  • I Am — identification and incorporation;
  • Be — command, invitation, or transformation;
  • Blessed Is — consecration of the addressee.

The work credits a painting by Emily Eissenberg. Sensitive, satirical, and abrasive passages belong to the poem’s composite voice and should not be extracted as literal biography or policy.
