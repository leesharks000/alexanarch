This manifesto names a disciplinary condition approached technically by the companion measurement, synthesis, and architectural documents. Frontier experimental high-energy physics has not ceased to be physics. Its empirical faculty, however, has become inseparable from machine-mediated representation, reconstruction, classification, and real-time selection. The decisive question is therefore no longer whether physics uses machine learning, but whether physical reality retains an effective path by which it can contradict the learned ontology through which it becomes data. We name the failure of that path the endogenous sophon. In Liu Cixin's fictional architecture (The Three-Body Problem, 2006), the sophons are particle-scale instruments deployed by an external civilization to corrupt Earth's high-energy physics experiments. They do not defeat human theoretical intelligence directly; they disable the experimental correction mechanism on which theoretical intelligence depends. The exogenous sophon makes reality appear unintelligible — noise without pattern, no experiment returning trustworthy results. The endogenous sophon operates differently: it makes reality appear prematurely intelligible. An unfamiliar physical event may be removed upstream, reconstructed as an ordinary object, assigned a low anomaly score, or preserved without the information required for later reinterpretation.
