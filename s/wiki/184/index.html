  • journal-state attribution: Yusef Kenning, 2015;
  • headnote, afterword, and collation: Johannes Sigil, 2026;

The two 2015 states are:

1. the blog state, dedicated to John Guzlowski; 2. the New Human 2 compiled state, attributed to Yusef Kenning.

The headnote treats the dead Google+ dedication link as a live example of textual afterlife: the inscription remains while its destination has vanished.

The critical afterword places the poem against:

  • Pound’s fascist broadcasts and later silence;
  • Ginsberg’s 1967 encounter with Pound;
  • Auden’s withdrawn claim that time pardons writers;
  • the heteronym as a made textual person;
  • the poem’s biblical, Miltonic, golem, Mars, and after-words structures.

The edition’s central reading is jurisdictional rather than exculpatory: a person made of words addresses a person now surviving primarily through words. The title remains a question, not a settled moral authorization.
