Semantic Liquidation: An Executive Summary

Lee Sharks · 2026-01-06 · deposit #255
AXN:0039.GOVERNANCE.🃏🗼♦️♆⚪🧡

Article

The five-stage mechanism is:

1. tokenization; 2. stripping; 3. attribution relocation; 4. value capture; 5. closure.

Three properties distinguish liquidation from ordinary transformation:

  • loss cannot be fully reversed;
  • value moves away from the source;
  • the destruction is obscured by a fluent or useful output.

The summary also distinguishes diagnosis from prohibition: it does not claim that all compression is illegitimate or that every transformation must be stopped.
