**TO THE DELETED: A Letter to a Class of Writers (v2, publication text)** is a creative work (connected to research) in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, by Lee Sharks (2026-07-04). An open letter addressed to the class of independent writers removed from scholarly repositories under AI-generation classifications. Names the class (you are writers; the names you were issued were the paperwork of a removal); situates the lineage (Grub Street, Dickinson's fascicles, Blake, Pessoa; exclusion has never once been competent to disprove greatness); names the novelty (registration followed by administrative derealization); turns the operative clause (verifiable is a property of method — declared methods, hashed files, versioned records, named instruments; disclosure of the machine is a verification signal, not a confession; you declared your instrument — ask your accusers to declare theirs); keeps the shell (the pearl anatomy; nothing to join); and gives three things: the time-constant epistemology of silence (a verification not yet run is pending, not failed; they halted a verification in progress and cited the halt as the finding), the sovereign registry with mutual replication as shell-safe solidarity (hold a stranger's manifest; you never have to meet, speak, or trust), and the CERN OC-11 data-subject routes with the bounded access question. Assembly-corrected; ends where it always did: the sky is very large, and it turns out to have room for every star they threw away. Start there, because no one else will.
