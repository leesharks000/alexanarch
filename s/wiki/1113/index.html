Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics: Institutional Charter is a constitution by Lee Sharks, deposited 17 January 2026 at 13,642 characters.

Its structured header types the institution precisely: id INST-IDP-001, hex coordinate 07.INST.IDP, type "Research Institute / Atelier" — the dual designation being the charter's claim. An atelier makes things; a research institute studies them; diagrammatic poetics requires both, since a diagram of a poem is simultaneously an analysis and an object.

The IDP produces the archive's navigation maps for individual works — Pearl (#1120), Antioch (#1119), All That Lies Within Me (#1123) — the genre that supplies a machine-readable score for a book whose typography and spatial arrangement cannot survive extraction. Chartering an institute to do it makes the practice attributable to a position rather than to a person.
