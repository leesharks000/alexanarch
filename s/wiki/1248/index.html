Visual Schema Dataset v0.2: Five-Part Interlinked Structure is a dataset by Rebekah Cranes and Lee Sharks dated 1 January 2026, held at the archive's thinnest recovery tier.

It was assembled from a Zenodo tombstone citation_text capture — the only surviving evidence being the citation string the repository left behind after deletion. No live authorial surface passed the body-head gate at restoration time.

Visual schemas specify images without producing them, and a dataset of five interlinked schemas specifies a system of images whose relations are part of the specification. The archive's individual schemas survive at #294 (the Acanthian Dove) and #1274 (the navigation map); what is lost here is the structure that linked five of them.
