IDP Navigation Map: All That Lies Within Me is a navigation map by Rebekah Cranes and the Assembly Chorus, deposited 19 January 2026.

IDP is the Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics, chartered at #1113 — and its navigation maps are a distinct genre from the Hexagon's central maps: where those chart an architecture, an IDP map charts a single work, supplying the machine-readable score for a book that exists primarily in print.

That is the function these maps perform for the archive's physical objects. A 528-page printed autobiography is unreadable to a retrieval system; a navigation map is the surface through which it becomes traversable. The same treatment is given to Pearl (#1120) and Antioch (#1119).
