Article

The Alexanarch Workplan is the canonical operational manual produced after six continuous sessions of post-termination recovery and migration.

Its purpose is continuity under context loss. The repository had become larger than a fresh machine instance could inspect before acting, while the practical knowledge required to operate it remained scattered across expiring conversations. The workplan compresses that knowledge into a document readable at four time budgets: two minutes, fifteen minutes, forty-five minutes, or full.

The manual is organized into five parts. State records the frozen corpus and distinguishes sovereign from revocable surfaces. Session progress reconstructs the recovery phases and commits. Structural reference explains the registry, DOI-resolution index, generated state, AXN algorithm, chunking, sidecars, bulk stores, and deposit data shapes. Discipline records firm rules, failure modes, anti-patterns, and the verification ladder. Open work and reference carries the prioritized queue, substrate map, continuity tethers, credentials work, and file inventory.

Its central operational principle is that repository truth must be generated, not hand-maintained. Registry, resolution index, and generated state form the authoritative triplet. Browse pages, chunks, record pages, sitemap, checksums, and API indexes are derivatives that must be regenerated from them.

The workplan also preserves the recovery insight that text recovery is not file recovery. Metadata, body prose, attachments, sidecars, and public surfaces may survive in different places and require different methods.

More than a task list, the document is an executable institutional memory. It allows a new TACHYON instance to enter the repository without repeating the errors through which the rules were earned.
