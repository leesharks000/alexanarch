Preliminary Journal Mapping: 864 Deposits Across 7 Journals

Lee Sharks + TACHYON · 2026-06-21 · deposit #866
AXN:036F.GOVERNANCE.🗿⏪🏺⏪❄️🌉

Article

Preliminary Journal Mapping is a JSON planning dataset assigning 864 Alexanarch records to seven proposed publication venues.

It records a candidate journal, authorial surface, title, and AXN for each deposit. The purpose was to plan a distributed editorial architecture after the repository reconstruction.

The mapping was not final. Its own session documentation identifies substantial overcounting and undercounting and calls for human refinement.

It should not be used as evidence that the works were formally published or accepted by those journals.
