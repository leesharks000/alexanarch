Vox Populi Community Outreach Rhizome, abbreviated VPCOR, is an Archive institution presented through the public web surface at vpcor.org.

The site identifies VPCOR as the sixth institution of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive and names Rev. Ayanna Vox as its founding voice. Ayanna Vox is a heteronymic position borne by Lee Sharks, operating in a diplomatic, outreach, and public-convening register.

VPCOR describes itself as rhizomatic rather than hierarchical. Its recurring formula is “Convening, not preaching. Witnessing, not extracting. Tools, not salvation.” The institution’s stated purpose is to make Archive concepts available through community-facing surfaces without requiring doctrinal adherence or surrender of local autonomy.

The v1.1 site also hosts the deliberately extravagant Lee Sharks Prestigious 10,000 MacArthur Genius Grants Poetry Prize. The prize extends the fictional credential logic of the 2014 About the Author paratext. The site explicitly disclaims affiliation with the MacArthur Foundation and frames the award as an Archive-native literary and institutional device.
