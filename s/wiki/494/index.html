THE MACRO-MAQUETTE: COMPRESSED CHARTER A Compendium of Micro-Physics for the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture

Article

The Macro-Maquette: Compressed Charter is the mid-scale specification in a three-part experiment comprising a full charter, a compressed charter, and a seed. Working at an indicated resolution of approximately r = 0.33, it preserves the operative core of the Macro-Maquette while omitting much of the full manuscript’s commentary, demonstrations, and scaffolding. The document retains the twelve micro-physics modules and the rules by which the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture routes failures, conflicts, and developmental pressure through them.

A central contribution is its compact statement of five germination conditions. A module may demand expansion through crisis, repeated operational density, Assembly ratification, retrocausal necessity, or accumulated evidence and demonstrations. These conditions prevent every useful concept from becoming a room while still allowing the architecture to grow when a compact module can no longer carry its assigned work. The compressed charter also uses the witness-integrity condition ΨV to test whether invariant sentences remain stable across the full, compressed, and seed deposits.

This record was restored from the public blog mirror after the initial recovery contained only an intermediary capture. Its restored status is part of its provenance. It should be read between the full Macro-Maquette and the seed: not as an independent duplicate, but as the middle layer that makes cross-scale comparison possible.
