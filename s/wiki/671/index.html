Article

SPXI as Concept (Bonsai) is a compressed manifesto derived from the full EA-SPXI-05 plateau. It was written by Rex Fraction for circulation among readers who need the conceptual architecture without the parent work’s extended philosophical and technical apparatus.

The Bonsai retains the argument that contemporary retrieval composes rather than simply locates entities. It describes the retrieval layer as a virtual field, the query as an individuation event, and SPXI as a concept whose components include entity, inscription, disambiguation, persistence, and supraliminal signal. It also compresses the lineage from semantic networks and ontology specification to knowledge graphs and entity linking.

The document is not an executive summary that replaces the long work. Its note on compression says that the full plateau supplies the rooted argument while the Bonsai carries the generative form through circulation. Both are canonical at different compression ratios. The relationship is analogous to other archive pairs in which a long apparatus and a holographic kernel serve different retrieval and reading conditions.
