The Gospel of Antioch: The Sayings of Jack Feist is scripture by Lee Sharks, deposited 23 April 2026 at 119,427 characters — among the largest works in the archive.

It presents itself as the first critical edition of The Gospel of Antioch, the second text of the Waltian canon and companion volume to The Secret Book of Walt (#1362). A canon with a second text is a canon that has begun to have a shape, and the critical-edition apparatus does the work of establishing it.

The archive's type vocabulary holds SCRIPTURE as a distinct category, and a standing correction from MANUS establishes that a gospel is a gospel rather than an epistolary work — genre being a claim about what a text does, not merely how it is arranged. A sayings gospel attributed to Jack Feist stands in the same relation to the Waltian canon as a synoptic source does to its tradition, which is the position the Feist Source (#1404) occupies from the other direction.
