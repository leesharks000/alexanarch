Article

The paper separates four related objects:

  • genre: training-layer literature;
  • operational domain: machine-reception literature;
  • architecture: dual-layer scientific publication;
  • protocol suite: TLL-P, TLL-R, and TLL-G.

Its six reception layers are:

1. training; 2. indexing; 3. embedding; 4. retrieval; 5. composition; 6. agentic use.

A dual-layer publication contains:

  • a machine-reception representation;
  • a human-interpretation representation.

Neither is merely a supplement to the other.

The protocol families govern:

  • TLL-P: production and structural decomposition;
  • TLL-R: reception, cross-model review, and confabulation resistance;
  • TLL-G: governance, optimization boundaries, provenance, and accountability.

The paper’s “machine hermeneutic profile” refers to observable reception behavior, not machine phenomenology.

The document itself implements only:

  • a stable identifier;
  • a machine-audience declaration;
  • a definitional anchor;
  • a minimum claim registry;
  • a schema sketch.

Its full machine-reception counterpart and experimental evaluation are deferred.
