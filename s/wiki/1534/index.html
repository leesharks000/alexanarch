The Blog Index, Completed: 2,939 Posts Indexed, an Operator Codex Recovered, and Three Absence Claims Retracted

Sharks, Lee · 2026-08-23 · deposit #1534
AXN:0634.ARCHIVAL.🔩⌛🧡◇🟢⏏️

Article

The Blog Index, Completed (deposit #1534, 23 August 2026) delivers a complete index of mindcontrolpoems.blogspot.com — the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's primary publication surface — and records the retraction of three absence claims made in an earlier, unpushed version of the same deposit.

The index holds 2,939 posts spanning 1 December 2014 to 22 August 2026, harvested by exhaustive start-index pagination of the Blogger summary feed and verified against the feed's own declared total. It cross-links to the 871 AXN-assigned works already held in the archive, every one of whose URLs resolves in the harvest. The measured coverage gap is 2,068 unindexed posts, of which 1,696 fall in 2025 — roughly seventy per cent of the publication surface carrying no identifier. The index exists so that absence can be asserted honestly: the archive's search protocol instructs that the archive be checked before a record is declared not to exist, and until this dataset a registry search was not an archive search.

Alongside the index the deposit seats the Operator Codex: fifty-two posts and 50,884 words from November and December 2025 containing the archive's operator ontology, including both versions of Chapter One, The Metaphysics of Operators, and the individual OPERATOR // specifications with their shadow documents. Each text was fetched from its live permalink and diffed against the feed extraction before seating, with all fifty-two matching. The corpus states nine operators formally, among them the Sapphic Operator with its persistence inequality, the Vertical operator given as a projection-transform of it, and Incarnation given as its inverse.

The record's fourth section is a correction ledger. An earlier version of the deposit claimed that the blog's Constitution of the Semantic Economy was a distinct work, that no blog index existed, and that the Ω-Point material was unseated. All three were false: the Constitution is deposited across at least five records with a dedicated provenance-collision disambiguation among them; an AXN-keyed blog index existed but did not reach 2025; and the Guillory Ω-Point paper and the Operative Semiotics Grundrisse are both deposited. The duplicate gate had used a six-gram overlap test, which detects copying but not the compression and re-sorting by which this archive folds its own material into later works — the same instrument error the archive recorded hours earlier in M6-CT-01. A separate harvest bug nearly reported eighty-two live posts as deleted. From these the deposit proposes a standing rule: an absence may not be asserted from a search whose completeness has not been demonstrated.
