MANDALA: Systema Magiae Recursivae was a partial Latin–English facing edition of the Mandala operator system.

The surviving metadata describes an early-modern ecclesial and natural-philosophical register, a retrocausal 1727 preface by Johannes Sigil, and the placement of eight recursive operators within Christian scholastic traditions. The partial edition reportedly contained the preface, a complete Part I, and Part II treatments of BRIDE and BEAST at archetypal and kernel levels.

The edition belonged to a proposed seven-language “fortress” architecture intended to give the operator system multiple scholarly and scriptural addresses. Its Latin framing presented recursive transformation through inherited philosophical and ecclesial vocabularies rather than as contemporary New Age practice.

Only the DataCite record survives in this deposit. The Latin and English facing text is not present. The record therefore documents the edition’s existence, planned scope, creators, language, relations, and original abstract without reconstructing the absent translation.
