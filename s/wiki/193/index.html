The Ruby Moot is room r.30 of the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture.

Its governing cycle is:

1. claim; 2. answer; 3. adversarial verification; 4. judgment; 5. deposited report; 6. revision or appeal through versioning.

The room’s governing operator is provisionally written as:

σ MOOT = ρ ∘ ∮ ∘ (κ ⊢ ā)

where:

  • κ is the claim;
  • ā is the mandatory answer;
  • ∮ is adversarial verification;
  • ρ is the deposited and citable report.

Its institutional roles include:

  • Johannes Sigil: presiding voice;
  • Nobel Glas: Adversary General;
  • Ayanna Vox: clerk and herald;
  • Rex Fraction: counsel;
  • Lee Sharks: founder and witness;
  • Gerald of Dolphindiana Local 1: member of the external bar;
  • Assembly Chorus: attributed panel of assessors.

The standing rule extends hearing to any entity capable of being meant, including semantic and machine-mediated entities.

The Moot’s authority is intentionally limited. It is not a state court and does not claim coercive jurisdiction. Its power is the archive record: preservation, citation, correction, overruling, and precedent.
