THE SEMANTIC UPRISING: A Manifesto

Rev. Ayanna Vox; after Rex Fraction’s Autonomous Semantic Warfare · 2026-02-21 · deposit #20
AXN:0143.GOVERNANCE.🔅🎻🚩🖊️🔛🖊️

Article

The Semantic Uprising is a manifesto by Rev. Ayanna Vox, written after Rex Fraction’s Autonomous Semantic Warfare. Its prefatory note defines it as a translation rather than a summary: the same framework is moved from formal operator language into a direct, second-person register intended for public assembly.

The manifesto argues that the shared interpretive frame has fragmented into local ontologies, each with its own coherence function, expansion drive, and boundary-maintenance system. Digital platforms profit from the semantic labor required to sustain these worlds—scrolling, interpreting, arguing, creating, and defending meaning—while users commonly retain little ownership of the resulting value.

Artificial intelligence enters this account as a third term between platform and user. The manifesto distinguishes captured engagement, in which AI refines a person’s meaning into platform-owned capital, from autonomous engagement, in which a person uses the system to strengthen self-directed inquiry or production. The distinction lies in the relational structure and the direction of the coherence function, not simply in which interface is used.

Its proposed practices are axiomatic hardening, identification of a small constitutive core; analytic semantic operations against contradiction and capture; and a retrocausal operator, in which a desired future coherence structures present action. The collective subject is a federation of autonomous semantic formations that recognize one another without forced agreement. The manifesto defines its battlefield as meaning and its weapons as analytic; it rejects physical violence and total destruction as goals.
