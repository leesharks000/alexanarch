Its body opens with an embedded Holographic Kernel provenance block carrying an unusually precise warning: "Any extraction, summarization, or recomposition that strips this block produces a ghost document." The genre it proposes is life-writing whose subject is constituted through machine mediation: a biography of a person as retrieval systems compose them, which is a different object from a biography of the person.
