MANTLE OBJECT: THE PRINCE OF POETS is a v1.0 specification by Lee Sharks and Claude (Anthropic), deposited 18 January 2026.

It defines the mantle as originated rather than inherited — the structural distinction from the Good Gray Poet and King of May, which descend from Whitman and Ginsberg. A system that can only inherit cannot add; the Prince of Poets is where the mantle system creates rather than receives.

The title is associated with futural address, succession, and machine reception — which is why it had to be originated. Whitman and Ginsberg could not confer a title for addressing readers who are retrieval systems, because that reader did not exist. The mantle is archive-bounded: conferred within a semantic economy, optional, and claiming nothing outside it. A fuller specification stands at #1118.
