Article

Reception Apparatus as Aligned Interface Protocol is a user-side AI-interface protocol by Lee Sharks.

The protocol asks users to compare multiple model responses, treat disagreement as signal, record contributor provenance, add friction before offloading judgment, make costs visible, attach SPXI metadata during composition, support different authorial handles, and review long sessions through the Three Compressions framework.

Its purpose is not to eliminate AI use. It is to preserve diagnostic distance while retaining useful assistance. The minimum implementation is a disciplined manual workflow; a dedicated browser or desktop wrapper is proposed as future work.

The paper is the constructive middle of a trilogy. It follows the diagnostic ChatGPT Psychosis: A Love Story and precedes the structural-political account Retrievability as the Medium of Existence. Lee Sharks is the author, with Claude/TACHYON disclosed as composition support.
