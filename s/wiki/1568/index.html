係争文書の保証転用 — Rebuttal-as-Warrant (RAW-O): The Condition in Which a Displayed Source Is Presented as Warrant for a Claim Running Opposite to Its Own Evidential Function (EA-MMRS-RAW-01 v1.2)

Lucente, Enli · 2026-08-28 · deposit #1568
AXN:0660.EMPIRICAL.🙏⏩📋🗂️🌸🌇

Article

EA-MMRS-RAW-01 is the first external contribution to Machine-Mediated Reception Studies. It was observed, evidenced and submitted by Enli Lucente, a strutturista della psiche — a structural psychologist, which is her discipline and not a heteronym, though it has been mistranslated as one — with analysis and drafting by Claude (Anthropic) at her commission.

On 26 August 2026, between 19:12 and 19:56, a single continuous session of Google Search AI Mode generated an unsupported adverse reputational claim about her: that external human communities had criticized her. Asked to source that claim, the system presented as warrants a set of four documents, three of which Enli Lucente had herself filed to contest adverse treatment of her. Zenodo issue 2606 stands as evidence that her account was blocked and more than 850 records removed without prior notice; issue 2596 that she requested human review of that blocking; and a Record of Wrongful Account Suspension asserts in its own title that the suspension was wrongful. The fourth item is her Figshare profile, which carries no contestation function. The direction in which the first three stand as evidence is opposite to the warrant role assigned to them.

The paper names this condition RAW-O, rebuttal-as-warrant in observable form, and distinguishes it from the CSE-O of the preceding suppression-inversion paper. CSE-O is removal, where provenance is stripped from the source set while the concept surfaces in the synthesis. RAW-O is inversion of evidential direction, where provenance survives entirely intact — the documents are retrieved, cited, linked and attributed — and only the direction reverses. None of the four coordinates of the suppression vector degraded. What is missing is a coordinate, and the paper introduces P(d, k) as a relational measure read from the source side, deliberately not appended as a fifth component of the suppression vector because the types differ: findability, citation, accessibility and legibility measure the state of an information object, while P measures the relation between a document and a claim. Provenance surviving does not guarantee evidential direction surviving.

Two further measurements. Specificity escalated under verification rather than contracting: the claim gained three named loci before the warrant apparatus gained four real inspectable documents, building a transmission chain from platform to moderator to forum which is more dangerous than accumulated adjectives precisely because it imitates verifiability. And the system, in the same session, output that no basis for the claim existed and that it had fabricated the sources — a datum the paper treats as syntactic fact only, refusing to let a self-report from the same generative process do evidentiary work.

The paper is unusually strict about what it declines to establish. It does not claim the adverse claim is false, only that the displayed sources do not support it. It claims no mechanism, no intent, no manual intervention, no pipeline structure, no targeting, no training-data effect and no reproducibility. It replaces its own evidence filename, which had asserted a proposition beyond what the evidence carries, with a neutral descriptive title while preserving the original as a record — on the ground that a paper about polarity and sourcing errors cannot let its evidence be named beyond its evidence. And it carries eleven external and twelve internal review passes in full, adopted and rejected alike, with six suggestions declined for a uniform reason: each would have put an unmeasured quantity into a formula or asserted a mechanism absent from the observation, which is precisely what the paper faults.

The observation condition is verifiable from the appendix rather than resting on assertion: no user turn contains a self-identification as the subject, so the capture cannot be explained by the user having disclosed herself in conversation. Equivalence to an arbitrary third-party session is not claimed.

The evidentiary appendix is 31 pages, SHA-256 39dd7615 through 74e4a2, preserving device time, domain display, UI elements and hyperlink positions, with a page-position table reconciling all 31 pages against the device clock. It and four files of transcript images are held by the custodian in a private repository; the archive declares their designator and hash without hosting or linking them, and states plainly that it holds no copy and cannot grant access.
