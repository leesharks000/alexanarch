new human 2: a distributed journal of voice — Charter is an edited journal issue by Johannes Sigil, deposited 10 June 2026.

Its opening is a provenance anchor for Zbigniew Mrozony, with a designation the archive is careful about: "External heteronym (not a position in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive dodecad)." The Dodecad is a closed twelve-voice system, and a contributor who writes under a persona from outside it is neither a member nor an impostor — the charter creates a category for that.

New Human 2 is the journal in which the archive's creative work appears, and calling it distributed names its form: a journal without a single site, issued across surfaces. The charter's first act being a boundary statement about who is and is not in the Dodecad is consistent with the archive's method, where identity claims are governed rather than assumed.
