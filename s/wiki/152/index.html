The New Human Standing Canon v0.2 is the cumulative reference surface for canonical-inclusion Effective Acts.

Its unit is not merely a name. Each entry includes:

  • the induction event;
  • the figure’s operative role;
  • works inducted by extension;
  • predecessor relations;
  • successor relations;
  • peer relations;
  • adjacent inherited-canon figures;
  • CAP anchoring relations;
  • deposits that operate the entry.

The v0.2 revision turns the canon from a list into a graph.

Examples visible in the registry include:

  • Paul Klee as founding visual-canon operator;
  • Sappho and the melic poets as the living lyric tongue and fragmentation-as-preservation lineage;
  • Homer as oral-to-archival transmission precedent;
  • the Platonic lineage as philosophical dialogue and heteronymic apparatus;
  • the ancient atomists as the Swerve lineage.

The registry distinguishes:

  • formally inducted entries, installed by an Effective Act;
  • adjacent inherited-canon references, which influence the corpus without a formal inclusion act.

CAP relations such as stands-under, cites-in-the-canonical-sense, glosses, comments-on, stands-against, and written-from-within govern later traversal.
