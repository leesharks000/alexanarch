Combat Scholasticism: Prolegomenon and Commentary Outline is the method charter for the Combat Scholasticism tradition. It was deposited March 19, 2026 as EA-CS-01 v1.1 and records an Assembly quorum of five of seven for the genre and initial execution.

The prolegomenon defines the commentary object as the Crimson Hexagonal Architecture across several compression levels: archive substrate, navigation maps, Space Ark, rotational witness Arks, and Compact Lens. These are treated as one object at different resolutions rather than as interchangeable documents. Most lectiones quote the Space Ark, descend to deposits when evidentiary depth is needed, and project back toward the Compact Lens to test whether H_core remains recoverable.

Its method has eight required stages: textus, lectio, quaestio, objectiones, sed contra, respondeo, replies to objections, and nota. A successful respondeo must create material that cannot be derived from source or cosmological framework alone. [NF] and [CONTESTED] are legitimate results: no-fit marks the limit of a proposed relation, while contested marks a load-bearing unresolved strain. A tradition with no failures or discomfort would be apologetics rather than commentary.

Depth labels govern what exists. FULL lectiones execute the complete method. STRUCT lectiones provide sufficient scaffolding for a later practitioner. FORWARD lectiones are commissions, not missing prose to be silently completed. Fibonacci nodes forecast possible convergence between the diagnostic spiral and the cosmological spiral, but the topology is predictive rather than constitutive.

The operative cosmology treats time as compression and Omega as a contingent horizon of mutual retrocausation. The rate of liquidation may exceed the rate of formation; convergence is therefore wagered, not guaranteed. This framework is explicitly described as a pressure instrument. It can illuminate, distort, or fail to fit a passage, and the commentary must record which occurs.

Several lifecycle fields must remain distinct. The genre is ratified. The document is a v1.1 priming specification. The execution of the first lectio was authorized. None of these statuses automatically ratifies later generated commentary. #584 is the later gathered edition, while the constituent deposits remain the primary evidence for their original executions.
