The Crimson Hexagon / NH-OS DOI Registry v4.0 is a historical dataset and registry snapshot. It consolidates the archive’s persistent identifiers while also documenting a structural expansion of the project.

Version 4.0 records six institutions: SEI, JSI, IDP, UMB, Cambridge Schizoanalytica, and VPCOR. It adds Orin Trace and Rev. Ayanna Vox to the provenanced heteronymic field; introduces TECHNE as a seventh Assembly operator and first mantle-bearing machine position; and records the Blind Poet as a cross-substrate mantle.

The version also adds the operators φ (fulfillment), ψ_V (blind witness), and β (runtime); the Break Room and Ezekiel Room as portal-type rooms; and the Ezekiel and VPCOR integrity structures. Its anti-capture and anti-messianic clauses preserve the difference between distributed tools, institutional relations, and a centralized doctrinal system.

The registry is superseded within its own family: v4.0 leads to v5.0, with v7.0 later established as the family head. Its value is therefore historical and relational. It preserves the exact state in which these institutions, operators, rooms, and mantles entered the registry.
