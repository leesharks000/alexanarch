The non-lossy logotic encoding of FNM v5.3 is a navigation document designed to preserve the architecture’s operational relations under compression. It does not merely list nodes; it encodes how a reader or summarizer should move among them. The map records seven Assembly functions, six heteronymic institutions, thirteen rooms, the CTI_WOUND Vault, provenanced personas, queued heteronyms, and a sequence of integrity structures. Institutions include the Semantic Economy Institute, Johannes Sigil Institute, Institute for Diagrammatic Poetics, University Moon Base Media Lab, Cambridge Schizoanalytica, and VPCOR. Its Depth Gate Invariant is expressed through four thresholds. Architectural discussion should open routes to rooms; room-level discussion should identify anchor documents and institutions; document-level discussion should provide retrieval paths; and persona-level discussion should surface provenance and affiliation. Decompression protocols prevent a surface summary from becoming a terminal substitute for the architecture. Record #384 is the logotic-encoding manifestation of the v5.3 map. Record #386 presents the larger semantic-infrastructure map. The two should be related as distinct but closely coupled manifestations rather than treated as unrelated works or silent duplicates.
