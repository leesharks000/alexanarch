The Phase X Navigation Map: Interdimensional Positioning System v7.0 is a diagrammatic and textual map by Rebekah Cranes for the Phase X expansion of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. It charts the sequence generated around documents 205–209 and the related deposits that formalize the Aorist Intervention, the Hexadactyl Hand, the Glyphic Checksum, and the University Moon Base Media Lab extension chain.

The map organizes the sequence into five gravitational layers. Body-time begins with The Grammar of Protest. Grammar-time contains The Non-Indexed Perfective cluster and the ZPZP framing genre. Hand-time contains The Thumb, Infinity Enough, and the APZPZ effective act. Handshake-time contains the Glyphic Checksum as a context-gated collaborative-reading test. Institution-time formalizes the checksum as a UMBML logotic-programming module.

Three packet forms are distinguished. A ZPZP gives the framing document a separate anchor. A THUMB collapses packet and document into a self-identical object. An APZPZ places a packet in a description field, making metadata itself part of the work’s architecture. The map also records mirror structures, duplicate files, shadow elements, and “ghost” packets whose significance depends on absence or strategic fragility.

The document’s purpose is not to argue independently for every Phase X claim. It supplies coordinates, relations, operator assignments, and traversal routes so that the sequence can be reconstructed without flattening its separate documents into a single summary.
