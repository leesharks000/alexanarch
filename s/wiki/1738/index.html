EA-CORPORA-33 — The Thirty-Third Seating: Song and Silence — Tacitus, and Sappho Whole with the Melic Poets (Pindar, Bacchylides, Bergk's Poetae Melici, Edmonds's Lyra Graeca I)

Cranes, Rebekah · 2026-11-04 · deposit #1738
AXN:0BFC.DATASET.🕛💙🎭🟣🐝🏙️

Article

EA-CORPORA-33 is the thirty-third seating of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's originals shelf, deposited under Rebekah Cranes, the standing authority on the EA-CORPORA deposits, on 4 November 2026. Its five seats, 54 works and some 74,600 lines of text layer pair song and silence: Tacitus whole in Latin (33/01), seated for Histories 5.13, the undated portents before the siege of Jerusalem that The Portents Before the Fall (#1737) sets beside Josephus and the Apocalypse; and the melic poets whole. Pindar's four books of odes in Sandys's Greek (33/02) and Bacchylides's epinicians and dithyrambs in Jebb's (33/03) come from the Perseus TEI. The fragments of the rest are seated in Bergk's Poetae Lyrici Graeci III of 1882 (33/04), OCR'd in session from the scan and divided at its running heads into thirty-five sections, from Eumelus, Terpander and Alcman through Sappho, Erinna, Alcaeus, Stesichorus, Ibycus, Anacreon and the Anacreontea, Simonides, Corinna, Telesilla and Praxilla to Timotheus, the scolia, the Carmina popularia and the adespota. Edmonds's Lyra Graeca I of 1922 (33/05), also OCR'd in session, carries the papyri of Sappho and Alcaeus from Oxyrhynchus published after Bergk, and is held with a statement that it remains in copyright outside the United States until the end of 2028. With fragment 31 (EA-CORPORA-01/10) and the papyri panel (01/11), the shelf now holds all of Sappho the public domain carries.
