Stabilized Node Watch: A Specification for Longitudinal Observational Infrastructure to Detect Composition-Layer Drift on Stabilized Public-Knowledge Nodes (v1.0)

Lee Sharks · 2026-06-08 · deposit #166
AXN:0301.GOVERNANCE.🗡️🏔️🧊➖🔽☁️

Article

Stabilized Node Watch addresses a monitoring gap between publication-based knowledge institutions and ephemeral composition-layer answers.

A stabilized node has:

  • extensive reference coverage;
  • a consensus core;
  • a documented contestation envelope;
  • high cross-domain citation density.

The program separates:

  • surface drift: directly observed change in outputs;
  • mechanism attribution: graded causal inference.

Its dual baselines are:

  • IOB — Initial Observational Baseline: the output distribution when a node enters the catalog;
  • RCM — Reference Commitment Model: an independently curated model of the node’s core commitments and legitimate disputes.

The proposed workflow includes:

  • node and control selection;
  • fixed query batteries;
  • multi-session and cross-surface captures;
  • proposition extraction;
  • six drift dimensions;
  • composite scoring;
  • hierarchical statistical analysis;
  • changepoint detection;
  • public diff visualization;
  • versioned federation.

Federated implementations may be Core, Extended, Experimental, or Non-comparable while preserving local curatorial independence.

The specification treats observation as a potential tax on unaccountable enclosure: making silent surface change publicly legible may alter incentives even before formal regulation.
