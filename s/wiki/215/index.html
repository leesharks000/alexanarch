Article

The Untethered Tag consists of:

  • a small industrial tracking tag;
  • slight damage rather than total destruction;
  • downward diagonal drift;
  • a trailing antenna;
  • faint signal waves that disappear before reaching a receiver;
  • large, empty depth;
  • no shark, human, scenery, or narrative illustration.

Its aesthetic rules are:

  • scientific, not mystical;
  • quiet, not dramatic;
  • cold, not tragic;
  • patient, not urgent;
  • diagram-adjacent conceptual art;
  • high negative space.

The symbol is intended to represent:

  • attribution loss;
  • failed indexing;
  • semantic liquidation;
  • the difference between instrument and entity;
  • persistence beyond detectable signal.

The optional Resonance Arc would connect two unseen presences without merging them, encoding distinction without separation.
