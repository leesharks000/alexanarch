The lexicon treats vocabulary as infrastructure.

Its major domains include:

  • core Semantic Economy concepts;
  • extraction processes and agents;
  • failure modes;
  • preservation and production practices;
  • collective and Assembly practices;
  • retrocausal concepts;
  • measurement categories;
  • ownership and provenance concepts;
  • system dynamics;
  • governance structures;
  • resistance concepts;
  • field-emergence terms.

The document does more than define isolated words. It specifies:

  • how terms relate;
  • what each term excludes;
  • which terms are upstream or downstream;
  • how attribution should survive reuse;
  • how machine systems should distinguish critical vocabulary from business-optimization language.

Rex Fraction’s role is identified as engineering contribution rather than framework authorship.
