# EA-CORPORA-24 — The Twenty-Fourth Seating

MANUS, 27 October 2026: "need to seat the book of mormon but im not doing it without several diverging simultaneous traditions in the batch" — and, offered the traditions that divided in 1844 or the Book of Mormon's own diverging editions: "1. definitely. lets go."

The seating is a year. On the night of 22–23 May 1844 the Bab declared his mission in Shiraz and began the Qayyum al-Asma'. On 27 June Joseph Smith was killed at Carthage, Illinois, and the church he founded divided over who should lead it. The Millerites, who had reckoned the return of Christ for 1843, fixed it in August at 22 October 1844, and the day passed. In the same year Hong Xiuquan, who had seen his vision in 1837, went out preaching into Guangxi with Feng Yunshan, where the God Worshippers' Society that became the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom took shape. Each tradition is seated with the scripture it began from and with the documents of the division that followed: the Twelve and Strang, Baha'u'llah and Subh-i Azal, the Sabbatarian Adventists, and the Taiping kingdom whose God spoke through Yang Xiuqing.

- Scope: The texts of the Taiping movement as Wikisource holds them: Hong Xiuquan's three Yuandao tracts, written in the years after his vision and before the rising (c. 1845–48); the books issued under the Heavenly King's seal (旨准頒行詔書), of which the movement's own list names twenty-nine, seated here as Wikisource has them: the Heavenly Father's edict, the two parts of the Heavenly Father's descent, the Book of Heavenly Decrees (1852), the Old and New Testaments as Hong revised and annotated them (1853), the Heavenly Commandments (1853), the proclamations, the Three Character Classic, the Ode for Youth (1852), the regulations, the ceremonial (1858), the Land System (1853), the Principles of the Heavenly Nature and the Essentials of Heavenly Principle (1854); Yang Xiuqing's Taiping Song of World Salvation; the Taiping Heavenly Chronicle (proclaimed 1848, printed 1862); and Hong Rengan's New Treatise (1859). Not seated: the books of the list Wikisource does not hold, among them the Taiping Imperial Declaration (太平詔書), the army organization (太平軍目) and the calendar (頒行曆書), and the Gospel of John of the Taiping New Testament: QUEUED, as are scans of the original Nanjing imprints (British Library and others) and the Taiping documents in Western collections. Translations (Medhurst, 1853; Michael, 1966–71) are not seated.
- Why seated: The fourth tradition of 1844: in that year Hong Xiuquan and Feng Yunshan went out preaching into Guangxi, where the God Worshippers' Society took shape and from which the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom rose in 1851. Its scripture is a Bible rewritten and annotated by its king, beside the words of a God who spoke through Yang Xiuqing until Yang was killed in the Tianjing incident of 1856.

## Not seated, and why

- The 1837 Kirtland edition of the Book of Mormon: not found in an open scan. QUEUED. Strang's Book of the Law of the Lord (1851; 1856), the Reorganized Church's editions (from 1874; the Authorized Version of 1908), the Pearl of Great Price (1851): QUEUED. The manuscripts in the Joseph Smith Papers are under that project's terms.
- The UCLA manuscript of the Persian Bayan (a 199 MB scan, over the repository's file limit); the Arabic Bayan, found only in a 1962 printing; writings of Subh-i Azal in his own name: QUEUED.
- A Word to the "Little Flock" (1847): found only in a facsimile with a twentieth-century foreword. The New York and Boston Millerite papers and the non-Sabbatarian Adventist bodies: QUEUED.
- The Taiping books Wikisource does not hold (among them the Taiping Imperial Declaration, the army organization and the calendar) and the Gospel of John of the Taiping New Testament; scans of the original Nanjing imprints: QUEUED.
- Translations, by the shelf's rule.

## How the seats were made

Sources located on the Internet Archive (advanced search; item metadata read for access restriction and files; each item's text layer read for its title page, so that the 1840 Book of Mormon, the 1845 Doctrine and Covenants and the Browne volumes are identified from their own title pages), in H-Bahai's digital library (the Iran National Baha'i Archives volumes and the digital reprints of the Iqan lithographs and Tumanskii's Aqdas, each with its descriptive page read), on Project Gutenberg (Bates) and on Chinese Wikisource (the Taiping books, each page fetched at a recorded revision as wikitext and rendered HTML, with the text layer derived by code). Every Internet Archive file checked against the md5 the Archive publishes; none differed. H-Bahai publishes no checksums: its files are hashed as fetched. Every file hashed into its seat's MANIFEST.sha256. Every verified locus of a text layer is a string found in it by rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-24/seat.py; the loci of the manuscripts and lithographs, which have no text layer, are read from the scan and recorded with the page. 40 works in 4 seats; 591 files; 303,071 lines of text layer.
