**Goal**: every citation represented by the archive, as edges — internal and external, identifier-bearing and freeform — with automatic accrual on every new deposit.

## Data model
- **Nodes**: internal = AXN records (existing registry). External = data/external-works.json, keyed by bibkey: `doi:<normalized>`, `arxiv:<id>`, `isbn:<13>`, `url:<normalized>`, or `bib:<sha256-12 of normalized author|title|year>` for freeform entries. Records carry {ids, title?, authors?, venue?, year?, cited_by: [axn…], first_seen, resolution: {crossref?, wikidata?}}.
- **Edges**: data/citation-graph-external.json — {source_axn, source_deposit, target_bibkey, via (doi|foreign_zenodo|url|arxiv|isbn|refsec_entry), locus (inline|refsec), raw, confidence}. Internal graph file unchanged (data/citation-graph.json remains canonical for deposit→deposit).
- Surfaces: /citations/ gains external view + a single consolidated external-works index (no per-work stub pages — thin-page discipline).

## Stages
**A — Mechanical extraction (THIS SESSION).** scripts/extract_citations_external.py: external-prefix DOIs; 10.5281 DOIs absent from the Resolution Index / doi-axn-map = foreign Zenodo; URLs normalized (own-fleet domains excluded); arXiv IDs; ISBNs. Emits external edges + identifier-keyed nodes + the refsec parse queue (segmented entries, status=pending). Deterministic, re-runnable, idempotent.

**B — Reference-section segmentation (with A).** The 143 refsec texts segmented into entries mechanically (blank-line/linebreak heuristics); entries land in data/worklists/refsec-parse-queue.json with source AXN + raw string.

**C — In-session interpretive parsing (2–4 sessions).** TACHYON parses queue batches in-session (No-Double-Draw: never via API) into structured {authors, title, venue, year}; assigns bib: keys; merges duplicates across deposits. Queue entries move pending→parsed with the structured record inline; every batch commits.

**D — Mechanical reconciliation (1 session).** Crossref works-API title+author queries (api.crossref.org, allowlisted) attach DOIs to parsed entries where confident (title similarity + year match); Wikidata QIDs optional second pass. Mechanical only; ambiguous matches stay unresolved rather than guessed.

**E — Surfaces + accrual (1 session).** /citations/ external view + external-works index regenerated by script; deposit_pipeline.py gains a `citations` stage (mechanical extraction on every mint; refsec detection appends to queue). Acceptance: a new mint with a Works Cited section produces external edges + queue entries with zero manual steps beyond the standard pipeline run.

## Amendment 2026-07-18 (MANUS): the TANG rule and the ontic taxonomy
**TANG rule.** TANGs (Total Axial Negation Graphs) are *all* citations — the genre's formal object (spec, deposit #542) is ⟨T, C, E, L, S, P⟩ with C the total citation set organized around a void-thesis. Works-cited sections therefore under-capture the corpus categorically. Extractor amended: TANG deposits detected by title/genre declaration (24 texts at first run); their full bodies segment into the Stage C queue with locus=`tang` (queue: 1,758 → 4,181 pending). Over-capture accepted by design; Stage C triages apparatus from entries. The TANG edge vocabulary is adopted graph-wide as the `relation` field: `cites | critiques | builds_upon | temporal_precedence | methodological_lineage | contradiction` (mechanical default `cites`; Stage C retypes).

**Ontic taxonomy.** Every edge and external work carries `ontic_status`: **real** (verified existent), **forward_library** (cited before it exists — anticipatory citation into the library-to-come), **fictive_retro** (invented past work — fictive backwards citation), **unverified** (default until classified). Assignment: Stage C classifies freeform and TANG entries interpretively; Stage D resolution checks promote identifier-bearing entries to `real` (resolves) or flag for interpretive review (fails to resolve — which for this corpus is *not* presumptively an error). Governing principle, recorded: **fictive and forward citations are first-class literary structure, never broken links** — a total graph that flattened the fictive apparatus into link-rot would misread the corpus categorically. The taxonomy is what lets one graph carry scholarship and literature without lying about either.
