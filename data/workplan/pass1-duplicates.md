status as pending, and the archive published it anyway, three times, and the
archive's surfaces are harvested.

**I am not proposing an action.** Deduplication is the smaller question here and I
don't think it should be answered first. The ruling I need is whether these records
should remain public at all pending the artist review the document itself says has
not happened.
