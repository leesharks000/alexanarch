# Version-field contradictions — for reading

restoration markers (`v0.1-semi`, `v0.2-restored`), and `[SUPERSEDED → vX]` titles,
where the title names the successor rather than the record. What remains is a genuine
contradiction: two work-version numbers, both specific, disagreeing.

Only #1397 is repaired, because only there did a third source adjudicate: its body's own
JSON-LD declared `"version": "6.1"`, agreeing with the title against the field. The rest
have no such tiebreak and are listed, not changed.
