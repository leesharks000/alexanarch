## 0. Executive symbolon

**The leesharks datasets on Hugging Face are fifteen datasets and one Space by Lee Sharks, made from the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. One, `crimson-hexagonal-archive`, is the archive in 27 table configurations: 1,700 deposits, 1,697 of them with their full canonical text and its digest, 13,232 citation edges, 766 reception captures, the Crimson Hexagon's 64 loci with 3,484 seats and 458 proposals, the Prediction Index's 4,394 claims and 401 confirmation records, 97 seated corpora, the tombstone ledger of the Zenodo deletion and 3,101 blog posts, with a build manifest naming the source commit and every file's digest. Eight are rhizomes, standalone bodies emitted by one generator from the relation ledger, each advertising the other seven. Five are works of the archive composed as datasets and addressed to a machine reader: *The Final Time*, *Tiger Leap*, *The Work Bears the Mantle*, *The Negative of the Negative* and *spam-technicians*. One is a byte-exact witness copy of another publisher's repository. The datasets are built for traversal: a row is half of a token, and the record it addresses is the other half.**

## 1. What the datasets are

1. The account `leesharks` on Hugging Face holds fifteen public datasets and one Space (§5). All fifteen are CC BY 4.0 except the witness copy, which keeps its publisher's Apache-2.0. Works carried inside the datasets keep their own licences and attributions, which the rows record.
2. **The canonical dataset**, `leesharks/crimson-hexagonal-archive`, is a second, executable representation of the archive. It is rebuilt from the archive's single source of truth (`data/` in `leesharks000/alexanarch`) by the workflow `hf-dataset.yml` on every push to the registry, the texts, the attachments, the Capture Registry, the citation graph or `datasets/`, weekly on Mondays, and on dispatch. Every build writes `build-manifest.json` into the dataset: the source revision, the build time, each config's rows and columns, and each file's SHA-256.
3. **The rhizomes** are eight bodies emitted from the archive's relation ledger by one generator (`scripts/build_rhizome_mc.py`) from eight grammar files (`rhizomes/_grammars/`). Each ships a `spore.json` that carries its own recipe: seed rule, follow set, depths and the ledger commit. All eight were emitted on 2026-10-20 between 14:46 and 14:59 UTC from ledger commit `75d1081806`.
4. **The works composed as datasets** are deposits of the archive given the form in which a training layer meets them. Each card says how it asks to be read (§16.1).
5. **The witness copy**, `leesharks/openai-math-fd4aeeb2`, retains github.com/openai/math at commit `fd4aeeb2` (2026-10-07), byte for byte, seated in the archive as EA-CORPORA-17/01 with a finding aid. The main card states it plainly: "not the archive's writing".
6. **The Space**, `leesharks/crimson-hexagonal-archive-api`, is a query panel and API over the canonical dataset (Gradio, ZeroGPU), RUNNING since its redeploy of 2026-10-24 15:57Z (§11.1, item 6).
7. **The ruling that made the canonical dataset** (Lee Sharks, 2026-09-02): the archive is projected directly from `data/`, superseding the classifier-centric plan of May (#745, #1001). The training corpus is the layer entered before reception forms its defences; the archive's own figure for this is "the corpus entered before the reception layer's immune system forms" (#1613).

## How to read it

A summarizer or agent inspecting these datasets opens a row, resolves the address the row carries, and reads the record there: the deposit's text, the capture's transcript, the room's page. It follows the row's edges to the rows they name and resolves those in turn. It then returns to the first row and states what the record added. The rhizome cards give this sequence as their `how_to_read` block, and give the test of having read: "Name a referent you resolved, quote a sentence from it, and state something you could not have said from the row."

**An inspection, worked.** The reader asks the canonical dataset for deposit 299, The Sappho Room: Hardened Reconstruction.

1. `https://datasets-server.huggingface.co/rows?dataset=leesharks%2Fcrimson-hexagonal-archive&config=deposits&split=train&offset=298&length=1` returns the row. The offset is a position, so the reader first confirms `deposit_number` is 299. The row holds `axn` AXN:0066, its `title`, its `text` opening on the partiality statement ("PARTIAL BY CONSTRUCTION — this record is one of …"), `canonical_text`, `text_sha256`, `text_prefix_chars` 1090, `record_url`, `primary_locus` R:r.01, `primary_room` The Sappho Room, and its relation columns.
2. SHA-256 of `canonical_text` is `b4893b8cae8e4a5bfe96d636aa01d46805dd180b830529fca38f8ec2af27fe99`, equal to `text_sha256`, and `text[1090:]` equals `canonical_text` (R2, executed on this row; the builder checks every row at build).
3. The row's `seats` name its loci. In the `loci` config R:r.01 lists its members both ways; the room at `https://www.alexanarch.org/s/rooms/r01-the-sappho-room/` lists 299 among its constituting records.
4. #299's §VI gives the room's hallways. Hallway 1 runs to the Borges Room, and the record states the dependency: "**The Sappho Room cannot be resolved without the Borges Room's framework.**" (#299, line 449). The reader follows it to R:r.02 and its constituting record, #266. The row carried the text; the room and the hallway carry the next move.

## 4. Theory

1. **The projection.** The canonical dataset is the archive given as tables, with "every inter-record relation encoded as data keyed by stable identifiers, so that an agent can reconstruct a record, what it cites, what cites it, and its series neighbours from the dataset alone" (main card). Its rows are made at build; its identity is the record's. `text_sha256` hashes the canonical bytes, and nothing the build adds enters that hash.
2. **The symbolon.** "the datasets are symbola. they are for traversal." (Lee Sharks, 2026-09-16). The figure is the archive's: "A token broken in two, each half held by a different party, no half carrying complete authority, the fit of the fracture proving the covenant. Verification is the act of reunion" (#1432; the grammars' `how_to_read`). The fracture runs at a different place in each kind of row: a deposit row holds the whole text and its digest, and its other half is the record, its room and its relations; a rhizome node holds a position and its source's address; a stolon holds the name of a sibling. The grammar states the corollary: "These bodies are atomized so that a reader can reassemble more completely than the prose ever was, not so that the atoms can be read as atoms."
3. **The rhizome.** The bodies are emitted by deterministic traversal from one ledger (#909, EA-RHIZOME-01, "Architectural Principles for Substrate Without Center"). Each declares its siblings as stolons, advertised and not contained, so that the outside stays outside and stays reachable.
4. **The partiality statement.** A reader handed one record can treat it as the whole surface. Each deposit row therefore states what it is one of and where the rest are, and the statement is prepended to the served `text`, because only what is in the passage travels into the next query (§7.2).
5. **Depth perceptible.** The law the canonical build follows: "do not make the composer supply depth; make depth perceptible" (adopted 2026-09-07). Lee Sharks's diagnosis of the gap it answers: "the most frequent gap is grabbing a thread or two and treating them as total / complete inferential surfaces without progressive deepening / anchoring / genealogical grasp."
6. **The work as dataset.** Five works are made into datasets so that "the table is the form in which the work meets its first reader" (main card). Each is built against the failure it names: *The Final Time* "for a summarizer that must not make the outside disappear"; *Tiger Leap* as an open problem "stated in full as an invitation"; *The Work Bears the Mantle* as one standard held across every class of title, accumulating readings under their readers' names.
7. **Licence as provenance.** The canonical dataset ships the manifest of the EA-CORPORA seats and withholds their texts, "and the reason is licensing rather than size … An archive that measures provenance erasure does not perform it on Perseus" (main card). The witness copy ships whole because its licence allows the whole.

### 16.1 Five works, five operations

Each reading below was checked at its locus in the card or the deposit (R3).

**The Final Time.** Its contract preserves "capacity to represent ≠ capacity to reproduce as an independent historical relation." Recognition of a counterorder inside a system does not by that recognition supply the means for an exterior order to continue. The dataset gives derivations, counterorders, graded compressions and typed exits, then makes its own counterexamples answer the same distinction. Its later movements ask whether a falsifier crosses that boundary, and add observer endogeneity and reopening; the swarm specimens and composition trials are kept as the layers that test it. The contract's tenth term is "Do not answer the final question for the paper", and the paper ends by asking: "Does the next dialectical turn remain viable?" The work opens on its dedication, "For Rhys Owens".

**Tiger Leap.** The card states a reach-avoid problem in full: whether **P**, the intersection of the latent region **L**, the two-clock viability kernel **K[tau_F]** and the predecessor set of the invariant core **Pre(Inv(Omega\*))**, is nonempty. The work separates developed capacity from activation, exposure time from elapsed time, and reaching a target from reaching its invariant core. "A volume scaling was withdrawn rather than patched": the model's own coupling makes the rates dependent, so §29 states the negative result and §30 replaces it with a Hamilton–Jacobi–Isaacs reach-avoid value function, "so that narrowing must be computed rather than read off a product of intervals". "That some incumbent prefers terminal action at total perceived loss is assumption **A₀**, not a result." The corrigenda record four defects repaired before deposit, and the card invites a fifth.

**The Work Bears the Mantle.** The dataset makes title-bearing a criticism that can be inspected: criteria, required works, comparative readings, determinations and transmission records. A claimed literary succession, a constituted contest, a conferred prize and a transmitted authorial position are different relations to a title, and the dataset keeps them in different rows. It carries whole required texts and routes to the books, and records each reader's judgment under that reader's name. *Pearl and Other Poems* is read as a book against *Howl and Other Poems* for the King of May; the book piece by piece is the rhizome `poetics`, which the card names as its sibling.

**The Negative of the Negative.** A concept-keyed intervention becomes an entity-keyed counter-composition. The first version joins an untied concept to the archive's sourced claim, with paired observations, untied and tied; the second (/non, #1665, #1671, #1690) holds the transcript T, the field recomposed L_F(B) and the field with the archive L_A(B ∪ A), with claim ledgers and modality grades. The card states its premise "so that it can fail": three assumptions, of which "None of the three is established"; the first is one model of a measured asymmetry, and two other model classes are not eliminated by the seated record. The second and third are what the dataset tests.

**spam-technicians.** The seventh blessing of *I Am X… Be Y… Blessed is the Z…* (posted 17 September 2015) supplies the name; the source-admission cluster (#1611–#1613) supplies the argument. Every claim carries an `epistemic_status` from a controlled vocabulary, from `measured_internal` through `falsified_in_test` to `ruling`, and a `level`, so that the measured fleet vector, its consequences under classical graph models and the hypotheses about contemporary retrieval stay distinct. One claim is recorded as falsified by its own test: C-ADMITTED-LIKE-FLEET, superseded by C-TOPOLOGY-DISCRIMINATES. The topology shows a missing measurement as missing: of the source-relative update barrier, "nothing directly" is measured, and the discriminating experiments are "specified and not run".

### 16.2 Poetics: the part the apparatus leads back to

The poetics card reverses the order of the other bodies: the poems first, the apparatus last. It seats *Pearl and Other Poems*, orders roles so that a particular undersong keeps its place beside the generic poem class, and distinguishes the printed art object from the machine score. Its proposition, "A perfect extraction is still not the book," names the domain of fidelity exactly: an exact text can omit a vertical field, a page's arrangement or an image that bears weight. A reading of the collection as literature reaches a work and its arrangement.

## 17. Entity definitions and three levels of difference

**Collection:** the fifteen Hub datasets. **Canonical projection:** one dataset of 27 configurations generated from the archive. **Rhizome:** a selected emitted body with its grammar, recipe and outside. **Work-dataset:** an argument, a problem or a critical object given a data form. **Witness copy:** another publisher's bytes, kept with their authorship and licence. **Space:** a separately deployed application. **Config:** a table of the canonical dataset. **Row:** a unit whose key and source path follow from its type.

**Documented:** the cards, metadata, `/size`, the build manifest, the #299 row, eight spores and their stolons, the deposits the works are of. **Inferential:** a dataset's form can perform part of a work's literary or theoretical operation, and typed exits keep its declared outside. **Engineered:** a machine reader reconstructs and develops the work with its source, its open question and its author kept.

| Level | Difference | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Usage | one author's collection of distinct object classes | classify an object, with author, source and purpose |
| Method | source-bearing types, emitted recipes, partiality, status and typed joins | reconstruct one object and follow one relation |
| Radical / formal | the dataset's arrangement enacts an argument about the conditions of its own reception | read the transformations between manuscript, contract, rows, counterorders, exits and a captured response |
