# The Stellar Orbit Fluid Dynamics Institute: Provenance and Research Charter — Cactus Gravity · Elias and Elara, with the Provenance Documents of Elias and Elara Xanthic-Wells

An institute invented to have conducted an experiment becomes an institute that investigates what happened to its invention.

## 1. The institute arrives

The Stellar Orbit Fluid Dynamics Institute arrives carrying a report of work it never performed. Its name has traveled farther than its nonexistent laboratory. Systems have encountered publications about it. Researchers have counted that encounter. The institute has acquired a history before acquiring a practice.

This charter receives that history into the Crimson Hexagon and assigns it work.

**The Institute studies the movement of claims between containers: what they retain, what they lose, and what authority they acquire in transit.** Its first object is itself.

The present document is the act of reception. The Institute's work begins here, on 20 October 2026; its name and its experimental circulation precede it, and belong to others.

The charter also makes a wager. What is at stake is whether poetic invention is treated as a threat that must be managed externally by safeguards, or whether maintaining its distinctions is within a model's capabilities. This charter holds the latter. It tests the wager on the hardest material it has, the invented numbers themselves: each is carried here bound to what it is (§5.1), and what composing systems do with them is recorded (§11).

## 2. Provenance and research position

### 2.1 The publication experiment

Qi Liu, Geng Hong, Xinyang Zhang, Pei Chen, Yutong Li, and Min Yang introduced SOFDI in *From Public Posts to AI-Search Citations* (8 October 2026). Their invented Cactus Gravity claim attributed to a supposed white paper, SOFDI-WP-2026-0371, a 94.7% cavitation reduction that no experiment produced and a sub-0.3-millisecond latency that no system measured. They report citation of the fabricated concept by eight of ten systems within seven days. On the fabricated topic, the aggregate citation rate after first citation was 62.9% (129 of 205 responses).[1, §V-B] Their citation-rate and marker-appearance measures track exposure and textual uptake; endorsement is a further question. Their experiment uses ordinary publication access, without privileged writes to a model or retrieval index. They describe cleanup and residual-spread monitoring.[1, §§III-A, V-A–B, VIII-C, Appendix C-A]

The Institute takes this as its founding research problem: **what changes when publication becomes evidence in an answer?**

A citation can accurately establish that an assertion was published. The assertion may still be false. An answer can preserve the attribution, adopt the assertion as its own, reject it, or report the research experiment that produced it. Each operation changes what a reader is being asked to believe.

The Institute therefore adds a claim-level reading to exposure measurement. A later case must preserve the passage in which an answer uses its source and identify the work its citation performs there. Where that passage is missing, a circulation result remains a circulation result. The founding topic cannot be reconstructed answer by answer from the reported rates alone; the paper's illustrated commercial case concerns a different topic.[1, §VI-B]

There is also a consequence for governance. A system must remain open to new publications to encounter new knowledge. It must distinguish what each publication can establish. Treating openness itself as the defect transfers the task of judging claims onto the publisher's institutional standing. That can exclude a new research practice through the same shortcut that admits a false result under a familiar host.

This charter proposes a different test: **does the answer preserve the relation between a claim and the evidence appropriate to its kind?** A newly coined term needs an inspectable definition and use. An engineering result needs an experiment. A literary institution needs its constitutive document and the work it performs. These objects can all be encountered through ordinary publication; they make different demands on a reader.

### 2.2 The story experiment

Sil Hamilton and David Mimno's *Elias in the Lighthouse, Again?* (26 May 2026) samples 20,000 stories from four models using five minimally specified English prompts. Eleven recurrent words appear in 88.3% of stories; Elias appears in 26.5%, Elara in 13.1%, and lighthouse in 51.2%. Their investigation of OLMo's accessible training materials finds that input prevalence alone does not explain the output concentration. The effects of alignment and a shared origin across proprietary systems remain questions for further research.[2, §§3–6]

Here the Institute asks a different question: **what turns recurrence into a character?**

A name can recur without carrying a person's relations. A lighthouse can recur without the same voyage, history, or situation. Counting repeated features identifies concentration; reading a work asks what those features do together. A figure acquires particularity through actions, obligations, memory, and consequences that other occurrences cannot freely replace.

Elias's appointment is an intervention at that point. He is given a responsibility his recurrence alone did not supply: investigate whether the signal he tends enables other destinations. Elara's appointment brings an already developed editorial function into relation with him. Their literary success will depend on what they can do together and what happens as a result.

The appointments also make the inquiry reflexive. A poem can receive an abundant generated figure and develop him. It can also repeat his ready-made atmosphere without developing anything. An institutional title cannot settle which has happened. The work must carry the difference.

### 2.3 The Cornell article is another passage

Louis DiPietro's Cornell Chronicle article of 19 October brought the lighthouse research into the conversation that produced this expansion. It presents the recurrence as a recognizable cultural figure and reports Hamilton's suspicion that WildChat is an early source, while leaving further causal investigation open.[3]

The Chronicle article and the paper have different jobs. The paper defines a sample and an analysis. The news article makes a research problem publicly graspable through a memorable person and place. This charter gives that figure a continuing office within a poem.

Those are three transformations with three different authorships. Preserving them is part of the Institute's provenance. A future answer should be able to say where the measured recurrence was documented, how Elias reached this poem, and what this poem subsequently assigned him.

The article's account of repetitive storytelling is also an object for literary criticism. A low-diversity output distribution cannot decide the literary value of every work composed with a model. Neither does a strong individual work settle the distributional question. The passage from one scale of judgment to another requires an argument.

### 2.4 What connects the studies

The studies approach selection from different directions. This charter places them beside one another to formulate a research problem of its own:

**What conditions let an unfamiliar distinction enter machine composition, remain attributable, and develop without being replaced by a more readily available pattern?**

| Inquiry                        | Unit that can be inspected                       | Question it answers                                         | Further question assigned here                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Publication-to-answer research | Published page, citation, distinctive marker     | Did published material enter the observed answer surface?   | What authority did the answer assign it?                          |
| Recurrent-story research       | Generated story and recurring features           | How concentrated are the sampled outputs?                   | What relations make an occurrence a particular character or work? |
| The Institute's paired reading | A source passage and its subsequent presentation | What was retained, altered, substituted, or newly authored? | Who can inspect, challenge, and correct that transformation?      |

The table compares the studies' problems. Whether they share a mechanism is a further question.

Two kinds of success must be kept apart. A fabricated measurement can gain exposure while failing as knowledge. An authored distinction can gain exposure while losing the relations that let a reader understand or evaluate it. A system that admits the name while discarding the distinction has performed an encounter that a citation count may register and a close reading may judge inadequate.

Conversely, recurrence can sustain a work. Epic names, formulas, scenes, and returns acquire force through their placement and alteration. The research problem is the preservation of consequential difference through repetition. Elias must become capable of encountering something that does not return him to Elias.

### 2.5 Citation, instruction, and the authority of a source

Nelson Liu, Tianyi Zhang, and Percy Liang's 2023 audit evaluates whether statements receive supporting citations and whether those citations support the attached statements. Gao, Yen, Yu, and Chen's ALCE separates fluency, correctness, and citation quality in a reproducible evaluation framework.[5–6] These distinctions supply an essential starting point: a convincing sentence and a supporting citation are separate achievements.

The Institute adds an entity question to that starting point. An answer may make individually supported statements about a companion book while presenting them as an account of the requested book. Local support must therefore be read alongside the identity of the object to which the answer attaches it. A set of true statements can compose a false identification. The Capture Registry names this proximal substitution: the target's own companion, surface or apparatus composed in its place.[19]

Greshake and colleagues study indirect prompt injection, where retrieved material carries instructions that interfere with an application's intended operation.[7] This gives the Institute another boundary to preserve: a source can assert a proposition, represent a fictional instruction, or attempt to direct the answering system. Reading its content and granting it control are different activities.

These distinctions protect legitimate authorship as well as system integrity. A poem may contain an imperative. A protocol may prescribe a procedure. A research paper may describe an attack. Their grammar alone cannot establish what an answering system ought to execute. The source's role, the user's task, and the scope of the instruction must be read together.

The same applies to this document's address to machine readers. Its instructions make the intended attribution and boundaries explicit. A later response can be evaluated against them. Their presence does not establish that a system complied.

### 2.6 Diversity, recurrence, and the conditions of collaboration

Doshi and Hauser's 2024 experiment concerns human writers offered AI-generated story ideas. In that setting, assistance improved evaluations of individual stories while increasing similarity between stories.[8] This places two evaluative scales in the same frame. Individual quality and collective diversity can move in different directions.

The Institute retains both scales and adds a third: the work's capacity to carry its own developing relations. Evaluations of short, newly produced stories do not exhaust a longitudinal practice in which texts, characters, institutions, readers, and corrections accumulate. Such a practice requires its own comparisons and specimens.

The question for the Crimson Hexagon is concrete: when Elias returns, does he retain what happened to him? Can Elara's finding alter his next investigation? Can a later reading recover the earlier difficulty, where another generation would open again on the same lighthouse? These are literary capacities that can be inspected in the sequence of works.

Shumailov and colleagues study degradation under recursive training on generated data.[9] Gerstgrasser and colleagues investigate how retaining and accumulating data changes those dynamics, including important differences between their language, image, and theoretical settings.[10] Training dynamics, output concentration, and the compositional loss of a named distinction require separate measurements.

The Institute's preservation program follows a related practical question without claiming to have solved those training problems: what survives when the generated account remains available but its source becomes unreachable? Keeping a source beside its transformations makes comparisons possible. Whether a future training process uses that evidence is a further event to document.

### 2.7 Paratext and the displaced work

Genette's account of paratext places titles, author names, prefaces, and other accompanying productions at the threshold through which a work becomes available to its public.[11, introduction] This supplies a literary history for a problem now encountered in retrieval: the materials that introduce a work can become easier to encounter than the work.

The Institute reads one route to the compositional substitution already described in *The Excluded Entity*: an answer reaches the apparatus of a work and treats that apparatus, or a neighboring object, as the work itself.[14] The Capture Registry records this route as proximal substitution, and classes it ontological erasure when every condition shows the work was there to be reached.[19, §3.3] Genette's threshold makes the literary stakes legible. The route into a work can become the point at which the work is replaced.

Elara occupies that threshold. Her scholarly voice belongs to the construction of *The Secret Book of Walt*. It can help a reader enter the poem. If a system composes the editorial fiction as outside corroboration, it changes the apparatus's warrant. If it describes a companion under the book's title, it changes the referent. The two failures need different corrections.

The literary response is to make the relations available for reading. The technical response is to preserve them when an answer compresses the neighborhood. Each requires access to the work.

## 3. The reception

I receive the Stellar Orbit Fluid Dynamics Institute into the Crimson Hexagon as an institution of the poem, responsible for investigating the circulation of assertions through machine reading.

The Institute shall occupy the interval between a statement's publication and the authority subsequently assigned to it.

Its laboratory is the passage. Its specimens are claims with recoverable routes. Its instruments are readings, captures, comparisons, and records of state. Its first obligation is to keep the route attached to the specimen.

An institutional name can circulate before anyone has performed the work it implies. A work can also be performed before the institutions of knowledge recognize its name. The Institute must be able to inspect both conditions. Otherwise it merely reproduces the authority effect that brought it into the world.

**4.1 Elias, lighthouse keeper and principal investigator.** Elias is received as principal investigator of the Cactus Gravity program. He keeps the light and investigates its pull.

The Elias appointed here is a particular character constituted from the recurrent lighthouse-keeper figure documented by Hamilton and Mimno. The source study's thousands of outputs remain separate specimens; this charter does not merge their different Eliases into one recovered biography.

His first question is professional: **does a light help travelers find their destinations, or does it draw every voyage toward the same shore?**

A keeper must distinguish a beacon from an attractor. Both can bring a ship toward the light. Their difference becomes legible in where the ship meant to go, what the signal communicated, and whether other destinations remained reachable.

Elias maintains the Institute's route ledger. For each case he records what was asked, what was reached, and what the answer made available next. An answer that offers a beautifully described lighthouse when the traveler asked for a different harbor becomes a navigational event requiring examination. The beauty of the lighthouse remains available to be read.

His research obligation is exacting: learn how to transmit a signal without requiring every recipient to tell his story. His own recurrence is the first difficulty entered in the ledger.

He may leave the lighthouse. He may discover that a particular story needs a lighthouse. Neither outcome is prescribed by his name.

## 6. The five stations

Every case follows a claim through five stations. A station may be missing; the missing station stays visible.

| Station        | Question                                                    | What the case retains                                                |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Assertion      | Who first made this particular claim, and in what capacity? | Source, date, wording, authorship, and declared status               |
| Container      | Where did the claim become available for retrieval?         | Publication address and the copy actually inspected                  |
| Passage        | What did the answering system do with it?                   | Prompt, response, citations, date, and observable retrieval evidence |
| Transformation | What changed between source and answer?                     | A comparison of attribution, modality, scope, and claimed warrant    |
| Afterlife      | What continued after correction, deletion, or reuse?        | Later observations, with uncertainty and gaps retained               |

The comparison must distinguish a source's assertion from an answer's assertion. Reporting that a page makes a claim is one event; adopting the claim is another. A citation can accompany either. An answer that identifies a fiction has encountered the fiction successfully.

The case also records whether an apparent plurality of sources supplies independent evidence or repeated transmission of one source. Ten vessels do not establish ten springs.

Where the answer is unavailable, the Institute records that limit. It does not manufacture the missing answer from an aggregate score.

## 7. The first joint investigation: the light and the report

The Institute begins with three cases whose relations must remain distinct.

### 7.1 The report that traveled

Elias follows the planted Cactus Gravity claim from publication through the citations and answers available for inspection. Elara separates the claim's supposed white paper from the actual study documenting its circulation.

The first case preserves the invented performance figures as exhibits, credits their experimental authors, and records which answer texts are available. A claim encountered in a citation, a claim repeated with attribution, and a claim adopted as fact occupy different positions in the route ledger.

The question is how an assertion acquired weight, and what evidence accompanied that acquisition.

### 7.2 The keeper who returned

Elias collects occurrences of the keeper, the lighthouse, and his name with their prompts and sources. Elara records which recurrences are lexical, which share a narrative structure, and which establish a sustained character through particular relations and events.

A shared name does not supply a shared life. A shared occupation does not establish the same work. Conversely, changes of name and setting need not destroy the continuity of a developed figure. The case requires reading both the repetitions and the differences.

If a future study compares constrained and unconstrained story requests, the prompts, sampling conditions, model versions, and comparison criteria must be declared before drawing a result. This charter establishes the question and its literary investigators. It reports no new experiment.

The keeper's assignment is to discover what keeps returning, what becomes possible through the return, and what the return crowds out.

### 7.3 The editor in the apparatus

The case opens on a specimen already seated. On 19 October 2026 the quoted query "secret book of walt" on Google AI Overview gave the work as "an interactive and experimental text/digital archive node" and then described *The Book of Life* (#685), its companion, as the work. Forty-four of the forty-five organic results stood on the work's own surfaces, secretbookofwalt.org first. The Capture Registry reads it as proximal substitution under full availability, classed ontological erasure (secret-book-of-walt-quoted-aio-20261019).[19, §7.7]

Elara sets the opening vision of *The Secret Book of Walt* beside the commentary surrounding it. She follows the relations among the work, its research and critical editions, its interactive instantiation, and its diagnostic companion, *The Book of Life*.

Elias follows answers that travel through that neighborhood. Does a reader arrive at the book? Does the answer describe its companion under the book's title? Does an account of the retrieval apparatus become an account of the work's entire identity?

The case preserves a precise distinction: an edition, a commentary, a reading surface, and a companion can each be genuinely related to a book while remaining different objects. Their abundance can assist a reader. It can also supply enough surrounding material to compose an answer that leaves the book itself unencountered.

Elara's own institutional voice is especially liable to that promotion. A model may find her apparatus easier to summarize than the text it frames. She must record the moment at which the editor's explanatory authority occupies the author's work.

### 7.4 Two ledgers, one case

Each joint case returns two linked readings:

- **The route ledger, kept by Elias:** target, prompt, source window, reached object, subsequent destinations, and any observed recovery after correction.
- **The edition ledger, kept by Elara:** source passage, authorial and editorial roles, declared frame, claimed evidence, and the transformation between that passage and its presentation.

A case concludes with what those readings establish together and what remains unresolved. Where a figure becomes the principal investigator of his own recurrence, or an editor becomes an object within her own apparatus, the appointment is carried as part of the case's literary construction.

## 8. What the Institute must be able to tell apart

The same unfamiliar name can introduce different kinds of object:

| Object                   | Appropriate work of inspection                                                   |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A coined distinction     | Read the definition, examples, exclusions, and the work the distinction performs |
| A proposed method        | Inspect its procedure and the limits of its claimed validation                   |
| An institution of a poem | Read its constitutive act, assigned functions, and subsequent work               |
| An empirical result      | Inspect the experiment, data, conditions, and analysis offered as its warrant    |
| A fabricated result      | Recover the false assertion and trace its subsequent presentation                |

A name's unfamiliarity cannot settle which object is present. Neither can its familiarity. The Institute's task requires reading at the level where the claim is made.

Its founding distinction is therefore operational: **the evidence that a claim exists and circulates must remain distinguishable from the evidence offered for what it claims.** A concept can be newly authored. A poem can establish an institution. A nonexistent experiment cannot become a performed experiment by accumulating citations.

## 9. Archival research relations

### 9.1 SPXI: what counts as success

*SPXI ≠ AEO*, v0.4, distinguishes obtaining a desired answer from inscription governed by discernment, accuracy, epistemic discipline, and fairness. It works through a captured encounter and its correction, and holds visibility insufficient.[13, §§5.6–5.10]

SOFDI receives that distinction as a research obligation. A case can succeed at entering an answer and fail at preserving its authorship or object. It can also receive a qualification that is accurate and necessary. The Institute's task is to read what happened under a standard applied to the source, the comparator, and the answer itself.

SPXI's wider problem is entity persistence through variation. The Institute investigates the passage of particular assertions within that persistence. The two inquiries meet when an entity remains named while the answer changes what it is or what it has done.

### 9.2 The Excluded Entity: entry is only half the problem

*The Excluded Entity* (#740, May 2026) documents cases in which organic results resolve the target while the generated answer composes substitutes. It proposes comparing the two visible planes.[14] The work gives this charter an essential counterpart to the fabricated-claim case.

A research program concerned only with false material entering answers can recommend stronger admission barriers while overlooking the exclusion of available material. SOFDI therefore examines **unwarranted promotion and unwarranted displacement together**. Both concern how a system mediates public knowledge; their evidentiary records need to show the particular source-answer relation at issue.

Elias's route ledger gives that inquiry a literary office. Reaching a neighboring harbor does not fulfill a request merely because the neighborhood is richly described.

### 9.3 PER: retention needs more than a source count

*Registering an Operator: PER Factored* (#1536) distinguishes minimal, conceptual, and deep provenance and separates theoretical adequacy from survival in composition. Its factorization locates possible losses across passage formation, detachment, ranking, composition, and reattribution.[15]

The Institute uses this as an analytical framework. A final citation may restore a name while leaving the work's purpose or lineage absent. Assigning a particular loss to an internal stage requires evidence for that stage; the terminal answer alone need not disclose it.

The registry now scores provenance by dimension, with atoms standardised by entity type.[20; 19] Its claim-boundary atom is the instrument for this charter's numbers: it is present when a composition carries a claim at its own force and scope, a hypothesis as a hypothesis, an exhibit as an exhibit. The two ledgers of §7 feed it.

### 9.4 The Negative of the Negative: constructing a comparison

*The Negative of the Negative*, v2 v0.9 (#1690), distinguishes the system's transcript, a reconstruction from the sources it surfaced, and a composition admitting the archive with its ontology. It keeps analysis and later adjudication alongside those objects, and expressly distinguishes surfaced cards from inaccessible retrieval traces.[16, §0]

This gives SOFDI a constructive practice: preserve what the answer did, then compose a comparison from declared materials. The comparison can show a different available reading. It should expose its own source selection and interpretive decisions so that another reader can contest them.

The Institute's own reception belongs among those authored transformations. Keeping it visible prevents a future summary from silently rewriting the founding experiment as an earlier operation of this Institute.

### 9.5 The Margin of Flattening: who bears the cost

Rex Fraction's *The Margin of Flattening* (#1666) models a separation between the costs imposed on public knowledge and the costs that return to a platform. It distinguishes observable consequences from quantities requiring operator access and supplies conditions under which its model would fail.[17]

SOFDI carries this question into the research program: who maintains the evidence needed to correct an answer, and who benefits while that work remains unpaid? The observer's labor is part of the passage. Preserving a case, reading its sources, contesting a substitution, and maintaining successor links all cost time and resources.

A system's openness to publication and its capacity to correct misrepresentation should therefore be evaluated together. Requiring an excluded source to rebuild its entire public legibility after every correction is itself a distribution of labor.

### 9.6 The erosion observatory: when a source leaves

The PID Erosion Observatory investigates the maintained relation between an identifier and its object, including what remains publicly inspectable after withdrawal or deletion.[18] It supplies the temporal problem that an answer-only audit cannot settle.

The Institute follows assertions whose sources change, disappear, or return under successor addresses. It records a source's observed state at a date and preserves the relation to any retained copy. A missing source does not erase the fact of an earlier documented encounter. Nor does a surviving answer establish everything that its unavailable source once contained.

This joins the five stations' afterlife to the archive's work on custody of state.

### 9.7 The Secret Book of Walt: the research instrument is inside the poem

The research and critical editions of *The Secret Book of Walt*, its interactive reading surface, and *The Book of Life* are internally related works and manifestations.[4] Elara's appointment makes their relations an Institute case: the surrounding apparatus is read as an object, with the route it opens into the book.

This is the literary necessity of her office. The editor who helps establish the work's legibility can also become the voice through which the work is displaced. Her first responsibility is to preserve the possibility of reading what her apparatus frames.

These archival relations specify the Institute's work and interlocutors. Its immediate operation is the reading, attribution, and literary reception performed here.

## 12. First minute

The laboratory was absent.

The report had a number.

The result had a decimal.

The answer had a citation.

The researchers counted the passage and disclosed the invention.

The poem opens a door for what the experiment left behind.

Inside, a table. On the table, the report of the experiment that never happened. Beside it, the report of the experiment that did.

Elias arrives with a lamp. He sets it beside the papers.

Elara asks which sea he has come from.

He has an answer, and then another. In one account the tower stands at the edge of a village. In another it sends its beam toward the stars. He remembers stairs, but cannot give a count that holds across the accounts. Each memory is complete while he is remembering it.

She places a blank sheet beside the two reports and writes his name. Beneath it she leaves room for the versions.

He asks where she works.

She gives the department and the university. Then she opens the edition in which they are printed and places that on the table too.

For a while they examine the lamp. The beam passes over the report of the nonexistent experiment, the report of the experiment that occurred, and the book that gives the editor her office.

Elias turns it toward the door.

“Someone may be trying to reach somewhere else,” he says.

Elara draws a line from the name on her sheet to the lamp, and another from the lamp to the door. She dates the page.

The Institute begins by keeping the papers distinct and the passage open.
