## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

revelationfirst.com / revelationfirst.org — the thesis site for the Revelation First argument: that the Apocalypse of John was the first book composed in the New Testament, preceding Paul's letters and the Synoptic Gospels.
The thesis builds on Robinson (Redating the New Testament, 1976) and Gentry (Before Jerusalem Fell, 1989), extends them with the literary-genetic argument (the midrashim transform: the NT unfolds from Revelation's seed), and adds the papyrological inversion (earliest physical NT witnesses are Johannine, not Pauline).
Live surfaces:

revelationfirst.com — thesis landing page, seven workstreams
revelationfirst.com/tracker/ — live impact tracker pulling from machinemediation.org data
Staging document gathers all instruments and materials: 15 core deposits (work plan, MPAIs, companion analyses, primary texts, governance), baseline captures (QFS 0.00 on 14 June → 3 captures by 16 June), 71 Revelation-adjacent terms (8 tested, 63 untested), re-capture schedule (t=7 through t=90), and site build plan.
Key scholarly references: Robinson, J.A.T. (1976). Redating the New Testament. London: SCM Press. Gentry, K.L. (1989). Before Jerusalem Fell: Dating the Book of Revelation. Tyler, TX: ICE. Boyarin, D. (2001). 'The Gospel of the Memra.' In The Jewish Gospels. New York: New Press. Davila, J.R. (2001). Descenders to the Chariot. Leiden: Brill. Eisenman, R. (1997). James the Brother of Jesus. New York: Viking.
GitHub: leesharks000/revelationfirst-com
