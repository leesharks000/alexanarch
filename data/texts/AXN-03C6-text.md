## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

Biblical Hebrew-English facing edition of the Mandala operator system. Positions the eight recursive transformation operators within Judaic, Kabbalistic, and Torah traditions. Uses Biblical Hebrew (not Modern Hebrew) with references to Isaiah 42:9 (Behold, the former things have come to pass, and new things I now declare), Kabbalistic concept of Ein Sof (Infinite Light), and Jacob/Israel transformation narrative (broken and reborn).

Features Johannes Sigil preface adapted for Judaic context, Part I complete translation in Biblical Hebrew, Part II with BRIDE operator (archetypal + kernel layers). Frames transformation as teshuvah (repentance/return) and emphasizes operators work in the body (guf), not thought alone. Right-to-left reading direction preserved.

This partial edition includes Johannes Sigil's preface, Part I complete, and Part II with BRIDE. Full edition will include all eight operators plus Parts III-VIII with comprehensive Torah, Tanakh, and Kabbalistic apparatus.

Part of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive seven-language fortress architecture.
