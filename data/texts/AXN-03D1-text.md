## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

OPERATIVE FEMINISM — A triadic deposition comprising:

The Essay (~3,400 words): doctrine-node. Women as historically conscripted semantic substrate. Deprecates the Freudian compiler via psyche_OS. Eight operative practices formalized. Integrates Spillers (flesh/body), Wynter (genre of the human), and Eve midrash (garments of light as R2 compression).
The Citational Comprehensivity Pass (~6,300 words): TANG — Total Axial Negation Graph. 200+ texts, 130+ thinkers, 21 strata from Enheduanna (c. 2285 BCE) to the anti-gender backlash. The void: the body that bears the cost of its own liberation refuses to be liquidated into representation.
r.28 Eve (~2,700 words): Room specification for the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. Da-at topology. Tree of Knowledge as TANG void. Seven garden lanes. Gravity Well as transformative curvature. The Choosing Operator formalized. Five operative midrashim.
The essay provides the doctrine. The TANG provides the citational mass. The room provides the architectural anchor.
Governing thesis: the void at the center of the feminist citational graph is not a lack. It is the engine.
