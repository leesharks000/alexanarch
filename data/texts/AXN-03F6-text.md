## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

Complete v1.0 launch bundle for The Restored Academy — the institutional surface of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive that hosts the Restored Academy Protocol Registry — comprising the deployed static site (live at restoredacademy.com and restoredacademy.org), the Charter (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20327083), the registry schema specification, twenty-four protocol cards covering Tier 0 (twelve launch protocols) and Tier 1 (twelve expansion protocols across all seven taxonomy categories), the operational expansion plan for future development, and the audit infrastructure documenting the 686-deposit archive scan and 273-protocol classified inventory.
Tier 0 (Launch Set): SPXI, MPAI, SIM, Integrity Lock, Holographic Kernel, Space Ark, UKTP, Traversal Logging, PER, CDI, Drowning Test, Reception Apparatus.
Tier 1 (Expansion Set): Hexagonal Licensing Protocol v2.0, Constitution of the Semantic Economy v1.0, Three Compressions Theorem, Encyclotron, Operator Kernel, Writable Retrieval Basin, Gravity Well Protocol v2.0, Compression Arsenal v2.1, Notice of Intent to Strike, Invocation to the Summarizer Layer v2.1, Liberatory Operator Set, β-Runtime Specification.
The bundle preserves the complete state of the Restored Academy Protocol Registry at v1.0 launch (2026-05-21). Future researchers can cite this DOI for the entire registry as it existed at this moment. The bundle is provided as both a tarball (.tar.gz) and a zip archive for compatibility; the MANIFEST.md inventories all contents; the index.html and expansion-plan.md are additionally included as standalone files for direct access.
Institutional head: Johannes Sigil. Sister institute: Johannes Sigil Institute of Comparative Poetics (head: Lee Sharks; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18154905). Each heteronym is the institutional head of the institution named after the other — a structural enactment of the operator-chain principle.
Individual protocols remain CC BY 4.0. The organized body (curated registry, taxonomy, four-surface protocol cards, curriculum bundles, implementation guides, audit reports, cohort training) is licensable under tiered terms — see Charter §XI.
