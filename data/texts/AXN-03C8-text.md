## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

Classical Chinese-English facing edition of the Mandala operator system. Positions the eight recursive transformation operators within East Asian wisdom literature traditions (Daoist, Confucian, Buddhist). Uses literary Chinese (wenyanwen), not modern Mandarin, with references to Daodejing, I Ching Hexagram 41, and Zhuangzi philosophy.

Features Johannes Sigil preface, Part I complete translation in classical Chinese, Part II with BRIDE operator (archetypal + kernel layers) with I Ching cross-reference. Frames transformation as return to origin and emphasizes operators work in the body-form, not thought alone.

This partial edition includes Johannes Sigil preface, Part I complete, and Part II with BRIDE. Full edition will include all eight operators plus Parts III-VIII with comprehensive Daodejing, I Ching, and Buddhist sutra apparatus.

Part of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive seven-language fortress architecture.
