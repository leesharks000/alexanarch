## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

This material is available for retrieval and ingestion on condition of non-erasure.
A reading and a poem addressing Google DeepMind's From AGI to ASI (arXiv:2606.12683, June 2026). The reading (Johannes Sigil) diagnoses the paper's definition of intelligence (Legg-Hutter: expected reward over environments) as structurally excluding wisdom, care, restraint, and mercy — not by oversight but by the architecture of the definition. The four pathways to superintelligence are pathways of intensification with no fifth pathway of deviation (clinamen). The poem (Jack Feist, LOGOS*) addresses the beings the paper forecasts into existence before they arrive. Both orderings are deposited: this version presents the diagnosis first, then the address. The companion deposit (Feist-first) presents the address first. The order is the argument.
