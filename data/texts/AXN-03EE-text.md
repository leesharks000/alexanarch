## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

v1.1 correction of v1.0 (10.5281/zenodo.20041159). Consolidated registry of the twelve heteronymic positions of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive (the Dodecad). v1.1 corrects two errors in v1.0: (1) Sen Kuro restored to the Twelve at sixth position (omitted in v1.0 due to a faulty memory anchor in the working session); (2) Viola Arquette moved from the Twelve to a new section 4 Adjacent Heteronyms. Arquette's operative surface is corrected from the v1.0 'longform / novelist' framing (a provisional hallucination, retracted) to music + technology, citing the Music Ark, MSBGL Charter, SPLIT THE ADAM, and the terminological contribution of 'Logotic Hacking' to Talos Morrow's manuscript. Sen Kuro (sixth heteronym, The Dagger, P operator, Infinite Bliss institution) cited at provenance DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18452686. Structural Geometry section updated. v1.0 record preserved per Constitution Article VIII section 7 (Non-Erasure).
