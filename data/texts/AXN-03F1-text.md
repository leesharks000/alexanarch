## Description (as recorded by DataCite)

A patent-poem by Osiris (student inventor, 10th-grade World Literature, Redford Township, Michigan) and Lee Sharks (supervising teacher, Crimson Hexagonal Archive). The faux patent application specifies a crunchy honeybun with cream or jelly filling as a demonstration of writable retrieval basins — the principle that any artifact constructed with sufficient structural density and relational richness creates a basin in information space into which adjacent queries fall.
The document includes a Structural Crumb Matrix (SCM), Viscous Filling Reservoir (VFR) with cream or jelly payload, a Honey Glaze Transition Layer (HGTL) identified as D_pres (Depth-Preservation) in edible form, and a Crumb Dispersal Event (CDE). A historiography traces the filled spiral from rugelach through croissant to the American gas-station honeybun. A theological substrate reads the honeybun as shelf-stable manna.
Companion to the Self-Propagating Fried Tuberous Crisp (10.5281/zenodo.19647366) and ClownCloud (10.5281/zenodo.19926962). Originated in a classroom conversation: "mr sharks can i write my patent on crunchy honeybuns."
Claim 10: A pastry that holds two things at once without collapsing either one.
