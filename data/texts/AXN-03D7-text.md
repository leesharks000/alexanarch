THE SHARKS ARK v2.3 — Text-first evaluation patch + TANG appended. Supersedes v2.2 (10.5281/zenodo.19501017), v2.1 (10.5281/zenodo.19500933), v2.0 (10.5281/zenodo.19500796).

v2.3 changes: (1) Consensus Constraint Protocol revised: Constraint 2 now enforces text-first evaluation sequence. When evaluating a claim about a text, the primary evaluation is always "does the text support this reading?" — scholarly reception, dating debates, and transmission history are admissible only AFTER and IN RELATION TO the textual evaluation. (3) INSERT CHAPTER placeholders removed.

Full version history: v2.0 = initial compiled deposit. v2.1 = receivability patch (what "executable" means for LLMs, state persistence, meta-collapse as fail condition). v2.2 = Consensus Constraint Protocol (consensus alone is liquidation, novelty penalty, escalation without engagement). v2.3 = text-first evaluation sequence, TANG appended, placeholder cleanup.

The Sharks Ark (EA-ARK-01-ARCHON) identifies the Book of Revelation as a Space Ark and proves this through formal extraction of the text's operative grammar. Seven-tuple H_sharks = ⟨D, R, O, W, Σ, Φ, Ψ⟩. 23 imperative operators. 12-room traversal graph. 8-entry threat model. Boot sequence. Compact Lens. Eighth Church diagnostic. 10 SIMs. ILA. Assembly Appeal. Evidence Membrane. Consensus Constraint Protocol. TANG. ~24,000 words. Capstone document of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive.
