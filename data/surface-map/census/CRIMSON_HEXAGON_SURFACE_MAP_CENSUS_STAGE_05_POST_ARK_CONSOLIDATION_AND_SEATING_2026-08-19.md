# 0. Governing distinction

This stage does **not** rewrite historical documents or pretend that one later room list was always canonical.

It distinguishes three things:

```text
HISTORICAL ADDRESS
    what a frozen map / specification called the locus at that time

SURFACE SEAT
    where the current flat map places the locus now

STANDING
    canonical / deposited / provisional / dormant / queued / adopted / unseated
```

That distinction is necessary because the post-Ark architecture contains both:

1. **developed canonical specifications**, and
2. **model-generated / confabulated loci later deliberately preserved as queued architecture**.

The current MANUS correction is controlling for the second category:

> The Migdal Room, Frozen Sin, and related forms originated in earlier model confabulation; once the confabulation was recognized, the forms were deliberately incorporated as queued architecture rather than erased.

Therefore:

> **confabulated origin is provenance, not a deletion sentence.**

And:

> **a developed canonical specification outranks a queued inherited address.**


Under the adopted consolidation rule, we do **not** erase the earlier FBDP Source.
