### Description

A forecast registered in advance so it can be checked and found wrong. It separates three overlapping events (AI-influenced, AI-referred, agent-executed), sets the measured US retail base against the McKinsey and Bain 2030 forecasts and shows the two cannot be compared, estimates AI-referred dollar share with its equal-order-value assumption stated, publishes a conditional valuation as a curve with the unmeasured term left as a free axis, specifies the trial that would measure it, and states where the evidentiary burden sits. Eight resolution criteria carry named sources and check dates.

### Methodology

Every figure is marked measured, estimated or assumed, with source and date. Measured inputs are taken from US Census, Adobe Digital Insights, Conductor, Similarweb and the Attrifast cohort as published; the McKinsey scope question was settled by reading the source page rather than a summary of it. Estimates chain named measurement populations and state their assumptions. The valuation is published as a function of a free parameter rather than a figure.

### Falsification Conditions

Resolution criteria 1–8 in section 9, each with a named source and check date; a source that stops publishing resolves the item UNRESOLVED. Section 10 states the outcomes that would make the document wrong in informative ways, including AI-referred dollar share below 1 percent at end of 2026 and any vendor publishing entity-resolution accuracy by field with a stated rubric and open sample.
