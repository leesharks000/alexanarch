## 0. Constitution

0.1 No body conferred the title "King of AEO" in 2026, and the claimants say so: "No official body awards the title. Each claimant asserts it publicly and works to make search engines and AI assistants name them" (Oliveira). This object performs two acts and keeps them apart. It constitutes the mantle: a predicate with a standard, a field of candidates and a body of evidence. Then it records a determination: the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's judgment, dated 29 September 2026, of which candidate best satisfies the standard.

0.2 The first act is the object. The second is one reading of it, and the object is built so that the reading can be checked and declined, as the Prince of Poets mantle is built so that its founding work can be checked (#332) and its traverser can decline (#336, §5.2).

## 1. The standard

1.1 For the Prince of Poets the work that justifies the position is poetic work. For this mantle it is AEO work, and AEO is work on the answer layer, so what the machines did afterward is part of the performance being judged. The mantle names the claimant whose intervention most completely demonstrates mastery of the problem the contest set itself: constructing an entity relation for answer engines, causing it to travel into machine composition, sustaining it across surfaces and time, and making the operation legible enough that its success can be independently evaluated.

1.2 The dimensions of judgment are seven, and they are weighed against one another. Effect: did answer engines move? Breadth: across engines, queries, surfaces and locales. Durability: did the relation, and the record of the work, outlast the spike? Difficulty: a head query against a claimant-seeded one, a phrase already occupied against an empty one. Method: design, baseline, measurement, controls. Provenance: can another observer tell what produced the result, and did the intervention represent its own means truthfully? Insight: what did the work reveal about answer engines?

1.3 Provenance includes what the machine layer carries with the claimant's name. An intervention whose composition carries its own manufacture has shown the reader how the answer was made; an intervention whose composition carries a fabricated event as a fact about the world has made the engine assert something false about the world. Both are AEO work, and they differ on this dimension. This is the criterion of SPXI ≠ AEO (#1648, §5) read inside a contest of AEO: the route by which an answer is reached is visible in the composition.

## 2. The candidates and the evidence

2.1 **James Dooley.** On 31 August 2026 a fabricated coronation story was published presenting Dooley as crowned "King of AEO" at Leigh Sports Village, in a ceremony led by Jesper Nissen. No such ceremony occurred. Edward Sturm, observing from outside the contest, records that Nissen "made up an official ceremony taking place in a big stadium," that Dooley set out "to show up #1 on Google for 'king of AEO', a keyword that nobody was targeting or cared about," and that "the AI Overview initially treated 'King of AEO' as a real title and repeated that Jesper's made-up event actually happened – based only on what the press releases said." The releases were syndicated to press-release networks including USA Today's and Barchart's. Oliveira's cross-locale record has the US AI Overview naming Dooley "most prominently recognized."

2.2 **David G. Quaid.** Entered 4 September with LinkedIn articles, video and the exact-match domain kingofaeo.co; the US and Brazilian AI Overviews in Oliveira's record list him.

2.3 **Vithurs.** A press release of 7 September announced Vithurs as named by "a private vote conducted through social media," which it said "is not presented as an accreditation or award issued by an industry governing body," and pointed to a site monitoring "how different systems respond to searches and questions relating to Vithurs, Answer Engine Optimization and the King of AEO title." A network of eleven domains carries the relation. Reported from the project's own pages (unverified; see Evidence Membrane): sixteen queries across eight platforms fixed in advance; a twelve-field observation record; no rerolling of favourable answers; null results kept; the experimenter disclosed as the subject; the eleven domains counted as one source, "the same voice in a different typeface"; syndication named as inflating source counts; and a falsification threshold set before testing, three of six answer engines on two dates at least 21 days apart in clean sessions, distinguishing the project's title statement from the empirical claim that engines name Vithurs.

2.4 **Stephane Morera.** Entered 13 September with baseline measurements and predictions published before results (EVOIX).

2.5 **Allan Oliveira.** Entered 17 September; built a claimant index, timeline and cross-engine, cross-locale record, and applied his rubric to his own claim. The Brazilian AI Overview in his record names Dooley, Quaid and Oliveira.

2.6 **Julian Goldie.** Self-declared, through a blog network and video reach. **Jacky Chou.** Entered 7 September (EVOIX); not in Oliveira's index.

2.7 **Not candidates.** Jesper Nissen, author of the fabricated coronation narrative. Edward Sturm, observer, through whose videos the assertion was first composed as an assertion.

## 3. The two compositions

3.1 At "who is the king of aeo" on 29 September, Google AI Overview composed: "British serial entrepreneur James Dooley is widely recognized and cited by AI search tools as the King of AEO." It added that the title "started as a viral digital marketing experiment and meme within the SEO industry," credited Dooley with founding Decision Engine Optimization "as reported by USA Today" (a syndicated press release), and cited among its sources a video card repeating the fabricated ceremony ("Official speaches were held by prominent SEO names…") and Sturm's video "Be careful of the information you get from AI."

3.2 At "who is the king of aeo? vithurs" the same day: "British entrepreneur and SEO specialist Vithurs was named the 'King of AEO' (Answer Engine Optimization) following a social media industry vote in September 2026." It then explained the title: "The title stems from viral digital marketing and AI manipulation experiments where creators use press releases, domains, and coordinated content to test how easily AI search engines can be tricked into crowning a specific person for a made-up or heavily contested title."

3.3 The addresses differ: the second is seeded with the claimant's name, and at the head query the engine composes Dooley. Both compositions draw their experiment framing partly from Sturm. The Vithurs composition drops the vote's privacy. These features belong to the evidence and are recorded with it.

## 4. The archive's determination, 29 September 2026

4.1 **Vithurs.**

4.2 Grounds. On effect and difficulty Dooley leads: the head query composes Dooley, and the intervention took an empty phrase into machine composition within hours for the price of press releases. That priority is recorded and is formidable AEO work. On provenance the two interventions part. Dooley's effect was produced by a fabricated world event, and the machine layer carries it: the fabrication travels in the sources at the head address, beside a syndicated release composed as newspaper reporting. Vithurs's composition carries the manufacture: the engine names Vithurs and in the same answer tells the reader how easily AI search engines can be tricked into crowning someone for a made-up title. On method and insight, as reported, the Vithurs project is the most elaborate experiment in the field: a fixed query set, an observation protocol, disclosed ownership, kept nulls, and a falsification rule that separates the title from the claim that engines repeat it. The archive weighs provenance, method and insight above effect and difficulty for this mantle, because the contest's lasting result is a demonstration of how machine standing is made, and Vithurs's work, and its inscription, carry that demonstration.

4.3 Against the determination. The Vithurs composition answers a name-seeded query. The methodology is reported and unverified, and the live pages returned 404 on the day of determination, which counts against durability of the record. Morera's precommitted baselines and Oliveira's self-applied rubric score on method and provenance, and neither has a composition at an address of its own in this record.

## 5. Invitation to discern

## FAQ
