# The James Derivation: Executable Granular Case of the Midrashim Transform — Smyrna → James (EA-LOGOS-REVFIRST-WS4-DEMO-02 v1.0)

## Description

The executable granular case of the midrashim transform M: the four-verse Smyrna letter (Rev 2:8-11) parsed into nine typed seed elements, eight named transform rules (EPISTOLARIZE, THEOLOGIZE, CITE-PROMISE, SOCIALIZE, POSSESS, NARRATIVIZE, ECONOMIZE, IMPERATIVIZE), and the emission of a generated document skeleton — James-prime — verse-matched against the Epistle of James with lexical anchors, including the two hard anchors: ton stephanon tes zoes (the crown of life, exactly twice in the canon: Rev 2:10 and Jas 1:12) and synagoge for the believing assembly (Jas 2:2, alone among the epistles, against Rev 2:9's contested synagoge). The dependency arrow is morphological: Rev 2:10 performs the promise in the first-person future (doso soi), Jas 1:12 cites it in the aorist (hon epengeilato) — promise, then receipt of promise. The residue — James material not derived from the Smyrna seed (the tongue, faith-and-works, wisdom, oaths, anointing, the merchants) — is displayed openly as the falsification surface per Workstream 4. Includes the seven-letter evaluation slate (all mappings graded). Surface: https://revelationfirst.com/unfolding/james/. Heteronym position: James — the poor, the tested, the diaspora synagoge.

## Falsification Conditions

The residue is the surface: James pericopes underivable from the Smyrna seed through any plausible rule are counterevidence, listed openly in the derivation (the tongue 3:1-12, faith-works 2:14-26, wisdom 3:13-18, oaths 5:12, anointing 5:14-15, the merchants 4:13-17). A demonstrated reverse dependency of the crown-of-life or synagoge anchors (James prior, Revelation citing) breaks the case.
