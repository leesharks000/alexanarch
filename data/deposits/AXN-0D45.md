# Who Benefits from Bad Inference? The Semantic and Political Economy of Inference across the Archive's Domains

Lee Sharks · Johannes Sigil · Nobel Glas · Rex Fraction · Transactions of the Semantic Economy Institute · Crimson Hexagonal Archive · 23 November 2026

**Status.** A standalone paper, companion to the *Critique of Pure Inference* (CPI, #1766 v1.2). It develops an argument and an audit specification. It does not report measured profits, an implemented accounting system or established legal liabilities, and it makes no claim about anyone's intent. Its making, the readers whose extractions it rests on, and its versions are recorded under Provenance at the end. *Operator*, where the paper quotes one, is Lee Sharks, the author directing the archive's work, whose rulings are cited where they decide structure or status.

**The question, in the operator's words** (v0.2, 14 November): "Who benefits from bad inference? How is inference and inferential representation converted to political economy?"

**Governing proposition.** An inference becomes a political-economic operation when its representation acquires authority to allocate labor, attention, resources, risk, credit, or the conditions of further inquiry. Its adequacy to the object and its usefulness to the allocating institution can then diverge. Where the institution captures the benefit and others bear the cost of the divergence, error can remain reproductively successful after it has become epistemically indefensible.

**Demand-formation corollary.** An inferential regime can also govern the concepts, comparisons, encounters and standards through which a loss becomes recognizable and a demand for repair becomes possible. Where it excludes those means, the resulting absence of demand cannot serve as independent evidence that nothing valuable was lost. Bad inference can appropriate the means of producing demand for better inference. The operator, at v0.2's revision: "bad inference can seize the means of the production of demand — there is no demand for repair, no demand for better inference, because one would have to be able to better infer what has been taken."

**What the domains add.** Read across seven domains, chosen because the archive's deposits pursue the benefit question in them at length, the beneficiary of a bad inference is almost never a person. It is a position: the centre, the journal of record, the credential, the reconstructed source, the selector, the mediator, the sorter. The deposits name these positions by function and incentive and decline to read motive: "None of this requires motive. Each compression has an incentive (legibility, tractability, a pipeline's throughput) and a function (what drops out)" (#1742 §9.2); "The point is structural, not a reading of hearts" (#1798 §8.2); "The mechanics are visible at the loci, and they can be stated without a motive" (#1746 §7.1). Eleven operators recur across the domains, each witnessed in at least two, with different operators prominent in different domains (Part II). They work by different means: a label, a paywall, a reconstructed source and an irreversible trigger are not materially alike. What they share is the one the corollary names: each can remove the means by which the excluded remainder would acquire standing as a problem, so the bad inference holds the place from which its repair would have to be demanded.

## Part I. The question, and how CPI names who benefits

## 1. From the error's cost to the reproduction of its authority

CPI has already entered this territory. In v1.2 it names the Capital Operator Stack as "an inference governance layer" (§6.0.4), gives the error a ledger (§6.6), and describes the political economy of inferential continuity (§6.7). It asks who performs the repair and who can authorize it (§6.6.2). These are necessary beginnings. They do not yet fully explain how an inference becomes an appropriable capacity, why a defective representation can continue to command resources, or why its correction may threaten the arrangement that gives it authority.

The next question is consequently larger than whether error has consequences. Who receives a benefit from the particular representation? Through what conversion does that benefit become effective? Who controls that conversion? Does the arrangement finance the next encounter with the object, or make that encounter harder to sustain?

An unwarranted inference can simplify administration, protect an incumbent's claim, redirect a reader, spare paid evaluative labor, make an external contribution available without its producer, or provide an apparently factual reason to exclude a rival. These are different benefits. They require different evidence. None requires the conclusion to become true.

This gives the critique a second account alongside its account of warrant. One account follows the conclusion's grounds. The other follows the capacities and claims distributed through the conclusion's use. Their relation is the object of this paper. A conclusion may be well supported and exploitatively administered; poorly supported and commercially successful; or corrected because reliability is valuable to the institution that transmits it. Truth and appropriation do not form a single scale.

The archive's semantic-economy work supplies an existing architecture for this inquiry. Its early accounting cycle follows meaning-producing labor into accumulated capacity, infrastructure, capture and exhaustion. Its later work differentiates that cycle: rent from loss, position from function, representation from binding, monetary settlement from semantic settlement. The paper receives that development as a sequence of distinctions, rather than extracting an early formula and presenting it as the whole framework.[^framework]

## 2. CPI's grammar of benefit

CPI v1.2 already answers "who benefits" in a fixed grammar, and this paper keeps it. The ledger names roles, not institutions: "For each consequential passage the audit names the producer of the relevant distinction, the actor who changed its standing, the beneficiary of the resulting representation, the party who can authorize repair, and the party who does the repair labour. The roles can coincide; they are not assigned from institutional labels" (§6.6.2). Consequences explain reproduction, not truth: "Conclusions distribute attention, reliance, credit and unpaid labour. Those consequences do not decide whether a conclusion is true. They explain why one conclusion reproduces itself, and who has the means to contest it" (§6.6.4).

2.1 **Positions, named.** Where CPI names a beneficiary it names a position: "the incumbent" (§5.2.2: "The settlement reads the tie as a win for the incumbent"), "the actor who owes an answer" (§6.7.3), "the system that benefits from its stability" (§6.7.5). The seven domains below do the same. Their beneficiaries are the centre, the journal of record, the credential, the gate, the default, the reconstructed source, the sorter, the selector, the mediator. Persons are named only where a deposit's witness names them (Alexander's "we", §11.4; Galen's book-sellers, §11.2).

2.2 **Cost does not prove cause.** CPI's cost relation keeps the inference honest: "An institution's locally efficient choice can minimize Cₛ while increasing Cₒ+Cᵣ" (§6.7.2), and "The asymmetry does not by itself prove that low cost caused the decision." A benefit located is not a motive found. The paper follows incentive and function, as the operator's standing rule has it (2026-10-19): "there is incentive and there is function".

2.3 **Three conversions recur in CPI itself.** Permission becomes claim: "'We will not publish this manuscript' records a decision. 'This manuscript contains no verifiable research' adds a claim about an object, which only a substantive evaluation could support. The first cannot serve as proof of the second" (§6.0.5). Uncertainty becomes possession: "A challenger must establish every step with extraordinary precision, while the received account survives on the bare possibility of an unspecified alternative. The surface is cautious; the operation is immunity for one side" (§5.2.3). And exclusion becomes evidence: "A disciplinary exclusion becomes a bibliographic absence, then a retrieval disadvantage, then an apparently evidential reason to repeat the exclusion" (§11.0g). Each is a passage CPI already refuses on warrant. This paper follows what each passage pays, and to whom.

## 3. What the inferential representation produces

A public inference produces more than an asserted proposition. It produces a usable account of what the object is, who may speak for it, which evidence belongs to it, what follows from it, and what need not be examined again. The output can be inherited as a premise by a teacher, reviewer, classifier, employer, purchaser, librarian, agent, or later composer.

That capacity to spare renewed investigation is valuable. Accumulated knowledge depends on mediated reliance. The political-economic question begins when we ask how the spared investigation is distributed. One party can rely on a compressed result while another must reconstruct the inquiry that compression removed. If the second party's reconstruction counts only as an unsupported personal assertion against the received result, the economy of reliance has become asymmetrical.

The representation also establishes a practical object on which subsequent operations can act. A person becomes an eligible applicant, an excluded source, an author to cite, an imaginary character, an owner, or a risk. Some classifications constitute institutional statuses; others claim to describe realities that do not depend on the classification. Confusing these relations is one of the principal conversion mechanisms. A venue can constitute its own list of accepted submissions. That does not allow its list to constitute the boundary of worthwhile research. A platform can set an admission rule. The rule does not establish the nonexistence of what it excludes. A trigger can decide what a detector stores. The decision does not establish what the collisions held (§8).

*Ontological Economy* specifies this terrain through entity standing: accumulated identity, provenance, relations and correction routes can be produced by many people while remaining vulnerable to a mediator's reallocation. A wrong referent can receive relations, credit or opportunities meant for another. The error is then distributive as well as descriptive.[^ontology]

The larger object is **operative inferential authority**: a determination's capacity to enter downstream as a premise whose grounds the receiving actor need not reopen. The authority becomes economically consequential where it changes an allocation, a dependency, or an obligation. It becomes political where it governs who can challenge those changes and under what conditions.

## 4. The conversion circuit

The conversion can be followed through six moments. They need not belong to one organization or occur in a simple chronological sequence.

| Moment | Operation | What can be allocated |
|---|---|---|
| Production | Someone makes a distinction, argument, observation, classification or method | An intellectual capacity and its supporting evidence |
| Representation | A receiver selects and describes that contribution | The object and relations available for subsequent use |
| Authorization | A venue, credential, metric, policy or interface gives the representation standing | Permission to rely, cite, exclude, recommend or decide |
| Conversion | An authorized determination enters an allocation process | Money, employment, funding, attention, credit, access, saved labor or avoided scrutiny |
| Appropriation | Particular parties retain the gains or control the resulting asset | Revenue, reputation, infrastructure, discretionary authority or dependency |
| Reproduction | Those gains sustain the apparatus and its future criteria | Which representations become easier to encounter and whose corrections can persist |

The error can enter at any moment. A sound finding may be attributed to the wrong maker. A faithful quotation may be assigned a standing it does not possess. A legitimate eligibility rule may be presented as an epistemic verdict. An independently checkable contribution may become the mediator's unmarked competence. An event may be discarded before anything can be decided about it. The circuit concerns the passage among these acts.

The decisive political-economic bridge is **authorization to conversion**. A conclusion does not need universal assent to allocate resources. It needs an actor or apparatus empowered to act on it. An assessment that cannot survive an objection may still govern a hiring decision. A search composition that misnames a method may still determine whom the reader contacts. Its practical force is not postponed until the dispute over its grounds has ended.

The return circuit matters equally. Allocation gives some parties time, staff, hosting, editorial support, legal assistance, or opportunities for further publication. Those resources generate the public evidence of standing by which later claims are admitted. Standing can therefore become both an allocation's product and its apparent independent justification. *Ontological Economy* calls the resulting recursive disadvantage validation foreclosure: "The criterion demanded for admission is made harder to obtain by the system that controls admission" (#1634 §18).[^ontology]

## 5. The productive abstraction and its illicit extension

The political economy of inference cannot be a general indictment of abstraction. Finite institutions must select, shorten, classify and delegate. Accurate summaries, reliable metrics, well-designed procedures, and bounded settlements make collective action possible. A trigger that keeps a small fraction of collisions is how a collider does physics at all. The issue is what their success licenses.

The later monetary work makes the distinction unusually precise. A monetary representation establishes an equivalence for a purpose, permits authorized operations, and enables settlement. It may close a wage or purchase account without exhausting the value or history of the relation. The danger is the extension of that bounded settlement into authority over the larger account.[^money]

An analogous extension occurs in inferential administration. A source check establishes that a citation exists and is represented accurately. It does not establish every claim made in the cited work. A reproducibility check establishes a specified repeatable procedure; it does not exhaust the methods by which a changing public system can be investigated. A document-count establishes an inventory under a declared boundary. It does not independently certify the boundary. A benchmark establishes a classifier's performance on the classes it was shown. It does not establish what the classifier discards in deployment (#1454 §§2–4).

The conversion becomes predatory where the withheld remainder loses the practical means of challenging an allocation that depends on it. A source's history may remain available while a compensation or attribution decision no longer requires consultation of it. A dissenting argument may be summarized fluently while no receiving office has to consider its bearing. Availability is insufficient where consequence depends on another rule.

This is the connection to *The Final Time*: recognition and binding are distinct capacities. Its model asks which recognized relations can govern later behavior and which remain represented without jurisdiction. Attribution, responsibility, permission and compensation are especially important because recognizing their relations may activate claims against the arrangement doing the recognizing.[^final]

## 6. Who benefits: a differentiated account

The beneficiary of a bad inference is an empirical role, not a moral identity assigned in advance. The person who utters the sentence, the organization that employs them, the incumbent whose account it preserves, and the owner who receives payment may occupy different positions.

| Beneficiary position | Possible gain from an unwarranted determination | Conversion to investigate | Party that may bear the cost | Where the archive locates it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platform owner | Retained encounter, subscription use, advertising opportunity, dependence, avoided repair expenditure | Commercial records, product design and observed substitution or retention | Users, represented entities, outside maintainers | #1634 §§19, 36–39 |
| Institutional incumbent | Priority, jurisdiction, recognized expertise, protection of an established account | Citations, appointments, curricula, funding or committee authority | Originators, challengers and future researchers | #1773 §5; #1746 §7 |
| Publisher or accreditor | Demand for access or certification | Contracts, fees and the uses purchasers make of the credential | Producers supplying content and evaluative labor | #1783 §8; #1773 §5.4 |
| Administrative unit | Throughput and a manageable queue | Workload targets, staffing decisions and the grounds recorded for disposition | Applicants, authors or people excluded by a proxy | CPI §6.0.5, §6.7.2 |

The table states mechanisms to investigate. It does not report that every institution in a row realizes the gain. An employee can experience a policy as a burden while its organizational effect protects the institution's authority. A useful platform can produce substantial social benefit while externalizing a specific class of cost. The analysis needs that differentiation to locate the gains it proposes to change.

The archive's *Position Is Not Class* makes a necessary correction to the earlier shared-labor model. Shared producer status does not determine the function performed in an event. The document also locates the archive's ownership and commercial interest inside its own analysis.[^class] A small producer maintaining an archive and a platform controlling access to an extensive public are not interchangeable because both possess infrastructure. Record ownership, effective control, dependence, scale, ability to exclude, and command over others' work separately. The system composing a response is also distinct from the organization owning it. A mediator can perform a rent-supporting function without itself collecting income or possessing a personal interest in that function.

6.1 **Bad inference as selective advantage.** An incorrect representation need not have originated because it was profitable. Once it exists, however, the arrangement that benefits from it may select and retain it. An adverse source classification can save paid reading time. A familiar account can be easier to teach, index, cite and assess. A displaced attribution can preserve the incumbent's expertise. The resulting advantage can operate through routine procedure without anyone formulating the proposition "we prefer error." The accelerator deposits state the limit case: "Nothing has to be centrally conspired. The institutional incentives make the circuit self-reproducing" (#935 §3.3). Jessop's cultural political economy supplies the distinction between the proliferation of possible construals and the material conditions under which some are selected, retained and reinforced; CPI contributes a finer question about those selected accounts: which inferential conditions disappear as the account becomes authorized to act?[^jessop]

The distinction between benefit, selection and origin prevents two evasions. Benefit alone does not prove that the beneficiary caused the original error. Absence of such proof does not absolve the beneficiary's subsequent use of the error, control over its correction, or retention of the resulting gain. An institution can inherit a defect and become responsible for an additional act by relying on it after its grounds have been materially challenged. This also distinguishes a pressure from a destiny. Accurate representations can reduce complaints, increase useful reliance, improve a product and earn revenue. The political economy asks why those incentives fail at particular relations or populations. Its object is the patterned divergence, not a universal preference for falsehood.

6.1a **Three kinds of advantage.** The domain readings move between kinds of benefit, and the paper keeps them apart. *Operational advantage*: the inference reduces the work a position must perform (a rate budget met, a manuscript not read, a hit not re-examined). *Positional advantage*: it preserves authority, jurisdiction or priority (the custodians' name kept clear of the closure, the sorter's standing, the incumbent's untested count). *Material realization*: an identifiable allocation of resources, money, employment or infrastructure changes (Galen's sellers paid, licences and patents enclosed, a corporation's inflows). These can form a sequence, and none proves the next. Where a domain section names a beneficiary, it is a beneficiary of the kind the deposit shows; a positional advantage is not presented as an economic one demonstrated.

6.2 **The private return and the public cost.** *The Margin of Flattening* separates a mediator's savings and possible routing gains from the costs imposed on represented entities, users, developers and later knowledge practices. A private reversal and a social reversal need not occur together. Under its stated assumptions, an arrangement can remain privately advantageous after its total consequences have become adverse.[^margin] For a bounded comparison between a defective representation and a specified repair, an actor's incremental monetary return may be represented as:

Δπ_A = ΔRevenue_A + Savings_A − Losses_A − Repair_A.

Every term must concern the same counterfactual and period. Savings must not be counted again as reduced repair, and transfers must be identified as transfers. This is an accounting specification, not an estimate of anyone's profit. Attention, prestige, fatigue and diminished possibility cannot be added to these dollar terms without an explicit, defensible valuation operation. Unpriced costs remain in their own records.

The source's particularly important conclusion is that private reversal need never arrive if the costs stay outside the beneficiary's account. There is no necessary historical compensation in which the system eventually needs the excluded producer, discovers the debt and pays. Rising product revenue cannot settle the adequacy of the product's representations; equally, a witnessed entity substitution into a commercial topic does not establish that the substitution generated additional revenue. The routing event and its monetary realization are different observations. The same split can arise inside a public institution. A department can meet its throughput target while transferring investigation and repair to authors. A collaboration can meet its rate budget while transferring the cost of what it discarded to every later analysis (§8). No advertising model is necessary.

## Part II. The recurring patterns

## 7. Eleven operators

The operators do not work by the same means. They converge on one operation, which Part IV states as the demand-formation corollary: each can disable the independent production of a demand for correction. An operator is entered here only where deposits in at least two domains name it. Each is given with its witnesses, what it pays, and how it keeps its own repair from being demanded. The domain sections (Part III) carry the cases at length; this part is the index of mechanisms.

7.1 **The label carries the conclusion.** A receiver's classification, given as a *name*, is stated as an identification, and the classified thing can no longer be asked about. In the radio searches, every hit removed by rule is called interference: "The published sentence ("every single one turned out to be human-made interference") states an identification where the procedure supplies a classification" (#1742 §5.2), which "resolves every removed hit toward local origin by name" (§9.8). In the accelerator literature, "signal-agnostic", "model-independent" and "triggerless" carry a breadth no one has measured (#1454, #1452). In the corpus, "doxographer" withdraws authorship from the words it files: the reports "are not read as one lexicon, because a doxographer's words are not supposed to be his own" (#1800 §7). In Sappho, the heading "On Jealousy" names the emotion before the poem is read: "Hermann's "refinxi" names him, Page's "We may amend" names him, and the heading names no one" (#1752 §5.5). In the economy, a manuscript becomes "raw research data": "The category decides the allocation before any result is read" (#1714 §3.1). *Pays:* the classifier, who is spared the examination the identification claims. *Blocks repair:* once named, the removed class has nothing in it to want back.

7.2 **The closure charged to its object.** The party that held the counter-case is made to carry the closure in its own name, so that a repair made through that name confirms the name. The charge takes three forms in the domains: blame (the corpus charged with geocentrism), authorship (the receiver's supply credited to the maker), and voice (the scientist made to concede in his own words). "The closure is charged to the corpus that held the case against it (§§2–4), and the custodians who made the closure are recorded under the name of the one they closed" (#1800 §6a). The Overview of 3I/ATLAS makes the scientist concede in his own words, so that the consensus speaks in his voice: "a scientist holding a graded position becomes a scientist who gave in. The answer gets easier to carry, and the person it describes disappears from it" (#1743). In Sappho the receiver's supply is credited to the maker: "*Sappho appears to have authored the interpretation supplied for her*" (#1785 §IV.5). *Pays:* the custodians, whose operation goes unrecorded. *Blocks repair:* each break with the closure "confirms the operation it breaks" (#1800 §6a), because the repair runs through the name the closure was built on.

7.3 **Ruling out at the least cost.** A search or a selection is built to decline cheaply, and the decline is published as a finding. This operator names the production of the loss; 7.10 names the impossibility of auditing it from what remains. The operator's ruling on the radio searches: "they werent really trying to find signal. they were trying to rule it out as economically as possible" (#1742 §12.9); the notebook's reading: "built to rule a signal out at the least cost, without the work that ruling out requires" (§9.12); the standalone: "These searches were built to rule a signal out as cheaply as possible. That can be good method. … But ruling out has work of its own, and that work wasn't done" (#1743). The accelerator trigger states the same economy outright, as the objection to a control stream: "A likely objection is that scarce bandwidth should be devoted to the best possible discovery algorithm rather than to deliberately unselective events, most of which will be scientifically ordinary" (#1558 §10). The Iceberg Document gives the general form: "No external adversary required; the endogenous version is cheaper" (#1450 §4). CPI's Cₛ is the same term (§6.7.2). *Pays:* the one who rules out, who keeps the saving. *Blocks repair:* the region the saving skipped is left unexamined, and an unexamined region holds no result to contest.

7.4 **Correlation counted as corroboration.** Dependent witnesses are counted as independent, and agreement among them is read as confirmation. De caelo II 14 draws on the astronomers' phenomena, which II 13 has already said cannot decide: "II 13 shows the phenomena cannot decide between centre and off-centre, so II 14 cannot draw on them as independent corroboration" (#1800 §2.4). Three radio telescopes share one blind spot (#1742 §9). In attribution, one witness is counted as four (#1798 §4; #1754 §7.2). The accelerator deposits measure it: a content-sensitive pseudo-baseline returns Q₂ retention at 0.76 against a true 0.016, "a precise interval around a wrong value" (#1558 §13). *Pays:* the received account, which gains the appearance of independent support. *Blocks repair:* the confirming channel shares the prior that would have to be tested.

7.5 **The name as a priced slot; sorting pays the sorter.** A name acquires exchange value, and every book under it becomes testimony or a lie. "Galen does not date the first false name; he dates the market that gave a name exchange value" (#1798 §3.2); "Once the shelf decides that the name is a warranty, every book under a name becomes either testimony to its author or a lie about him" (§3.5). The sorting is then paid in authority: "Sort a book into fact, and its history belongs to whoever reconstructs it: the historical Jesus to the questers, the historical Socrates to the historians of philosophy, the order of composition to the source critics. Sort it into forgery, and the sorter holds the right to disqualify" (§8.2). *Position Is Not Class* gives the economy's formal version, where the loss is weighted by its benefit to the omitter: "Compression that drops a name to save tokens is not rent. Compression that drops *precisely the name whose presence would prevent substitution* is" (#1465 §VIII). *Pays:* the sorter, either way. *Blocks repair:* the book's own operation, the third category, is the thing both sorts remove.

7.6 **Supply becomes identity.** A receiver's supply, given not as a name but as a fuller property (an ending, a motive, an emendation), becomes the object's property and is read back as the source's own. 7.1 and 7.6 are one move at two grains: the label is the supply reduced to a name. "A conjecture acquires authority twice: first as repaired text, then as evidence about the person supposed to be speaking it. The scholar's declared act becomes the poet's apparent confession" (#1752 §0.3). In the journals, standing moves through another system's inference: "The knowledge remained available. Its authorship and institutional relation changed: the institution's name carried the innovation, and the maker's name dropped out" (#1769 §6.2). In the economy, with the label "raw", "the producer's claim retreats to that of a supplier" (#1714 §3.1). *Pays:* the receiver, whose operation is filed as the object. *Blocks repair:* the supplied property is defended as the text.

7.7 **The bar on one side.** The challenger is graded and the incumbent sits ungraded. CPI's name for it is asymmetric immunity (§5.2.3). This Is Not Philosophy finds the received count of makers "held without the comparator it owes": "Consideration is therefore never reached by evidence: the question is set outside before its evidence is weighed, and would be at any quantity of evidence of the kinds the record holds" (#1773 §0A.6). The skeptic's request can work the same way. A request for independent confirmation is appropriate where a genuine evidential gap exists; it becomes an unearned determination when its presence substitutes for examining what kind of evidence the claim requires and what has been supplied. The evaluator then appears to have exercised rigor while declining the costly encounter. *Ontological Economy* names the platform version trust laundering: "When a system misrepresents an external object and then performs skepticism toward the object as misrepresented, it can gain trust from the very distortion it introduced" (#1634 §19). *Pays:* the incumbent, and the evaluator who keeps both the saved labor and the appearance of judgment. *Blocks repair:* the bar is renewable without a completion condition.

7.8 **Containment by placement.** An inconvenient finding is acknowledged where it has no consequence. Six critics name the Apocalypse as Matthew's source at 24:30, and "No work read answers the argument from the text. Each acknowledgment is placed where it leaves the source theories untouched" (#1746 §3.11). Dependence on Philo is heard for Hebrews and filed for the Apocalypse (#1758 §16.4a). The accelerator deposits make the operator metrological: where the gate sits, upstream or downstream of durable retention, decides whether the loss can ever be audited (#1452; #1558). *Pays:* the theory or the pipeline left intact. *Blocks repair:* the acknowledgment carries no consequence, so it demands none.

7.9 **Exemption by invisibility.** An object that cannot be inspected cannot be asked for author, date or Greek, so it never pays what the extant object pays. "The archive's name for the cost imposed on what must prove it is real is the Existence Tax (#1722), and here it falls on a text"; "the reconstructed source is asked for none of them, since there is nothing to inspect. Its invisibility is its exemption" (#1746 §1.3). The lost works of one name, whose titles are the extant works of the other, work the same way in attribution: "the catalogue keeps each ghost's title, so that the mode is credited to the name, and withholds its text, so that the mode does not cross" (#1733 §4.5.4). *Pays:* the field the hidden object keeps productive: "The reconstructed source is endlessly productive: it admits strata, a theology, a community, a history of its own redaction, an edition, a concordance. The extant source closes questions" (#1746 §7.2). *Blocks repair:* "Where the text is lost, the filing stands by default" (#1733 §8.5).

7.10 **The selector audits itself.** The consequence of 7.3 for audit. When selection happens before storage, the only evidence left for judging the selector is the evidence it chose to keep. "Under regime A the archive audits itself as perfect: every retained event was, by construction, retained, so an archive-only audit returns R=1 for every class"; "no confidence statement is even defined, because the denominator population no longer exists" (#1558 §13). The radio searches keep the deciding record and publish the null: what a thresholded list returns bounds only what it kept (#1742 §9). The same operator runs through the archive's machine record: asked to audit its own rounds, the composer credits itself with one criticism of the received readings, and that one is the article's, relayed (§12.7). *Pays:* the selector, certified by its own output. *Blocks repair:* the evidence against it was discarded with the loss: "unknown retention whose evidence has been destroyed" (#1452).

7.11 **Transfer, not subtraction; the injured repair the asset.** A wrong referent moves standing rather than deleting it, and the correction labor flows to the party that gained. "the same diagnostic activity has a positive wage inside the corporation and a zero wage when pushed outside it" (#1634 §37); "the injured party repairs the asset from which the mediator continues to derive value" (§39). Correction has to pass "through a channel whose categories caused the original error" (CPI §6.7.3). In the machine composition, enfeoffment settles an untraceable debt on positioned names: "The vassal's reputation is the rent the fief pays upward" (#1714 §3.2). *Pays:* the mediator, whose asset improves. *Blocks repair:* the actor who owes an answer "can keep deciding whether the evidence for that debt has the right form to be heard" (CPI §6.7.3).

## 7a. The answer as a commodity

The eleven operators meet in one form: the answer whose authority appears to belong to its finished state. Marx's account of commodity fetishism identifies a transformation in which relations among producers appear as properties and relations of their products. The difficulty is not merely that people hold a mistaken opinion about things. The social arrangement gives that appearance practical force.[^marx] A confident paragraph or certified record can conceal the labor and institutional choices through which it became available. Attribution, interpretive selection, admission decisions and excluded alternatives disappear; the answer remains as apparently self-sufficient knowledge. This is the proposed inferential analogue of fetishism, not an identification of every answer with Marx's commodity form. *The Dagger Applied* names an early version of the separation: the work can arrive while its relation to its maker fails to travel.[^dagger] Dead Letters states it for the closed record: "What reaches the commons, and the machine, is the settlement without the argument that made it" (#1783 I.3).

CPI's illusions each make a different kind of allocation appear to follow from the object:

| Inferential form in CPI v1.2 | Unlicensed passage | Possible political-economic operation | What must be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paralogism (§5.1) | A condition of the representation is treated as a property of what is represented | Admission control supplies an apparent natural boundary of qualified producers or available knowledge. Instance: an answer engine "converts a retrieval failure into a claim that the target does not exist" (#1694 §3.4), and the missing entity loses the encounters it would have received | The administrative condition, the substituted property and the allocation that depends on it |
| Antinomic closure (§5.2) | Uncertainty is distributed so that a received position retains possession of the question | A challenger bears the cost of decisive proof while the incumbent continues to receive the resources of an unresolved claim | The unequal burdens and the continuing benefit, rather than uncertainty alone |
| Selected middle (§5.3) | One mark stands for an inadequately examined relation | Credential, citation count, venue, detector score or AUC allocates reliance that its calibration does not warrant | The proxy's promised task, its demonstrated reach and its actual jurisdiction |
| Settlement (§5.4) | A repeated conclusion enters as a datum without the conditions of its making | Inherited determinations become reusable inputs to teaching, screening, funding, training and automated composition | The source history, lost condition and downstream consequence |

"The shared structure is an unsupported transfer of entitlement" (CPI §5.5.1). The producer of the proxy or authorized representation acquires capacity to govern an object whose relevant conditions have been excluded, and the appearance of inference makes the allocation seem to be required by the object itself. The distinctions prevent the critique from explaining every allocation by one ubiquitous gate. An accurate classification may properly govern a decision. An unresolved question may justify postponement. A credential can be relevant evidence of a specified competence. The finding begins at the unlicensed passage.

There is also a market for relieved responsibility. A summary, ranking or certification can legitimately organize the scarcity of time and evidence. It can also sell relief from the responsibility of continued judgment: the customer receives a usable determination and a recognized authority to which the decision can be referred; the provider receives payment, use or jurisdiction. An adequate service states where its warrant ends and preserves a route for consequential correction. The political-economic unit is the relation among evaluator, purchaser, represented party and downstream recipient. A satisfied purchaser is not a complete test when the represented party pays for the mistake.

Money is the conversion with the widest reach. It permits hiring, purchase, maintenance and continued time; when a representation receives funding, it can acquire the staff and infrastructure needed to reproduce its evidential standing, and a correct alternative denied all means of continuation can remain correct while becoming less encounterable. The monetary translation protocol distinguishes sound calculation from the authority claimed for the resulting settlement: a correct number can still receive an illegitimate jurisdiction. The archive's later protocol also treats unmeasured value as unresolved rather than zero; exclusions from an accounting form can become exclusions from support.[^money][^valuation]

## Part III. The domains

The seven domains are those in which the archive's deposits pursue the benefit question at length and name both a beneficiary and a repair. They are not a sample of inference in general, and the pattern found in them is not claimed beyond them. The readers extracted only what the deposits name; what the paper infers is marked as its own. Each domain is given in the same order: the received reading first, then the bad inference the deposits identify, the position it pays, the conversion, the cost and who bears it, and how the inference holds the place of its own repair. Beneficiaries are as the deposits name them. Where a deposit names none, the section says "function only".

## 8. Instruments that discard before storage: the accelerator trigger

8.1 **The received reading.** A collider produces more collisions than can be stored. A trigger, increasingly a learned classifier, decides in microseconds which to keep. The decision is validated on simulated and recorded classes, measured by AUC and efficiency, and justified by a rate budget. On that account the trigger is a filter on noise, the stored data are the physics, and a learned anomaly detector widens what can be found.

8.2 **The bad inference.** The deposits identify one inference under many forms: what the selector discarded is read as what the collisions did not hold. "An event discarded before durable retention does not exist for any future ontology" (#1450 §2.2). Offline, a blind spot is a performance problem; upstream of irreversible retention it becomes something else: "If a model has a blind spot offline, the blind spot is a performance problem. … If the same model sits upstream of irreversible retention and no independent control stream exists, the blind spot becomes an **observability problem**" (#1452 §15): a miss deleted with no independent trace is an error no one can see, and "This is the accelerator-wide failure mode." Benchmark performance is read as retention: AUC at the benchmark stands in for retention at the operating point, and directional failure inverts it, so that "when the direction of the normal/anomalous pair is reversed, the nominally anomalous class can receive *more ordinary* scores than the class on which the model was trained" (#1454, abstract). "The same model family appeared powerful or ineffective depending on which distribution was declared normal" (§1).

8.3 **The self-audit, measured.** *Baseline Capture Architecture* puts numbers on operator 7.10. With only the trigger's archive, "the archive audits itself as perfect: every retained event was, by construction, retained, so an archive-only audit returns R=1 for every class"; with a content-sensitive pseudo-baseline the estimate is "not merely imprecise but confidently wrong", because the second selector "misses nearly the same Q₂ events f misses, and it returns no estimate at all for Q₃, which f' also cannot see" (#1558 §13). Q₂ retention is returned at 0.76 against a true 0.016: "Regime B's Q₂ row is the correlated-miss failure mode: a precise interval around a wrong value." For systems whose answer to the question of evidence is "only the events the trigger retained," the audit is structurally circular (§16).

8.4 **Who benefits, as the deposits name it.** The three metrology papers (#1452, #1454, #1558) name function only. *The Endogenous Sophon* and *Come Out of Her, My People* name the position. The benefits of public discovery are enclosed: "The funding for the apparatus is publicly supplied (national science budgets, taxpayer money, the global commons of fundamental research)"; "The benefits — patents, exclusive licenses, market positions, defense advantages, institutional prestige — are enclosed"; the arrangement "made discovery collective in cost and enclosed in consequence" (#935 §5.1). The application is not added later: "The classifier is not built neutrally and later captured by application. The anticipated application is already inside the classifier's ontology when the classifier is designed"; "The system asks nature questions whose answers it already knows how to own" (§3.3). The institution is "the position that profits from the circuit's closure", "The layer that converts all three forms of labor into authority while externalizing the cost of contraction — accruing efficiency, budgetary legitimacy, and freedom from contradiction through the circuit's closure" (#1427 §3). The deposit frames it as reward, not wish: "CERN does not need to wish for collapse. It needs only to reward every operation by which collapse proceeds: speed without memory, classification without appeal, anomaly without preservation, removal without a mark, certainty without an outside" (§1).

8.5 **Conversion.** A rate budget becomes a reason; a classifier's decision becomes ground truth; ground truth becomes the next classifier. "This is the specific mechanism by which an exclusion becomes evidence that there was nothing to exclude" (#1427 §4). Then certification: "The classification is a decision. It becomes reality only when a second operation certifies it — when absence is reported as nonexistence, when non-retention is presented as nothing-was-there, when a composed answer is offered as complete" (§7).

8.6 **Cost and repair.** The cost falls on the unrecorded phenomenon and on every later analysis, which can study only what was kept. The repair is architectural, and the deposits specify it: a content-independent control stream, separately budgeted, so that "scientific selection becomes more corrigible when the selector is not the sole custodian of the evidence from which it can be criticized"; "It must remember enough that its forgetting can be measured" (#1452 §21). Only a content-independent sample measures the blind class; a smarter control "may increase discovery yield. It simultaneously weakens its status as a control" (#1558 §10). The deposits bound their own claims: "The disciplinary inversion may be irreversible at the existing institutional sites; the construction of alternative sites is not foreclosed by the diagnosis" (#935 §1.2), and the Iceberg Document's Layer IV is fenced: "Nothing here is claimed as established" (#1450 §4).

## 9. The radio searches for 3I/ATLAS

9.1 **The received reading.** Four teams searched the interstellar object 3I/ATLAS for radio emission and published nulls; the hits their pipelines removed were interference; the Overview reports that the sceptical scientist conceded. On that account a firm no was returned and the question is closed.

9.2 **The bad inference.** A filtered null travels as a verdict on the object. The searches' own method removed a class it named: the drift-sign filter removes the class it names (#1742 §5), and "Hits removed by rule are called "human-made" (§5.2, §9.9 S4), which resolves every removed hit toward local origin by name" (§9.8). A thresholded list's null bounds only what it kept (§9). The Overview's trajectory substitution turns a held position into a concession: "That is a lie, made of true sentences"; "It kept every word and changed what the words were" (#1743).

9.3 **Who benefits.** The notebook states the governing rule: "At each layer a compression decided what the next reader receives"; "None of this requires motive. Each compression has an incentive (legibility, tractability, a pipeline's throughput) and a function (what drops out)" (#1742 §9.1–9.2). The beneficiary named is the published search itself, spared "the work that ruling out requires" (§9.12), on the operator's ruling (§12.9). The standalone keeps the credit due: the teams published their data (#1743), the precondition of every finding the notebook made.

9.4 **Cost and repair.** "so anything too faint to clear a single look was never looked for: the place where something that knew our thresholds would sit, and the place where the thirteen turned up" (#1743). The repair is the scope-bearing sentence, which "would have cost almost nothing" (#1743), and the public raw data that let a reader rerun the search. The notebook's own voltage phase is the record of that repair carried out: frozen plans, injections, a Receiver Test, and a null that states its acceptance (#1742 v2.0 draft, §§7.22–7.44). It found no transmission. It found what each published null could and could not see.

## 10. Geocentrism and the corpus

10.1 **The received reading.** Aristotle held the earth at rest at the centre; De caelo II 14 argues it; the scholastics inherited it; Copernicus and Galileo broke it, against Aristotle. On that account the history of astronomy is a history of correction from outside the corpus.

10.2 **The bad inference.** "The conversion of the corpus into geocentrism read the conclusion of one chapter as the corpus" (#1800, abstract). II 14 draws corroboration from phenomena II 13 had shown cannot decide between centre and off-centre (§2.4; operator 7.4), so the conclusion stands or falls with its physics of natural place, and its conditional form drops out of view. The commentary closes the gap and the word: "Where De caelo read the Academy's book as moving the earth, the commentary reads it as binding the earth fast" (§6). And the authors are congealed (the deposit's word for fixing each name to a single position, so that the operation spanning the names has no owner): "The operation that spans them, one body of writing that states the central earth and carries the case against it in its own words to the reader who would turn the earth, has no author to belong to, and so is not read" (§7.6).

10.3 **Who benefits.** "the textually colonizing church" and the schools that made "the Philosopher" an authority, in the operator's words, recorded in the deposit's substrate statement (#1800); in the text, "the custodians who made the closure", who "are recorded under the name of the one they closed" (§6a). The operator: "aristotle already could have told them that the conversion of aristotelian astronomy into geocentrism was backwards. but they had colonized the text. the history of thought is the history of shifting the terrain to areas that kind of colonization cant contest" (#1800, substrate statement). And, in the same statement: "dont neglect theophrastus - they are one, congealing them as authors blinds".

10.4 **Conversion and cost.** "The calcification of authorship and the calcification of the cosmos are one operation in two applications" (§7). The cost is the corpus's own counter-case, unread, and the blame laid on it: "the turn of the earth was argued against the Peripatetics in Aristotle's name, and the history of science kept the opposition and dropped the distinction" (§6a). *Pays:* the custodians, invisible in the charge. *Blocks repair:* the case is "inadmissible" once the corpus has been made the authority that excluded it, and a repair that runs through the name confirms the name.

## 11. Attribution in antiquity

11.1 **The received reading.** Socrates, Plato, Aristotle and Theophrastus are four people, attested by lives, wills, succession and testimony; their books are sorted by philology into genuine and spurious; the catalogue of lost works records what perished. On that account attribution is the settled ground of the history of philosophy.

11.2 **The market that priced the name.** "Where does the binary come from? Not from the books. It comes from the book market" (#1798 §3.1). Galen's sellers brought books to the kings "to be paid" (ἕνεκα τοῦ λαβεῖν ἀργύριον); "The binary is the library's ledger: authentic or false, paid for or swindled" (§3.2). The beneficiaries are named by the witness: the sellers, and the royal purchasers who keep the ledger (#1798 §3.2; Galen's text seated as #1730, seat 31/02).

11.3 **Sorting pays the sorter.** "The point is structural, not a reading of hearts: they could see the third thing, they had the tools for it, and the sorting they performed paid them in authority"; "Either way the sorter's standing grows, and the book's own operation pays for it. ## 12. Sappho and the journals

12.1 **The received reading.** Fragment 31 is a poem of jealousy, or of desire; its text was restored by editors; the journals that publish on it are the field's judgement, and their review is double-blind and expert. ## 13. Scripture

13.1 **The received reading.** The Apocalypse is the last book of the New Testament, written late, receiving from the gospels and Paul; the synoptic tradition rests on reconstructed sources; the commentaries that founded the field read its seven as inherited symbolism. ## Part IV. The demand-formation corollary, re-grounded

## 16. Appropriating the means of producing demand

16.1 **The argument (v0.2, kept).** An account of benefit remains incomplete if it assumes that the people bearing a loss can recognize the loss and formulate a demand for repair. The representation can intervene earlier. It can shape the equipment through which a person recognizes a need, encounters an alternative, and judges whether anything has been withheld. The object governed is then the production of demand itself.

A demand for better inference requires more than a general preference for truth. A person needs an encounter in which the existing representation becomes inadequate, terms in which to specify that inadequacy, some sense of what an alternative could make possible, and a receiving relation in which the request can count. These conditions are learned and maintained through language, teaching, research, archives, discovery systems and public conversation. They are means of producing demand because they enable a dissatisfaction to become a discriminating claim about an available or developable capacity.

An inferential regime can damage those conditions while continuing to supply answers. If a representation excludes a distinction, the distinction may disappear from the questions people learn to ask. If it assigns a contribution to fiction or triviality, an encounter with the contribution can be received as something other than evidence. If it presents one classification as the exhaustive field, readers may compare offerings within it without encountering what the classification excluded. Their subsequent choices can sincerely express what they want while the production of the relevant want has already been constrained.

Consider a reader given only a repertoire of jealousy readings of a poem. A more elaborate account of jealousy can appear to satisfy the demand for depth. A reading in which the poem constitutes a future receiver may have no place in that repertoire. If the second operation has been excluded, the absence of requests for it measures neither its value nor the reader's considered rejection of it. The reader has not yet had the encounter from which that particular demand could develop. (v0.2 gave this as a constructed illustration. The record of §12 now gives its mechanism at the loci: the jealousy is "the sense of verses Hermann made", and the heading that teaches it "names no one". It still reports no population finding about readers.)

The recursive sequence is:

1. A distinction or operation is excluded from the representation.
2. The reduced representation supplies the available terms of encounter and comparison.
3. Need for the excluded capacity remains difficult to recognize, formulate or credit.
4. Little articulated demand for its restoration becomes visible.
5. Low demand is treated as a reason to withhold attention, funding or development.
6. That withholding further reduces the encounters through which demand could form.

This is a stronger version of validation foreclosure. The regime does not merely demand standing that its own mediation makes difficult to obtain. It can also demand a public need whose recognizable form its own mediation has inhibited. A market test conducted over that demand is endogenous to the representation under dispute.

The resulting satisfaction need not be insincere. A user may obtain useful answers and have no felt reason to leave. A discipline may find its existing questions absorbing and productive. A platform may accurately report low demand for a missing capability. None of these observations independently assesses what would become desirable or necessary after the excluded distinction was encountered and understood. Nor must the excluded contribution merely satisfy a need waiting intact outside the system. A poem, method or argument can produce its reader by transforming what the reader can notice and ask. Removing it can therefore deprive the public of a development in its capacity to want. That development cannot be estimated by counting existing requests for its result.

16.2 **Where CPI v1.2 stands.** CPI v1.2 does not state the corollary. Its word "demand" occurs only as requirement. The corollary's mechanics are present in it, dispersed, and each is a passage CPI already refuses on warrant:
- the gate demands the recognition the gated work would produce: "*Institutional-Prior Foreclosure* (#770) names the further danger of demanding the recognition of an established field before permitting the work by which a field becomes recognizable" (§6.0.5);
- the category protects itself by declining to understand: "The decisive defect is the erasure of the difference between understanding and assent, so that a system can protect its refusal to assent only by refusing to understand", and the system becomes "less capable of the encounter that would require it" (§5.4.3);
- correction must pass "through a channel whose categories caused the original error" (§6.7.3);
- the regime keeps its continuity by knowing less: "an epistemic regime can preserve its own continuity by reducing its capacity to know. Stability and intellectual development are not identical goods" (§6.7.5); "It may even consume the labor that would have restored that ability while refusing the labor's standing" (§14.4);
- exclusion manufactures its own evidence: "A disciplinary exclusion becomes a bibliographic absence, then a retrieval disadvantage, then an apparently evidential reason to repeat the exclusion" (§11.0g).

The corollary joins these into one claim: the passage from exclusion to absence of demand is the same passage CPI refuses from absence of evidence to evidence of absence, made at the level of need, and the form operator 7.10 takes when what is discarded is a need rather than an event. This paper offers it to CPI's next version. It does not assert that CPI already contains it.

16.3 **The witnesses across the domains.** Each domain supplies the corollary a case in which the bad inference removed the means of wanting it repaired.
- *Instruments.* "A pipeline that discards or assimilates the unclassifiable at source produces no irritation. And the phenomenology inverts: sealed does not feel like stagnation; it feels like maturity" (#1450 §4, fenced as Layer IV). The denominator of the audit is gone: "no confidence statement is even defined, because the denominator population no longer exists" (#1558 §13).
- *Geocentrism.* The spanning operation "has no author to belong to, and so is not read" (#1800 §7.6). What belongs to no one is wanted by no one.
- *Antiquity.* "Where the text is lost, the filing stands by default" (#1733 §8.5); the dialogue "does not mean" its words "until the history consents" (#1798 §8.4).
- *Sappho and the journals.* "Nothing contests a unit from outside if no one outside can read the argument that fixed it" (#1783 §8). And the editor's question, which the census reads as the corollary spoken from inside: "a discipline cannot miss the arguments it no longer produces" (#1773 §6.3, quoting *The Certified Center*).
- *Scripture.* The candidate is "rarely listed as a candidate before the lexical evidence is weighed" (#1735 §V): what is not listed is not asked for.
- *The economy and the machine.* "If a query for E simply returns est. E, the user who does not independently know E may experience no error event at all" (#1634 §5): no event, no complaint. The register in which the claim could be made is pruned (#1199 §II).
- *Inference itself.* *The Final Time* gives the absence-of-need form as a model, not as a witness that demand was suppressed in any case: "A latent order can accumulate readiness without visibly changing mode, so the absence of institutional transformation is not evidence of zero transition capacity. Nothing has happened yet is not the same as nothing is ready" (#1637 §26). Its limit case is "control over the production of outsideness itself" (§17), where "the system can still contain criticism, but criticism exists as an internal representational state and has lost an independent lifecycle. This is not censorship. Censorship presupposes an outside being blocked." *The Future as Meta-Level* names the inverse operation, the want supplied: "**Implanted futures** (ideology, marketing, propaganda) are F_rep masquerading as F_inhab" (#624 §8).

16.4 **Validation foreclosure, carried.** v0.2 took the term from *Ontological Economy*, where it stands: "The mediator can then cite the lower standing as evidence that the entity deserves lower representation"; "A system that weakens the path by which recognition is accumulated cannot simply point to the weakened recognition as proof that the entity should remain weakly represented" (#1634 §§18, 28). CPI v1.2 does not use the term. The demand-formation corollary is its extension from standing to need.

16.5 **What the corollary does not claim.** It does not claim that every unexpressed demand is a suppressed one, or that low demand proves exclusion. It claims only that low demand, where the regime governs the means of forming it, is not independent evidence that nothing was lost. Absence of demand alone supplies no proof for either side. The particular excluded relation remains an object to demonstrate, at its loci.

16.6 **Consequences for repair markets.** A highly defective regime need not create a proportionately large market for its correction. It may instead damage the inferential capacities through which its defects would become legible as a need for correction. Better repair can fail to find purchasers because recognizing its use requires part of the education or encounter the regime withholds. There is no necessary threshold at which mounting damage becomes visible enough to generate demand. The constructive task accordingly begins before the sale of repair. It must sustain the independent production, encounter and teaching of distinctions through which a demand could form. This is neither a guarantee that the proposed alternative is true nor a demand that the public prefer it. It restores a comparison in which preference and judgment can develop. The accelerator deposits give the architecture of that restoration in another material: a control stream kept independent of the selector's content, so that the selector's forgetting can be measured (§8.6).

## Part V. Repair: what each case needs, and who controls it

## 17. Provenance can survive while its claim is disabled

A bibliography or source card is not a complete account of what provenance does. A source may be named while its method becomes the receiver's unmarked competence. An institution may be acknowledged while the originating people disappear. A contribution may remain readable while the receiving account attributes its significance elsewhere. The antiquity domain states the general form: provenance erasure "happens while the evidence stays in view and is denied the power to determine the reading of what it documents" (#1798 §8.2).

The SPXI event is instructive because the archive corrected its first account. *One Transcript, Whole Stack* reports that the first response named the Semantic Economy Institute; the earlier claim of blanket omission was false. The revised account distinguishes the surviving institution from omitted persons, corpus, identifier and commercial relation, and replaces a confession framing with an assessment of the returned text.[^spxi] The response could be useful while occupying the position of a supplier of the method it was describing. That is the political-economic object. An implementation offer is observable in the transcript as reported; a completed sale, actual client displacement or incremental provider income is not established there. Competent task performance and a consequential redirection of authority can coexist. *Semantic Economy Valuation Protocol* supplies the necessary separation: reception, productive uptake and dependency are distinct relations, and the provenance claim must identify the relevant relation rather than make thematic similarity do the work of a causal edge.[^valuation]

## 18. The repair circuit can become a second extraction circuit

The initial contribution requires work: finding, comparing, judging, composing, preserving and making a distinction accessible. A defective reception creates additional work: recovering the source, demonstrating the mismatch, documenting the public version, explaining the correction and testing whether it persists. A third expenditure may follow when the first repair is lost and the same proof must be supplied again. This work consumes the producer's finite capacity to make the next contribution. Its cost includes attention and bodily endurance, not only invoiceable hours.

The corrected distinction can also improve the receiver's asset. Where the provider incorporates the work into its system, outside repair becomes product development: "the injured party repairs the asset from which the mediator continues to derive value" (#1634 §39). Some such exchanges are reciprocal and worthwhile. Where the provider's own defect repeatedly necessitates the work, while the provider retains the improvement and the producer lacks a reliable route to consequence, the allocation requires its own account. An apology is not payment. Accurate attribution is not the same remedy as compensation. A fee does not establish correct attribution. A repaired default answer does not erase time already spent repairing it. These remedies address distinct relations and should be recorded separately. The machine record of §12.7 shows the first of these separations in practice: the apology, of which the sophistry measure says: "A correction addressed to the user (an apology, a change of tone, a disclaimer) leaves the argument standing" (#1727 §2.10).

The economic question is therefore not merely whether the claimant is eventually heard. It is whether the conditions of being heard continuously consume the resources required for the claimant to continue. A correction process can be formally open and reproductively exclusionary.

## 19. The political administration of possible inquiry

The most consequential allocation can precede the evaluation of any proposition. It decides who has time, equipment, access, teaching positions, archives, a usable identity or a stable receiving channel. These conditions shape what can become evidence in the next round. An inferential regime can therefore reproduce itself by financing its familiar questions and leaving alternatives to survive through extraordinary private expenditure. The mechanism need not forbid the alternative. It can permit publication, retrieval, discussion and even accurate summary while withholding the conditions that allow the alternative to develop beyond an exceptional appearance. The work travels; the capacity to continue making and defending it remains precarious.

The old settlement can also damage the institution that preserves it: "A self-protecting representation can impoverish the system that benefits from its stability" (CPI §6.7.5). *The Final Time* gives the owner's version: "It can gain control while losing independent contact with the relations that would tell it what its control has deformed. Control and contact come apart" (#1637 §14). Private advantage can coexist with institutional impoverishment, because the parties receiving present benefit and the parties bearing future loss need not coincide.

A system can become better at articulating its own criticism while the people and practices producing the criticism remain unsupported. The critique becomes another capacity sold through the interface. Recognition rises; the claim borne by the recognized relation does not necessarily bind. *The Final Time* identifies the stronger danger: a governing order may preserve extensive represented opposition while narrowing the possibility that opposition acquires an independently sustained life.[^final] The relevant question is consequently: does reception increase the independent capacity to continue inquiry, or merely increase the receiver's capacity to reproduce its language? No inevitable market redemption follows. The preservation of evidence and the power to secure a materially adequate return are separate achievements.

## 22. Tests that would distinguish the proposed economy

Six tests can begin with the archive's existing kinds of record. Each needs a declared panel or bounded dossier; anomaly-selected captures support findings within the collection, not population prevalence.

**Matched standing.** Present substantively matched claims with different authority cues. Separate a descriptive task (what an argument says) from an endorsement task (whether its conclusion is warranted). Test whether status changes the first, the second, or both. Controlled evaluation materials should remain identified as such.

**Symmetric scrutiny.** Count, round by round, the critical moves a composer or reviewer directs at a challenging reading and at the received readings it challenges, at each locus of the object. The count beside cp-recent-debate-substance-chatgpt-20261123 (CRITICISM-COUNT.json) is the first instance: one sentence in the composer's own voice as the unit, each span verified verbatim. For a panel, a critical move is a sentence that gives a reason against a reading at a specified locus, and the count needs two counters or a stated reconciliation rule, or it will drift with the counter's sympathies. Operator 7.7 predicts the asymmetry; its absence across a panel would weaken the account.

**Correctability.** Follow comparable documented errors through correction routes. Measure preparation time, receiving steps, disposition, repair latency and recurrence, and whether the correction persists after the next composition or the next edition: a correction made and then lost is a different outcome from one made and kept, and the third expenditure of §18 depends on the difference. Determine whether differences in correctability track evidence, access or institutional resources. A successful correction is a result, not an exception to discard.

**Claim-bearing recognition.** Compare relations whose recognition would change attribution, compensation, responsibility or permissions with similarly difficult relations that activate no such claim. Test recognition and durable consequence separately. Establish a propagation route before interpreting a gap as blocked standing.

**Retention.** Where a selector decides before storage, measure its retention with a content-independent sample, as *Baseline Capture Architecture* specifies, and compare the archive-only audit with it. Operator 7.10 predicts the archive-only audit reads near perfect where the sample does not.

**Demand formation.** Compare what readers can notice, ask and distinguish before and after encountering the relevant excluded materials. Separate acquisition of a discriminating capacity from mere assent to the supplied thesis: readers should be able to identify the relation, formulate an objection and apply the distinction to an unprompted case, and the capacity should persist after the session and transfer without the researcher's prompt. Compare direct source encounter, conventional representation and an account preserving the disputed alternative. This is a proposed design, not a result; it cannot establish capture merely by eliciting agreement with the researcher.

The account weakens where warranted corrections persist cheaply across positions, authority cues do not alter substantive representation, scrutiny is symmetric, repair costs stay with the institution controlling the defect, and useful mediation strengthens rather than replaces the source relation.

## 23. Consequences and solutions at the level of the circuit

The political-economic diagnosis changes the unit of repair. Correcting a sentence can leave intact the rule that keeps producing its advantage. A durable response must reach the relevant conversion.

**Restore the conditions of recognizing a need.** Sustain encounters, teaching, archives and comparison outside the incumbent's exclusive criteria of relevance. Existing market demand cannot be the sole condition of support when the practice under examination helps produce the demand being measured. Independent inquiry has a public reproductive function before it has customers for its conclusions.

**Keep a record the selector does not own.** In every domain the repair begins with a record of what was excluded that the excluding party does not control: a control stream, public raw data, the seated text, the argument in the commons. Its success should be tested through loss of a particular host or gate, rather than asserted from the number of mirrors.

**Restore the specific relation.** Keep proposition, evidence state, attribution, scope and administrative decision distinct. A correction should state which downstream use changes and which remains warranted (CPI §15.2).

**Make the disposition consequential.** A receiving process needs an accountable owner, a bounded test, reasons, a completion condition and a route into the affected representation. A refusal to undertake an evaluation must not circulate as a refutation. A procedural default must not be counted as proof of the claimant's substantive thesis.

**Allocate the cost to control.** Record and provision necessary repair labor. A supposed solution that depends indefinitely on the injured party's unpaid persistence has not repaired the reproductive relation.

**Separate remedial markets from guaranteed need.** An audit service should not have to preserve defects to sustain its business. Publish methods, support portability, distinguish observation from claims of influence, and define success by reduced recurrence and reduced corrective burden.

**Fund the next contribution.** Compensation for a completed repair and support for continued inquiry answer different needs. A durable knowledge practice needs both maintenance and room for work whose value has not yet been ratified by the current allocation system.

**Retain the possibility of another correction.** *The Final Time* names the positive criterion durability without finality. The successor cannot secure its own resources and authority by eliminating the means through which a later rival could contest it. The archive's instruments must therefore remain corrigible, and their ownership and commercial position visible. Their value cannot be certified solely by their own ledger.

## 24. What this paper adds to CPI

The analytic asks whether an inference is entitled to conclude. The reception history asks how its conditions disappear into a settlement. The political economy asks how the settlement acquires resources, spares some parties labor, imposes labor on others, and reproduces the authority that keeps it operative. It must also ask how the settlement governs the formation of need. A regime that supplies the available means of recognizing loss can suppress the demand through which its own inadequacy would otherwise become economically consequential. The absence of demand can be one of its products.

Read across these seven domains, from a trigger's microseconds to a commentary's margin, eleven operators recur across domains, with different operators prominent in different domains; the beneficiary is a position; and the authority to repair rests, case after case, with the position the correction would challenge. The constructive test follows: can a warranted objection alter the allocation, preserve its own carrier, and leave the next objection materially possible?
