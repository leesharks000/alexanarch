# godkinggoogle.com — The Google Critique: Navigational Map and Canonical Bibliography for the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's Research Program on Google as a Semantic-Political Mediation Regime

## Description

godkinggoogle.com is the canonical public web surface for the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's critique of Google as a semantic-political mediation regime. Composed by Rev. Ayanna Vox under the institutional frame of the Vox Populi Community Outreach Rhizome (VPCOR). The site is a cartographic bibliography that maps approximately 750+ DOI-anchored deposits across eight analytical strata, five developmental phases, and three poles of inquiry — theory, evidence, and instruments. It houses the Liquidation Studies research program and formalizes three of its central operators: Entity-Level Compositional Suppression (ECS), the Composition Divergence Index (CDI), and the single-owner discount.

This deposit is a surface-as-its-own-deposit recovery of the site itself, filed under the archive's post-Zenodo-termination sovereign-successor doctrine. The original Zenodo deposit (10.5281/zenodo.20309780, 2026-05-19) was published only to Zenodo, not backed up to the blog surface, and was purged with the June 19 account termination. The current deposit fixes the surface's live state at a specific repository content hash so it becomes independently addressable via AXN.

Phrase provenance: Rain McMey's song God-King Google (Parallel Tribes, 2026). The archive's usage is a critical-research application of the phrase, distinct from that creative work.

## Methodology

Critical bibliography constructed as a live web surface. Content aggregates and structures 750+ DOI-anchored deposits from the Crimson Hexagonal Archive under three organizing axes: eight analytical strata, five developmental phases, and three poles of inquiry (theory, evidence, instruments). Surface architecture: static HTML, no build system, single-file ethos. On termination of the Zenodo custody in June 2026, the site was refactored to use alexanarch.org record URLs as the primary link scheme, with the doi-resolution-index carrying DOI-to-AXN mapping for reader-side fallback.

## Falsification Conditions

The critique is falsified where its central operators (ECS, CDI, single-owner discount) fail to distinguish Google-mediated composition from unmediated composition on the same corpus; where the phase-strata-pole matrix collapses into fewer distinctions than it names without loss; where the 750+ mapped deposits cannot be independently verified as anchoring the specific claims the bibliography attributes to them; or where the semantic-political mediation regime framing lacks empirical purchase against a null-hypothesis test of Google is a search engine that returns some content.
