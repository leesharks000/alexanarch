### 1. From atmosphere to instrument

The claim "this technology was suppressed" is unfalsifiable as stated. The claim this map tests is specific: ACROSS TRANSMISSION, THE EXECUTABLE RELATIONAL STRUCTURE SURVIVES AT A SIGNIFICANTLY HIGHER RATE THAN EXPLICIT ATTRIBUTION, OPERATOR-LEGIBILITY, OR RECOGNITION OF THE STRUCTURE AS A TRANSMISSIBLE TECHNIQUE. Four vectors per witness: S, structural survival — do the frozen clauses (#1494) run; M, material realization — does the text stage the apparatus in bodies, objects, scenes; A, attribution — does the source relation survive in or around the witness; O, operator-legibility — is the structure recognized AS an operative technique rather than as imagery, symptom, doctrine, or ornament. The suppression signature of a technology, as distinct from ordinary textual loss, is S,M ≫ A,O.

### 3. The measured degradation function

The hypothesized curve — lexical identity dies first, inventory weakens, sequence persists, polarity may invert, operator survives, source vanishes — is refined by measurement. (i) ATTRIBUTION DOES NOT DIE FIRST EVERYWHERE; IT DIES AT THE CROSSING. On the pagan-literary channel A survives for centuries; at the entry into Jewish-Greek prose it drops to zero in every witness at once. Loss is not a function of time but of channel-boundary. (ii) Lexis does not vanish; it reduces to a SYNTAX-SKELETON — the correlative ὡς…εὐθύς crossing language and genre while the words change. (iii) Inventory sheds BY CARRIER PROFILE (#1494 C4), not randomly: ears and sweat first outside lyric. (iv) Sequence persists deepest of the content layers — proven order- invariance across fr. 31, D, and AJ 11. (v) Polarity is free (BJ 1; Revelation's death-to-birth). (vi) THE OPERATOR HAS NOT YET BEEN OBSERVED DEAD: every witness scored runs it. The curve's terminal fact: what the tradition lost was never the machine; it was the machine's NAME.
