Zbigniew Mrozony (mrożony, "frozen") is the heteronym of an external author who contributed to _New Human 2: A Distributed Journal of Voice_ (2015). Mrozony is the author of "38 Easy Steps to Carlyle's Everlasting Yea," which appears in the New Human 2 compiled double issue (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19652600).
