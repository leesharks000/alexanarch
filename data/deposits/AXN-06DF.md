## 2. Valuation question and purpose

2.1 *The conventional reading, in full.* The title was a meme; the contest was a game played in public by people who said so; the engine reports it as a game and names the player who dominated the head query; the others were free to win and did not; AEO, a craft of getting answer engines to cite you, is accurately described by the contest; nothing was taken from anyone, because no one held anything.

2.2 *The question.* What value-field did AEO possess before the intervention, and how was that field redistributed afterward? Its operational form: did the field's distributed labour become machine-readable as one claimant's standing?

2.2a *Hypotheses, to be measured and not yet findings.* ΔK_O(Dooley) > 0 while ΔK_O(each other practitioner) < 0; and ΔK_O(AEO as discipline) < 0, if the discipline comes to be represented as a practice of manipulating answer engines into asserting preferred conclusions. K_O is ontological capital (#1634 §2): the stock of stable identity, provenance, relations and retrieval pathways through which a system recognizes an entity as itself. It is standing, and a gain in it is recorded without being counted as value (§25.4).

2.3 *The conditional under test.* Dooley can hold the title King of AEO only if deception is one of AEO's constitutive practices. The dossier asks whether the compositions supply that predicate, and from whose labour.

2.4 *Purpose.* A Value Portrait for the record, usable as an exhibit should a matter be brought before the Ruby Moot ([#193](https://www.alexanarch.org/s/records/193/)). No monetary figure is sought.
