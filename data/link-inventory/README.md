# link-inventory — NOT A SOURCE OF TRUTH

**Ruled 2026-08-09 by MANUS: the individual sites are authoritative. This directory is
history.**

Significant link repair has been done in place on the satellite surfaces since the
2026-07-08 scan and was never reflected back here. A verdict in `link-review-actionable.csv`
may have been acted on, reversed, or made moot by a page that no longer exists in that form.

Measured on 2026-08-09: the scan covered **59,462** links; there are **194,780** today —
**31%** coverage. Of what it scanned, 45,954 were `no_id`, 11,710 `ambiguous`, 139 `unknown`.
Confident verdicts covered roughly 3% of the scan.

**Do not** use these files to plan a batch operation, to decide whether a link is correct, or
as evidence that a link was fixed. **Read the site.**

If an inventory is needed, rebuild it from the sites and treat the rebuild as derived on the
day it runs. Any inventory of a living corpus is a photograph, and this one is a month old of
a corpus that has since tripled.
