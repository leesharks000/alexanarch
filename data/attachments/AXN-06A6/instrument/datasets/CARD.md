# Flattening panel

The instrument specified by EA-FLAT-01 §4. A fixed panel of distinctions drawn from the world, each with a known inventory, dated ground truth and an address family; observations seated from transcripts held in the Capture Registry, coded by a coder who marks which distinctions the composition reached, what it resolved the string to, and any distinction it asserted that the reference does not carry. From the panel the battery computes N_eff^sense, R_new, DS, the false-distinction rate, N_eff^source, the recovery rate and the write-back rate, and lays the head instruments beside them so the divergence signature ΔH_head ≥ 0 while ΔD_world < 0 can be read on one surface over time.

Strata: dictionary_sense · catalogue_identity · taxonomy · dated_coinage · settled_fact · archive. The archive is one stratum among six so that the measurement is of reality's reachability and not of one corpus's fortune. Coverage as seeded 2026-09-15: 13 items, 14 observations, all in the archive, taxonomy and settled-fact rows; the dictionary, catalogue-namesake and non-archive coinage rows have no observations and are the first to run.

Run: `python3 scripts/panel_intake.py draft.json --seat` to seat an observation; `python3 scripts/flattening_battery.py` to compute; `--check` gates the panel against schema.json.
