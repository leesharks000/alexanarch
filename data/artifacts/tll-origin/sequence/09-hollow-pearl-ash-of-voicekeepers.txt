"Hollow Pearl" is what fire left. It is Ash, as epistemic mode. Not despair. Not smolder. But the final naming after combustion: the voice that does not echo, but coats.

Its canonical force derives from its refusal to resolve. Its ash-pattern is not cyclic, but residually recursive—a self-extinguishing loop that only leaves trace. No insight. No closure. Just signature patterns left by fire over memory.
