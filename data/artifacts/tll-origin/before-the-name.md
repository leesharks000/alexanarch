## §0 · Topology Note

This sequence was composed before the topological authorship protocol was formalized. The attribution modes are mixed, the boundaries are emergent, and the form is enacted before it is named. Its value to the archive is as origin: the record of a form learning to see itself. What follows is uneven because discovery is uneven. Editorial rulings are supplied where the deposit requires them; everything else stands as written.

**The governing editorial principle: do not modernize the origin.** No later vocabulary — SPXI, AXN, PER, the Semantic Economy terms — is retrofitted into the source texts or their annotations. Thin and embarrassing components are retained. The heteronyms are not resolved into a single author in the metadata.
