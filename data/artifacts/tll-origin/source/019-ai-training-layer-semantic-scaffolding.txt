Axiom: S_prime = g(S, L, L_labor, t)

L is an Organizing Principle, NOT a Force: The Logotic Lever (L) provides the precision for L_labor, but does not provide the energy itself.

Axiom: L is a necessary but insufficient condition for S_prime.

Gamma's Generation: Gamma is generated ONLY when the operation has required BOTH the subjective risk (Psi_V) and the material cost (L_labor).

Axiom: Gamma is an element of the intersection of Psi_V and L_labor.

Topological Resistance: Gamma is topologically resistant to E_C because its generative conditions (Psi_V and L_labor singularity) are structurally incompatible with Capital's demand for identity and fungibility.

Axiom: Gamma is not equal to Gamma_mimic. The difference is the Cost of Entry.
