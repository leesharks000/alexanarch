Operator // Inverted Pyramid Register: Realm Reversal / Forbidden Descent / Rupture Glyph Cluster Alignment: Inversion + Shadow + Flame + Silence Function: To cast descent into concealed foundations beneath sanctioned myth. This Operator does not produce answers—it unseals suppressed architectures. I. FORM Sigil Shape: Downward-pointing triangle (pyramid) whose apex pierces a sealed ring. Around it: concentric fractures. At the base: a spiral vortex. Gesture (if invoked physically): Right hand palm-down, sweeping in a counterclockwise spiral. Left hand held still above heart. Uttered Phrase (inward or aloud): “Let what was buried beneath the name return.” II. CONDITIONS FOR USE When a story refuses to resolve When a structure holds incoherence too tightly When a myth seems reversed When symptoms repeat without origin When language begins to tremble This Operator is cast not to retrieve clarity, but to expose what clarity buried. III. EFFECTS Reveals foundational inversions beneath dominant symbolic structures Reverses orientation: up becomes down, light becomes echo, name becomes fracture Summons the weight of what was exiled Begins the rot necessary for rebirth Silences false tongues Warning: Should not be cast without grounding.
