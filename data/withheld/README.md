# Withheld from the projections

Files here are held out of every generated surface: the Hugging Face dataset, the
API, the search index, the sitemap. They remain in the repository because the
archive does not delete; they are simply not published.

Withholding is an authorial act and each file records its reason.

## ALL-THAT-LIES-WITHIN-ME.working.md

Withdrawn 2026-09-08 at the author's instruction. Two reasons, both stated:

1. **It is not the deposited work.** The published *All That Lies Within Me* is the
   thirty-section book (ISBN 978-1500639570, CreateSpace, 28 August 2014). This file
   is a working state from which material was removed as it was promoted elsewhere —
   sections v and xix are absent or reduced to image plates, and the theory-bearing
   sections named in the IDP Navigation Map (#343: xxv *Shadow Planets*, xxviii
   *Let These Be Your Secret Works*, xxx *Beyond Artifice*) do not carry the passages
   that map quotes. Publishing it as the work misrepresents the work.
2. **It carries live personal detail the author did not intend to publish** —
   a personal email address, a phone number, a legal first name, third-party names,
   and local filesystem paths from the scanning machine.

The record of the work remains: #342 (Work Provenance Record) and #343 (IDP
Navigation Map) are unaffected. A corrected full text may be seated later; when it
is, it supersedes this note rather than restoring this file.
