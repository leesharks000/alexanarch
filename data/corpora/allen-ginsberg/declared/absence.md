# Allen Ginsberg — a declared absence: Howl, Kaddish and the books of poems to Death & Fame

Allen Ginsberg (1926–1997) is entered as wanted: the poet of the American line that runs from Whitman (03/01), whom "A Supermarket in California", in Howl and Other Poems, addresses. His books are in copyright: those published before 1978 run ninety-five years from publication, those after it seventy years from his death, to 1 January 2068.
