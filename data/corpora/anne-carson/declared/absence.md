# Anne Carson — a declared absence: the books from Eros the Bittersweet to The Gender of Sound, and the translations from the Greek

Anne Carson (b. 1950), awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature on 8 October 2026, is entered as wanted. She is living, and her works open seventy years after her death. They stand on the shelf's own ground: If Not, Winter translates the Sappho the shelf holds as fragment 31 (01/10), Autobiography of Red is built on Stesichorus, Economy of the Unlost reads Simonides with Celan.
