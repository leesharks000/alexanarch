# Ars Poetica, wanted — a declared absence: the copyrighted moderns, the Language writers, conceptual writing and the contemporary arts of poetry, with the public-domain arts no copy could be found for

The arts of poetry the twenty-ninth seating could not hold, declared beside the ones it holds (ars-poetica, 29/04; avant-garde-ii, 29/05), at the author's direction to 'go further into language poetry and contemporary ars poetica for wanted' (2026-10-28). Most are in copyright and open on the dates given; eleven are open now and wanted because no copy could be fetched this session.
