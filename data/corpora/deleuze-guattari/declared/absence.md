# Deleuze and Guattari — a declared absence

This seat holds no text of Gilles Deleuze (1925–1995) or Félix Guattari (1930–1992). Their works are in copyright. It names what the shelf would hold, who holds the rights and when each work opens, beside the seats of the precursors they read (EA-CORPORA-27/01–27/06).

Life plus seventy years: Guattari's works open on 1 January 2063, Deleuze's and the joint works on 1 January 2066 (a joint work runs from the death of the last surviving author). In the United States: ninety-five years from first publication, the foreign works' copyright restored in 1996 (URAA): a work published in year Y opens on 1 January of Y + 96.
