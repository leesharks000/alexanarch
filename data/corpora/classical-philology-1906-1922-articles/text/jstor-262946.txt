NOTES AND DISCUSSIONS 



ON CICERO DE DIVINATIONE i. 80 

Mr. Pease's proposal (Classical Philology, XIII [April, 1918], 211) to 

move mentis away from sensu seems unquestionably correct. But it should 

be put between eum and vis quaedam. The "homoeographon" mentis vis 

was the cause of the omission of the word which was subsequently restored 

to the text but not to its right place. 

j. p. postgate 
Liverpool
