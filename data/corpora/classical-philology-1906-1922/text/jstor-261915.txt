Hellas and Hesperia or The Vitality of Greek Studies in 
America. Three Lectures. By Basil Lannead Gildbe- 
SLEEVE. New York: Henry Holt & Co., 1909. Pp. 130. 

These lectures do not present themselves at the bar of a "journal 
devoted to research," and Professor Gildersleeve cries alvov ivipa Kopos and 
is unwilling to have his nothings monstered. But we may be permitted 
to congratulate the audiences that first enjoyed all this witty wisdom 
and to recommend its enjoyment in book form to all who accept the 
Horatian ridentem dicere verum. The excellent portrait that fronts the 
title-page enhances the value of the volume to the author's many friends, 
pupils, and admirers, among whom we wish to be counted.
