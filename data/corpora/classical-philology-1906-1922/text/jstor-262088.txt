This monograph was written to prove the thesis that "all of (sic) the 
meanings of yap are derivatives, more or less remote, of the reason-idea

apa," Miss Misener holds that " the time of its formation so antedates

Truly, an analysis to have pleased the heart of Aristotle! To rest me, 
I turn to Apollodorus and construct a family-tree of the descendants of 
Oipavos and IN}. No guilty yap can have escaped. Were it not for the 
footnotes packed with further examples, one might infer that a separate 
pigeon-hole had been provided for every yap in the extant literature. 
Though evidently much exact thinking has been put into it, yet to me 
the classification seems often fictitious ; the carver not always to have hit 
the joints. For example, there is surely no organic distinction between 
yap in declarative clauses and yap anticipatory; and even Miss Misener 
herself, in her treatment of confirmatory yap in declarative sentences, 
admits four times over that it " approaches," or " fuses," or " intermingles," 
or "blends" with the causal yap and the explanatory yap. It is, indeed, 
a fluid particle, no more to be fenced into garden-plots than water that 
flows over submerged fields. 

It is a pity that the candidate did not use her rich material to prove 
the antithesis of her thesis, viz., that from corroborative yap (" indeed,"
