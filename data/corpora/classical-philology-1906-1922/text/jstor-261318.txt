In a brief preface the editor gives account of the relation of his edi- 
tion to manuscripts and previous editions. Since the few who deign 
to read Galen resort to the edition of Kuhn, it is well to know that 
it has no critical value. Our present editor, who has previously given 
evidence of sound method and critical insight, makes it possible to dis- 
pense with Kuhn in another work and thereby adds to our obligations; 
but while it may be said, as Helmreich says of himself (I. xii), that even 
he has not considered the claims of all known manuscripts, we cannot 
assume to have even approximately a definitive text.
