The book concludes with an ingenious argument to the effect that

suggested. This last does not pass in our judgment the bounds of mere 
conjecture. 

The Swedish scholar Soederhjelm seems to have first taken up the

whose subjects are also the subject of the principal verb, the infinitives 
whose subjects are the object of the principal verb, and so other groups.
