# Tao Lin — a declared absence: the books from you are a little bit happier than i am to Leave Society, and the mandalas

Tao Lin (b. 1983) is entered as wanted, the mandalas with the books. He is living, and his works open seventy years after his death. The mandalas, which he began drawing in 2014 and first sold on eBay, are images whose every reproduction is his to license.
