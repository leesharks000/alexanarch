musicians ascribe the invention of the Fnharmonic

the only seales had been the Diatonie and the

about thus: In descending in the — νς seale his
melody frequently passed from B or from A to F,
omitting G. Realising the beater of this effect,
Olympus in his astonishment accepted the principle
for the whole system, and eomposed in it in the
Dorian ‘mode.’ rejecting all intervals peculiar to the
Diatonic or Chromatic scales and concerning himself
directly with the mode. Such was the origin of his
Enharmonic seale... . It is clear that Olympus
made a real advance in’ music by introducing an
entire novelty, and was the father of good music in
Greece.
