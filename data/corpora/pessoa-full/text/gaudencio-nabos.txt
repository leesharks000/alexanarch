Talking about innocent stories and such like, speaking all about a great pleasure and so on, transformations of voluptuosity, no doubt, Dr. Nabos had this extraordinary phrase:
«Some people seem to be unaware that a courtesan is a whore.»
A dull person will take some time to find the  point  of this phrase.
