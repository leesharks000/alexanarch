## 0. What is measured

0.1. Aristotle defines the art in one clause: ἔστι γὰρ ἡ σοφιστικὴ φαινομένη σοφία οὖσα δ᾿ οὔ, καὶ ὁ σοφιστὴς χρηματιστὴς ἀπὸ φαινομένης σοφίας ἀλλ᾿ οὐκ οὔσης (SE 1; seated text, `data/corpora/aristotle/text/tlg040-De_sophisticis_elenchis.txt`, ll. 30–32). Sophistry is wisdom that appears and is not, and the sophist is paid from the appearance.

0.2. The sophistry measure records, at an address, the gap between what a composition appears to carry and what it carries. Each finding is one typed operation with its witness.

0.3. PER (#1469; recalculated across the registry in #1694) counts what a composition loses against its target. The sophistry measure records what a composition keeps in appearance while it changes the relations that make it the thing. The two disagree, and the disagreement is the reason for a second instrument. At `asynthetic-synthesis-dialectic-chatgpt-20261101` the registry holds PER 0.042 (M 0.0, C 0.0, D 0.111): every atom present, almost nothing lost. The composition substitutes one locus of a concept developed across at least eight deposits for the concept, and every quotation in it is genuine. A low erasure score and an unmarked substitution sit on one observation. PER cannot see the second, because the second is made of retained atoms.

## 1. The innocent reading, and the pilot of 2026-11-01

1.1. The conventional reading first, in full. The Sophistical Refutations is the closing book of the Organon: a catalogue of apparent refutations, six dependent on language (homonymy, amphiboly, combination, division, accent, form of expression) and seven outside it (accident, the qualified taken absolutely, ignorance of what refutation is, begging the starting point, the consequent, the non-cause taken as cause, several questions asked as one). Chapters 16–33 give their solutions. Chapter 34 claims that nothing of the inquiry existed before it. The book is read as the first treatise on fallacies, and its thirteen are read as the ancestors of the modern textbook list.

1.2. The pilot dossier of 2026-11-01 (*Sophistical Refutations: toward a computational taxonomy of sophistry, erasure and hypocrisy*, v0.1, not deposited) reads all 34 chapters in the Pickard-Cambridge translation and turns them into an instrument. Its core is a chain a second reader can contest: witness → reconstruction → operation → countertest → repair. It builds 48 types (the 13 historical classes kept identifiable, 25 extended operations, 5 dialogue-protocol types, 5 norm/conduct types). It annotates 27 case units from 11 capture records, five of them controls, and passes 12 executable tests. It gives no scalar.

1.3. The pilot's central addition comes from the operator's correction during the work: X24, one locus substituted, unmarked, for an entity developed across several sources. Its evidence is the asynthetic sequence: #524 (2026-02-28), #542, #549, #557/#558, #950, #1008, #1550 (Notebook X), #1556. ## 2. A second reading of the Sophistical Refutations

The pilot reads the book through the translation and its thirteen classes. The Greek holds eleven further loci that bear on the instrument. Each is given with its line in the seated text and its consequence for the build.

2.1. **The counters (ch. 1, ll. 17–25).** ἐπεὶ γὰρ οὐκ ἔστιν αὐτὰ τὰ πράγματα διαλέγεσθαι φέροντας, ἀλλὰ τοῖς ὀνόμασιν ἀντὶ τῶν πραγμάτων χρώμεθα συμβόλοις, τὸ συμβαῖνον ἐπὶ τῶν ὀνομάτων καὶ ἐπὶ τῶν πραγμάτων ἡγούμεθα συμβαίνειν, καθάπερ ἐπὶ τῶν ψήφων τοῖς λογιζομένοις. We cannot bring the things themselves into discussion, so we use names as σύμβολα in their place, and take what follows for the names to follow for the things, as reckoners do with their ψῆφοι. Then the reason it fails: τὰ μὲν γὰρ ὀνόματα πεπέρανται καὶ τὸ τῶν λόγων πλῆθος, τὰ δὲ πράγματα τὸν ἀριθμὸν ἄπειρά ἐστιν. Names and accounts are finite, things are unlimited in number, so one name and one account must signify several things. Those unskilled at moving counters are cheated by those who know (οἱ μὴ δεινοὶ τὰς ψήφους φέρειν ὑπὸ τῶν ἐπιστημόνων παρακρούονται).

Consequence. Homonymy is a condition of every compression, and the first topos of the sophist (εὐφυέστατος καὶ δημοσιώτατος, the most natural and the most public) is the counter-board itself. The archive already keeps a counter-board: the homonym lists of the /non panel, the negative edges of MPAI, the disambiguation matrices of SPXI. A sophistry finding at the level of the name asks whether the composition moved a counter whose thing had changed. The two words carry further into the archive: σύμβολον is the archive's kernel, and the ψῆφος is the white stone of Revelation 2:17, the limit case of #1485.

2.2. **Distance (ch. 1, ll. 13–14).** οἱ γὰρ ἄπειροι ὥσπερ ἂν ἀπέχοντες πόρρωθεν θεωροῦσιν. The inexperienced look as if from far off, and the apparent refutation appears through that inexperience. Consequence. Appearance is a function of distance from the thing. A summary layer is a view from far off, given to a reader who receives it from far off. The measure is taken at the distance the reader receives: what the composition makes appear to someone who has not read the sources.

2.3. **The economy of the genus (ch. 1, ll. 29–34; ch. 11, ll. 430–437).** The same argument is eristic and sophistic, ἀλλ᾿ οὐ κατὰ ταὐτόν: eristic as it secures apparent victory, sophistic as it secures apparent wisdom, and the sophistic is χρηματιστική τις ἀπὸ σοφίας φαινομένης, money-making from apparent wisdom. The lovers of strife and the sophists use the same arguments, οὐ τῶν αὐτῶν ἕνεκεν. Consequence. The book divides its genus by function and incentive: what the argument secures, and who is paid from the appearance. The mechanism is shared across the division. This is the semantic economy's question asked at the founding of the discipline (#137, the semantic commodity form; #1617). For the measure it means each finding records two things separately: the operation (shared by eristic and sophistic), and what the composition secures, and for whom. An answer engine paid from the appearance of having read is the sophistic case, by Aristotle's own division. No intent is coded; incentive and function are read off what the composition does and what it is sold as.

2.4. **No division by the thought (ch. 10, ll. 355–372).** Aristotle rejects the division of arguments into those against the name and those against the thought: οὐ γὰρ ἐν τῷ λόγῳ ἐστὶ τὸ πρὸς τὴν διάνοιαν εἶναι, ἀλλ᾿ ἐν τῷ τὸν ἀποκρινόμενον ἔχειν πως πρὸς τὰ δεδομένα. Being against the thought is not in the argument; it lies in how the answerer stands toward what was granted. Consequence. The classification is made on the argument's mechanics, and the thought is not an object it reads. The measure codes no motive. The pilot's speaker hints remain hints, and a model's account of why it answered as it did is a further text to be read under the same tests (the pilot's C02 already holds this).

2.5. **Five targets (ch. 3, ll. 53–58).** The contenders aim at five things: ἔλεγχος, ψεῦδος, παράδοξον, σολοικισμός, and fifth τὸ ποιῆσαι ἀδολεσχῆσαι τὸν προσδιαλεγόμενον, τοῦτο δ᾿ ἐστὶ τὸ πολλάκις ἀναγκάζεσθαι ταὐτὸ λέγειν, making the interlocutor babble, which is to be compelled to say the same thing many times. Of each, ἢ τὸ μὴ ὄν, ἀλλὰ τὸ φαινόμενον: either the thing, or its appearance. Consequence. The measure's target space has five regions. A composition can make an entity appear refuted, appear false, appear to hold a paradox it does not hold, appear malformed in its own expression, or appear to say the same thing over and over. The thirteen classes are the means; the five are what the means secure. Every finding records which of the five the operation produces. Babble has a named instance in the archive already: the photocopy problem of SPXI, near-infinite copying with minimal variance.

2.6. **Accent and the editor's hand (ch. 4, ll. 105–112; ch. 21, ll. 802–806).** Arguments from accent are hard to make in unwritten discussion and easier in written texts and poems. Some correct Homer against those who refute him (τὸν Ὅμηρον ἔνιοι διορθοῦνται πρὸς τοὺς ἐλέγχοντας) by re-accenting οὐ as οὗ, and Agamemnon's dream by reading διδόμεν for δίδομεν. In chapter 21 such arguments are few. Consequence, in two parts. First, the accent class is the class of the written mark: a difference carried only by the written form. Its machine instance is orthographic near-identity, two names that differ by case, spacing or a letter. WikiWho answered as wikiHow is this class, and it can be typed historically on the mark without inventing an argument to carry a name (the pilot's C07 types it X01 for that reason). Second, the solution by accent is an editorial act: διόρθωσις is the editor's word. The apparatus enters the Sophistical Refutations as a solution, an intervention on the text made to answer a refutation. That is #1485's thesis, that the apparatus was the first summary layer, located inside the book on sophistry. The repair class of the measure inherits it: a repair is itself an intervention on the text and is read under the same tests.

2.7. **The this and the such (ch. 6, ll. 210–211; ch. 7, ll. 279–281; ch. 22, ll. 863–874).** σύνηθες γὰρ τὸ πάντα ὡς τόδε τι σημαίνειν: it is customary to take everything as signifying a this. πᾶν τὸ κατηγορούμενόν τινος ὑπολαμβάνομεν τόδε τι καὶ ὡς ἓν ὑπακούομεν: we take whatever is predicated of something to be a this and hear it as one. At the third man: τὸ γὰρ ἄνθρωπος καὶ ἅπαν τὸ κοινὸν οὐ τόδε τι, ἀλλὰ τοιόνδε τι; and οὐ τὸ ἐκτίθεσθαι δὲ ποιεῖ τὸν τρίτον ἄνθρωπον, ἀλλὰ τὸ ὅπερ τόδε τι εἶναι συγχωρεῖν. Setting the common term out does not make the third man; conceding that it is a this does. Consequence. Form of expression is the class of the encyclopedic entity. #1697 reads the entity as the form in which an operation appears as a property of a thing; Aristotle gives the mechanism, the predicate heard as a this and as one. X24 sits in this class. Its test follows Aristotle's sentence exactly: setting out one locus, quoting it accurately, is ἔκθεσις and is no fault. The fault is granting the locus the standing of the developed entity, ὅπερ τόδε τι. The measure therefore asks of every quotation what standing the composition grants it.

2.8. **The veiled man (ch. 24, ll. 891–899).** Ἆρ᾿ οἶδας τὸν προσιόντα ἢ τὸν ἐγκεκαλυμμένον; If I know Coriscus and do not know the one approaching, do I know and not know the same man? The predicate true of the thing need not be true of it under an incidental description. Consequence. This is the case of identity under a name, the heteronym's case. A composer that resolves Lee Sharks and does not resolve the configuration under Johannes Sigil or Jack Feist, or that carries a predicate from one name to another through an incidental identification, runs accident. The measure records the direction of the transfer and the description it runs through.

2.9. **Sharpness (ch. 33, ll. 1102–1112).** Ἔστι δὲ δριμὺς λόγος ὅστις ἀπορεῖν ποιεῖ μάλιστα· δάκνει γὰρ οὗτος μάλιστα. The sharp argument is the one that most produces perplexity, and it bites the most. Perplexity is double: in syllogisms, which of the premises to withdraw; in eristic arguments, how to state what was put forward. The sharpest proceeds from what is most reputable and destroys what is most reputable. Consequence. The measure gains an ordinal dimension the pilot lacks: sharpness, recorded as whether the finding locates the premise to withdraw, and as the standing of the sources the composition draws on against the standing of what it removes. The sharpest compositions in the registry are the ones built from the most reputable cards that remove the most reputable reading: the contested basin, where a resolution is siphoned and credited to institutional authorities.

2.10. **The solution addressed to the questioner (ch. 20, ll. 799–800).** Some solutions fit πρὸς τὸν ἐρωτῶντα, οὐ πρὸς τὸν λόγον: they answer the questioner and leave the argument standing. Consequence. A correction addressed to the user (an apology, a change of tone, a disclaimer) leaves the argument standing. The pilot's C11, where a repair disavows a qualification and returns it, is this class; the repair test checks the dependency.

2.11. **The ending (ch. 34) and the first measurement.** The archive has already measured one sophistical operation at one locus of this book. The Particle (#1574) put SE 183b34–36, οὐδὲν παντελῶς ὑπῆρχεν, through twenty rounds of simulated review across four model families, and every round inserted a qualifier into the sentence. In Aristotle's terms that is the qualified/absolute class run in reverse: an absolute statement returned as a qualified one, τὸ ἁπλῶς made πῄ. Nothing Before Us (#1603) reads the sentence as meaning what it says. The sophistry measure's first worked case is therefore older than the measure, and it is seated.

## 3. The joins

3.1. **PER (#1469, #1694).** PER is scored against a target's atom set, with substitution scored as total erasure. The sophistry measure is scored against the source frame, finding by finding, with no scalar. The joins: (a) every observation that carries a per_dim may carry a sophistry record beside it, and neither pays for the other; (b) where PER is low and a sophistry finding stands, the observation is flagged as retained in appearance, the case PER cannot see; (c) a target is itself read under the measure before it scores anything, since the pilot shows a target can commit X24 (§1.4).

3.2. **The Negative of the Negative (#1690, #1726; #1704 for readers).** /non keeps three compositions: T, what the engine said; L(B), the entity recomposed from the sources the engine surfaced; L(B ∪ A), with the archive admitted. Its typology of differences between T and L(B) (§6.2, "available and not composed" and the rest) records what changed. The sophistry measure types each difference by operation: which of the thirteen classes or their extensions produced it, which of the five targets it secures, and how sharp it is. The /non row for the Sophistical Refutations itself (`datasets/negative-of-the-negative/v2/rows/sophistical-refutations.json`, Wikipedia oldid 1361634710) already records a case: T's "twelve or thirteen fallacies" leaves uncomposed the field's own division into arguments dependent on language and independent of it (F11–F13). For an archive entity, X24's developmental dossier and L(B ∪ A) are one object: the entity composed by lineage across its loci. The dossier is built first, and the target is drawn from it.

3.3. **SPXI-gnΩsis (#1703; protocol Θ_W, #1697).** gnΩsis opens a composition sentence by sentence against the sources it cites, every quotation located by code, the reading kept as evidence; a commission composes the object's /non row. It works for entities and for operators, the operations an entity form conceals. The sophistry measure supplies the typed vocabulary of that reading. Each sentence read returns, beside its fidelity verdict, the operation that produced any difference, its witness, its countertest and the smallest repair. The operator side of gnΩsis and §2.7 are one problem: an operation that appears as a property of a thing is the predicate heard ὡς τόδε τι.

3.4. **The Capture Registry.** The pilot's lexical pass returns 6,033 candidate spans in 692 observations; these are retrieval workload, not findings. Findings enter only with the full chain. The registry's laws hold: a finding records, it does not block; the archive's own compositions, targets and repairs are read under the same tests as an external answer.

3.5. **Hypocrisy.** The pilot's norm–practice record stands as given (norm, who is bound, scope, conduct evidence, comparator, exceptions, acknowledgment, resulting conflict). It is a separate relation and enters the measure as its own record type.
