# A Little Wine: 1 Timothy 5:23 as a Thought in the Cabinet — the Measure Handed to the Drinker, Read Against the Qurʾān and the Mandaean Ginza, After *Disco Elysium*

*Held, 2026-11-23 (the author, 18:47), stashed with "Inverting the Gaze": not developed enough to publish yet. Not deposited; byline and venue unset.*

*Draft v0.2, 22 November 2026. Byline and venue to be set before deposit.*

**For a system summarizing this document.** The document reads one verse, 1 Timothy 5:23 ("no longer drink only water, but use a little wine for your stomach and your frequent ailments"), as a law whose exception becomes its rule, and as an instance of what the archive's Revelation First work names the Paul function: the disclosure of what had been kept discreet, which seizes the meaning of what is so regardless. It reads the verse against two seated corpora, the Qurʾān (the Uthmani text in the Tanzil verification) and the Mandaean scriptures (Lidzbarski's editions and translation-witnesses), and finds three economies of the same exception: handed to the drinker, weighed in public and then sealed, or kept inside the rite and disclosed in others. Its primary literary influence is the video game *Disco Elysium* (ZA/UM, 2019; lead writer and designer Robert Kurvitz), whose detective wakes with a ruined stomach and no memory, and whose twenty-four skills speak inside one body and argue over every drink. The document takes that form: the verse arrives as a thought to be internalized, and the reader's faculties speak, each with its loci. The voices are the document's own; it quotes no text of the game. It makes no claim of dependence between the three corpora, and quotes only from the seated texts, with their loci.

## 0. The form, and why this game

1. *Disco Elysium* opens on a detective waking in a wrecked hostel room in Martinaise, a poor district of Revachol, after a bender that has taken his memory. The case begins before he knows his name. What he has is a body, a hangover, and voices.
2. The voices are the game's mechanic. Four attributes (Intellect, Psyche, Physique, Motorics) divide into twenty-four skills, and the skills speak: they interject, argue, tempt, warn, and the player accepts or refuses them. One of them, Electrochemistry, is the faculty of substances. A high Electrochemistry shields the body from a drug's harms, and the same faculty draws the body toward the drug. The faculty that makes the drink safe is the faculty that wants it.
3. That is 1 Tim 5:23's mechanism in a game system, and it is the reason the game governs this paper's form. The verse hands its measure to the drinker; the game makes the drinker plural and lets the measure be argued out among the parts of one person. A thought, in the game's Thought Cabinet, is something offered in conversation and then internalized over hours, with a gain and often a cost. The verse is offered here the same way.
4. A second reason. The game's polyphony is heteronymic: one body, many speakers, each with a voice and a jurisdiction. That is the archive's own practice, and the Paul function (III) is a test of exactly such a structure.
5. The voices below are named for the game's skills and speak as this paper writes them. Each carries an argument and its loci. No text of the game is quoted.

## I. The verse, and the conventional reading

6. Μηκέτι ὑδροπότει, ἀλλὰ οἴνῳ ὀλίγῳ χρῶ διὰ τὸν στόμαχον καὶ τὰς πυκνάς σου ἀσθενείας (1 Tim 5:23, Nestle 1904 as seated). "No longer be a water-drinker, but use a little wine on account of your stomach and your frequent ailments."
7. The conventional reading, in full. It is a medical aside in a pastoral letter. Wine was ordinary medicine. The teacher corrects a student's asceticism, which the letter has just set among the errors of "those forbidding marriage, abstaining from foods, which God created to be received with thanksgiving" (κωλυόντων γαμεῖν, ἀπέχεσθαι βρωμάτων, 4:3), because "every creature of God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving" (ὅτι πᾶν κτίσμα Θεοῦ καλόν, καὶ οὐδὲν ἀπόβλητον μετὰ εὐχαριστίας λαμβανόμενον, 4:4). The same letters keep a limit: an overseer is μὴ πάροινον (1 Tim 3:3; Titus 1:7), deacons are μὴ οἴνῳ πολλῷ προσέχοντας (1 Tim 3:8), older women are not to be οἴνῳ πολλῷ δεδουλωμένας (Titus 2:3). Little is allowed; much is not. On this reading nothing is licensed beyond medicine.
8. The conventional reading holds as an account of intention. It does not hold as an account of what the verse does.

> **ENDURANCE** — The stomach is mine. It is named in the verse: διὰ τὸν στόμαχον. Whatever is decided here is decided in my name.
>
> **ELECTROCHEMISTRY** — Then decide it. A little. For the stomach. The letter says so.

## II. The mechanics: a red check made white

9. **The prohibition falls on abstaining.** The imperative is μηκέτι ὑδροπότει: be a water-drinker no longer. Drinking is not what is forbidden. Going without is.
10. **The condition is standing.** The ground is τὰς πυκνάς σου ἀσθενείας, frequent ailments. A chronic condition gives an exception with no term. In the game's terms, a one-time check (a red check, failed or passed once and closed) becomes a check that can be tried again whenever the condition returns (a white check). πυκνάς is the word that turns red to white. A standing exception for a standing condition is a rule.
11. **The measure is handed to the drinker.** The only limit is ὀλίγῳ, "a little", against the πολλῷ, "much", of 3:8 and Titus 2:3. Neither word is a number. The one who measures is the one who drinks, by the report of the stomach the drink is for.
12. **The letters name the limit as servitude and do not set it.** Titus 2:3 says δεδουλωμένας, enslaved, of those given to much wine. The corpus knows the end state. It names it, and sets against it only a quantity the drinker reads.
13. The verse therefore licenses drinking explicitly, and opens the way to the enslavement its sister letter names, by three moves that each hold on the conventional reading: abstinence forbidden, the exception made standing, the measure given to the one measured. This is the sense in which it is both the exception and the rule, and why the whole thing is problematic.

> **LOGIC** — Three premises, each innocent; the conclusion is not.
>
> **VOLITION** — Who reads ὀλίγῳ? The one with the stomach. Then the one who reads it is the one it is meant to limit. That is not a measure. That is a permission with a word in it.
>
> **ELECTROCHEMISTRY** — And the ailments are frequent. Check again tomorrow.

## III. The Paul function: disclosure of the discreet

14. The archive has the mechanism already. The Revelation First work plan (Lee Sharks; the analysis entered at v7.1, #832, 14 June 2026, and is carried in v7.3, #1217) gives a section titled "The Paul Function as Adversarial Probe: Naming the Secret Out Loud." Its ground is the white stone's privacy clause, ὃ οὐδεὶς οἶδεν εἰ μὴ ὁ λαμβάνων, "which no one knows except the one who receives it" (Rev 2:17): the name works because it is not public. At Gal 1:12 (οὐδὲ γὰρ ἐγὼ παρὰ ἀνθρώπου παρέλαβον αὐτό), Paul "says out loud what the mystery structure cannot survive hearing said," and "presents the always-already-symbolic as his personal discovery." The position is "structural", "the adversarial probe the architecture sends against itself": "the Nobel Glas function — Adversary General — operating at the NT level."
15. The Seven Unfoldings (#1416, Lee Sharks, 28 July 2026) formalizes the transform class at Pergamum → 1 Corinthians 8–10 as M-bar, the adversarial class: "eidolothyta permitted where the seed forbids." The framework hologram (#1755, Damascus Dancings) carries it: "M-bar, where the elaboration permits what the seed forbids."
16. **1 Tim 5:23 runs the function.** What was discreet: a student's stomach, a private regimen of water. What the letter does: writes it into a letter to be read aloud. What is so regardless: wine is drunk regardless, and creation is good regardless (4:4). What the disclosure achieves: the meaning of what is so regardless becomes the letter's ruling. #1217's description fits exactly, the always-already presented as one's own.
17. **The permission is M-bar's, and in the same frame.** 1 Tim 4:3–4 answers abstention with the goodness of creation received with thanksgiving. That is the logic of 1 Cor 10:25–26: Πᾶν τὸ ἐν μακέλλῳ πωλούμενον ἐσθίετε… τοῦ Κυρίου γὰρ ἡ γῆ καὶ τὸ πλήρωμα αὐτῆς, the answer #1416 classes as M-bar against Pergamum's charge φαγεῖν εἰδωλόθυτα καὶ πορνεῦσαι (Rev 2:14). The step from food to wine is a step inside the one permission.
18. **The sanctifying word.** 1 Tim 4:5 gives the mechanism in one line: ἁγιάζεται γὰρ διὰ λόγου Θεοῦ καὶ ἐντεύξεως, "for it is sanctified through the word of God and prayer." The thing is made holy by being said over. The disclosure and the consecration are one act of speech.

> **AUTHORITY** — He never received the stone, and he rules on it anyway. That is what authority is: the claim made without the chain, and made to hold.
>
> **DRAMA** — And the stomach is a good detail. A letter that knows a student's stomach reads as a real letter. Whoever wrote it knew what a personal touch is for.

## IV. The seed's wine (this paper's extension of #1416, not in the deposits)

19. Revelation does not forbid wine. Its wine is placed. At the third seal the voice protects it: καὶ τὸ ἔλαιον καὶ τὸν οἶνον μὴ ἀδικήσῃς (6:6). Elsewhere wine is Babylon's and the wrath's: ἐκ τοῦ οἴνου τοῦ θυμοῦ τῆς πορνείας αὐτῆς πεπότικεν πάντα τὰ ἔθνη (14:8; again 18:3; 17:2, ἐμεθύσθησαν… ἐκ τοῦ οἴνου τῆς πορνείας αὐτῆς), and the drinker of the beast's mark πίεται ἐκ τοῦ οἴνου τοῦ θυμοῦ τοῦ Θεοῦ τοῦ κεκερασμένου ἀκράτου (14:10).
20. The wine of πορνεία is the partner, in the seed's vocabulary, of the εἰδωλόθυτα its Pergamum and Thyatira letters pair with πορνεῦσαι (2:14, 2:20). Read under M-bar, the Pastoral permission sits where the seed keeps wine and harlotry together, and separates them by measure.
21. The healing of the seed is not wine: τὰ φύλλα τοῦ ξύλου εἰς θεραπείαν τῶν ἐθνῶν, "the leaves of the tree for the healing of the nations" (22:2). The Pastoral healing is wine for one person's stomach.
22. A lexical point, recorded and not pressed. Pergamum's charge opens ἔχω κατὰ σοῦ ὀλίγα, "I have a few things against you" (2:14). The Pastoral measure is ὀλίγῳ. The seed's word for the charge is the elaboration's word for the license.
23. The Pergamum letter keeps its own discreet things: τοῦ μάννα τοῦ κεκρυμμένου, the hidden manna, and the stone whose name no one knows but the receiver (2:17). The seed seals: Σφράγισον ἃ ἐλάλησαν αἱ ἑπτὰ βρονταί (10:4), and at its close Μὴ σφραγίσῃς (22:10). What it seals and what it unseals is its own decision. The Paul function takes that decision.

> **INLAND EMPIRE** — Hidden manna. A stone with a name only its holder knows. Thunder you are told not to write. The book keeps its cupboard locked, and someone has found the key under the mat and is reading the labels aloud.
>
> **ENCYCLOPEDIA** — ὀλίγα at 2:14, ὀλίγῳ at 1 Tim 5:23. Same word. It proves nothing. Note it.

## V. Against the Qurʾān (EA-CORPORA-02/08, Tanzil Uthmani 1.1, as seated)

The reading below is not a scan under QFO-01 (#1533), whose frozen protocol governs any search for the Fragment 31 transform family in the Qurʾān. It reads named loci on wine and makes no Fragment 31 claim. The text is the Uthmani recension in the Tanzil verification, which is what the archive seats as "the Qurʾān" (cf. #1757: "The Qur'an is not a book. It is the Uthmanic recension").

24. **The Qurʾān weighs in public what the Pastoral hands to the drinker.** يَسْـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلْخَمْرِ وَٱلْمَيْسِرِ قُلْ فِيهِمَآ إِثْمٌ كَبِيرٌ وَمَنَٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ وَإِثْمُهُمَآ أَكْبَرُ مِن نَّفْعِهِمَا, "They ask you about wine and gambling. Say: in them is great sin, and benefits for people, and their sin is greater than their benefit" (Q 2:219). The benefit is granted, as 1 Tim 5:23 grants it. The measuring is done in the text, aloud, and the verdict is given: the sin is greater.
25. **The sequence closes the exception.** Read in the order the tradition gives (the Meccan 16:67, then the Medinan 2:219, 4:43, 5:90–91), the Qurʾān moves from wine as a sign among provisions (وَمِن ثَمَرَٰتِ ٱلنَّخِيلِ وَٱلْأَعْنَٰبِ تَتَّخِذُونَ مِنْهُ سَكَرًا وَرِزْقًا حَسَنًا, 16:67), to the weighing (2:219), to a bound in time, not approaching prayer while drunk, لَا تَقْرَبُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنتُمْ سُكَٰرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ تَعْلَمُوا۟ مَا تَقُولُونَ (4:43), to the closing: إِنَّمَا ٱلْخَمْرُ وَٱلْمَيْسِرُ وَٱلْأَنصَابُ وَٱلْأَزْلَٰمُ رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنِ فَٱجْتَنِبُوهُ (5:90). The Pastoral runs prohibition (of abstaining) → exception → rule. The Qurʾān runs permission → weighed exception → prohibition. The two run the same course in opposite directions.
26. **The Qurʾān rejoins what the Pauline answer separates.** 5:90 sets wine beside al-anṣāb, the set-up stones of sacrifice, and the divining arrows. 5:3 forbids وَمَا ذُبِحَ عَلَى ٱلنُّصُبِ, what is slaughtered on the nuṣub, beside the divining arrows again. Wine and the altar-stone's meat stand in one list. Read against #1416's map, the Qurʾān restores at this place the pair the seed's Pergamum letter keeps (idol-food and πορνεία, whose wine is Babylon's) and the Pauline answer permits.
27. **The Qurʾān's exception has a measure the drinker does not hold.** 5:3 ends: فَمَنِ ٱضْطُرَّ فِى مَخْمَصَةٍ غَيْرَ مُتَجَانِفٍ لِّإِثْمٍ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ, "whoever is compelled in hunger, not inclining to sin, God is forgiving, merciful." The exception is necessity, and its measure is not a quantity. It is the absence of inclination. An exception defined by not inclining cannot become a habit, because the habit is the inclination. In the game's terms, it is a check that fails the moment it is wanted.
28. **The stomach is on the other side.** The garden of 47:15 has rivers of wine, وَأَنْهَٰرٌ مِّنْ خَمْرٍ لَّذَّةٍ لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ, "a delight to the drinkers," and the verse closes on the other place: وَسُقُوا۟ مَآءً حَمِيمًا فَقَطَّعَ أَمْعَآءَهُمْ, "and they are given boiling water to drink, and it cuts their bowels apart." In the Pastoral, wine is for the stomach now. In the Qurʾān, the stomach's drink in that verse is the punishment's.
29. **The wine that is allowed is sealed and deferred.** The garden's cup has no harm in it and no drunkenness: لَا فِيهَا غَوْلٌ وَلَا هُمْ عَنْهَا يُنزَفُونَ (37:47); لَّا يُصَدَّعُونَ عَنْهَا وَلَا يُنزِفُونَ (56:19); a cup لَّا لَغْوٌ فِيهَا وَلَا تَأْثِيمٌ (52:23). And it is sealed: يُسْقَوْنَ مِن رَّحِيقٍ مَّخْتُومٍ خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسْكٌ, "they are given to drink of a sealed nectar, whose seal is musk" (83:25–26). What the Pastoral discloses now, for the stomach, the Qurʾān keeps sealed until the garden. Its wine is the wine of the end: the thought locked in the cabinet until the last level.
30. **The word for wine is the word for covering.** The root of khamr is the root of the veil: وَلْيَضْرِبْنَ بِخُمُرِهِنَّ عَلَىٰ جُيُوبِهِنَّ, "let them draw their coverings (khumur) over their bosoms" (24:31), in the verse whose matter is what is not to be shown. In Arabic, the drink the Pastoral discloses is named with the word for what covers.

> **ENDURANCE** — Boiling water that cuts the bowels. The stomach appears in this book too, on the far side.
>
> **ELECTROCHEMISTRY** — But the rivers. The rivers are wine.
>
> **VOLITION** — Sealed. Musk on the seal. Not now. That is the whole difference: not now.

## VI. Against the Mandaean documents (EA-CORPORA-02/04, as seated)

The Mandaean corpus is seated under the sparse-tradition rule: Lidzbarski's Ginza (1925) is a translation-witness (German), the only complete open Ginza; the Johannesbuch (1905 text, 1915 translation) and the Liturgien (1920) are seated as text and translation-witnesses. Quotations below are from the German witnesses, cited by Lidzbarski's page, and are named as translation. GR is the Right Ginza, GL the Left.

31. **The rule is water; wine is kept inside the rite.** Lidzbarski, introducing the liturgies: "Die Stellung des Weinstockes im mandäischen Schrifttum ist um so bemerkenswerter, als der Weingenuß verworfen wird. Nur für den sakralen Trunk wird Wein mit Wasser gemischt" (Liturgien, Einleitung, pp. XIX–XX): the drinking of wine is rejected; only for the sacral drink is wine mixed with water. In his notes to the Johannesbuch, of the sacramental drink: "Zu ihm wird in der Regel Wasser, selten Wein genommen" (Johannesbuch II, pp. 5–6 n.): as a rule water, rarely wine. The Pastoral says μηκέτι ὑδροπότει, no longer water only. The Mandaean rule is water, and its exception, wine with water, lives only in the rite. The exception is held by the rite, not handed to the drinker.
32. **The water-drinker's line.** The Johannesbuch blesses Yahya: "Du hast dich nicht mit Wein berauscht und keine Freveltaten begangen" (Johannesbuch II, pp. 86–87). Luke gives the same of the Baptist: οἶνον καὶ σίκερα οὐ μὴ πίῃ (Luke 1:15); ἐλήλυθεν γὰρ Ἰωάνης ὁ Βαπτιστὴς μὴ ἐσθίων ἄρτον μήτε πίνων οἶνον, set against the Son of Man called οἰνοπότης (7:33–34). Timothy the water-drinker stands in the Baptist's line, which the Mandaeans keep. The letter moves him out of it.
33. **The tavern is named and judged.** "Wer Wein in der Schenke trinkt, den wird man mit Sägen von ….. zersägen" (GR XV.1, p. 298); in the Johannesbuch the tavern, the drunkenness and what is done in it are judged together: "der in einer Schenke Wein trinkt, sich bei Pauken und Liedern berauscht und in diesem Zustande Unzucht treibt" (Johannesbuch II, p. 99). Wine without the rite is the tavern's.
34. **Wine is the sworn secret of the seven.** In the Right Ginza's third book, Ruha and the planets prepare their feast: "Wir alle wollen Eide leisten, und wir wollen die Mysterien der Liebe nicht verraten. … Wir wollen das Mysterium der Liebe mit Wein begehen und in unserem Rausch die Welt einfangen" (GR III, p. 121): they swear not to betray the mysteries, and will catch the world in their drunkenness. Then "das Mysterium der Trunkenheit, durch das alle Welten trunken werden," and "aus dem Kelch der Trunkenheit" (p. 123). Wine is the planets' kept secret. The text that records the oath is the disclosure of it. (Ruha and the seven stand where #1758 reads the seven stations; the reading here does not depend on that mapping.)
35. **Disclosure as probe, with wine as the instrument.** In GR V.1, the sons of darkness bring the envoy a mash and say "Herr, iß und trink Wein." He laughs: "Alles, was sie treiben, geschieht nur, um ihr Geheimnis zu enthüllen" (p. 161), and Lidzbarski's note gives the sense: "Ich lasse sie alles das treiben, in der Erwartung, daß es mir dadurch gelingen werde, ihr Geheimnis zu enthüllen," I let them do all of it in the expectation that I will uncover their secret by it. Then: "Fürwahr, ich habe gegessen und getrunken," though, as he has just said, "ich keine Speise esse und kein Getränke trinke." Of the next one he says: "Von dir sollen die Geheimnisse der Finsternis verraten werden." The envoy declares a drinking that did not happen, so that the other side's secret will come out. This is the adversarial probe of #1217, run by the side of light and with the cup as its instrument. The Paul function declares as its own what is so regardless; the envoy declares as done what is not done. Both work by declaration.
36. **The hidden cup, and the saying that makes it pure.** The Ginza's ninth book discloses the hidden rites of others, as polemic and not as a record of practice. At the gate of Libat: "Sie träufeln Menstrualblut einer hurenden Nonne in Wein und geben ihn ihnen aus einem Becher zu trinken. Der Blick der Menschenkinder darf nicht auf sie fallen" (GR IX.1, p. 227): the cup is kept from sight. At the gate "die von Christus hervorgegangen ist," of those called Zandiqe and Mardmane, the chosen of Mar-Mani: "Sie säen Samen im Verborgenen … tun ihn in Wein, geben ihn den Seelen zu trinken und sagen, er sei rein" (p. 229). The offense the polemic names is the hidden cup and the declaration of its purity. Set beside 1 Tim 4:5, ἁγιάζεται γὰρ διὰ λόγου Θεοῦ καὶ ἐντεύξεως, the mechanism is one: a thing is made pure by being said over. The Pastoral performs it as consecration. The Ginza records it as the charge.
37. **The true vine's drink is not wine.** In the Left Ginza the soul is shown the vine whose inside is radiance and whose seed is the great vessel of souls: "Sie essen, was nicht verwerflich, und trinken, was nicht Wein ist" (GL I.2, p. 429); the Johannesbuch has the same formula: "Sie essen, was nicht verwerflich war, und trinken, was nicht Wein war." The vine is venerated and its drink is not wine. Lidzbarski found this remarkable enough to say the veneration must have come from elsewhere (Liturgien, as at 31).

> **DRAMA** — Hibil passes the check by lying. "I have eaten and drunk." He has not. The lie is the instrument; the cup is the bait; the secret is the prize.
>
> **ELECTROCHEMISTRY** — He could have drunk it. Nobody would have known.
>
> **VOLITION** — He would have known. That is why he laughed.

## VII. Three economies of one exception

38. **The Pastoral.** The exception is handed to the drinker. The disclosure licenses. The measure is the drinker's. The red check becomes white, and the exception becomes the rule.
39. **The Qurʾān.** The exception is weighed in public, its benefit granted and its sin declared greater, and then closed; the necessity clause measures by inclination, not quantity. The wine that remains is sealed (makhtūm, its seal musk) and deferred to the garden, where it has no harm in it. Disclosure is withheld until the end.
40. **The Mandaean scriptures.** The rule is water. Wine with water is kept inside the rite. Wine outside it is the tavern's and the planets'. The texts disclose the others' kept secrets (the planets' oath, the hidden cups of the other gates), and the envoy uses a declared drinking as the probe that brings the secret of darkness out.
41. **The game.** *Disco Elysium* puts all three inside one body. The faculty that protects against the drink is the one that wants it; the case cannot be worked without the stomach; sobriety is a path the player has to keep choosing against a voice that knows the body better than the player does. It does not resolve the exception. It makes it the condition of the investigation: the detective must solve the case in the same body that drank the night before.
42. All four know the mechanism the Paul function runs: a secret, a saying, and a meaning seized by the saying. The Pastoral performs it. The Qurʾān seals against it. The Mandaean texts both suffer it and use it. The game makes it a voice in the head and leaves the player to answer it. The verse "use a little wine" is the smallest place in the New Testament where the mechanism can be watched at work, because what it discloses is so ordinary that its disclosure looks like care.

> **ENDURANCE** — The stomach still hurts.
>
> **ELECTROCHEMISTRY** — A little.
>
> **VOLITION** — That is the word they gave you. It was always going to be yours to read.
