Record semantics

Every exposed record's dc:description carries its audit capsule: severity, declared defects, and repair status from the registry-wide pre-registration audit. Records under active restoration are withheld until restored; declared absences (Lacuna Marks) are exposed as part of the archive's record. Selective harvesting by datestamp is fully supported — restored records enter the feed by datestamp bump.

Native persistent identifiers are AXN (content-derived, six-glyph checksum); legacy DOIs resolve via the DOI resolution index. Conformance: passes the openarchives.org OAI-PMH validator (2026-08-02).
