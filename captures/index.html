Capture Registry — withdrawn

EA-WG-CAPTURES-01 · withdrawn from publication 2026-08-12 · not deleted
This dataset has been withdrawn by its author. It should not be cited, harvested, or relied upon in any version published between 2026-08-11 and 2026-08-12. Earlier versions are also affected in part and are under review.

Why

The Capture Registry records machine-composed answers about a body of work, and its value depends entirely on the distinction between what a machine composed and what a reader wrote about it. In a series of automated repair passes on 2026-08-11 and 2026-08-12, that distinction was broken. Quoted passages were extracted from analyst descriptions and seated in the registry as though they were verbatim machine transcripts. An audit against independent copies of the same records finds:

  • 119 of 207 seated transcripts are verbatim substrings of their own entry's description field — quotations lifted out of a reading and relabelled as the thing being read.
  • 49 of those descriptions are byte-identical to analyst summaries preserved under a different field name in an earlier, independent copy of the registry, so their character as summaries is not in doubt.
  • 130 seated transcripts are shorter than the description they were taken from — fragments presented as wholes.
  • 15 entries assert a transcript and simultaneously carry a status saying no transcript was seated, the residue of one pass reversing another.
  • Every entry carried a READ-VERIFIED marker asserting a per-entry comparison that was not performed. The verification was a bulk screen of transcript openings; the marker claimed more than the act. This is the most serious defect, because it instructs a future reader not to re-check.

What is not affected

The underlying evidence is intact. Screenshots, per-capture manifests, companion transcript files, and independent copies of the registry held in three other repositories are unharmed, and every historical version of every record has been recovered into a working database for reconstruction. Nothing has been deleted. The withdrawn files are retained in the repository under quarantine/capture-registry-20260812/ so that the defective state remains auditable.

What happens next

The registry will be rebuilt record by record from the recovered sources, with the provenance of each field stated and with verification claims limited to what was actually done. It will return only when its transcripts can be distinguished from readings of them by evidence rather than by assertion.

Enquiries and any prior use of the affected versions: the author can be reached through alexanarch.org.
