What this mirror is. On 2026-06-19 the Zenodo account holding the Crimson Hexagonal Archive was terminated and 1,136 records deleted in a single sweep. This dataset preserves every layer the platform still exposed as of 2026-07-12. The chronological layer is total: microsecond removal timestamps and removed_by for all 2,027 objects — 1,915 of 2,005 staff removals landed in the first five minutes at ~6.6 objects per second, and every one of the ten pre-termination deletions was the uploader’s own curatorial act. The descriptive layer is partial by the platform’s choice, not ours: the rich embedded record JSON (creators with heteronym affiliations, abstracts, file checksums, terminal statistics) was served on tombstone pages until 2026-07-12T01:50:52Z and gone by 02:03:55Z — the surface closed mid-harvest. The 57 captures taken before that window, and the 1,079 stripped pages taken after it, are both preserved here.
Reception finding. For every deleted record, the platform continues to serve the full citation text naming every heteronym — while the works themselves are gone. The tombstone is the last surface on which the Dodecad still publishes.
