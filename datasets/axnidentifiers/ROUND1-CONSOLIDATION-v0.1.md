## PART III — DIVERGENCES → MANUS

1. **Founding price:** $25 [TECHNE] · $29 [ARCHIVE] · $49–95 [TACHYON] · $50–100 [PRAXIS] · $125 [LABOR] · $150/25-file [SOIL] · $750/500-file [SURFACE]. 2. **First-offer shape:** single work (five mantles) vs Rescue-25 multi-file [SOIL] vs 500-file corpus [SURFACE]. These can be sequenced (single → 25 → corpus) rather than chosen — but the *first* one is a ruling.
3. **Domain policy:** split .org-utility/.com-commercial [ARCHIVE · TECHNE · SOIL · SURFACE] vs .org-canonical + .com-redirect during pilot [LABOR]. LABOR's maintenance argument vs the boundary-legibility argument.
4. **Storage/custody as recurring offer at all** (open-ended obligation): TACHYON flags constitutional risk; LABOR defers; SOIL sells a *check* not custody. Ruling needed before any recurring language ships.
