datasets/prediction-index/

6 files · 15.7 MB

The archive's predictions as data: 4,394 claims (forecasts, standing claims, unbuilt tests, historical theses and priority records) read out of a defined corpus — every deposit's seated text, the own-voice fields of its two large datasets, the text its repairs added and its attachments — each registered at its earliest statement, with every later statement quoted and dated (stated, attested, interpreted, indexed), its dated revisions, its series, the mode of each speculative specification, the falsifier kind of each standing claim, and the records that claim it came true or failed, marked first-party where they are. A second reading of 40 random deposits measured recall at 0.945. The 194 subject pages that read it live at operativesemiotics.org/predictions, each claim also at its own stable route. Every build has an id (builds.json). Generated by operative-semiotics scripts/index/build_index.py; never hand-edited. Earlier passes stand beside it: prediction-ledger (falsification conditions and resolved outcomes) and prediction-registry.json (the August extraction whose counts this supersedes).
