# Back-projection for EA-HK-HISTLOGOS-01 (scope A)

The reader is given two files, 00-kernel.yaml and 01-ontology.yaml, and nothing else, and does not search or open any record. From the two files alone the reader writes, in order: (1) the framework's name, central node, founding records with their states, author and byline; (2) the claim; (3) the stations in order with their records, and the lives; (4) the spine; (5) the states on each axis with their dates and records, and which are the author's; (6) the evidence rule for biographical identity and the status of the lifespan constraint; (7) the erasure axis; (8) the current consensus genealogy of the Logos, its faces, its status on every body, and the symmetric ledger; (9) what the two files leave the reader unable to reconstruct, and which records the reader would open to reconstruct it; (10) the lines of research. The operator scores against scope A's must_survive list (threshold 0.85), and scores traversal separately: answer (9) must name records.
