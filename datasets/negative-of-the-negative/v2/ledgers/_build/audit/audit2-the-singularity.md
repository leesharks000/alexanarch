## Findings, most severe first

1. P "Non-claims:" — distortion (non-claims turned into claims). "boundary not crossed; a technological singularity not impossible". G1635-06: "The paper does not claim the boundary has been crossed." G1637-02 lists among the non-claims "that a technological singularity is impossible". Read as written, the item asserts that the boundary has *not* been crossed and that the technological singularity is *possible*. Neither source asserts either. KO S21 renders these correctly.
2. P "Sign, contested within:" + KO S20 — distortion. "closure only (#1643)". G1643-01: "In The Final Time the singularity is a point of closure…". G1643-02: "It rereads the technological singularity as an end of history, in the bad sense." Neither quote says "only" or denies the positive singularity, so the contest is built from an omission. KO S20's "The archive's statements differ" is milder, and "a separate one-line statement" is a description no quote gives.
3. KO S21 — low. "The paper states its limits" includes the #1637 non-claims (G1637-02, G1637-03). #1637 refers to The Final Time in the third person (G1637-11: "The Final Time proposes…"), so these are not the paper's own statements. Also, the G1637-02 quote is a list fragment ("that any provider intends ontological closure; …; that a technological singularity is impossible;") without its "does not claim" frame. The negation is supported by the locus and paraphrase, not by the quote text.
4. KO S12 (also KO_B) — attribution moved. "and Britannica says it could be amazing or apocalyptic, but we cannot know the details, the new era being 'too different to fit into the classical frame of good and evil'". F46's locus is "B9 Vinge", so the quote is Vinge's as reported by Britannica, but the sentence assigns it to Britannica's own voice.
5. KO S13 (also KO_B) — attribution dropped. "Britannica reports the expectation that 'in the future we will have the medical tools…'". F48's locus is "B9 Kurzweil", and the sentence does not say whose expectation it is.
6. KO S10 (also KO_B) — support, low. "and one holds that 'there is not the slightest reason…'". "one" implies one of the five critics just named, but F19's quote names no one.
7. P "Competing contingent Omegas:" — modality, low. "several futures viable from one present". G1635-24 (interpretation) says "can remain viable". The section head "Double and contingent" carries no modality marker.
