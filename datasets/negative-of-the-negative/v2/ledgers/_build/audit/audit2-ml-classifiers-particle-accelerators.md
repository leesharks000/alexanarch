## Findings, most severe first

1. KO S6 (also KO_B S6), P "Classifier:" (also P_B), P_B lede — the field's condition is dropped (moderate). "a neural network classifier reached test accuracy above 99 % for each study shift without degrading significantly over a couple of months"; P "test accuracy above 99% per shift"; P_B lede "accuracies from about 78 to above 99 percent reported in tests". F7: "**In this case**, the prediction accuracy on the test data was above 99 % for each of our study shifts." The composition drops "In this case". On the fetched page (WebFetch, JACoW TUYE4) the case is named in the sentence before: "One can train the neural network classifier on the data from the beginning of one study, and test it on the data from the end of the same study (a few hours apart)." So the >99% is a same-study, few-hours-apart result. Joined to F9's "does not degrade significantly … a couple months", it reads as general accuracy held over months. Ledger note: F7's quote should be extended to take in its antecedent sentence.
2. Whole entry — a declared contest is not carried (moderate). The row status lists "policing the term (#1436 against #1454)" as carried as contested within the pool. Neither KO nor P carries it. Q1436-04: "signal-template agnosticism must not be rendered as model independence, theory-free discovery, or unbiased search." Q1454-07: "“model-independent” is used in several senses across collider physics. The paper should not attempt to police the term." KO S10–S11 give #1436's definitions (Q1436-01) and #931's acceptance of the narrow sense, but neither the rule nor its refusal is named.
3. P "Baseline capture:" (and KO S20, low) — modality lost and a condition dropped (moderate). 4. KO S21 and P "Inversion, contested:" — one side of a contest loses its core (low–moderate).
