## Findings, most severe first

1. KO S16 — attribution moved (medium). "and a third that a heteronym must \"survive as a person\", with biography, style and plausible independence, or be a transparent pseudonym". N136-01's quote ("a heteronym must survive as a person, … otherwise it is not a heteronym but a transparent pseudonym.") does not name whose premise this is, and the ledger paraphrase says "The parent premise". In #136 the sentence is introduced as "The parent paper's central evidentiary move … is the following (parent §VIII, and throughout)", so the premise belongs to #123, Socrates as Orthonym. A "third" position on person or position is therefore #136's report of #123. That is the same paper the sentence then cites on the other side of the mask question (N123-04).
2. KO S16 — the contest is built by truncation (medium). "on the mask, one paper holds that \"A single heteronym is a mask\" and another that \"The heteronyms are not disguises; they are *operations*\"". N666-03's quote continues: "A single heteronym is a mask; a system of heteronyms is an authorial architecture." N123-04 speaks of heteronyms in a configuration. On a system of heteronyms the two quotes agree, and the disagreement comes from cutting #666's second clause. N348-01 ("Heteronyms … are not pseudonyms, masks, or personas"), already cited in S15, is the quote that actually opposes #666's first clause. The asterisks in "*operations*" are markdown carried into the prose.
3. P "Pseudepigrapha, contested within:" + KO S19 "against that line" — contest not supported by the cited quote (medium). P: "set apart by concealment (#123); listed as an inherited named position (#68)". KO: "the Secret Name, against that line, lists an \"Inherited\" position, in which a disciple writes under a teacher's name, the Pauline school among its cases". N68-02's quote ("**Inherited** — a disciple writes under a teacher's name (Second Temple scribal workshops; the Johannine community; the Pauline school)") says nothing about pseudepigrapha or heteronymy. The deposit's own list, two lines below, gives "**Heteronymic** — a created name functioning independently" as a separate kind. Both #68 and #123 therefore place the disciple-under-teacher's-name case outside the heteronym. What the quotes do support is a difference over concealment (#123) against a legitimate named position (#68). Neither "pseudepigrapha" nor "against that line" is in #68's quote. (The row status lists "pseudepigrapha #123/#68" as contested.)
4. KO S6 (also KO_B) + P "Counts:" (also P_B) — hedge dropped (medium). KO: "on Zenith's reckoning, more than one hundred fictitious authors, about thirty of whom signed a significant work". P: "over a hundred, three full-fledged (Zenith)". F45: "If we include his childhood riddlers and humorists, Pessoa created more than one hundred fictitious authors in whose name he wrote or at least planned to write something." The count is conditional, and it includes planned authors. Both renderings drop the condition and the "at least planned" scope.
5. KO S22 — modality (low). "On these corpora a measure finds … and that a heteronymic configuration can instantiate a master–student relation, Pessoa being \"the existence proof\"". N1570-05 is an interpretation ("**Master-student and heteronymic configuration are not competing relation-types.** A heteronymic configuration can **itself instantiate** …"), but it sits under "a measure finds" alongside the documented findings N1570-01/03/04 and N1572-04. So it reads as measured.
6. KO S20 + P "No floor:" — hypothesis grade and an uncarried contest (low). KO: "another paper names \"one self-dividing maker\" as the production model the corpus is consistent with". P: "one hidden person unasserted (#136), one self-dividing maker named (#1658)". N1658-02 is a hypothesis. KO keeps the quote's "consistent with" but adds no grade. P drops "consistent with", so "named" stands without a hedge. The row status lists "maker-count #136/#1658" as contested, but neither unit says so. The N1658-02 quote says "the corpus" without naming it, while #1658 ¶27 identifies it as "The Platonic, Theophrastan and Aristotelian corpus". Set beside #136, the KO sentence reads it as #136's "founding corpus".
7. P "Defined:" — scope dropped and wording garbled (low). "a heteronym as an independent authorial figure written from a constructed position." N667-01: "A heteronym, in Pessoa's sense, is an independent authorial figure … — the writing emerges from a constructed position". Without "in Pessoa's sense", the definition of Pessoa's category becomes a general one. In the quote the writing emerges from the position, while the item says the figure is "written from" it. KO S11 renders it correctly.
8. KO S15 + P lede — the archive's definition presented as unified (low). KO S15: "In the archive's own theory the heteronym is defined as a function"; P lede: "The archive further defines it as an authorial function." N96-02 ("A heteronym is a person.") is cited in the very next sentence. KO S16 then frames #96 as one answer to "what that function is", which places a person-position under the function definition. (The #96 text goes on: "Not a persona. … Not a 'functional equivalent' …".) P's section head "Function, contested within" partly corrects the lede.
9. KO S16 + P "Person or position:" — scope truncated (low). "a registry that \"The heteronyms are not separate persons; they are differentiated positions\"". N90-02 continues "through which Lee's bearing is articulated". It concerns the Dodecad's own heteronyms, while N96-02 states a general ontology. Cutting the clause makes the contest look broader than the quotes are.
10. KO S21 — modality (low). "Pound the middle term where the mask becomes a position the poet inhabits". N1560-02 is an interpretation, but the appositive states it as fact with no grade. "seats … as \"one practice at three stages\"" (N1560-01, interpretation) is acceptable as the seat's own act.
11. P "Ethical practice:" — definition halved, wrong section (low). "defined as sustaining a named function without falsely reducing it to a civil identity". N940-01 continues "and without falsely presenting it as an independent civil person". The item also sits under the head "Function, contested within", but no cited quote contests the ethics. KO S17 renders it whole.
12. KO S14, S17, S19 — names not in the quotes (low/informational). The deposit titles "The Meta-Heteronym", "EA-HETERONYMY-01", "Socrates as Orthonym" and "the Secret Name" are correct per data/registry.json, but no cited quote contains them (N682-01 says "This essay"; N940, N123 and N68 carry no titles).
13. KO S15 — construction variant (low). "heteronyms as \"authorial functions\" that are no pseudonyms, masks or personas". This negation-plus-affirmation pattern is the one the rule targets. Stating Y alone ("authorial functions") with N348-01 quoted would avoid it.
14. KO S10 (also KO_B) — support, low. "strong heteronymic qualities can be discerned in W. B. Yeats, in works some of which came about twenty years before Pessoa began his career, through the fictional names Red Hanrahan, Owen Aherne and Michael Robartes". F26 gives the qualities and the twenty years but no names. F29 ("Scanning the collected poems of Yeats, one comes across three fictional names") gives the names but does not tie them to the earlier works or to the qualities. The "through" is the composer's.
