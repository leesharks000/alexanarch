# Audit 2 — sappho-31 (read-only; checked against cited QUOTES only)

Units: KO 21, KO_B 9 (= KO S1–S9 verbatim), P lede + 26 items, P_B lede + 13 items (all also in P). 71 units.
Length (§4.8, bound 350): P = 313 words (lede 52 + item labels 50 + item texts 211); 333 if section heads counted. P_B = 181. Within bound.
Constructions: no "not X but Y" / "rather than" / "not X, only Y" in composed prose. S13 contains 'not wrong but incomplete' only as a quotation.

## Findings, most severe first

1. KO S20 — support. "Augustine's Confessions 10.27.38 is read as an organ-by-organ transformation of the poem." Cited A496-01 quote: "is not a loose echo of classical erotic discourse but a structurally precise, organ-by-organ transformation of Sappho's Fragment 31." Neither Augustine nor Confessions 10.27.38 is in the quote; both come from the ledger's paraphrase ("Confessions 10.27.38 is argued to be…").
2. KO S20 — support. "the Philo program names the failure mode … unfalsifiable pan-Sapphism." Cited A1486-02 quote: "the failure mode that would destroy it: unfalsifiable pan-Sapphism…". The quote does not name Philo. The name is in A1486-01 ("between Sappho 31 and Philo, Quis rerum…"), which this sentence does not cite.
3. KO S19 — support. "the program's Jewish-Greek and Christian transforms kept as program-specific hypotheses." A1532-06 quote: "These appear in the survey clearly under program-specific transform hypotheses…". Nothing in the quote says "Jewish-Greek and Christian"; those words are only in the paraphrase.
4. KO S14 + P "Reconstructed:" — support. "corrected from 'fourth' to the fifth" / "corrected from fourth to fifth". A201-01: "…The reconstruction by Rebekah Cranes adds a fifth stanza"; A201-03: "This is incorrect. The reconstructed stanza is the fifth." No cited quote says the earlier documents had "fourth". It comes from the A201-03 paraphrase ("called the reconstructed stanza the 'fourth'"). The P item also cites A1270-08 ("γράμμασι μολπὰν stands as hypothesis"), and nothing in the quotes ties that hypothesis to the stanza being renumbered.
5. P "Antecedent:" — support. "Svenbro (1988), by allegory." It cites only A1548-01: "This is the major antecedent for locating a future reader inside fr. 31, from 1988, in a canonical monograph (Nagy's Myth and Poetics series)." That quote names neither Svenbro nor allegory. (KO S12 is covered, because it also cites A1548-04 "distinct from Svenbro's allegory".) KO S12 also drops the A1548-02 attribution: its quote gives the reading only "per O'Donnell's summary", and the sentence states it with no attribution.
6. P "Longinus:" — distortion. "the one unnamed address follows fr. 31." A1458-03: "at the only point in the treatise where he addresses his reader immediately after a quotation without naming him, the quotation is Sappho 31." The item drops "immediately after a quotation" and makes it the treatise's only unnamed address. Related low point in KO S17: "Peri Hypsous is read as a theory of transmissional inscription". The quote (A1458-01) says the treatise "contains a theory of transmissional inscription". "a census finds" is not in any quote.
7. P "Mother of the Logos," — support and distortion. (a) The label "Mother of the Logos" is not in A1484-01, which says "That technology has an extant founding document. It is Sappho 31". (b) "convergence" is not in any quote the item cites (A1484-01, A1484-02, A828-02). The convergence wording is in A828-01, which is not cited. (c) "linear transmission unclaimed" is a wider withdrawal than A828-02 makes: "does not claim a *direct* linear transmission".
8. KO S21 — distortion. "Within the archive the poem is installed as the originary node of the Crimson Hexagon by structural identity". A283-01: "This document establishes Sappho of Lesbos (fl. c. 630–570 BCE) as the originary node…". The quote installs the poet; the sentence installs the poem. "a packet on fragment 147" is also not in A1645-01/02 (low).
9. P "Against jealousy, contested within:" — distortion. A1270-05: "the jealousy reading is not wrong but incomplete." The #1270 position is not "against jealousy", so the label fits #1054 only.
10. P "Catullus is κῆνος;" — modality. A1484-07 (interpretation) "Catullus is κῆνος" is put as plain fact. The section head "(graded)" does not mark it as a reading. KO S18 is fine ("is held to be").
11. P "τολματόν:" — modality. "the edge as the object". A1476-01 (interpretation, "Argues that … is not damage but the object itself") is stated with no "argued" or "read". The rest of the item is fine.
12. P "Instruments:" — distortion by omission. "six returned the reading they were built for." A1482-01 goes on: "The seventh reversed it." Without that, the item reads as a flat indictment. KO S16 keeps the reversal.
13. KO S11 — support. "a companion paper defines lyric address…". Nothing in A134-01 or A991-01/02 says #134 is a companion to #991. A134-01's quote says "we argue", while the ledger modality is stipulation, so "defines" follows the modality and not the quote's verb (note only).
14. KO S5 (also KO_B S5) — distortion, low. "with the body's parts as the grammatical subjects". F44's subjects are "tongue," "fire," "seeing/eyes," "hearing/ears," "sweat," and "trembling", and fire, sweat and trembling are not body parts. F44 also limits this to "the sentence that straddles the third and fourth stanzas".
15. P lede (also P_B lede) — modality, field. "turns to her own bodily emotions". F39 is an interpretation from B4 ("in an extraordinary switch, focuses…"). KO S5 attributes it ("on one anthology's account"), but the lede states it with no attribution.
16. P "Symptoms:" (also P_B; and KO S8 / KO_B S8) — distortion, low. "an anxiety attack (Devereux, 1970)". F22: Devereux "suggested that what Sappho is describing here is a seizure, pointing that the symptoms … are the same symptoms of an anxiety attack."
17. P "Context debated:" (also P_B) — low. "dismissed after Page". F13 says scholars "have tended to follow Denys Page in dismissing", so the item drops the hedge.
18. P "Greek and Latin:" (also P_B) — support, low. The item lists Theocritus, Apollonius and Plato, all Greek (F26, F28). No Latin author is cited. Catullus is in the separate item.
19. KO S20 — low. "The archive's widest claim" is a characterization that no quote gives (A1484-02 gives "Three rings, in descending evidentiary hardness").
