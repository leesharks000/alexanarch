## Findings, most severe first

1. KO S18 — distortion (contradicts the quote). "Platform and AI capitalism is stated, under failure conditions it names, as a historically novel regime, the industrialization of semiotic control". E550-01: "It is not surveillance capitalism, platform capitalism, data colonialism, … — though it inherits from all of these. It is a historically novel regime: the industrialization of semiotic control." The quote says the regime is *not* platform capitalism. (The paraphrase: "What AI platforms build is stated as a novel regime".) Modality: E550-01 is a hypothesis, and "is stated … under failure conditions" signals that it can be falsified but never says "hypothesis".
2. KO S15 — distortion (a conditional applied as a verdict). "Grady's redefinition, which removes authority, allocation and scarcity, is read as performing ideology". E533-05 (stipulation) is a conditional diagnostic: "If the framework redefines political economy to exclude power, it is performing ideology". E533-03 says the redefinition removes "authority, allocation, and scarcity", not "power". The sentence closes the gap between the two without any quote doing so.
3. KO S21 + P "Measured:" — support, ambiguous in context. KO: "an AI Overview is recorded as having displayed the critical framework until the morning of 5 January 2026, after which 'the political economy has been removed'". P: "the frame removed, 5 January 2026". The quotes (E247-02, E247-01) do not name the query. In an entry on political economy, a reader takes it as the Overview for "political economy", but the ledger paraphrase of E247-01 says it was the Overview for "semantic economy". "after which" also orders §II.4 after §II.5, which the quotes do not show.
4. P "Revived:" (also P_B) — distortion. "the 1970s, OPEC". F48: "An oil embargo imposed by OPEC in the 1970s showed the world that nations without a powerful military presence could still strike at other nations". F48 is not about the field's revival. The revival claim is F45, which does not mention OPEC.
5. P "Limits:" — support. "its own disembodiment risk". The item cites E727-07 and E727-09. The risk is E727-08 ("risks reproducing the blindness it critiques"), which is not cited, and "disembodiment" is only in E727-08's locus and paraphrase.
6. KO S13 — distortion, low-medium. "A further position keeps that definition, defining political economy as the study of how power, institutions, law…". E727-02 gives a different definition from the field's F1, F15 and F27 (power, institutions, law, labor, markets, money → value), so "keeps that definition" is unsupported.
7. KO S14 — low. "its packet describes itself as a machine-readable bridge that uses the established field as parent object without trying to make the term its own". The second half is E726-01, which is #726 describing the packet ("it did not try to make 'political economy' ours"), not the packet describing itself. The same merge appears in P "A bridge:".
8. KO S17 — low. "the Semantic Economy as the political economy of meaning, Register 4". E18-01 names Register 4 as "the political economy of meaning". E1644-01 calls the Semantic Economy "the political economy of meaning". No quote says the Semantic Economy is Register 4. KO S18 has a related low point: "its routing term G" — the quote (E1666-04) does not say what G is.
9. KO S22 — low. "a first use given as 2025-01". E704-02's quote is only `first_use: "2025-01"`, with nothing saying what was first used.
10. P "Critique:" — low. "Kritik" is in no cited quote.
