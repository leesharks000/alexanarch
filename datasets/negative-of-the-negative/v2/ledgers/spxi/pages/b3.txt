SPXI (Semantic Packet for eXchange & Indexing) is a formal protocol specification designed for the durable inscription and structural defense of digital entities and technical terms within AI-mediated knowledge and summarizer layers. [1, 2, 3] 
Core Concepts and Purpose
* Entity Inscription: It provides a framework for publishers and developers to durably anchor definitions, concepts, and metadata so they resist "entity dissolution" or misinterpretation during AI summarization. [1, 2] 
* Beyond GEO: It functions as a broader retrieval architecture containing Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) methods as a proper subset, adding explicit ontological-layer entity construction. [1] 
* Website Implementation: Standards like the SPXI for Websites Protocol define technical stacks to apply these semantic inscription rules directly to web platforms. [1] 
(Note: "SPXI" is also used as a ticker symbol for financial products like the BetaPro S&P 500 Daily Inverse ETF, and refers to a Single-Pixel X-ray Imager in scientific imaging hardware). [1, 2, 3]
