About
The Data Provenance Initiative builds public measurement infrastructure for understanding AI data, model ecosystems, and real-world AI use.
We are a volunteer collective of AI researchers conducting large-scale audits of the systems and resources that shape modern AI. Our work began with training data provenance: tracing datasets from origin to creation, cataloging sources, licenses, creators, and documentation practices. It now also covers web consent, open model markets, concentration of power, and how people use AI systems in practice.
