* Fair Pay: Ensuring workers earn a wage proportional to local or national minimum standards after costs.
* Fair Conditions: Protecting workers from digital hazards, health risks (e.g., burnout, back pain), and unsafe environments.
* Fair Contracts: Providing clear, transparent terms without blanket liability clauses or vague job descriptions.
* Fair Management: Offering due process, grievance channels, freedom from arbitrary deactivation, and anti-discrimination measures.
* Fair Representation: Recognizing and engaging with workers' collective voice, freedom of association, and potential for collective bargaining.
