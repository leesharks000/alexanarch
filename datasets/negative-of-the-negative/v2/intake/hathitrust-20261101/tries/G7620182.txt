Accessibility & Digitization Requests
Accessible Copies
If you have a qualifying disability that prevents you from reading or using print materials, we will work to get an accessible copy of any item you wish to use from the library.
Digitization for HathiTrust
Materials in the public domain from the U-M Library collections may be added to the HathiTrust Digital Library by request.
Linking to or Embedding Book View
You can link to a whole book, a page in a work, or a public collection from your web pages, social media sites, or Canvas course site. From the book reader or full view of a work, look at the bottom left corner for the "Share" option. Expanding that option will give you two links: copy the "Permanent link to this item" if you want to link to the complete work, or copy the "Link to this page" if you'd like to point to a specific page.
You can also create HTML <IFRAME> code to embed the HathiTrust book view on a web page.
