Welcome to Erythros Press & Media, LLC

Marxists Internet Archive Publications

Since 2009, Erythros Press and Media, LLC has distributed works published by Marxists Internet Archive Publications worldwide. Most publications are sold through this site, but many are also available through our seller accounts on Abe Books and Amazon. Proceeds from the sale of MIAP books go to Marxists Internet Archive to ensure that site’s continued operation and improvement.

Marxists Internet Archive is not affiliated with any authors or products that are not published by Marxists Internet Archive Publications. The association of MIAP by and through the business relationship of Erythros Press and Media, LLC does not imply that the MIA collective approves of or agrees with the positions or conclusions of authors published by Erythros Press and Media, LLC.
