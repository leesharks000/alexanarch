
The Classical Receptions Journal (CRJ) is a peer-reviewed academic journal published by Oxford University Press. It is a primary venue for scholarship examining how ancient Greek and Roman texts, ideas, and material culture have been received, translated, adapted, and reinterpreted from antiquity to the modern day.

The Classical Receptions Journal (CRJ) has emerged as a key forum for exploring how artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems recontextualize antiquity, formalizing this field of study through critical peer-reviewed scholarship and strict academic oversight.

Rather than viewing AI purely as a data-processing utility, contributors to CRJ evaluate it as a dynamic "horizon of reception" that actively shapes how the public and scholars perceive the ancient world. The journal's major contributions to the study of AI-mediated classics center around several distinct themes: 
1. Conceptualizing AI as a "Horizon of Reception"
Scholars publishing in CRJ have begun to interrogate the mechanisms of Large Language Models (LLMs) and search-engine AI products (such as Google’s AI Overviews) as standardizers of classical knowledge. Research highlights how AI acts as an interpretive filter. When an AI system aggregates, condenses, and outputs summaries of complex ancient philosophies, mythological tales, or historical events, it creates a new layer of mediation. CRJ scholarship maps how these systems strip away classical nuance, effectively becoming the modern gatekeepers of how antiquity is digested by the public. 
2. Differentiating Machine Recombination from Human Intertextuality
The journal has been a focal point for debates regarding AI creativity vs. classical reception theory. Authors draw parallels and contrasts between how LLMs function and how ancient authors practiced imitatio (imitation) or intertextuality. While an AI model generates text by identifying and recombining existing linguistic patterns in a repository of pre-existing classical texts, human reception transforms those inherited forms to open entirely new imaginative and subversive possibilities. CRJ helps frame the theoretical boundaries of what constitutes genuine "reception" versus algorithmic prediction.
