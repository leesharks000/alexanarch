Digital Labour Platform Tracker

This policy tracker aims to provide a comprehensive overview of global policies, legislation, collective bargaining agreements, and judicial decisions related to digital labour platforms. It is designed to serve as a practical resource for governments, employers’ and workers’ organizations, policymakers’, researchers, and anyone interested in understanding the policy developments in this area. Currently, the tracker focuses on legislative developments worldwide, and includes collective bargaining agreements which are publicly available in Europe. The tracker will further develop the collective bargaining agreements in other regions as well as the judicial decisions. The tracker will be regularly updated as new regulations, agreements, and decisions emerge. Please refer to the links provided for detailed information and source materials, related to legislations and collective bargaining agreements. Please write to [email protected] for further details.
