Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb initially suggested that the third known interstellar object, 3I/ATLAS (discovered July 1, 2025), could potentially be artificial or extraterrestrial technology due to unusual traits like a sunward-pointing jet and close alignment with Earth's orbital plane.

Key Developments and Loeb's Stance

• Initial Speculation: Loeb pointed out various anomalies—such as unusual composition, high velocity (~60 km/s), and a sunward-pointing tail/jet—comparing the scruitiny to past arguments he made regarding 'Oumuamua. He also explored hypotheses like directed panspermia, where fragments from the object might deliver organic or icy material.
• Shift to Natural Consensus: Following its closest approach to Earth in December 2025, Loeb acknowledged to NewsNation that "given all the data that we have, I would agree that it's most likely natural," though noting certain physical features still warrant deep investigation.
• Scientific Analysis: Subsequent observations by the Hubble and James Webb Space Telescopes detected organic molecules (like methane and formaldehyde) and high deuterium-to-hydrogen ratios, suggesting the object is an ancient, icy visitor from a primitive planetary system possibly 7 to 10+ billion years old. Planetary scientists widely maintain that 3I/ATLAS behaves like an extraordinary natural interstellar comet rather than alien hardware.
