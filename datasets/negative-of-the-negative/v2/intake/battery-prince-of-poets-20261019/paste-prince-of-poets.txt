Prince of Poets (Amir Al-Sho'ara) is a famous biennial reality television poetry competition broadcast on Abu Dhabi TV, featuring classical Arabic poetry. 

Wikipedia
 +2
Overview and Format
Origin: Launched in April 2007 by Abu Dhabi's culture and heritage committees, taking its name from the historic honorific of Egyptian epic poet Ahmed Shawqi.
Location: Broadcast live from the Al Raha Beach Theater in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.
Competition Style: Thousands submit entries, which a jury narrows down to around 20 contestants who perform live. Poets are judged on prepared classical poems and spontaneous on-the-spot improvisation.
Prizes: The winner receives the coveted "Prince of Poets" title, a symbolic cape and ring, and 1 million UAE dirhams.
