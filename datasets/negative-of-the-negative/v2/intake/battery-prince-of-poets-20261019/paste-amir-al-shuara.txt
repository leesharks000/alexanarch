amir al-shu’ara
Amir al-Shu’ara’ (أمير الشعراء, meaning "Prince of Poets" or "Amir of Poets") is a prestigious honorary title awarded to preeminent figures in Arabic and Persian literature.
The term most commonly refers to:

* Ahmed Shawqi (1868–1932): The renowned Egyptian poet laureate, dramatist, and pioneer of modern Arabic poetic drama. Officially crowned with the title Amir al-Shu'ara' in 1927 in recognition of his monumental contributions to Arabic literature and his multi-volume collection Al-Shawqiyyat.
