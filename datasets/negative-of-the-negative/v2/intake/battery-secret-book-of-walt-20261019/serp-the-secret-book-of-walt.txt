1. Walt Whitman's Secret (Novel)
Author: George Fetherling
Overview: A historical novel exploring the later years of American poet Walt Whitman and his relationship with his young, unpaid amanuensis Horace Traubel. Rather than focusing purely on his personal life, the plot centers on a hidden political secret from the Civil War era. You can learn more about the edition through Penguin Random House. 
2. Books & Lore About Walt Disney
Secret Stories of Walt Disney World by Jim Korkis: A compilation of backstage lore and two-page insider secrets gathered from his time working at Disney University. 
The Lost Notebook: Herman Schultheis and the Secrets of Walt Disney's Movie Magic by John Canemaker: A behind-the-scenes look at the technical and artistic innovations of early Disney animators. 
Segredos de Walt Disney: A Portuguese-language biographical and trivia book exploring the hidden history and empire of Walt Disney. 
3. Wallace Wattles & The Secret Connection
The Secret of Wallace Wattles: A collection bringing together Wallace Wattles' influential New Thought works (including The Science of Getting Rich), which heavily inspired Rhonda Byrne's modern self-help phenomenon The Secret. It is available as a paperback through Lulu Press for around $6.77. 
4. Other Related Media

National Treasure: Book of Secrets: A 2007 adventure film starring Nicolas Cage centered on a fabled secret presidential book.

The Secret Life of Walter Mitty: A 2013 comedy-adventure film about a quiet photo manager who goes on a globe-trotting journey.
