AI and Faith brings the wisdom of the world’s religions to the AI ethics conversation.

Our Mission

Empowering faith traditions, technologists, and civil society to cultivate wisdom, advance human flourishing and dignity, and shape technology responsibly in the age of artificial intelligence.

 
 

Our Vision 

AI and Faith is a global network of faith leaders and communities, scholars, technologists, ethicists, educators, and partner organizations, working for a future in which artificial intelligence serves humankind under five strategic pillars.

  1. Wisdom and Human Formation
  2. Education and Public Understanding
  3. Interfaith Dialogue and Community
  4. Research and Thought Leadership
  5. Responsible Innovation
The world’s great religions offer ancient wisdom that can help us navigate those changes.

 

Each of these issues is, at its root, deeply moral. And for the 85 percent of the world’s population who claim a religious affiliation, these issues are deeply spiritual as well. AI and Faith believes the world’s religions offer ancient wisdom that should guide the development and use of AI technology in contemporary society.

So we bring people together to talk about it.

We encourage, connect and equip tech creators, academic researchers, ethicists, theologians and other related professionals to engage the world in the moral and ethical issues around artificial intelligence and related technologies. We want to shape the discussion in ways that are deeply thoughtful and life-affirming.
