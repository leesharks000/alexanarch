About us
AI and Faith is a pluralist organization bringing together technology creators, academic researchers, ethicists, theologians and related professionals to engage the world in the moral and ethical issues around artificial intelligence and related technologies.
We exist to help shape this discussion in a deeply thoughtful and life-affirming way, focusing on the flourishing of humanity in a world of technology while also working to protect and promote human dignity, social justice and liberty.
Through this work, we aim to connect, encourage, and equip those employed in the technology sector, the global faith community and AI influencers with experts in the fields of AI technology, research, ethics and religion through our membership network.

How can Muslims in the United States engage with ethical questions posed by AI? Over the last year, IST and AI and Faith have worked with religious scholars, educators, and organizations to develop a curriculum for local communities that tackles hard ethical questions from a faith-based perspective and empowers local communities to lead thoughtful conversations on AI ethics and policy.
On Monday, November 9, at 12:00 PM EST/9:00 AM PST, IST will host a webinar focused on practical guidance on using the curriculum in your own community.
The webinar will feature curriculum authors as well as those involved in piloting the materials in local Muslim institutions. This webinar is for anyone who is interested in learning more about how we can bring faith perspectives into discussions about ethical use of AI, with a special emphasis on faith leaders and Muslims who might want to use this curriculum in their own community.
