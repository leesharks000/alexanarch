==Human Authored==
The guild's '''Human Authored''' project offers authors the opportunity to certify that:{{quote|the text of the book was written by a human and not generated by AI, with the exception of minimal uses such as AI applications that check spelling and grammar or for research.}} The certification is available for books published in the United States, and is free for members of the guild and available for a fee ($10 {{as of|2026|lc=yes}}) for non-members. Books with this certification are listed in an open-access  database which can be checked online.<ref>{{cite web |title=Human Authored Certification |url=https://authorsguild.org/human-authored/ |website=The Authors Guild |access-date=27 October 2026}}</ref> Kit Eaton, writing in ''[[Inc. (magazine)|Inc.]]'' in October 2024 ahead of the launch of the project, suggested that the certification would be akin to food being labeled "[[Organic certification|organic]]".<ref name=eaton>{{Cite news |last=Eaton |first=Kit |date=October 8, 2024 |title=Here's Why 'Human Authored' Will Become the 'Artisanally Crafted' Pitch of the AI Age |url=https://www.inc.com/kit-eaton/heres-why-human-authored-will-become-the-artisanally-crafted-pitch-of-the-ai-age/90985727 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260112233627/https://www.inc.com/kit-eaton/heres-why-human-authored-will-become-the-artisanally-crafted-pitch-of-the-ai-age/90985727 |archive-date=January 12, 2026 |access-date=October 27, 2026 |work=Inc |language=en-US}}</ref>
