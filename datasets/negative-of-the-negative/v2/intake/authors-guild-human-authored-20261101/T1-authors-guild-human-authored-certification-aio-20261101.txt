Core Rules and Scope

* Allowed AI Uses: Minimal, auxiliary tools like spelling and grammar checks, research, outlining, indexing, or brainstorming do not disqualify a work.
* Excluded Content: Audiobooks, translations, and non-book formats require separate filings or are excluded.
* Limits: Authors are capped at 10 title certifications per year without special permission.
