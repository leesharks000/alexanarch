Vesuvius Challenge is an open science research organization reading the carbonized Herculaneum scrolls without unrolling them. Anyone can help: we run a $2M competition, with prizes every month.

Our story​

All milestones

Vesuvius erupts, burying a villa and its library of papyrus scrolls under twenty meters of ash.

The scrolls are carbonized by the heat, but also preserved. For centuries, as almost every other ancient text decays, the library of the Villa of the Papyri waits underground, intact.
