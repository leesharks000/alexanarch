 International Institute of Social History (IISH)

 The IISG is a leading international archive and research institute in the field of social history, the history of labour and social movements.

 Since its foundation in 1935, the institute has built up one of the world's largest socio-economic history collections and conducted research on social inequality, globalisation, the environment and migration.

 The IISG cooperates with the Huygens Institute and the Meertens Institute in the Academy's Humanities Cluster (HuC) .
