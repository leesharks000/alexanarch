Semantic liquidation is a theoretical concept from

the Semantic Economy framework describing how platform operators extract and transform human meaning into monetizable assets, stripping away its original context, authorship, and intent. It acts as a primary mechanism of value extraction in digital and artificial intelligence platforms. 2

The Core Mechanism

Semantic liquidation operates through specific

transformations in how language and meaning are handled by technology: 2 e Dissolution: Situated, contextual human meaning is broken down into easily retrievable, discrete units (e.g., data points, training text snippets). 2 e Erasure: The original authors or the somatic, emotional labor used to generate the meaning are obfuscated. ?
