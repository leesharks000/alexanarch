 Home About About The HathiTrust Research Center HathiTrust Research Center (HTRC) enables computational analysis of works in the HathiTrust Digital Library (HTDL) to facilitate non-profit research and educational uses of the collection. HTRC, which is co-located at Indiana University and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, engages in research and development for computational text analysis of massive digital libraries. Leveraging data storage and computational infrastructure at Indiana University and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, the Center creates and maintains a suite of tools and services for text-based, data-driven research--such as HTRC Algorithms and Data Capsule--and engages in cutting-edge research on large-scale data analysis. HTRC operates under a non-consumptive research paradigm: HTRC makes available the collection for computational analysis, while remaining clearly within the bounds of the fair use rights courts have recognized as applying to text analysis. The Center is committed to breaking new ground in the areas of non-consumptive text mining, allowing scholars to fully utilize content of the HathiTrust Library. Relationship to HathiTrust HathiTrust is a partnership of academic and research institutions, offering a collection of millions of titles digitized from libraries around the world. The HathiTrust Digital Library (HTDL) is a digital preservation repository and highly functional access platform that continues to grow as HathiTrust partners, primarily academic libraries in the United States, contribute newly digitized content. Individual access and preservation are important concerns for HathiTrust. It allows users to search for and build collections of digitized works, and to read those in the public domain. The Research Center’s focus is on the aggregate strengths: what can we learn from so many books? Digitization has enabled large-scale questions that we couldn’t ask before, and the Research Center is here to help you ask them while working within the restrictions of intellectual property law. Future of the HathiTrust Research Center In late October 2024, HathiTrust Executive Director Mike Furlough announced that HathiTrust will cease data access and funding the HathiTrust Research Center (HTRC) at the end of 2026. For now, HTRC will continue its current services and support, but eventually, some of these services and data (still to be determined) may be retired or integrated into the general HathiTrust Digital Library. For up-to-date information on the transition and sunsetting process, please visit our HTRC Transition Guide . After 2026, The HTRC team plans to continue its own research agenda in text-data mining, cultural analytics with large text corpora and cultural heritage collections, feature extraction, and non-consumptive research methods. We also hope to continue providing services, data sets, and community support to the research community. While these announced changes will be challenging for HTRC, they also present many exciting opportunities, such as working with new partners and expanding the focus of our work beyond the HathiTrust corpus. Over the next two years, in addition to assisting HathiTrust with its transition, we will be working diligently to develop a new identity and organizational structure for our future work. We welcome ideas and feedback from our many users, collaborators, and funders as we plan this exciting future. For more information on our research output and impact see the links below: HTRC Publications and Presentations HTRC Research Impact Other Services Advanced Collaborative Support: Assisting on specialized questions HTRC Advanced Collaborative Support offers specialized expertise, developer time, and compute resources to researchers who apply for and are awarded support. Scholarly Commons: Training researchers HTRC Scholarly Commons provides training and researcher support for those teaching with and using HTRC. Affiliates of the Scholarly Commons are available for workshops and webinars, and they will also consult about specific scholarly projects or pedagogical applications. Monthly Maintenance HTRC Analytics is inaccessible the second week of every month, from 7 a.m. ET Sunday through at least 12 p.m. ET Monday during routine, planned maintenance. HTRC services are unavailable during this time, including the HTRC Analytics site and Data Capsules environment. Please refer to the documentation for upcoming dates, and directions for how capsule users can ensure their data is safely stored during maintenance.
