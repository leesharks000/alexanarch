Español abajo. In Spanish below.
Labor Notes Conferences are the biggest gatherings of grassroots union activists, union reformers, and all-around troublemakers out there! Register by March 1 for a big discount. Don’t miss a weekend of inspiration, education, and agitation.
Join thousands of union members, officers, and labor activists who are on the front lines in our workplaces and our communities, organizing new workers and agitating together. Meet troublemakers from across the country and around the world.
More than 200 meetings and workshops include creative organizing tactics, beating apathy, running for local union office, winning contract campaigns, bargaining over technology, assertive grievance handling, and reviving the strike.
Organize with others in your union, industry, or campaign. Face-to-face meetings to share tactics and swap notes are the heart of the Labor Notes Conference. Join an existing industry, union, or caucus meeting—or contact us to set one up.
WHEN: Friday, April 19, 9 a.m. to Sunday, April 21, 3 p.m.
WHERE: Hyatt Regency O'Hare, Rosemont (Chicago), Illinois

Featuring the Great Labor Arts Exchange!
Every year the Labor Heritage Foundation organizes a national gathering of workplace activists, artists, and labor educators who are strengthening the movement with songs, poems, hip-hop, posters, cartoons, puppets, and more.
Once again the Great Labor Arts Exchange is part of the Labor Notes Conference and included in your registration. Labor arts workshops and performances will be woven throughout the weekend.
Arrive early for the maximum dose of labor culture! We'll get started ahead of the main conference, at 2 p.m. on Thursday, April 18. Email info[at]laborheritage[dot]org to learn more.
Song, Poem, Drag, and Hip-Hop Contest:The Great Labor Arts Exchange's annual contest will be held live at Labor Notes. To enter the contest, fill out this form. Limit one entry per registered conference participant. Cash prizes are awarded by popular vote of the audience, and the winners perform in the Saturday night concert.

Covid Precautions:
To keep everyone safe, we are asking everyone to wear masks throughout the conference, though we cannot guarantee that everyone will abide by the request to mask. We recommend N95, KN95, KF94, or FFP2 masks, not just a plain surgical mask, because the research shows these are much more effective at reducing the transmission of Covid. Read more on this here.
Hotel Booking:
Reserve now. Rooms go fast! Our reserved rooms at the Hyatt and several other nearby hotels are already full, but you can still book rooms with hotels in the area. There are many hotels clustered in close walking distance in Rosemont. The conference is in the Hyatt Regency O’Hare in Rosemont which is a short walk (0.5 miles) from the CTA's Rosemont Blue Line station, with connections throughout Chicago.

CONFERENCIA DE LABOR NOTES 2024
La conferencia de Labor Notes es el evento más grande de activistas, reformadores sindicales y todos trabajadores rebeldes. Regístrese antes del 1 de marzo para un descuento. ¡No se pierda esta conferencia llena de inspiración, educación y agitación!
Acompaña a miles de miembros sindicales, líderes y trabajadores activistas que están en la primera línea en nuestros lugares trabajo y nuestras comunidades, organizando a nuevos trabajadores y agitando juntos. Conozca a rebeldes de todas partes del país y del mundo entero.
Más de 200 reuniones y talleres que incluyen tácticas creativas de cómo organizarse, combatir la apatía, presentarse como candidato sindical, ganar un contrato laboral, negociar sobre la tecnología, ganar las quejas y revivir la huelga.
Organiza con trabajadores en tu sindicato, industria o campaña. Reunirse cara-a-cara para compartir tácticas y experiencias es fundamental para la Conferencia de Labor Notes. Participa en juntas de tu sindicato, comité o industria – o contactanos si quieres organizar una reunión.
CUÁNDO: Viernes, 19 de abril, 9 a.m a domingo, abril 21, 3 p.m.
A DONDE: El hotel Hyatt Regency O'Hare, Rosemont (Chicago), Illinois

Presentando el Great Labor Arts Exchange!
Cada año el Labor Heritage Foundation organiza un encuentro nacional de activistas laborales, artistas y maestros que fortalecen nuestro movimiento con canciones, poemas, hip-hop, carteles, dibujos, marionetas y más. ¡Llega temprano! No vas a querer perder todas las obras de arte de nuestro movimiento. Empezamos antes de la conferencia principal a las 2 p.m. el jueves 18 de abril. Para aprender más manda un correo electrónico a info[at]laborheritage[dot]org.
Concurso de canto, poesía y hip-hop:
El concurso anual de The Great Labor Arts Exchange se llevará a cabo en vivo este año en Labor Notes. Regístrate aquí para competir. Límite de una entrada por persona registrada. Todos pueden votar por su obra favorita, y los ganadores de voto popular recibirán un premio en efectivo y la oportunidad de presentarse en el concierto de sábado en la tarde.
