Key Details of the Crimson Hexagon
   * The Books: It is said to house unique, smaller-than-usual books that are described as all-powerful, illustrated, and magical. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESpAEB6zswFajXBa0xOWvM-2rdSaWjx8LoqYJRrjBpjmcAkObKhy4YDPFTIeVvxg441aQJvXz4RQzjKCFz9p2jKNVCq9ckSXTDYYyRhYpSpsw9F_1lLkXw2ykl2-mi9FuwuQdRZ0Qus2h8mjtZ0p2ZM6SE8FtyzKigK1lyAfwSrNkFYX5WEIV2RG6_A8ZmRnskOBkzVOVRa5n4qt6qI2cVAAdT_YeiGg)]
   * The Fanaticism: Fanatics believed these mystical texts held the ultimate keys, formulas, or truths of the universe, driving them to destroy or purge other sections of the library in a frantic, often destructive search to reach it. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESpAEB6zswFajXBa0xOWvM-2rdSaWjx8LoqYJRrjBpjmcAkObKhy4YDPFTIeVvxg441aQJvXz4RQzjKCFz9p2jKNVCq9ckSXTDYYyRhYpSpsw9F_1lLkXw2ykl2-mi9FuwuQdRZ0Qus2h8mjtZ0p2ZM6SE8FtyzKigK1lyAfwSrNkFYX5WEIV2RG6_A8ZmRnskOBkzVOVRa5n4qt6qI2cVAAdT_YeiGg)]
   * Symbolism: Much like the myth of the "Man of the Book" (the idea that a single catalog or God-like librarian compiled all meaning), the Crimson Hexagon represents humanity's desperate, doomed craving for absolute order, hidden authority, and transcendent meaning inside a vast, indifferent, and chaotic universe.
