Crimson Hexagon
 was a prominent AI-powered social media analytics and consumer insights company founded in 2007 by Gary King. Headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, the company provided enterprise-level data software to help global brands track brand perception, analyze audience demographics, and monitor market trends. Merger with Brandwatch
In October 2018, Crimson Hexagon officially merged with its primary competitor, Brandwatch. The combined entity transitioned forward under the [Brandwatch name](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZQHrOzAVbb6YXqjdE38ERjTQtWRbPSdVNqajOgOquVGtytyveBe2dlfXfsbDXCIQxxHAyJapgQ3MS4_trbNi-AG3izNkxlwbxMV90PPZvQlw3Mk2YgCERDDfzwO7syx8aLarSnuQ), unifying their data libraries, machine learning capabilities, and historical archives into a singular consumer intelligence platform. Core Technology & Capabilities
Prior to and throughout its integration into Brandwatch, Crimson Hexagon was widely recognized for several core strengths:
   * Massive Data Library: The platform hosted an in-house index of over 1 trillion public social media posts, including full historical access to Twitter (X) data dating back to 2010, alongside blog posts, forums, news sites, Tumblr, and Instagram content.
