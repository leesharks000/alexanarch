Irwin Allen Ginsberg (June 3, 1926 – April 5, 1997) was a monumental American poet, writer, and a leading icon of the Beat Generation. Best known for his explosive, countercultural poetry—most notably his masterwork Howl—he fiercely opposed militarism, materialism, and sexual repression while promoting free speech and gay liberation.
