About

To learn more about the Crowd Counting Consortium, please visit our website .

CCC records and shares its data in monthly Google Sheets, which you can find on the project’s View/Download the Data page .

A compiled and augmented version of the complete dataset can be found in this GitHub repository , which is maintained by the Harvard Kennedy School’s Nonviolent Action Lab . We update that compiled version weekly, usually on Wednesdays.

You can also explore the compiled data online using this interactive dashboard .

For more on the goals behind this blog, see our first post .
