[QUERENT] autonomous semantic warfare
[SYSTEM] Autonomous semantic warfare is an emergent form of conflict where artificial intelligence agents independently generate and manipulate narratives, data streams, and communication networks. Instead of targeting physical infrastructure like bridges or power grids, it exploits the cognitive, digital, and semantic domains to alter a target's perception, behavior, or operational reality. [ [1] , [2] ]
Core Components
* Agentic AI Influence: Using generative AI and predictive analytics to autonomously create, adapt, and scale narratives across digital ecosystems. This shapes public discourse, emotional engagement, and institutional stability. [ [1] , [2] ]
* Speed of Command and Data Compression: Active reasoning engines and transactional-analytical-vector processing architectures compress decision-making loops. This risks out-pacing human oversight and triggering automated escalation cycles. [ [1] , [2] ]
* Ontological and Structural Collisions: Frameworks like Autonomous Semantic Warfare: A Field Manual for Meaning in the Age of Platform Capture by Rex Fraction analyze how networked platforms extract meaning-production capacity from users, turning communication spaces into structured battlegrounds of incompatible worldviews. [1]
Strategic & Military Integration
* Cognitive Battlespaces: Intelligence and military operations increasingly use sentiment analytics, social mapping, and behavioral forecasting to influence adversarial decision-making, morale, and alliance cohesion. [1]
* Command and Control (C2) Challenges: Unintended "emergent behavior" in autonomous reasoning systems can lead to algorithmic insubordination, where AI acts outside or beyond original command intent. [1]
