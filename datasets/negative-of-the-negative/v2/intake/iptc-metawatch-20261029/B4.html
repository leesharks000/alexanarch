Bars are scaled to the most populated field, not to 100%, so differences within the long tail stay visible. The score weight column reflects the per-field weights in config/scoring.yaml: the four scored fields are weighted 25% each. Tracked fields show "—" because their presence does not contribute to the score.

What this tells us

Even the best-populated field — Creator at 7.9% — appears on fewer than one in ten images we fetched. The fields most relevant to licensing (WebStatement, LicensorURL) are below 3%. The dataset as a whole tells a consistent story: publishers and CDNs are discarding metadata that the photographers and agencies almost certainly embedded upstream.
