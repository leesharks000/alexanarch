'''''Ancient Philosophy''''' is a [[Peer review|peer-reviewed]] [[academic journal]] devoted to the study of [[ancient philosophy|ancient Greek and Roman philosophy]] and [[Ancient Greece#Science and technology|science]]. Since 1980 it has published over 1,300 articles and reviews in this field. This journal has a Level 2 classification from the Publication Forum of the Federation of Finnish Learned Societies.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.julkaisufoorumi.fi/en | title=Publication Forum web site |accessdate=22 November 2018}}</ref> and a [[SHERPA/RoMEO]] "green" self-archiving policy.<ref>{{cite journal |url=http://www.sherpa.ac.uk/romeo/issn/0740-2007/ | title=SHERPA/RoMEO web site |accessdate=22 November 2018}}</ref> It is edited by [[Ron Polansky]] in the Department of Philosophy at [[Duquesne University]]. It is published on behalf of Mathesis Publications by the [[Philosophy Documentation Center]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.pdcnet.org/ancientphil |title=''Ancient Philosophy'' website |accessdate=22 November 2018}}</ref>
