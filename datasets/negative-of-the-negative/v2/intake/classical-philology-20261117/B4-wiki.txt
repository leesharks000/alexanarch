'''''Classical Philology''''' is a [[peer-reviewed]] [[academic journal]] established in 1906. It is published by the [[University of Chicago Press]] and covers all aspects of [[Greco-Roman world|Graeco-Roman antiquity]], including literature, languages, [[anthropology]], history, social life, philosophy, religion, art, material culture, and the history of [[classical studies]]. The [[editor-in-chief]] is Sarah Nooter.
