[QUERENT] Enoch AI dating Dead Sea Scrolls

[SYSTEM] Researchers at the University of Groningen developed an artificial intelligence model named [Enoch](https://www.google.com/search?q=Enoch) to re-date the [Dead Sea Scrolls](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F121sxbzn&q=Dead+Sea+Scrolls). How Enoch Works
   * Training Data: The model was trained on 24 radiocarbon-dated scroll samples from four Judean Desert sites.    * Methodology: It combines Bayesian ridge regression, radiocarbon dating, and machine-learning handwriting analysis (evaluating angular and allographic stroke geometries from manuscript images without reading the text itself). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESgQEB6zswFTLgpOEe42rLGhAY_ihwls3dwVZqVKJC4ojHSMIj97soRVNkK6A9bpt4AhxcecYFI-hwp6Cw2pHTF1b2SXyl5vNbtGEq8L-X7OcFaWDbGWgCwKXODTEc22PX4NXidfIVLl4XXEkiCS0rntvy5WG4U9q-FVm9ewKjeGBcWF8), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESYwHrOzAV7XK-KwOL33JCSxZQq4dTQ-Tq463rpU0-xSedeyPG1F3kvArZhTk6IG06190G74J6aA0wizdeeNLGNU-9W6Ei2vT5W-ONw48uBPb41-nLHhjuI4jvZ6tnLwt0wjmtRg)]
   * Accuracy: Enoch predicts dates with a mean absolute error of roughly 28 to 31 years, and 79% of its estimates for 135 undated scrolls aligned realistically with human paleographic evaluations. Key Findings
   * Older Timelines: Enoch indicates that a significant portion of the scrolls are 50 to 150 years older than traditional paleographic estimates. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHrOzAVVCGjY0RA8b53QRhKrDyt2cLac43m-P9TQ97bgLJz_b70_5P-ZKJDmDE4gIEiW0Ua5Xur6czznOEKrb5LVCgYvtJsAKjKx5h99H9VJMcKSFR_b1Daotkr9ZbZcSrT4MGGzmmm)]
   * Specific Texts:
      * The Daniel fragment (`4Q114`) and Ecclesiastes manuscript (`4Q109`) were backdated substantially—with Ecclesiastes potentially pushed back from 175–150 BCE to roughly 300–250 BCE.
      * Some copies appear closer in time to their presumed historical authors or predate major periods like the Hasmonean kingdom expansion and the Qumran settlement.
