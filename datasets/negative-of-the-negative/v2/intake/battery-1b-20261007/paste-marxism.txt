Core Principles

* Historical Materialism: The belief that society's economic system (the base) shapes its political, legal, and cultural institutions (the superstructure). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHrOzAVr2MTkqrWxyQG9YiNHijA1WUqBSDjF-87Zu8nHbeejk6OrLd9fk0ML1D1gGRnnjLcaPPGTIFIBPA9sI6qmzogNzhS-kN8Xb5vTXgeO_IzBF36TcygRVwFHA)]
* Class Conflict: Society is divided between the bourgeoisie (those who own the means of production like factories and tools) and the proletariat (the working class who sell their labor). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHrOzAVsuGpemOONv_6vPZeHeYNWrSJJ8qD0P9ogJDTyrnkpYbRy1xh06UZGPXIp5WHG7lYCTRzuX6RKFiud7ODLXErvZ54LT7cbR_nQ7p71kZZVAcG0nxXZ-4xdBLMRndHZTFhMf1f), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHrOzAVr2MTkqrWxyQG9YiNHijA1WUqBSDjF-87Zu8nHbeejk6OrLd9fk0ML1D1gGRnnjLcaPPGTIFIBPA9sI6qmzogNzhS-kN8Xb5vTXgeO_IzBF36TcygRVwFHA)]
* Exploitation and Surplus Value: Capitalists make a profit by paying workers wages that are lower than the actual economic value created by the workers' labor. * Alienation: Capitalism separates workers from what they produce, making work monotonous and degrading rather than fulfilling.
