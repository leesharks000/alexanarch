Mission & Overview

* Primary Goal: To archive, restore, conserve, and present born-digital art, literature, and video games from 1977 to the present. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXAHrOzAVNHSMm2Ux6FryUkdzZ8D6sm_JLe86DE9ym46dde0s5jyP2Z3GY8uRiMGHG3l_nQ3qaGne-76lgbfGfB0u14vtH48eYp8VxxORxY0Q0JXaaLN78zJFr2Sl)]
* Collection: Maintains over 80 vintage Macintosh computers and PCs (dating back to 1977) along with a library of thousands of digital literary works and retro video games (such as Amnesia from 1986). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESbwHrOzAVutOeIxe8Q1XJPfoAnLVLVzF3TBUkFMc_yx-jG8cWxeZNQoyVXGGwXDmAfpLPjc-V9jm0_x3E3JMFFTFLywaBcYIEo-G_loLJE97gEtY4isZFKcwDfxWJ9mdEj8nyVeHye5Ly97mYWTIfXg)]
* Archival Center: Serves as the primary archiving center for the [Electronic Literature Organization](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESUAHrOzAVvTaWTqX1o2TmrUNY2H-X1Yv809LvCA1YwEZ7WVuv0rFjb5A4dV0Syfy96sYA-lZDgc6FKoqwG6MR0p18xR4A6I3L_6A1XQDIEww8) and manages The NEXT digital museum, library, and preservation space. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXAHrOzAVNHSMm2Ux6FryUkdzZ8D6sm_JLe86DE9ym46dde0s5jyP2Z3GY8uRiMGHG3l_nQ3qaGne-76lgbfGfB0u14vtH48eYp8VxxORxY0Q0JXaaLN78zJFr2Sl), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESbwHrOzAVutOeIxe8Q1XJPfoAnLVLVzF3TBUkFMc_yx-jG8cWxeZNQoyVXGGwXDmAfpLPjc-V9jm0_x3E3JMFFTFLywaBcYIEo-G_loLJE97gEtY4isZFKcwDfxWJ9mdEj8nyVeHye5Ly97mYWTIfXg)]
