"Of the present inquiry, on the other hand, it was not the case that part of the work had been thoroughly done before, while part had not. Nothing existed at all… On the subject of deduction [syllogizing] we had absolutely nothing else of an earlier date to mention, but were kept at work for a long time in experimental researches." Why Aristotle Made This Claim

* The Invention of Formal Logic: Before Aristotle, philosophers like Plato and the Sophists argued using logic implicitly, but no one had analyzed the structure of arguments. Aristotle isolated the mechanics of reasoning into what we now call syllogistic logic—the process of deriving a necessary conclusion from two premises (e.g., All humans are mortal; Socrates is human; therefore, Socrates is mortal).
* Contrasting Rhetoric: In the same passage, Aristotle notes that teachers of rhetoric (like Gorgias) simply gave their students ready-made speeches to memorize. Aristotle wanted to give his students the actual system or tool (Organon) to evaluate any argument's validity on their own.
