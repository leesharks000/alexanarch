[QUERENT] Nanopublications

[SYSTEM] A nanopublication is the smallest independent unit of publishable scientific information, represented as a machine-readable knowledge graph snippet rather than a traditional research paper. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESTAHrOzAV1yR1C6Ffp-mJYbjicBIQjplNbIkm1DPUmGjplHWIHrhfd_KnQTn3nZnd2etExBDERCfJkZKv2Clw6GjnOCm6jj2nsx-gVZk), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZgHrOzAVHHcCYMwchIQdtXG0q8p-tpdrN_-MS1SXUpFnut9sAtEfFkWgmGqLulEfczlsfUNnpcIseOQtly0ECDQBG4tTkw_cojKjZMk9SO20C4MEHaBgXz5rp7zoyFo0wfGsWBmJYw)]
Core Structure
Every nanopublication is built using Resource Description Framework (RDF) triples and consists of three mandatory parts: [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaQHrOzAVT30WsgNwtI0KXwg-_GIDeC5PLJF7UcX4OWuVacfZntN8sGCKhu_7lSqiGmWLb3sW8xyBUQG_eTof3qFedDl4gD92_ZbHpAfCK6j8muKPrwMO03aveO5z0Uf3OrVVbsLSQASaFQ), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShwEB6zswFWE5BZijtwjt9Re1Ur-t_mG0A6ZZI1r4t95onFRxZig5Ms8nYV8IwXXBF50xNEFX5VodqJNMvhnh3TVRki9wmjjlRbX1ICuuWk1Wq8xlFBYMI4vrgfMwrv1C33E2_10RMAIeLdayTF-FqKjknWkHf1US3qfyZT_APb0EraRhahBSZ_k)]

* Assertion: A single, atomic scientific statement or claim (e.g., a specific relation between a gene and a disease). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESTAHrOzAV1yR1C6Ffp-mJYbjicBIQjplNbIkm1DPUmGjplHWIHrhfd_KnQTn3nZnd2etExBDERCfJkZKv2Clw6GjnOCm6jj2nsx-gVZk), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShwEB6zswFWE5BZijtwjt9Re1Ur-t_mG0A6ZZI1r4t95onFRxZig5Ms8nYV8IwXXBF50xNEFX5VodqJNMvhnh3TVRki9wmjjlRbX1ICuuWk1Wq8xlFBYMI4vrgfMwrv1C33E2_10RMAIeLdayTF-FqKjknWkHf1US3qfyZT_APb0EraRhahBSZ_k)]
* Provenance: Metadata detailing how the assertion was generated, including the methods, parameters, and primary source or paper. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShQEB6zswFb3rkEAMLJU3gbOi0LNmRqtOJ4t07gTM0nmJ59PZ-Q_kHqif2vF3lIGgQI5CsWF4B3fjajhGO5UGTin5prWH77KCf2F1WMQcVCI0YB-6NbFTClr_OZ4BZVrF2tsFlG7-dGyuRMW4zXDjlgWGh6APKxek81WB0kD_SG9c5ftYWHrT), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShwEB6zswFWE5BZijtwjt9Re1Ur-t_mG0A6ZZI1r4t95onFRxZig5Ms8nYV8IwXXBF50xNEFX5VodqJNMvhnh3TVRki9wmjjlRbX1ICuuWk1Wq8xlFBYMI4vrgfMwrv1C33E2_10RMAIeLdayTF-FqKjknWkHf1US3qfyZT_APb0EraRhahBSZ_k)]
* Publication Info: Metadata identifying the creator, creation timestamp, and reuse or licensing terms. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShwEB6zswFWE5BZijtwjt9Re1Ur-t_mG0A6ZZI1r4t95onFRxZig5Ms8nYV8IwXXBF50xNEFX5VodqJNMvhnh3TVRki9wmjjlRbX1ICuuWk1Wq8xlFBYMI4vrgfMwrv1C33E2_10RMAIeLdayTF-FqKjknWkHf1US3qfyZT_APb0EraRhahBSZ_k)]

Key Features

* Machine-Readable: Formatted using Semantic Web standards (such as RDF and OWL) so computers and AI tools can automatically search, aggregate, and reason over the data. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESawHrOzAVX5UkC9JbjtfWIgkF64MpBAWZI5udRUGD9Di-6-A_BSk8hpnCEXL9-eEbOqYWOAiBG0V2giyYxkH4EYX3PZrkCRsL-whH5WWNR82K6xuCiFRr_Ce82nTEl42SHIuvBuTv1ucNF_la), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZgHrOzAVHHcCYMwchIQdtXG0q8p-tpdrN_-MS1SXUpFnut9sAtEfFkWgmGqLulEfczlsfUNnpcIseOQtly0ECDQBG4tTkw_cojKjZMk9SO20C4MEHaBgXz5rp7zoyFo0wfGsWBmJYw)]
* Citable and Attributed: Each nanopublication can be uniquely identified and tracked on a decentralized network, giving researchers credit for releasing granular data. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEScwHrOzAV4oAGTmcIh3VH0PN2fT1_9kN8-xSHvrKR7qbaZdoNDloNdhArlyoUAeJPR3ntLZW8hGi07-wdGt9sm62oFtKxLDqjfQpMSC8Npc_FUBC32fXVuPNoA7e2Blkf1IkQYgVJ22S3B8ZLpq-zHkyIKeg), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESTAHrOzAV1yR1C6Ffp-mJYbjicBIQjplNbIkm1DPUmGjplHWIHrhfd_KnQTn3nZnd2etExBDERCfJkZKv2Clw6GjnOCm6jj2nsx-gVZk)]
* Life Sciences Focus: Millions of nanopublications have been created, heavily concentrated in fields like genetics, pharmacology, and bioinformatics. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZQHrOzAVn6dh5sSNnva08Mh7s5y8l0JTOUaDX6nodz1rzdNDjoo1i3PJHS7Aqd_2MKZnVuNfLNEuMpRpsHg1vqB5ebrsJ7avSl3HXtpPYluSMrCGgjYlWtcEJU69L9P3gAsEXKwg), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaQHrOzAVT30WsgNwtI0KXwg-_GIDeC5PLJF7UcX4OWuVacfZntN8sGCKhu_7lSqiGmWLb3sW8xyBUQG_eTof3qFedDl4gD92_ZbHpAfCK6j8muKPrwMO03aveO5z0Uf3OrVVbsLSQASaFQ)]
* Tools and Access: You can explore or publish them using platforms like [Nanopublications](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESTAHrOzAV1yR1C6Ffp-mJYbjicBIQjplNbIkm1DPUmGjplHWIHrhfd_KnQTn3nZnd2etExBDERCfJkZKv2Clw6GjnOCm6jj2nsx-gVZk) or client tools like `nanopub-py`.
