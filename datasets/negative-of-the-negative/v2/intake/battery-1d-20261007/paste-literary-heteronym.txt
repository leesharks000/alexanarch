Difference from Pseudonyms

* Pseudonyms (Pen Names): Simply a false name used by an author who continues to write as themselves.
* Heteronyms: Independent personae operating outside the author's own personality, sometimes even critiquing or translating each other's work. The creator writing as themselves is referred to as the "orthonym".
