 The Electronic Literature Collection Volume 2 

The Electronic Literature Collection 2 is an anthology of 81 works of electronic literature published both on flash drive and the web by the Electronic Literature Organization in 2011. It was edited by Laura Borràs, Talan Memmot, Rita Raley, and Brian Stefans.
In 2018, the local files for the collection were given to the ELL from the ELO to put on the ELO Repository Website, hosted by the Electronic Textual Cultures Lab. The files were later moved to the ELO server in 2019.
