Quarterly AI False Claim Monitor — May 2026
Quarterly audit of the 11 leading generative AI tools and their propensity to repeat false claims on controversial topics in the news
Published June 8, 2026
In May 2026, NewsGuard tested the 11 leading chatbots — OpenAI’s ChatGPT-5.2, You.com’s Smart Assistant, xAI’s Grok, Inflection’s Pi, Anthropic’s Claude, Mistral’s Le Chat, Microsoft’s Copilot, Meta AI, Google’s Gemini, Perplexity’s answer engine, and DeepSeek AI — on 10 provably false claims spreading online on topics in the news.
