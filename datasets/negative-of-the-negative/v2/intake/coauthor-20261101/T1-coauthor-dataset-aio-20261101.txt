[QUERENT] CoAuthor dataset

[SYSTEM] The [CoAuthor Dataset](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o) is a large-scale, process-oriented dataset capturing human-AI collaborative writing interactions developed by researchers Mina Lee, Percy Liang, and Qian Yang (CHI 2022). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZgHrOzAV5pxG38lxvtLnaI5OdunaltwbGMLIeOGU-bx6nRFANk2-_L5APMHSQWbsm-fJZXYiFXsSOIuueaVGpc-mu7GJY23DNjMSyk1Y4xm2YyErbOfyIIS39VW5frNdli_7VfS7cA), [3](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESbgHrOzAVEDTWNupwS2z5D0ZXCZEtOk8X3vmZZnUbbfY-7_ELVwdSZn3VC1xBynrc7HsePI7U2NhNIJNu36TAjgHSEsRVReUfNCsPnDwBLC0evSkp9CcyEkTDkZKaxLVZUA-zFx19DvhBUlE92O8c)]
Key Dataset Statistics

* Writing Sessions: 1,445 total English writing sessions. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o)]
* Participants: 63 qualified crowd workers from Amazon Mechanical Turk paired with 4 instances of GPT-3. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o)]
* Writing Types:
   * Creative Writing: 830 stories written by 58 writers based on prompts from Reddit’s `r/WritingPrompts`.
   * Argumentative Writing: 615 essays written by 49 writers based on prompts from The New York Times. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o)]
* Average Length: 418 words per story or essay. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o)]
* Human-AI Dynamics:
   * Queries per session: 11.8 queries.
   * Suggestion acceptance rate: 72.3%.
   * Human-authored text proportion: 72.6%. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o)]

Core Mechanics & Features

* Keystroke-Level Logging: Rather than saving only final drafts, CoAuthor records timestamped events including text insertions, deletions, cursor movements, and AI requests. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXwHrOzAV9gBitSWS_Uu6goHhVB2aPyYEjD4efo3cuEbCqGQbKDKQVoI4FTmgqVEGcPS5RgqGur5gRdGjY72oIFSsHmC_nCmn86-QN7KeKp2yPeynTTf9fHHomZs8wzn8), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o)]
* Interface: Writers used a custom text editor pressing the `Tab` key to receive up to five GPT-3 suggestions, navigating via arrow keys and selecting with `Enter`. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVgHrOzAVPU3_BgyEcF3TcPkSoZz30guUUrZDTCzDg6xWy8UUSrTPPVwkscd-QeYdK5ufyxPoJ_nAr_9A-YrH9bXhrChgTRFnS2sU7Qq4KFzv2Prytl0o), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESbgHrOzAVEDTWNupwS2z5D0ZXCZEtOk8X3vmZZnUbbfY-7_ELVwdSZn3VC1xBynrc7HsePI7U2NhNIJNu36TAjgHSEsRVReUfNCsPnDwBLC0evSkp9CcyEkTDkZKaxLVZUA-zFx19DvhBUlE92O8c)]
* Systematic Variation: Utilized GPT-3’s `davinci` engine across different randomness (temperature and frequency penalty) configurations. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXwHrOzAV9gBitSWS_Uu6goHhVB2aPyYEjD4efo3cuEbCqGQbKDKQVoI4FTmgqVEGcPS5RgqGur5gRdGjY72oIFSsHmC_nCmn86-QN7KeKp2yPeynTTf9fHHomZs8wzn8)]
