[QUERENT] NaNoGenMo

[SYSTEM] NaNoGenMo (National Novel Generation Month) is an annual programming challenge where participants spend the month of November writing computer code to procedurally generate a novel of 50,000 or more words. The Core Goal and Rules

* The Goal: Write and run code between November 1 (12:01 am GMT) and December 1 (12:01 am GMT) to output a text work of 50,000+ words. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVAHrOzAVgZXxkjJBVzNfv2uQl3kz5_EOGJl7gQ7HgsqudGizQoSslR7Yc71Qxx-ZfmaEG1JL3pNcQIpIc37omSVn-_cchpUmkPBGr7yAJDA7TCa80w)]
* Definition of a Novel: The definition is flexible. Participants can use traditional procedural text, Markov chains, simulations, data mashups, or creative formatting (such as guides, collections, or repeated structures).
