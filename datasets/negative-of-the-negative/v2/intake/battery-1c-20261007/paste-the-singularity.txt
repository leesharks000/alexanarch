Potential Impacts and Risks

* Optimistic Views: Proponents like Kurzweil envision human-machine integration, the end of severe disease, and the elimination of physical labor scarcity.
* Existential Risks: Critics and safety researchers warn of permanent loss of human control, economic disruption, and severe existential threats to humanity.
