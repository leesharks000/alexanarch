What is AIM?

* Real-Time Tracking: It continuously aggregates and analyzes world news using the Event Registry platform, processing over 150,000 news articles daily to detect AI-related events. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESYAHrOzAVsifMuhOaNwDAlzQjnIy4vrRKr8I29XG5C1WVNVzL3bhCTNw4fI2laOpcyKH7J9IvN8a-ZLn_J0sSyfVklrlhS5SXPO5ES6BEJry4oAExji4krUQwGXL-QXS8LA)]
* Evidence Base: It provides policymakers, developers, and the public with empirical data on how AI risks materialize globally. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEScgHrOzAVwqGhkYf9SorcbvbHFWp_yWUbilndOVejGwP1BWdb70lPllHnV1OjwaTm5Y9FgGTIZq7Kp8R6J_FH6eqmP3iFvVP96kVDPEpPu6PtUwb2-QUKtq1yM-9TeqGc3XFOXGl2Ii12XPQC4GTLKczY8Q)]

* AI Incident: An event where the development, use, or malfunction of an AI system results in actual harm (such as injury, health damage, critical infrastructure disruption, or human/fundamental rights violations).
