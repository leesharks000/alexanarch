Struggling Against the Suppression of Ideas

      This site exists to struggle against the fascist-like suppression of progressive ideas and thought by reactionary governments and their agents anywhere in the world. We will attempt to publicize such suppression and—when possible—to post documents and publications that have been suppressed elsewhere. For more information about this web site see: About BannedThought.net
