AI Overview
An AI registry (or AI overview capture registry) is a centralized, live catalog used by enterprises to inventory, manage, and govern all artificial intelligence assets—such as machine learning models, autonomous agents, and specific use cases—deployed across their infrastructure.

Core Purpose & Benefits
As organizations scale, they often build AI systems across fragmented platforms (like Google Vertex AI, Azure, and Databricks). An AI registry acts as a single, consistent management layer to solve the following challenges:


Collibra
Centralized Visibility: Documents who owns each AI tool, its exact deployment environment, data lineage, and lifecycle history.


Atlan
Risk and Compliance: Maps models against international regulations, including the EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework (RMF), and ISO/IEC 42001.


Atlan
Automated Intake: Platforms like trail connect directly to governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) tools or agent environments (like ServiceNow, OneTrust, or Microsoft Copilot Studio) to continuously pull new AI assets into the registry.

Distinguishing AI Registries from AI Trackers
Depending on your exact goals, you might be looking at one of two distinct software categories:
Feature	Enterprise AI Registry (e.g., Collibra, trail)	AI Overview Tracker / Scraping Registry (e.g., Honeyb AI, AIOviewTrack)
Primary Goal	Compliance, asset tracking, internal AI risk management.	SEO monitoring, competitive analysis, marketing visibility.
What it Captures	Model metadata, source code, data lineage, trust metrics.	Live snapshots of Google Search AI Overviews, brand mentions, cited source URLs.
Target User	CISOs, Chief Compliance Officers, Legal, Risk Teams.	SEO Managers, Digital Marketers, Brand Strategists.
Technical Mechanism	API integrations into MLOps environments and software pipelines.	DOM scraping via Playwright, mobile proxies, or SERP APIs.
If you are attempting to build or configure a system that automatically grabs and logs Google search layouts, you can use specialized tools like Advanced Web Ranking or custom Playwright scripts. If you are looking to store cloud-native agent components, systems like Google Cloud's Agent Registry provide specialized catalogs for developer tools.
