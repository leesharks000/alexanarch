Key Facts

* Launch Date: March 28, 2011
* Name Origin: Named after Veronica Saß (daughter of politician Edmund Stoiber), whose thesis was the first project documented ("Vroni" is a German nickname for Veronica).
* Scope: Documents hundreds of academic works with percentage breakdowns of copied text, side-by-side comparisons, and the status of university degree revocations.
* Process: Findings are officially reported to the relevant university's office of academic integrity before public release.
* Recognition: Awarded the Zedler-Preis für freies Wissen (Zedler Prize for Free Knowledge) by Wikimedia Germany in 2012.
