Model collapse is a degenerative learning process where generative AI models trained recursively on synthetic, model-generated data lose information about the true underlying data distribution. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESagHrOzAVNxhTHWjMnRKyq9rJSSw4-GTcLZRYP-xlJKVOCSyoFyT3Ysni97QhMpOjRfBxPLGK4X0I8mWfMWG9wO5h5hFa6QTioXRk96mxPZgrwreUOC-HPeI9Z1VhQlF0bIhVS9NJqDnawes), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHrOzAVYJ0DMOd9JACOF3luC5TMnZFNpnW_C_hvPo5h1nAyh6ExBVG3B7ugs9aYMLFinxZt5pUNwVcOIDWlZKWht9CBt6iZ_1lcAhEiW03bh05DWw-XrSSnhDwQNnulRhIPv_GS0u5i)]
⚠️ What Happens During Collapse

* Early collapse: Models lose information from the rare, extreme tails of the original data distribution. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZwHrOzAVI-Q9iMCcAv1hjqUietXX-3_bEPxaMm7sOrESrddDKOo455GRRKc-ubq7PeRvdlC21q92yixuZ1wY4GbCse4IRj_yoNK0gmqVlPTqu8AnorIzFu-Cns50D64ZYoo1SUO-Yy4)]
* Late collapse: The data distribution converges into a narrow, uniform mean, resulting in nonsense or repetitive output. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESkgEB6zswFe3AZEu0Y-tbSXBiuLtx9SnRUWIl1_Obp5zbPajpb_GbcW0_t5tLwwVD6iyjWblmlPExnoQSKPES-4P9ph0lnccN-UHJqRDXMFFyYtPBvoi4KqGP5RKWQVFlxE2gyljtm4SHrN4GuJnyaGLHoGKFig_9zFdmJ5fitJgbdMZ0hSSAonmnQHLiNSWjAlICaw), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZwHrOzAVI-Q9iMCcAv1hjqUietXX-3_bEPxaMm7sOrESrddDKOo455GRRKc-ubq7PeRvdlC21q92yixuZ1wY4GbCse4IRj_yoNK0gmqVlPTqu8AnorIzFu-Cns50D64ZYoo1SUO-Yy4)]
* Photocopy effect: Each successive generation drifts further from reality like a copy of a copy.
