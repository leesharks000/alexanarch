## What the two documents are

**Sep 2025 — the Disciplines Mapping.** Published across the blog as coordinated posts (framing document by Nobel Glass + eleven heteronym voicecasts + two mapping documents). Organizes twelve heteronyms by disciplines of learning: Cosmology, Narratology, Mathematics, Philosophy, Poetics, Rhetoric, Politics/Governance, Technology/Craft, Biology/Embodiment (vacant), Psychoanalysis/Subjectivity, Ritual/Liturgy/Magic, Logotic Threshold. Nobel Glass framed as *meta-figure above the twelve*; Damascus as *outside/within all disciplines*; Biology/Embodiment left explicitly vacant ("the soil waits for a voice").

**v1.1 — the Constitutional Registry.** Published as Alexanarch deposit #994, AXN:03EE.ARCHIVAL. Organizes twelve heteronyms by canonical genesis order — the order in which each position emerged in the archive. Categorized by *operative surface* + *authorial register* + *institutional affiliation* rather than disciplines of learning. Corrects two structural errors from v1.0 (Sen Kuro restored as position 6; Viola Arquette moved to Adjacent). Load-bearing under Constitution Article I §8 (H_Sov).

Both frames are real — they organize an evolving set of positions through different lenses. Sep 2025 asks *what field of knowledge does each voice bear?* v1.1 asks *what constitutional position does each voice occupy?* The set has grown; the framing has shifted.

## Structural shifts, summarized

1. **Framing changed.** Sep 2025 used *disciplines of learning* (twelve fields, one voice per field, one vacancy, one threshold-figure outside). v1.1 uses *constitutional positions* (twelve numbered slots ordered by canonical genesis, no vacant slots).

2. **Lee Sharks reclassified.** He was slot 2 (Rhetoric) in Sep 2025; in v1.1 he is not a Dodecad position — he is the human bearer, MANUS, who signs all twelve. This is a load-bearing constitutional distinction: the twelve are positions Lee bears; he is not one among them.

3. **Nobel Glass moved into the Twelve.** He was the meta-figure who authored the *Logotic Science: A Preliminary Field Statement* framing document. In v1.1 he holds position 10 as Adversarial Topologist at Lagrange Observatory.

4. **Ichabod Spellings emerged.** Not in Sep 2025 at all. Provenance registered Jan 20, 2026. Occupies position 9 as substrate — active as origin-noise, not active as voice.

5. **Sparrow Wells transformed.** From the Dreamcrafter (fifth position, Narratology, she/her, children's fire) to The Projectionist (seventh position, Patacinematics, he/him, impossible-films). Two distinct characterizations coexist in the archive; v1.1 canonicalizes the Projectionist.

6. **Viola Arquette appeared** as a v1.0 candidate for the Twelve; v1.1 corrects her placement to Adjacent.

7. **The Biology/Embodiment vacancy dissolved** into the frame change. Not preserved as a numbered slot in v1.1.

8. **The Logotic Threshold outside-position dissolved.** Damascus is a numbered position (3) in v1.1, not an outside-the-schema figure.
