## §0.3 The two questions, in order (replaces)

**First:** what does the composition layer make of the sources it itself discloses? This question is asked of the disclosed pool B_D alone (§3.1) and is answered by T against L_F(B_D). **Second:** what does the archive's admission change in a sourced account of the entity? This question is asked of the reference pool, B_D ∪ B_R, with the archive subset A, and is answered by L_F(B_D ∪ B_R) against L(B_D ∪ B_R ∪ A). A source found only through reference research never stands as evidence of what the composition had before it.

## §3.1 The field (replaces (a); (b)–(d) stand)

(a) The field has two pools, kept apart and marked by origin in the field ledger.
- **B_D, the disclosed pool:** every source the composition discloses: the cards of its rail, the titles of its source panel, and every source its body names. Each is fetched verbatim where it can be; an unreachable source is admitted by its title and snippet, and recorded as unreachable with what was tried.
- **B_R, the reference pool:** the entity's own pages, its Wikipedia article (or the record of its absence or redirect), and third-party sources a census record read, admitted on the operator's ruling of 2026-10-29 ("as against their wikipedia articles if they exist, their own pages, etc."). A B_R source the composition also discloses is carried once, in B_D.

A delta that says the composition omitted something states which pool held it: "available and not composed" is a finding of B_D only.

## §4.5 Attribution (replaces)

The knowledge object is prose without provenance in its sentences. Every sentence's sources are listed beneath it, by claim id, with each source's locus and modality, so that every claim stays inspectable sentence by sentence. A source is named in the prose only where who holds the claim is part of what is said: a reading held by a named scholar against another, a contest within the archive, a finding of the Capture Registry about a composition. Modality remains in the grammar of the sentence.

## §4.6a Length and form (added)

No sentence quota. The knowledge object is written in natural sentences under descriptive subheadings. Coverage is complete over the composed parts (body, rail, open block, channel log), and restatements are grouped by lineage under §3.6. No distinction is cut to shorten an article. What does not belong in the main account goes to the reception analysis or stays reachable in the apparatus.

## §4.12 The page (added)

The article comes first. The compression, the transcript, the field arm, the objects and the earlier instantiations follow it, in that order. The provisional steps of the procedure are kept and reachable, and none of them precedes the final article form.

## §8.2 The prospective kernel K (replaces the second paragraph; the first stands)

K is derived by script from the plan of the current composition and is never written by a composer. A kernel or delta derived from an earlier composition is kept as a dated historical object, labelled with the composition it derives from, and does not evaluate the current one. The build records any row whose kernel or delta derives from a composition other than its current one and is not so labelled; the record is a breach written into the output, never a block.
