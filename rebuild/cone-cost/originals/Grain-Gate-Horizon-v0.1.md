## Bindings and reading key

The verbal lines bind the glyphs; they are part of the checksum. This is a compression of a conditional argument, not a report that the pictured trajectory has been measured in a provider's system. An arrow in a dependency or gate sequence depicts the specified path. It does not independently establish a causal finding.

### Recovery test

A reading has lost the checksum if it makes narrowing identical to impoverishment; treats correct exclusion as harm; turns source persistence or priority into truth; substitutes possession or quotation for operative use; infers necessary descendants from missing vocabulary; drops the assumptions of either reach or repair-cost proposition; makes external mismatch automatically available as pressure; or identifies the Existence Tax, recursion, sophistry and developmental loss as one quantity.

A reading must recover the two costs and the three spaces. It must also recover the possibility that the source is wrong, the narrow basin is fertile, the proposed opening is damaging, or the ontology admits the correction. These are conditions of the argument, not escape clauses added after a failed prediction.
