### Two findings that change the work

**The Arch-Philosopher Mantle has no deposit.** The phrase appears in nine bodies —
#116, #119, #458, #1002 and the Space Ark documents — but there is no mantle instrument
for it, though there is one for the Blind Poet (#9/#905), Prince of Poets (#332/#1118),
King of May (#333/#1117) and Good Gray Poet (#334/#1115). restoredacademy.com/sigil/
already states the mantle and its four inhabitation conditions, and the O-Chain lineage
*Sappho → Plato → Plotinus → Porphyry/Iamblichus → Damascius → Sara Rappe → Johannes
Sigil.* **The site holds a doctrine the archive does not.** Either the mantle should be
deposited, or the site is quoting something I have not found.

**Spellings is deceased, and the plan should say so rather than treat it as a gap.**
Registry §3.8: `CANONICAL // SUBSTRATE // DECEASED`, affiliation *foundational substrate
— the noise floor of the Crimson Hexagon.* His portrait 404s **because there is no
portrait**, not because one is missing, and his affiliation is not a body and must not
be given one. His home should be a **memorial page**, not an institutional one — the
substrate from which the other heteronyms emerged, with the autobiography (#342 / #1124)
and the transfiguration provenance (#268) at its centre.

**Registry #90 v1.1 is stale in the same way its Logotic creator fields were.** §3.1
names only JSICP for Sigil and omits the Restored Academy; §3.12 gives Feist as a
"logotic-textual function" and never mentions the Space Ark. Both are corrected by later
documents. **A v1.2 is warranted**, and it is a MANUS instrument, not a repair.

## 4 · What a heteronym tab contains

Six blocks: **the figure** (portrait, role, description — verbatim from
`/about/heteronyms`, avatar served from alexanarch); **the institution** (charter chip,
one line, omitted where none exists); **provenance** (registration document, registry
#90, and the governing sentence); **publications** (sectioned, chips to the standard);
**the journals** the heteronym edits or publishes in; **captures** (where a machine met
the voice — 266 entries, naming Sigil 35 times, Morrow once).

Two blocks depend on work not yet done. **Institution images are on the blog and cannot
be located until the image triage runs** — 1,970 images across 1,805 posts, unclassified,
at `alexanarch.org/triage/`. Every tab can ship without them.
