## Reading

1. **The event is a 12-second transient, not a drifting line.** M4b fitted a track starting at 1625.2936106 MHz and drifting +1.69 Hz/s across ON0013. At full resolution, near that track, every 6-second row of ON0013 sits at the noise (peaks 1.3–1.8 times the row median) except two. Rows 32–33 (195–207 s) stand at 32.9 and 29.7 times the median. That event is:
   - broad, more than 3 times the median over 109 and 144 bins, spanning 305 and 422 Hz;
   - falling in frequency, with its peak moving from f0 + 451 Hz to f0 + 250 Hz between the two rows;
   - polarized, with X/Y 4.5 and then 2.1;
   - comb-structured: power alternates bin by bin, a 2.08-bin period (about 5.8 Hz), which is a pulse repetition of about 0.17 s.

   M4b's long track is that event, fitted as a line.
2. **The same class of transient stands in the OFF scans.** Rows with any bin above 8 times the median in the band f0 − 1 kHz to f0 + 3.3 kHz:
   - ON0013: three rows (29, 32, 33).
   - OFF0014 and ON0015: none.
   - OFF0016: rows 17 and 20–23. Peaks up to 17.0 times; across rows 20–23 the peak falls from f0 + 136 Hz to f0 − 655 Hz; X/Y 0.45.
   - ON0017: rows 18–20, 22 and 26–29. Peaks up to 77.1 times, falling within each group; the same 2-bin comb.
   - OFF0018: rows 17 and 28–30. Peaks up to 48.5 times; across rows 28–30 the peak falls from f0 + 1477 Hz to f0 + 585 Hz; the same 2-bin comb.

   Short, strong, frequency-falling, pulse-combed emissions come and go in this band in ON and OFF pointings alike. ON0013's event is one of them. By the aggregate location test (C4850's amendment), a family that stands in the OFF scans as in the ON scans is not at the comet. The channel lies in the 1616–1626.5 MHz band, the one ChatGPT's list names for Iridium. The transmitter is not identified here.
3. **The translated-shape test (Part 1) stops at its self-test, as frozen.**
   - The constructed motif was recovered 20 of 20 at 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0 of the noise rms. The winning lags were 16 or 64, with shifts matching the injected translation.
   - Noise passed in 7 of 100 trials, against an allowance of 10.
   - The stationary tone was not flagged (p = 1.0).
   - The hopping tone was flagged at p = 0.05. That is the floor with 19 permutations, a single realization, and no more than the noise rate. But the frozen criterion was that neither tone be flagged, and that the run stops if the injections fail. The shape score was therefore not run on the telescope data.
   - The first 32 frames of each scan are saved, in three slices and both feeds (crops_00NN.npz, hashed, outside git), so the test can be run at the author's word.
