# RESULTS-ata-look: EXPLORATORY open look, ATA public spectra (not a frozen test)

Access audit (ata/map_ata.py, 2026-11-04): 3,192 .fbh5 files listed and every header read, 0 read errors; 48 directories returned HTTP 403 below their parents, 39 of them 3I/ATLAS scan directories (2025-07-05: 13; 2025-07-09: 13; 2025-07-30: 9; 2025-07-10: 4). Fully public 3I/ATLAS spectra: 2025-07-03 (896 files, 1000-3688 MHz, 28 tunings x 16 scans x 2 beams) and 2025-07-08 (2,296 files, 6376-9064 MHz). Under the rule (truly and fully public data only), the July 5, 9, 10 and 30 sessions are out of scope. Every target frequency of the look lies in the July 3 session.

Method (ata/look.py). Ten rest frequencies: HI 1420.405752; Wow! 1420.4556 and 1420.726; OH 1612.231, 1665.4018, 1667.359, 1720.53; C03 1665.633777; 2380.0; 2 x HI 2840.811504. For each of the 16 scans: a window of +-30 kHz along three paths: the comet's true Doppler path from Horizons at the ATA site (drift -0.165 Hz/s at 1420 MHz, -0.184 at 1666, -0.263 at 2380: the class the published positive-only filter removed), the same path with its drift sign flipped (the class the filter kept), and fixed at the dish. Beam 0 on-source, beam 1 reference, recorded simultaneously. Features: scan-average z >= 4 or single-sample z >= 5 in one beam with the other beam quiet (|z| < 2 average, < 4 any sample, +-2 channels). Background: the same with the beams swapped, and the flipped path. Structure recorded per feature: peak sample, share of power in the top two samples, lag-one autocorrelation, the 19-sample on/off pattern.

Result.
- Counts, true comet path / its beam swap / flipped path / its swap / dish-fixed / its swap: HI 596/655/577/700/584/581; WowE 556/624/557/613/572/610; WowM 614/637/600/603/571/601; OH1612 468/449/466/465/569/475; OH1665 459/482/458/469/574/550; C03 500/513/481/525/566/533; OH1667 499/490/492/485/581/603; OH1720 441/433/445/417/520/475; 2380 418/432/420/413/507/473; 2HI 408/469/404/493/531/547. The true path never stands above its own swap or the flipped path by more than the spread among them.
- Persistence: no comet-frame scan-average feature recurs at the same rest frequency (+-4 Hz) in more than two of the 16 scans for any target; the backgrounds reach two as well.
- Strongest features: single-sample spikes at z 7-8 with scan averages near 2, the same spikes appearing on the true and flipped paths (the paths differ by tens of Hz within a scan), and swap spikes of the same size (to 8.6). Their on/off patterns show no structure beyond one or two isolated samples.
- No natural cometary OH appears in the comet frame (expected at the object's July distance; no frame calibrator from this session).

Reading. In the publicly available ATA session, at these ten frequencies, the drift class the published filter removed holds nothing that its sign-flipped twin, the reference beam, or the dish-fixed path does not hold equally. The sign discrepancy stands as a discrepancy in the documented method; at these frequencies on July 3 it hid nothing this look can see. Bounded by: one session; +-30 kHz windows; 16.78 s resolution; the thresholds above.
