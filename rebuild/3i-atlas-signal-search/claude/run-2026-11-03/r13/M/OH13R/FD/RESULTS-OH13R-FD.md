# RESULTS — OH13R-FD: the curvature excess against the original detector's false-drift selections

Plan frozen before any FD statistic (FROZEN-OH13R-FD.sha256), amended once before any FD statistic was computed (amendment 1, two implementation defects in fdstream.py; FROZEN-OH13R-FD-amend1.sha256). At the author's word of 2026-11-21 13:21 ("lets tie up those loose ends").

1. **Selections (step 1).** The original detector at dv = 0 reproduced the catalogue exactly (13 clusters at the catalogue's rest frequencies). Over the 40 false-drift offsets (default_rng(11)) it found 150 clusters (mean 3.75, maximum 8), as the catalogue's context record gives them (fd_selections.json, fdselect.log). Four fell within 1.5 kHz of a known line or the edges and were scored as references; 146 false-drift selections entered the aggregate, with 869 matched controls on their own false tracks. Eleven true-frame candidates and OH13R's 72 controls ran alongside.
2. **Check (step 2).** The streaming D3 reproduces OH13R's stored-window D3 on every true-frame window: 5,976 values compared, worst relative difference 2.3 × 10⁻⁴ (fd_check.json). The true-frame aggregate at d = 2, X is p = 0.0001 (mean Δ 2.18 over 11 candidates), meeting the plan's p ≤ 0.001. The implementation is right and the run continues.
3. **The false-drift aggregate (step 3), fd_reading.json.** At d = 2, X: mean Δ 0.357 over 146 selections, aggregate p = 0.023. Beside it: d = 1, X p = 0.017; d = 1, Y p = 0.059; every other lag and feed p ≥ 0.26 for the false frames. The true frames: d = 2, X p = 0.0001; d = 32, Y p = 0.043; d = 8, X p = 0.077; the rest ≥ 0.11.
4. **Decision, as frozen.** p = 0.023 ≤ 0.05 at d = 2, X: **the excess belongs to the original detector's selection, and OH13R's aggregate is closed as a selection effect.** Windows chosen by the detector carry lag-2 phase curvature in X whether or not they lie on the comet's track.
5. **Recorded, not decision-bearing.** The false-drift excess is smaller per selection (Δ 0.36 against 2.18 at the true frame), and it is spread rather than concentrated: the largest single false frame (F17, one selection, Δ 7.48) removed, the aggregate is p = 0.044 (exploratory, computed after the reading). The selection effect does not by itself account for the size of the true-frame excess; the plan fixed the decision on the sign of the test, not its size, and the decision stands. A plan to measure the size against selection, if wanted, would be a new frozen plan.

No candidate resolves (OH13R); the aggregate is closed (here). OH13R is complete.
