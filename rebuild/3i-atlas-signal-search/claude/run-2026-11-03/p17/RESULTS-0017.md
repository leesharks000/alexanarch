# RESULTS-0017: is the 16 arcmin header position of GBT ON scan 0017 a pointing or a metadata artifact? (exploratory; public data only)

1. The header pattern across the whole night (p17/headers_0002.json: all 432 rawspec.0002 headers, 24 scans, 6-31 nodes per scan). Every node carries an identical header position and start time for a given scan. Each cadence runs ON, OFF 60 arcmin north, ON, OFF 60 arcmin east, ON, OFF 60 arcmin south. Measured against the comet's Horizons position at each scan start (p17/header_offsets.json, p17/slew_fractions.json):
- The first ON of each of the four cadences (0013, 0029, 0045, 0061) sits on the comet: 0.043, 0.063, 0.023, 0.007 arcmin.
- Every one of the other 20 headers lies on the straight line between the previous scan's nominal position and its own, within 2.2 arcmin of that line (most within 1), at a fraction of the slew completed that ranges from 0.00 to 1.01 (median 0.71).
- OFF 0032's header sits on the comet (fraction 0.00): a header written before the slew east began. OFF 0030 is at 0.19 of its slew north, OFF 0014 at 0.51, OFF 0066 at 0.55.
- The four ON scans that return from the east OFF: 0017 at 0.73, 0033 at 0.60, 0049 at 0.67, 0065 at 0.68. 0017's 16.0 arcmin is the ordinary case of this pattern.
Reading: the header position is a snapshot taken at a variable point inside the slew to the scan's target. It records where the dish was when the header was written; it does not record where the dish observed. This agrees with ChatGPT's reading of the rawspec/hpguppi source (status copied once at file start).

2. Settling in the data itself (p17/settle.json; blc23 rawspec.0002, 1.07 s samples). The first twelve samples of 0017 stay within +0.02 to -0.75 percent of its later median power, with no step or ramp; the starts of other scans vary by up to 3 percent with no relation to their header offsets (0013, on the comet by its header, starts 2.8 percent high). L band carries heavy interference here (later scatter 0.5-2.2 percent), so this test can show only that no large transient marks 0017's start.

3. Which sky 0017 saw: Galactic hydrogen at 1420 MHz (blc24 rawspec.0002, 0013-0018; p17/hi_profiles.json). The hydrogen profile rises steadily across the on-source scans (peak fraction 0.183, 0.209, 0.229 for 0013, 0015, 0017) as the elevation climbs from 8.7 to 13.9 degrees, so the on-target profile drifts with time. With that drift extrapolated linearly from 0013 and 0015, 0017 departs from the on-target prediction by 0.25 percent rms across the line, against 2.3 percent rms for the east OFF 60 arcmin away. Projected onto the east-OFF difference, the residual coefficient is -0.04 (bootstrap 95 percent interval -0.05 to 0.14); a pointing 16 arcmin east would give about 0.27 if the hydrogen changes linearly toward the east OFF. Uncorrected for the drift, the same projection gives 0.24 for 0017 and 0.19 for the two on-target scans 0015-0013, so the drift correction carries the test; its assumption is a linear drift over the twenty minutes.

Reading. The public data argue that 0017 observed the comet: its header is the ordinary mid-slew snapshot that 20 of the night's 24 headers show, and its hydrogen profile matches the on-target sky after the elevation drift. The antenna record for 05:08:17-05:13:15 UTC would still settle it directly; it is not public. With the header objection weakened, the thirteen (draft section 7) return to their earlier status: a reproducible, beam-sided, comet-frame excess sensitive to normalization and threshold, unconfirmed in another band (S band) or at Parkes.
