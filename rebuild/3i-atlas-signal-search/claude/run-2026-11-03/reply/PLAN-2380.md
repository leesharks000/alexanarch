# PLAN-2380: reply-frequency doublet search, Parkes 2025-07-31 and GBT 2025-12-18 (frozen before any look at 2380 MHz in either dataset)

Question. Is there a narrowband two-tone signal near 2380 MHz, the frequency of the 1974 Arecibo message (frequency-shift keyed, 10 Hz shift, 10 bit/s, under three minutes), with the two tones about 10 Hz apart, on-source and absent off-source, in any of three frames?

Frames (position of 2380.000 MHz at each sample time):
- topo: 2380.000 MHz at the dish.
- comet: 2380 x (1 - v/c), v the Horizons range-rate of C/2025 N1 from the observatory.
- bary: 2380 x (1 - v_b/c), v_b the barycentric correction toward the source position in the file header (astropy radial_velocity_correction, kind barycentric) taken with the sign that maps a barycentric-rest 2380 MHz to its topocentric arrival frequency.

Data.
- Parkes: PKS_Jul_31_2025, the node whose band holds 2380 MHz (blc15, 2368-2496 MHz), six 0000.fil scans, 2 Hz x 15 s, 20 samples.
- GBT: blc24 cadence 0029 (2214-2401 MHz), six rawspec.0000.h5 scans (ON/OFF), 2.794 Hz x 6.08 s, 49 samples, read by HTTP range through h5py.

Method. Per scan normalise r = X / median over samples - 1 per channel. For each frame, map every sample onto an offset grid u (channels from the frame position of 2380 MHz at that sample, integer shift), and average the scan's samples on that grid. Robust z per scan-average spectrum and per sample.
Doublet statistic D_s(u) = min(z(u), z(u+s)). Target spacings: Parkes s in {4,5,6} channels (8-12 Hz); GBT s in {3,4} (8.4-11.2 Hz).
- Persistent doublet (scan-average): D >= 4 for some target s in at least two on-source scans at u within +-2 channels of each other, and D < 3 for every target s at u +-2 in every off-source scan. Clustered within +-2 channels.
- Single-sample doublet: D >= 5 for some target s in one on-source sample; vetoed when any off-source scan average has D >= 3 or any off-source sample has D >= 4 within +-2 channels. Clustered per scan within +-2 channels.
- Window: |u| x channel width <= 15 kHz.
- Swap: off-source scans as target, on-source as veto.

Controls.
- Frequency controls: the same procedure at 2380 + k MHz, k in {-10, -8, -6, -4, -2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10}, on both instruments.
- Spacing controls at 2380: Parkes s in {7} and {9} channels (14, 18 Hz); GBT s in {6} and {7} (16.8, 19.6 Hz), each treated as its own spacing set.

Lead, per instrument and frame. Target count (persistent + single-sample) above every frequency control, above the swap, above both spacing controls, and at least one persistent doublet in the target. Every target doublet is listed with frame, offset, scans, z of both tones, whether or not the lead fires.

Injection before the run (Parkes): a doublet 10 Hz apart at comet-frame 2380 MHz + 3 kHz, in all 20 samples of the three on-source scans, each tone z ~ 6 in the scan average. It must be recovered as a persistent doublet in the comet frame.

Laws record; they do not prevent. A deviation is written into the results.
