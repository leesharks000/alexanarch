## YOUR TASK

You are given two verses of a Greek visionary book. Write what a teacher's likeness of them would be: 250–450 words of continuous koine narrative prose, in Greek, in which a teacher of the past, with followers around him, says what these verses say by means of a short invented story from common life. Precede it with a short log, in English, of each decision you made and what in the source it came from.

**Constraints.**
- Do not consult, quote from memory, or imitate any specific ancient narrative. Your job is derivation from the source.
- No translation of the source or of your passage.
