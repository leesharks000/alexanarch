Revelation First · working demonstration · 26 November 2026

The midrashim transform, run

Each card takes a sentence of the Apocalypse (or of Philo, upstream of it), applies a short list of named operators, and gets a prediction: which words the later text keeps, in what order, which it drops, what it puts in their place. The prediction is then checked against the Greek of the later text, against rival sources, and against every verse of the New Testament and the Septuagint.

Untick an operator and the card runs again without it. The derivations were written with their targets in view, so a pass shows that the change can be stated in these operators; the controls and the search show how few other verses fit the same statement.

Texts: Nestle 1904, Swete's Septuagint and Cohn–Wendland's Philo as seated in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive (alexanarch.org). A member is matched with accents and case removed. The search counts come from the engine's run over the seats and do not change when an operator is switched off.
