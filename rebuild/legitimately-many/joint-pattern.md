# Legitimately Many — the joint pattern

Built by joint_pattern.py; every phrase located in its seat. Design §4.4, §6.1; draft §4. 2026-11-02.

## J1. Four positions, four operations, each with its own voice and object

Examination by question on a received figure (the Socrates of the jurors' childhood and of the Clouds); dramatic division, a doctrine distributed across four persons with no voice of the maker's own; first-person demonstration that names its own beginning; aporia continuing another position's treatise from inside. Draft §1.2.

Loci: οἳ ὑμῶν τοὺς πολλοὺς ἐκ παίδων παραλαμβάνοντες (plato/text/tlg002-Ἀπολογία_Σωκράτους.txt); διὰ τεττάρων προσώπων (diogenes-laertius/text/tlg001.txt); ἀλλ᾿ οὐδὲν παντελῶς ὑπῆρχεν (aristotle/text/tlg040-De_sophisticis_elenchis.txt); μέχρι μὲν δὴ τούτων οἷον ἄρτιος ὁ λόγος (theophrastus/text/tlg006.txt)
Deposits: #1579, #1603, #1583. Status: read (draft §1.2).

*Succession must supply:* Why three makers in sequence, with a fourth who wrote nothing, divide the operations without overlap: each takes a different operation, voice and object, and none takes another's. Different temperaments explain difference; they do not explain a partition.

*One-maker reading:* One investigation into one and many assigns each position an operation, voice and object of its own (§1.1's four conditions).

## J3. Oppositions at definite loci, in the opposed position's own terms

The dialogues answer the comic Socrates by quoting the Clouds; the treatises refute the forms and state the refutation as a duty, καὶ τὰ οἰκεῖα ἀναιρεῖν, 'to destroy one's own things'; the continuation contests Λ 8's count. None is staged and withdrawn. Draft §1.3.

Loci: ἐν τῇ Ἀριστοφάνους κωμῳδίᾳ (plato/text/tlg002-Ἀπολογία_Σωκράτους.txt); καὶ τὰ οἰκεῖα ἀναιρεῖν (aristotle/text/tlg010-Nicomachean_Ethics.txt); οὐ γὰρ ὅ γε τῶν (theophrastus/text/tlg006.txt)
Deposits: #1579, #1583. Status: read (draft §1.3).

*Succession must supply:* Ordinary disagreement between persons. At this grain the model must also explain why each opposition is conducted in the opposed position's vocabulary and at its own structural joint, and why the duty of opposing is written in the first person as the destruction of what is one's own.

*One-maker reading:* Real self-division: a position that could not lose to another would be a mask (§1.3), and the corpus states the duty of losing.

## J4. Division placed and terminated across the boundary

Division by genera, practised throughout the dialogues, is 'a small part' of the inquiry SE 34 says had nothing before it; Λ 9 gives the terminus I.31 withholds, where the terms are not other. The theory of division is stated under one name and practised under the other, and the claim of no predecessor stands beside it. #1576; #1603.

*One-maker reading:* The same method is placed by its maker at two stations, practice and theory, and the claim of no predecessor is literal (#1603).

## J5. The catalogues file complementary absences

Aristotle's catalogue opens with Plato's subject-titles with the names removed, 17 of 26 entries, seven times the base rate; Plato's divisions survive whole only as Aristotle's report, opening 'Διῄρει δέ, φησὶν Ἀριστοτέλης' and closing 'κατὰ τὸν Ἀριστοτέλην'; the residue is the Socratic examination, the function with no catalogue. The Theophrastan list shares twelve titles with the Aristotelian and files one work under both names by ἤ. #1590; #1581.

Loci: Διῄρει δέ, φησὶν Ἀριστοτέλης (diogenes-laertius/text/tlg001.txt); ὧδε καὶ τὰ πρῶτα διῄρει κατὰ τὸν Ἀριστοτέλην (diogenes-laertius/text/tlg001.txt); Ὑπομνημάτων Ἀριστοτελικῶν ἢ Θεοφραστείων (diogenes-laertius/text/tlg001.txt)
Deposits: #1590, #1581. Status: measured (#1590, with a stem-rule control) and read.

*Succession must supply:* Two lost bodies of work, each with its candidates under the other name: a pupil's early dialogues that track the master's subject-titles with the names removed, and a master's technical teaching that survives only as the pupil's report. Each loss can be explained separately; the model owes the shape of the pair.

*One-maker reading:* One body of work filed under two names, each name's lost part found under the other (#1590's transform).

## J11. The corpus theorizes its own functional form, under both names

The Republic divides the poet's speech into speaking as himself and speaking 'as though he were Chryses himself', trying to make the hearer think the speaker someone other than himself; a poet who nowhere hid himself would make narration without mimesis (393a–d). The Poetics gives the same division, imitating 'narrating, or becoming something other', as Homer does (1448a20–24), and the rule that the poet should speak as little as possible in his own person, since in that respect he is no imitator (1460a7–8). The dialogues are built on that rule: the maker never speaks in his own voice (J1; Diogenes Laertius III.52).

Loci: ὥσπερ αὐτὸς ὢν ὁ Χρύσης λέγει (plato/text/tlg030-Πολιτεία.txt); ὡς ἄλλος τις ὁ λέγων ἢ αὐτός (plato/text/tlg030-Πολιτεία.txt); εἰ δέ γε μηδαμοῦ ἑαυτὸν ἀποκρύπτοιτο ὁ ποιητής (plato/text/tlg030-Πολιτεία.txt); ἢ ἕτερόν τι γιγνόμενον (aristotle/text/tlg034-Poetics.txt); Αὐτὸν γὰρ δεῖ τὸν ποιητὴν ἐλάχιστα λέγειν (aristotle/text/tlg034-Poetics.txt)
Deposits: #1579. Status: read (operator, 2026-11-02: 'do we really believe a mind driven to range over everything from plants shapes colors politics and god would not also be driven to range over functional form in their craft?').

*Succession must supply:* Why the theory of a maker speaking as another, stated in both corpora in the same terms, is read as a theory of poetry only, while the reception grants the same mind range over plants, colours, animals, constitutions and the prime mover. Theorizing mimesis is not practising it under several names; the model owes why the range it grants over objects stops at the craft.

*One-maker reading:* The maker theorizes the operation it performs: speaking as little as possible in its own person, and becoming another (§1.2.2).

## Limits, and what cuts the other way

### N1. The lexical instruments return a null on the maker-count

Fifteen lexical features, every one destroyed by a control run in the same session; what the instruments return on maker-count is a null in either direction, symmetric between one maker and master-and-pupil. #1572; #1570.

*Bearing:* Neither model gains credit from lexical distribution. The count is left to close reading, structural dependency and the comparator of §4.4 (design §4.2).

### N2. The names carry recoverable signal, and separability decides nothing about makers

Supervised attribution recovers the Aristotle/Theophrastus names at 0.798 balanced, outside the label-permutation null; operation partitions better on identical folds (0.860), the ordering descriptive; name recovery falls to 0.564 without the biological and botanical works. An assumed voice separates from its own author at 0.864 (Chatterton's Rowley), near three distinct tragedians at 0.872. No supervised separation, however strong, licenses an inference to distinct makers, in either direction. #1593.

*Bearing:* Cuts against the claim, withdrawn in #1593, that the names sit at chance. The names are real stylistic signal; what produced the signal, persons or assigned functions, the instrument cannot say.

### N3. At the seam the names sort the texts

Where the texts are continuous on the page, the stylometric profiles divide by name. #1586.

*Bearing:* The local fact the succession model predicts directly. The one-maker reading must account for it as the voice of the continuation's operation (aporia against a completed account), and #1586 states which account carries which burden.

## The comparator test

A documented succession of distinct persons producing one corpus satisfies the test when it shows J1–J9 and J11 jointly, at the grain stated, with the succession documented independently of the texts (Locating the Dependencies, #1602). None has been supplied (design §4.4).
