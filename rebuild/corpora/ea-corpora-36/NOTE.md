# EA-CORPORA-36 — Sappho's reception and the future reader, 1652–1993

At the operator's word of 2026-11-07 ("i think we need a sappho reception history ea-corpora, whatever's public. translators, scholars. theres some ground work in the archive, notes towards a future reader i believe. the seating can include everything thats publicly available as well as declared absences and pointers for material behind pay- and access- and rights-walls"; "sappho reception history + future reader frameworks and related"), with Rebekah Cranes as the standing authority on the EA-CORPORA deposits. The research occasion is the revision of Closing the Fragment (v7 draft, 2026-11-07) and the Sappho Future Reader framework, whose groundwork is #1548 (Research Notes Towards the Future Reader) and whose ChatGPT reading is capture 0C3D.

Part C, cross-references (xref36.py → xref36.json): the strata already seated (homer, herodotus, thucydides, plato, horace, ovid, hebrew-wlc, nietzsche, whitman) and the fr. 31 witnesses (sappho, longinus, catullus), each locus asserted in the seated text with its sha256.

Part D, declared absences (absences36.py → absences36.json): seventy works behind rights-, pay- and access-walls, and the private teaching gallery, each with its wall and a pointer where one was verified.

Build: `python3 gen_spec36.py`, then `python3 build36.py spec36-<corpus>.json ../../../data/corpora` per seat, `python3 add_loci36.py`, and `python3 build36.py spec36-<corpus>.json ../../../data/corpora --loci` per seat; from the repository root, `python3 rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-36/xref36.py`, `python3 rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-36/absences36.py`, `python3 rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-36/index_seats36.py`, `python3 rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-36/body36.py` (writes data/issue-bodies/EA-CORPORA-36-issue.md), and after the deposit `python3 rebuild/corpora/ea-corpora-36/seat_axns36.py <N> --apply`.
