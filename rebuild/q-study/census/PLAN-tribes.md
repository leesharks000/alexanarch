# Plan, frozen before any count: the mourning tribes and the clouds

Occasion. Census 1 (PLAN-mark13.md) returned the trigrams πασαι αι φυλαι / αι φυλαι τησ / φυλαι τησ γησ shared by Matt 24:30 and Rev 1:7 and by no part of Mark 13. Matt 24:30a is Matthew's addition to Mark 13:26. This observation was made after census 1 and is not a result of it; this plan tests it.

Question. Where, in the seated Greek, do three elements stand together: a form of κόπτομαι (normalized stem κοψ or κοπτ or κεκοπ), a form of φυλή (stem φυλ, excluding φυλακ- and φυλασσ-), and a form of νεφέλη (stem νεφελ)?

Texts. LXX (lxx-swete, all files); NT (gnt-nestle1904, all books); Josephus (josephus/text); Philo (philo/text); the Greek pseudepigrapha (second-temple-pseudepigrapha/text); Justin's Dialogue (justin-dialogus/text); Irenaeus (irenaeus-adversus-haereses/text, the Greek and the Latin, which can only match on Greek words quoted in it); Eusebius, HE (eusebius-historia-ecclesiastica/text).

Window. For versed texts (tab-separated reference and text) the window is three consecutive verses; for unversed text it is 60 consecutive tokens, stepped by 20. Normalization as in census 1.

Reported. Every window holding all three elements, with its reference or its line, and the window's text. Hits in the same work closer than one window are merged.

Reading. A combination found only in Matt 24:30 and Rev 1:7 before the second-century writers is a marked contact between Matthew's special matter and the extant Apocalypse. Its scriptural antecedents (Dan 7:13, Zech 12:10–14) are extant and are reported. The census decides no direction.
