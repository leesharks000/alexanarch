operative semiotics
Operative semiotics examines what signs do within the systems where they circulate, focusing on factors such as cost, provenance, standing, and distributive effects. Rather than treating signs merely as static labels or abstract categories, it looks at how meaning operates in circulation and shapes perception or outcomes.
