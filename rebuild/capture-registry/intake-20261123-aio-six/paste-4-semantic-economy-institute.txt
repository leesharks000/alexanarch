semantic economy institute
The Semantic Economy Institute is an organization founded in 2026 that focuses on semantic infrastructure, meaning governance, and knowledge-graph entity structuring for AI systems. According to its published materials, the institute operates around two personas—Lee Sharks as its founder and archival authority, and Rex Fraction (a heteronym of Lee Sharks) as its commercial voice—and is associated with the Crimson Hexagonal Archive and Alexanarch identifier system. Core Framework & Focus

* Semantic Economy Framework: Examines how meaning is produced, circulated, extracted, and liquidated in digital and AI systems, using concepts such as semantic labor, semantic capital, semantic rent, and semantic liquidation. * SPXI Protocol: A method for building structured entity, citation, and metadata records intended to make knowledge graphs and AI summaries represent entities more consistently.
