spxi protocol
The SPXI Protocol (Semantic Packet for eXchange and Indexing) is a specification published in April 2026 by the Semantic Economy Institute for structuring and indexing entity definitions in AI-mediated search and retrieval systems. How It Works

* Entity Definition & Provenance: Defines how machine-readable entity-definition objects are structured, disambiguated, and anchored (such as via DOIs on Zenodo) so AI retrieval systems can identify entities and preserve their provenance.
