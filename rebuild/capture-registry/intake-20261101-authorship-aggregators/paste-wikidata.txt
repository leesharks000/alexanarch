* Open License: All data is released under a Creative Commons CC0 public domain dedication, meaning anyone can use, copy, and modify it freely. * Items and Identifiers: Each entity gets a unique "Q-number" identifier (such as `Q42` for Douglas Adams), while properties use "P-numbers" (such as `P69` for "educated at"). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESawHrOzAV-q1sLRWmSZ3t4UztkdKVWvwlDZbOiqYokVLdbNJhzAYa99ArZlnMVJPZ28_7kWvbsl-N3PQ3JRlY2PhO6x0KTgz8ROB8_vO33foNafXgDiryk_IucQa8aiQJmUh7LlVB93FCNM2e), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHrOzAVMBcUGlBEKqCxYHzXJg898tTMhn2y-ajFU3Q8X5_yyeLHBYjSuN43ts2kXdnkTf0_s9kdPqIaMLT2gWjIFpBhFfgB2N5WPZy2g5LGPs3Hvx_0aLLUyfh05rYvYwrRJ5Ry8frO)]
* Statements and Triples: Information is stored as subject-predicate-object statements (claims) that link entities to values, often backed by references to external sources. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZwHrOzAVkf7JKxew0chGfQQGHkrEmvPS5FJcj1gsKRIqgbrq-4yt_reEMI4HwyISwgbv4wNKmOFT3gq_r3l31uZNPprlFmCdho4pUnOw30b8vhMR11Zxy1Sxye2fiPMkgxQhDdD076c), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESawHrOzAV-q1sLRWmSZ3t4UztkdKVWvwlDZbOiqYokVLdbNJhzAYa99ArZlnMVJPZ28_7kWvbsl-N3PQ3JRlY2PhO6x0KTgz8ROB8_vO33foNafXgDiryk_IucQa8aiQJmUh7LlVB93FCNM2e)]
* Multilingual: Labels, descriptions, and aliases exist across hundreds of languages so content can be managed and viewed globally.
