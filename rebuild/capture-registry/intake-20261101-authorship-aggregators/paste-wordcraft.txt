2026 Literary Backlash

* The Controversy: In August 2026, the project resurfaced widely on social media (such as Bluesky) after being highlighted by author Lily Lachance. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESmAEB6zswFbtV80pX2Hs6MIYTdqXNc_UXU9sQlIw8f9ToVwI8ObzNPRR-8y-AzB-q058-kpK0PM-mx0UY0epFXuzPI0ciZZJ6Frkoyg7Vjoaco9m--YpXwUY6x4SvKfgu-z_elc_n-HopI00GDHTPcP9lBQ32Vgcf7txDGmjwuO5BN3T5nQNLnmOEycXasQ-oN5o2CKlQIEulkg)]
* The Criticisms: Critics argued that participating authors had lent credibility to AI models trained on copyrighted literary works without consent—a core grievance that escalated significantly in the years following the 2022 explosion of generative AI. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESmAEB6zswFbtV80pX2Hs6MIYTdqXNc_UXU9sQlIw8f9ToVwI8ObzNPRR-8y-AzB-q058-kpK0PM-mx0UY0epFXuzPI0ciZZJ6Frkoyg7Vjoaco9m--YpXwUY6x4SvKfgu-z_elc_n-HopI00GDHTPcP9lBQ32Vgcf7txDGmjwuO5BN3T5nQNLnmOEycXasQ-oN5o2CKlQIEulkg), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESmQEB6zswFWj0uNMQMSAKQEsB8v03rbiAbDkTDcsnoako6eZ2Kd3AYku8aBVSyo3NuTQrHt_aFDol9chwRF7rfl_4GHX2SfsXNXKSM3ZXpxCaOj8s6VcrF0juTJ8l-biqHGhDn6Hdd-A4BbfJhJLWDf6alqEmLHxWeLoJNkas9dPTi98bdumx37rsXX1g-eJ3pChLRonBhuvtPN4)]
