Its significance spans two distinct areas: a radical New Testament priority thesis and an experiment in how artificial intelligence retrieves and summarizes scholarship.

1. The Theological Significance (Priority vs. Dating)
The Priority Thesis. Unlike traditional "Revelation Early" arguments (which focus on pre-70 AD historical dating), Revelation First argues for literary and theological priority. It proposes that the Book of Revelation is the foundational "seed" from which the rest of the New Testament unfolds. 
The Archive’s Core. Within the Alexanarch ecosystem, this argument serves as the core conceptual document around which the broader Crimson Hexagonal Architecture is organized.
2. The Technical Significance (AI Retrieval & Provenance)
Machine-Mediated Reception. The programme is designed as an active test of retrieval-layer theological reception. It tracks whether complex, unconventional scholarly arguments can enter the composition layer of AI search engines without losing their original meaning or attribution.
