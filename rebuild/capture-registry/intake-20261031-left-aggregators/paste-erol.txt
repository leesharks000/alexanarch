Core Definition

* Anti-Revisionism: Opposition to abandoning orthodox revolutionary theory. It arose as a post-WWII critique of official communist parties—particularly following the Soviet Union's de-Stalinization under Nikita Khrushchev in 1956—which anti-revisionists viewed as insufficiently revolutionary. Groups later split to align with either Chinese or Albanian political and ideological positions during the Sino-Soviet split.
