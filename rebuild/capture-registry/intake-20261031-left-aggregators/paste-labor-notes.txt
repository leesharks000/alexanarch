Labor Notes is an American media and organizing project founded in 1979 by labor activist Kim Moody. Operating as a voice for the "troublemaking wing" of the labor movement, it reports on rank-and-file union democracy, strikes, and independent worker-led organizing efforts. The organization is headquartered in Detroit, Michigan, with an additional office in Brooklyn, New York.
