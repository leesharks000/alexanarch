The International Institute of Social History (IISH; Dutch: Internationaal Instituut voor Sociale Geschiedenis, IISG) is one of the world's premier archives and research centers dedicated to global labor and social history. Located in Amsterdam, Netherlands, the institute specializes in documenting how labor relations, socio-economic inequalities, and social movements have evolved over time.
