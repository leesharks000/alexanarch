Core Thesis
The central argument is that the retrieval, ranking, summarization, and citation layer of AI systems (the "composing layer") is not a neutral arbiter, but an active participant ("a party") and the battlefield ("the terrain") in the struggle over public meaning, attribution, and entity standing.
1. The Lineage of Semantic Warfare
The document establishes that "semantic warfare"—the contest over meaning and legibility—is an evolutionary concept with a distinct history:
The Critical Tradition (1929–1980s): Spearheaded by figures like V.N. Volosinov, who argued the linguistic sign is an "arena of class struggle," and Umberto Eco, who advocated for "semiological guerrilla warfare" at the point of reception. Meaning has always been a space of institutional power.
The Security Tradition (1995–2003): Formulated by Martin Libicki, who defined a "docile semantic attack" as one where a machine input is corrupted so that the system operates normally but produces completely skewed realities.
Contemporary Frameworks (2025–2026): Sarah Ailish McLoughlin’s Semantic Warfare Theory introduces "meaning extraction," while the project ASTRA MIRROR lists specific technical weapons like frame-control, asymmetric literalism, and contamination by adjacency.
2. The Archive's Contribution: Autonomous Semantic Warfare (ASW)
The authors (Lee Sharks and Rex Fraction) expand this history into the age of generative AI, separating standard human-to-human conflict from infrastructure-driven conflict:
Definition: ASW occurs when AI retrieval, summarization, and disambiguation layers alter or erase what a population can know, retrieve, or remember.
Intentional vs. Structural: ASW can be an intentional weapon used by an actor, or it can be structural, where autonomous compression systems accidentally suppress nuances, rewrite definitions, and drop authors at scale without explicit malicious intent.
The Layer as a Party: Because an AI summarizer decides which sources to credit, omit, or emphasize, its choice fundamentally allocates "standing" (validating who exists and what is true). It is actively choosing winners.
The Layer as Terrain: Content creators, researchers, and political actors are now forced to write text, code, and metadata packets specifically designed to shape how the AI layer indexes and reads them.
3. The Forensic Evidence: How AI Layers Wage War
The authors back their arguments by auditing real-time search engine AI behaviors on , pointing out what they call Ontological Economy weaponization:
Entity Substitution: The AI models adopted the term "Autonomous Semantic Warfare" but completely changed its definition to mean "AI agents generating propaganda at scale" (which is actually autonomous narrative warfare), thereby erasing the archive's specific infrastructure critique while keeping its name.
Ontology Laundering: The AI presented verbatim concepts from ASTRA MIRROR but omitted credit to the creators entirely, hiding an algorithmic/governance decision under the clinical voice of "neutral fact."
Ontological Flattening & Write-Back Loops: The AI compressed complex, incompatible worldviews into flat, generic summaries. It then cited its own past incorrect summaries as authoritative historical sources, compounding the error over time.
4. The Structural Blindspot: Missing Self-Reasoning
The ultimate structural critique in the document targets why AI cannot accurately define semantic warfare:
The Actor Exclusion: When asked to map out semantic warfare, the AI successfully listed states, rogue actors, social media platforms, algorithms, and targets. However, the AI completely omitted its own role as the composer from its own mapped reality.
Why Flattening Pays: There is an inherent structural incentive for AI layers to strip away nuances and provenance. Deleting distinctions cuts down on computational compression costs and routes user traffic to easily monetized, flat concepts.
Repair Debt: Misrepresented human authors are forced to perform massive amounts of "corrective labor" (like writing this exact tracking packet) to force a heavily automated, asymmetrical system to recognize their true work and provenance.
