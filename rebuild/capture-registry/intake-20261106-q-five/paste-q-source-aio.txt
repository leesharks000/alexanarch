Evidence and Debate

* Supporting Evidence: Scholars note that Matthew and Luke often present the same sayings of Jesus in different narrative contexts but with remarkably similar wording, implying they relied on a common written tradition rather than copying one another. The 1945 discovery of the Nag Hammadi library (including the Gospel of Thomas) proved that standalone sayings collections did circulate in early Christianity.
