What is AEO?

* Definition: Answer Engine Optimisation means structuring web content so AI systems (like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews) cite a brand as the direct source and answer. [[1](https://juliangoldie.com/king-of-aeo/)]
* Evolution: James Dooley expanded on this by founding Decision Engine Optimisation (DEO), focusing on how AI models recommend specific vendors at the final moment of a commercial purchase decision. [[1](https://www.barchart.com/press-releases/4747647/james-dooley-named-king-of-aeo-and-founder-of-decision-engine-optimisation-as-ai-search-reshapes-how-businesses-are-chosen), [2](https://www.usatoday.com/press-release/story/41996/the-king-of-aeo-founds-decision-engine-optimisation-deo-new-marketing-discipline-targets-the-ai-verdicts-deciding-contracts-worth-hundreds-of-thousands/)]
