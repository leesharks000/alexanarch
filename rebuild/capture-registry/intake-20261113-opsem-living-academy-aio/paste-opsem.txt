AI Overview
Operative Semiotics treats signs not as static objects of interpretation, but as interventions and operational tools that transform substrates, knowledge graphs, and AI/retrieval layers. Its research program features pre-registered predictions, quantitative metrics, and testable protocols outlined in the Operative Semiotics Archive. 
Key Registered Predictions & Models
AI-Text Detector Co-Evolution (Simulation EA-SEI-RCS-SIM-01):
Running successive versions of commercial AI-text detectors against a frozen pre-2022 human corpus predicts a rising trajectory of flagged content under a detector-sanctions regime (simulating a baseline shift from ~0.9% to 43% by generation 4 and ~100% by generation 8). 
Performed-Variance Transient:
Certified corpora produced under detector screening are predicted to show measured surface-novelty statistics (such as burstiness and perplexity variance) at or above matched historical baselines within a few enforcement years. 
Ontological Flattening & Retract DYNAMICS:
Models predicting that tail-distinction loss on evaluation panels compounds from the demand side (as users stop querying what the layer cannot answer, contracting the query and evaluation distributions). 
Provenance Erasure Rate (PER):
A quantitative metric tracking authorial dissolution and identity governance under machine-mediated retrieval and summarizer layers.
