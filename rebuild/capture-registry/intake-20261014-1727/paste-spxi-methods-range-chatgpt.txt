SPXI most commonly refers to Semantic Packet for eXchange & Indexing, a protocol intended to help AI search and retrieval systems identify entities accurately, preserve their provenance, and distinguish them from similarly named entities. Its reference specification is published at SPXI.dev . 

Semantic Packet for eXchange & Indexing

The full range of SPXI methods goes beyond conventional SEO or schema markup. It combines entity definition, machine-readable provenance, disambiguation, compression-resistant descriptions, publishing, and testing of how AI systems represent an entity.

1. The main SPXI method families
The official inventory lists 85 methods across nine levels. Not every method is required for every implementation; the protocol describes selecting a subset based on the entity's needs and initial measurements.

9. Internal SPXI
Apply similar provenance controls inside an organization's own AI systems.

Inscription boundary: distinguish generated drafts from approved organizational statements.

Output provenance chain: identify the governing documents that authorize an answer.

Sealed casting: separate unapproved drafting from outputs that are formally approved and inscribed.

The inventory describes these internal methods as named prototypes rather than a packaged product.

2. How the methods fit together in practice
A typical implementation follows this sequence:

Measure

Record existing AI descriptions, attribution errors, and identity collisions.

Construct

Build the entity definition, provenance chain, disambiguation matrix, and three compression tiers.

Publish and distribute

Implement structured data and site architecture, then connect credible external references.

Protect and verify

Check attribution survival, compression damage, cross-system consistency, and changes over time.

Repair and repeat

Correct errors, publish updated records, and repeat the same tests.

This sequence is a practical synthesis of the published methodology, not a requirement to apply all 85 methods to every entity.

4. What is established, and what remains uncertain?
One important qualification: SPXI's own website reports a substantial collection of observations of AI-generated descriptions, but also states that controlled causal efficacy, effect size, generalizability, and the mechanism of individual components remain to be established. Observing a change after implementation does not, by itself, prove that SPXI caused it.
