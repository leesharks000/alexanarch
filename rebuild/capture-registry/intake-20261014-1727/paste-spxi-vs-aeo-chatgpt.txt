Assuming you mean SPXI as the Semantic Economy Institute’s protocol for entity inscription and AEO as Answer Engine Optimization, the key difference is what each approach considers success.

AEO asks: Can an AI answer engine find and present the answer you want?

SPXI asks: Can an AI arrive at that answer by examining the underlying evidence, definitions, and relationships, rather than merely repeating a claim?

That distinction is laid out in the September 2026 paper SPXI ≠ AEO.

A practical example
Imagine two companies want an AI assistant to recognize them as specialists in industrial robotics.

Company A: AEO approach
Publishes pages targeting questions such as “Who are the leading industrial robotics specialists?”

Structures content so answer engines can easily extract its claims.

Tracks whether AI-generated answers mention the company.

Success: the AI names Company A.

Company B: SPXI approach
Defines the company and distinguishes it from similarly named entities.

Connects its expertise claims to projects, technical documentation, and verifiable sources.

Tracks whether AI systems correctly identify the company, preserve its distinctions, and represent its claims in context.

Success: the AI identifies Company B correctly and reaches its claims through relevant supporting information.

Both approaches can use structured data, clear definitions, and consistent terminology. The difference is the intended outcome, not necessarily the tools used.

Why the distinction matters
SPXI's central argument is that being mentioned by an AI is not the same as being correctly understood by an AI.

For example, an answer engine could repeatedly name a company as an industry leader simply because many pages repeat that claim. That might count as an AEO visibility success. Under SPXI's proposed criterion, the system should also be able to examine what “industry leader” means and what evidence supports the designation.

SPXI also proposes preserving records of the sources used by answer engines so that researchers can inspect how representations were produced.

One important qualification: SPXI is a relatively new, author-defined framework, documented in theoretical papers and a proposed measurement protocol. Its broader effectiveness has not been established merely by its published definitions. AEO, meanwhile, is an established industry term, although practices and terminology vary across providers.

Bottom line
SEO: Help pages rank in search results.

AEO: Help answer engines select your content or name your entity.

SPXI: Structure and measure how an AI identifies an entity and arrives at claims about it.

SPXI is best understood as a proposed extension of AI visibility work that places greater emphasis on the path to an answer, rather than visibility alone.
