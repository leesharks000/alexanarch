SPXI (Semantic Packet for eXchange & Indexing) is a formal protocol specification designed for the durable inscription and structural defense of digital entities and technical terms within AI-mediated knowledge and summarizer layers. Core Concepts and Purpose

* Entity Inscription: It provides a framework for publishers and developers to durably anchor definitions, concepts, and metadata so they resist "entity dissolution" or misinterpretation during AI summarization. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWAHrOzAVYmrpdhlcFXZr1rmPd_N6syxqKGzmykDf1u6wRmY2f2W4tlN8hx-KglMPnjm-7h4NbPI8xZTFQi9pX__e9LIK0sh5MhQBfgb0JAbg4xR7K9mUTxQ), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWwHrOzAVlg3vLyxh1WHbQD4e4_WdSjhhnC1njBySp_tAcR0c_l8e420dO65oGy4BKyUK-yxEs3zNDuQrBpfSXK-Ys4UBJ5K3etNcdYHukFslnNGe__EH9_Y2uu8)]
* Beyond GEO: It functions as a broader retrieval architecture containing Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) methods as a proper subset, adding explicit ontological-layer entity construction. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfwHrOzAVKJygr1iD9-HPS9uWdqZ_t6X_pQdw1PowAVWSt5Q-h5PfNwoYApk4apzcgBdRMt-GobPtXDvJYi_10tmBFIAtsYO3avrF_B42A6NFIShvXFslmrlAMx2IYMgOWWB5SOp-L4g_p5TMN0GSepQPt9Bq-FkiRn799tGFbaI)]
* Website Implementation: Standards like the [SPXI for Websites Protocol](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWwHrOzAV7-E86P6Gc5Oc8x5iB3IFxKcZKszgKWaincOTYTlMV3mvpS7J630T0p5V13xLHnD9mt1gbBaCZMzzSo2XSwtFXDvSNWWF4jcv3yRI1np5S9otVFmLbpU) define technical stacks to apply these semantic inscription rules directly to web platforms.
