It defines how an entity should be composed, structured, and inscribed so that AI systems can retrieve it consistently and semantically. 🧩 Where SPXI Operates in the Retrieval Stack SPXI works at the entity-definition layer—the part of the retrieval stack responsible for composing entities before they are indexed or ranked 1 . Above raw content extraction (GEO). Above SEO-like ranking layers. At the layer where the entity’s semantic identity is formed. This means SPXI governs how an entity is represented, not how it is ranked or parsed. 🌀 Core Concept: The “Retrieval Basin” SPXI is designed to shape the retrieval basin—the prior compositional state that future retrievals tend to converge toward 1 . When an entity is first defined, its initial composition becomes a basin. Subsequent retrievals and updates gravitate toward that basin unless reshaped. SPXI provides the rules for shaping or re‑shaping that basin while it is still mutable. This concept is important in modern AI retrieval because large models tend to stabilize around early representations unless explicitly guided. 📦 What SPXI Packets Represent Based on the sources, SPXI packets are: Semantic objects describing an entity’s identity. Designed for exchange and indexing across AI systems 1 . Intended to ensure consistent retrieval, even across different AI agents or platforms. 🔍 Why SPXI Exists The protocol addresses a growing need in AI retrieval systems: Entities must be machine-interpretable, not just textually described. Retrieval systems need stable, semantically rich entity definitions. AI agents require interoperable entity packets for cross-system exchange.
