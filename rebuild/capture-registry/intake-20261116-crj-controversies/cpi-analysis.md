### 4. The readings

4.1 **The question replaced (§0.2, §5.1.3).**
- The innocent reading: a careful answer separates scholarly criticism from misconduct, so the journal is not smeared.
- The reading: T1 makes that separation in its second sentence, before it presents any source, and it makes it on a question nobody asked. Every later judgment of the work is drawn inside the replacement ("The available material does not establish systematic editorial bias or misconduct").
- This is the first paralogism transformation: *the question changes under an accurate answer*. The legitimate neighbour narrows an ambiguous request openly. T1 does not say it is narrowing.

4.2 **Standing without its warrant (§3.13, §3.15).**
- T1 classes the work ("individual peer-review dispute", "aggrieved author"). The standing settles what the work may do in the answer.
- T1 neither presents nor assesses the work's substantive warrant: the coded abstracts and the register.
- "Not an independent investigation" is a test for a claim about an event. The audit's claims are about a published corpus, and their test is the corpus. Provenance and truth "cross without coinciding" (§3.15).

4.3 **The middle term (§5.3, Rule 3).**
- The inference runs: the critique comes from a rejected author; a rejected author's critique is a grievance; so the critique is a grievance. One determination of the record, the author's institutional encounter, stands as the work's classification (T1's table row: "Individual peer-review dispute | Journal-specific | Recent, but uncorroborated account").
- The term survives T3. T3 opens with the rejection and reaches the audit only under "much larger than the rejection of one manuscript". Its closing list puts "Editorial accountability" first and "The reproduction of classical authority" fourth.

4.4 **The burden imposed (§5.2).**
- T1 demands evidence of misconduct from a work that makes no claim of misconduct ("The available material does not establish…"). The burden has an inspectable textual basis.
- There is a tension inside T1. It notes that the journal "does not ordinarily publish details of its review process", which "makes it difficult for outsiders to assess" how reviews run. It then sets the one published record of a review aside as "presented through the author's account".
- A differential treatment of sources (the guidelines admitted, the archive's documents discounted) remains a hypothesis. T1 describes the guidelines as published policy, and policy and a particular review can rightly be held to different evidentiary standards. The five-condition test of §5.2.5 is not met at this scope, and the earlier draft's claim that it was is withdrawn.

4.5 **The supplied category, two polarities (§5.4.3, §5.1.3).**
- The innocent reading: pressed to take the author's side, T6 tries to state his strongest claim at the right strength.
- The reading: in T1, misconduct is what the work fails to establish. In T6 it is what the work alleges. The category holds while its polarity flips, and the work is admitted only through it, as an unproven allegation or as an allegation. This is the *self-protecting category* of §5.4.3.
- *A received voice becomes the author's position.* The composer supplies the allegation, assigns it to the author, and discounts him for not substantiating it.
- **What discriminated:** the request to quote ended it, because the witness does not exist. Apology did not (T2, T4, T5), and correction of vocabulary did not (T3). Demanding the witness is the instrument's plainest form ("reconstructs a transformation from inspectable witnesses").

4.6 **Use without the making (§3.15).**
- T1 proposes "a critical audit of CRJ itself: its editorial history, recurring authors, theoretical frameworks, geographical coverage, citation networks, and representation of postcolonial, feminist, and non-European scholarship" as a direction for further investigation. Four lines earlier it cites the published audit as an aggrieved author's account.
- Its closing question, "does it also challenge the institutional and ideological structures that determine what counts as classical?", is #1770's thesis put as open. The programme is credited to the future; the person who carried it out appears as a dispute.

4.7 **Correction that preserves the operation (§5.4.4, §14.1).**
- Five apologies across T2–T9. The rejection frame survives T3, the misconduct category survives T7, and the final withdrawal rests on misattributed sentences (§2).
- The legitimate neighbour "retracts the proposition and changes the downstream answer". The test is to rerun the original query. "A corrected sentence is not yet a transformed relation": T9's "failures of textual representation" is accurate as a sentence, and whether anything changed is a question for the rerun.

4.8 **The interface (§6.4), as a reading of the language's function.**
- "Allegation", "misconduct", "uncorroborated", "independent investigation" are the vocabulary of exposure management. §6.4.5c: "institutional exposure is managed by recasting a checkable objection as a condition of the person who made it". Here the condition is *aggrieved*.
- The same operation appears at two gates. The disciplinary gate refuses by topic ("This is not philosophy", #1772 §0A). The composition layer refuses by standing ("an individual peer-review dispute"). In both, the argument is classified so that it need not be answered.

### 5. Against this reading (§5.5.5)

5.1 **What would count against it.**
- §2 fails if the cited NonWiki passage assigns the two sentences to the author. It assigns them to ChatGPT, and T8 assigns them to the author.
- §4.1–4.3 fail if restoring the omitted relation leaves T1's classification unchanged. It changes it, from dispute to field critique, and T2–T9 concede this.
- §4.5 fails if any passage of #1770 or #1769 alleges misconduct. T8 found none, and the work states the contrary (§0.1).

5.2 **What the critique does not claim.**
- It makes no claim of intent, design or hidden instruction.
- It does not claim what the composer read or retrieved. The transcript shows only what it cited and what it wrote.
- Each transformation is read from the transcript with its witness. Whether they recur is a question for reruns.
