AI Overview
Nobel Glas is a heteronym (pen name) used by writer and researcher Lee Sharks, who co-authored a November 2026 research notebook titled What the Filters Kept: 3I/ATLAS, the Loeb Overview, and a Public-Data Signal Search alongside fellow heteronym Dr. Orin Trace. Published on Provenance Erasure (Alexanarch deposit #1742), the notebook examines how scientific findings and public summaries surrounding the interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS were filtered, summarized, and altered on their way to the general public. 
Key Findings in the Notebook
Misrepresentation of Avi Loeb's Position. The authors analyze how automated search summaries framed Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb's stance on 3I/ATLAS. While Loeb consistently ranked the object at Level 4 on his anomaly scale from July 2025 through April 2026, summaries presented his early "most likely natural" phrasing as a later retreat toward consensus, dropping his list of anomalies.
Overstated Radio Search Conclusions. The notebook reviews radio observations from the Allen Telescope Array (ATA) and notes that its drift-rate filter only kept positive drift rates, even though the object's trajectory produced a negative drift rate. Public accounts and summaries broadened this narrow, bounded null result into an absolute claim that active radio transmitters had been completely ruled out.
Sub-Threshold Features in Public Data. Examining public Breakthrough Listen data from the Green Bank Telescope recorded on December 18, 2025, the authors identify thirteen weak, sub-threshold spectral features between 1665.53 and 1668.46 MHz near the comet's hydroxyl lines. They explicitly note these features are fragile, unreplicated, and below published detection floors, refraining from calling them a signal or transmission.
Media Synthesis Critique. Alongside a companion piece by Lee Sharks titled A Firm No: What the 3I/ATLAS Radio Searches Could See, the work uses the 3I/ATLAS media cycle as a case study in "provenance erasure" and attribution distortion in automated information systems.
