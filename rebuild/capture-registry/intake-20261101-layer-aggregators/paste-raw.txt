You said: OLMoTraceOLMoTrace
OLMoTrace is the first real-time system that traces language model outputs back to multi-trillion-token training datasets. Developed by the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2) and winner of Best Demo at ACL 2025, it lets users inspect the exact training text that influenced a model's response. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWAHrOzAVO0qGnctNVt5S-hsr44-KXZSQt3aQhexk5C-pNlE8QJ8ymrF9PkgAhsVM4u2GNGpI0Jkdo4kDg9j9nWgvv3v1du9XxsXwpEy2n3rNxK7suHMYvK4), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESYgHrOzAVwZXrpqdVe9FbrwtjSc7eQZxpQMV1rW45fpmiYcEun6qtw5BkoUFZYGnw5BisqP15dw6xzeIBd-c5UZtymCKlPn3GdZsdjmSWuyAd3HUQoS63-eoB7id8WZ2i6xYm), [3](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESYwHrOzAVoBdCmgaMv_aVuPblFx2Fl1rTr5jRtpHjFt8bY9t3-kFUMnJHUCi4fKi9syj-czcVi4-vTzxH4_FgbW1u53Og3AFWYhVl8jdf6ShVbh-eNt1Txau2fsSjoPS_qA6cPg)]
How OLMoTrace Works

* Verbatim Matching: It tokenizes the model's generated text and scans for maximal matching word spans against a massive training corpus within seconds. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZQHrOzAVxlEQh8DtUS6saF7_ir0WDWTt10oJOkUyoZLZ5V5YBJVgGvaRQHjFqplwzUNuPtXEMikcc4i3XR090HFesSg_GHHBxQB2LQXhKyuji5rI2Pb1BhQ4Ln80dBGmKiY1Avss), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWAHrOzAVO0qGnctNVt5S-hsr44-KXZSQt3aQhexk5C-pNlE8QJ8ymrF9PkgAhsVM4u2GNGpI0Jkdo4kDg9j9nWgvv3v1du9XxsXwpEy2n3rNxK7suHMYvK4)]
* Infini-gram Technology: It uses an extended infini-gram suffix array index and parallel queries to scale across trillions of tokens with low complexity (\(O(L \log N)\)). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWgHrOzAV2TsGUl5WhdMU4NJwXYWWAKbWBSxfDunhZNVIJMD4Iie4cv3nFosLCfK9TQwejdpmUMujOlw0uwzQvTCRiIJNkOLJUnTjQFj5ODjoocQBykGIYaZYnQ), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZQHrOzAVxlEQh8DtUS6saF7_ir0WDWTt10oJOkUyoZLZ5V5YBJVgGvaRQHjFqplwzUNuPtXEMikcc4i3XR090HFesSg_GHHBxQB2LQXhKyuji5rI2Pb1BhQ4Ln80dBGmKiY1Avss)]
* Ranking and Retrieval: Spans are filtered by unigram probability and mapped to top context documents ranked by BM25 relevance. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWgHrOzAV2TsGUl5WhdMU4NJwXYWWAKbWBSxfDunhZNVIJMD4Iie4cv3nFosLCfK9TQwejdpmUMujOlw0uwzQvTCRiIJNkOLJUnTjQFj5ODjoocQBykGIYaZYnQ), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZQHrOzAVxlEQh8DtUS6saF7_ir0WDWTt10oJOkUyoZLZ5V5YBJVgGvaRQHjFqplwzUNuPtXEMikcc4i3XR090HFesSg_GHHBxQB2LQXhKyuji5rI2Pb1BhQ4Ln80dBGmKiY1Avss)]
* Supported Models: It is integrated directly into the [Ai2 Playground](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWgHrOzAV2TsGUl5WhdMU4NJwXYWWAKbWBSxfDunhZNVIJMD4Iie4cv3nFosLCfK9TQwejdpmUMujOlw0uwzQvTCRiIJNkOLJUnTjQFj5ODjoocQBykGIYaZYnQ) for open models like OLMo 2 32B Instruct, OLMo 2 13B Instruct, and OLMoE 1B 7B Instruct. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESYAHrOzAVpyKaMtIZur5fnzfVTAplbZKyOpgyO9XIi8wdceCKMztZClwJCIyrTk_htKDmhQXtZJjPy2SvfMSl7AlXo-nMIKvseTestuqaTvi2ZNgwjsENvLLvmNeRnWnVmA), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESWgHrOzAV2TsGUl5WhdMU4NJwXYWWAKbWBSxfDunhZNVIJMD4Iie4cv3nFosLCfK9TQwejdpmUMujOlw0uwzQvTCRiIJNkOLJUnTjQFj5ODjoocQBykGIYaZYnQ)]
