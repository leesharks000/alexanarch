The HathiTrust Digital Library is a massive, large-scale collaborative digital repository containing more than 18 million digitized volumes. Founded in 2008 and administered by the University of Michigan, it functions as a partnership between major global academic and research institutions to preserve the cultural record and provide long-term access to the public good.

How Access Works
Access permissions vary depending on your institutional affiliation and the copyright status of the material:


University of Michigan Library
User Status	Public Domain Material	Copyrighted Material
General Public	Full-text online viewing and single-page printing	Snippet/text-search only (to locate relevant page numbers)
Logged-in Member (Partner University)	Full-text viewing and complete book PDF downloading	Text-search only, except under specific institutional exemptions
Important Tips for Users
Log In Early: If you are a student, faculty member, or researcher at a partner university (e.g., University of Michigan, University of Wisconsin, University of Calgary, or even NYPL cardholders), always click Log In at the top right and select your institution. This unlocks complete PDF downloading and the "Collection Builder" tool to save your research lists.
Accessibility Services: Patrons with certified print disabilities can request full downloadable copies of any item in the database via the HathiTrust Accessible Text Request Service.
Bot Protection Measures: HathiTrust enforces security measures to reduce bot traffic, which can occasionally cause slowness. Logging in with your official institution or library card helps bypass these temporary blocks.
