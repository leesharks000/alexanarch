 The Consortium
 Initiated in 2009, the Consortium on Electronic Literature (CELL) is an international, open-access collaboration providing centralized access to literary databases, archives, and institutional repositories. CELL connects distributed collections to ensure born-digital creative works, kinetic poetry, hypertexts, and interactive media remain discoverable and preserved across generations of computational technology.
