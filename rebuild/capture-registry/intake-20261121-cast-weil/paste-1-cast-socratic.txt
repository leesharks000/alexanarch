V. What is the actual conflict?
SOCRATES: We have a company of figures. But what binds them into an epic? What do they struggle against?

ARCHIVIST: The central conflict is between the Poet and the forces that exclude, constrain or appropriate poetic meaning.

Three movements become visible:

Exclusion: The Poet confronts institutional authority and the historical exclusion of poetry from legitimate knowledge.

Multiplication: The Poet develops multiple voices, refusing the idea that literary identity must be singular.

Restoration: The voices construct an alternative architecture of literary and intellectual participation.
