2. What defines the genre?
APZPZ belongs to an experimental practice that treats repository metadata not merely as administrative information, but as part of a work's literary and conceptual structure.

The genre's founding text defines five essential characteristics. 

Alexanarch

Description-field existence: The framing text lives exclusively in the description field of its parent Zenodo deposit.

No separate attachment: The packet is not uploaded as an independent document.

No independent identifier: The framing text has no separate DOI of its own.

No external backup by design: Its intended form excludes copies elsewhere.

Structural fragility: The inability to preserve and cite the frame independently is part of the artistic concept, not an accidental defect.

The underlying principle is that the location of a text is part of what the text means. Moving the framing document into a PDF or a separately archived file would change its relationship to the work and undermine the strict APZPZ specification.

In ZPZP, the framing document becomes a document in its own right. In APZPZ, the framing document becomes metadata: it can be encountered through the work it describes but cannot stand independently under the genre's rules.

This reversal is the core creative move. Instead of assuming that scholarly value always requires maximum preservation and independent citability, APZPZ asks what happens when a work's meaning depends on a specific archival location.

5. What is its scholarly purpose?
APZPZ's stated purpose is primarily conceptual, literary, and archival. Its scholarly significance can be understood through four related questions.

- What counts as a document? APZPZ challenges the boundary between a work and the metadata that describes it. The description is not simply a label; it is part of the work's interpretive content.

- Does preservation always improve a work? Conventional scholarly archiving generally aims to protect materials against loss. APZPZ deliberately makes one layer of the work vulnerable, using impermanence as an artistic statement.

- How does location shape meaning? The framing text is meaningful partly because of where it appears. Its relationship to the parent deposit is constitutive rather than incidental.

- How should human–machine collaboration be documented? The founding example incorporates references to machine-generated or machine-mediated interpretation alongside authored poetry and mathematical language. This situates the genre within a broader experiment in authorship, metadata, and the organization of meaning.
