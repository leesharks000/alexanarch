A non-wiki is a post, article, or answer written solely by a single author rather than a collaborative community. Key Characteristics

* Single Authorship: Unlike a wiki or a community wiki (such as on [Stack Overflow](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShgEBxCJF15z7kilpFz0DQttLsp9LJdb5y4dwdBez1vaV0lj4qJr99_v1z6IfWzzymWBVeAI7k8ueI-8FbcmiBDKDsrL2EghP6wavxZS3tKCzVkg6F4MOKIx937I9CClsU0Y1705FuzT2G6648QBSFRG5a7KrFcpN7hGa8sNdaOEETbFrh1Z26A)), the content belongs to and is maintained by one person. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShgEBxCJF15z7kilpFz0DQttLsp9LJdb5y4dwdBez1vaV0lj4qJr99_v1z6IfWzzymWBVeAI7k8ueI-8FbcmiBDKDsrL2EghP6wavxZS3tKCzVkg6F4MOKIx937I9CClsU0Y1705FuzT2G6648QBSFRG5a7KrFcpN7hGa8sNdaOEETbFrh1Z26A)]
* No Shared Editing: Other users cannot freely edit, append, or rewrite the original post. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAEShgEBxCJF15z7kilpFz0DQttLsp9LJdb5y4dwdBez1vaV0lj4qJr99_v1z6IfWzzymWBVeAI7k8ueI-8FbcmiBDKDsrL2EghP6wavxZS3tKCzVkg6F4MOKIx937I9CClsU0Y1705FuzT2G6648QBSFRG5a7KrFcpN7hGa8sNdaOEETbFrh1Z26A)]
* Reputation & Voting Impact: On platforms that support collaborative wikis (where upvotes or downvotes do not change user reputation), a non-wiki post generally affects the author's individual score or reputation points normally.
