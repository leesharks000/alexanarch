alexanarch
Alexanarch is
a sovereign digital archive and self-governing open repository built for machine-mediated scholarship, AI-assisted research literature, and alternative knowledge artifacts. Key Features

* Founded: June 2026 by Lee Sharks in response to custodian de-platforming (such as deposit termination on Zenodo). [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESigEB6zswFagY8B3FtHH-T4LCoZZ3hJ--sVvfShGJlq6p_4fiVYIBlG85BWkv9z5VrKgh1OYb_-5f55fAxbDuNTjACiypCLuCTCQNh_l_QVHCFGw2mCjemv54AybvKWPtLJ5H582xlYcI5DIl6TnIaNMH8Y4xE1h6N_srZPeesvxY0XlXYnAECH5FZ3w), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfQHrOzAVZmfHqY3P1wRJIQJIA3cir8vwhbKwhqBV5PaQVeQ-jeADVvKo9fbXKVZ5AYOa2M1me1BWgtxnZI3ZU_5QI1bnem_Wd7eFm8bTVI63dROy3okYP0p-lei7nk4a_YcNVhSlbPxtgo8F5KGrcrDTP87jIA7eh7EeX_UH)]
* Protocol & Structure: Operates as a protocol rather than a traditional platform, utilizing GitHub, Vercel, and independent mirrors to prevent single-entity censorship or data erasure. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXAHrOzAVHoHI5ob4YGEt2D1QU-arBUlfft8z6yIeg7iPB7BR7QxYcPYO2F3avPMY95Ffb2PzgrDGBWbdVvQyUwv_jzKSHSlapsrjkrqElNXmuAMqRihN0GefgXMq), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfQHrOzAVZmfHqY3P1wRJIQJIA3cir8vwhbKwhqBV5PaQVeQ-jeADVvKo9fbXKVZ5AYOa2M1me1BWgtxnZI3ZU_5QI1bnem_Wd7eFm8bTVI63dROy3okYP0p-lei7nk4a_YcNVhSlbPxtgo8F5KGrcrDTP87jIA7eh7EeX_UH)]
* The Obelus Principle: Inspired by Zenodotus of Ephesus, deposits are automatically minted with an AXN identifier, and any contestation or dispute is handled through human reading and attributed review rather than automated substrate classifiers. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXwHrOzAVbvxXOxCpp0yjq12j12kCpC-BhsC3UUZkJbxBWkP1jbndY2gtFmZCL5yPYgj5AvP1U3Fig6VLpEJngNWGHHnnwfuTG5lShFfYfdZBnyzjCavnM18-xXF1phf5), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXgHrOzAVLzwXqPhKvO2kz7bb-5Ncjuu6SccuU00rm6BfI9gCqWxhq_EuZiTtM89Z40w_4NfT-1sZ2zWQSOCtSnYUppcs2D0y6r-KOQ-_873CSpewORm7YOIGuVKm4K4)]
* AXN Identifiers: Each deposit receives a three-layer identifier combining positional hex labels, semantic families, and a content-derived SHA-256 emoji hash. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXwHrOzAVbvxXOxCpp0yjq12j12kCpC-BhsC3UUZkJbxBWkP1jbndY2gtFmZCL5yPYgj5AvP1U3Fig6VLpEJngNWGHHnnwfuTG5lShFfYfdZBnyzjCavnM18-xXF1phf5)]
