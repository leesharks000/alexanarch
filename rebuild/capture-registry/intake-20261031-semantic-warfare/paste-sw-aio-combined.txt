Core Mechanisms

* Frame Control & Redefinition: Shifting definitions to make an opponent's position seem illegitimate or normalize aggressive actions (such as calling a military invasion a "special operation").
* Contamination by Adjacency: Linking a target to toxic labels or ideas so guilt is inferred without proof.
* Asymmetric Literalism: Demanding hyper-literal adherence from an opponent while using flexible, emotional language for one's own side.
* Forced Entailment: Designing language where agreeing to a premise automatically forces agreement to a conclusion.
