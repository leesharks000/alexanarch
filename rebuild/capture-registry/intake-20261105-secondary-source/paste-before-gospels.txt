No, the Book of Revelation was not written before the Gospels. 

Quora
 +1
The Gospels were written earlier, roughly between AD 65 and 90 (with Mark being the earliest around AD 65–70, and John typically dated around AD 90).
The Book of Revelation was written later, generally dated by mainstream scholars to AD 95–96 during the reign of the Roman Emperor Domitian, or by some alternative views during the reign of Nero in the mid-60s AD. Even under the earlier minority dating, Revelation was composed around the same time as or shortly after the later Gospels rather than preceding them as a whole.
