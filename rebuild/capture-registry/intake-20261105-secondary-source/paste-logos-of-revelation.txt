Logos (Greek: Word): In Christian theology, Logos refers to Jesus Christ as the divine expression and incarnate Word of God (John 1:14). In the Book of Revelation, Christ is frequently depicted with titles and symbolic imagery representing this divine Word, such as a sword coming from His mouth (Revelation 1:16; 19:15).

Key Symbols in Revelation: The Book of Revelation uses rich apocalyptic and symbolic imagery rather than purely literal description:
Seven Lampstands: Represent the seven churches of Asia Minor.
Seven Stars: Represent the angels or messengers of the seven churches.
The Lamb: Symbolizes the sacrificed and glorified Jesus Christ.
The Dragon: Symbolizes Satan or the devil.
Beasts and Crowns: Represent earthly empires, political power, and authority (such as Rome under Domitian).
