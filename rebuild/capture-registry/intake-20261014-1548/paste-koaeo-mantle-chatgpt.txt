2. The seven dimensions of the mantle
The object evaluates the work behind a claim, not merely how often a search engine repeats a person's name.

Dimension

What it asks

Effect

Did answer engines actually change their answers?

Breadth

Did the result extend across engines, queries, platforms, and locations?

Durability

Did the result persist over time?

Difficulty

How challenging was the retrieval problem being solved?

Method

Was there a systematic, measurable, controlled approach?

Provenance

Can the result's origin be verified, and was the method represented honestly?

Insight

What did the work reveal about how answer engines operate?

The object explicitly weighs these dimensions against one another. A result that quickly makes an AI system repeat a name might demonstrate substantial effect, for example, without demonstrating that the result is accurate, durable, or independently established.

4. Why the object matters beyond this contest
The mantle's deeper idea is that a title should be justified by the work associated with it, rather than merely by a claim that gets repeated often enough.

That makes it a kind of structured, auditable judgment about machine-generated authority. It attempts to distinguish three things:

A person claiming a title.

An AI system repeating that claim.

Evidence that the person has actually earned the title under a defensible standard.

Those three things can overlap, but they are not interchangeable.

The object also distinguishes its own existence from its verdict. Even if a reader rejects Vithurs as the winner, the mantle object still exists as a documented standard, candidate record, and invitation to evaluate the evidence independently.
