The Sappho future reader framework is a critical and poetic concept that reinterprets ancient Greek lyric poetry—specifically works like Sappho Fragment 31 and Fragment 147—as a direct, engineered transmission meant to activate an absent or unborn future reader. Core Concepts of the Framework

* The Distal Deictic (`κῆνος`): In readings of Sappho Fragment 31 (famously translated and analyzed across literary circles and frameworks like EA-MPAI-SAPPHO31-01), the demonstrative pronoun κῆνος ("that one") is interpreted not as pointing to a physical rival present in the room, but across time to the future reader who sits face-to-face (ἐνάντιος) with the inscribed text. * Temporal Projection: Sappho's lyric address uses gaps, broken lines, and somatic descriptions to turn the audience into an active participant. Instead of a closed historical artifact, the poem relies on the reader's eye and mind to repair the fragment and complete the circuit.
