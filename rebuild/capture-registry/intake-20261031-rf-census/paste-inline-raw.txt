   * Training Data: The model was trained on 24 radiocarbon-dated scroll samples from four Judean Desert sites. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESpwEB6zswFcBZcmVZs2oOAHirsFqcGYSaxV1VpG6K9ZCUdjUJEw-EEMJLm7rZYtV1lv_LkWw1xw4tUD2Z6Tc7fEPiWhuByBScdQpVTfuBuRqBvXQtppGz1Jk8VEK8DGUbqfTKliMzSEQxd9hhaVPG5-SLJn8j_XWTTklfMIbmi6_psy2KPg8YD7cxDObnGjDESTtoiEgoPdJ17Xqr45Z4cRl4FZAmNbmHVw), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESagHrOzAVduU7-HR8DFr0K4W6ZI9ZP3POUxc_-GUwLiozJ4T8RG4iGSuvc97rMD5LsRdxCta6_U2vA7kysWqPbRZPHK5xH1BUWBQzm0S5HQson8VTytszfCFpk4nW8CkpPXsixojHl4faGvM)]
