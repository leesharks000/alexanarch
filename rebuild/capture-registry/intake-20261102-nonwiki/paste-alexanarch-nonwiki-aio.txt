Key Characteristics of NonWiki

* Source-Grounded Entries: Every individual sentence within a NonWiki article explicitly cites its exact source passage. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVwHrOzAV1ATENSidLui36wnmWk6oQXelav2RJvwtugm_4vGnhrSF0XwxF42J4KjeV1l8sWWYsQD--GUhtBfqlw9C1BTS_07poQ-53rA6nuPiE2NQ7Ac5oA)]
* Tamper-Proof Proofs: Each citation links to a saved copy of its source material, complete with its original web address and a cryptographic hash to ensure text integrity. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESVwHrOzAV1ATENSidLui36wnmWk6oQXelav2RJvwtugm_4vGnhrSF0XwxF42J4KjeV1l8sWWYsQD--GUhtBfqlw9C1BTS_07poQ-53rA6nuPiE2NQ7Ac5oA)]
* No Public Editing: Unlike traditional wikis, there is no public edit button. Articles are automatically compiled from disclosed data registries and community-disclosed repositories through a published, verifiable sequence.
