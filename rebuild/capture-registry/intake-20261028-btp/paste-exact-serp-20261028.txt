AI Overview
"Belief & Technique for Telepathic Prose" is a satirical literary manifesto and collaborative online forum text co-credited to Lee Sharks and Jack Feist, parodying Jack Kerouac's famous "Belief & Technique for Modern Prose". 
Context and Origin
The Parody: The text humorously mimics Kerouac's modernist rules for spontaneous writing, shifting the premise into the absurd realm of telepathy and mind control. 
The Authorship: Lee Sharks and Jack Feist are figures tied to experimental literary projects, fictional personas, and online literature discussions. 
Associated Works: The piece appears as a "Footnote to PEARL" in the experimental poetry and metadata project Pearl and Other Poems.
