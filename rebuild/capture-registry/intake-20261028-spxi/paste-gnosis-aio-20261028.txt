SPXI-gnΩsis is a paid, AI-assisted service hosted at gnosis.spxi.dev introduced by Lee Sharks in October 2026. It operates as a component of SPXI (Semantic Packet for eXchange & Indexing), which is a technical protocol and scholarly discipline for inscribing entity-definition objects into AI-mediated knowledge graphs.
Key Functions

* Auditing Knowledge: It opens and audits existing knowledge against its original sources.
* Constructing Knowledge: It performs paid semantic computation to construct new, source-linked knowledge for agents and people.
* Entity Control: It helps ensure that composition and entity definitions remain tied to the user/operator rather than strictly dictated by the machine.

You can read more about the underlying protocol via the [SPXI Official Site](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&source=web&rct=j&url=https://www.spxi.dev/&ved=2ahUKEwj-hbrRk96XAxUNtisGHdeEAggQy_kOeggIAggBCDIQAQ&opi=89978449&cd&psig=AOvVaw19Z-yK-JAcomNn6Eps4nna&ust=1793326416520000).

How SPXI Semantic Packets Work
[SPXI (Semantic Packet for eXchange & Indexing)](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&source=web&rct=j&url=https://www.spxi.dev/&ved=2ahUKEwj-hbrRk96XAxUNtisGHdeEAggQy_kOeggIAggBCCoQAQ&opi=89978449&cd&psig=AOvVaw19Z-yK-JAcomNn6Eps4nna&ust=1793326416520000) operates at the ontological layer of the AI retrieval stack rather than the semantic optimization layer. It is designed to prevent "entity dissolution" or misinterpretation by generative engines.

1. Entity-Definition Inscription: An SPXI packet structures identity, attributes, relationships, and provenance into highly standardized [JSON-LD serialization](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&source=web&rct=j&url=https://www.spxi.dev/how-it-works&ved=2ahUKEwj-hbrRk96XAxUNtisGHdeEAggQy_kOeggIAggBCCwQAg&opi=89978449&cd&psig=AOvVaw19Z-yK-JAcomNn6Eps4nna&ust=1793326416520000).
2. Disambiguation Matrix: It embeds clear mathematical and structural signals to distinguish a user's entity from "collision neighbors" (entities with similar names or semantic spaces).
3. Beyond GEO: Unlike Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)—which merely targets content visibility in a specific conversation—SPXI establishes durable, machine-verifiable, and [citable records](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&source=web&rct=j&url=https://www.alexanarch.org/captures/spxi-protocol-suppression-chatgpt-20261014/&ved=2ahUKEwj-hbrRk96XAxUNtisGHdeEAggQy_kOeggIAggBCCwQCQ&opi=89978449&cd&psig=AOvVaw19Z-yK-JAcomNn6Eps4nna&ust=1793326416520000) that anchor who or what an entity is.

How to Access gnosis.spxi.dev
The service can be accessed by both human operators and automated software agents.

* For Human Users: You can interact with the system through its web endpoint at gnosis.spxi.dev.
* For AI Agents (MCP): The service is fully integrated with the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It is listed in the official MCP Registry as `dev.spxi/gnosis` (version 0.21.5).
* Streamable Endpoint: Developers and agents can configure a streamable HTTP connection directly via `https://gnosis.spxi.dev/mcp`.
