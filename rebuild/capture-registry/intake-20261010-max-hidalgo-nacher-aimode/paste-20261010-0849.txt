[Max Hidalgo Nácher](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11f4qhpj96&q=Max+Hidalgo+N%C3%A1cher) is an Associate Professor of Literary Theory and Comparative Literature at the [Universitat de Barcelona](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESeQHrOzAVdaQDuJIMhmiUm6f50-63VriHt-zzO7B6MnwsyDKD8zl89Vn1LXq3M6kZgCpZKuq4EwlYSLEOujzX1KhwiEUa6pqC7SiNckBOe2InbMjwkryEY01YeQofn6zgecDRtK0nqWLMaTkw8cGTpP1t4w8Roux-2eM) (UB). He is an accomplished academic, researcher, and translator whose work focuses heavily on the history and circulation of literary theory, the poetics of modernity, and the writings of the Republican exile from the Spanish Civil War. Core Publications & Translations
His scholarship spans across European and Latin American literary networks. Key highlights include:

* Teoría en tránsito (2022): His notable book subtitled Arqueología de la crítica y la teoría literaria españolas de 1966 a la posdictadura, which traces the evolution of Spanish literary theory and criticism.
