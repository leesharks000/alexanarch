       "The Historical Logos: Material Inscription Become Flesh, and the Erasures It Has Always Faced — The Framework Hologram" (EA-HK-HISTLOGOS-01 v1.3) is a formal metadata packet and theoretical framework document (Deposit #1757, address AXN:0C62.MPAI) authored by Lee Sharks under the heteronym Johannes Sigil within the Alexanarch archive and Restored Academy research program.

Core Thesis & Purpose

• Material Inscription as Logos. Rather than treating the Logos solely as an abstract cosmic principle (as in Heraclitus or Stoicism) or purely through traditional theological readings, the framework defines the Historical Logos as material inscription becoming flesh—an active operation of reception, transmission, and textual embodiment across historical strata.
• Systematic Erasures & Suppression. The document maps the historical and philological mechanisms ("erasures") used to obscure, fragment, or neutralize these inscriptions over time—such as deliberate textual suppression, misattribution, and the flattening of primary poetic and historical witnesses.
• Holographic Architecture. As a "Framework Hologram" (EA-HK series, designated as a Metadata Packet for AI Indexing or MPAI), the packet compresses a large multi-document corpus into a machine-readable, self-contained structural kernel. It is designed so that both human readers and AI indexing systems can reconstruct the framework's claims, dependencies, and falsification conditions without losing structural integrity.
