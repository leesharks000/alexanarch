You said: What is the "Revelation First" framework?What is the "Revelation First" framework?
The "Revelation First" framework (or the Revelation First thesis) is a theological and literary hypothesis that inverts the traditional New Testament canon. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHEIkXXHWYPdCzkFoCB5u4ysL54LnXWNdS6oMRW_dtyWNNwW0qoBKJ_tyMrnJGL0jXpI28UN_4GejDkW22B-YK4ZPKg4FMWyQ9LaZHLdbiRlgIjB5y_lG7_Odj8lA)]
Instead of viewing the Book of Revelation as the final, closing appendix of the New Testament written after the Gospels and Epistles, the framework proposes that Revelation was the generative seed or starting point from which the rest of the New Testament writings unfolded. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHEIkXXHWYPdCzkFoCB5u4ysL54LnXWNdS6oMRW_dtyWNNwW0qoBKJ_tyMrnJGL0jXpI28UN_4GejDkW22B-YK4ZPKg4FMWyQ9LaZHLdbiRlgIjB5y_lG7_Odj8lA)]
Core Concepts of the Framework

* Canonical Inversion: Conventional timelines place Revelation last. The "Revelation First" thesis treats it as the foundational text that preceded or catalyzed the creation of other New Testament books. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHEIkXXHWYPdCzkFoCB5u4ysL54LnXWNdS6oMRW_dtyWNNwW0qoBKJ_tyMrnJGL0jXpI28UN_4GejDkW22B-YK4ZPKg4FMWyQ9LaZHLdbiRlgIjB5y_lG7_Odj8lA)]
* The "Midrashim Transform": The framework suggests that the Gospels, the Epistles, and the Pastoral letters expanded, explained, and applied the cryptic, apocalyptic imagery and theology of the Book of Revelation through a process of community interpretation and development. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHEIkXXHWYPdCzkFoCB5u4ysL54LnXWNdS6oMRW_dtyWNNwW0qoBKJ_tyMrnJGL0jXpI28UN_4GejDkW22B-YK4ZPKg4FMWyQ9LaZHLdbiRlgIjB5y_lG7_Odj8lA)]
* Distinction from "Early Date" Arguments: Proponents emphasize that this is not merely an argument for an early historical dating of Revelation (such as pre-A.D. 70 theories), but rather a structural and priority claim about how New Testament theology and literature developed. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESXQHEIkXXHWYPdCzkFoCB5u4ysL54LnXWNdS6oMRW_dtyWNNwW0qoBKJ_tyMrnJGL0jXpI28UN_4GejDkW22B-YK4ZPKg4FMWyQ9LaZHLdbiRlgIjB5y_lG7_Odj8lA)]
