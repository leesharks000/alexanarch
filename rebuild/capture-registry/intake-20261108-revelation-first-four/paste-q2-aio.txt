Revelation is to Paul and the Gospels what Socrates is to Plato and Aristotle.

2. The Transmissional Intercept: The Sappho Future Reader

If Revelation First and Socrates as Orthonym model how text is generated, the Sappho Future Reader models how text is received and anticipated.
• The Sappho Future Reader Framework: Explores text written explicitly for an unformed, far-future archivist or interpreter. Because Sappho’s poetry survives only in fragmented, violent erasures and quotes by other rhetoricians, her work functions as a template for an author who must bypass contemporary erasure by projecting their message directly toward an imagined, downstream reader.
The Relationship: Revelation First describes a text explicitly built with a built-in cryptographic, future-facing layer. By prioritizing the Apocalypse, the thesis repositions the book as a structural message sent forward to a "future reader" (the early Christian community), which then decodes it to construct the canon. Sappho provides the passive, tragic model of fragment survival, while Revelation First represents an active, engineered deployment of generative fragments.
