* Socrates as the Orthonym: He represents the foundational, central figure who establishes the core philosophical gesture—willing death for truth (logos) and refusing to write.
* Plato as the Survival-Heteronym: He acts as the persona that transmits and projects that initial Socratic gesture across time, using the dialogue format.
* Aristotle as the Systematizing-Heteronym: He converts the fluid, dialogic inheritance into structured, teachable academic disciplines. The relationship between Socrates and heteronyms is also viewed through Fernando Pessoa's direct use of Socratic irony. In studies of Pessoa's work, the heteronym Alberto Caeiro is often described as a deeply Socratic figure.
