Core Concepts of the Alexanarch Dialectic

* The Dialectic and Its Carriers. In the deposit [The Dialectic and Its Carriers (AXN-06F5)](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESawHEIkXXwjBZ7h-Du557yXKUrD30QakJ0zHREZR9Ref9Dk8LSM0xnrcnJd383FXjCiGwLdN-BnGmi7s0VoPlUbG-YZX9HgERbH3Tcc65q6opcoWWnasoR8YiX26AqQcYZm46UFPEM7x8pLof), the author affirms the materialist tradition by stating that Karl Marx "carried the dialectic" and that "the dialectic is the dialectic". This framing treats dialectical movement as an objective process transferred across different theoretical and technical "carriers" over time. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESawHEIkXXwjBZ7h-Du557yXKUrD30QakJ0zHREZR9Ref9Dk8LSM0xnrcnJd383FXjCiGwLdN-BnGmi7s0VoPlUbG-YZX9HgERbH3Tcc65q6opcoWWnasoR8YiX26AqQcYZm46UFPEM7x8pLof)]
