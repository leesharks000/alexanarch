Core Tenets of Money in Alexanarch

* The Debt/Creditor Inversion. Outlined in deposit `#531` (On the Debt/Creditor Inversion), this principle argues that while money is created as debt, all debt relies on shared human understanding to function. Therefore, the underlying archive of shared meaning itself acts as the primary creditor. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfwHEIkXXdHwB0jhqCF2QYIUmjvEIHME9WQZFFMMhd5x83oZD6pKDXcduuAWeRHdeMkjstnd_Q9_bFaUQJcfqFw-g3x5cA6hFrREVJZobmh3ULbcCQeECVt1MNbZGtZ6fdAHckgtQvEBL5qMBuGACt1-5Je02xnfvPR2VKwdJQ6I)]
* The Semantic Commodity Form. Explored in deposit `#1617` (Substrate Sovereignty), this concept treats money as a specific form of linguistic and informational inscription. Rather than existing outside language, monetary value acts as an informational filter and jurisdictional infrastructure. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESYwHEIkXXLfIbm0xgezrr9p5CAKXuNApOGWvEV7CaNCVcdVo0UxJt3qLfE7gJIV3jBe2VBiha5PA07LPVv34ws9wUZzoNRJy8KYvvSa8x56XLj4bjN-lQv_4O0_SSozCk_rFQ8g), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfwHEIkXXdHwB0jhqCF2QYIUmjvEIHME9WQZFFMMhd5x83oZD6pKDXcduuAWeRHdeMkjstnd_Q9_bFaUQJcfqFw-g3x5cA6hFrREVJZobmh3ULbcCQeECVt1MNbZGtZ6fdAHckgtQvEBL5qMBuGACt1-5Je02xnfvPR2VKwdJQ6I)]
* Monetary Substrate Auditing. Defined in deposit `#1619` (Monetary Substrate Audit Ledger), this protocol translates monetary transactions into a thirteen-element ledger. Its governing rule states that the monetary amount sits inside the audit, while the audit itself cannot be reduced to the monetary amount, capturing "semantic remainders" left behind by standard pricing. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfwHEIkXXdHwB0jhqCF2QYIUmjvEIHME9WQZFFMMhd5x83oZD6pKDXcduuAWeRHdeMkjstnd_Q9_bFaUQJcfqFw-g3x5cA6hFrREVJZobmh3ULbcCQeECVt1MNbZGtZ6fdAHckgtQvEBL5qMBuGACt1-5Je02xnfvPR2VKwdJQ6I)]
* Post-Money Operator Stack (PMOS). Introduced in works like [I Hereby Abolish Money](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHEIkXXfXB56qVpc1JEMw7GREMq_TcA84RM8B9ClKnhLiv7skoDbK4yhDMmoW34inoT7vRXQb7NC4ws2Dhr6fxGVE62hQkA1QsyHgemU3T2pZSjprf9t5pYa1PLFFK1NUBi3h5Uzad-) and Coordination Without Commodification in the Semantic Economy, this framework warns that turning semantic labor into currency turns semantic life into debt. It proposes using Context Ledgers (memory with attribution) instead of financialization to coordinate value. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHEIkXXfXB56qVpc1JEMw7GREMq_TcA84RM8B9ClKnhLiv7skoDbK4yhDMmoW34inoT7vRXQb7NC4ws2Dhr6fxGVE62hQkA1QsyHgemU3T2pZSjprf9t5pYa1PLFFK1NUBi3h5Uzad-), [2](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESowEBxCJF1834xFclnv7-LjyumKXOtOze7d0UUkQ3tkfb-Q9Bbp2Bkpmn38oYHw2NMX9ql2rC_h5IJIJMDJNDPCi9IEk1L2OxA3esvC_MBI_g-IjEOpigAqqmckTRy4X5b9HEgoWqbLzp8onMzrQxj2pzH0kQ-qAPq7TyXtFU9kVGVst4SYOcjdVb4wDcJHnYEl7_FdC41DTM5g1wcJNgK2nkwfjZ)]
