## In §5, after the paragraph ending "the worked example's anchor case."

**Target-attributable means the target's own records.** As written, Overview Admission Strength counts sources "target-attributable," and for an entity query the target was read as the author. Capture 04 shows why that reading fails at a work's address. Counting cards attributable to the author, admission is 3 of 5 and CDI = 1.00 − 0.60 = +0.40, moderate. Counting cards that are the work's own records (its deposits, its editions, its site), admission is 0 of 5 and CDI = 1.00 − 0 = +1.00, maximal, with N = 3 for a title-exact query as §5 specifies (at N = 45, organic resolution is 44/45 and CDI = +0.98). For a work, target-attributable sources are the work's own records; the author's other pages about the work are the ring around it.
