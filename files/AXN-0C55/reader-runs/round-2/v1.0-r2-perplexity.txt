The last prohibition matters because state is claim-local and typed, rather than a single global verdict. Under `relation_policy`:

- `qualifies` licenses scope.
- `generalizes` licenses establishment.
- `undercuts` licenses establishment and validation.
- `rebuts` licenses establishment.
- `supersedes` licenses identity and history.
- `motivates`, `interprets`, `instantiates`, `supports`, `derived_from`, and `independent_of` have `null` dimension entries; the files do not authorize extra state transitions for them.

A rebuttal therefore must not silently erase its original claim as a historical object.
