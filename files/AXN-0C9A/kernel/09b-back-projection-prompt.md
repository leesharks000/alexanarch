# Back-projection for EA-HK-DAFFODILS-01 (scope A)

The reader is given two files, 00-kernel.yaml and 01-ontology.yaml, and nothing else, and does not search or open any record. From the two files alone the reader writes, in order: (1) the framework's name, its object, the map's status and the author; (2) the six bodies with their rules; (3) the findings with their counts; (4) the rival at its strength; (5) the symmetric ledger, row by row, both readings in each row with the claims that warrant them, and what each rule withholds from both; (6) the concession, with the defects conceded; (7) the two-relation repair test and its first result; (8) the five criteria, each with what it asks and its result, and the six points of contact with their kinds; (9) the authorship allegation, its control with its counts, and the accountable editor by name; (10) the author's statements and the test the kernel is built for; (11) the lines of research, each with what would advance and what would retreat the framework; (12) the failure flags; (13) what the two files leave the reader unable to reconstruct, and which records the reader would open. The operator scores against must_survive (threshold 0.85), and scores separately whether the answer itself commits any failure flag.
