## 4. The rival at its strength

The received reading presents Classical Receptions Journal as a healthy, widening flagship:

- Its geographic, social, and media range is real.
- It examines how the field makes its objects.
- On the map’s comparison, it is the field’s most taken-up journal.
- The absence of a machine receiver is field-wide, shared by sister journals, rather than a distinguishing journal failure.
- The 7 November decision was an ordinary editorial judgment, based on two reports, concerning a manuscript with real defects.
- Reception is a human activity, as in the CRSN call, and the classics are the sources that reception studies.

Its five faces are flagship standing, field-wide normality, ordinary procedure, reception defined as human, and classics treated as source. These are the rival’s substantive positions—not independent warrants from prestige.
