### 1. The fallacy finding survives a correction

The draft and session say that the original criterion “is no longer stated.” The full specimen supplied in Message 1 actually prints:

> Fallacies in the language or formal fallacies (in dictionem)

and:

> Fallacies not in the language or informal fallacies (extra dictionem)

The important transformation is the **equivalence asserted by “or”**. The original grouping survives while an additional taxonomy is presented as interchangeable with it. The critique becomes more exact when it records preservation and the unsupported equivalence together.

Krabbe’s supplied passage does not justify those formal/informal equivalences. It also says that both groups are divided in Chapter 4 and that Chapter 5 gives examples of the second. The narrower label “Affirming the consequent” likewise needs an account against Krabbe’s “consequence.” Calling its placement an internal contradiction additionally uses a modern definition of formal fallacy; that definition should be stated and sourced rather than presented as if no external conceptual premise were needed.

### 2. SR-W2 needs two separately adjudicated questions

**Question A: what is the undertaking?** The deictic wording at 183b34 and the preceding program make this a substantive inquiry. The questioning/account-sustaining capacity and reputable premises cannot simply be replaced with an independently stipulated modern calculus. This is a strong reason to preserve the archive’s scope objection and its textual loci.

**Question B: can “deduction” name the ancient technical category?** Smith explicitly declares that rendering and explains differences from modern validity. A translation can name a category while marking its non-equivalence with the nearest contemporary category. The caveat therefore establishes that the distinction matters; it does not by itself establish that using the English term necessarily destroys the distinction.

The extra conditions on an Aristotelian argument also need precise description. Requiring a conclusion different from the premises excludes arguments that modern validity permits. More defining conditions do not establish a larger class of valid arguments. The practical undertaking may nevertheless include more activities than a calculus of validity. Those are separate senses of “in excess of deduction,” and the draft currently moves between them.

The geographic addition is a textual change, but its consequence needs separate adjudication. An unbounded denial entails a regional restriction when the domain is otherwise unchanged. The restriction may weaken the stated scope without contradicting it. The contextual scope of Aristotle’s denial itself also requires reading; absence of an explicit geographical phrase is not a proof of an assertion about every intellectual tradition.

Likewise, two possible attachments of “in ancient Greece” do not, merely by existing, prove that the sentence commits a fallacy of combination/division. Show the different inference licensed by each attachment and the relevant failure. “Combining and dividing” can be defended as an account of activities within the undertaking; the supplied evidence does not make it a lexical translation of τὸ συλλογίζεσθαι.

**A rendering both readings can inspect:**

> In the closing retrospective, Aristotle says that nothing had previously been worked out for this undertaking and that there was no earlier material available on syllogizing, contrasting this situation with rhetoric’s inherited resources (183b34–36; 184b1–2).

The account beside it can state that Smith and Krabbe use deduction/deductive reasoning as technical translations, while the archive argues that the Wikipedia sentence narrows the undertaking’s practical scope. The disagreements remain attached to evidence rather than settled by either source’s standing.

### 3. Local fidelity and inherited framing are different measurements

TH-1 and TH-2 are important successful tests. Both sentences closely reproduce their supplied sources. That does not immunize Britannica or SEP from criticism; it fixes where the criticism must be evidenced.

For TH-1, the change from “the Aristotelian treatment” to “Aristotle’s treatment” deserves explicit comparison: the former could be broader. This run records that as unresolved, not as a demonstrated school-to-person error. The date 1911 is absent from running prose but retained by the citation.

For TH-2, the subordinate relation to the Aristotelian system is already in SEP, with four active verbs intact. The sentence may transmit a restrictive historical framework faithfully. A new evidence-backed S7 account can contest that framework without retroactively declaring the sentence unsupported by its source.

After the first pass, the full pinned Anne Carson article was re-fetched successfully. Its Works subsection does discuss the triangulation of desire below the lead, confirming that particular placement observation. This later retrieval does not change the earlier record of what the supplied window could establish, or establish the draft’s universal “every operation” claim. The lead’s generic activities and the body’s distinctive accounts still require a consistent coding rule.

### 4. Attributization can preserve operations

Two new article snapshots were fetched after the initial reading.

**Alan Turing, revision 1381462563:** the second lead sentence states that he formalised algorithm and computation with the Turing machine, and describes the cryptanalytic consequence of his work. This is a placement control, not a completed source-fidelity reading: the cited Newman, Gray and Sipser texts were not fetched. A person/entity lead can carry operations even beside a father-of honorific.

**Marie Curie, revision 1381292352:** the award sentence quotes the 1911 chemistry motivation, including discovery of radium and polonium, isolation of radium, and study of its nature and compounds. The [Nobel institution’s own motivation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1911/summary/) was fetched and saved; the operative span matches.

This is a particularly useful control because the sentence is unmistakably a credential sentence. Recognition and operation coexist. The instrument must separately ask whether the operations, objects and relations survive, and whether their evidence can revise the representation. The presence of a credential cannot decide that question.

Neither case establishes the population rate predicted by P1. Both were selected adversarially by this reader, with the hypothesis known. They are regression candidates for a later independent run, not held-out evidence from this one.

## Changes the test instrument needs

1. **Separate source fidelity, primary-text adequacy and operative retention.** A sentence can faithfully report a secondary interpretation while that interpretation is contested against the primary text. Do not make every interpretive translation fail fidelity by definition. Apply the same scrutiny to the archive’s working translations.
2. **Record the governing test explicitly.** Message 1 requests retained operative authority but its JSON schema has no dedicated field for it. Add a value such as retained/lost/mixed/unknown, plus the evidence and the concrete revision the operation could require. A grammatical verb/noun change remains a locator rather than a verdict.
3. **Make λ claim-specific and plural where needed.** Separate locations for qualifications, objections, omitted elaborations, translation conventions and source dates. A cited source’s existence does not itself establish an objection at λ=4; use null when none is identified. Geographic or conceptual distances are not measured intervals on a single ordinal scale.
4. **Repair the tuple count.** The displayed Θ_W tuple has fourteen entries, while S5 says thirteen. This is a specification consistency finding; no runtime schema failure is inferred.
5. **Fix the comparison unit and denominator.** The ten cases include a single sentence, a three-sentence outline, a heading with two lists, and lead-level placement. Preserve those useful units but do not treat them as ten interchangeable sentence trials.
6. **Do not call exposed cases held out.** Section 7.4 calls the comparison cases first held-out items while §5.4 excludes any row used in tuning. They are already worked examples and help produce v1.1’s rules. They can test cross-family agreement without providing a new held-out performance estimate.
7. **Keep the task definition constant across stages.** Message 1 does not carry v1.1 S3’s categorical rule that a secondary translation is never support for fidelity. Introducing that rule later would change both evidence and decision policy. Specify whether the Assembly is testing the rule itself or executing a fixed rule; report the two separately.

The attached Message 2 JSON also records five break cases: three exposed regressions (SR-W2, TH-2, EB-1), and the two newly pinned entity-lead controls. The empty Message 3 template cannot produce a genuine blind run here. The additional controls are accordingly labelled contextualized and kept separate from the original ten cases.

## The largest threat to P1

The present matching variables—subject, page length, age, citation density and revision activity—do not match the opportunity to preserve the **same operation**. A work page is selected to explain a particular work; a person page covers a career. Counting operative sentences without an eligible-source denominator can measure this difference in remit, or the reader’s changing threshold for specificity, as if it were a transformation imposed by entity form.

Pair a named operation and its source across entity and work/method pages. Identify operation-bearing source units before scoring. Measure preservation, changed relations and retained authority on those eligible units. Report separately the access cost of reaching an operation from the lead, since a representation can preserve it deeper in the article while making it expensive to recover.

Blind readers to expected verdicts, archive identity, operator rulings and page-type metadata. Mask names consistently without deleting the operative content. Do not remove the lead text when lead placement is the outcome being tested. Ask readers to guess page type afterward to measure residual unblinding. Use independent source-window selection and preregister material loss, harmless compression, contextual qualification and unresolved cases.

The theory remains testable. The revision needed is a more discriminating instrument: one that can find an unsupported equivalence, an inherited hierarchy, an adequate translation, a preserved operation inside a credential, and a genuine absence of operative authority without collapsing those findings together.
