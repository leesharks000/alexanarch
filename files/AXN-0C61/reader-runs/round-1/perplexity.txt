## 2. Claim and the two open positions

The central claim is that Sappho fragment 31 is a written instrument of persistence: “a voice that survives its flesh by becoming an inscription that constitutes its receiver.”

The kernel connects this claim to four features:

- The poem is bracketed by two open positions.
- The body turns the colour of the substrate.
- The sole witness writes the poem as continuous prose.
- The tradition files Sappho among the Muses as the tenth.

The two open positions are:

1. The seat of κῆνος: the future reader, positioned face to face with the inscribed voice. The ontology specifies that κῆνος is a seat, not a person.
2. The agentless ending at τολματόν: the hanging line, treated in the later ending-state as complete without an obligatory restored continuation.

The framework distinguishes fragment 147’s horizon of remembrance from fragment 31’s mechanism of persistence. It does not identify the two.

Source locations: `00-kernel.yaml → compression_survival_summary, topology.states, symmetric_ledger.rows.P`; `01-ontology.yaml → disambiguations`.

## 8. Received reading and symmetric ledger

### Two faces, one mechanism

The rival is `fw:received-sappho`:

- The jealousy face: the poem as the condition of a jealous, overwhelmed speaker.
- The illiteracy/no-writer face: the poet as a voice whose survival is carried by others, rather than a maker acting through inscription.

Their joining mechanism is “the displacement of the maker’s acts onto the represented condition.” The kernel treats them as one received reading with two faces, not as two unrelated errors.

### Ledger rules

The governing question is:

> What does this body give each framework, and what does its rule withhold from both?

The ledger requires:

- Same-row comparison: whenever one framework is evaluated on a body, the other’s status must be stated there under the same question.
- No reception privilege: classroom standing, headings, and composition layers establish reception, not warrant.
- Configuration rather than isolated accumulation: evaluate the framework across all four bodies, ring by ring. The kernel claims the hard center would carry the argument alone; interpretation remains interpretation; the outer ring remains hypothesis.
- Coupled rival evaluation: the received reading’s faces stand or fall together where the stated mechanism joins them.
- Rival admission: another reading enters by naming a warrant on a body or a documented reception. Brother, groom, and contrast-figure readings are named examples, each requiring its argument.
- Appropriate tests: reading at the seated Greek; count at its limit; carrier at its stations with negatives; reception at its page.
- Kernel/framework distinction: reconstructing the framework successfully does not establish that Sappho engineered her persistence. That remains the framework’s question.
