What this page is. This is the computational half of minting: the identity kernel and checksum are derived here exactly as the registry derives them (AXN v2: six emoji from the first six bytes of the SHA-256 of canonical bytes, via the canonical 256-glyph table). What this page is not: a registry entry. Registration — assignment of a hex position and entry into the public record — flows through the deposit transport. Anyone holding a copy of a work can verify it here without asking alexanarch.org or any custodian for permission; if this site disappears, the algorithm survives in the public implementation and in every mirror.
